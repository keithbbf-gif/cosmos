# Two jobs — Keith 2026-09-25

The two jobs are the same rail, two seats.

1. **Standalone COSMOS CODE.** It runs with no Core. Its own attempt dir, its own evidence files, its own stop gate. A person can point it at a repo and use it. It does not write `live/`. It does not hold the pen.
2. **COSMOS CODE in the core of COSMOS.** The same loop. Publish, spend, and the pen go through Core. CCr alone lands `live/`. cDeck may show it. cDeck does not become the loop.

One package. Job 1 must work if Job 2 is absent. Job 2 must not grow a second loop.

The 13 projects are reference, not these two jobs. We do not run all of them.

## Lineages (do not mix)

- Pi → Oh-my-pi: same core, opposite bet. We take the Pi bet.
- Cline → Kilo: IDE family. Chrome, not the rail.
