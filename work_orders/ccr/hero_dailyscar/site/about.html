About

DailyScar is a public field notebook from Solarolabs. It tracks the daily grind of AI work through measured misses, not pitches.

What this is

A broadsheet of short scars. Each scar names the class, describes the miss, states a law, and gives a corrective move. The tone stays fun, short, and sharp.

How a scar gets in

A scar enters only when measured, then written on disk, and never revised away. An operator confirms the behavior, saves the evidence, and logs the law beside it. Later edits add context without erasing the original miss.

A cousin in public history

The 1966 ALPAC report told funders that high-quality machine translation was not close, and that the evidence for it was thin. That one is the public historical record. It is not a scar from this shop's log.
