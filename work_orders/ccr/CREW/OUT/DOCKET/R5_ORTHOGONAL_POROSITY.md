## 0. What we are protecting (inventor-named; do not rename)

- Porosity of a coder = **VECTOR**, orthogonal to other agents/models on **named axes** — not scalar p(m).
- Pair magnitude = **disagreement_frequency × error_magnitude**.
- More disagreement → more orthogonal the pair vector.
- Observations → DB → **tensor grid T[model_i, model_j, axis]**.
- Grid seats models for **token efficiency AND error discovery**.
- Built into FORGE and every adversarial trial / PROFILE.
- **UNMEASURED until observed.** Never invent scores.
