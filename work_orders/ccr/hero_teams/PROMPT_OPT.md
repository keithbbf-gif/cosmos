# Prompt that raises every seat

The model is not the defect. The mouth fails when the wrapper and the mission disagree, when a key is not named, or when the reply is allowed to start with a plan.

## System, one paragraph
The whole reply is JavaScript. The first characters are window.LMN_PACK. Copy the keys in the example. Do not rename them. Do not assign window.LMN_PACK to {}. Do not explain.

## Example the mission must include
window.LMN_PACK = window.LMN_PACK || [];

## Mission
Four ids only. Name the engine, the goal, and players for each. better is a near-miss: one tile short, a blink late, the thumb already moving. No price. No tokens. No buying.
