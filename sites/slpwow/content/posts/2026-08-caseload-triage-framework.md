Urgency inflation is a systems problem, but clinicians still need a defensible order of operations. This framework is for **team conversations**, not unilateral gatekeeping.

## Sort by safety and participation, not convenience

Priority tier examples:

- **Tier A:** Swallow safety, sudden communication loss affecting medical care, imminent discharge planning without AAC.  
- **Tier B:** New referrals with no services where IEP/504 or care plan timelines are statutory.  
- **Tier C:** Progress monitoring and consultative supports that can cycle monthly.

Write the tiers with your admin and nursing/education partners so pushback lands on policy, not personality.

## Protect documentation time

Burnout spikes when triage steals note-writing blocks. Block **non-billable** documentation the same way you block evaluations — otherwise tiers collapse into evening charting.

## Say the quiet part with data

A simple table — referrals in, active cases, minutes available — beats moral arguments. Update it monthly in staff meetings.
