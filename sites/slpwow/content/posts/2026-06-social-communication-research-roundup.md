School teams keep adopting social skills curricula faster than fidelity data can follow. Recent syntheses emphasize **explicit teaching**, **peer involvement**, and **generalization planning** — not token economies alone.

## What tends to work

Programs that combine direct instruction, coached peer interactions, and teacher carryover with measurable fidelity show moderate effects on conversational initiation and topic maintenance for autistic learners and peers with social communication disorder profiles.

## What often fails

Pull-out groups without generalization plans, worksheets disguised as interaction, and goals that punish neurotypical masking instead of functional communication.

## Fidelity questions for your PLC

- Are peers trained with scripts or left to “be natural”?  
- Do goals reference observable behaviors in classroom settings?  
- Is progress measured outside the speech room?
