Dynamic assessment (DA) does not have to mean a separate kit. These five-minute routines fit between classes and give you **modifiability data** for reports.

## 1. Novel word learning

Introduce a nonsense object name (“This is a **teb**”). Model, ask for repetition, then embed in a short story with two retrieval probes five minutes later.

## 2. Sentence repetition with a twist

Present a grammatically complex sentence, allow one reformulation, then **change one lexical item** and ask the student to hold the frame.

## 3. Minimal teaching on a barrier game

After a baseline attempt, give **one** scripted cue (e.g., “name size before color”). Score change on the next three trials only.

## 4. Narrative micro-coaching

30-second video clip → first retell → single graphic organizer → second retell. Compare episodic structure, not just MLU.

## 5. Written summarization probe (upper grades)

Provide a 120-word passage at instructional level. Score baseline summary, teach a **one-sentence frame**, rescore.

Record cue level and time; DA is about **responsiveness**, not a single static score.
