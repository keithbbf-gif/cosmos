Teams often write beautiful cue hierarchies in plans, then session notes show **the same step for months**. Try tying fade decisions to **accuracy windows**, not calendar time alone.

## Sample fade map (articulation)

1. **Simultaneous production** — fade when ≥90% across 20 trials in structured words.  
2. **Immediate imitation** — fade when generalization probes hit ≥80% in phrases.  
3. **Visual / written prompt** — fade when spontaneous sentences meet criterion twice consecutively.  
4. **Self-rating** — introduce when student can detect errors ≥70% of the time.

## Language parallels

For complex sentences, fade from combined model + graphic organizer to organizer alone, then to self-generated organizer only after planning pauses.

## Document the fade

ASHA-aligned notes should show **what changed**, not only accuracy. One line suffices: “Reduced cue from simultaneous to imitative per hierarchy step 2 criterion met 9/1–9/5 sessions.”
