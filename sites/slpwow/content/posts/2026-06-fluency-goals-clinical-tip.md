title: "Writing measurable fluency goals without fixing stuttered moments"
description: "Clinical tip — align stuttering goals to communication participation, not fake fluency counts alone."
date: 2026-06-22

---

Fluency goals that only track “syllables stuttered per minute” can undermine counseling approaches centered on **open stuttering** and participation. Pair motor speech strategies with outcomes the client values.

## Sample goal stems (adapt, do not copy blindly)

- “Given a self-rating scale, student will use chosen fluency strategy in structured presentation, in 4/5 opportunities, as measured by clinician observation and student report.”  
- “Student will educate one novel communication partner about stuttering using a script or AAC backup, in 3/4 trials.”

## Document stuttering openly

Notes should describe **behaviors and strategies**, not shame language. Involve the client in goal selection when culturally appropriate.

## Coordination with teachers

Provide teachers language for **wait time**, turn-taking, and bullying response — not instructions to finish sentences for the student.
