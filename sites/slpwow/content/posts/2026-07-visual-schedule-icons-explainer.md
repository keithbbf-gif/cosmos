Open-licensed icon packs can accelerate AAC and classroom visual supports. They can also **fragment** systems when every teacher picks a different style.

## Good fit

- Core vocabulary boards where icons match motor access paths  
- Consistent fringe vocabulary for daily routines (bus, cafeteria, therapy)  
- Training sets for staff learning partner-assisted scanning

## Poor fit

Replacing a student’s established high-tech symbol set “because it’s prettier.” Switching symbol sets without a migration plan violates good AAC practice.

## Implementation checklist

1. Confirm license (CC, commercial use, attribution).  
2. Map icons to words the student already uses.  
3. Pilot one environment for two weeks; collect partner fidelity data.  
4. Document the decision in the communication plan.
