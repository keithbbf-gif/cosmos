Free trial counters in Google Sheets are everywhere. The failure mode is spending more time formatting cells than cueing students. This explainer keeps the sheet **small, shareable, and goal-aligned**.

## What one tab should contain

- Student ID or initials (follow district de-identification rules)  
- Date, target phoneme or language goal tag  
- Trial count, accuracy, highest independent cue level  
- One generalization column (classroom/home observation)

## What to leave out

Avoid auto-color rainbow charts for admins who never open the doc. Avoid duplicate tabs per teacher unless data governance requires it.

## Sharing and FERPA

Share with **need-to-know** staff only. Turn link sharing off; use district accounts. Never mix PHI in comment threads.

## Linking to goals

Each row’s goal tag should mirror IEP/POC language enough that progress reports write themselves: “Goal 2.b — complex sentences; see rows 14–26.”
