Burnout interventions fail when they assume spa days. These **15-minute resets** are built for back-to-back IEP weeks.

## Transition breath + wrist reset

Between rooms, two minutes of paced breathing plus wrist/shoulder mobility reduces stacked tension before the next group.

## Note sprint, then close the tab

Set a timer for 15 minutes post-session charting. When it rings, stop mid-sentence with a bracketed continuation cue — finishing marathons at night trains the wrong habit.

## One-sentence debrief partner

Text a trusted colleague one sentence: “Hardest thing today was ___.” Asymmetric venting beats rumination loops.

## Weekly ‘no’ practice

Decline or defer **one** non-required task with a polite script. Muscle memory for boundaries matters.
