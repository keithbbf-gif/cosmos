<h1>About SLP WOW</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>SLP WOW is a professional community site for speech-language pathologists who want practical resources and collegial conversation—without wading through patient-facing content.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>Mission</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Surface useful tools, summarize news that affects our scope of practice, and make word lists and templates easier to find. Quality over volume; cite sources when discussing evidence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>Relationship to WOW Therapies</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>WOW Therapies is a private practice serving clients in Southeast Arkansas. SLP WOW is the clinician-facing sibling brand—same commitment to clarity, different audience.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>Editorial standards</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li>No PHI in public posts or forum threads</li>
<li>Disclose conflicts when recommending products</li>
<li>Distinguish clinical opinion from peer-reviewed evidence</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
