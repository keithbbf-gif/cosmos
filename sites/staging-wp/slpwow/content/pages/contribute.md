<h1>Contribute to SLP WOW</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We welcome guest articles, word-list contributions, and resource links from licensed SLPs and supervised graduate clinicians (with faculty approval).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>What to send</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li>Pitch or draft in Google Doc / Markdown</li>
<li>Short author bio and credentials</li>
<li>References for clinical claims</li>
<li>Confirmation you hold rights to any handouts attached</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>What we decline</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li>Patient identifiers or clinical vignettes without IRB/ethics approval</li>
<li>Unverified miracle cures or non-evidence-based programs presented as standard care</li>
<li>Duplicate content scraped from other sites</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Contact: <em>editor@slpwow.com</em> (update before launch).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
