District forms differ, but the preparation pattern is similar.

## 48 hours before

- [ ] Current present levels with **observable** communication behaviors
- [ ] Progress on existing goals (graphs help)
- [ ] Draft goals mapped to educational impact, not only test scores
- [ ] Accommodations/consultation minutes clarified
- [ ] Interpreter confirmed if needed

## During the meeting

Lead with strengths. Tie recommendations to classroom participation and access to curriculum. Avoid jargon without a plain-language summary.

## After

Distribute amended documents on time and note who will train staff on new accommodations.

*Adapt to your district’s legal counsel guidance.*
