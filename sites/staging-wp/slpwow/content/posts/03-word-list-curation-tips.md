Flat PDF word lists age quickly. A little structure up front saves hours across semesters.

## Minimum viable metadata

| Field | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|
| Target phoneme / process | Filtering |
| Position | Initial/medial/final |
| Syllable shape | CV, CVC, multisyllabic |
| Grade band / theme | School alignment |
| Dialect note | Transparency for /r/, vowels |

## Quality checks

- Remove proper nouns clients cannot relate to
- Flag homework words that need caregiver coaching
- Version the list (`v2026-03`) when you revise
