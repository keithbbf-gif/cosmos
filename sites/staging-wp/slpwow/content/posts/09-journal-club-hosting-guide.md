Journal clubs fail when meetings run long and articles feel irrelevant. Design for **45 minutes** and a clear takeaway.

## Format

1. 5 min — clinical question
2. 15 min — methods & population (skim-friendly slide)
3. 15 min — results & limitations
4. 10 min — “What would we do differently Monday?”

## Article pick heuristics

- One setting match (school, SNF, outpatient)
- Recent but not necessarily headline-grabbing
- Open-access or shared PDF with copyright respect

SLP WOW may host a quarterly club—volunteer facilitators can sign up via Contribute.
