Between Practice Portal updates, Leader articles, and state association alerts, it is easy to miss a change that affects billing or eligibility. Try a **monthly 20-minute scan** instead of reacting to every notification.

## Suggested routine

1. Bookmark ASHA Practice Portal sections that match your setting (schools, health care, private practice).
2. Subscribe to one digest—state association or ASHA Leader—rather than five overlapping lists.
3. Log changes that affect **documentation, codes, or eligibility** in a shared team note.

## When to escalate

If an update touches Medicaid, school eligibility, or telepractice rules in your state, loop in your administrator or compliance contact the same week—not at audit time.
