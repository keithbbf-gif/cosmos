Families rarely need the full ICD vocabulary on day one. Colleagues benefit from precise terms in documentation and research chats.

## With clients and families

- “Stuttering,” “bumpy speech,” or the term the client prefers
- Focus on participation, accommodation, and strategies—not shame

## In documentation & CE

- Distinguish developmental disfluency from persistent stuttering
- Note avoidance behaviors and impact on quality of life
- Reference evidence-based approaches you are implementing

ASHA and the Stuttering Foundation offer public-friendly handouts you can link from your practice site—not duplicated here to avoid stale URLs.
