This snapshot summarizes themes commonly cited in CAS reviews—**not** a substitute for reading primary sources or clinical judgment.

## Themes clinicians discuss

- **High practice dose** with careful shaping of motor plans
- **Multisensory cueing** faded systematically
- **Prosody and functional words** early in the plan
- **Caregiver training** for short, daily practice

## Documentation tip

Note baseline motor speech signs, therapy approach, dose (minutes/week), and functional outcomes (intelligibility in conversation, participation). Payers and teams increasingly ask for measurable functional change.
