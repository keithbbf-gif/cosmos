Screening tools differ by facility. These reminders support **communication**, not tool substitution.

## Clarify roles

- Nursing screen: who passes, who flags, who to call
- SLP eval: when instrumental study is indicated
- Diet changes: only with authorized orders

## Red flags to escalate promptly

New wet voice, fever with aspiration risk, significant weight loss, or patient report of food sticking.

## Documentation

Record screen result, referrals made, and interim NPO/diet instructions per protocol.

Pair with your facility’s approved form—do not invent a new screen here without medical leadership sign-off.
