Telepractice expanded access—and paperwork. A short pre-flight reduces risk.

## Before first tele session

- Verify **state licensure** and payer telehealth policy for each client
- Obtain informed consent covering technology limits and privacy
- Use a HIPAA-appropriate platform; document which one
- Plan backup (phone) if connectivity fails

## Session note essentials

Location of client and clinician, platform, participants present, activities, client response, and plan—same rigor as in-person when payer requires it.

Laws vary by state and payer; this is educational, not legal advice.
