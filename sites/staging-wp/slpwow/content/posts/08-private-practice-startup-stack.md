There is no universal stack, but founders often over-buy before caseload exists.

## Start here

1. **Scheduling + reminders** tied to your calendar
2. **Billing or superbill workflow** aligned with your payer mix
3. **Secure notes** with BAA on file
4. **Simple website** (WordPress + GeneratePress on MochaHost is one path) separate from professional community sites like SLP WOW

## Defer until revenue supports it

Fancy CRM, multiple telehealth vendors, and niche SEO packages. Measure no-show rate and collection time first.
