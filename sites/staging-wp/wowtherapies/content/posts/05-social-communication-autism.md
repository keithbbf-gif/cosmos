Autism affects how people process social information and communication. Speech-language therapy can target **pragmatic language**: using words and nonverbal cues to connect with others.

## Common goal areas

- Initiating and maintaining conversation
- Understanding perspective and figurative language
- Participating in group play or classroom discussion
- Self-advocacy and communication of needs

## Neurodiversity-affirming care

Effective therapy builds skills **and** respects how the child communicates best. Goals should be chosen with families and, when appropriate, the child—focusing on quality of life, not conformity for its own sake.
