After stroke, traumatic brain injury, or certain illnesses, thinking and communication often change together. Speech-language pathologists address **cognitive-communication**: the overlap of language, memory, attention, and executive skills used in daily tasks.

## Therapy may focus on

- Following multi-step directions in real-life contexts
- Organizing medications, schedules, or finances with compensatory strategies
- Conversation skills in social and work settings
- Written communication for email or forms

## Family role

Caregivers learn how to cue without taking over, reduce distractions, and celebrate functional gains. Goals should reflect what the person wants to do again—call family, manage appointments, return to hobbies.
