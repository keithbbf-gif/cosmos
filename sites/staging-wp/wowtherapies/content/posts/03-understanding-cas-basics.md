Childhood apraxia of speech (CAS) is a **motor speech disorder**. The brain has difficulty planning and coordinating the movements needed for clear speech. It is not caused by weak muscles or hearing loss, though those should always be ruled out.

## How CAS may appear

- Inconsistent errors on the same word
- Difficulty moving smoothly from sound to sound
- More trouble with longer words or sentences
- Better imitation in some contexts than spontaneous speech

## Treatment approach

Evidence-based CAS therapy emphasizes **high repetition**, **multisensory cues**, and **careful progression** from sounds to words to connected speech. Progress can be steady but often requires consistent therapy and home support.
