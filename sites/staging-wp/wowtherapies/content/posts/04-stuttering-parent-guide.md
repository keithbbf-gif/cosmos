Most children have moments of disfluency as language grows. Stuttering that persists beyond early childhood, or that bothers your child, deserves professional input.

## Helpful responses at home

- Give extra time to finish thoughts without finishing sentences for them
- Keep eye contact and unhurried listening
- Reduce pressure in speaking situations (one question at a time)

## When to consult an SLP

- Stuttering lasts more than six months
- There is tension, struggle, or avoidance of speaking
- A family history of stuttering and new symptoms appear

Fluency therapy for children often involves both the child and caregivers. Early support can improve confidence and communication participation.
