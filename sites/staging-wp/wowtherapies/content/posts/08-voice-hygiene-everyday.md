Voice problems often build gradually from strain, dehydration, reflux, or overuse. Small daily habits reduce irritation and support therapy outcomes.

## Habits that help

- Hydrate steadily through the day
- Limit shouting and prolonged whispering
- Use a portable amplifier in loud classrooms when possible
- Warm up the voice before heavy use; cool down afterward
- Address throat clearing—try a hard swallow or sip of water instead

## When to see a professional

Hoarseness lasting more than two weeks, pain, or sudden voice loss should be evaluated by an ENT and may include SLP voice therapy.
