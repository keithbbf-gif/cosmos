Carryover at home works best when practice fits your routine. Five minutes most days beats a long session once a week.

## Keep it playful

- **Sound hunts:** Find objects that start with the target sound during a walk or snack time.
- **Books on repeat:** Choose one picture book and pause for the child to fill in a repeated word.
- **Celebrate effort:** Praise clear attempts, not only perfect productions.

## What to avoid

- Long correction chains that frustrate your child
- Practicing only at the table—speech happens everywhere

Your therapist will assign specific sounds and levels (words, phrases, sentences). Follow their cues so home practice matches the clinical plan.
