<h1>Adult speech-language services</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Communication and swallowing changes can affect independence, relationships, and quality of life. Treatment focuses on functional goals—what matters to you day to day.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>Clinical focus areas</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li><strong>Cognitive-communication</strong> — attention, memory, problem solving, and organization after injury or illness</li>
<li><strong>Dysphagia</strong> — swallowing safety strategies; coordination with physicians and dietitians</li>
<li><strong>Voice</strong> — vocal hygiene and therapy for strain, weakness, or neurologic voice disorders</li>
<li><strong>Motor speech</strong> — support for dysarthria and related neurologic conditions</li>
<li><strong>Aphasia &amp; language</strong> — understanding and expressing language after stroke or brain injury</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>Referrals &amp; documentation</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Physician referrals may be required depending on diagnosis and insurance. Bring recent medical notes, medication list, and insurance card to your first visit.</p>
