<h1>Pediatric speech-language therapy</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Early support can make a lasting difference. WOW Therapies helps children build understandable speech, strong language skills, confident communication, and social connection.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>Areas we address</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li><strong>Articulation &amp; phonology</strong> — speech sound development and patterns</li>
<li><strong>Stuttering</strong> — fluency strategies for children and teens</li>
<li><strong>Childhood apraxia of speech (CAS)</strong> — motor planning for speech</li>
<li><strong>Language</strong> — understanding and using words, sentences, and stories</li>
<li><strong>Autism &amp; social communication</strong> — joint attention, conversation, and pragmatic skills</li>
<li><strong>Early intervention</strong> — play-based therapy for toddlers and preschoolers</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>What families can expect</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sessions are tailored to your child’s age, interests, and goals. You will receive practical home practice ideas—not busywork—so progress continues between visits.</p>
