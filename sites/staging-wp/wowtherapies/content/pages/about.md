<h2>Christina Chambers, MS, CCC-SLP</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Christina Chambers is a speech-language pathologist with more than <strong>16 years</strong> of clinical experience serving pediatric and adult clients. She founded WOW Therapies to offer approachable, evidence-based care for families and adults in <strong>Southeast Arkansas</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Christina’s work spans school-based, medical, and private practice settings. She values clear explanations, measurable goals, and collaboration with caregivers, teachers, and healthcare teams.</p>
