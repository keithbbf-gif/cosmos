About WOW Therapies

A private practice focused on access, communication, and quality of life for clients across Southeast Arkansas.

Christina Chambers, M.S., CCC-SLP

Christina Chambers is a speech-language pathologist with more than sixteen years of experience in early childhood diagnostics and speech-language therapy. Her background includes comprehensive evaluations and treatment for young children, along with adult rehabilitation for cognitive-communication, dysphagia, and voice needs following stroke and other neurological events.

Philosophy of care

  • Access — therapy should be reachable and understandable, not buried in jargon
  • Communication — every plan ties back to real conversations, relationships, and independence
  • Quality of life — progress is measured by what clients and families care about day to day

Pediatric & adult scope

Pediatric work includes articulation, phonological disorders, stuttering, childhood apraxia of speech, autism-related social language, and receptive/expressive language. Adult work includes cognitive-communication, swallowing (dysphagia), and voice. WOW Therapies is a speech-language pathology practice; when your care team includes occupational or physical therapy, coordination happens around shared functional goals.

Credentials

Christina holds the Certificate of Clinical Competence in Speech-Language Pathology (CCC-SLP) through the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA).
