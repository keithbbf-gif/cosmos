Who we serve

Led by Christina Chambers, SLP, with more than sixteen years in early childhood diagnostics and speech-language therapy, WOW Therapies offers individualized care — never a one-size-fits-all program.

Children & teens

Articulation, phonological disorders, stuttering, childhood apraxia of speech, autism-related social language, and receptive or expressive language delays.

Learn about pediatric therapy →

Adults

Cognitive-communication after injury or illness, dysphagia (swallowing), and voice changes following stroke or other neurological conditions.

Learn about adult therapy →

Families & caregivers

Plain-language education, home strategies, and coordination with schools and medical teams when appropriate.

What to expect

We do not publish patient stories or reviews on this site. Instead, you can expect a respectful intake process, goals you help shape, and progress discussed in clear terms — without pressure or gimmicks.

Evaluation first

We learn how communication or swallowing affects real life, then recommend a plan based on clinical findings and your priorities.

Collaborative care

When you consent, we can share strategies with teachers, physicians, or other therapists (OT/PT included at the care-team level when relevant to your goals).

Educational notice: Information on this website is for general education only and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified clinician for concerns about you or your child.
