# Childhood apraxia of speech: early signs to discuss with your SLP

**Childhood apraxia of speech (CAS)** is a motor speech disorder: the brain has difficulty planning and coordinating the movements for clear speech. It is **not** caused by weak muscles or low intelligence, and it requires careful differential diagnosis.

## Signs that prompt a thorough evaluation

- Limited consonants and vowels despite trying to talk
- Inconsistent errors — “dog” may sound different each attempt
- Difficulty imitating simple syllables when prompted
- Groping or searching movements of the mouth
- More clarity in automatic phrases (“oh no”) than new words
- Language understanding may be stronger than spoken expression
