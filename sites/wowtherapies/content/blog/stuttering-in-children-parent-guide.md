# Stuttering in children: calm, informed next steps

Many children pass through short bursts of disfluency while language grows. **Stuttering** is different when repetitions, stretches on sounds, or blocks happen often, increase over weeks, or come with tension or avoidance.

## What to listen for

- Whole-word or part-word repetitions (“can-can-can I go?”)
- Prolonged sounds (“sssssnake”)
- Silent blocks — mouth open but no sound
- Facial or body tension, or avoiding speaking

## What helps at home (while you seek guidance)

- Give extra time to finish thoughts; avoid finishing sentences for them
- Keep eye contact and unhurried listening
- Reduce corrections like “slow down” or “start over” in the moment — those can increase pressure [CITE NEEDED: Stuttering Foundation or Lidcombe/early intervention evidence summary]

## When to call an SLP
