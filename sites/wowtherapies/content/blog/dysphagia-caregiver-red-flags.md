# Dysphagia at home: when to call your medical team

**Dysphagia** means difficulty swallowing safely. Speech-language pathologists evaluate swallowing and recommend textures, strategies, and exercises in coordination with physicians.

## Red flags — seek prompt medical care

- Coughing or choking on liquids or solids regularly
- Wet or gurgly voice after eating or drinking
- Fever plus new cough after meals (possible aspiration)
- Unplanned weight loss or dehydration
- Food sticking in the throat every meal

## What SLP evaluation may include

Clinical swallow exam and, when ordered, imaging studies. Recommendations might include modified food textures, compensatory maneuvers, or referral for further testing.

## At mealtimes while you wait for appointments

- Sit fully upright; stay upright after eating
- Small bites and sips; avoid rushing
- Do not hide pills in food without pharmacist and physician approval

## Educational disclaimer

This list is not exhaustive. Call 911 for choking emergencies or sudden breathing difficulty.
