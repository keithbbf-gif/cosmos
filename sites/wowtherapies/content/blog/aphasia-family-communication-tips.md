# Aphasia after stroke: communication tips for families

**Aphasia** affects how a person understands or produces language after stroke or brain injury. It does **not** affect intelligence. Families in Southeast Arkansas often become communication partners overnight — these tips complement formal speech-language therapy.

## Make conversations easier

- One topic at a time; quiet room when possible
- Yes/no questions before open questions
- Write key words, draw, or use photos
- Confirm meaning: “You’re asking about dinner — right?”
- Allow extra time — resist filling every silence

## What to avoid

- Talking down or speaking loudly by default (hearing may be fine)
- Correcting every word in public
- Excluding the person from decisions “because it’s faster”
