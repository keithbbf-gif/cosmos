Pediatric speech-language therapy

Playful, structured support for children who need help with speech sounds, language, fluency, or social communication — grounded in more than sixteen years of early childhood diagnostics experience.

Areas we commonly address

  • Articulation & phonology — difficulty being understood; patterns of sound errors
  • Receptive & expressive language — following directions, vocabulary, grammar, telling stories
  • Stuttering — repetitions, prolongations, or blocks; impact on confidence
  • Childhood apraxia of speech (CAS) — planning and coordinating speech movements
  • Autism & social communication — conversation, perspective-taking, and functional language at home and school

How therapy can look

Sessions are tailored to your child’s age, interests, and goals. Parents and caregivers are partners: you receive practical strategies to practice in daily routines — not homework for its own sake.

When to consider an evaluation

If teachers, your pediatrician, or you notice persistent difficulty being understood, limited vocabulary for age, frustration around speaking, or trouble interacting with peers, a speech-language evaluation can clarify next steps. See our parent guides or request an evaluation.
