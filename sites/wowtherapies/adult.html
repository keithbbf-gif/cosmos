Adult speech-language pathology

Respectful, goal-oriented therapy for adults rebuilding communication, swallowing safety, or voice after illness or injury — with support for families and caregivers.

Clinical focus areas

  • Cognitive-communication — attention, memory strategies, problem-solving, and functional language for work and home
  • Dysphagia — swallowing evaluation and treatment in coordination with your medical team
  • Aphasia & language after stroke — understanding and expressing language, reading and writing as needed
  • Voice — changes after stroke, neurological conditions, or overuse; breath support and vocal hygiene

Care that fits real life

Goals come from what matters to you — ordering at a restaurant, managing medications, returning to hobbies, or staying safe at mealtimes. When occupational or physical therapy is part of your broader plan, we communicate as a team around your priorities (WOW Therapies provides speech-language pathology; OT/PT may be separate providers).

Getting started

A physician referral may be required by your insurance plan. Contact us to discuss scheduling an evaluation and what documents to bring.

Educational notice: Swallowing changes can be urgent. Seek emergency care for choking, aspiration pneumonia symptoms, or sudden inability to swallow — do not rely on this website in an emergency.
