Families often wonder whether a child’s speech is “on track.” While every child differs, clinicians look for steady growth in understanding words, using words, and joining conversations.

## Understanding vs. using words

Receptive language (understanding) usually expands before expressive language (speaking). If understanding seems limited compared with peers, an evaluation can clarify whether support is helpful.

## When to ask questions

Consider a consultation if you notice limited vocabulary growth over several months, frustration during communication, or difficulty being understood by familiar listeners past early childhood.
