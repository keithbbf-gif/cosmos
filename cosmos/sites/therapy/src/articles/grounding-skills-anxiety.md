When anxiety spikes, your nervous system may feel stuck in high alert. Grounding invites attention back to the present moment.

## 5-4-3-2-1 senses

Notice five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell, and one you taste. Move slowly — there is no perfect pace.

## Breath pacing

Try a gentle inhale for four counts and exhale for six. Lengthening the exhale can signal safety to the body.

## When to seek more support

If anxiety limits daily life or you are in crisis, contact a licensed professional or local emergency services. This article cannot assess your individual situation.
