## 1. Local demand context

### 1.1 Place and travel radius

Monticello is the Drew County seat and a small regional hub (University of Arkansas at Monticello, Baptist Health Medical Center–Drew County, Southeast Arkansas Education Service Cooperative). Nearby communities that sit in a realistic same-day driving radius include Warren (Bradley County), Star City (Lincoln), McGehee and Dumas (Desha), Hamburg and Crossett (Ashley), Lake Village / Dermott (Chicot), Rison (Cleveland), and, at the outer edge, Pine Bluff (Jefferson).

### 1.4 Early intervention (birth–3)

Arkansas’s IDEA Part C program is **First Connections** (Arkansas Department of Education lead agency). Services are in the child’s natural environment (home, childcare, community). Speech-language pathology is a listed early-intervention service. Families choose among certified local providers; the service coordinator can produce a county provider list.

First Connections rules (Code of Arkansas Rules; program policies) require the SLP (or SLPA under supervision) to:

- Hold an Arkansas license in good standing,
- Complete First Connections Core Competencies certification and ongoing PD,
- Enroll in Arkansas Medicaid.

**Flag:** The published First Connections rule text at 20 CAR § 1002-617 currently says the license must be “in good standing with the **Arkansas State Medical Board**.” The SLP licensing board is **ABESPA** (Arkansas Department of Health), not the Medical Board. Treat that wording as a **document conflict to confirm with First Connections** before relying on it.

Statewide referral line: (800) 643-8258; `firstconnectionsreferrals@ade.arkansas.gov`. ASHA’s Arkansas state page lists a First Connections contact at ADE (verify current name/email on the ASHA or First Connections site before using).

SEARK Coop HIPPY and early-childhood special education are parallel preschool pipelines, not the same as Part C.

### 1.6 Telepractice overlap

ABESPA rules allow telepractice if a professional relationship is established, the standard of care matches in-person care, records are kept, and **the clinician is licensed by ABESPA when the patient is in Arkansas**. Correspondence-only care is not enough. Out-of-state clinicians must be Arkansas-licensed (or apply within the first 30 days under older rule language — confirm current rule). Arkansas Medicaid therapy telemedicine is reimbursed like in-person services and is subject to the same unit caps (manual §214.600).

Drew County’s 80.5% broadband-subscription rate and rural last-mile quality mean telepractice is a **supplement**, not a full substitute, especially for evaluations, pediatric feeding, and dysphagia.

### 1.7 Statewide workforce vs local access (do not conflate)

ASHA’s 2024 certified SLP-to-population ratios rank **Arkansas #1 nationally** (101.0 CCC-SLPs per 100,000 residents; 3,118 certified SLPs vs ~3.09 million people). That is a *state* figure. It does **not** prove Monticello is saturated.

Counter-signals that local access is still thin:

- Meaningful Movement (Monticello pediatric OT/SLP clinic, founded by locals who returned in 2023) states on its site that families were “driving sometimes over an hour” or relocating for pediatric therapy.
- ACMC Speech Therapy (Crossett) markets “why travel for care.”
- ASHA 2024 Schools Survey: **80%** of West South Central respondents (AR, LA, OK, TX) said job openings exceeded job seekers; **26%** of school SLPs nationally named a local SLP shortage as the top barrier to a manageable caseload.
- Arkansas school SLPs reported a median **actual** caseload of **40** and a “manageable” caseload of **39** (ASHA 2024 caseload report) — little slack. State caseload guidance lists a **maximum of 45** (ASHA 2025 state caseload chart, citing ADE).

**Uncertainty:** No public 2026 vacancy list for Monticello or Drew Central SLP positions was found during this research. Statewide supply being high and *local* access being thin can both be true.

## 3. Business models for a self-contractor SLP

### 3.1 Model A — 1099 / purchased-service contractor to schools or the coop (usually the first dollar)

**How it works.** A district or SEARK Coop pays an hourly or daily rate for IEP evaluation, treatment, meetings, and paperwork. The contractor invoices; the payer issues Form 1099. Arkansas Medicaid’s therapy manual (§201.300) expressly allows a school district, education service cooperative, or Early Intervention Day Treatment program to **employ or contract** therapists. If the entity **bills Medicaid** for the contractor’s work, the performing SLP must meet §202.310 and be Medicaid-enrolled (or, if *employed*, may instead request a Practitioner Identification Number).

**Caseload pattern.** ASHA 2024 Arkansas school median actual caseload **40**; state maximum **45**. Rural itinerant work is fewer students per site, more windshield time. Contractors are typically paid only for **billable or contracted hours** (direct + specified indirect). IEP meetings, evaluations, and make-up minutes are the hours that blow up a too-low hourly rate.

**Published pay benchmarks (not SE AR quotes):**

| Source | Figure | Geography / notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASHA 2024 Schools Survey | Contractor median **$52/hr** full-time, **$58/hr** part-time | National; 12% of school SLP respondents were contractors |
| Same survey | Rural contractors **$50/hr** FT, **$55/hr** PT | Population-density split |
| Same survey | South contractors overall **$50/hr**; South FT **$48/hr**; South PT **$57.28/hr** | Census South (includes Arkansas) |
| Same survey | Telepractice-office hourly median **$51/hr** (contractors) | National |
| Same survey | Arkansas median **academic-year salary** (employees) **$62,000** | Reportable states table; 9–10 month |
| Dumas SD 2024–25 published contract list | District Speech Pathologist **$73,925** | One named W-2 employee; not a 1099 rate |
| ADE rule 6 CAR § 189 | **$5,000/year** incentive for **certified, full-time employed** school/coop SLPs with CCC and an Arkansas teaching license | **Explicitly excludes purchased-service contractors** |

**Implication:** A 1099 rate that only matches the ASHA South FT contractor median ($48) can lose to a W-2 district job once the $5,000 bonus, benefits, and unpaid-summer risk are counted. The 1099 path wins on flexibility, multi-district stacking, and (sometimes) a higher headline hourly rate. **Get written local quotes; do not use these medians as a bid.**

Out-of-state public-contract examples ($70–$115/hr teletherapy or Illinois district contracts) are **not** Arkansas prices and should not be used in a Monticello bid.

### 3.2 Model B — 1099 to a clinic, hospital, home-health agency, or staffing firm

Examples of *possible* counterparties (capacity unknown): ProCARE/Therex, Baptist Health hospital or home health, BCMC, ACMC, SNF rehab companies, pediatric clinics that need overflow. ASHA 2023 Health Care Survey medians: **$45/hr** full-time, **$50/hr** part-time; per-visit median **$62.16** (higher in home health). These are **employee/contractor wages paid by facilities**, not owner collections.

Home-health and SNF work is typically **per visit**, with unpaid documentation and drive time. Rural visit density is the constraint.

### 3.3 Model C — First Connections / preschool itinerant

Enroll as (or join) a certified local provider program, complete FC PATHS Core Competencies, take IFSP cases in homes and childcare. Pay is usually Medicaid (and/or program rates — **obtain the current First Connections fee schedule; not located as a simple public hourly list in this review**). Caregiver-coaching model, high travel, cancellations when families are not home.

### 3.4 Model D — Independent private practice (Medicaid / commercial / private-pay)

Form an LLC/PLLC, get Type 1 + Type 2 NPIs, enroll in Arkansas Medicaid (and optionally Medicare and commercial plans), lease or share space (or go mobile). Collections equal **allowed amount minus denials, no-shows, and overhead** — not the ASHA wage tables.

Arkansas Medicaid therapy **outpatient SLP under the OT/PT/SLP manual is for beneficiaries under 21 in EPSDT**. Adult therapy under that manual is **not covered**; adults route through hospital, home health, hospice, Adult Developmental Day Treatment, or physician manuals. That single rule **steers a Monticello private practice toward pediatrics** unless the owner also enrolls in Medicare/home-health/hospital arrangements for adults.

Posted Arkansas Medicaid maximums (DHS therapy fee schedule, run date 27 May 2026):

| Code | Medicaid max | Typical use (CPT; confirm with current manual) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 92507 | $29.02 | Individual speech treatment |
| 92507-UB | $23.20 | Assistant |
| 92508 | $7.92 | Group |
| 92521–92524 UA | $49.44 each | Fluency / sound / language / voice evaluations (as listed) |
| 92607 | $111.38 | SGD evaluation (as listed) |
| 96112 U6 / 96113 U6 | $108.00 / $54.00 | Developmental testing (as listed) |

**Critical uncertainty — what is a “unit”?** The manual pays SLP services “per unit,” with **six units per week** before an extension-of-benefits request. CPT 92507 is nationally an **untimed** treatment code (often one unit per day), while PT 97110 (same $29.02 on this fee schedule) is a 15-minute code. Whether Arkansas Medicaid treats 92507 as one session or as a 15-minute slice **must be confirmed with a current AR Medicaid biller or provider representative** before any revenue model. If 92507 = one session, six units/week ≈ six visits/week per child. If 92507 were treated like a 15-minute code, six units = 90 minutes/week. Those are completely different businesses.

DHS raised individual 92507 from $25.24 to **$29.02** effective 1 April 2023 (16% then 15% staged increases under EO 19-02).

### 3.5 Model E — Hybrid (most realistic for this locale)

Example shape (illustrative, not a recommendation): 3–4 school days under a coop/district 1099 + 1–2 late-afternoon/Friday pediatric clinic or First Connections blocks + optional summer medical/ESY. Matches the school calendar, uses empty summer for medical or EI, and avoids depending on one payer.

### 3.6 1099 vs W-2 vs owner (decision table)

| | School/coop **employee (W-2)** | School/coop **1099** | Clinic/home-health **1099** | **Owner** private practice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pay shape | Salary + possible $5k ADE bonus | Hourly, school year | Hourly or per-visit | Collections minus overhead |
| Benefits | Typically yes | No | No | Self-funded |
| Summer | Unpaid or optional ESY | Unpaid unless ESY contracted | Year-round possible | Year-round possible |
| Control of caseload | Low | Medium | Medium | High |
| Credential extras | ADE ancillary license + IDEAS modules | ABESPA (ADE license if the district requires it — **ask**) | ABESPA; facility privileging | ABESPA + NPI + payer enrollment |

## 4. Licensing and credentials (high level)

*Not a licensing opinion. Read the current ABESPA rule PDF and ADE/Medicaid manuals before filing.*

### 4.1 Arkansas Board (ABESPA)

- **Board:** Arkansas Board of Examiners in Speech-Language Pathology and Audiology, Arkansas Department of Health, 4815 W. Markham, Slot 72, Little Rock, AR 72205; (501) 537-9151; `abespa@arkansas.gov`.
- **SLP license:** Master’s or doctoral degree in communicative disorders; passing Praxis **or** ASHA certification; **no less than 36 weeks** full-time professional experience (or part-time equivalent) under a licensed SLP (Ark. Code Ann. § 17-100-302; ABESPA Rule §2).
- **ASHA CCC since 1 Jan 1993** is accepted as evidence of the required degree (Rule §2.8). ASHA’s state page: “The ASHA CCC is accepted as meeting requirements for licensure.”
- **Provisional license** for the clinical fellowship: apply **within 30 days** of starting the experience; renewable annually; expires and cannot be renewed after **36 months** from initiation.
- **30-day grace:** on a completed initial packet, ABESPA describes a 30-day grace period to begin work while the application is processed.
- **Fees:** ADH page states that **starting 16 July 2023**, the application fee is **$5** (was $100) and inactive/reactivation **$2**, per Act 114 of 2023. Confirm current online portal amounts.
- **Renewal:** licenses expire **June 30** each year. CPE: **10 hours/year** (15 if dual-licensed), at least 5 in content area I (ASHA Arkansas page and ABESPA graduate presentation).
- **ASHA membership is not required** for state licensure (ABESPA Q&A).
- **Licensee search:** linked from the ABESPA board page (“Current ABESPA Licensee List”).
- **SLPA:** bachelor’s in SLP or an SLPA associate program plus fieldwork hours (see ABESPA application page). Supervision limits differ between ABESPA and Medicaid — read both.

### 4.2 ASHA CCC-SLP

2020 CFCC standards (in effect for applications received under those standards): CAA-accredited (or candidacy) graduate program; Praxis; Clinical Fellowship of **≥36 weeks and 1,260 hours**, ≥80% direct clinical contact, mentor who holds CCC-SLP with 9 months’ post-cert experience and 2 hours of supervision CE. Application: [ASHA SLP Certification](https://www.asha.org/Certification/SLPCertification).

Many school and medical contracts **prefer or require** the CCC even when ABESPA would license without ASHA membership.

### 4.3 School (ADE) credential

ASHA’s Arkansas page: school SLP uses an **Ancillary License — Speech Language Pathology (K-12)**: master’s or ASHA CCC, national exam, criminal background check, and IDEAS modules (parental involvement, child maltreatment, teen suicide awareness, dyslexia awareness). Continuing education listed as **36 clock hours/year** for the school credential (different from ABESPA’s 10).

Arkansas Code § 17-100-104 / Medicaid §202.330 describes a **licensure exemption** for a person working *solely* under a public school system who holds a valid ADE speech therapist / SLP certificate. A **self-contractor who also sees clinic, home-health, or First Connections clients cannot rely on that exemption** for those settings. When in doubt, hold the ABESPA license.

### 4.4 ASLP-IC (interstate compact)

Arkansas **enacted** the Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology Interstate Compact (SB91, 2023; Ark. Code Ann. § 17-100-401 et seq.). ABESPA’s site links the compact and posted 2026 rulemaking notices.

As of this research (14 September 2026), **Arkansas is not yet issuing or honoring compact privileges**. ASLP-IC’s July 2026 fee table lists Arkansas privilege status as **“not yet.”** CompactConnect registration was described as open for Louisiana, Ohio, Tennessee, and West Virginia only. **Until Arkansas onboards, an Arkansas practice still requires an Arkansas license** (or the temporary 30-day cooperation pathway for out-of-state visitors — confirm with ABESPA). Louisiana’s operational status matters only if you also want to treat patients physically in Louisiana (Ashley County is near the state line).

### 4.5 Arkansas Medicaid

- Portal: `https://portal.mmis.arkansas.gov/armedicaid/provider`
- Individual and group applications; Type 2 NPI if paid to a business TIN.
- SLP participation (§202.310): CCC **or** education/experience to qualify for the CCC **or** actively in the CF, **plus** license in the state of residence.
- Under-21 EPSDT therapy manual; 6 units/week without extension; physician/CNP referral and prescription within 12 months; treatment plan; session-level documentation.
- Schools/coops/EIDTs may contract and bill; performing provider on the claim.
- Telemedicine covered on the same terms as in-person.
- Fee schedule: DHS “Occupational Therapy, Physical Therapy, and Speech-Language Pathology Services,” run date 27 May 2026.

### 4.6 Medicare (adult medical path)

CMS: obtain NPI via NPPES, then enroll through **PECOS** (or CMS-855I) as a **Qualified Speech Language Pathologist**. Needed if you will bill Part B for adult outpatients. Hospital/home-health Part A work is usually billed by the agency, not the 1099 individually — confirm the contract.

### 4.7 Commercial insurance

No Arkansas-wide SLP commercial-fee schedule was found. Meaningful Movement’s third-party listings mention Medicaid and Aetna — **unverified on the clinic’s own insurance page** (the public homepage did not list plans). Expect a long credentialing queue and a pediatric, Medicaid-heavy mix given local income.

## 5. Startup checklist

Order is practical, not a legal sequence. Have an Arkansas attorney and CPA review entity and tax choices.

### Credentials and payer IDs

1. Confirm **ABESPA** license (or provisional) is active; calendar **June 30** renewal and 10 CPE hours.
2. Confirm **CCC-SLP** (or CF plan + mentor) if contracts will require it.
3. If bidding school *employee* roles: ADE ancillary license + IDEAS modules.
4. Apply for **Type 1 NPI** (individual) at [NPPES](https://nppes.cms.hhs.gov). If an LLC/PLLC will be the pay-to entity, also apply for **Type 2 NPI**. Taxonomy commonly used for SLPs is 235Z00000X (confirm on NPPES).
5. **Arkansas Medicaid** enrollment (individual; group if paid to TIN). Upload license, W-9, EFT, disclosures. Report NPI in the portal so claims do not deny “provider not on file.”
6. **Medicare PECOS** only if you will bill Part B as the supplier.
7. **First Connections** Core Competencies (FC PATHS) if taking birth–3.
8. CAQH / commercial credentialing if you will bill private plans (optional, slow).

### Business infrastructure

9. **Entity:** Arkansas Secretary of State LLC Certificate of Organization (Form LL-01) — online filing fee **$45**, paper **$50**. Professional-service companies must use a **PLLC/PLC** name style on the SOS form. SOS may require licensing-board authorization for a professional LLC — **ask ABESPA and SOS; do not assume**. Franchise-tax report is due **May 1** of the year after formation (amount not confirmed here).
10. EIN from IRS; separate business bank account; bookkeeping that tracks **mileage by purpose**.
11. **Insurance (get quotes; no premium published here):** professional liability (malpractice), general liability, hired/non-owned auto if driving to homes/schools, cyber/HIPAA if you store PHI. Many school contracts specify minimums and additional-insured endorsements. ASHA members are often pointed to association-endorsed markets — compare independently.
12. **Contracts:** written scope (direct, evals, IEPs, make-up minutes, no-shows, mileage, materials, cancellation of school days, ESY, telepractice), invoicing cadence, 1099 language, non-solicit (read carefully), termination, records custody. Have counsel review.
13. **Compliance:** HIPAA (if you are a covered entity or BA), FERPA (school records), Medicaid documentation §204.200, abuse-reporting, background checks the district will require.

### Operations

14. **Travel radius:** pick a hard cap (many rural contractors start at 30–45 minutes). Price mileage or build it into the hourly rate. IRS standard mileage rate is a tax topic for a CPA, not a bid formula by itself.
15. **Scheduling:** school bell times + drive + Medicaid 6-unit/week cap + IEP calendar. Hamburg’s late-July start vs Crossett’s mid-August start is a real stacking problem.
16. **EHR / billing (commonly marketed to SLPs; not endorsements):** school work often lives in the **district IEP system** (you may not need a second clinical EHR for school-only 1099). Clinic/Medicaid work needs documentation + claims: products frequently marketed to therapy practices include Fusion, TheraPlatform, WebPT, and SimplePractice; some contractors use a clearinghouse plus a spreadsheet. Choose based on CMS-1500 / Arkansas Medicaid billing, not marketing. School-only contractors sometimes invoice from a timer + PDF.
17. **Materials and tests:** standardized language/articulation kits, AAC trial devices, portable endoscopy is **not** a startup item unless you are building a swallow clinic with hospital privileges.
18. **Policies:** no-show, cancellation, private-pay fee sheet, sliding scale (optional), supervision if you will use an SLPA (Medicaid: max 5 assistants; immediate availability including telecom; in-person observation every 30 days — §203.000). ABESPA SLPA ratios may be tighter (ASHA page: 2 FT or 3 PT). **Follow the stricter applicable rule.**

### 6.4 Scenario C — Hybrid school + Medicaid pediatric clinic (unit-ambiguous)

**School piece:** 16 paid hours/week × 36 × $50 = **$28,800**.

**Clinic piece (two unit interpretations — pick one after you verify):**

- *If 92507 = one treatment session* at $29.02, 8 paid sessions/week × 46 weeks (year-round minus holidays) = **$10,679**. Combined gross ≈ **$39,500**.
- *If a unit were 15 minutes* and you bill 4 units/hour for 6 clinic hours/week: 24 units × 46 × $29.02 ≈ **$32,038**. Combined gross ≈ **$60,800**.

Those two clinic interpretations differ by ~$21,000. **Do not budget until a biller confirms Arkansas’s 92507 unit.** Also apply the 6-units/week/child cap, extension-of-benefits lag, ~10–20% denial/no-show haircut, and the fact that evaluation codes (9252x at $49.44) are occasional.

### 6.5 Scenario D — Home-health / medical per-visit overlay (summer)

**Assumptions:** 8 visits/week × 12 summer weeks × $62.16 (ASHA 2023 per-visit median) = **$5,967**. Useful as *gap-fill*, not a living.

## 7. Go-to-market in Monticello / SE AR

### 7.1 Who actually buys SLP time here

1. **SEARK Education Service Cooperative** — related-services and early-childhood staffing for ~15 districts. Main number (870) 367-6848. ADE Data Center lists director contact `nathan.white@searkcoop.com` (verify). Ask for the **special education / related services / early childhood** lead, not a cold email to a 2018 name.
2. **District LEA supervisors and superintendents** — Monticello SD (870) 367-4000; Drew Central (870) 367-5369. Coop board packets (2023 vintage) named superintendents (e.g., Sandra Lanehart, Monticello; Kim Barnes, Drew Central). **Those names age quickly; call the board office and ask.**
3. **First Connections** service coordination — (800) 643-8258 — ask how a new SLP joins the **Drew / Bradley / Lincoln / Desha** provider list and whether the county is underserved.
4. **Hospital rehab directors** — Baptist Health–Drew County (870) 367-2411; BCMC outpatient therapy (870) 226-4190; ACMC speech (870) 364-5576. ACMC already sells school contracts; they may be a competitor *or* a PRN employer.
5. **Existing small practices** that may need overflow rather than another competitor: Meaningful Movement; SEARK Speech & Swallow; Therex/ProCARE; Fox Speech (if they need a local 1099).
6. **ArkSHA** (Arkansas Speech-Language-Hearing Association, `arksha@arksha.org`) — state conference and member list; useful for a mentor, not a job board by itself.

### 7.2 Timing

- **January–April:** district and coop staffing for the next school year; boards approve calendars and contracts before typical May teacher-contract deadlines.
- **July–August:** last-minute itinerant holes when someone resigns; Hamburg-type early starts.
- **Year-round:** First Connections, hospital PRN, home health, private pediatric (Meaningful Movement’s access-gap story is not seasonal).
- **Summer:** ESY, First Connections, medical/swallow — the only way a school-primary 1099 avoids a zero-revenue quarter.

### 7.3 Pitch that matches this locale (substance, not slogans)

Lead with **coverage reliability and IEP-minute completion**, not “passion.” The public due-process record is about **minutes not delivered**. Offer:

- A defined travel polygon (e.g., Drew + Bradley + Lincoln, or Drew + Desha).
- Evaluation turnaround time you can keep.
- Make-up minute protocol.
- Telepractice backup when weather or staffing fails, with ABESPA-compliant consent.
- Medicaid-ready documentation if the district/coop bills EPSDT.

Do not under-bid the ASHA South contractor band just to “get in.” Mileage will do that for you.

### 7.4 Networking that is actually local

- SEARK Coop PD calendar and speech/EC job-alike meetings (coop site lists professional development).
- UAM School of Education partnerships (district list overlaps the coop) — useful for preschool/child-find relationships, not an SLP degree pipeline.
- Pediatric medical staff at Baptist Health–Drew County and local clinics for under-21 Medicaid referrals (prescription/referral is required for Medicaid therapy).
- Childcare directors (First Connections natural environments).
- ArkSHA and ASHA SIG communities for a **mentor SLP who has billed Arkansas Medicaid**, which is a specialized skill.

### 7.5 What not to do

- Do not mass-email every NPI name in §2. Many records are stale home addresses.
- Do not market telepractice-only into Arkansas without an Arkansas license.
- Do not assume the ADE $5,000 bonus applies to a 1099.

## 8. Risks and constraints

| Risk | Why it is local / structural | Mitigation (research, not advice) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rural travel** | Multi-county book; low density; fuel and dead time | Hard radius; paid mileage; cluster days by town |
| **School calendar** | ~36 paid weeks; mismatched district start dates (Hamburg vs Crossett) | ESY + medical/EI summer; do not stack incompatible calendars |
| **Reimbursement delay** | Medicaid extensions, school AP cycles, 1099 invoicing | Cash reserve of several unpaid weeks; never staff to “expected” AR Kids volume |
| **Unit-cap / auth** | 6 SLP units/week before QIO extension | Build the clinic schedule around the cap; train on DMS-671 |
| **Adult Medicaid gap** | Therapy manual does not cover 21+ | Adult work via hospital, HH, Medicare Part B, or private-pay |
| **Payer mix** | Low income, 11.9% uninsured under 65 | School contracts first; private-pay menu as a minority |
| **Telepractice + broadband** | 19.5% of households without a broadband subscription; ABESPA license-in-state rule | Screen connectivity; keep in-person evals |
| **Compact not live** | Cannot use ASLP-IC privileges for AR yet | Full AR license; watch CompactConnect onboarding |
| **Coop as incumbent** | SEARK already employs SLPs and bills as an LEA | Sell overflow, leave coverage, specialized skills (AAC, bilingual, feeding) |
| **ACMC school-contracting** | Crossett hospital already offers school contracts | Differentiate on Drew-centric travel, not on “we also do schools” |
| **Litigation / compliance** | H-20-02 missed-minute finding | Document every session; never paper over missed minutes |
| **1099 classification** | Districts sometimes treat contractors like employees | Counsel on IRS factors; do not accept W-2 control at 1099 pay |
| **Bonus clawback (if you take W-2)** | 6 CAR § 189 three-year stay / prorated repayment | Read the rule before taking the $5k |
| **Population decline** | Nearby counties −6% to −11% since 2020 | Diversify counties; do not lease a large clinic on growth assumptions |
| **Caseload cap 45** | ADE/ASHA chart | Contract language on max students and extra-hour pay |
| **CF supervision** | Provisional license needs a qualified on-site/tele supervisor | A new graduate cannot “just 1099” without a mentor structure |
| **Document conflict** | First Connections text vs ABESPA as licensing board | Confirm in writing with the program |
