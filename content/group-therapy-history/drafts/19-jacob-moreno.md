**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Jacob Lévy Moreno was born in 1889 in Bucharest and died in 1974. He liked origin stories, including his own. Vienna's Theatre of Spontaneity (1921 in his telling), New York, Sing Sing, the Hudson School maps, *Who Shall Survive?* (1934), the Beacon stage: the era essay on psychodrama holds the objects. This essay holds the man who would not share a phrase without a fight.

## Immigrant theater and a medical letterhead

He trained in medicine. He wanted more than a prescription. Spontaneity, encounter, a cosmic vocabulary that later Californians would thin into a weekend — those were his goods. American psychiatry could use a showman. It could also resent one. He published journals, ran institutes, and wrote as if history were a court in which he was always filing.

The 1932 Philadelphia claim — that he coined "group therapy" and "group psychotherapy" at an APA meeting after prison work encouraged by William Alanson White — is his story, repeated in friendly timelines. Pratt, Lazell, Marsh, and Burrow were already in rooms. A coinage is not a first gathering. Keep both facts.

Helen Hall Jennings's sociometric collaboration is easy to drop from a hero slide. Do not drop it. Maps of choice in a girls' school are a joint product and a power product.

## Marriage as a school

Zerka Toeman Moreno is the next essay. He was the name that sold tickets. She was, for decades, the teacher who made the method transmissible without his body in the room. A history that ends in 1974 with his death is a history of a brand. The work continued.

Charisma is a historical force. It is also a risk. Visitors described Beacon as theater. Theater trains imitators. Untrained imitators injure. His publicity habit and the later ethics of consent are in tension. This pack will not reconstruct a session to color a paragraph.

## Against the class

His useful quarrel, for this calendar, is with the lecture group. He wanted interaction, role, a protagonist, auxiliaries — a different physics than Pratt's diary audit. AGPA's New York analytic world wanted still another physics: talk, transference, a conductor who did not put anyone on a stage. The three physics still exist. Trainees still pick one and call the others "not group."

He did not win the medical-school textbook slot. Yalom did. He won a parallel profession with its own diplomas. Parallel is not failure. It is how methods survive a canon.

## What not to do with Moreno

Do not try a role reversal after a blog post. Do not call yourself a psychodramatist because you own a scarf. Do not use prison stills. Do not generate his face; confirm a Commons file or use the 1934 title page.

Do not award him the invention of sitting in a circle. People sat in circles before 1889.

A small-city clinician who never runs psychodrama still uses words he helped make ordinary: encounter, role, the idea that a group has a structure you could draw. Drawing a structure is not sociometry. Sociometry had rules and a book. The leak is the afterlife.

## Sing Sing, children, and the publicity machine

Prison work and children's theatre are easy to list as a humanitarian résumé. They are also sites where consent is structurally weak. A man who liked publicity and a prisoner who cannot leave the yard are not equals in a scene. Later correctional psychodrama programs inherited the inequality. Some named it. Some did not.

He wrote as if spontaneity were a cosmic principle. Cosmic principles sell in California later. In 1934 they sat inside a book that also wanted to be a science of choices. The double want — prophet and measurer — is the career. AGPA's measurers and Esalen's prophets both stole a piece. He would have sued both in prose.

Bucharest, Vienna, New York: three cities, three languages, a man who made English carry a method that still sounds slightly translated. Translation is not a defect. It is the immigrant's mark this pack has been tracking since Foulkes and Lewin.

Do not put him on a "father of" poster. Do not generate a face. Confirm a file or use 1934 type.

A man who files history as a lawsuit will win some footnotes and lose the medical-school slot. Losing the slot is not the same as losing the stage. Drama therapists, sociometrists, and organizational people who draw network maps are his parallel children. Parallel children do not always speak. This pack makes them neighbors on an INDEX so a talking-group syllabus cannot pretend the stage was a side show.

## How to hold a showman

A showman who also drew maps is easy to split into clown and scientist. He refused the split. This pack refuses the split by giving sociometry and the stage one era essay and one figure essay. Splitting him across two files is not a split of the man. It is a way to keep a phrase-fight from eating a reformatory map.

Later organizational people who draw network graphs without saying Beacon are a success he would have litigated in prose. Litigation in prose is his genre. This page will not litigate. It will date 1921, 1932-as-claim, 1934-as-book, 1974-as-death. Dates are the only prizes this series awards.

Zerka's essay is next on the INDEX on purpose. Read it. A calendar that ends with his death is a ticket office. She kept the theater open as a school.

Beacon as a town is a New York-state fact, not a shrine. Do not scrape a current street view and caption it as his stage. Stages move. The 1934 book stays.
