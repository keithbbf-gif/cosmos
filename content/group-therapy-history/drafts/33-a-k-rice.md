**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Albert Kenneth Rice was born in 1908 and died in 1969. He worked in the Tavistock world that had already taken Bion's group papers and the Institute's industrial studies and asked a further question: can a *conference* be the instrument? *Learning for Leadership* (1965) is a dry title for a method that still shakes people. Leicester in the UK, later the A. K. Rice Institute events in the United States, put participants into a temporary organization whose task was to study itself — small groups, large groups, inter-group, the staff as an authority.

This is not a psychotherapy billed to an insurer. It is also not a trust fall. People have used it to see how they take up a role. People have used it to injure and to convert. A method that studies authority will attract people who like authority.

## From factory to conference

Tavistock Institute work on mines and factories (Trist and others) treated organizations as sociotechnical systems. Rice's leadership book takes the small-group nerve of that world and stages it. The stage is a campus, not a Beacon theater. The drama is rank.

The American institute that bears his name is a descendant with its own fights. This page will not referee them. It will say: naming an institute after a dead man is how a method tries not to die. Death in 1969 was early. The conferences continued.

## Not a T-group, not encounter

Bethel wanted feedback and democracy. Encounter wanted candor and a peak. Group relations wants the study of the system you just made. The three get mashed in catalogs. Keep the mash off this site.

A consultant who sells "Rice work" to a hospital without saying who can fire whom is selling a costume. He wrote about leadership. Leadership in a real hospital includes a budget. The conference budget is a fee. Different sovereignties.

## What not to do with Rice

Do not fly in a group-relations event as a fix for "culture." Do not put *Learning for Leadership* on a patient handout. Do not scrape institute photos of identifiable members.

Type: "A. K. Rice, 1908–1969."

A small-city administrator who has never heard of Leicester still lives in organizations that use "role" and "boundary" as if those words were native English. Some of that English leaked from this book. Leakage is not training. Training, in that tradition, is a conference you chose (or your employer chose — note the difference).

## Leicester as a temporary country

A conference that studies itself is a temporary country with visas (fees), borders (time), and a government (staff). Citizens panic, pair, fight, and depend. He thought leadership could be learned by living that country for a week. Living it is not the same as running a hospital. Hospitals do not end on Friday.

The American institute that took his name after 1969 is a reception history. Receptions argue. This page will not join the argument. It will say a dead man's name on a living institute is a claim of continuity. Continuity is always partly a wish.

Industrial Tavistock and conference Tavistock share a letterhead. The miner and the manager did not share a fee. Keep the two studies on one shelf and two ethics.

A temporary institution with a fee is a laboratory that knows it is pretending. Pretending is not a lie if everyone agreed to the week. Trouble starts when a real hospital hires the week as a fix. He wrote about leadership, not about magic. Magic is what vendors add. This pack will not add it.

## Ahmedabad, Leicester, a name after 1969

Before the leadership book there was a factory book. *Productivity and Social Organization: The Ahmedabad Experiment* (Tavistock, 1958) is the industrial object: Calico mills, sociotechnical systems, the miner and the manager on one letterhead and not on one fee. Trist and the Glacier work sit on the same shelf. This page will not collapse a mill study into a conference week. Industrial Tavistock and conference Tavistock share a building. They do not share an ethics.

Leicester, in the UK group-relations calendar, is the place name that later American brochures used as perfume. Perfume is encounter's cousin. *Learning for Leadership* (1965) is dry on purpose. A week that studies authority will attract people who enjoy authority and people who have been injured by it. Both pay. A fee is a boundary. A Friday is a boundary. A staff consultant who cannot fire you is a boundary a real workplace does not share. Hospitals do not end on Friday. Safety, in that tradition, includes who can leave on Wednesday.

He died in 1969. The A. K. Rice Institute of the United States is a reception that took a dead man's name so a method might not die. Receptions argue. This page will not join the argument. Continuity is always partly a wish. Close the vendor brochure. Open 1958 and 1965. 1908–1969.
