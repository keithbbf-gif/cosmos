Gary M. Burlingame is living as of last verification (2026-09-14). He is a Brigham Young University researcher whose name sits on reviews and handbooks that asked, across the 1990s–2010s, whether small-group treatments move symptoms and under what conditions. This essay uses those public papers. No health speculation. No campus-photo scrape. No slogan.

## Aggregation as a historical event

Encounter leaders said the peak could not be counted. Lieberman counted a fashion. Changing the rules changes the number. This pack will not say "research proves group works." It will say a research program existed, it often found effects, and it kept stumbling on dropout, fidelity, measures, and who was never in the trial.
