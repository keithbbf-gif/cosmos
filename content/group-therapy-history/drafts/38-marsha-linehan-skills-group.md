## A group that admitted it was a class

Process-group culture had spent years pretending the teacher was not a teacher. Linehan's skills room put the teacher back, with homework, a board, and a contract that was not "say whatever comes." The honesty is historical. Pratt would have recognized the class word. She did not need Pratt. She needed a way to deliver skills without pretending the delivery was a process group.

The 1991 paper is a dated trial, not a slogan. This site will not say what it "proved" beyond what a reader can find in the paper: a study, a population, limits. The 1993 manual is copyrighted. Internet photocopies are not a license.
