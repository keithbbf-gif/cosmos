# Claims guardrails — group-therapy history pack

Educational history only. These sentences must never go on wowtherapies.com, in ads, or in schema markup.

This file is a refuse list. If a draft drifts toward any line below, cut the line. Do not "soften" it into a how-to.

## Never say (treatment / DIY)

- Any instruction a reader could follow as a session: icebreakers, go-arounds as homework, empty-chair scripts, doubling / role-reversal steps, sociometry as a parlor game, "ask the group these ten questions."
- "Try this at home," "start your own process group," "self-help circle," "you can Yalom your way out of…"
- Twelve Step instructions, slogans assigned as homework, or "go to a meeting tonight" as medical counsel. AA history is allowed. AA as a protocol is not.
- Dosing of psychiatric medication, taper advice, or "ask your doctor about [drug]" as content.
- "This method is the gold standard for [diagnosis]."
- "Studies prove group works better than individual" without a named trial, a dated review, and a sentence about limits.
- Crisis instructions that replace emergency services. A single line — "If you are in danger, call local emergency services" — is enough. Do not write a safety-planning protocol, a scapegoat-intervention script, or a suicide-watch group recipe in these essays.

## Never say (medical / legal)

- That WOW Therapies diagnoses, treats, or "specializes in" a DSM/ICD condition **in these history posts**. Service pages are a different file and a different review.
- That reading an essay is therapy, group counseling, or a substitute for a licensed clinician.
- That a reader should join, leave, or stay in a group.
- PHI, composite patients, or "a member once told me" stories. No invented vignettes with identifying color.
- Guarantees of outcome.
- Anything a reasonable reader would hear as a diagnosis of themselves or their child.

## Never say (history that is too neat)

- "Pratt invented group therapy in 1905." (He ran a tuberculosis *class*. The later profession adopted him as a founder. Mutual aid, revival meetings, and hospital wards already knew how to sit people in a ring.)
- "Moreno invented the group." (He named psychodrama and sociometry and fought Slavson for decades. He did not own the circle.)
- "Bion proved that groups have basic assumptions the way bodies have organs." Name *Experiences in Groups*. Leave the metaphysics arguable.
- "Encounter groups were harmless growth." Lieberman, Yalom, and Miles (1973) documented casualties. Say so.
- "AA is group therapy." The fellowship refused that name. Historians may compare forms. The brand must not collapse them.
- That DSM-era manuals "ended ideology" in groups. They changed billing language. They did not end argument.
- Conversion-therapy groups, "ex-gay" circles, or re-education camps as success. Name the harm. Do not teach the method.
- Refrigerator-mother or schizophrenogenic-family groups as fact.

## Never say (brand / SEO)

- "Best group therapist in Arkansas" or any superlative about the practice.
- Disease keywords paired with a CTA ("overcome social anxiety in group — book now") on these URLs.
- Schema `MedicalTherapy`, `Drug`, or `treats` / `indication` on history posts. `Article` is enough.
- Fake author credentials.
- Patient testimonials in this folder.

## Allowed

- Dated description of what a school claimed, who paid for it, who dissented, and what later researchers revised.
- Named trials and reviews as historical events (Lieberman–Yalom–Miles 1973; Burlingame cohesion reviews; Pratt's 1907 *JAMA* paper).
- Controversy, including racism, sexism, colonial psychiatry, cultic encounter leaders, and eugenic hospital wards, when it is part of the record.
- A short "why a contemporary clinician still reads this" closing that does **not** become a protocol.
- The educational footer already in each draft.

## Living people

Yalom, Rutan, Leszcz, Burlingame, and others who were alive at last verification (2026-09-14) are not public property. No health speculation. No unsourced private life. Public books, public lectures, and public interviews only.

## If asked for both a history and a how-to

Print the history. Refuse the how-to in one sentence: this series does not publish treatment protocols or group-leadership manuals.

## Review stamp

2. Portrait license is in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` (or the post uses type only).
3. No line from the never-say lists remains.
4. A human editor has read it aloud once.
