# Bibliography — group therapy history pack

Consolidated sources cited across `drafts/`. A date in an essay should match a line here or carry `[VERIFY]`. This is a working shelf, not a complete historiography.

Last pass: 2026-09-14.

## Classes, churches, interwar wards

- Pratt, Joseph H. "The Class Method of Treating Consumption in the Homes of the Poor." *JAMA* 49 (1907): 755–759.
- Pratt, Joseph H. "The Class Method in the Home Treatment of Tuberculosis and What It Has Accomplished." *Boston Medical and Surgical Journal* 166 (1912): 280–288.
- Solis-Cohen, Myer. "Some New Features in the Class Method of Treating Tuberculosis." *JAMA* (1910). Dates Pratt's inauguration to 1 July 1904 — hold against the 1905 Emmanuel-class dating in later retrospectives.
- Pratt, Joseph H. Thought Control Class papers, Boston Dispensary, 1930s; see also H. S. Rosenbaum et al. on the later psychosomatic class. `[VERIFY]` specific 1930 opening citation before print.
- Ambrose, G. "Joseph Hersey Pratt, M.D.: The Man Who Would Be Osler." (biographical essay; use for life dates 1872–1956, not for efficacy claims).
- Worcester, Elwood, Samuel McComb, and Isador H. Coriat. *Religion and Medicine*. New York: Moffat, Yard, 1908.
- Gifford, Sanford. *The Emmanuel Movement*. (historical monograph — confirm edition on upload).
- Lazell, Edward W. Group lectures at St. Elizabeths, early 1920s; see later reviews in the group-therapy histories. `[VERIFY]` 1921 vs neighboring years before treating 1921 as a carved date.
- Wender, Louis. "The Dynamics of Group Psychotherapy and Its Application." *Journal of Nervous and Mental Disease* 84 (1936): 54–60.
- Marsh, L. Cody. "Group Treatment of the Psychoses by the Psychological Equivalent of the Revival." *Mental Hygiene* 15 (1931): 328–349.
- Marsh, L. Cody. "Group Therapy and the Psychiatric Clinic." *Journal of Nervous and Mental Disease* (1935).
- Marsh, Lindus Cody. JAMA obituary, Dec. 1949, 141: 1316 — died 4 Nov. 1949, Tucson, aged 66 (hence c. 1883–1949). Albany Medical College 1929; Kings Park and Worcester State; later Tucson.
- Burrow, Trigant. *The Social Basis of Consciousness*. New York: Harcourt, Brace, 1927.
- Burrow, Trigant. Later Lifwynn / phyloanalysis papers. Do not collapse Burrow into Moreno or Foulkes.

- Foulkes, S. H., and Eve Lewis. "A Study in the Treatment of Groups on Psycho-Analytic Lines." *British Journal of Medical Psychology* 20 (1945): 175–184.

- Trist, E. L., and others — Tavistock Institute postwar papers. Name the paper when you lean on it.

## Moreno, Slavson, AGPA, children, social group work

- Moreno, J. L. *Who Shall Survive?* Washington, D.C.: Nervous and Mental Disease Publishing, 1934.
- Moreno, J. L. Later Beacon / psychodrama manuals. Copyright sits on late editions; point, do not reprint protocols.
- Moreno, Zerka T. Later psychodrama texts and interviews. Living-estate care: she died 2016.
- Slavson, S. R. *An Introduction to Group Therapy*. New York: Commonwealth Fund, 1943.
- Slavson, S. R. *Analytic Group Psychotherapy*. New York: Columbia University Press, 1950.
- AGPA institutional history: agpa.org "AGPA History" (1943 AOA luncheon; 1942 logo dating; 1952 rename). Durkin et al. 1971 anniversary essay.
- Scheidlinger, Saul. Essays on children's groups and on AGPA's conceptual pluralism (see 1991 *IJGP* piece).
- Coyle, Grace L. *Social Process in Organized Groups*. New York: Richard R. Smith, 1930.
- Coyle, Grace L. *Group Work with American Youth*. New York: Harper, 1948.
- Konopka, Gisela. *Social Group Work: A Helping Process*. Englewood Cliffs, N.J.: Prentice-Hall, 1963.
- Wolf, Alexander, and Emanuel Schwartz. *Psychoanalysis in Groups*. New York: Grune & Stratton, 1962.
- Paid Notice: Deaths WOLF, ALEXANDER. MD. *New York Times*, 27 September 1997 (death 25 September 1997).
- Durkin, Helen E. "The Theory and Practice of Group Psychotherapy." *Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences* 49 (1948): 889–901.
- Durkin, Helen E. *The Group in Depth*. New York: International Universities Press, 1964.

## T-groups, encounter, AA, harm

- Lewin, Kurt. Field-theory and group-dynamics papers, 1930s–1940s; Research Center for Group Dynamics (MIT, later Michigan).
- NTL institutional memory: 1946 New Britain workshop (Connecticut Interracial Commission); Lewin d. Feb. 1947; Bethel 1947 first summer. Prefer NTL's own legacy page plus Benne/Bradford/Lippitt accounts over "Lewin founded Bethel in 1946."
- Alcoholics Anonymous. *Alcoholics Anonymous*. New York: Works Publishing, 1939. (Later editions © AAWS. Do not reprint Steps as a clinic protocol.)
- Kurtz, Ernest. *Not-God: A History of Alcoholics Anonymous*. Center City, Minn.: Hazelden, 1979.
- Rogers, Carl R. *Carl Rogers on Encounter Groups*. New York: Harper & Row, 1970.
- Whitaker, Dorothy Stock, and Morton A. Lieberman. *Psychotherapy Through the Group Process*. New York: Atherton, 1964.
- Lieberman, Morton A., Irvin D. Yalom, and Matthew B. Miles. *Encounter Groups: First Facts*. New York: Basic Books, 1973.
- Lieberman, Morton A. *Doors Close, Doors Open: Widows, Grieving, and Growing*. New York: G.P. Putnam's Sons, 1996.
- UCSF Department of Psychiatry. "In Memoriam: Morton Lieberman, PhD." 25 February 2019.
- Yalom, Irvin D., and others — Stanford encounter research papers of the late 1960s.
- Synanon: journalistic and historical accounts of Charles Dederich, 1958 founding, later violence and the 1970s criminal cases. Name the harm. Do not teach "the Game."
- Berne, Eric. "Transactional Analysis: A New and Effective Method of Group Therapy." *American Journal of Psychotherapy* 12 (1958): 735–743.
- Berne, Eric. *Transactional Analysis in Psychotherapy*. New York: Grove, 1961.
- Berne, Eric. *Games People Play*. New York: Grove, 1964.

## American textbook, CMHC, skills, multifamily, research

- Yalom, Irvin D. *The Theory and Practice of Group Psychotherapy*. New York: Basic Books, 1970. Later editions (with Molyn Leszcz from the 5th) are dated objects; cite the edition you mean.
- Community Mental Health Centers Act, 1963 (U.S. Public Law 88-164) — statutory object, not a clinical manual.
- Linehan, Marsha M. "Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment of Chronically Parasuicidal Borderline Patients." *Archives of General Psychiatry* 48 (1991): 1060–1064.
- Linehan, Marsha M. *Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment of Borderline Personality Disorder*. New York: Guilford, 1993.
- Linehan, Marsha M. *Skills Training Manual for Treating Borderline Personality Disorder*. New York: Guilford, 1993. Later editions include a 2015 second edition. Do not excerpt skills.
- McFarlane, William R., et al. "Multiple-Family Groups and Psychoeducation in the Treatment of Schizophrenia." *Archives of General Psychiatry* 52 (1995): 679–687. Report the paper's own numbers as historical claims; do not convert them into a 2026 promise.
- McFarlane, William R. *Multifamily Groups in the Treatment of Severe Psychiatric Disorders*. New York: Guilford, 2002.
- McFarlane, William R., et al. "Portland Identification and Early Referral: A Community-Based System." *Psychiatric Services* 61 (2010): 512–515.
- Alonso, Anne. *The Quiet Profession: Supervisors of Psychotherapy*. New York: Macmillan, 1985.
- Alonso, Anne, and J. Scott Rutan. *IJGP* essays; Rutan and Alonso 1979 on intimacy/individuation in group (as later Rutan/Stone/Shay editions cite it).
- Marquard, Bryan. "Dr. Anne W. Alonso, at 73." *Boston Globe*, 2 September 2007.
- Ormont, Louis R. *The Group Therapy Experience: From Theory to Practice*. New York: St. Martin's, 1992.
- Rutan, J. Scott, and Walter N. Stone. *Psychodynamic Group Psychotherapy*. Lexington, Mass.: Collamore Press, 1984. Later Guilford editions add Joseph J. Shay (5th ed. 2014).
- Rutan, J. Scott. "Making Society's Groups More Therapeutic." *International Journal of Group Psychotherapy* (1989) — AGPA presidential address, 11 February 1988.
- Fuhriman, Addie, and Gary M. Burlingame, eds. *Handbook of Group Psychotherapy*. New York: Wiley, 1994.
- McRoberts, Chris, Gary M. Burlingame, and Matthew J. Hoag. "Comparative Efficacy of Individual and Group Psychotherapy." *Group Dynamics* 2 (1998): 101–117.
- Barlow, Sally, Gary M. Burlingame, and Addie Fuhriman. "Therapeutic Applications of Groups: From Pratt's Thought Control Classes to Modern Group Psychotherapy." *Group Dynamics* 4 (2000): 115–134.
- Burlingame, Gary M., Addie Fuhriman, and Julie Mosier. "The Differential Effectiveness of Group Psychotherapy." *Group Dynamics* 7 (2003): 3–12. Cite the year and inclusion rules; do not say "research proves group works."
- Burlingame, Gary M., and Bernhard Strauss, eds. Later handbooks. Living-author care.
