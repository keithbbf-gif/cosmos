# Bibliography — group-therapy history pack

Working list for staging copy-check. Prefer the edition named in an essay's YAML. If a date in an article disagrees with this file, mark `[VERIFY]` in the article; do not silently "fix" a living scholar's citation.

This is not a reading-list CTA and not a complete library.

## Founding medical and hospital texts

- Pratt, Joseph H. "The Class Method of Treating Consumption in the Homes of the Poor." *JAMA* 49, no. 9 (1907): 755–759.
- Pratt, Joseph H. "The Class Method in the Home Treatment of Pulmonary Tuberculosis." *Boston Medical and Surgical Journal / NEJM* (1926).
- Schilder, Paul. *Psychotherapy*. New York: W. W. Norton, 1938.
- Wender, Louis. Papers on hospital group methods in the 1930s; see later surveys in Scheidlinger.
- Marsh, L. Cody. "Group Treatment of the Psychoses by the Psychological Equivalent of the Revival." *Mental Hygiene* 15 (1931): 328–349. `[VERIFY]` exact subtitle against a library copy.

## Psychodrama and sociometry

- Moreno, J. L. *Who Shall Survive?* Washington, DC: Nervous and Mental Disease Publishing Co., 1934.
- Moreno, J. L. *Psychodrama*. Beacon, NY: Beacon House, various volumes from 1946.
- Moreno, Zerka T. *To Dream Again: A Memoir*. New York: Mental Health Resources, 2012. (Use as memoir, not as license for workshop stills.)
- Marineau, René F. *Jacob Levy Moreno, 1889–1974*. London: Routledge, 1989.

## Group analysis, Northfield, Tavistock

- Bion, W. R., and John Rickman. "Intra-Group Tensions in Therapy: Their Study as the Task of the Group." *The Lancet* 242 (1943): 678–681.
- Bion, W. R. *Experiences in Groups and Other Papers*. London: Tavistock, 1961. (Essays from *Human Relations* from 1948.)
- Foulkes, S. H. "Group Analysis in a Military Neurosis Centre." *The Lancet* 247 (1946): 303–306.
- Foulkes, S. H. *Introduction to Group-Analytic Psychotherapy*. London: Heinemann, 1948.
- Foulkes, S. H. *Therapeutic Group Analysis*. London: Allen & Unwin, 1964.
- Harrison, Tom. *Bion, Rickman, Foulkes and the Northfield Experiments*. London: Jessica Kingsley, 2000.
- Main, T. F. "The Ailment." *British Journal of Medical Psychology* 30 (1957): 129–145.
- Pines, Malcolm, ed. *The Evolution of Group Analysis*. London: Routledge, 1983.
- de Maré, Patrick, Robin Piper, and Sheila Thompson. *Koinonia: From Hate, through Dialogue, to Culture in the Large Group*. London: Karnac, 1991.

## T-groups, encounter, research

- Lewin, Kurt. *Resolving Social Conflicts*. New York: Harper, 1948. (Posthumous collection.)
- Bradford, Leland P., Jack R. Gibb, and Kenneth D. Benne, eds. *T-Group Theory and Laboratory Method*. New York: Wiley, 1964.
- Lieberman, Morton A., Irvin D. Yalom, and Matthew B. Miles. *Encounter Groups: First Facts*. New York: Basic Books, 1973.
- Yalom, Irvin D. *The Theory and Practice of Group Psychotherapy*. New York: Basic Books, 1970 (and later editions).
- Frank, Jerome D. *Persuasion and Healing*. Baltimore: Johns Hopkins Press, 1961 (later eds.).
- Burlingame, Gary M., Addie Fuhriman, and Jennifer E. Johnson. Reviews of cohesion and outcome — use the dated review named in the research essay; do not cite "Burlingame says group works" without a year.

## American group therapy and AGPA

- Slavson, S. R. *An Introduction to Group Therapy*. New York: Commonwealth Fund, 1943.
- Durkin, Helen E. *The Group in Depth*. New York: International Universities Press, 1964.
- Wolf, Alexander, and Emanuel K. Schwartz. *Psychoanalysis in Groups*. New York: Grune & Stratton, 1962.
- Rutan, J. Scott, Walter N. Stone, and Joseph J. Shay. *Psychodynamic Group Psychotherapy*. Various editions (Guilford).
- Ormont, Louis R. *The Group Therapy Experience*. New York: St. Martin's, 1992.
- Alonso, Anne, and J. Scott Rutan. Papers on the Boston tradition; see AGPA *International Journal of Group Psychotherapy*.
- Agazarian, Yvonne M. *Systems-Centered Therapy for Groups*. New York: Guilford, 1997.
- Scheidlinger, Saul, and Gerald Schamess. "Fifty Years of AGPA 1942–1992." *International Journal of Group Psychotherapy* 42 (1992): 1–22.
- AGPA. "AGPA History." https://agpa.org/agpa-overview/agpa-history/ (institutional narrative; check against Scheidlinger).

## Social group work and settlements

- Addams, Jane. *Twenty Years at Hull-House*. New York: Macmillan, 1910.
- Konopka, Gisela. *Social Group Work: A Helping Process*. Englewood Cliffs, NJ: Prentice-Hall, 1963 (later eds.).
- Schwartz, William. "The Social Worker in the Group." In *New Perspectives on Services to Groups*, NASW, 1961.
- Andrews, Janice. biographies of Konopka; University of Minnesota collections. `[VERIFY]` before quoting letters.

## Mutual aid

- Alcoholics Anonymous. *Alcoholics Anonymous* ("Big Book"). New York: Works Publishing / AA World Services, 1939 (later eds.). Cite as a historical publication. Do not reproduce Steps as a worksheet.
- Kurtz, Ernest. *Not-God: A History of Alcoholics Anonymous*. Center City, MN: Hazelden, 1979.
- White, William L. recovery-history essays. Use a dated piece; White is a historian of recovery, not a protocol.

## Therapeutic community, CMHC, structured groups

- Jones, Maxwell. *The Therapeutic Community*. New York: Basic Books, 1953.
- Joint Commission / CMHC Act (U.S., 1963) as statute history — cite the Public Law number in the era essay.
- Whitaker, Dorothy Stock, and Morton A. Lieberman. *Psychotherapy through the Group Process*. New York: Atherton, 1964.
- Later CBT group manuals (Beck, Free, White) — name as dated books in the structured-groups essay. Do not excerpt worksheets.

## Latin America and other rooms

- Pichon-Rivière, Enrique. *El proceso grupal*. Buenos Aires: Nueva Visión, various collected eds. Cite the collection used; Spanish titles stay in Spanish on first mention.
- Tubert-Oklander, Juan, and Reyna Hernández de Tubert. English-language introductions to the Argentine school — name the book if quoted.

## Balint

- Balint, Michael. *The Doctor, His Patient and the Illness*. London: Pitman, 1957.
- Balint, Enid, and Michael Balint. later Tavistock papers. `[VERIFY]` before assigning a first-group date.

## Surveys a copy editor may keep open

- Scheidlinger, Saul. historical reviews in *IJGP*.
- Barlow, Sally H. *Specialty Competencies in Group Psychology*. Oxford, 2013 (profession, not a how-to for this site).
- Brabender, Virginia, and April Fallon. group-psychotherapy textbooks — use for orientation, not as a hidden protocol.

## Living authors

Yalom, Rutan, and others living at last verification (2026-09-14): cite public books only. No workshop gossip.
