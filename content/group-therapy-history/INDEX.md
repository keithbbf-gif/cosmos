# Editorial calendar — WOW Therapies, history of group therapy

Forty-four publishable essays: sixteen era pieces and twenty-eight figures. Status in YAML is `publishable` (quality-cleared for **staging** review). Nothing in this folder is cleared to write itself onto the live wowtherapies.com sitemap.

All files live under `content/group-therapy-history/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`.

This pack is the group-heritage lane. It is complementary to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` (individual consulting room) and to the SLPWOW speech-pathology history pack. Do not merge the calendars.

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-four essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):

| Wave | Suggested order | Pieces | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — the usual origin story, then the quarrel | 1–6 weeks | 01, 02, 17, 04, 18, 19 | Circles before clinics, Pratt, psychodrama, both Morenos |
| 2 — war hospitals | 7–12 | 03, 05, 06, 20, 21, 27 | Interwar wards, Northfield, Foulkes, Bion, Main |
| 3 — laboratories and fellowships | 13–20 | 07, 08, 09, 22, 30, 43 | T-groups, encounter, AA, Lewin, Lieberman, Addams |
| 4 — children, social work, profession | 21–28 | 12, 14, 23, 37, 38 | Activity groups, AGPA, Slavson, Konopka, Schwartz |
| 5 — communities and manuals | 29–36 | 10, 11, 13, 26, 28 | TC/CMHC, CR groups, CBT groups, Jones, Balint |
