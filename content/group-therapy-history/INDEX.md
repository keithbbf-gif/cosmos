# Editorial calendar — WOW Therapies, history of group therapy & figures

Forty-four publishable essays: sixteen era pieces and twenty-eight figures. Status in YAML is `publishable` (quality-cleared for **staging** review). Nothing in this folder is cleared to write itself onto the live wowtherapies.com sitemap.

All files live under `content/group-therapy-history/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`. Drafts live in `drafts/`.

This pack is the **circle** lane. Complementary to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` (consulting-room heritage) and to the SLPWOW speech-pathology history pack. Do not merge the three calendars.

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-four essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — inventing the class | 1–6 weeks | 01, 02, 03, 17 | Circles before the clinic, Pratt, Emmanuel, Pratt figure |

Hold any piece that still carries `[CITE NEEDED]` or `[VERIFY]` on a date you would print.

A 2026-09-14 writer pass built the pack from dated rooms (Emmanuel Church, Hollymoor, Bethel, JBG, Boston Dispensary) rather than from a "father of group therapy" list. `check_pack.py` fails an era under 1,200 words or a figure under 1,000. Deepen further with sources, not recap.
