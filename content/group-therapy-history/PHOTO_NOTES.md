# Photo notes — group-therapy history series

Text is the product. Images are evidence or atmosphere, never a fabricated witness.

Companion: `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` (the license table). Graphics agents start there.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or CC portraits listed as `pd` or `cc` in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`.
- Period objects: title pages (Pratt 1907 *JAMA* if a PD scan; Moreno *Who Shall Survive?* 1934; Yalom 1970 is still © — do not scrape the jacket), PD hospital plans, PD engravings of Hull House, PD Bethel street scenes if the photograph is PD.
- Original diagrams drawn for this series: a dated timeline, a map of rooms (Boston / Vienna / Northfield / Bethel / Buenos Aires), a family-tree of *schools* (not of patients).
- Type treatments: name, dates, one short epithet.

## Banned

- AI-generated faces, "historical photographs" that are not photographs, or "what X would look like."
- Scraped workshop / TED / university / AGPA conference headshots.
- Patient artwork or sociograms with identifiable people.
- Children in activity groups as thumbnails.
- Atrocity or ward-restraint photographs as headers for Northfield, Jones, or Main.
- Stock "smiling support circle" or "therapist with clipboard."
- Before/after faces.
- Anything that implies a WOW Therapies group room you have not photographed with consent.
- AA coin close-ups used as if the fellowship licensed them. AA literature and marks have their own terms.

## Alt text

Name what is in the frame. "Hull-House courtyard, Chicago, published 1910, looking toward the residents' dining hall." Not "the birth of group work heals the city."

For type-only posts: no empty `<img>`. The heading is the image.

## WordPress featured image

If `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` says `no-portrait` or `confirm`, the featured image is a **series template** (wordmark + essay title), not a guessed face. Build one template; reuse it.

On upload day, open the Commons or museum URL again. Paste the credit block from `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` into the media caption **and** under the image in the post.

## Color and crop

Do not colorize PD photographs and call them contemporary. Do not crop a group so that a bystander becomes "the" portrait. Do not flip a face for layout.

## Other lanes

Do not reuse SLPWOW speech-pathology graphics or individual-therapy-history portraits here as decoration unless the license row says the file may travel. Different pack, different eye.

## If the graphics agent and the writer disagree

The license table wins. A beautiful unlicensed face does not ship.
