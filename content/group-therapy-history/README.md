# WOW Therapies — history of group therapy

Staged educational pack for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com). **Do not write these files onto the live site from this folder.**

Forty-four publishable essays (sixteen eras, twenty-eight figures) plus editorial ops. This is the **group** lane: Pratt classes, psychodrama rooms, Northfield, T-groups, mutual-aid fellowships, social group work, AGPA. Complementary to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` (the individual consulting-room pack) and to the SLPWOW speech-pathology history pack. Do not merge the three calendars.
