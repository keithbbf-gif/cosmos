# Style guide — WOW Therapies, group therapy history series

This pack is educational history for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com): the circle as a clinical and social object. It is **not** the SLPWOW speech-pathology series and **not** a reprint of `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/`. Do not retell Van Riper here. Do not retell Freud's whole century here. If a figure already has a full essay in the sister pack (Rogers, Yalom, Linehan), this pack takes the **group** angle only and says so in the opening.

`voice_check: human` means the writer draft passed this file. `voice_check: edited` means a human editor read the piece aloud and tightened grammar, voice, and guardrails. Neither value is a byline and must not appear on the public page.

## Who is speaking

A historically literate magazine writer who has sat in enough circles to know that a group is not a cheaper hour and not a miracle. Warm without being pastoral. Precise without being a textbook. The reader may be a curious client, a family member, a student, or a clinician. Do not talk down. Do not talk shop as if the room were empty of patients.

WOW Therapies is a Southeast Arkansas speech-language practice brand. These essays are **heritage education**, not a menu of groups and not a claim that the clinic practices every school named here.

## Hard bans (fail the draft)

Do not use:

- "In today's rapidly evolving…"
- "It's important to note"
- "delve" / "delve into"
- "landscape" as a metaphor for a field
- "robust"
- "leverage"
- "unlock"
- "cutting-edge"
- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "In conclusion"
- "Moreover" / "Furthermore" stacks
- "Whether you're a … or a …"
- empty dualities ("not just X, but Y"; "both an art and a science")
- throat-clearing first paragraphs that delay the first fact
- three-beat synonym piles ("heal, grow, and thrive")
- "the father of group therapy" unless you are quoting a myth and immediately puncturing it
- "Pratt invented group therapy"
- "gold-standard treatment" as marketing
- invented quotes
- AI-generated faces or "period photographs" that never existed

Also drop the cousins: "navigate," "tapestry," "plethora," "utilize," "harness," "elevate your," "the future of," "at the forefront," "a holistic approach," "empower," "in this article we will explore," "journey of healing" as filler, "safe space" as a slogan without a dated source.

## Do this instead

1. Open on a dated event, a room, a document, or a named quarrel. The first sentence should be checkable.
2. Mix sentence length. A short sentence after a long one. Do not write every paragraph as topic sentence + three examples + wrap.
3. Name the book, paper, hospital, or society. *The Theory and Practice of Group Psychotherapy* (1970). Hollymoor / Northfield. Emmanuel Church. Bethel, 1947. The Jewish Board of Guardians.
4. Mark uncertainty. `[CITE NEEDED]` or a plain "historians disagree" beats a clean falsehood. Pratt's 1904 vs 1905 start is a disagreement, not a rounding error.
5. Put Yalom, Moreno, and Bion in their century. Do not offer them as current treatment manuals.
6. Give women's work, social-group-work, and non-psychiatric rooms full essays, not a closing paragraph titled "also, social workers."
7. Stop when the piece has said the thing. Target 1,000–1,800 words for figures, 1,200–2,000 for era essays. Cut the recap paragraph if it only restates the headings. `check_pack.py` fails an era under 1,200 words or a figure under 1,000. Deepen with dated scenes, not with recap.

## Claims posture

See `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`. History may describe a method. History may not teach a reader to self-administer it. No session scripts, no skill worksheets, no "start a group in your living room."

Efficacy sentences, if any, stay historical and sourced ("Lieberman, Yalom, and Miles (1973) reported…") and never become "join a group and your depression will lift."

Living people get extra care: no gossip, no unsourced health claims.

## Portraits

Public domain or Creative Commons only. Document the file in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`. If there is no clean license, use type or a period object — never a generated face. See `PHOTO_NOTES.md`.

## Series voice, in one paragraph

Write as if *The New Yorker* and *Group Analysis* had to share a desk, and the desk was in a small-city clinic that still answers its own phone. The past is allowed to be strange. The present is not allowed to be sold. A circle can be a school, a ward policy, a revival, a research instrument, or a kindness. Name which.
