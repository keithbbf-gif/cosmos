# Style guide — history of herbal medicine, drugs, and supplements

- Voice: magazine history, not wellness marketing. Short sentences, dated anchors, named texts (papyri, pharmacopeias, statutes).
- Every draft carries `status: draft` until Keith clears publish.
- Use `era_focus` in front matter; verify uncertain dates with `[VERIFY]` in prose.
- Citations array in YAML — Grok writer fills; graphics agent does not invent references.
- Embed figures immediately after the H2 they support (`<!-- graphics-pack:v1 -->` block).
- Front matter `figures:` lists relative paths from the article file (synced by `embed_graphics.py`).
