# Photo and figure policy

- **SVG first** under `assets/<slug>/`. Regenerate with `scripts/generate_graphics.py`.
- **No stock herb glamour shots** masquerading as pharmacopeia evidence.
- **Line plates** only for comparative materia medica figures — stylized stems/leaves, Latin labels.
- **Trade maps** are schematics, not GIS or navigation charts.
- **Plant → principle** diagrams are historiographic process labels, not lab SOPs or synthesis routes.
- Label speculative diagrams in both SVG footnotes and markdown captions.
