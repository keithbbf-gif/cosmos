# Photo Notes

Prefer **schematic and historical** images: herbals, gardens, labels, statutes, maps, botanical plates. Do not commission or generate "scientist in a white coat holding a glowing herb." Do not fake wet-lab scenes. Do not put a stock-photo shaman on an essay about a specific nation.

Each draft already has a photo slot. This file is the pack-level license map and a fallback list.

## House style for the slot

```
> **Photo:** [object, artist or collection, date if known]
> **Caption:** [what the reader should notice; one or two sentences]
> **License note:** [PD-US, CC, museum terms, or "rights check"]
```

Captions do work. "Old herbal" is not a caption. "Woodcut of *Papaver somniferum* from the 1543 *New Kreüterbuch* of Leonhart Fuchs, showing the capsule the later alkaloid chemists actually wanted" is a caption.

## Image types that work

1. **Manuscript pages** — Ebers (facsimile), Vienna Dioscorides, Badianus, *Bencao Gangmu* woodblock, *Canon* title pages.
2. **Botanical plates** — Fuchs, Jacquin, Sowerby, *Curtis's Botanical Magazine*, Japanese honzō.
3. **Places** — Padua *orto*, Chelsea Physic Garden engravings, Kew Palm House (historic photo), Tisenjoch landscape (not a posed Ötzi reconstruction unless labeled reconstruction).
4. **Objects** — pharmacy jars (albarello), Shaker herb packages, Lydia Pinkham bottles, cinchona bark samples, USP title page 1820.
5. **Documents** — first page of the Pure Food and Drug Act, Harrison Act stamp, DSHEA enrolled bill, Federal Register ephedra rule.
6. **Schematics** — simple maps (trade in bark, monsoon Unani, Project 523 institutes). Draw these as original one-color maps if plates fail. Original maps are cleaner than a stolen textbook figure.

## Image types that fail

- AI "ancient apothecary, cinematic lighting"
- Close-up of capsules in a palm
- Uncredited "African healer" or "Amazon shaman"
- Recreations of Ötzi's last meal plated like a restaurant
- Live peyote or ayahuasca brew photography that functions as a recipe
- Before/after bodies
- Logo salad of supplement brands

## Reconstruction and remains

Ötzi reconstructions are usually **copyrighted museum or artist work** (South Tyrol Museum of Archaeology). Use a map of the find, a PD photo of the pass, or a licensed museum still — and say *reconstruction* if a body image is used. Mummies and human remains: follow the holding museum's rules; when in doubt, use the associated artifacts (the polypore, the tools), not the corpse.

## Portraits

Sertürner, Withering, Ibn Sīnā (later imaginary portraits), Li Shizhen, Tu Youyou (modern press: rights). Many "Avicenna" images are early modern inventions. Caption them as such. Do not pretend we have a studio photograph of Dioscorides.

## Alt text

Write alt as if the photo were described to someone who will not see it: "Woodcut of a foxglove in flower, Fuchs 1543, leaves opposite, bells hanging." Not "image of a plant."

## Credit line in CMS

`Collection, object number, date. License. Photo: [photographer if separate].`
