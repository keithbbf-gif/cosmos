# Bibliography — herbal medicine history pack

Working list of sources named in article YAML `citations:` and in prose. Not a complete library. Official publishers (USP, BP, EDQM, FDA, EMA, WHO, CITES, CBD) remain the check for living documents.

## Ancient and medieval texts

- Edwin Smith Papyrus — surgical cases; plant dressings belong more to Ebers than to Smith.
- Mesopotamian plant and prescription tablets (URU.AN.NA / Šammu šikinšu neighborhood; see specialist editions).
- Hippocratic corpus — treat as a library, not a single author. Do not treat “let food be thy medicine” as a stable quote.
- Pedanius Dioscorides, *De materia medica* (1st c. CE).
- Galen, compound-medicine and *On the Powers of Simple Drugs* tradition.
- Ibn Sīnā, *al-Qānūn fī al-ṭibb* (Books 2 and 5 for simples and formulary).
- Al-Rāzī, *Kitāb al-Ḥāwī*; smallpox/measles treatise in Latin transmission.
- *Shennong bencao* tradition; *Xinxiu bencao* (659) as a dated Tang state herbal.
- Caraka and Suśruta saṃhitā literature (layered; dating is specialist).
- Hildegard of Bingen, *Physica* (and the related *Causae et curae* file).
- Plan of St. Gall; Walahfrid Strabo, *Hortulus*; *Capitulare de villis*.
- Martín de la Cruz / Juan Badiano, *Libellus de medicinalibus Indorum herbis* (1552).
- Garcia de Orta, *Colóquios dos simples* (Goa, 1563); Clusius Latin.
- William Withering, *An Account of the Foxglove* (1785).
- Edward Stone, letter to the Royal Society (1763).

## Statutes and official books

- London Pharmacopoeia (1618); British Pharmacopoeia (1864–); European Pharmacopoeia (EDQM).
- United States Pharmacopeia (1820–); USP–NF.
- Pure Food and Drug Act (1906), 34 Stat. 768.
- Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (1938); Kefauver–Harris Amendments (1962).
- Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act (1994), Pub. L. 103-417; 21 U.S.C. 321(ff).
- 21 C.F.R. Part 111 (2007 dietary-supplement cGMP); 21 C.F.R. 101.36; 21 C.F.R. 119 (ephedra).
- Directive 2004/24/EC (traditional herbal medicinal products); 2001/83/EC.
- WHO *Monographs on selected medicinal plants* (from 1999); Alma-Ata 1978; Traditional Medicine Strategy papers.
- CITES (1973; in force 1975); CBD (1992); Nagoya Protocol (2010; in force 2014).
- Family Smoking Prevention and Tobacco Control Act (2009).

## Claims posture

Primary sources beat a blog’s memory. Living monographs are licensed or official pages — do not pirate assay tables into figures. Flag weak plant identifications **soft** or `[VERIFY]`. This pack is historical and educational; see `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`.
