## Stage map

1. **Origins** — channel, demos, funding scar, early dialogue, the Chomsky split.
2. **Symbols** — lexicons, transducers, charts, frames, slot dialogue.
3. **Counts and HMMs** — corpora, hidden chains, n-grams, IBM alignment.
4. **Structured prediction** — maxent, MEMM, CRF, perceptron, SVM.
5. **Vectors** — Firth through Word2Vec, GloVe, fastText.
6. **Neural warmup** — Bengio, SENNA, LSTM, RNNLM.
7. **Seq2seq** — the 2014 dare, attention, the Moses handoff.
8. **Tasks and rulers** — BLEU, treebanks, parsers, topics, shared tasks, 2016 bruises.
