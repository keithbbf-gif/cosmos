# Novelty fence — NLP before transformers

This folder is a **public-history** box. It exists so a later public
page can talk about language technology without touching anything that
is still private or unfiled.

## In

- Published papers, books, patents that already issued, and
  well-documented demos (Georgetown-IBM 1954, ELIZA 1966, etc.).
- Standard textbook accounts (Jurafsky & Martin; Manning & Schütze;
  Rabiner's HMM tutorial).
- Shared-task history (MUC, ACE, CoNLL, WMT, TREC).
- Widely reported industry artifacts (Moses, Pharaoh, SENNA,
  word2vec binaries, Google News vectors).

## Out

- Any private system, service, ledger, occupancy scheme, or product
  architecture.
- Any unfiled method, combination, or implementation detail.
- Any "we built" claim that is not already in the public record.
- Customer data, credentials, spend, live paths.
- Quotes long enough to substitute for the original paper.

## Voice of uncertainty

If a date or attribution is fuzzy in the public record, the draft says
so. It does not invent a precise statistic to sound sure. Interpretation
is labeled as interpretation.

## Not a novelty opinion

These drafts are not legal advice and not a novelty opinion on anyone's
filings. They retell a public field. That is the whole job.
