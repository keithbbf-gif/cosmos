People send a deposit and then shop for a different table in their head. The oak does not shop. The oak is already becoming a cut list. This piece is the short list of what the money bought, so the next argument is smaller.

## It buys

**Identification.** Boards pulled and marked for this job (UCC § 2-501). That mark is why cancellation changes after the pull (piece 29).

**A slot.** Other work waits or goes around you (piece 19). Jumping you later will cost someone a call (piece 23).

**A start event.** The clock on the ack can start (piece 25). Without the check, the date is a costume.

**Procurement.** Hardware and stone the shop would not have ordered (piece 22). Those POs may not reverse clean.

## It does not buy

**A new object.** After spec freeze, a new overhang is a change order (piece 08, piece 30). The deposit does not reset the drawing.

**A matched phone.** Color lives in the room (piece 31, piece 32). The check does not make a filter true.

**A holiday.** Thanksgiving is a pile-up (piece 24). Money does not move the kiln (piece 20) or the recoat window (piece 21).

**Storage forever.** A ready piece you cannot take is a storage ticket (piece 27).

**A path that does not exist.** If the landing vetoes the crate (piece 14), the deposit does not widen the rail. It may fund a split or a smaller top — if you change-order it in time.

**Silence.** You still owe approvals and a last payment. The shop still owes calls if the stack slips.

## Dealers

Do not tell the household the deposit "locks the price forever" unless the ack says the quote cannot move with lumber or freight. Many quotes expire (piece 03). A deposit on an expired quote is a mess. Re-ack.

Do not tell the household the deposit "covers freight." Freight is often a separate line (piece 34, piece 45).

## Homeowners

If you are not ready to freeze a drawing, you are not ready to deposit. That is not a sales close. It is a mercy. Keep the money. Keep looking. Send the RFQ when the problem is one sentence (piece 06).

## A receipt you can read in a year

RFQ number, REV letter, amount, form of payment, date cleared, start event, pointer to cancellation schedule (piece 29), pointer to freeze (piece 30), "not freight unless listed," "not a finish approval." Put a copy in the job jacket and a copy in the household email.

If the household calls in month three and says "but we paid," you will want that receipt next to the cut list. If the shop calls and says "but you changed the leaf," they will want it next to REV D.

A deposit without this paper is a magnet on a drawer and a future argument.

## What to tell a spouse who did not send the check

The check bought oak and a slot, not a right to redesign after freeze, not freight unless listed, not a phone match, not a holiday. If they want a different table, that is a change order or a cancellation with a clock (piece 29, piece 30). Saying this once in the receipt email saves a three-person call in month two.

## The sentence on the receipt

"Deposit for RFQ-__, REV __, applied to lumber and slot. Not a full payment. Not an approval of changes after freeze. Terms on ack." Boring. Correct.

The cut list will not read your second thoughts. Buy the first thoughts on purpose, or do not buy yet.
