**Disclaimer.** Educational shop talk. Not a quote, not a contract, and not legal advice. Deposit terms, lead times, and cancellation rules live on the acknowledgment you actually sign. A shop in another state may run a different clock.

The return-air was behind the sideboard. In July the room went stale and the homeowner pulled the piece off the wall and scratched the floor. The quote had been perfect. The room had been live. Nobody wrote the grate.

Empty-room measures lie (piece 09). The live room has air, power, heat, and doors. Those objects steal inches and they steal goodwill. Put them on the RFQ or meet them on the punch list (piece 37).

## Power

Outlets, switches, dimmers, USB plates, alarm pads. Photograph with a tape (piece 07). A case that covers the only outlet is a power strip and a marriage. A cutout or a pass-through is a custom line (piece 12). A grommet is a hardware lead time (piece 22).

If the piece needs power inside, say who wires it. Wood shops are not electricians unless they are.

## Air

Supply and return. A blocked return is a comfort complaint that will be blamed on the furniture. A supply that blows a finish for a year is a finish complaint (piece 32). Write the grate. Raise the plinth, cut a notch, or move the piece. Moving is a design choice. Notching is a shop choice. Both are money.

## Heat

Radiators, stoves, fireplaces. Clearance is a fire and a finish problem. A table next to a stove will move differently in winter (piece 36). A bookcase over a radiator is a humidity machine. Some of this is code. Some of this is a shop saying no (piece 42).

## Doors and drawers in the room

The room's doors, not the case's. A table that blocks the kitchen swing. A dresser that blocks a closet. A bench that blocks a French door. Measure swings open (piece 11, piece 13). Write the door that slams because of the dog.

## Data and ugly plates

Thermostats, cable, internet. Covering a thermostat is how you fund a service call. Relocating it is another trade.

## Windows and sun

A finish that lives in a south window is a different finish (piece 31, piece 32). Write the window. A bench in a bay needs the template (piece 16).

## Pets and people

A dog bed that is not moving. A wheelchair pass. A kid's path to the back door. These are not cute. They are aisles (piece 13) and they are why a "perfect" 96-inch table is a bad table.

## Dealers

Plan views omit plates. Send a wall photo at 18 inches and at 48 inches off the floor. Unpretty. Sufficient.

## The RFQ lines

A list headed LIVE ROOM: outlet L, return R, radiator, door swing, thermostat. One photo each. One sentence of intent: notch / relocate / keep clear.

The quote that forgot the grate was not cheap. It was unfinished. Finish the room on paper before the saw finishes the piece.
