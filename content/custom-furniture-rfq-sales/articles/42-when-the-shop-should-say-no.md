The stair had vetoed the crate (piece 14). The holiday had vetoed the stack (piece 24). The household wanted both vetoes ignored and a rush fee (piece 23). The honest product was no. No is how the shop still has a reputation in this county when the dinner happens on the old table.

Refusing work is not a failed close. This pack does not close. It tells when a yes would be a lie.

## Physics

The landing will not open. The wood will not stay a 96-inch solid slab glued dead to an apron (piece 32). The can in the garage will not be a finish system (piece 39). The house is still wet (piece 36). Say the veto. Offer a smaller object or a later month if one exists. Do not offer a spell.

## Calendar

The queue is full of names that already paid (piece 19, piece 26). Jumping them for a stranger's turkey is a choice you will own (piece 23). If you cannot defend a date, do not write it (piece 25). Part 435 will care on remote channels. Your neighbors will care on every channel.

## Paper

Two people in the house, two drawings (piece 06). A dealer PO that demands 10-day custom (piece 05). A "lifetime heirloom" card you cannot honor (piece 38). A deposit before a drawing (piece 03). No.

## Capacity

A shop with one spray room and a flu week is a shop that should stop taking November. Heroic yeses become January plus a scar. Empty the whiteboard on purpose (piece 24).

## How to write the no

One sentence of cause, one sentence of alternative, no insult. "We cannot fit a 96-inch crate on that landing; we can split the base or we can build 84." "We cannot make Thanksgiving; we can do the week of January 12 or you can take a made-to-order in the rack stain." "We will not pour that can; we can sample a house system in your room."

Do not write a novel. Do not write a coupon. Do not write "call today" energy.

## Dealers

A mill that never says no will say it in the form of a slip and a shrug. Prefer the mill that nos a special you should not have sold. Then go sell a length that fits.

If you, the dealer, should say no to a household — path, budget, date — do it before you RFQ the mill. The mill is not your courage.

## Homeowners

A no can feel like a snub. Read the cause. If it is physics, thank them. If it is capacity, ask for the next open stack. If it is taste dressed as physics, get a second shop (piece 40) — and still measure the landing.

## Keep a no log

Date, RFQ, cause (physics / calendar / paper / capacity), alternative offered. The log is how you defend a later accusation that you "would not help." It is also how you see a month of yeses that should have been nos. A shop that never logs a no is a shop that will log remakes.

## After the no

Leave the door open for a different job. Do not keep a maybe on the whiteboard to be polite. Maybes block slots. Polite is a clean no and a clean board.

## No is cheaper than a porch

A crate in the rain is a yes that should have been a no. The office embarrassment of refusing a dinner date is smaller than the porch. Choose it while the tape is still in your hand, in June if you can (piece 24). The household can still eat. The shop can still sleep.

The porch in the rain is a yes that should have been a no. Choose the smaller embarrassment. Choose it in the office, in June if you can, with the tape still in your hand.
