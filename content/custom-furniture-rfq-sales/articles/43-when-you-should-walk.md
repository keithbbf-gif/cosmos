The website had a Buy button on a "custom" table and a clock that said two weeks. The stair had not been measured. The deposit was a card form with no drawing. That is not a shop having a bad week. That is a walk.

Piece 42 is the shop's no. This is yours. Dealers and households use the same eyes. The tickets differ (piece 33). The flags do not.

## No paper

They will not write a quote (piece 03). They will not send an ack. They want money to "hold" a ghost. They laugh at spec freeze (piece 08, piece 30). UCC § 2-201 is not a vibe. It is why adults write.

## Cart costume on custom

A price without inches, path, or finish rules (piece 01). A *from* tile sold as the bid. A website ship stamp that cannot survive the stack (piece 19) — and if the channel is internet or phone, 16 CFR Part 435 is standing in the doorway (piece 25).

## The tape is a joke

They will not visit and they will not let you own the inch (piece 17). They shrug at the landing (piece 14). They call white glove a wider rail (piece 34). They will not crate-out a number (piece 35).

## The calendar is a joke

Every job is 12 weeks, empty or slammed. Rush is always for sale (piece 23). Nobody calls when it slips. The only date is a hallway.

## The money is a joke

Deposit before a drawing. No refund language of any kind, even before lumber (piece 26, piece 29). A new progress invoice that was never on the ack (piece 27). A balance surprise at the curb with a card they will not take.

## The mouth is a script

Scarcity without a whiteboard. Price dressed as a moral category. "Heirloom" as a warranty (piece 38). Insults about the other shop instead of a scope grid (piece 40). A closer who talks over the millworker.

This pack banned those phrases for a reason. If you hear them, you are not in this folder's shop.

## Dealers

Walk a mill that will not initial a packet (piece 41), that never says no (piece 42), that ships your routing guide into the void, that wants you to collect 100% and then floats the special on their lumber. Your household will still call *you* when the crate dies.

## Homeowners

Walk if two people in your house cannot sign one sentence (piece 06) — walk *yourselves* until you can. Do not hire a shop to referee a marriage. Walk a budget that cannot fund the stack you actually want; buy a smaller object or wait (piece 04).

## Take the packet with you

Object sentence, inches, path, photos, budget ceiling. That packet is yours. The shop that wanted a card without a drawing does not get to keep your work. The next shop gets a cleaner RFQ because you already walked the landing. The walk was not wasted. The button was.

## Silence is a flag

A shop that will not answer a written question about the stack, the deposit, or the path is already telling you how they will handle a slip (piece 25). One slow day is a shop. A week of silence on an RFQ you sent complete is a walk. Take the packet. Keep the tape.

## How to walk

A short email: we are not moving forward; thank you for the time. No speech. No one-star if they were merely not the fit. Save the stars for lies and silence.

Take your packet to the next shop. The packet was the work. It travels.

## What is not a walk

A higher integer with a fuller scope (piece 41). A no that came with an alternative (piece 42). A date in January instead of a lying November. Those are the job working.

The landing will still be there next year. The card form does not have to be. Leave the button. Keep the tape.
