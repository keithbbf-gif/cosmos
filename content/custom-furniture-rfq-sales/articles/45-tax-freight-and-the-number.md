The hallway said eighty-four hundred (piece 03). The quote said seventy-two for the object, eleven for white glove, and tax the household had not put in the marriage budget. Nobody lied with energy. They collapsed three lines into one mouth.

A quote that is one integer is a hallway. A quote that splits object, freight, and tax is a document you can fund. This pack will not tell you your rate. Rates live in a jurisdiction and a fact pattern. It will tell you to stop hiding the truck in the oak.

## Three lines

**Object.** The piece, the finish, the shop labor, the buyouts the shop is providing (piece 22).

**Freight / service.** Dock, liftgate, threshold, room, crate, a smaller truck for a dirt road (piece 34, piece 35). Estimates should name assumptions. "Freight extra" with no class is an incomplete quote.

**Tax.** Who collects, on what, delivered where. Dealer resale certificates, out-of-state ship-to, a mix of labor and goods — these change outcomes. `[VERIFY]` with whoever actually files. Do not let a salesperson guess a percent in a driveway.

If a line is unknown, write UNKNOWN and a date you will fill it. Do not write 0 because it felt clean.

## Why households blow it

They fund the hallway integer and then meet the truck. They thought "delivered" was in the oak (piece 34). They thought a deposit was the last money (piece 27). Split lines fix this more than a pep talk.

## Why dealers blow it

They advertise a mill price as a delivered retail. They forget the routing guide costs. They forget tax on a delivered special versus a pickup. Their POS has one field (piece 19). Add fields or add a note. The household will not read your mind.

## Installation and other trades

Scribe, electrician, stone set — if they are not in the object line, they need a fourth line or a "by others." A pretty integer that assumed the electrician was free is a costume (piece 41).

## Validity

Lumber and freight move. A quote that is good thirty days (piece 03) should say whether freight is a hold or a re-estimate at ship. Diesel is not sentimental.

## Resale paper is not a vibe

A dealer certificate, if it applies, belongs in the vendor packet with the RFQ (piece 05), not in a Friday scramble when the invoice already has tax. Households do not have that paper. Out-of-state ship-to can change the answer. This pack will not pick your rate. It will say: the person who files the return should see the ship-to before the salesperson invents a percent in a driveway.

## A hallway translation

Hallway: "about eighty-four." Quote: object 7200, freight 1100 room-of-choice stairs, tax TBD on ship-to. Household budget: 8400. They are short the tax and any redelivery. Either raise the ceiling or drop the service to threshold and take the last flight of stairs yourselves. That choice is the job. A single integer would have hidden it until the truck.

## UNKNOWN is a legal word

If freight or tax is not known, write UNKNOWN and a date you will fill it. Do not write 0. A zero that becomes eleven hundred at the truck is a hallway lie even if nobody meant it. Households can fund a ceiling that includes a guess. They cannot fund a surprise they were told was nothing.

## The ack lines

Object $ __. Freight $ __ (assumptions). Tax $ __ or "to be calculated on ship-to." Other trades: listed or by others. Validity window.

The hallway can keep its one number as a ballpark if you label it. The ack should keep its three. Then the budget is about a table, a truck, and a state — not about a feeling.
