## Pieces

### Wave 1 — Quote-only and the RFQ

- **[Quote-only is not a brush-off](articles/01-quote-only-is-not-a-brush-off.md)** — `quote-only-is-not-a-brush-off` — 1157 words  
  The corkboard price list is last season's oak. A quote-only shop is not hiding a button. It is refusing to sell a number that the room has not earned.

- **[An RFQ is a job, not an email](articles/02-rfq-is-a-job-not-an-email.md)** — `rfq-is-a-job-not-an-email` — 977 words  
  A subject line that says 'table??' is not a request for quote. A job packet is. Here is the packet.

- **[Three prices that are not quotes](articles/03-three-prices-that-are-not-quotes.md)** — `three-prices-that-are-not-quotes` — 990 words  
  A number in the air, a number from last year's list, and a number on a signed ack are three different objects. Mix them and you will fight.

- **[Custom, made-to-order, and stock are three products](articles/04-custom-vs-made-to-order-vs-stock.md)** — `custom-vs-made-to-order-vs-stock` — 1031 words  
  If the shop already has a cut list in a drawer, you are not buying custom. You are buying a known object that has not been sawn yet. Say so.

- **[The dealer RFQ that fits on one page](articles/05-the-dealer-rfq-one-page.md)** — `the-dealer-rfq-one-page` — 939 words  
  If the millworker needs your voicemail to finish the takeoff, the RFQ failed. One page. The fields that actually price.

- **[The homeowner RFQ when you do not have a catalog number](articles/06-the-homeowner-rfq-without-the-catalog.md)** — `the-homeowner-rfq-without-the-catalog` — 940 words  
  You do not need a SKU. You need a room, a path, a tape, and one problem stated in a sentence a millworker can build.

- **[Photos that help a quote, and photos that lie](articles/07-photos-that-help-and-photos-that-lie.md)** — `photos-that-help-and-photos-that-lie` — 915 words  
  A ruler in the frame is worth more than a pretty room. A filtered table is a lie the shop will not eat.

- **[The approval drawing is the order](articles/08-the-approval-drawing-is-the-order.md)** — `the-approval-drawing-is-the-order` — 907 words  
  The drawing with a revision number is the piece. The email that said 'love it' is not. Sign the elevation or do not start the oak.

### Wave 2 — Measure

- **[Tape on the wall, not the wish](articles/09-tape-on-the-wall-not-the-wish.md)** — `tape-on-the-wall-not-the-wish` — 932 words  
  The tape does not care what the listing said. Hook it, write the inch, and date the page. A wish is not a dimension.

- **[Rooms that are not square](articles/10-rooms-that-are-not-square.md)** — `rooms-that-are-not-square` — 959 words  
  The corner that is 91 degrees is the job. A perfect rectangle on the drawing is a wish the plaster will not honor.

- **[A dining table that fits the room](articles/11-dining-table-that-fits-the-room.md)** — `dining-table-that-fits-the-room` — 962 words  
  Eight people is a chair count, not a length. The wall, the pass, and the leaf decide the inches.

- **[A case piece against a baseboard](articles/12-case-piece-against-a-baseboard.md)** — `case-piece-against-a-baseboard` — 881 words  
  The dresser is not the width of the wall. It is the width of the wall minus the baseboard, the outlet, and the door that has to open on Tuesday.

- **[Island and aisle numbers](articles/13-island-and-aisle-numbers.md)** — `island-and-aisle-numbers` — 947 words  
  An island is a piece of furniture that has to let a dishwasher open. The aisle is the job. The top is the souvenir.

- **[The path through the house](articles/14-the-path-through-the-house.md)** — `the-path-through-the-house` — 888 words  
  A table that fits the room and dies on the landing is not a table. It is a porch. Walk the path with a tape before you quote the top.

- **[Ceiling fans, pendants, and the height you forgot](articles/15-ceiling-fans-and-pendants.md)** — `ceiling-fans-and-pendants` — 773 words  
  The pendant is already in the room. The table is not. Measure the air between them before you quote a thick top and a flower vase.

- **[When the tape is not enough: templates](articles/16-templates-vs-the-tape.md)** — `templates-vs-the-tape` — 760 words  
  A curve will not sit still in a notebook. Cardboard is cheap. A remake is not. Say when the tape has lost.

- **[Who owns the tape](articles/17-customer-measure-vs-shop-measure.md)** — `customer-measure-vs-shop-measure` — 780 words  
  If the shop holds the tape, the shop owns the inch. If you email the inch, you do. Write that on the drawing. A disclaimer without a warning is a shrug.

- **[The inch the quote forgot](articles/18-allowances-the-quote-forgets.md)** — `allowances-the-quote-forgets` — 679 words  
  The wall is never empty. Outlets, returns, radiators, and a door that slams are part of the inch. If they are not on the RFQ, they will be on the punch list.

### Wave 3 — Lead time

- **[Lead time is a queue, not a stamp](articles/19-lead-time-is-a-queue-not-a-stamp.md)** — `lead-time-is-a-queue-not-a-stamp` — 914 words  
  A date is a stack of waits. Queue, kiln, mill, finish, hardware, truck. A rubber stamp that says 12 weeks is a costume for that stack.

- **[Lumber is already a calendar](articles/20-lumber-is-already-a-calendar.md)** — `lumber-is-already-a-calendar` — 839 words  
  The oak is either in the rack, in the kiln, or on a truck. That sentence is a date. 'We'll find some' is not.

- **[Finish days are not idle](articles/21-finish-days-are-not-idle.md)** — `finish-days-are-not-idle` — 739 words  
  A finish room that looks empty is working. Recoat windows and cure are the job. Stacking chairs on a green top is how you buy a remake.

- **[Hardware and stone gate the wood](articles/22-hardware-and-stone-gate-the-wood.md)** — `hardware-and-stone-gate-the-wood` — 768 words  
  The mill can be done and the job can still be a box on the floor. Hinges, stone, glass, and fabric have their own whiteboards. Write them on the RFQ.

- **[Rush is usually a lie you buy](articles/23-rush-is-usually-a-lie-you-buy.md)** — `rush-is-usually-a-lie-you-buy` — 810 words  
  A rush fee can buy overtime. It cannot buy a kiln. It cannot invent a hinge on a boat. If the stack will not compress, the fee is a souvenir.

- **[Holidays and the showroom season](articles/24-holidays-and-the-showroom-season.md)** — `holidays-and-the-showroom-season` — 741 words  
  Everyone wants the table before Thanksgiving. The whiteboard already knew. A holiday is not a surprise. It is a pile-up you can see from June.

- **[The date on the acknowledgment](articles/25-the-date-on-the-ack.md)** — `the-date-on-the-ack` — 912 words  
  The date on the ack is the date. The date in the hallway is a maybe. If the stack slips, you owe a sentence — and on remote orders, you may owe a Rule notice.

### Wave 4 — Deposits, freeze, finish

- **[Why a shop takes a deposit](articles/26-why-we-take-a-deposit.md)** — `why-we-take-a-deposit` — 797 words  
  A deposit is not a souvenir and not a tax. It is lumber and a place in line. The percent lives on the acknowledgment, not in a slogan.

- **[Deposit, progress, balance](articles/27-deposit-is-not-the-whole-price.md)** — `deposit-is-not-the-whole-price` — 783 words  
  A deposit starts the job. A progress payment, if you use one, meets the mill. The balance meets the truck. Mixing those three is how a curb turns into a scene.

- **[What the deposit buys — and what it does not](articles/28-what-the-deposit-buys.md)** — `what-the-deposit-buys` — 639 words  
  The check buys oak and a name on the board. It does not buy a new drawing after freeze, a filter-matched stain, or a date the kiln cannot keep.

- **[Cancellation after the cut list](articles/29-cancellation-after-the-cut-list.md)** — `cancellation-after-the-cut-list` — 847 words  
  Before the saw, cancellation is a conversation. After the cut list, it is a specially manufactured object. The UCC has a neighborhood for that. This page is not your lawyer.

- **[Change orders have a clock](articles/30-change-orders-have-a-clock.md)** — `change-orders-have-a-clock` — 669 words  
  An inch of overhang after the tenons are cut is not a tweak. It is a new apron. Change orders have a clock. Miss it and you are buying a second piece.

- **[Finish samples in the room light](articles/31-finish-samples-in-the-room-light.md)** — `finish-samples-in-the-room-light` — 706 words  
  A board that slept on the table knows more than a phone. Approve color in the room you will live in, or admit you approved a lamp.

- **[It will not match the phone](articles/32-it-will-not-match-the-phone.md)** — `it-will-not-match-the-phone` — 744 words  
  Oak moves. Lots differ. Phones lie. A delivered table that is alive is not a defect. A delivered table that was built wrong is. Learn the difference before the truck.

- **[Dealer price and the floor](articles/33-dealer-price-and-the-floor.md)** — `dealer-price-and-the-floor` — 649 words  
  House, floor, and sold special are three tickets. MAP is a policy, not a personality. The mill needs the object, not your margin.

### Wave 5 — Freight, warranty, walking

- **[Dock, threshold, room of choice](articles/34-freight-white-glove-threshold.md)** — `freight-white-glove-threshold` — 671 words  
  Dock is the truck. Threshold is the sill. White glove is pads and assembly. None of them invent a wider stair. Name the service. Measure the path.

- **[Crating is part of the job](articles/35-crating-is-part-of-the-job.md)** — `crating-is-part-of-the-job` — 643 words  
  The crate is the object that turns the landing. Blanket-wrap is a different gamble. Price the box. Do not photograph only the top.

- **[Site conditions before the truck](articles/36-site-conditions-before-the-truck.md)** — `site-conditions-before-the-truck` — 642 words  
  A table built for a lived-in room will protest a wet slab and a closed-up August. Climate is a spec. Write it before the truck, not after the cup.

- **[The punch list at the door](articles/37-punch-list-at-the-door.md)** — `punch-list-at-the-door` — 636 words  
  Walk the piece in the room you approved, the same day. Joints, size, finish versus the board, the crate corners. A month-later filtered photo is a weaker witness.

- **[Warranty versus as-specified](articles/38-warranty-vs-as-specified.md)** — `warranty-vs-as-specified` — 686 words  
  Custom is warranted as specified. Structure should not fail. A lifestyle should not be in the card. 'Heirloom' is a hope. Put the real sentences on paper.

- **[When they bring their own stone, stain, or cloth](articles/39-customer-supplied-materials.md)** — `customer-supplied-materials` — 669 words  
  A leftover can from the garage is not a finish system. COM is a calendar and a flaw risk. If they supply it, write who inspects it and who owns a miss.

- **[How to shop three shops without becoming a problem](articles/40-shopping-three-shops.md)** — `shopping-three-shops` — 678 words  
  Send the same job to three shops. Compare scopes, not integers. A cheaper number that dropped the leaf is not a win. It is a different table.

- **[The cheap quote is a different piece](articles/41-the-cheap-quote-is-a-different-piece.md)** — `the-cheap-quote-is-a-different-piece` — 645 words  
  A low number that dropped the leaf, the visit, and the crate is not a bargain. It is a different object wearing your room's name.

- **[When the shop should say no](articles/42-when-the-shop-should-say-no.md)** — `when-the-shop-should-say-no` — 682 words  
  A no is a product. The kiln, the landing, the calendar, and the drawing can each veto. Saying it early is cheaper than a porch.

- **[When you should walk](articles/43-when-you-should-walk.md)** — `when-you-should-walk` — 701 words  
  Walk if there is no drawing, no ack, a cart price on an unmeasured room, or a date nobody will put on paper. Another shop exists. Another landing does not.

- **[The one-sitting RFQ checklist](articles/44-the-one-sitting-rfq-checklist.md)** — `the-one-sitting-rfq-checklist` — 631 words  
  Print it. Write the job name at the top. If a box fails, you are not sending an RFQ yet. You are sending a wish.

- **[Tax, freight, and the number on the quote](articles/45-tax-freight-and-the-number.md)** — `tax-freight-and-the-number` — 676 words  
  The number in the hallway is rarely the number that ships. Split the object, the truck, and the tax. Then the budget is a budget.

- **[A commercial RFQ is not a house letter](articles/46-commercial-rfq-vs-house.md)** — `commercial-rfq-vs-house` — 758 words  
  A day room is not a dining room. Typicals, docks, infection control, and a ribbon date are the packet. Do not send a Pinterest letter to a facility.
