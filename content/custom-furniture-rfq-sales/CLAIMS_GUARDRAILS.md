# Claims guardrails — custom furniture RFQ / quote-only pack

Internal. Not a blog post. Counsel still owns the last read.

These drafts teach dealers and homeowners how a custom shop quotes, measures, calendars, and takes money. They are not a price list and not an acknowledgment. They still sit next to a future contact form. A sentence that would be false on an ack is false here.

BBF / Bradley Brand Furniture is the internal lane. Do not put the company name, a named living salesperson, or an invented deposit percentage in public copy.

## The document, not the vibe

A quote is an offer (or a step toward one). An acknowledgment is the writing the shop will actually perform. A website paragraph is neither.

Goods over the UCC writing threshold are the ordinary case. UCC § 2-201(1) wants a writing signed by the party against whom enforcement is sought. UCC § 2-201(3)(a) is the specially manufactured goods exception: goods made for this buyer, not suitable for sale to others in the ordinary course, with a substantial beginning or procurement commitments *before* notice of repudiation. Arkansas enacted Article 2 as Ark. Code Ann. § 4-2-201. Recite the idea. Do not tell a reader their oral handshake is "binding." That is a fact pattern for counsel.

16 CFR Part 435 (Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule) applies to orders solicited and placed by mail, internet, or telephone. If the shop states a ship time, it needs a reasonable basis. If it states none, the default clock is thirty days after a properly completed order (fifty if the buyer applies to the seller for credit at the time of order). Delay notices, cancel-and-refund options, and when silence is not consent are in § 435.2. A showroom handshake with a written ack is a different channel. Do not smear the Rule onto every dining table. Do not pretend the Rule never applies because the piece is "custom."

Magnuson-Moss (15 U.S.C. § 2301 et seq.) governs written warranties to consumers. "Lifetime" and "full" have meanings. A custom piece sold "as specified" still cannot hide a written warranty's limits.

FTC Made in USA / unqualified U.S.-origin claims are assembly-plus-content rules. A top finished in Arkansas on imported hardware is not automatically an unqualified "Made in USA" stamp.

TSCA Title VI / CARB is about composite wood formaldehyde — relevant if a quote includes plywood, MDF, or particleboard. Solid hardwood is a different sentence.

## Never say (or imply)

### Sales theater

- A cart price on a custom SKU that has not been measured or specified.
- "Free quote" if the visit is a paid site measure. Say which.
- "We'll beat any quote" without a dated, written policy.
- "Only two slots left" without a shop note you can show. Scarcity without a queue is a lie.
- "Investment piece" as a substitute for the number.
- "Heirloom" as a warranty.

### Money

- A house deposit percentage, progress split, or restocking fee as if it were statute. Those live on the ack. `[VERIFY]` until they do.
- "Deposits are always non-refundable." State law and the writing control. Specially manufactured goods *can* leave the shop holding lumber and labor; that is why shops take deposits. It is not a license to invent a forfeit.
- "Your deposit earns interest" unless the shop actually escrowed it that way.

### Time

- A ship date without a reasonable basis (Part 435 when that Rule applies; honesty when it does not).
- "12 weeks" as a universal custom-furniture number.
- Treating a penciled "maybe Thanksgiving" as an ack date.
- Skipping a delay notice because the piece is handmade.

### Measure and fit

- "We are not responsible for customer measurements" as the only sentence if the shop also refused a site measure. Say who owned the tape.
- Invented clearances. Cite NKBA, AWI, or Architectural Graphic Standards — or `[CITE NEEDED]`.
- Promising a piece will "fit any stair" without a path measure.

### Warranty and wood

- "Solid wood never moves."
- "The table will match this phone photo."
- "No VOC" / "non-toxic" as a consumer stamp without a named finish and a named standard.
- Unqualified "antique restoration" claims on a new piece.

### Channel

- Inventing a current Lowes / Wayfair / Dillard's program. Heritage-page channel history is history. `[VERIFY]` before any "we supply X" sentence.
- Listing SNF / hospital projects as current contracts unless the file is open.

## Allowed shapes (still need the ack)

- "Quote-only: the number comes after spec, measure, and a written ack."
- "A deposit holds a queue slot and buys material. The split is on the acknowledgment."
- "Lead time is queue + lumber + mill + finish + bought-out hardware + freight. The date is the date on the ack."
- "If we take your measure, we own the tape. If you email inches, you do."
- "Custom after the cut list is a different piece. Change orders reopen price and calendar."
- "16 CFR 435 applies to mail, internet, and telephone orders. Read the Rule. This page is not your delay notice."

## Process

1. If a sentence would be false on an acknowledgment, it is false in a blog caption next to a contact form.
2. Dealer one-pagers and homeowner emails get the same list.
3. Cite the UCC section, the CFR, the NKBA guideline, or flag `[CITE NEEDED]` / `[VERIFY]`.
4. Empty seats stay empty: counsel and a named closer are not invented here to launder a deposit rule.

## Empty seats

Counsel is not named here. House deposit percent, published lead weeks, and cancellation refunds wait on the live acknowledgment.
