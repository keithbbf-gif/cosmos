# Photo notes

No fake showroom. No stock couple on a white sofa presented as our floor. No unredacted customer drawing with a real address and a deposit amount.

These drafts are text-first. Images, if any, come from Keith's library (`D:\BBF\BBF Photos`) or are clearly labeled diagrams. Caption every figure in the draft *and* here before a public import. Photographer unnamed until file metadata is pulled. Never invent a credit.

## Allowed

- Shop-library frames: tape on a wall, elevation on the door, crate at a stair, finish board in room light, ack on the counter (redact names and dollars).
- Typeset diagrams: dining clearances, delivery-path turns, lead-time stack, deposit vs progress vs balance.
- Public-domain NKBA-style *original* diagrams we draw ourselves. Do not scrape a copyrighted guideline page as a hero.
- Tables set in type. Those carry more information than a lifestyle hero.

## Banned

- AI-generated "our Warren factory" that is not the shop.
- Unsplash sofas as implied line.
- Unredacted customer POs, addresses, or card receipts.
- Before/after "makeover" faces.
- Lifestyle "investment" spreads.
- Competitor catalog pages presented as ours.

## Alt text

Describe the figure, not the vibe. "Typeset diagram: 36-inch chair clearance, 42-inch pass" — not "the table of your dreams."
