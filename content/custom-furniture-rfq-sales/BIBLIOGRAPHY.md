# Bibliography — custom furniture RFQ / quote-only pack

Consolidated citations. Article front matter repeats the ones that piece actually uses. Dates are the versions these drafts were written against. Recheck eCFR, FTC, NKBA, and the live shop acknowledgment before a public import.

Not legal advice. Not a complete library. Not a quote.

## Contract and commercial law

- UCC § 2-201 Formal Requirements; Statute of Frauds — https://www.law.cornell.edu/ucc/2/2-201
- UCC § 2-201(3)(a) specially manufactured goods exception (substantial beginning or procurement commitments before notice of repudiation).
- UCC § 2-204 Formation in General; § 2-206 Offer and Acceptance; § 2-207 Additional Terms in Acceptance or Confirmation (battle of the forms — dealer POs).
- UCC § 2-501 Insurable Interest in Goods; Manner of Identification of Goods.
- UCC § 2-508 Cure by Seller of Improper Tender; § 2-601 Buyer's Rights on Improper Delivery; § 2-606 What Constitutes Acceptance of Goods.
- UCC § 2-709 Action for the Price (especially identified goods; goods not reasonably resalable).
- Arkansas enactment: Ark. Code Ann. § 4-2-201 et seq. Recheck the current code. This pack is not Arkansas-specific legal advice.
- Arkansas Deceptive Trade Practices Act, Ark. Code Ann. § 4-88-101 et seq. — unfair or deceptive acts. Cite as a *class* of state law; do not invent a furniture-deposit subsection.

## FTC / advertising / remote orders

- 16 CFR Part 435 — Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule. eCFR: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-435
- 16 CFR 435.2 — reasonable basis for stated ship time; 30-day default (50 days if buyer applies to seller for credit at order); delay option notice; when silence is not consent.
- FTC, *Business Guide to the FTC's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule* — https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule
- FTC Made in USA / unqualified U.S.-origin claims — https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-made-usa-standard
- Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, 15 U.S.C. § 2301 et seq.; 16 CFR Parts 700–703.

## Measure and planning (cite the edition you hold)

- National Kitchen & Bath Association (NKBA), Kitchen Planning Guidelines — work-aisle and landing-area figures. Recheck the current edition before printing a number as NKBA's. [VERIFY edition]
- Architectural Graphic Standards (Wiley) — dining, circulation, stair, and door clearances as used by architects. Edition in the shop library: `[VERIFY]`.
- Time-Saver Standards for Interior Design and Space Planning — seating widths and table clearances. `[VERIFY]` edition.
- AWI (Architectural Woodwork Institute) Standards — architectural woodwork tolerances, shop drawings, and finish systems when the quote is millwork, not case goods. https://www.awinet.org/
- ANSI/BIFMA furniture safety and performance standards — commercial seating and tables. Relevant to healthcare / hospitality RFQs, not a consumer warranty stamp.

## Materials and finish (adjacent; do not convert to "non-toxic")

- EPA TSCA Title VI / composite wood formaldehyde — https://www.epa.gov/formaldehyde/formaldehyde-emission-standards-composite-wood-products
- California Air Resources Board (CARB) ATCM for composite wood — still the language many mills print on plywood stamps.
- Finish VOC: cite the product SDS and the named rule (SCAQMD, OTC, etc.). Do not write "no VOC" as a shop slogan.

## Freight

- Bill of lading as the contract of carriage. NMFC classification for crated furniture. White-glove vs threshold vs dock is a service description, not a statute.
- Carrier inspection windows and "concealed damage" language live on the BOL and the carrier rules, not on a blog.
