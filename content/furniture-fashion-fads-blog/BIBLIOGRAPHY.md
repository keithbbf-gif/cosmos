# Bibliography

Sources used in the drafts, plus gaps marked `[CITE NEEDED]` in the copy. This is a working list for the editor, not a bluff of completeness. Do not add circulation figures, market-share percentages, or influencer quotations unless a page is in hand.

## Designers, shops, objects

- Charles and Ray Eames, lounge chair and ottoman, Herman Miller, 1956. Company and museum records (MoMA; Herman Miller archives).
- Hans J. Wegner, CH24 Wishbone / “The Chair,” Carl Hansen & Søn, 1949.
- Florence Knoll, Knoll Planning Unit interiors; Eero Saarinen, pedestal / Tulip group, Knoll.
- Ettore Sottsass and the Memphis group: meeting at Sottsass’s Milan apartment, 11 December 1980; first collection at Arc ’74, Milan, mid-September 1981. Domus, “Memphis Design Movement,” gives 18 September 1981 and 55 objects (Carlton, Casablanca, Bedin’s Super Lamp). Memphis Milano’s own history page gives 19 September 1981. Drafts note the one-day split.
- Karl Lagerfeld on furnishing a Monte Carlo apartment in Memphis: quoted in later press as calling it “love at first sight” and “the Art Deco of the ’80s” (Artnet News recap of a 1983 German-magazine remark). Treat as secondary until the 1983 page is pulled.
- Dorothy Draper: Greenbrier redecoration, late 1940s; The Tides and other commissions — confirm each building against Draper’s published work before captioning. `[CITE NEEDED]` where a draft flags a specific commission date.
- William “Billy” Haines, Hollywood furniture and interiors, 1930s–60s.
- Charles Hollis Jones, Lucite furniture, Los Angeles, 1960s–70s.
- Philippe Starck, Louis Ghost chair, Kartell, 2002.
- Zanotta Sacco, designed by Piero Gatti, Cesare Paolini, Franco Teodoro, 1968.
- Eileen Gray, Bibendum chair (bouclé), 1920s — museum collections.
- Thos. Moser: Tom and Mary Moser, shop in New Gloucester, Maine, grange, 1972; Continuous Arm Chair designed 1977; move to Auburn, 1987. Company timeline (thosmoser.com/timeline) claims hand-signing from 1977. Hartford Courant, 27 April 2007, on the 35th year and first fully upholstered line.
- Gustav Stickley, *The Craftsman*, vol. 1 no. 1, October 1901 (Wisconsin / Library of Congress scans). Run of the magazine 1901–1916. United Crafts / Craftsman Workshops, Eastwood, N.Y. Stickley’s dislike of the sales word “Mission” is consistent in secondary literature; pull his wording before treating a quote as exact `[CITE NEEDED]`.
- L. & J.G. Stickley, Fayetteville, N.Y., 1905; successor operations, Manlius, N.Y. Stickley Brothers, Grand Rapids, 1891. Stickley Museum history pages.

## Houses and rooms

- Miller House and Garden, Columbus, Indiana: Eero Saarinen, Alexander Girard interiors, Dan Kiley landscape; commissioned early 1950s, completed 1957. Conversation pit documented in Newfields / IMA materials and in the house Wikipedia compilation of published measurements (~15 ft square). Now a Newfields site. Saarinen’s later unease that the pit had become a cliché: `[CITE NEEDED]` before treating as his words.
- Balthazar Korab photographs of Miller House: Library of Congress, Korab Collection.

## Retail and catalogs

- Design Within Reach: Rob Forbes, founded 1999 (Forbes has described the 1998 apartment problem). Washington *Post*, “A Catalogue Comes to Town,” 3 June 2004. Stanford GSB profile of Forbes. Herman Miller acquisition of DWR, 2015 (DWR “About” page).
- *Dwell*, first issue October 2000; Lara Hedberg Deam, founder; Karrie Jacobs, “The Fruit Bowl Manifesto.” US Modernist scan of the debut issue; *Dwell* 20th- and 25th-anniversary retrospectives.
- IKEA: Ingvar Kamprad, 1943. First U.S. store, Plymouth Meeting, Pennsylvania, 12 June 1985. Lisa Belkin, “Swedish Chain to Open American Store,” *New York Times*, 23 May 1985. “IKEA Gambles On America’s Furniture Taste,” Washington *Post*, 31 March 1986 (solid pine vs lacquered particleboard prices).
- Restoration Hardware / RH: Stephen Gordon, Eureka, California, 1979; later Corte Madera; Gary Friedman era; rename to RH. Cloud sofa: *Architectural Digest* on the 2015 launch; Business of Home interview with Timothy Oulton on the RH partnership and Cloud development.
- Rachel Ashwell / Shabby Chic: first shop, Santa Monica, 1989 (company “Our Story”; 30th-anniversary post, 2019). Los Angeles *Times*, “Turning Shabby Ick Into Shabby Chic,” 19 October 1996 (Malibu rental 1988; book). *Shabby Chic*, Harper, 1996 (ISBN 0-06-098204-7).
- *Fixer Upper*, HGTV: Chip and Joanna Gaines, Waco, Texas; pilot 23 May 2013; series through 3 April 2018.
- Pottery Barn: East Village origins commonly dated 1949; Williams-Sonoma acquisition commonly dated 1986. `[CITE NEEDED]` on both before treating as hard.
- West Elm: Williams-Sonoma launch, 2002.
- Rooms To Go: Jeffrey Seaman, 1991, Florida. First-store city and original tagline: `[CITE NEEDED]`.
- Ethan Allen: Theodore Baumritter and Nathan Ancell; early-American line; Beecher Falls, Vermont, factory. Founding year often given as 1932; factory date 1935 vs 1936 left `[CITE NEEDED]` in the draft.
- Wayfair: 2002 (company history).
- High Point Market / Southern Furniture Exposition Building: High Point Museum and market-authority histories. Attendance figures omitted on purpose.
- Pierre Deux: Pierre Moulin and Pierre LeVec (not an invented third founder). Los Angeles *Times* 1990 and *New York Times* 1999 coverage. Founding year `[CITE NEEDED]` if printed as a hard date.
- Brown Jordan: Robert Brown and Hubert Jordan, Pasadena, 1945; Morning Glory iron at Bullock’s Wilshire, 1945; Leisure aluminum and vinyl lace, 1948; Tamiami, Hall Bradley. *Furniture Today* 70th-anniversary piece.

## Bradley Brand / bbfur (soft context only)

- Product listings (Amazon and others) for Bradley Brand Furniture LLC, Warren, Arkansas: solid oak / hardwood islands and case pieces; company copy says no particleboard, MDF, paper, or plastic; “Bradley Lumber Company was founded in 1902 in Warren, Arkansas” and claims a former rank as a large flooring mill. Treat 1902, the mill rank, and the material ban as **company copy** until a primary record (charter, directory, mill history) is attached `[CITE NEEDED]`.
- Contractor directory listing: Bradley Brand Furniture, LLC, 501 Pennington St., Warren, AR; year established on that listing 2008 — a legal-entity date, not a mill date. Do not collapse the two.
- Optional later image source: BBF catalog pages and old sales PPT decks in house archives. Not in this pack. Do not caption them as if they were.
