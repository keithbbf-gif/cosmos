# Index

Forty-two magazine drafts on how American rooms get a year attached to them — taste cycles, status claims, the retail picture that arrives before the chair, and the hangover object that still works after the picture is embarrassing.

Bradley Brand Furniture (bbfur) appears only as a quiet contrast: hardwood that can be refinished versus a demo-room costume that dates with the stain. No sales close. Company history stays labeled as company copy until a primary record is attached.

Read in order for the arc. Jump by object if you are in a specific hangover.

## Arc

1. **Thesis** — 01, 14, 15. How a year attaches, who prints the picture, what still works.
2. **Decades** — 02 through 06, plus 16–19. Boom, hangover, the next costume.
3. **Materials and color** — 08, 09, 20, 21, 38–40, 42. The substrate under the fashion.
4. **Types** — 10, 11, 22–30. Sofas, patios, beds, cabinets, islands, the wall that held a television.
5. **Retail machines** — 31–37. High Point, brand houses, IKEA paper, the financed room, the vintage ticker.
6. **Marketing voices** — 07, 12, 41. Quiet luxury, wellness, the hotel bedroom.
7. **The shops that do not look up** — 13. Moser, Stickley, and the hardwood habit beside them.
