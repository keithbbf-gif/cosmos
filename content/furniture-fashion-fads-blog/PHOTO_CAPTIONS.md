# Photo captions

Period rooms, ads, catalog pages. Prefer public-domain and museum collections first. License trade ads and manufacturer brochures before publish. Do not download random Pinterest interiors and pretend they are cleared.

## Preferred wells

- Library of Congress (Gottscho-Schleisner interiors; Korab Collection for Miller House).
- Smithsonian / Cooper Hewitt object records.
- Metropolitan Museum of Art (open-access object photography).
- Newfields / Indianapolis Museum of Art — Miller House documentation.
- US Modernist / museum scans of *Dwell* and period magazines for research; rights separately.
- IKEA Museum catalog covers; Herman Miller / Vitra / Knoll archives (license).
- High Point Museum; company archives (Thomasville, Ethan Allen, Brown Jordan) by request.

## Optional own-source later

Bradley Brand Furniture catalog pages and old sales PPT decks may be captioned once an editor pulls them from house archives. They are **not** in this pack. Do not write captions as if the scans exist. When they arrive, use IDs `FFF-BBF-01` onward and mark `rights: BBF own-source`.
