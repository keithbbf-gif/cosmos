# American Dining Room Furniture, 1700–Now

A series for buyers, designers, restorers, and anyone who has inherited a table that does not match the room. One form, shop, region, or hour per article. History is footed to named work. Gaps are marked `[CITE NEEDED]`.

Bradley Brand works Arkansas hardwoods. These drafts teach how American dining furniture was made and used. They do not sell chairs.

## How to read the series

Start with the room. Then take a table you already own, or walk the shops in order: colonial ports, Federal sideboards, factory chairs, the open plan. Labor chapters sit inside the century they belong to, not as an appendix.
