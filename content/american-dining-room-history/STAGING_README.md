# Staged for BBF SEO — american-dining-room-history

**Status:** staged only. Every article is `status: draft`. Do not set WordPress publish dates. Do not paste these files onto a live domain until an editor pass and a photograph plan exist.

## What this pack is

Forty-four magazine essays on American dining-room furniture from about 1700 to the present. They are the history-pillar research spine for Bradley Brand / BBF. They teach forms, shops, woods, labor, and rooms. They do not sell SKUs.

## Files

- `drafts/<slug>.md` — canonical articles
- `writer-slugs.json` — 44 slugs, reading order
- `INDEX.md` — reading order + publish queue
- `STYLE_GUIDE.md` — voice, citations, banned phrasing
- `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` — series sources
- `PHOTO_CAPTIONS.md` — plates to shoot or license
- `RIGHTS.md` — verified PD/CC sources (no AI)
- `staged/images/` — downloaded Met Open Access and Commons plates
- `staged/EMBEDS.md` — reusable `<figure>` / SEO caption blocks
- `staged/image_sources.yaml` — slug → file → license registry
- `MANIFEST.md` — word counts

## SEO rules

- One slug owns one `primary_keyword`.
- `meta_description` is a search snippet, not a slogan.
- `seo_intent` is informational unless a later editor changes it.
- Cross-link siblings. Do not cannibalize `the-room-is-invented` with a second “history of the dining room” lede.
- History hub month (pillar map): 2027-04. Factory-vs-one-off: 2027-08. Supporting slugs wait.
- No therapy, supplements, AI, or COSMOS copy.

## Brand

Bradley Brand may appear where Arkansas or southern hardwoods are the subject (`arkansas-southern-hardwood-dining`, and a sentence in vernacular or woods hours). No product names. No commission CTA in the body.
