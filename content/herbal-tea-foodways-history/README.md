# Herbal tea as foodways history

Forty-five **draft essays** on cups that are not *Camellia sinensis* — and
on the English grocery phrase that keeps pretending they are.

This is educational foodways and cultural history. It is **not** a
protocol, a shop, a materia medica, or a list of indications.

## Read this first

1. [`GUARDRAILS.md`](GUARDRAILS.md) — what a sentence may and may not do.
2. [`CITATIONS.md`](CITATIONS.md) — named public documents and the honesty rule.
3. This index — one-line theses, in stage order.
4. Any single draft. They stand alone. They do not stack into a treatment.

If you only open one file besides the guardrails, open
[`stage-01-how-to-read/01-what-this-folder-refuses.md`](stage-01-how-to-read/01-what-this-folder-refuses.md).

## Voice and status

Human essay voice. `status: draft` and `voice_check: writer-pass` on every
essay file. The series is **staged** (seven argument stages) and
**git-staged** as a proposal, not published as canon and not wired into
COSMOS Core.
