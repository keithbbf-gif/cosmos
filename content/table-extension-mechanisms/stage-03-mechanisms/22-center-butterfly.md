# Pivot, unfold, key

A folding center leaf is a pack that has to become a
plane.

Two boards, hinged on their meeting edge, usually
with barrel hinges so the knuckle does not make a
ridge. The pack lives under the split top on a
shelf or on pins. You pull the halves apart —
equalizers help — reach in, lift the upper half,
and rotate the pack up around a pair of pivot
pins. It unfolds as it rises. At the top of the
travel it lies on the aprons or on ledges. You
push the halves in. Keys enter mortises. The
table is longer.

Fine Woodworking's versions are the ones I have
in my head. Three locating keys across the width.
The key near the leaf's own hinge sits on the
lower half so it clears the pivot block when the
pack is moving. You cut those mortises late,
after the motion is true, because the last
adjustments are the only adjustments that matter.

Miss any one of those jobs and the thing walks.

If the pivots are shy, the unfolding pack hits
the underside of the top. If they are proud, the
open leaf sits high. If the shelf sags, the pack
starts the day at a bad angle and the lift
becomes a wrench. If the keys are early work,
they fight the motion you later tuned. If you
omit keys because the catalog did, the leaf is a
raft between two docks.

The hinge line is a third seam. Rectangles have
two seams when you add one leaf; this motion adds
a seam in the leaf itself. You can hide it with
careful grain. You cannot hide it from a
straightedge if the hinges pull. Cheap hinges
will.

I do not specify this mechanism for a table that
needs two extra leaves. The undercarriage becomes
a suitcase. One folding leaf of 18 to 24 inches
is a guest or two. That is a real Tuesday-to-
holiday delta for a lot of rooms. Greed is how
these get bad: two packs, extra pivots, a top
that is more hardware than wood.

Open it once a month even if no one is coming.
Wood that only moves at Thanksgiving will tell
you so. A little wax on the slides, a check that
the pack still clears, a look at the keys. Five
minutes. Less than the argument about who gets
the crack.
