# What a good extension is

An extension is not a larger
table. It is a table that can
change size and remain a table.

I use this as an acceptance
test. If a design fails it, I
do not care how the photograph
looks.

**1. Two honest sizes.** Closed
sitters and open sitters, counted
with elbows, written down. The
closed object is handsome enough
to live with. The open object
reaches the holiday number
without inventing seats over
gates or on top of seams that
rock.

**2. One plane.** A straightedge
across every joint, closed and
open, after the locks are
thrown. Height match inside a
fingernail. No grin. No valley
that gathers peas. February
counts. A shop in June does not
get to grade this alone.

**3. A motion a real person can
perform in the room that exists.**
One pair of hands if the
household is one pair of hands.
Clearance at the ends. No
lecture. No Allen key from a
lost drawer. If it needs two
people, the design should say
so on the underside, not in a
manual that will not be there.

**4. A home for the extra
surface.** Undercarriage, fitted
case, or a press in the same
climate. Loose boards without a
home are a second piece of
furniture you have refused to
admit. Lost leaves are a
known scar. Do not re-specify
the scar and call it
tradition.

**5. A floor story that matches
the span.** Legs, pedestals,
trestles, a fifth foot, a sag
bar. The roast has to sit on
something that is not optimism.
Knee space is part of the
story. Stealing a sitter to
save a leaf is a choice you
name, not a surprise you serve.

**6. A skirt or a language that
survives being open.** Either
grow the apron or stop
pretending you have a box.
Do not lantern the slides
unless the slides were
designed to be seen.

**7. Materials on one contract.**
Solid with solid, veneer with
veneer, same room, same finish
schedule. No leaf from a
different weather. No refinish
that orphans the closet.

**8. Failures that announce
themselves.** Bind, drag, a
catch you can feel. Not a
silent motor that dies when
the hall is full. Service
without removing the top
unless the top was meant to
come off.

**9. Permission to refuse.**
Some sculptures should not
extend. A second table is
legal. A card table at the
end is legal. A no is a
mechanism.

If this sounds like a lot, it
is because dinner is a lot.
The leaf is still just a
board. The mechanism is
whether that board can join
the meal without making the
meal about the board.

I am not done. A wish that
passes this test can be built.
A build that fails it is not
an extension. It is a larger
surface and a smaller trust.
The series stops here because
the law is the point of the
history. Everything before
this draft was evidence.
