In December 2022 and January 2023, school districts and a few universities banned ChatGPT on the network. New York City's public-school block (announced in early January 2023, later walked back toward "teach it") was the emblem. Teachers had spent the winter break grading essays that were fluent and empty. Students had spent it discovering that InstructGPT's helpfulness (March 2022) plus a chat box (30 November) was a homework machine.

Detectors arrived: GPTZero, Turnitin's AI score, OpenAI's own classifier (launched 31 January 2023, shut down 20 July 2023 as unreliable). Liang et al. (2023) and a pile of follow-ups documented the failure mode everyone felt: non-native English writers got flagged more; a student who edited a draft could dodge; a teacher who trusted a percentage was doing numerology. OpenAI's shutdown post is the rare vendor sentence worth framing. They could not defend the tool.

## What the bans got right and wrong

Right: the old take-home essay, as a *measurement* of writing, broke. If the artifact can be sampled in twenty seconds, it is no longer a measurement. That is a psychometrics fact, not a moral one.

Wrong: the network ban as a policy. Students had phones. The ban taught them to hide the tool, not to critique it. Schools that kept the ban into 2024 were mostly protecting a feeling.

The grown-up responses, visible by 2024 in places that had time to think: in-class writing, oral defense, process artifacts (notes, versions), assignments that use the model *and* require a critique of its errors. None of these are new pedagogy. They are older pedagogy, reinstated because the cheap artifact died.

## What a defensible assignment looks like now

A prompt the student must *improve*, with a log of the model's first wrong answer. An oral five minutes on the paper. A dataset the student collected. A local fact the crawl does not have. A critique of a fabricated citation you planted. None of this requires a detector. It requires a teacher who has time, which is the actual scarce object, and a union contract that does not pretend the time appeared because a chatbot did.

Universities that sold "AI across the curriculum" in 2024 without changing assessment sold a brochure. The campuses that changed assessment first, tools second, are the ones whose transcripts still mean something.

## Tutors, the other product

Khanmigo (Khan Academy + GPT-4, announced March 2023) and a wave of "Socratic tutor" wrappers tried to make the model ask questions instead of emit the essay. Sometimes it worked. Often the student asked once and got the answer anyway. The defect is the same helpfulness reward as the rest of RLHF. A tutor that will not give the answer is a worse Arena model and a better classroom model. You cannot optimize both with one checkpoint.

Accessibility gains were real: explanation in a second language, a first draft for a student with a disability who then edits, a worked example at 11 p.m. when no human is paid to be there. Those gains do not cancel the measurement break. They sit next to it.

## The detector hangover, in numbers you should not use

OpenAI's classifier page (July 2023) said the tool would not work well enough to keep live. That is the primary source. Third-party tools kept selling percentages to districts. A 2023–24 literature (Liang et al. and follow-ups) showed higher false-positive rates on non-native writing. If a district still uses a percentage as a disciplinary trigger in 2026, they are buying a product the originating lab already abandoned. Ask for the false-positive table on *your* student population. If the vendor will not give it, you have the answer.

International baccalaureate and some exam boards moved toward in-person components and oral. US state testing mostly did not. The mismatch is why a transcript from 2025 is harder to read than one from 2019. That is not the model's fault. It is an assessment-governance lag.

## What not to ship at a school

A detector score as a disciplinary number. A "humanizer" as a product (that market is a confession). A vendor that will not sign a student-data agreement. A voice tutor that records a child's house (see the voice draft).

A 2026 "AI literacy" unit that is a tool demo without a fabrication unit. If the student cannot mark a hallucinated citation, the unit failed.

## Opinion

ChatGPT did not ruin school. It retired a measurement that was already a take-home honor system. Detectors tried to resurrect the honor system with a false number. That was the actual scandal.

The assignment is the product. Change the assignment. Leave the percentage off the student's record.
