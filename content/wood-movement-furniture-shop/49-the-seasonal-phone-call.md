# The seasonal phone call

It comes in January or it
comes in August. They name
the noun they can point at.
You have about a minute
before the call becomes a
verdict on the shop. Do not
spend that minute on the
word humidity.

## The first questions

When did you first see it.
Was it there in the other
season. Can you send a
picture in raking light,
and one of the back or the
underside. Is there a vent
or a stove in the frame.
Did anyone add screws,
wrap it, store it, move it
to a porch.

You are sorting costumes:
live width locked, live
width free but house
extreme, glue that never
bit, a warp that is not
seasonal. The second
picture does most of the
sort.

## The first sentences

Name the part. "The sides
grew." "The field shrank
and the breadboard did
not." "The top is still
fastened tight at the
ends." Then the next
step: I can come free
those screws. We can wait
until October and look
again. That joint needs
to come back to the shop.

If the card already named
the winter stripe, you
can point at the card
without sounding like a
shrug. If the card is
silent, you own that
silence and you still
do the sort.

## What not to send

A Handbook table. A
paragraph about EMC. A
blame sentence about
their heat. A quote for
filler on a seasonal
line. Anger. A promise
that it will "go back"
if you have not seen
the back.

## When to go in person

A dining top. A kitchen
of drawers. Anything
with a split that might
be a lock. A client who
is already writing a
review in their head.
Take a driver, a
hygrometer you can
leave, a plane if you
fitted a drawer in a
dry week and you know
it. Do not take a
syringe of putty as
the plan.

## When the house is the job

A logger in the hall
for a week, with
permission, is cheaper
than an argument. If
the hall sits at 20
percent, you are in a
care conversation and
maybe a humidifier
conversation, not a
refund for oak. If the
hall sits at 50 and
the top split, you are
in a fastener
conversation. Say which
one you are in.

## After the call

Write the ticket: what
they said, what you
saw, what you freed,
what you refused to
fill. The next season
will call again if the
lock is still there.
The book is how you
do not do the same
shrug twice.

The seasonal phone
call is the series in
one conversation. Piece
first. Back second.
Year third. Next step
that is a tool, not a
noun from a pamphlet.
You can be kind
without being fog.
Kind is the driver.
Fog is the lecture.

## A call that was a hinge

They said February
humidity had twisted the
door. The second picture
showed a hinge mortise
cut after finish, raw,
screws in a proud lip.
The house RH was
ordinary. We sealed the
mortise, longer screws
into long grain, and
the latch came home.
If I had started with
EMC I would have
missed the mortise and
sent a pamphlet. The
sort is why the second
picture exists.

## A call that was the hall

Same month, different
house. Breadboard proud,
field free, underside
travelers in place,
logger in the hall at
22 percent for a week.
We left a hygrometer,
named the proud on the
card again, and did
not sand the step. They
were angry until July
when the step went shy
and they sent a kinder
picture. The book had
both pictures. That is
how a call becomes a
year instead of a
verdict.

Ask for the second
picture before you
offer a date. The
first picture is the
feeling. The second
is the fasteners.
You need both. You
do not argue with a
feeling.
