# A cut changes the queue. Muscle is no longer first.

Energy deficit is the most important protein modifier of the 2020s and the least glamorous. Gwin, Pasiakos, Churchward-Venne and the USARIEM line spent the decade showing that the textbook 20–25 g whey feeding was written in peacetime.

## The papers, without the press release

Gwin et al., *Nutrients* 2020 (PMC7469068): during energy deficit, a larger share of dietary amino acids is pulled into whole-body and energy-yielding jobs. Muscle protein synthesis is not the only customer. Field studies that handed soldiers a standard whey serving during severe deficit did not look like the energy-balance lab.

Gwin et al., *Clinical Nutrition* 2020/21: high vs standard EAA intakes during deficit — whole-body turnover moves; the muscle biopsy is harder to impress.

Gwin et al., *JISSN* 2021: 10 adults, three 5-day −30% periods. After a load-carriage plus lifting session, EAA-enriched whey (34.7 g protein, 24 g EAA) beat iso-nitrogenous whey and a mixed meal for whole-body net balance. Mixed-muscle MPS: no difference.

Gwin et al., *MSSE* 2024: add the extra energy as more EAA, not as carbohydrate, on top of EAA-whey, and post-exercise MPS during underfeeding looked more like energy-balance MPS. That is a specific, high-EAA, not-very-food-like drink.

ISSN 2023’s EAA stand independently says EAA needs rise in a caloric deficit. Hector/Phillips and Longland sit underneath as the body-composition practice: keep protein high (often **2.0–2.4 g/kg**) and keep lifting.

## What I do on a cut that is still a sport

- Raise protein toward **2.0–2.4 g/kg** before I raise anyone’s hopes.
- Do not cut the lifting.
- Use food first; use an EAA-enriched intact protein when appetite is gone and the deficit is real — that is the 2021 paper, not a lifestyle brand.
- Watch the whole person. If the “cut” is actually low energy availability, we are in the next draft.

A deficit is not a disease. Starving a training athlete until systems fall over is how you wander toward one. Protein is a holding action. Energy is the plot.
