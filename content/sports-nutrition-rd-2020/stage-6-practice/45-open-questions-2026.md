# What the labs are still arguing about in 2026

A series that pretends the science is finished is a catalog. Here is the unfinished list I would hand a doctoral student, or a coach who wants to know why two smart people just contradicted each other on a podcast.

## 1. Does a 100 g meal change a training block?

Trommelen 2023 changed the acute story. Witard and Mettler argued about efficiency. Nobody has run the boring 12-week trial: same daily protein, same lifting, even meals vs one or two large ones, trained men *and* women, hypertrophy and strength as the endpoints. Until that exists, distribution is a habit.

## 2. Trained women, plant patterns, 12 weeks

Hevia-Larraín is untrained men and soy. Monteyne’s older-adult vegan day is three days of tracers. We need a trained female vegan-vs-omnivore training study at matched protein. Moore, Sygo and Morton already asked for sport-like, phase-controlled work. Still waiting.

## 3. Deficit: whole-body vs muscle

Gwin keeps showing whole-body NET moving when the biopsy does not, or MPS holding only with large EAA loads. What does that mean for a 16-week cut in a weight-class athlete? We are inferring from five-day studies.

## 4. Cold: can you time your way out?

Fuchs 2020 and 2025 say cooling after lifting taxes amino-acid use. We do not have a clean “eat, wait two hours, then cool” crossover that rescues the incorporation. Staffs are guessing.

## 5. Collagen endpoints that are not PINP

Lee 2024 moved a blood marker. We still want tendon mechanical and imaging outcomes in athletes, with a complete-protein control, before anyone should sound sure. No treatment language in the meantime.

## 6. Fermented and algal proteins

Mycoprotein and mealworm did the biopsy work. Dairy-identical fermentation has not, in the public literature I will cite here. Candidate, not proven.

## 7. Masters, chronic pre-sleep

Acute overnight 40 g works. Reis 2020 already said the chronic older-adult training add-on is thin. Still thin.

## 8. The next consensus rewrite

ISSN’s protein stand is 2017. The EAA stand is 2023. UEFA is 2021. IOC REDs is 2023. The protein stand that swallows Trommelen, Pinckaers, Gwin, and Fuchs has not been signed. When it is, this series will look dated in the way a good draft should: overtaken by a letterhead that finally caught up.

Until then, the human voice is: **eat enough protein, train, fuel the day, do not invent a disease claim, and stay suspicious of anyone who sounds finished.**
