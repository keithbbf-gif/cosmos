# Free-form EAAs are a tool. Dinner is still dinner.

Essential amino acids in a sachet rise in the blood like they have somewhere to be. Intact protein has to be chewed, emptied, and digested. That difference is real. The 2023 ISSN EAA position stand is the document that finally treated it as its own topic instead of a footnote in the 2017 protein stand.

## What the 2023 stand actually claims

Ferrando, Wolfe, Hirsch and colleagues put a long list on the table. The lines I keep:

- Free-form EAAs raise peripheral EAA concentrations quickly and stimulate MPS.
- At rest, small doses (they cite **1.5–3.0 g**) can move MPS; the rest-day curve looks like it plateaus around **15–18 g**.
- You do not need the non-essentials in the sachet for that MPS signal.
- Free-form EAAs can stimulate MPS more than the same grams of intact protein — because the sachet *is* the essentials, with no extra nitrogen ballast.
- Repeating EAA doses through the day did not, in the work they review, blunt the next meal.
- Energy deficit raises the EAA problem. Muscle is not first in line when the whole body is short.

That last point is the bridge into Gwin’s military-style deficit trials, which get their own draft. The stand is not a hypertrophy cookbook. It is a kinetics and MPS document.

## Where people get cute

I have watched athletes replace lunch with 6 g of leucine-heavy crystals because a podcast said “protein is just EAAs.” That is not what the stand says. A food meal brings energy, the non-essentials your body will otherwise make from the essentials, micronutrients, and a slower tail of amino acids. Trommelen’s 100 g milk-protein day is a slow-tail story. A sachet is a spike.

Gwin 2021 is the practical hybrid: **EAA-enriched whey** after exercise in a 30% energy deficit beat iso-nitrogenous whey and a mixed meal for *whole-body* net balance. Mixed-muscle MPS did not differ between those three treatments. Read that twice. The whole body can look better on paper while the biopsy does not. If your goal is “look after the whole athlete in a cut,” the enriched whey is interesting. If your goal is “this will grow the vastus,” the paper did not show that.

Gwin 2024 pushed it further: adding *more energy as EAA*, not as carbohydrate, to an EAA-whey drink helped keep post-exercise MPS up during underfeeding. That is a specific, expensive, not-very-tasty protocol in a lab. It is not a grocery list.

## How I would use EAAs without turning into a catalog

- Travel day, no refrigeration, session in 40 minutes: a full EAA dose (think the stand’s mid-to-upper range, not a 2 g tease) is better than nothing.
- Hard deficit, appetite is gone, protein foods are bouncing: an EAA-enriched intact protein is the 2021–2024 evidence-shaped option. Still try to eat food.
- Normal training week, normal kitchen: I buy food. The 2017 protein stand still starts there.

EAAs are not a disease treatment and they are not a moral upgrade on chicken. They are a fast way to put essentials in the blood. Use them when speed or volume of food is the constraint.
