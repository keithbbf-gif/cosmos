# Masters athletes need the meal to be bigger, not mythical

“Anabolic resistance” is a real laboratory phrase. It is also a phrase that gets used to sell 40 g shakes to anyone over 40. The 2020s data are more specific than the sales deck.

## What the trials in older people actually showed

Oikawa 2020: healthy women, mean age 69, 30 g whey vs 30 g collagen twice a day for six days, unilateral lifting. Whey raised acute MPS at rest and after exercise, and raised integrated (deuterated-water) MPS over the week. Collagen did not do that job. Quality, not a birthday, was the split.

Nunes 2022’s subgroup: extra protein helped lean mass in people ≥65 when intake landed in about **1.2–1.59 g/kg** with resistance exercise. Younger adults needed the higher band (≥1.6) to show the same kind of increment. That is a meta-analytic pattern, not a prescription to keep a 70-year-old rower at 1.3 forever. It is a reminder that the *first* problem in many older cohorts is that they were eating close to the RDA.

Kouw’s pre-sleep work in older men: 40 g of casein before bed raised overnight myofibrillar synthesis; 20 g did not. Reis 2020 noted that chronic pre-sleep plus training looks less proven in older men than in younger ones. Acute overnight feeding still has a dose.

Monteyne 2021 took healthy older adults (~66 y) and fed 1.8 g/kg for three days, either mostly animal or fully vegan with mycoprotein doing a lot of the work. Daily myofibrillar synthesis, rested and exercised, did not differ by pattern. Age did not make “vegan” a failed label when the grams were high and the food was serious.

## How I coach a 52-year-old who still races

- I do not diagnose sarcopenia in a person who is still lining up. That word is a clinical category. It is not a nickname.
- I do push **per-meal protein up** — 30–40 g, not 15 g — because the older-adult literature keeps showing that small feedings under-trigger.
- I keep daily intake in the athletic band, often **1.6–2.2 g/kg**, especially if they lift. Tagawa 2020 said resistance training is what keeps the lean-mass return from shrinking as intake climbs.
- I care about teeth, cooking, and breakfast more than I care about a new isolate brand.

Masters sport is training plus recovery plus a calendar. Protein is one recovery tool. It is not a youth serum.
