# Match week is a carbohydrate story with a protein bass line

Football, rugby, lacrosse, basketball: the week is periodized. MD-1 looks like a buffet. MD+1 looks like a hangover and a plane. Protein should be the thing that does not yo-yo while carbohydrate does.

## UEFA 2021, read as a protein document

Collins and the expert group wrote nine topics. Protein shows up as remodeling, not as a match-day ergogenic.

- Daily target they were willing to put in ink: **1.6–2.2 g/kg**, with EPL recalls already at 2–2.5.
- Meal pattern: three to four feedings at about **0.4 g/kg**.
- Recovery from play: 20–25 g high-quality protein every 3–4 hours. They note that protein does not acutely restore muscle *function* even when it raises synthesis. Do not expect a shake to fix a 90-minute drop in sprint quality.
- Pre-sleep: players under-eat it (≈0.1 g/kg); they want ≈0.4–0.5 g/kg.
- Rehab and energy restriction: push protein up, not down.
- Alcohol after the match: they cite evidence that large intakes can interfere with myofibrillar synthesis. That is a recovery note, not a sermon I need to enlarge.

Congested fixtures are a carbohydrate-and-sleep crisis first. Protein still needs a seat at MD+1 breakfast when the hotel only has pastry.

## What I actually do with a squad

I do not change protein on MD-2 because the GPS said 4 km of high speed. I change the starch. I check that the protein foods survived the travel menu. I put a 30 g option in the locker-room fridge for the people who cannot face steak at 11 p.m. I do not hand out BCAAs as a match-day identity (see the BCAA draft).

MD+1 is where the plan usually becomes pastry and coffee. UEFA already said glycogen resynthesis can be behind after an evening game because players miss carbohydrate targets. They also said protein does not give you your sprint back that morning. So I write a breakfast that is both: yogurt and oats, eggs and toast, a shake and a banana if that is all they will do. The protein is the bass line so the remodeling day is not empty. The carbohydrate is the headline.

If the squad is mixed vegan and omnivore, I use Hevia-Larraín and Pinckaers as the calm voice: match the grams, watch the leucine, stop the mythology. If someone wants to ice the legs at 1 a.m. after a night match, that is a recovery-staff call. If they also want a hypertrophy block on MD+2, I put Fuchs 2020 on the table and let them choose the fight.

Congested fixtures (Thursday–Sunday) are not a new protein target. They are a test of whether the 0.4 g/kg meals still exist when the bus is the dining room. Trommelen 2023 is my permission to make the post-match meal large. It is not permission to skip Saturday breakfast.

## What this is not

Match-week protein is fueling and remodeling, not illness prevention, injury treatment, or a substitute for medical staff when a player is unwell.
