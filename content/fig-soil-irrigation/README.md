# Fig soil, drainage, mulch, irrigation, container media

Staged draft set. Not published copy. Not a mix-bag cult. Not a promise that
your clay will behave.

These pieces are for a person who already has a fig, or is about to put one in
a hole, and wants the dirt and the hose told in a human voice. The center of
gravity is **USDA Zone 8a** in the humid South — South Arkansas dirt, heat,
thunderstorms, and a collection that lives mostly in **3- and 5-gallon** pots
with a smaller in-ground set. Zone 7 and Zone 9 sit in the room the whole time,
because 8a people borrow methods from both and get hurt when they borrow the
wrong one.

This pack **unpacks** the soil and water bullets on
[Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/)
and the Promix / field-capacity sentences on
[An Introduction to Figs](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/).
It does not rewrite those pages. It does not invent a new A/B trial.

## What “staged” means here

Editorial status on every draft is `status: staged` — ready for a human editor,
not live, not SEO-published.

The *work* is also staged. Read in this order if you are actually trying to keep
roots alive:

| Stage | Folder tag | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `read` | name the dirt | Texture, pH, salt, tests, compaction, what the top two inches say |
| `fix` | change the hole | Berms, raised beds, the bathtub effect, hardpan, first two summers |
| `cover` | put a blanket on | Mulch kinds, the trunk, fabric, grass, rust litter, the irrigation clock |
| `water` | run the hose | Establishment, swell, split, rust, drip vs sprinklers, freeze water |
| `pot` | what is in the can | Promix, perlite, coir, moisture-control bags, lift test, saucers |

A manifest lives in `_manifest.toml`. Drafts live in `drafts/`.

## Voice

First person, a yard in 8a, neighbors in 7 and 9. PapaFig. Specific dirt and
hose talk. Opinions with reasons. Catalog language and “perfect soil mix”
recipes get pushed off the table. If a number is already on FigRoots (Promix HP,
~70% field capacity, pot-up before June, 6–8 hours of sun), the draft cites
that page. It does not invent a second trial.

## Do not treat this as

- A substitute for your county extension or a soil test
- A recipe that turns your clay into loam
- Advice to dump Promix into a clay pit
- A California deficit-irrigation program for an Arkansas #3
- A fumigation manual

## Sources that informed the set (not copied)

- Texas A&M AgriLife, *Figs* (Kamas, Nesbitt, Stein; HORT-PU-025 / 2015 PDF):
  well-drained soil, sand-to-clay, root-knot on sand, shallow fibrous roots,
  plant 2–3 inches deeper than nursery, no fertilizer at planting, organic
  mulch, water before a hard freeze
- UC IPM, *Cultural Tips for Growing Fig*: lime-tolerant, sensitive to excess
  sodium and boron, plant on a ridge or mound where drainage is poor, refill
  with original soil, deep infrequent water on established trees, avoid
  overhead when you can
- UAEX / Arkansas fig culture notes: well-drained, 6–8 hours of sun, drought
  drop, freeze-to-the-ground recovery
- NC State fig culture: mulch instead of cultivating the shallow mat; late
  nitrogen makes tender wood
- LSU AgCenter Pub. 1802: old tomato / okra / tobacco ground and root-knot
- FigRoots live pages named above, plus [Outdoor](https://figroots.com/outdoor/)
  and [Fig Pops](https://figroots.com/fig-pops/)

Field practice still outruns paper. The drafts say so.
