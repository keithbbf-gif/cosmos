Open-eye figs take rain in the ostiole and then they take
yeast and a dried-fruit beetle and then they take your
appetite. TAMU wanted closed eyes for the Gulf for this
reason. [Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/)
said humid climate, tight eye, smaller fruit. That is the
variety half. The hose half is: do not bathe a softening
open eye.

When the radar is green and the fruit is coloring, I skip.
The sky paid. Adding sprinklers is how you keep the eye
wet into the night. Morning sun is the dry-off I want. Dusk
water is the opposite of that sentence.

After a souring rain I pick what I can use and I get the
rest off the tree if it has gone to wine. Leaving sour
figs is how you keep the beetles interested. That is
sanitation, not irrigation, and it still belongs in a
water week because the water week caused it.

Tight-eye names forgive a storm better. They still split.
They still drop if the roots swung. Skipping the cycle is
for them too if the hole is already a lake. The skip is
not a variety prize. It is hydraulics.

A rain barrel that filled overnight is not an order to
empty it onto the pots in the morning. The pots already
ate. Save the barrel for the dry Tuesday. We hand-water
shop pots from rain barrels in winter because the trees
are barely moist and the well can rest. Summer well, winter
barrel — that split is already in our notes. Do not invert
it because the barrel looks full and you feel industrious.

Zone 7 souring is a shorter season of the same physics.
Zone 9 souring is why LSU names and tight eyes have a
fan club. 8a souring is an August tax on open-eye dessert
names I grow anyway because I want the flavor. I pay the
tax with a skip and a pick, not with a story that the
hose would have helped.

If you cannot skip because your timer does not know the
sky, you do not have a timer. You have a leak with a
clock. Put a rain skip on it or put a hand on it. The
fruit is the sensor. The eye is open. Act like you can
see it.

A full rain barrel in the morning is not an order to
empty it onto wet pots. Save it for the dry Tuesday.
Get sour fruit off the tree so the beetles move on.
Tight-eye names still skip if the hole is a lake.
The eye is open. The timer that cannot see the sky
is a leak. Put a hand on it. The fruit is the
sensor.

Open eyes pay an August tax here. I grow a few anyway
because I want the flavor. I pay with a skip and a
pick, not with a story that another cycle would have
helped. The sky already wrote the cycle.
