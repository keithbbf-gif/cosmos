People treat compost and mulch like one pile. Compost is a
meal. Mulch is a climate. You can skip a meal. You cannot
skip July on bare clay and call it a climate.

I will put finished compost on a keeper ring in spring — a
thin layer, wide, off the trunk — and sometimes again in
fall if the tree is one I want through a winter. That is
the CenTex habit and it matches a shallow mat. Then I put
chips on top of that so the compost does not bake into a
crust or wash into the lawn. Compost naked in a South
Arkansas sun is a brick by June. Compost under chips is a
meal that stays.

I do not compost every pot. We fertigate when the trees are
moving. A #3 of compost on top of Promix is how you make a
gnat lid. A thin scatter of castings I can live with. A
dump I cannot.

Winter mulch on in-ground figs in 8a is mostly still the
same chips. I do not pile a foot of hay against a trunk
and call it a coat unless I am stuffing a cage for a young
plant, the way TAMU pictures, and I pull that hay back in
spring. The all-year mulch is the chip ring. The winter
extra is a different tool. Do not confuse them or you will
leave a soaked heap on a crown through a January thaw.

Refresh before the pottery month. In our calendar that
means looking at the ring in May, not in August when you
are already behind. Chips disappear. Hay disappears.
Leaves disappear into a slick. If I can see dirt, I am
late.

Zone 7 can lean harder on the fall compost-plus-mulch as
a little insulation if the trunk stays visible. Zone 9
can skip the fall meal if the tree is already lush and
the rust is the real fight — extra nitrogen late is
tender wood and more leaf to get sick. NC State’s late-
nitrogen warning travels. 8a I will feed a weak keeper
in spring and leave August alone.

If you only have time for one habit, mulch. The meal can
wait. The griddle cannot. I have skipped compost for a
year on a tree that was already putting a foot of wood.
I have not skipped chips on purpose and liked what I
saw. The climate is the job. The meal is a kindness.

If the tree is already putting a foot of wood and the
fruit holds, I skip the fall meal and I do not skip the
chips. Late nitrogen is tender wood. NC State said it.
August compost that is still hot is a late meal I do
not need. Spring is the meal I will pay for. July is
the blanket I will not skip. That split has saved me
from a lush September and a bitten December.
