Jack has said the potted trees run a lot of worm castings
and perlite. I have built mix from perlite we buy from an
expander, plus topsoil, castings, leaf compost, aged
chicken manure. I will not sell a secret ratio as law. I
will sell this: if water stands on top, the pot is lying.

Figs drown quieter than they dry. A wilted plant in a
light can is a drink. A wilted plant in a cinder-block
can is a swamp. Learn the difference with your hand, not
with a product name.

After a drink I want water in the saucer I am about to
dump, not a pond on the surface five minutes later. A
pond on the surface means the mix sealed, the holes
clogged, or you have a cake that will not take water in
the middle. All three are a lie. The fig believes the
lie until the roots sour.

Holes in the can are not optional. One sad hole in a
pretty ceramic is a vase. Elevate the pot so the holes
can drain. A pot flat on a plastic tray that never
empties is a pond you can see. Dump it. Mosquitoes and
sour roots like it more than the fig does.

In a shop in winter, a saucer can hide a freeze puck. I
would rather have a pot that drips on a tray you empty
than a pot that sits in a bath. Barely moist for the
winter shuffle. A wet puck at 15°F is how a #3 dies in
the roots while the stick still looks brown.

If the mix was good in May and stands water in August,
the mix collapsed. Peat and compost shrink. Roots fill.
The can is now a root ball with a skin of dirt. Pot up
or root-prune and refresh. Do not jab a stick in twice
and call it aeration. That is a performance.

Zone 7 standing water in a can is an ice cube tray.
Zone 9 standing water is a warm swamp. 8a standing
water is the August smell I can find with my eyes
closed. I have tipped those cans. I do not miss the
smell. If the top ponds, believe it. Change the mix or
change the can. Do not change the fertilizer first.
The pot already told you the truth. It stood there
and waited for you to look.

Elevate the holes. Dump the saucer. If May drained
and August ponds, the mix collapsed — pot up or
refresh, do not jab a stick twice. A pond on the
surface is a sealed cake, a clogged hole, or both.
Believe it. Change the mix or the can. Do not change
the fertilizer first. The pot stood there and waited
for you to look.

Winter shop trays get emptied too. A wet puck at
15°F kills roots while the stick stays brown. Barely
moist. Drip, dump, sleep. The lie is quieter in
January. It still kills.
