A fig that is sizing wants even moisture. Texas A&M’s
shallow, drought-sensitive mat is not a suggestion in
that week. UAEX: dry trees drop fruit. I have watched a
tree dump a crop after a proud dry spell because I
wanted to be Mediterranean. The tree was in a pot. The
pot was on gravel. The Mediterranean was a feeling.

Even is the word. Not a swamp. Not a bone. If you have
been watering like a guest all month, do not become a
hero the week the fruit softens. A binge after a drought
is how you split. A drought after a binge is how you
drop. Pick a lane and stay in it while the crop is on
the wood.

In-ground keepers in clay that drains can ride a normal
8a rain through swell if the ring is mulched and the
county is not crunchy. If the county is crunchy, I drag
the hose to the dripline and I do a deep drink *before*
the fruit goes soft, then I keep the rhythm. I do not
invent a new rhythm the night the first fig droops.

Pots do not ride the county. A #3 or #5 in our July can
need water daily in a bad week. That is not the Outdoor
page schedule for bulk starts. Hold that thought. Bulk
starts are wood we can replace, watered once or twice a
week in shade. A loaded can in fruit is a livestock
chore. Lift. Drink. Do not perform deficit.

I turn the sprinklers off, or I switch to drip, when
the fruit is softening if I can, because overhead wets
eyes and invites souring on open-eye names. Tight-eye
names forgive more. We still have a collection to walk.
I am honest about the compromise. Ten trees can have
drip at swell. Three hundred can have a skip when the
sky is already working.

Zone 7 swell can collide with a dry June and a short
season — do not lose the crop to pride. Zone 9 swell
collides with rain and rust — even water, dry leaves if
you can. 8a swell collides with thunderstorms that undo
your careful week. You cannot out-hose the sky. You can
avoid adding a binge on top of it.

The fruit will tell you. Soft, drooping, color coming:
keep the even. Hard and stalling: check water and heat
before you buy a fertilizer that says bloom. There is
no bloom booster that replaces a drink the week the
tree asked for one.

If the fruit stalls hard and the leaves are theater at
2 p.m., I check shade and lift before I check a bloom
bottle. Heat wilt is not a fertilizer deficiency. A
loaded #5 on gravel can need a daily drink in a bad
week and still want afternoon relief. Even water. Dry
leaves if you can. No hero binge the night the first
fig droops. The crop is on the wood. Stay in the lane
you were already in.
