[An Introduction to Figs](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/)
already told beginners to buy a known bag. Promix HP is
the one we named. It is consistent. It is not cheap.
Field capacity on that page is about 70 percent — damp,
not dripping — the feel you want when you firm mix
around a stick.

The hand test is the same idea as a
[Fig Pop](https://figroots.com/fig-pops/). Squeeze a
fist. Water should not run. The wad should not dust. If
you cannot find that feel, you are guessing every time
you water.

HP is the high-porosity bag. People buy a cheaper peat
mix that says “potting” and then they add nothing and
then they wonder why the can stays wet for a week. HP
already has air. Most of us still add more. A vegetable
gardener will argue with you. That argument is the
point. Water should leave. If it will not leave, the
fig will.

I would rather teach the squeeze than hand you a
moisture meter. I would rather you buy one honest bag
this year than invent a recipe from five aisles. When
you have fifty cans, the bag cost hurts and you start
building your own. That is later. The first ten trees
can be HP plus perlite you can see. The Intro page is
the beginner door. This draft is why that door exists.

Do not use HP as a shaft in a clay hole. I have said
that. Promix in the ground is a cup. Promix in a can
with holes is a climate you can dump if it sours.

Fertigate after the plant is a plant, not while it is a
stick. The Introduction already said after you pot up
to about a 3-gallon you start fertigating every time
you water. That sentence assumes a rooted plant, not a
cutting in a pop. Mixer settings **[VERIFY]**. I will
not print a siphon ratio as a law.

Zone 7 beginners should still buy the bag. Their winter
is not a reason to use yard dirt. Zone 9 beginners
should still buy the bag. Their nematodes are a reason
*not* to use yard dirt. 8a beginners get the same bag
and the same squeeze. The feel does not have a zone.
The feel has a fist.

If you only remember one number from this pack, let it
be the feel, not a ratio. 70 percent field capacity is
a damp wad. It is not a lab. It is how we told you to
root a stick. It is still how I want a #3 to start the
week. Wet it. Squeeze it. Then water the plant, not the
calendar.

HP is the door, not a personality. When the library
grows, you will build your own and you will still
want that same fist test. Do not pour the bag into a
clay shaft. Fertigate a plant, not a stick. A first
ten trees can live on HP plus rocks you can see. The
next fifty will tempt you into a cheaper peat brick.
Keep the squeeze even when the SKU changes. The wad
is the law I will stand on. The bag is just a door.
