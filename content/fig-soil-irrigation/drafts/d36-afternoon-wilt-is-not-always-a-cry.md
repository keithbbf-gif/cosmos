A fig in South Arkansas sun at 2 p.m. will droop like
it is dying. At dusk it will stand up and look at you
like you are the drama. I have watered that theater
and then sat in a swamp until Friday.

True drought wilt does not recover at dusk. The leaves
stay tired. The pot is light. The dirt dusts. The
fruit shrivels or drops. That plant wants a drink, and
it wanted one this morning.

Heat wilt recovers. The pot is still heavy. The dirt
still smears. The leaves look rude at 2 and polite at
7. That plant wants afternoon shade more than it wants
another gallon. [Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/)
already split the country: full sun in the North; in
the South, some shade in summer and lots of water.
Lots of water is not the same as water at 2 p.m.
because the leaves acted.

A black pot on gravel is an oven. Roots in a thin can
see every degree. Lift. If it is light, drink, then
move it off the griddle. If it is heavy, move it off
the griddle and do not drink. The wilt was the oven.
I have done the 3 p.m. version of a pot-up on asphalt.
The plant told me about it for two weeks.

In-ground trees hide heat wilt longer. Shallow roots
still cook under bare clay. Mulch. An east wall is
kinder in August than a west wall that reflects. Hours
first — UAEX wants 6 to 8 if you hope to harvest —
then which hours. Deep shade is not Southern best
practice. Afternoon relief is.

Zone 7 afternoon wilt in a short summer is often a
real drink. Their heat is shorter and their pots are
already a winter plan. Zone 9 afternoon wilt is a
lifestyle. 8a afternoon wilt is the daily lie. Learn
the recovery. It will save you from a Tuesday that
soured a can.

If you cannot tell theater from thirst yet, lift the
pot or poke the ring *before* you open the hose. The
leaf is an actor. The weight is a witness. I believe
the witness.

Move the black can off the griddle before you open
the hose. If it is light, drink, then shade. If it
is heavy, shade and do not drink. I have done the
3 p.m. pot-up on asphalt. The plant told me for two
weeks. In-ground trees hide the oven longer. Mulch.
East wall. Hours first, then which hours. The leaf
is an actor. The weight is a witness. I believe the
witness.

If you cannot tell theater from thirst yet, do the lift
before the faucet. Two weeks of sulk after a 3 p.m.
asphalt pot-up taught me more than a chart. Shade is
water you do not pour.
