# Rights and attribution

## Policy

- **No synthetic or AI-generated photographs.** Every raster image in `assets/` is a licensed photograph from Wikimedia Commons (or a U.S. government work where noted in `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`).
- **Schematics** (`schematic-*.svg`) are original line art created for this guide, dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Redistribution:** JPEG/PNG files may be redistributed under their respective licenses when attribution and share-alike terms are preserved. SVG schematics may be reused without restriction.
- **Trademarks:** Product and retailer names appearing in Commons descriptions (for example, showroom photos) are used only in factual captions; no endorsement is implied.

## Caption credit line (HTML)

For WordPress/WXR export, each `<figure>` includes a `<figcaption>` with a human-readable caption. Where required by license, the figcaption ends with a short credit, for example: `Photo: MeRyan / CC BY 2.0 (Wikimedia Commons).`

Full provenance table: `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`.

## DMCA / corrections

If you believe an asset is mis-attributed or not licensed for this use, remove the file from `assets/`, replace the embed with the remote URL noted in `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`, and file a correction in the content queue. Do not substitute unverified stock or generated images.
