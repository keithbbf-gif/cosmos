Overhang is not a style. It is knee space, and
knee space changes with how high you sit.

NKBA seating clearances, the ones I write on
every Bradley island that claims to host:

- 30-inch top (table): 24 inches wide by 18
  inches deep per person.
- 36-inch top (counter): 24 wide by 15 deep.
- 42-inch top (bar): 24 wide by 12 deep.

The higher the perch, the steeper the legs,
the less depth you need. That is the only
reason a bar-height island can be skinnier
and still claim seating. It is not a reason
to put 12 inches of overhang on a 36-inch
top and call it generous.

I treat those depths as the overhang to the
finished box, not to a decorative corbel
that eats the knees. If a bracket hangs down
into the 15 inches, you do not have 15
inches. You have a shin-knocker and a
brochure number.

Minimums versus comfort: 12 inches at
counter height is a minimum I will allow
for a stool someone uses to put on shoes.
It is not dinner. Fifteen is dinner for
most adults. Eighteen at table height is
how you keep a chair from feeling like a
mistake.

Stone wants support sooner than wood. A
12-inch stone fly can sometimes live on
the slab’s own stiffness, depending on the
stone and the thickness. A 15-inch fly
wants steel or corbels. I do not guess. I
ask the fabricator. Then I ask them again
if the stone is a busy granite with a
fissure near the edge.

Wood can fly a little farther before it
feels dangerous, and it will bounce before
it cracks. Bounce is still a failure if
you were selling solidity. Brackets on
wood are not an insult. They are how a
1.5-inch top stays a table.

Write the overhang on the plan as a number
and a height, together. “15 inches at 36”
is a spec. “Breakfast bar” is a wish. I
will build the spec.

One more number people forget: the top’s
thickness eats the gap to the stool. A
2-inch wood edge or a stacked stone edge
is not the same underside as a thin
laminate. Measure to the finished
underside, not to a line on an elevation
that ignored the build-up. If the gap
goes under 10 inches, the overhang can
be perfect and the seat will still feel
wrong. Height and fly are a pair. Do not
spec them on different days.
