Order matters. The pretty order —
stools, slab, boxes, then “we’ll
make the aisles work” — is how
you get a lie.

**1. Measure the room you have.**
Tape, floor hump, door swings,
slab path. Draft 18. If you
cannot touch a number, it is
not a number.

**2. Name the job.** Prep, serve,
cook, or a mixed pair you can
say out loud. Drafts 06 and 15.
If the job is “everything,”
refuse the everything.

**3. Refuse if you should.**
Draft 17. Peninsula, cart, or
a better wall run. A Bradley
dealer can sell those without
shame.

**4. Give the aisles their inches.**
42 or 48 on work. 36 on walks.
32 / 36 / 44 behind stools.
Drafts 09, 16, 31. Write them
on the plan before the rectangle.

**5. Size the leftover.**
Minimums in draft 10. Depth math
in 12. Length and people in 13.
Triangle clip in 14. If the
leftover is a cart, buy a cart.

**6. Pick the top for the Wednesday.**
Wood versus stone, draft 19, then
the specific drafts 20–27. Weight
on the box, 28. Do not pick a
slab from a phone while the
aisles are still fiction.

**7. Draw landings before stools.**
If a sink or cooktop is in the
top, the shoulders come first.
Stools get the remainder. If
the remainder is a lie, draft
36.

**8. Overhang as a number at a
height.** Drafts 29, 32, 33.
Support on the shop drawing.
Blocking in the Bradley order.

**9. Power as an edition, not a
habit.** Drafts 37–41, 43. Ask
the AHJ. Pop-up or on-or-above,
or a future path. GFCI. Circuits
for machines. No 2016 side
outlet pretending to feed a
toaster.

**10. Light as two layers.**
Draft 42. Hood is not a pendant.

**11. Stools last, but not in
another decade.** Draft 34.
Measure tuck, gap, and width.
Write them on the spec sheet,
draft 46.

**12. Name the bays.** Draft 45.
Chase bay exists. Mixer fits.
Trash is not a surprise.

Then order. Not before.

I keep this sequence on one page
in the job folder. When a meeting
jumps to door styles, I read it
out loud. Door styles are a
pleasure. They are not step one.
The island will outlive the
meeting. It should be born in
this order so it does not spend
its life apologizing.
