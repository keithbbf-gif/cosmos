This is not a form to make the job
feel official. It is the shortest
way I know to keep a showroom
conversation from floating. Fill
it in the room, with a tape, with
the people who will cook standing
there. If a line is blank, we are
not ordering. A pretty blank is
still a blank.

I have watched a dealer and a
household agree with their mouths
and disagree on every number. The
sheet is how we catch that before
Bradley cuts a box. Hand it over
filled. Do not hand it over as a
homework packet. The homework is
the walking.

**Refuse line**

We will not spend the aisle to
win the photograph.

Signed, household: ____
Signed, dealer / designer: ____
Date: ____

Keep a photo of the taped floor
with this sheet. Dates match.
If the sheet says 48 on the cook
aisle and the photo shows 40, the
photo wins and we fill the sheet
again. I would rather reprint a
page than reprint a stone.

The refuse line is not decoration.
If nobody will sign it, we are
not in a Bradley island job yet.
We are in a photograph job. Those
can go to someone else.
