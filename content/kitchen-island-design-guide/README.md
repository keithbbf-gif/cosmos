# Kitchen island design guide — Bradley islands

Staged drafts. Not published. Not a brochure.

This folder is a design guide for **Bradley islands**: full-access cabinet islands,
sized to the room, finished like furniture, sold through a dealer who will
stand in the aisle with a tape. The writing is meant to be read out loud
to a homeowner who is about to spend real money.

If you want the short path: read `drafts/47-sequence-measure-to-stools.md`,
then the spec sheet in `drafts/46-spec-sheet-for-a-dealer.md`. Everyone
else should start at `01` and walk the stages.

## What this is

Forty-eight staged drafts under `drafts/`. Each file is a standalone piece
with the same frontmatter (`id`, `slug`, `title`, `stage`, `status`,
`topics`). Together they cover the five jobs the brief named:

| Stage | Job | Drafts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1-brief` | House voice, what a Bradley island is | 01–02 |
| `2-history` | How the island got into the middle of the room | 03–08 |
| `3-proportions` | Aisles, height, depth, length, landings, refusal | 09–18 |
| `4-materials` | Butcher block vs stone, and the honest hybrids | 19–28 |
| `5-seating` | Overhang, stools, traffic behind the diner | 29–36 |
| `6-electrical` | 2023 NEC, GFCI, pop-ups, light, circuits | 37–43 |
| `7-bradley` | The box, storage, dealer spec, sequence, aftercare | 44–48 |

`status: staged` means Website-GC dest is **staged**. Nobody clicks publish
from this tree.

## Voice

Human. A designer who has walked too many kitchens and will tell you not
to put an island in a room that cannot take one. Numbers come from NKBA
planning guidelines and the NEC edition your inspector actually uses.
Guidelines are not law. Local amendments win.

We do not invent SKUs, lead times, or warranty language. Bradley
construction notes in the later drafts describe **typical full-access
practice** (wider drawers, no-charge size mods, dovetail drawers,
soft-close hardware, U.S./Canada build). Confirm the current dealer
book before you quote a client.

## How to use a draft

1. Read the frontmatter. If the topic is not your fight, skip it.
2. The first paragraph is the claim. If you disagree, write in the
   margin. Do not “improve” the sentence into marketing.
3. Measurements are finished-floor and counter-frontage unless noted.
4. Electrical drafts are for conversation with a licensed electrician
   and the AHJ. They are not a permit set.

## Provenance

Researched against:

- NKBA *Kitchen Planning Guidelines with Access Standards* (public PDF,
  2022 posting of the current guidelines). Paraphrased. Not copied.
- NFPA / CMP-2 commentary on NEC 2023 `210.52(C)(2)` and `210.52(C)(3)`
  (island and peninsula receptacles optional; location rules tightened;
  future provision required if you skip the outlet).
- Trade practice on wood movement, overhang support, and stone weight.
  Shop judgment where a number is a shop number, not a code number.

Code cycles and NKBA editions move. Date-stamp a reprint before you
hand it to a client.
