# Portrait sources — licenses, or an honest blank

**Rule.** Public domain or Creative Commons only. Never generate a face. Never crop a still from a documentary, training tape, or workshop you do not have rights to. Never use a university, institute, or "headshot" page as if it were free.

Graphics agents: read this file before drawing. If `status` is `no-portrait`, set type (name, dates, one object) or a **period object** listed below. Do not "fill in" a likeness.

Last verified against Commons/museum/GPO pages: 2026-09-14. **Re-open the file page on upload day.** Licenses get edited.

## Status key

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pd` | Use. Copy license line under the image. |
| `cc` | Use. Keep BY/SA text. |
| `confirm` | Commons *claims* PD/CC; author unknown or US status thin. Do not use as a featured image until a human re-reads the file page. |
| `no-portrait` | No clean still of the person. Type or period object only. |

## Why this table is mostly blanks

Family therapy became a public field after 1950. Almost every useful photograph of Ackerman, Bowen, Satir, Minuchin, Haley, Selvini Palazzoli, Berg, White, and the living writers is still in copyright (institute archives, press, workshop photographers).

A 1963 Imogen Cunningham portrait of Gregory Bateson at the Smithsonian National Portrait Gallery (NPG.2003.8) is **© 1963 Imogen Cunningham Trust** — not a web image. Bali and Iatmul photographs *by* Bateson show other people and often carry Library of Congress usage conditions. They are not Bateson's headshot and they are not clip art for "the family."

A 2004 Flickr / Festival della Scienza photograph of Mary Catherine Bateson (CC BY-SA 2.0) is **not** Gregory Bateson and must not be cropped into a fake founder portrait. Commons category pages for Virginia Satir currently surface grave photographs and mislabeled files — not a clean studio still.

Type is the default. That is the assignment: **portrait PD/CC notes only.**

## Credit block (paste under every image)

```
Image: [title], [artist/photographer], [year].
Source: [museum or Commons URL].
License: [PD / CC BY 4.0 / CC0] — [link to license].
```

If you cannot fill every bracket, do not upload the image.

## What "never fake faces" means

No Midjourney / DALL·E / Grok / Firefly "portrait of Virginia Satir in a 1964 office." No face-swap. No "historical composite." A typographic poster is more honest than a plausible lie.
