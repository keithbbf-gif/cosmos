# Portrait sources — licenses, or an honest blank

**Rule.** Public domain or Creative Commons only. Never generate a face. Never crop a still from a documentary, training tape, or workshop you do not have rights to. Never use a university, institute, or "headshot" page as if it were free.

Graphics agents: read this file before drawing. If `status` is `no-portrait`, set type (name, dates, one object) or a **period object** listed below. Do not "fill in" a likeness.

Last verified against Commons/museum/GPO pages: 2026-09-14. **Re-open the file page on upload day.** Licenses get edited.

## Status key

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pd` | Use. Copy license line under the image. |
| `cc` | Use. Keep BY/SA text. |
| `confirm` | Commons *claims* PD/CC; author unknown or US status thin. Do not use as a featured image until a human re-reads the file page. |
| `no-portrait` | No clean still of the person. Type or period object only. |

## Why this table is mostly blanks

Family therapy became a public field after 1950. Almost every useful photograph of Ackerman, Bowen, Satir, Minuchin, Haley, Selvini Palazzoli, Berg, White, and the living writers is still in copyright (institute archives, press, workshop photographers). A 1963 Imogen Cunningham portrait of Gregory Bateson at the Smithsonian National Portrait Gallery is **© 1963 Imogen Cunningham Trust** — not a web image. Bali and Iatmul photographs *by* Bateson show other people and often carry Library of Congress usage conditions. They are not Bateson's headshot and they are not clip art for "the family."

Type is the default. That is the assignment: **portrait rights notes, PD/CC only.**

## The two clean objects (re-open on upload day)

1. **John Elderkin Bell, *Family Group Therapy*, Public Health Monograph No. 64**, U.S. Department of Health, Education, and Welfare, Public Health Service, 1961. Printed by the U.S. Government Printing Office. A work of the United States federal government. **PD.** Use the title page, not a modern reprint jacket. Claremont Colleges and other libraries hold copies; a scan of the *1961 GPO page* is the object.

2. **William Healy, *The Individual Delinquent*,** Little, Brown, 1915. U.S. publication 1915. **PD** as a 1915 U.S. book. Title page only. Do not use photographs of named children from the volume as thumbnails.

If a later editor finds a Commons file of Ackerman, Bowen, Satir, or Minuchin with a *named* photographer who died more than 70 years ago *and* a U.S. publication date that makes the file PD, upgrade that row. Until then, do not "fill in."

## Credit block (paste under every image)

```
Image: [title], [artist/photographer], [year].
Source: [museum, GPO, or Commons URL].
License: [PD / CC BY 4.0 / CC0] — [link to license].
```

If you cannot fill every bracket, do not upload the image.

## What "never fake faces" means

No Midjourney / DALL·E / Grok / Firefly "portrait of Salvador Minuchin in the style of 1974." No face-swap. No "historical composite." A typographic poster is more honest than a plausible lie.
