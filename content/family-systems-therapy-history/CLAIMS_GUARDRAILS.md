# Claims guardrails — family systems history pack

Educational history only. These sentences must never go on wowtherapies.com, in ads, or in schema markup.

This file is a refuse list. If a draft drifts toward any line below, cut the line. Do not "soften" it into a how-to.

## Never say (treatment / DIY)

- Any instruction a reader could follow as a session: genograms to draw, miracle questions to ask, circular-question lists, empty-chair or family-sculpture how-tos, "try this paradox at dinner," MST "session one" checklists, EFT hold-me-tight exercises.
- "Try this at home," "use this worksheet," "self-treat," "you can differentiate your way out of…"
- Dosing of psychiatric medication, taper advice, or "ask your doctor about [drug]" as content.
- "This method is the gold standard for [diagnosis]."
- "Studies prove [school] works better than [school]" without a named trial, a dated review, and a sentence about limits.
- Crisis instructions that replace emergency services. A single line — "If you are in danger, call local emergency services" — is enough. Do not write a safety-planning protocol in these essays.

## Never say (medical / legal)

- That WOW Therapies diagnoses, treats, or "specializes in" a DSM/ICD condition **in these history posts**. Service pages are a different file and a different review.
- That reading an essay is therapy, counseling, family therapy, or a substitute for a licensed clinician.
- PHI, composite patients, or "a client once told me" stories. No invented case vignettes with identifying color. Published, named case material from a book (the La Salles in *Families of the Slums*; the composite family in *The Family Crucible*) may be mentioned as *published text*, not rewritten as if it happened in Arkansas.
- Guarantees of outcome.
- Anything a reasonable reader would hear as a diagnosis of themselves, their child, or their parents.

## Never say (history that is too neat)

- "The family causes schizophrenia." Bateson et al. 1956 is a communication paper. It hardened, in some clinics and in the public ear, into an etiology. Later authors, and some of the original group, had to walk that hardening back. This pack describes the paper and the injury. It does not revive the etiology.
- Schizophrenogenic-mother (Fromm-Reichmann 1948) or Lidz/Wynne family-blame theories as fact. Name them as mid-century theories that harmed women. Name expressed-emotion research (Brown, Birley, Wing; Vaughn and Leff) as a *different* literature — relapse prediction, not maternal guilt — and do not collapse the two.
- "Bowen proved that mothers who work damage children." He did not. Do not recycle popular misreadings of differentiation or of "family projection."
- "Minuchin proved poor families are disorganized." *Families of the Slums* (1967) is a dated study of a specific Wiltwyck sample. It is not a character reference for a social class.
- "Satir's warmth heals everyone." Warmth can wash out danger. A violent home is the problem.
- Refrigerator-mother or Bettelheim's autism claims as fact.
- Conversion-therapy "success." Name the harm. Do not teach the method.
- That a one-way mirror is a neutral scientific instrument. It trained a generation. It also turned a household into a specimen. Consent forms got longer for a reason.
- That DSM-III (1980) "ended ideology" in family therapy. Manuals arrived. Argument did not leave.

## Never say (brand / SEO)

- "Best family therapist in Arkansas" or any superlative about the practice.
- Disease keywords paired with a CTA ("fix your family today — book now") on these URLs.
- Schema `MedicalTherapy`, `Drug`, or `treats` / `indication` on history posts. `Article` is enough.
- Fake author credentials.
- Patient testimonials in this folder.

## Allowed

- Dated description of what a school claimed, who paid for it, who dissented, and what later researchers revised.
- Named trials as historical events (early MST studies; Johnson's EFT outcome papers as *dated publications*, not as a booking argument).
- Controversy, including racism, sexism, class, colonial psychiatry, and the one-way glass, when it is part of the record.
- A short "why a contemporary clinician still reads this" closing that does **not** become a protocol.
- The educational footer already in each draft.

## Living people

Johnson, McGoldrick, Boyd-Franklin, Falicov, Boss, Walsh, Hardy, Epston, Madanes, Aponte, and others who were alive at last verification (2026-09-14) are not public property. No health speculation. No unsourced private life. Public books, public lectures, and public interviews only.

## If asked for both a history and a how-to

Print the history. Refuse the how-to in one sentence: this series does not publish treatment protocols.
