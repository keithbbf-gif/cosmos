# Claims guardrails — family systems therapy history

Educational history only. These sentences must never go on wowtherapies.com, in ads, or in schema markup.

This file is a refuse list. If a draft drifts toward any line below, cut the line. Do not "soften" it into a how-to.

## Never say (treatment / DIY)

- Any instruction a reader could follow as a session: blank genograms to complete, miracle questions to ask at dinner, circular-question lists, chair-moving scripts, sculpting directions, paradoxical homework, "ask yourself these ten questions," EFT hold-me-tight steps, narrative externalizing worksheets.
- "Try this at home," "use this worksheet," "self-treat," "you can differentiate your way out of…"
- Dosing of psychiatric medication, taper advice, or "ask your doctor about [drug]" as content.
- "This method is the gold standard for [diagnosis]."
- "Studies prove [school] works better than [school]" without a named trial, a dated review, and a sentence about limits.
- Crisis instructions that replace emergency services. A single line — "If you are in danger, call local emergency services" — is enough. Do not write a safety-planning protocol in these essays.

## Never say (medical / legal)

- That WOW Therapies diagnoses, treats, or "specializes in" a DSM/ICD condition **in these history posts**. Service pages are a different file and a different review.
- That reading an essay is therapy, counseling, or a substitute for a licensed clinician.
- PHI, composite patients, or "a client once told me" stories. No invented case vignettes with identifying color.
- Guarantees of outcome.
- Anything a reasonable reader would hear as a diagnosis of themselves, their child, or their mother.

## Never say (history that is too neat)

- "Bowen invented family therapy" as if Ackerman, Bell, Bateson, Satir, and a child-guidance clinic were not already in the room.
- "Minuchin invented poor families as a clinical object." (Social work and Wiltwyck were already there; he wrote them into a school.)
- "Satir was only warmth." (She was MRI's first training director. Warmth was a method and a politics.)
- "The family causes schizophrenia." Name the 1950s papers. Name the walk-back. Do not revive the etiology.
- Refrigerator-mother or schizophrenogenic-mother as fact. They are historical injuries.
- Conversion-therapy "success." Name the harm. Do not teach the method.
- That a one-way mirror is a neutral scientific instrument. It is a power arrangement with a later consent literature.
- That Milwaukee "replaced" structural work, or that narrative "ended" systems. Schools accumulate. They do not erase.

## Never say (brand / SEO)

- "Best therapist in Arkansas" or any superlative about the practice.
- Disease keywords paired with a CTA ("fix your family today — book now") on these URLs.
- Schema `MedicalTherapy`, `Drug`, or `treats` / `indication` on history posts. `Article` is enough.
- Fake author credentials.
- Patient testimonials in this folder.

## Allowed

- Dated description of what a school claimed, who paid for it, who dissented, and what later researchers revised.
- Named trials and manuals as historical events.
- Controversy, including racism, sexism, class, mother-blame, and the university-clinic problem, when it is part of the record.
- A short "why a contemporary clinician still reads this" closing that does **not** become a protocol.
- The educational footer and Claims box already in each draft.
- Honest cross-links to the sibling therapy-history pack (`families-systems-therapy`, `virginia-satir`, `salvador-minuchin`, `insoo-kim-berg`).
