**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Ivan Boszormenyi-Nagy and Geraldine M. Spark published *Invisible Loyalties* with Harper & Row in 1973. The title is the claim: that a household runs on debts and credits a stranger cannot see, and that a child can pay a bill that is not theirs. Nagy had come from Hungary, from a psychiatric and psychoanalytic training, and from the Eastern Pennsylvania Psychiatric Institute, where he had been seeing families since the 1950s. EPPI is the under-told campus. Georgetown and Philadelphia photograph better in American memory. This pack will keep EPPI on the map.

*Between Give and Take* (Brunner/Mazel, 1986), with Barbara R. Krasner, is the later, more systematic object. Contextual therapy's public vocabulary — loyalty, entitlement, parentification, a ledger — is easy to turn into a moral scorecard. Scorecards are this page's refuse list. History may name the words. History may not invite you to rate your relatives.

Parentification is the word that escaped the shop. Social workers already knew the oldest daughter who translates, cooks, and soothes. Nagy gave the pattern an ethical sentence: a child is owed a childhood. That sentence can be a relief. It can also become a prosecution of a poor household that needed the oldest daughter to function. Context, in his title, was supposed to prevent that prosecution. Imitators skipped the context and kept the scold.

He talked about a relational ethic at a time when many American schools preferred strategy or structure. Ethic is a word that makes some clinicians nervous, as if a moral vocabulary were unscientific. Nervousness is a historical tone. This pack will not resolve whether contextual work is a therapy or a moral philosophy with a waiting room. It will say the 1973 book exists, the 1986 book exists, and both are still in copyright as designed objects.

No ledger clip art as science. No generated "Hungarian psychiatrist with a family Bible." Type: Ivan Boszormenyi-Nagy, 1920–2007. Spark and Krasner keep their names in the citations. Co-authors disappear in founder stories. This pack will not disappear them.

A small-city hour that still notices a child translating a parent's rage is near this hallway. Noticing is not a contextual protocol. This series will not help you compute a ledger. Computation is a clinical act with a person who consented to sit down.

Nagy died in 2007. The word "parentified" now lives in social-media captions. Captions are not *Invisible Loyalties*. The distance between a Harper & Row hardback and a caption is the distance this essay exists to keep.

## EPPI on the map

Georgetown and Philadelphia Child Guidance photograph better in American memory than the Eastern Pennsylvania Psychiatric Institute. Memory is a syllabus. Nagy's shop sat at EPPI. This pack will keep putting EPPI back on the map until a reader can hold more than two East Coast addresses.

Parentification escaped into captions that prosecute poor households for needing an oldest daughter. Nagy's "context" was supposed to prevent that prosecution. Imitators skipped the context. This page is a caption on the imitators, not a scorecard for your relatives.

Spark (1973) and Krasner (1986) stay in the sentence. Founder stories drop them. This pack will not.

## Loyalty as a public word

"Invisible loyalties" traveled farther than EPPI. Church workshops and social-work classrooms now use the phrase without the 1973 book's caution. Travel thins. This page will not restore the thickness by inviting you to list your debts. Lists are scorecards.

He talked about fairness in a century that preferred cleverness. Fairness is an unfashionable historical tone. Unfashionable is not the same as unused. Rural households already know about unpaid bills between generations. Knowing is not "folk Nagy." Treating it as folk Nagy is a professional theft this pack has already refused.

## Entitlement as a word that soured

"Entitlement" in ordinary American speech now means a tantrum. In the 1973 book it meant something closer to what a child is owed. Words sour. Souring is why a history page has to keep a spine next to a caption. This page will not restore the 1973 sense by inviting you to compute what you are owed. Computation is a clinical act.

Philadelphia teaching rooms heard him in the same city as structural fame. Same city, different ethic. Maps that have only Minuchin in Philadelphia have lost EPPI and a ledger vocabulary. This pack puts both back.
