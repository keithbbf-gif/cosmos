**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Mara Selvini Palazzoli, Luigi Boscolo, Gianfranco Cecchin, and Giuliana Prata published *Paradosso e controparadosso* in Italian in 1975. Jason Aronson issued the English *Paradox and Counterparadox* in 1978. Four names. A city. A book that taught the English-speaking field to say "Milan" as if it were a method.

The Centro per lo Studio della Famiglia was a research and treatment shop aimed, in the early years, at households organized around a psychotic or anorectic member. That clinical target is a historical fact. It is not this site's target. The team's later fame in American workshops often sanded the target off and kept the rituals: a long pre-session, a team behind glass, a carefully worded message, an interval between meetings.

In 1980 *Family Process* printed "Hypothesizing—Circularity—Neutrality: Three Guidelines for the Conductor of the Session." The title is a teaching object. This page will name the three words so a reader can recognize them. It will not define them as a mini-manual and will not offer a circular-question list. A list is a worksheet.

The team later split. Selvini Palazzoli and Prata went one way, toward invariant prescriptions and a more programmatic research. Boscolo and Cecchin went another, toward a more conversational, curiosity-minded teaching that American students found easier to sit through. Splits are ordinary. What is less ordinary is how completely "Milan" remained a single slide in U.S. syllabi after the shop had become two shops. This pack will not pick a successor. It will keep 1975/78 and 1980 on the table and let the split be a split.

Neutrality, as a word, drew feminist fire. A stance that claims not to take sides in a household with a beating or a silencing can be a side. Goldner and others said so in the American journal the Milan team had used as a mailbox. The quarrel is part of the history. A clinic site does not get to reprint neutrality as a virtue without the quarrel.

No Centro staff photographs. Type: Milan team, 1975–1980 public objects. Do not scrape an Italian workshop still.

Tom Andersen's later reflecting team (1987) is a cousin, not a child. Cousins share glass and a doubt about secret seeing. They do not share a 1975 Italian book. Keep the border.

A small-city clinician who still thinks a good question is better than a good lecture is near this hallway. Thinking that is not a Milan protocol. This series will not teach you to hypothesize about your relatives. Hypothesizing, in 1980, was a team sport with a one-way mirror and a week to recover. A blog is not that sport.

Selvini Palazzoli died in 1999. The English word "systemic" outlived her and now means, in some American mouths, anything that is not strictly individual. That widening is a vocabulary event. It is not a license to call every school meeting "Milan." This page keeps Milan in Milan, in 1975, in 1980, and in a split.

## English slides, Italian book

American trainees met Milan as three words and a ritual of leaving the room. Italian readers met a 1975 book about households living with severe symptoms. The distance between those meetings is the history. This pack will not pretend a 1980s hotel module was the Centro.

Boscolo and Cecchin's later teaching, more curious and less programmatic, is why some Americans still say "Milan" with affection. Selvini Palazzoli and Prata's later programmatic research is why others say it with a flinch. Affection and flinch are both data. This page will not referee.

Neutrality-as-side is the feminist caption. The caption stays even if a reader loves circular questions. Loving a question is not a reason to print a list of questions. Lists are worksheets.

## Interval as a technology

Long gaps between meetings were part of the Milan research ritual. Gaps are a technology. They are also a luxury. A county clinic that sees a household next week is not "failing to be Milan." It is living inside a different clock. Clocks belong in history. This page will not tell you to wait a month. Waiting a month is a protocol.

The 1980 title's three words escaped into quizzes. Quizzes are worksheets. Name the title. Do not reprint the guidelines as steps.
