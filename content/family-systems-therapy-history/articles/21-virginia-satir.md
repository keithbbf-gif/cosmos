**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Virginia Satir was born on 26 June 1916 in Neillsville, Wisconsin, and died on 10 September 1988 in California. She trained as a social worker at the University of Chicago, worked at the Illinois Psychiatric Institute, directed family-therapy training at the Mental Research Institute in the early 1960s, and then left the engineer's table for a traveling practice that made her, by the 1970s, one of the faces of a new idea: you do not treat the "identified patient" as if the rest of the table were furniture.

## A social-work geography

Neillsville to Chicago to Palo Alto is not an institute geography. It is a social-work geography. She talked about a Wisconsin farm table as if it were clinical data: people learn early whether their feelings are allowed at dinner. Treat that as her origin story, not as nostalgia copy. The University of Chicago and the Illinois Psychiatric Institute gave her a profession. MRI gave her a title and a quarrel. She never sounded like Watzlawick. That difference is why this pack keeps both lineages.

*Conjoint Family Therapy* (1964) taught clinicians to bring the household into the same hour. She watched communication as weather: blaming, placating, computing, distracting — stances a person takes when self-worth is cheap. This page names the stances so a reader can recognize a vocabulary that leaked into workshops. It will not turn them into a party game. *Peoplemaking* (1972) took the same ideas to a public that had not signed a clinic consent. Public vocabularies get used on families that have not consented to a workshop.

The "iceberg" metaphor later associated with her teaching is a training-room object. This page will not turn it into a handout. The historical point is that she insisted the visible fight was not the whole person.

## Warmth as a method, and its shadow

Students remember warmth. Warmth can be a lamp. It can also be a flood that washes out danger. A family in which someone is violent does not need a sculpture first. It needs a plan. Satir knew more about this than the parody of her suggests. The parody exists because international workshops in the 1970s and 1980s made change look like a weekend, and because the human-potential hangover would take any living room that would have it.

She sculpted families in space. She asked people to stand where their loyalty stood. Experiential work is easy to write as theater because it sometimes was theater. Whitaker sat near her temperature. MRI sat far from it. A first family hour in a small-city practice is closer to her social-work origin than to a California marathon: who else is in this story, and who has been nominated as the problem?

She left MRI. The leaving was not a failure of theory. It was a decision about which room she wanted: a research institute or a family that would stand up and show its loyalties in space.

## After 1988

The Avanta Network and later Satir institutes carried a training model around the world. Like every posthumous network, they had to decide how much of the founder was a method and how much was a presence that cannot be franchised. A counseling site can honor the method without selling the presence. WOW Therapies is not hereby advertising a Satir program.

Ordinary American counseling still uses her fingerprints without the citation. "Who has been nominated as the problem?" is an intake ethic. Genogram talk in a first hour often has her warmth and Bowen's pencil at the same time. The general psychotherapy pack has a shorter Satir figure. This one is the family-systems deep lane. It will not reprint that shorter essay. It will say the same ethical thing at more length: growth language is not a delay of safety.

Do not scrape Avanta workshop stills. There is no clean licensed portrait. Type: Virginia Satir, 1916–1988.

Do not take a sculpture home from this page. If a household is in trouble, the essay is not the appointment.

The usable inheritance is the refusal to let a child be the only name on the form. The rest is training, a supervisor if the hour becomes a stage, and a consent form her public workshops did not always have to write at this length.

She liked to teach that the problem was not the problem — that coping was. Like most attributed teachings, it is true until it is not. A violent home is the problem. A hungry home is the problem. Theory is for the hour after those facts are named. This pack will not print the line as a verified quotation with a page number it does not have. It will print the warning.

International rooms made her a traveling founder. Some were serious. Some were the hangover. A historically literate reader can hold both. A clinic that still answers its own phone does not need the hangover.

## 26 June 1916, 10 September 1988

Those are the hard dates. Neillsville is a small Wisconsin city, not a mythic farm without a post office. Chicago is a university and an institute. Palo Alto is MRI and a leaving. California is the traveling years and the death. Four places, one social worker who became a public face.

The general psychotherapy pack's Satir essay is shorter and sits next to Rogers and Minuchin in a wide counseling calendar. This essay sits next to MRI and Whitaker. Different neighbors. The deep-lane version keeps the leaving of MRI, the 1964 conjoint word, and the warning about weekends. It will not reprint the other pack's paragraphs.

Science and Behavior Books is a Palo Alto imprint. Local imprints are how West Coast training became a spine before New York noticed. Noticing later is a New York habit. Date 1964 anyway.
