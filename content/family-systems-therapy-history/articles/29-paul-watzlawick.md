**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Paul Watzlawick was born on 25 July 1921 in Villach, Austria, and died on 31 March 2007 in Palo Alto. He studied in Venice, spent time at the C. G. Jung Institute in Zürich — a fact that surprises readers who know him only as the cool author of paradox — and joined MRI in 1960. With Janet Beavin Bavelas and Don Jackson he published *Pragmatics of Human Communication* in 1967. With Weakland and Fisch he published *Change* in 1974. He also wrote smaller, meaner, funnier books about how people make themselves miserable with excellent reasons. Those books are public. This page will not turn them into a self-help list.

## A European in a California institute

MRI needed a theorist who could write. Jackson could fund and found. Weakland could watch. Haley could polemicize and leave. Watzlawick could make a theory of interaction sound like a finished object. *Pragmatics* taught a generation that one cannot *not* communicate; that every message has a report and a command; that digital and analog, symmetrical and complementary, are names for trouble in a couple. It is a theory book. It is also a style: dry, slightly amused at American sincerity.

The Jung Institute year is not a secret second career. It is a reminder that "MRI man" is a later flattening. He had already been through a European education that included a school this pack's general-psychotherapy cousins treat as a church. He left the church and kept the irony.

## Change, and the ethics of interruption

*Change* is the brief-therapy book. First-order, second-order; more of the same; the terrible simplifiers. This page will not teach a paradox. Paradox used as a stunt on a family that has not consented to a laboratory is a historical object, not a tip.

His popular books — *The Situation Is Hopeless, But Not Serious* is the title people remember — made MRI's temperament available at a bookstore. Popularization is a mixed gift. It is how a 1967 theory left the institute. It is how a 1967 theory became a dinner-table diagnosis of someone else's marriage. Do not diagnose a relative with a complementary schismogenesis. Bateson had already named the runaway. Watzlawick made it readable. Readable is not a license.

Feminist readers still apply. Complementary can be a polite name for a husband who talks and a wife who accommodates. Watzlawick's dryness does not excuse a clinician who uses his vocabulary to call a patriarchy a pattern and then "rebalance" it without naming the law.

## Inheritance

A small-city clinician can steal one habit. Notice that silence is also a message. Then do not narrate that notice as a triumph. Then stop.

Do not scrape MRI or press photographs. Type: Paul Watzlawick, 1921–2007.

If a household is in trouble, this page is not the appointment.

The usable inheritance is a prose temperature. American family therapy can get warm (Satir), meteorological (Bowen), theatrical (Whitaker), or strategic (Haley). Watzlawick is the one who sounds like a man who has already heard your insight and is waiting for the interaction. Waiting is an ethic if it is in service of accuracy. It is a cruelty if it is in service of being the smartest person behind the glass.

Villach to Venice to Zürich to Palo Alto is a Central European itinerary that ended in the same town as Jackson and Weakland. He outlived them both. The 2007 death is a public date. The books are the career. Janet Beavin Bavelas's name on *Pragmatics* is not a courtesy; later she continued a research life in communication that is not this pack's subject but is a reason not to say "Watzlawick's book" as if it were a solo.

The general psychotherapy pack does not give him a figure essay. The family-systems deep lane does, because *Pragmatics* is still the book a student is handed when someone says "communication theory" and means a clinic.

## Jung Institute, then a refusal of the church

Zürich is not a secret second career. It is a European education that included a school the general psychotherapy pack treats as a church. He left and kept the irony. Irony in *Pragmatics* is a style. Irony in a one-way room can be a cruelty. Date 1960 as the MRI arrival. Date 1967 as the spine. Date 2007 as the Palo Alto death.

Beavin, Jackson, Weakland, Fisch — his pages are crowded with names. Crowded is accurate. "Watzlawick said" is a later flattening. Complementary-as-polite-patriarchy is the feminist remainder. Dryness-as-accuracy is the usable remainder. Dinner-table diagnosis is the refuse list. Villach, 1921. Three names on the 1967 cover. Keep them.
