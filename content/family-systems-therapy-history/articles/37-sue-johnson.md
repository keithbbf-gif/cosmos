## Attachment walks into a couples hour

Family therapy's heroic decades often treated the couple as a system of power or a structure of coalitions. Johnson's school treated the couple as an attachment relationship: protest, withdraw, a cycle that can be named without being a Haley task. ## 1988 Greenberg & Johnson; ICEEFT as a later institute

The couples model begins in print as Leslie S. Greenberg and Susan M. Johnson, *Emotionally Focused Therapy for Couples* (Guilford, 1988), after Ottawa outcome papers in the mid-1980s. *The Practice of Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy: Creating Connection* (first edition Brunner/Mazel, 1996; later Brunner-Routledge / Routledge editions, including 2004 and 2019) is the manual most training programs mean by "Johnson." *Hold Me Tight* (Little, Brown, 2008) is the bookstore book. *Love Sense* (Little, Brown, 2013) is a later popular spine. Date the object you are holding.

The International Centre for Excellence in Emotionally Focused Therapy (ICEEFT) later organized certified training. An institute is not a 1958 storefront and not a 1966 MRI brief center. This pack will say an institute exists. It will not print a 2026 fee schedule or a hold-me-tight exercise.
