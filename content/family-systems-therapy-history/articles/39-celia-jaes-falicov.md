**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Celia Jaes Falicov published *Latino Families in Therapy* in 1998 (later editions followed) and spent a career treating migration, ecological context, and cultural meaning as structure, not as spice. MECA — her map of migration, ecology, family organization, and family life cycle — is a training-room object this page will name and will not teach. She was alive at last verification in 2026. Living writers get extra care: public books, public academic posts, no gossip, no scraped photographs.

## 1998 is later still

If Boyd-Franklin 1989 is late, 1998 is a reminder that "ethnicity on the syllabus" (McGoldrick 1982) did not automatically produce a book that put Latino families at the center of a clinical theory. Falicov had been writing for years before the 1998 spine. The book is the object a general reader can hold.

Migration, in her pages, is not a footnote about "adjustment." It is a reorganization of who is present, who sends money, who is the parent on which side of a border, what a child is asked to interpret. A child who interprets for parents is a parentified child in one dialect and a family's survival method in another. Structural labels without a migration map will choose the first dialect and call it science.

José Szapocznik's brief strategic family therapy in Miami is a neighbor on the manuals shelf: a structural-strategic hybrid written down for Latino families and for funders. Falicov is not a manualist in that sense. She is a theorist of context. Keep the addresses straight.

## Against spice

American family therapy liked to add "culture" in week twelve. Spice is how a white clinic keeps its 1974 map and sprinkles cumin on it. Falicov's argument is that the map was already cultural and did not say so. Minuchin's "enmeshment" and Bowen's "cutoff" can misread a kinship that sleeps closer and calls more, or a kinship that is stretched across a border. The after-the-mirror essay in this pack is the place that argument becomes an ethic of consent and staff. This figure essay is the place it has a name.

Later editions of the 1998 book, and later papers, kept arguing with a field that would rather have a festival than a theory. This draft will not inventory every edition. A live page should cite the one on the desk.

## 1988 *Family Transitions*; 1995 *Family Process* essay
