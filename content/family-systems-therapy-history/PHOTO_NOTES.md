# Photo notes — family systems therapy history

Text is the product. Images are evidence or atmosphere, never a fabricated witness.

Companion: `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` (the license table). Graphics agents start there.

This assignment is **portrait PD/CC notes only**. If the file page is not public domain or Creative Commons, the post is type. No generated faces. No "period photographs" that never existed.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or CC portraits listed as `pd` or `cc` in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`. This pack has almost none. That is not a failure.
- Period objects: a pre-1930 title page with a clean scan license; a GPO or museum photograph of a *building* (not a patient); a typographic treatment of *Family Process* 1(1) (1962) that does **not** scrape a copyrighted cover.
- Original diagrams drawn for this series: a dated timeline of 1954–1990; a map of addresses (Palo Alto, Georgetown, Philadelphia, Milan, Milwaukee, Adelaide); a family-tree of *schools* (not of patients).
- Type treatments: name, dates, one short epithet.

## Banned

- AI-generated faces, "historical photographs" that are not photographs, or "what X would look like."
- Scraped workshop / institute / university headshots (Bowen Center, Satir Global, Minuchin Center, MRI, Ackerman Institute, Dulwich Centre, BFTC, AAMFT, AFTA).
- Patient artwork, session stills, one-way-mirror photographs of living families.
- Children as thumbnails.
- Stock "therapist with clipboard and smiling family."
- Before/after faces.
- Genogram photographs presented as free illustrations of "how family therapy works."
- Book jackets treated as public-domain art.
- Anything that implies a WOW Therapies treatment room you have not photographed with consent.

## Alt text

Name what is in the frame. "Type treatment: Murray Bowen, 1913–1990, on a cream field." Not "the birth of family therapy."

For type-only posts: no empty `<img>`. The heading is the image.

## WordPress featured image

If `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` says `no-portrait` or `confirm`, the featured image is a **series template** (wordmark + essay title), not a guessed face. Build one template; reuse it.

On upload day, open the Commons or museum URL again. Paste the credit block from `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` into the media caption **and** under the image in the post.

## Color and crop

Do not colorize PD photographs and call them contemporary. Do not crop a group so that a bystander becomes "the" portrait. Do not flip a face for layout.

## Sibling lanes

Do not reuse SLPWOW speech-pathology graphics here. Do not steal a Freud Halberstadt from the therapy-history pack and caption it as Bowen. Different essay, different eye.

## If the graphics agent and the writer disagree

The license table wins. A beautiful unlicensed face does not ship.
