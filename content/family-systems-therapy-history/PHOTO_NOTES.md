# Photo notes — family systems history series

Text is the product. Images are evidence or atmosphere, never a fabricated witness.

Companion: `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` (the license table). Graphics agents start there.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or CC portraits listed as `pd` or `cc` in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`.
- Period objects with a clean license: the 1961 U.S. Public Health Service monograph *Family Group Therapy* (Bell; GPO — U.S. government work, PD); a 1915 title page of Healy's *The Individual Delinquent* (U.S. publication 1915, now PD); a nineteenth-century family-group painting used as *art* and captioned as art.
- Original diagrams drawn for this series: a dated timeline of institutes (Palo Alto, Philadelphia, Milan, Milwaukee, Adelaide); a map of *schools*, not of patients.
- Type treatments: name, dates, one short epithet.

## Banned

- AI-generated faces, "historical photographs" that are not photographs, or "what Minuchin would look like."
- Scraped workshop / AAMFT / university / Avanta / Dulwich / Bowen Center headshots.
- Patient artwork, family sculptures photographed in a workshop, or stills from training tapes. Those rooms had people in them. The people did not sign a web license.
- Children in experiments or reform-school stills as thumbnails (Wiltwyck boys; Strange Situation stills).
- One-way-mirror "gotcha" photographs of a family who cannot consent now.
- Stock "happy family on a couch with a smiling therapist."
- Before/after faces.
- Anything that implies a WOW Therapies treatment room you have not photographed with consent.
- Bali / Iatmul photographs by Bateson used as if they were portraits of Bateson, or as decoration for "the family." Those images have named Balinese and Iatmul people in them. LOC exhibition files often carry usage conditions. Do not scrape them into a blog header.

## Alt text

Name what is in the frame. "Title page of John Elderkin Bell, Family Group Therapy, Public Health Monograph 64, U.S. Government Printing Office, 1961." Not "the birth of family therapy gazes into the American home."

For type-only posts: no empty `<img>`. The heading is the image.

## WordPress featured image

If `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` says `no-portrait` or `confirm`, the featured image is a **series template** (wordmark + essay title), not a guessed face. Build one template; reuse it.

On upload day, open the Commons or museum URL again. Paste the credit block from `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` into the media caption **and** under the image in the post.

## Color and crop

Do not colorize PD photographs and call them contemporary. Do not crop a group so that a bystander becomes "the" portrait. Do not flip a face for layout.

## Other lanes

Do not reuse SLPWOW speech-pathology graphics here. Do not reuse general-psychotherapy Freud/Jung portraits as if they founded family therapy. Different pack, different eye.

## If the graphics agent and the writer disagree

The license table wins. A beautiful unlicensed face does not ship.
