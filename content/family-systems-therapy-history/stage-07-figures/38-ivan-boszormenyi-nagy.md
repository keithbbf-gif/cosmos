Ivan Boszormenyi-Nagy was born in 1920 in Hungary and died in 2007. He came to the United States after the war and built, at the Eastern Pennsylvania Psychiatric Institute and in Philadelphia teaching rooms, a therapy that talked about ethics when other shops talked about strategy.

*Invisible Loyalties* (1973) with Geraldine Spark and *Between Give and Take* (1986) with Barbara Krasner are the public objects. This page will not let the co-authors fall off. Founder stories drop women who held the other end of the sentence. The sentence in 1973 is about debts a child should not have to pay.
