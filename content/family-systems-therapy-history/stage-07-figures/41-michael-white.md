**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Michael White was born in 1948 and died in 2008. He worked in Adelaide, including in psychiatric hospital cultures he later argued with, and he built, with colleagues at Dulwich Centre, a shop the North Atlantic had to import. Importing is not inventing. American weekends that start the story in California have lost the map.

*Narrative Means to Therapeutic Ends* (1990), with David Epston, is the public object. Epston is living (verified 2026): public books only, no scrape, no gossip. *Maps of Narrative Practice* (2007) is the late teaching book, a founder trying to leave a map that is not a cult. Maps that become cults are a historical risk in any school that talks about stories. White knew the risk. Knowing is not the same as preventing.

He liked documents, outsider witnesses, and a politics of whose description gets to be official. That politics can serve a child who arrived already named as the problem. It can also become a style that never risks a direct sentence. Both possibilities are in the record. This pack will not pick a winner.
