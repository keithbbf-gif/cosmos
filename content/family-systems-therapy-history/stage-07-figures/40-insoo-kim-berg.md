Insoo Kim Berg was born in 1934 in Korea and died in 2007. The sister survey already has a calendar portrait. This deepen holds the door she opened into public agencies: child welfare, schools, rooms that would never see a Palo Alto hotel.

She trained in social work in Chicago and then built, with Steve de Shazer and colleagues at the Brief Family Therapy Center in Milwaukee, a method that could be taught on a Thursday. Thursday is a political fact. Caseworkers do not get a two-year institute. They get a Thursday. Berg wrote as if that constraint were a design problem, not a spiritual failure.

*Family-Based Services* (Norton, 1994) is the object this page names first, because it is aimed at the rooms that actually met her. de Shazer's 1985 and 1988 books are the neighboring objects. This pack will not collapse her into a partner credit. It will also not invent a marriage story. Public books only.
