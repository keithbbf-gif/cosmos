**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Nancy Boyd-Franklin published *Black Families in Therapy* with Guilford in 1989. Celia Jaes Falicov published *Latino Families in Therapy* in 1998 (second edition 2014). Kenneth V. Hardy has spent public decades arguing that race is not a special-interest hour. Froma Walsh's resilience books (1998 and later) tried to move the field from damage-as-identity to strength-as-description — a move that can help and can also become a cheer. These are different books. This page is the hallway they share: after the mirror, who was the specimen?

Early family-therapy tapes are full of households that did not look like the men behind the glass. Poor families, Black families, immigrant families, families already in a court file — they were available to clinics. Availability is not consent in the later sense. A 1967 Wiltwyck book can be a solidarity object and a seeing object at once. Holding both is the job.

Boyd-Franklin wrote about kinship as wider than the nuclear diagram, about church, about the therapist's own race as a fact in the room. Those sentences are public and dated. This page will not turn them into a cultural-competence checklist. Checklists are how institutes metabolize a book they do not want to be changed by.

Falicov wrote about migration, the "ghosts" of the sending country, and the error of treating Latino as one family. Hardy wrote, in public lectures and books, about the rage and the silence American racial history puts on a teenager. Living writers: public documents only. No health speculation. No scrape.

Consent thickened. Ethics codes, IRB habits, a general nervousness about recording — these changed what a 2026 trainee is allowed to show a class. The change is incomplete. Workshop videos still circulate. This pack will not link them. Linking is a second seeing.

Class is not solved by a race module. A rural white household in a county court file and a Black household in a city clinic can share a position in front of a professional team and not share a history. Falicov's migration lens and Boyd-Franklin's kinship lens and a Wiltwyck rent ledger are three objects. This page refuses to blend them into "diversity."

No stock "multicultural family." Type: 1989, 1998, public Hardy lectures.

A small-city clinic in Southeast Arkansas already knows, if it is paying attention, that a family hour is a racial and class event even when no one names the event. Knowing that is not a protocol. This series will not teach you how to "address race in the first session." It will say the books exist, the glass had a politics, and a history site that skips this hallway has skipped the field.

WOW Therapies does not, by naming Boyd-Franklin, claim a specialty. Naming is the opposite of a specialty. It is a refusal to let 1974 talk as if 1989 had not been written.

## Three books, no blend

Boyd-Franklin 1989, Falicov 1998, Walsh 1998, Hardy in public: these are not "diversity." They are dated arguments about kinship, migration, strength-as-description, and race as a fact in the room. Blending them into one warm paragraph is how a field stays unchanged.

Wiltwyck 1967 can be a solidarity object and a seeing object at once. Holding both is the job. Availability of poor families to a teaching clinic is not later consent. Later consent is incomplete. Workshop videos still move. This pack will not link them.

A rural white household in a county file and a Black household in a city clinic can share a position in front of a professional team and not share a history. Shared position is not shared story. This page refuses the blend.

## Walsh as a neighboring caution

Froma Walsh's resilience books tried to move the field from damage-as-identity to strength-as-description. The move can help. It can also become a cheer that a court or a school uses to close a file. This page names 1998 as a neighboring object, not as a slogan.

Hardy's public teaching and Boyd-Franklin's 1989 book and Falicov's 1998 book remain three doors. A history site that uses one door and calls the house finished has finished nothing.

## IRBs and the incomplete thickening

University IRBs and clinic ethics codes thickened recording rules after the teaching-tape century. Thickening is incomplete. This writer will not invent a lawsuit year. Incompleteness is the honest word. Honesty is why this page refuses to link a workshop video even when the video is famous.

Three books remain three books. Blend is the refuse.
