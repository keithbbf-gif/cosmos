**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Tom Andersen published "The Reflecting Team: Dialogue and Meta-Dialogue in Clinical Work" in *Family Process* 26, no. 4 (1987): 415–428. The paper is a northern European answer to a North Atlantic habit: secret talk behind glass. In Andersen's telling, the team came out, or the family listened to the team on purpose, and the conversation about the conversation became part of the hour.

This page will not teach you to run a reflecting team. A team is a staffing pattern, a courtesy culture, and a consent practice. A blog is none of those.

Andersen was writing from Tromsø and a Scandinavian welfare world in which a team's politeness had a different political weather than a 1970s Philadelphia teaching clinic. American imports turned the paper into a module: two rooms, a switch, a ritual of "wondering." Modules can be respectful. They can also be a new theater that still treats the family as an audience. Andersen's later interviews worried about technique. Worry is part of the history.

The paper sits next to the Milan split and next to Harlene Anderson and Harry Goolishian's collaborative-language work as a 1980s doubt about expert seeing. This pack will not merge those rooms. Collaborative language systems is a neighboring American/Galveston object. Andersen is a *Family Process* pagination. Borders keep a syllabus honest.

No mirror photographs of families. Type: Andersen, 1987.

A small-city clinic will almost never have a reflecting team. The inheritance, if any, is a question: do we talk about people as if they cannot hear us? History cannot staff your agency. It can keep 1987 from sounding like a hotel trick.

## Tromsø is not a hotel

Andersen wrote from a northern university and a welfare culture in which a team's politeness had a different political weather than a 1970s Philadelphia teaching clinic. American imports turned 1987 into a hotel module: two rooms, a switch, a ritual of wondering. Modules can be respectful. They can also be a new theater that still treats the family as an audience.

Harlene Anderson and Harry Goolishian's collaborative-language work is a neighboring Galveston/American object. This pack will not merge Tromsø and Galveston. Borders keep a syllabus honest.

A county clinic will almost never staff a reflecting team. The inheritance is a question about talking as if people cannot hear us. Questions are not floor plans. Floor plans are how a blog becomes a protocol. This series does not publish treatment protocols.

Andersen died in 2007. The pagination — *Family Process* 26(4) — is the object this page wants to outlive the module.

## Meta-dialogue without a kit

"Dialogue and meta-dialogue" in the 1987 subtitle is easy to turn into a kit: first we talk, then we talk about the talk. Kits are protocols. This series does not publish treatment protocols. It publishes a pagination and a northern address.

American reflecting-team weekends sometimes forgot the paper's worry about technique. Forgetting the worry is how a revolt against secret seeing becomes a new secret seeing with better manners. Manners are not the 1987 point. The point was whether a family had to be a specimen. Specimens do not belong on this site, even as stock.

## Glass turned around, not removed

1987 did not abolish the mirror. It argued with secrecy. American weekends sometimes heard "better manners" and bought a switch. Switches are hardware. Hardware is not the paper. The paper is *Family Process* 26(4). Pagination outranks a hotel floor plan.

A county clinic without glass can still ask whether it talks about people as if they cannot hear. Asking is not a team. This series does not staff your agency and does not publish a floor plan.

## 2007 close

Andersen died in 2007. The module outlived him. This essay would rather *Family Process* 26(4) outlive the module. Tromsø remains the weather. A hotel switch remains an import. County clinics remain unstaffed for teams. Unstaffed is not a failure to be 1987.

## Import without the welfare state

American weekends imported a Scandinavian courtesy without the staffing that made the courtesy cheap. Courtesy without staff becomes a ritual two people perform in a borrowed room and then call a team. This page will keep saying *Family Process* 26(4) until the ritual cannot talk over the paper.
