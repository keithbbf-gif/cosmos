**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

The American Association of Marriage Counselors organized in 1942. The later American Association for Marriage and Family Therapy is a successor name, a larger tent, and a license-adjacent guild. Use AAMFT's own dated pages if you print a year for a bylaw. This pack will not invent a convention vote.

AFTA — the American Family Therapy Academy — dates itself to 1977. An academy is not an association. An association is not a state board. A state board is not a school. American family therapy spent the last third of the twentieth century braiding those four until a rural client could not tell whether "LMFT" meant a theory, a test, or a trade group. Braiding is how a workforce is built. Unbraiding is this page's job.

*Family Process* (1962) was the journal. The institutes — Ackerman, MRI, Georgetown, Philadelphia, Milwaukee, Dulwich, Milan — were the shops. The associations were the mailing lists and the ethics codes. The boards were the examinations and the continuing-education hours. A person could belong to all four and still not practice like Bowen. A person could practice like Satir and never join. Membership is not a method.

Manuals arrived unevenly. Structural and strategic shops had tapes that functioned as manuals. MRI and Milwaukee wrote short books that could be taught on a Thursday. Insurance later asked for a diagnosis on an identified patient even when the theory refused the idea of an identified patient. That request is a historical joke with a body count in paperwork. This pack will not tell you how to bill. It will say the joke happened.

Evidence culture — trials, manuals, "empirically supported" lists — treated some couple and family protocols as countable and left others as traditions. Countable is a political status. It is not a moral one. A 1978 Whitaker book will never look like a 2004 couple-therapy manual. Managed care bought the manual. This site will not call that purchase a scientific court.

No AAMFT logo without permission. Type: 1942, 1962, 1977, and the word *license* as a committee fact.

A small-city clinician in Arkansas lives under a state board whether or not they love 1956. Living under a board is not "doing family therapy." It is a legal location. This series does not offer a path to licensure. It offers the difference between a guild and a school.

## Four objects, one braid

Journal, shop, guild, board. A person could belong to all four and still not practice like Bowen. A person could practice like Satir and never join. Membership is not a method. This page exists to unbraid the four so a rural client can tell an ethics code from a theory.

AAMFT's own dated pages should win any year-fight about a bylaw. This writer will not invent a convention vote. AFTA 1977 is an academy, not a board. State boards are political-administrative objects. They do not prove a 1956 paper.

If wowtherapies.com ever names a family-therapy service, that page needs counsel and a different review. This page is heritage. Heritage does not bill. Heritage does not become a path to licensure.

## Identified patient, still

Insurance portals still want one name. Family-therapy theory spent fifty years arguing with that name. The argument did not win the portal. Portals are a historical joke with a body count in paperwork. This pack will not teach you how to fill the portal. It will say the joke happened, and that a license is not a victory over 1956. It is a legal location.

## Continuing education as weather

CE hours are how a guild stays a guild. Hours are not a school. A Saturday on "systems" can be Bowen, Satir, a billing webinar, or a miracle-question leak. This pack exists so a Saturday cannot pretend to be 1956. 1942, 1962, 1977 remain the institutional years. Use the associations' own pages before you print a bylaw.

## LMFT as a legal location

An LMFT credential is a legal location, not a proof that 1956 was right. Locations have tests and fees and CE weather. This URL will not sell a path to a test. 1942, 1962, 1977 remain the institutional years. Heritage does not bill.
