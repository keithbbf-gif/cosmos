**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

A reader should not have to guess which folder holds the object they came for. This page is a map. It is not a merger.

**Whole psychotherapy house** — `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/`. Pinel, Freud, Rogers, Beck as a survey figure, Bowlby, Fanon, Herman. The systems hallway there is one essay: `families-systems-therapy`. Satir, Minuchin, and Berg appear there as calendar portraits. Use those pages if you want the house. Use this folder if you want the hallway's rooms.

**CBT deepen** — `content/cbt-history-deep/`. Thought records as historical objects, IAPT as a political object, Beck's 1961 inventory and 1979 manual. Do not look for them here. A family hour that borrowed a thought record is a later hybrid, not a 1956 paper.

**ACT and mindfulness** — `content/act-mindfulness-therapy-history/`. Hayes 1999, Kabat-Zinn 1979, Teasdale 2000. Linehan appears there only in a mindfulness frame. She is not a family-systems founder. Do not file her here to thicken a pack.

**SLPWOW speech-pathology heritage** — Van Riper, fluency, swallowing, school SLP. Caregivers sit in those hours. Sitting is not a reason to rewrite Wiltwyck as a feeding clinic. Keep the lanes.

**Trauma-informed deepen** (when that pack is on the shelf) — Herman and the later clinic words. This folder mentions tapes and consent. It does not rewrite trauma theory.

Cross-links should name slugs, not paste paragraphs. If an editor finds a Freud paragraph in this folder, cut it. If a writer finds a genogram how-to in the survey, that is the survey's fence, not this page's job to fix by adding another how-to.

WOW Therapies does not become a university by hosting four history lanes. It becomes a site that can send a curious reader to a dated object instead of a booking widget. Sending is the map's only verb.
