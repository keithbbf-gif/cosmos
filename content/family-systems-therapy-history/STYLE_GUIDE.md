# Style guide — WOW Therapies, family systems history series

Educational history for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com). Complementary
to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` (one survey essay on systems) and
to the SLPWOW speech-pathology series. Do not retell Van Riper here. Do not
retell the full Freud-to-Herman calendar here. Do not retell Beck's manuals
except as a neighboring object.

`voice: essay` means the writer draft aimed at this file. A later human editor
may tighten grammar and guardrails. Neither value is a byline.

## Who is speaking

A historically literate magazine writer who has sat in enough family hours to
know that a theory is not a household. Warm without being pastoral. Precise
without being a textbook. The reader may be a curious client, a family member,
a student, or a clinician. Do not talk down. Do not talk shop as if the room
were empty of the people who have to drive home together.

WOW Therapies is a Southeast Arkansas practice brand. These essays are
**heritage education**, not a menu of services.

## Hard bans (fail the draft)

Do not use:

- "In today's rapidly evolving…"
- "It's important to note"
- "delve" / "delve into"
- "landscape" as a metaphor for a field
- "robust"
- "leverage"
- "unlock"
- "cutting-edge"
- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "In conclusion"
- "Moreover" / "Furthermore" stacks
- "Whether you're a … or a …"
- empty dualities ("not just X, but Y"; "both an art and a science")
- throat-clearing first paragraphs that delay the first fact
- three-beat synonym piles ("heal, grow, and thrive")
- "the father of…" unless you are quoting a myth, not endorsing it
- "gold-standard treatment" as marketing
- invented quotes
- AI-generated faces
- "try this at home," "self-treat," session scripts
- "holistic wellness journey," "let's dive in," "let's explore"
- schizophrenogenic-mother, refrigerator-mother, or "the family causes
  schizophrenia" as fact
- a genogram, miracle question, circular-question list, or chair-moving
  script a reader could run at the kitchen table

Also drop the cousins: "navigate," "tapestry," "plethora," "utilize,"
"harness," "elevate your," "the future of," "at the forefront,"
"empower," "in this article we will explore," "journey of healing" as filler.

## Do this instead

1. Open on a dated event, a room, a document, or a named quarrel. The first
   sentence should be checkable.
2. Mix sentence length. A short sentence after a long one.
3. Name the book, paper, hospital, or society. *Families and Family Therapy*
   (1974). Bateson et al., *Behavioral Science*, 1956. Wiltwyck. *Family
   Process* 1(1), 1962.
4. Mark uncertainty. `[CITE NEEDED]` or "historians disagree" beats a clean
   falsehood. MRI's founding year is 1958 in Jackson's institutional sentences
   and 1959 in some later encyclopedias; say so.
5. Put living people in public documents only.
6. Give women's work, Black family scholarship, Latino family scholarship, and
   the feminist revolt full essays, not a closing paragraph titled "diversity."
7. Stop when the piece has said the thing. Target 900–1,600 words. Cut the
   recap paragraph if it only restates the headings. Deepen with dated scenes,
   not with recap. `tools/lint_claims.py` fails a draft under 900 words.

## Claims posture

See `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`. History may describe a method. History may not
teach a reader to self-administer it. No session scripts, no blank genograms,
no "try the miracle question tonight."

Efficacy sentences, if any, stay historical and sourced and never become
"family therapy will help your household."

Living people get extra care: no gossip, no unsourced health claims.

## Portraits

Public domain or Creative Commons only. Document the file in
`PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`. If there is no clean license, use type or a period
object — never a generated face. See `PHOTO_NOTES.md`. Almost every
twentieth-century family-therapy figure in this pack is `no-portrait`. That
is correct. That is the assignment.

## Series voice, in one paragraph

Write as if *The New Yorker* and *Psychotherapy Networker* had to share a desk,
and the desk was in a small-city clinic that still answers its own phone. The
past is allowed to be strange. The present is not allowed to be sold. A
genogram is a historical object. It is not this page's homework.

## Footer (every article)

Keep the educational note already in the draft. Keep the **Claims box**. In
WordPress they become reusable blocks. Do not shrink them to a gray afterthought.
