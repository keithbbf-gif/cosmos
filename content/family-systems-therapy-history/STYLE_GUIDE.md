# Style guide — WOW Therapies, family systems history series

This pack is educational history for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com): how the unit of treatment became more than one person. It is **not** the general psychotherapy pack and **not** the SLPWOW speech-pathology series.

`voice_check: human` means the writer draft passed this file. `voice_check: edited` means a later editor read the piece aloud and tightened grammar, voice, and guardrails. Neither value is a byline and must not appear on the public page.

## Who is speaking

A historically literate magazine writer who has sat in enough family hours to know that a theory is not a household. Warm without being pastoral. Precise without being a textbook. The reader may be a curious client, a family member, a student, or a clinician. Do not talk down. Do not talk shop as if the room were empty of the people who have to drive home together.

WOW Therapies is a Southeast Arkansas practice brand. These essays are **heritage education**, not a menu of services and not a claim that the clinic practices every school named here.

## Hard bans (fail the draft)

Do not use:

- "In today's rapidly evolving…"
- "It's important to note"
- "delve" / "delve into"
- "landscape" as a metaphor for a field
- "robust"
- "leverage"
- "unlock"
- "cutting-edge"
- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "In conclusion"
- "Moreover" / "Furthermore" stacks
- "Whether you're a … or a …"
- empty dualities ("not just X, but Y"; "both an art and a science")
- throat-clearing first paragraphs that delay the first fact
- three-beat synonym piles ("heal, grow, and thrive")
- "the father of…" unless a contemporary actually used that phrase and you are quoting the myth, not endorsing it
- "gold-standard treatment" as marketing
- invented quotes
- AI-generated faces or "period photographs" that never existed
- schizophrenogenic-mother, refrigerator-mother, or "the family causes schizophrenia" as fact
- a genogram, miracle question, circular-question list, or chair-moving script a reader could run at the kitchen table

Also drop the cousins: "navigate," "tapestry," "plethora," "utilize," "harness," "elevate your," "the future of," "at the forefront," "a holistic approach," "empower," "in this article we will explore," "journey of healing" as filler.

## Do this instead

1. Open on a dated event, a room, a document, or a named quarrel. The first sentence should be checkable.
2. Mix sentence length. A short sentence after a long one. Do not write every paragraph as topic sentence + three examples + wrap.
3. Name the book, paper, hospital, or society. *Families and Family Therapy* (1974). Bateson et al. 1956. Wiltwyck. The 1962 first issue of *Family Process*.
4. Mark uncertainty. `[CITE NEEDED]` or a plain "historians disagree" beats a clean falsehood. MRI's opening year is 1958 in Jackson's own institutional sentences and 1959 in some later encyclopedias; say so.
5. Put Bowen, Minuchin, Satir, Haley, and the Milan team in their century. Do not offer them as current treatment manuals.
6. Give women's work, Black family scholarship, Latino family scholarship, and the feminist revolt full essays, not a closing paragraph titled "diversity."
7. Stop when the piece has said the thing. Target 1,000–1,800 words for figures, 1,200–2,000 for era essays. Cut the recap paragraph if it only restates the headings. `check_pack.py` fails an era under 1,200 words or a figure under 1,000. Deepen with dated scenes, not with recap.

## Claims posture

See `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`. History may describe a method. History may not teach a reader to self-administer it. No session scripts, no skill worksheets, no "try this at home" protocols, no blank genogram to fill in.

Efficacy sentences, if any, stay historical and sourced ("Henggeler's early MST trials reported…") and never become "structural therapy will fix your teenager."

Living people get extra care: no gossip, no unsourced health claims. Public books, public lectures, and public interviews only.

## Portraits

Public domain or Creative Commons only. Document the file in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`. If there is no clean license, use type or a period object — never a generated face. See `PHOTO_NOTES.md`. Almost every mid- and late-twentieth-century family therapist fails that test. Type is not a failure. It is the honest choice.

## Series voice, in one paragraph

Write as if *The New Yorker* and *Family Process* had to share a desk, and the desk was in a small-city clinic that still answers its own phone. The past is allowed to be strange. The present is not allowed to be sold. A theory of families can injure as well as help; say so when the record says so.
