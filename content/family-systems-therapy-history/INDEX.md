# Editorial calendar — WOW Therapies, family systems therapy history

Forty-four essays: sixteen era pieces and twenty-eight figures. Status in YAML is `draft` (writer-cleared for **staging** review, not for the live sitemap). Nothing in this folder is cleared to write itself onto wowtherapies.com.

All files live under `content/family-systems-therapy-history/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`.

This pack is the family-systems heritage lane. It is complementary to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` (general psychotherapy) and to the SLPWOW speech-pathology pack. Do not merge the three calendars.

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-four essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):

| Wave | Suggested order | Pieces | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — the unit changes | 1–6 weeks | 01, 02, 17, 04, 18, 19 | Before the family hour, child guidance, Ackerman, Palo Alto, Bateson, Jackson |
| 2 — three American houses | 7–14 | 06, 07, 08, 21, 22, 23 | Bowen, Satir, structural; the three figure essays |
| 3 — strategy, Milan, context | 15–22 | 05, 09, 10, 11, 20, 26, 27, 25 | MRI, Haley/Madanes, Milan, Nagy |
| 4 — revolt and brief | 23–30 | 12, 13, 14, 30, 31, 32, 33, 35 | Feminist revolt, Milwaukee, narrative, Berg, White, Epston, Carter |
| 5 — the room after the glass | 31–38 | 15, 16, 34, 38, 39, 41, 43, 44 | Consent, manuals, McGoldrick, Boyd-Franklin, Falicov, Aponte, Walsh, Hardy |
| 6 — remaining figures | 39–48 | 03, 24, 28, 29, 36, 37, 40, 42 | Schizophrenogenic aftermath, Whitaker, Weakland, Watzlawick, Hoffman, Johnson, Boss, Wynne |

Hold any piece that still carries `[CITE NEEDED]` or `[VERIFY]` on a date you would print.

`check_pack.py` fails an era under 1,200 words or a figure under 1,000. Deepen with dated scenes, not recap.
