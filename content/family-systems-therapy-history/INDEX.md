# Editorial calendar — WOW Therapies, family systems therapy history

Forty-six essays in **seven stages**. Status in YAML is `draft` (writer-cleared for **staging** review, not for the live sitemap). Nothing in this folder is cleared to write itself onto wowtherapies.com.

All files live under `content/family-systems-therapy-history/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`.

This pack is the family-systems heritage lane. It is complementary to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` (general psychotherapy) and to the SLPWOW speech-pathology pack. Do not merge the calendars.

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-six essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):

| Wave | Stages | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 01 | Fence, reading rules, sister pack, portrait refusal |
| 2 | 02 | Precursors and the 1956–1962 objects |
| 3 | 03–04 | American houses, then strategy / Milan / context |
| 4 | 05 | Feminist revolt, Milwaukee, narrative, the mirror after consent |
| 5 | 06–07 | Institutions, then founder and later-figure essays |

Hold any piece that still carries `[CITE NEEDED]` or `[VERIFY]` on a date you would print.

`tools/lint_claims.py` fails a draft under 900 words. Deepen with dated scenes, not recap.
