# Bibliography — family systems history pack

Consolidated sources named in the essays. This is a working shelf, not an exhaustive literature review. Dates are publication years of the edition the writer used as a historical marker. Mark `[VERIFY]` if a later editor cannot match a title to a library record before live publication.

Last assembled: 2026-09-14.

## Precursors and child guidance

- Healy, William. *The Individual Delinquent*. Boston: Little, Brown, 1915. (U.S. 1915; title page is PD.)
- Judge Baker Foundation. Early Boston reports, 1917–.
- Mudd, Emily. Marriage Council of Philadelphia, founded 1932 (institutional history).
- Bowlby, John. "The Study and Reduction of Group Tensions in the Family." *Human Relations* 2 (1949): 123–128. (Family-systems frame only; full Bowlby life is the general psychotherapy pack.)
- Bell, John Elderkin. "Family Group Therapy as a Treatment Method." Paper, Eastern Psychological Association, Boston, 24 April 1953. Reprinted with comment in *Family Process* 6 (1967).
- Bell, John Elderkin. *Family Group Therapy*. Public Health Monograph No. 64. Washington, D.C.: U.S. Government Printing Office, 1961. (U.S. government work; PD.)
- Midelfort, C. F. *The Family in Psychotherapy*. New York: McGraw-Hill, 1957.

## Schizophrenia, mothers, and the later correction

- Fromm-Reichmann, Frieda. "Notes on the Development of Treatment of Schizophrenics by Psychoanalytic Psychotherapy." *Psychiatry* 11 (1948): 263–273.
- Lidz, Theodore, Stephen Fleck, and Alice R. Cornelison. *Schizophrenia and the Family*. New York: International Universities Press, 1965.
- Wynne, Lyman C., Irving M. Ryckoff, Juliana Day, and Stanley I. Hirsch. "Pseudo-Mutuality in the Family Relations of Schizophrenics." *Psychiatry* 21 (1958): 205–220.
- Bateson, Gregory, Don D. Jackson, Jay Haley, and John Weakland. "Toward a Theory of Schizophrenia." *Behavioral Science* 1 (1956): 251–264.
- Bateson, Gregory, Don D. Jackson, Jay Haley, and John Weakland. "A Note on the Double Bind — 1962." *Family Process* 2 (1963): 154–161.
- Brown, George W., J. L. T. Birley, and John K. Wing. Expressed-emotion papers, 1960s–1970s (relapse prediction; not a maternal-guilt theory).
- Vaughn, Christine, and Julian Leff. Expressed-emotion and relapse papers, mid-1970s.
- Weinstein, Deborah. *The Pathological Family: Postwar America and the Rise of Family Therapy*. Ithaca: Cornell University Press, 2013.

## Palo Alto, MRI, communication

- Bateson, Gregory. *Naven*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1936.
- Bateson, Gregory. *Steps to an Ecology of Mind*. San Francisco: Chandler, 1972.
- Jackson, Don D. "The Question of Family Homeostasis." *Psychiatric Quarterly Supplement* 31 (1957): 79–90.
- Jackson, Don D., ed. *The Etiology of Schizophrenia*. New York: Basic Books, 1960.
- Watzlawick, Paul, Janet Beavin Bavelas, and Don D. Jackson. *Pragmatics of Human Communication*. New York: Norton, 1967.
- Watzlawick, Paul, John Weakland, and Richard Fisch. *Change: Principles of Problem Formation and Problem Resolution*. New York: Norton, 1974.
- Weakland, John, Richard Fisch, Paul Watzlawick, and Arthur Bodin. "Brief Therapy: Focused Problem Resolution." *Family Process* 13 (1974): 141–168.
- Haley, Jay. *Strategies of Psychotherapy*. New York: Grune & Stratton, 1963.
- Haley, Jay. *Uncommon Therapy: The Psychiatric Techniques of Milton H. Erickson, M.D.* New York: Norton, 1973.
- Ray, Wendel A., and others. Later MRI institutional histories. Opening year: Jackson's own 1958 sentences; some later encyclopedias print 1959. Mark both.

## Bowen

- Bowen, Murray. Papers from the NIMH Family Study, 1954–1959; later collected and reissued by the Bowen Center.
- Bowen, Murray. "On the Differentiation of Self." Presented 1967; later in *Family Therapy in Clinical Practice*.
- Bowen, Murray. *Family Therapy in Clinical Practice*. New York: Jason Aronson, 1978.
- Kerr, Michael E., and Murray Bowen. *Family Evaluation*. New York: Norton, 1988.
- The Bowen Center for the Study of the Family. Institutional biography of the NIMH years and the 1975 Georgetown Family Center.

## Satir and experiential

- Satir, Virginia. *Conjoint Family Therapy*. Palo Alto: Science and Behavior Books, 1964.
- Satir, Virginia. *Peoplemaking*. Palo Alto: Science and Behavior Books, 1972.
- Napier, Augustus Y., and Carl Whitaker. *The Family Crucible*. New York: Harper & Row, 1978.
- Whitaker, Carl A., and William M. Bumberry. Later collected papers on symbolic-experiential work.
- Avanta Network / later Satir institutes: posthumous training history, not a license to scrape workshop stills.

## Structural

- Minuchin, Salvador, Braulio Montalvo, Bernard G. Guerney, Jr., Bernice L. Rosman, and Florence Schumer. *Families of the Slums*. New York: Basic Books, 1967.
- Minuchin, Salvador. *Families and Family Therapy*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1974.
- Minuchin, Salvador, Bernice L. Rosman, and Lester Baker. *Psychosomatic Families: Anorexia Nervosa in Context*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1978.
- Minuchin Center for the Family. Institutional biography (Wiltwyck c. 1957–1965; Philadelphia Child Guidance director 1965–1975; emeritus to 1981).
- Aponte, Harry J. *Bread & Spirit: Therapy with the New Poor*. New York: Norton, 1994.

## Strategic and Milan

- Haley, Jay. *Problem-Solving Therapy*. San Francisco: Jossey-Bass, 1976.
- Madanes, Cloé. *Strategic Family Therapy*. San Francisco: Jossey-Bass, 1981.
- Selvini Palazzoli, Mara, Luigi Boscolo, Gianfranco Cecchin, and Giuliana Prata. *Paradox and Counterparadox*. New York: Jason Aronson, 1978 (Italian earlier in the 1970s).
- Later Milan split: Selvini Palazzoli and Prata's "invariant prescription"; Boscolo and Cecchin's later work on curiosity and irreverence.
- Cecchin, Gianfranco. "Hypothesizing, Circularity, and Neutrality Revisited." *Family Process* 26 (1987): 405–413.

## Contextual

- Boszormenyi-Nagy, Ivan, and Geraldine M. Spark. *Invisible Loyalties*. New York: Harper & Row, 1973.
- Boszormenyi-Nagy, Ivan, and Barbara R. Krasner. *Between Give and Take: A Clinical Guide to Contextual Therapy*. New York: Brunner/Mazel, 1986.

## Profession, journal, association

- *Family Process* 1, no. 1 (1962). Founding project of Nathan Ackerman, Don D. Jackson, and Jay Haley (first editor, through 1969).
- Broderick, Carlfred B., and Sandra S. Schrader. History chapter on AAMC (1942) → AAMFC (1970) → AAMFT (1978).
- American Association for Marriage and Family Therapy. Institutional history pages.

## Feminist revolt and gender

- Hare-Mustin, Rachel T. "A Feminist Approach to Family Therapy." *Family Process* 17 (1978): 181–194.
- Hare-Mustin, Rachel T. "Family Therapy May Be Dangerous for Your Health." *Professional Psychology* 11 (1980): 935–938.
- Walters, Marianne, Betty Carter, Peggy Papp, and Olga Silverstein. *The Invisible Web: Gender Patterns in Family Relationships*. New York: Guilford, 1988 (paperback catalogues often 1991).
- Goldner, Virginia. Gender papers in *Family Process*, mid-1980s.
- Carter, Betty, and Monica McGoldrick, eds. *The Changing Family Life Cycle* (later *The Expanded Family Life Cycle*). Various editions from 1980 onward.
- Smith College. Betty Carter papers finding aid (Ackerman training 1970–72; Women's Project 1977; Family Institute of Westchester 1977–1997; d. 2012).
