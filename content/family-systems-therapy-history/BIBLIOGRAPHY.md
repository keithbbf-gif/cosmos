# Bibliography — family systems therapy history

Named public documents this pack actually opened, or named as objects a reader can open. This is an honesty list, not a graduate syllabus. A missing page number is a missing page number. Do not upgrade a secondary sentence into a primary one.

Last verified as a writer list: 2026-09-14. A later editor should re-open the ones marked `[VERIFY]` before live import.

## Precursors and the unit

- Ackerman, Nathan W. *The Psychodynamics of Family Life*. New York: Basic Books, 1958.
- Bell, John Elderkin. *Family Group Therapy*. Public Health Monograph No. 64. Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Health, Education, and Welfare, 1961.
- Fromm-Reichmann, Frieda. "Notes on the Development of Treatment of Schizophrenics by Psychoanalytic Psychotherapy." *Psychiatry* 11 (1948): 263–273. (The "schizophrenogenic mother" sentence lives here as a historical injury, not as a finding this pack endorses.)
- Lidz, Theodore, Stephen Fleck, and Alice R. Cornelison. *Schizophrenia and the Family*. New York: International Universities Press, 1965.
- Wynne, Lyman C., Irving M. Ryckoff, Juliana Day, and Stanley I. Hirsch. "Pseudo-Mutuality in the Family Relations of Schizophrenics." *Psychiatry* 21 (1958): 205–220.

## Palo Alto and MRI

- Bateson, Gregory, Don D. Jackson, Jay Haley, and John Weakland. "Toward a Theory of Schizophrenia." *Behavioral Science* 1, no. 4 (1956): 251–264.
- Bateson, Gregory. *Naven*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1936.
- Bateson, Gregory. *Steps to an Ecology of Mind*. San Francisco: Chandler, 1972.
- Jackson, Don D., ed. *Communication, Family, and Marriage*. Palo Alto: Science and Behavior Books, 1968.
- Watzlawick, Paul, Janet Beavin Bavelas, and Don D. Jackson. *Pragmatics of Human Communication*. New York: W. W. Norton, 1967.
- Watzlawick, Paul, John Weakland, and Richard Fisch. *Change: Principles of Problem Formation and Problem Resolution*. New York: W. W. Norton, 1974.
- Weakland, John H., Richard Fisch, Paul Watzlawick, and Arthur M. Bodin. "Brief Therapy: Focused Problem Resolution." *Family Process* 13, no. 2 (1974): 141–168.
- Mental Research Institute. Institutional histories (MRI's own dated pages). Founding year: 1958 in Jackson-era sentences; 1959 in some later encyclopedias. Do not flatten.

## Journals and associations

- *Family Process* 1, no. 1 (March 1962). Founding editors: Jay Haley (editor), with Ackerman and Jackson as the institutional parents. `[VERIFY]` exact masthead titles against a library copy before printing a caption.
- American Association for Marriage and Family Therapy. Successor name history from the American Association of Marriage Counselors (founded 1942). Use AAMFT's own dated institutional pages.
- American Family Therapy Academy (AFTA). Founded 1977. Use AFTA's own dated pages.

## Bowen

- Bowen, Murray. "On the Differentiation of Self." Presented 1967; later collected.
- Bowen, Murray. *Family Therapy in Clinical Practice*. New York: Jason Aronson, 1978.
- Kerr, Michael E., and Murray Bowen. *Family Evaluation*. New York: W. W. Norton, 1988.
- Friedman, Edwin H. *Generation to Generation*. New York: Guilford, 1985. (A neighboring object: congregations as emotional systems.)
