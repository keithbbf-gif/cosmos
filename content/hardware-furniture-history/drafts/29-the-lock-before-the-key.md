A lock is a social device before it is a puzzle.

We tell the Egyptian story because it is a good story: a wooden bolt, wooden pins that drop into holes, a wooden key that lifts the pins. The principle is real. You will not meet that lock on a walnut secretary. You will meet a hasp, a ward, a bolt that throws into a slot. You will meet a household.

Furniture locks, for most of the time people have had furniture worth locking, are about the people inside the house. A servant. A child. A clerk. The version of you that should not open the till. A neighbor is a later anxiety. A professional burglar is a still later one, and when he arrives the locks get names and patents and shop-window challenges. Before that, a chest has a hasp and a padlock, or a built-in lock whose key is a bit of iron that only has to be slightly right.

The key is not even always a key in our sense. A pin through a hasp. A sliding bolt on the inside of a cupboard that only opens if you already are inside the room. A hidden catch. These are locks. They do not photograph as well as a Bramah pipe.

I am not romantic about the old security. A lot of it is theater, and the next essay is about that. I am romantic about the admission: this drawer is not for everyone. A house that locks a drawer is a house that has a private. The private might be money, or letters, or tea, or a bottle. The lock does not care. The lock is the word *no* in iron.

Padlocks on a hasp are portable law. You can take the law to market. A built-in lock stays with the object and makes the object a small building. Chests that traveled liked padlocks. Desks that stayed liked tills. The difference is a lifestyle you can still read on the iron.

When we restore a desk and leave the lock seized, we leave the word rusted shut. When we yank the lock out because we lost the key and we want the drawer, we delete the word. I have done the second thing. I have also spent an evening with oil and a broken key and a swear. The swear is part of the social device. Someone, once, was not supposed to get in. Tonight that someone is you.
