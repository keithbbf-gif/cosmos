Thomas Chippendale drew brasses. That is not the same as making them.

The 1762 *Director* has plates that cabinetmakers still steal from. Rococo, pierced, willow, the backplate as a little riot around a bail. In the notes on another plate he describes brass work generally made from wooden moulds. He knew founding. An invoice to Harewood House, 26 August 1774, charges for carving patterns in wood, then casting, then chasing. So: sometimes the shop, or a founder under the shop's eye, did the whole dance. Pattern, pour, chase. The best rooms got the chasing. The lesser bedrooms, if the reading of that trade is right, got the same pattern with less finishing. That is not a moral failure. That is a business.

It is still a mistake to say "Chippendale hardware" as if the man stood at a crucible for every chest that wears his silhouette. St. Martin's Lane had cabinet founders. William Bent lived in the same lane. Birmingham could send a crate. Chippendale was shrewd enough to buy what a specialist could make faster. The *Director* is a pattern book. Pattern books are how you get other people's labor to look like your name.

The backplate is the part people mean. A bail without a plate is a handle. A plate is a face. Chippendale-period plates go wide, get pierced, stop relying on chasing the way the earlier William and Mary thin plates did. The shape is the decoration. Later Federal work will stamp and roll and put an eagle on. This moment is still about piercing and silhouette.

If you are looking at a "Chippendale" chest in the wild, the hardware is the first place a lie lives. Colonial Revival loved this silhouette. They recast it, then recast it again, then made it in stamped steel and called it antique brass. The piercings on a revival plate are often too even, the metal too thin, the nuts too modern, the screws too long. The wood may still be old. The handshake may be 1910. Both can be true.

The willow and the batwing are dealer words. Useful. Also a way to stop looking. A willow plate has a lobed outline that droops like a leaf. A batwing spreads. Both can be pierced to the point of lace. Both can be solid and still read as the type. The piercing is where a cheap recast fails: bridges too thin, or too thick, or a sprue scar left as a blob on a curl. Period plates can be rough on the back and still refined on the face. That split is the chasing.

I do not need to take the name away from Chippendale. He earned the drawing. I need to give the pour back to the men who poured. A backplate is a collaboration that the brand erased. The draft is here to put the founder back in the lane.
