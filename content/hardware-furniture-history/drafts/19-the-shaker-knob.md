A Shaker knob is a refusal you can hold.

Wood. A mushroom. A tenon into the drawer front, or a screw if the year and the shop went that way. Cherry, maple, sometimes walnut. No plate. No eagle. No chasing. The hand finds a hemisphere and the drawer comes. If you have used a good one, you know it does not pinch, does not rattle, does not heat up in July the way a dark brass bail does. It is a kindness made of the same material as the case, which is a theology if you want one and a shop efficiency if you do not.

I am wary of Shaker as a brand. The catalogs ate it. You can buy a "Shaker knob" in three diameters from a company that has never believed anything. The shape survives the belief. That is not nothing. It is also not the same as a knob turned in a community that treated ornament as a problem.

They locked things. I want that in the record. The refusal is ostentation, not security. A community that shares goods still has stores, offices, medicines, a place a child should not go. A wooden knob and a till lock can live on the same drawer. The handshake is humble. The bolt is not a sermon.

Reproductions fail in two directions. Too perfect: a CNC mushroom with a finish like candy, stained to match a factory cherry that never grew. Too rustic: a raspy blob that thinks faith is roughness. The old ones I have trusted are quiet. Tool marks if you look, not if you shout. A finish that is oil or milk paint or just time. They wear on the equator, where the fingers pull.

Communities differed. Hancock is not Pleasant Hill. A knob from one shop will not photocopy another. Collectors still talk about mushroom profiles the way lock people talk about bows — a little too much, and still useful. I care about whether the tenon is wood into wood. A later machine screw through a mushroom is a repair or a factory shortcut. Both exist. The tenon is the older handshake.

If you put brass on a Shaker case to "dress it up," you have misunderstood the case or you have decided the case is a style and not a sentence. You are allowed to decide that. You are not allowed to call the brass original. The knob is the original. It is still in production because it was right, and because it is cheap, and those two facts have been dancing since the first catalog stole the hemisphere.
