# Staging

Quality over speed. Each piece in this series walked three stages before it earned a file in `drafts/`.

## Stage 1 — Research

Facts, dates, trade names, and the places sources disagree. Written into `research/NOTES.md` and `research/SOURCES.md`. Uncertainties stay visible. A date that will not sit still (handmade vs machine screws, for instance) is not rounded into a slogan.

No draft starts from a vibe.

## Stage 2 — Outline

One argument per piece. Not a heading dump. Collected in `outlines/_index.md`. If the outline cannot say what the piece is *for*, it does not go to stage 3.

## Stage 3 — Draft

Full voice. These are the files in `drafts/`. Frontmatter records cluster, stage, and the sources that actually touched the prose.

These drafts are still drafts. They have not been line-edited against a physical study collection. Joan Parcher has ten thousand pieces of hardware. This series has a desk and the published record.

## Stage 4 — Hold

Not started. Revise against objects, trade catalogs, and a second pair of eyes. Do not polish these into marketing.

## Rules that survive every stage

1. Name the object before the era.
2. Prefer a maker, a patent, or a town over "people have always…"
3. Say when hardware was replaced. Most of it was.
4. Do not date a piece from one screw.
5. Do not invent a witness.
