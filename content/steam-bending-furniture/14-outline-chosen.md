# Outline we are keeping

Shop in the hands. History in the joints. BBF in the room the whole time, not as a sticker at the end.

## Title (working)

**Wood will crumple. It will not stretch.**  
Subhead: Steam-bent furniture, the old way, for a hardwood shop in Bradley County.

## Spine

1. **Open on the physics you can feel.** Compression vs tension. Why a sawn curve is a different, weaker object. One sentence that we make rectangles and that a rectangle is not the only honest shape.

2. **The blank.** Species we already know. Grain, pith, 1:15, riven vs sawn. Moisture window. Green / kiln / air-dry fight, settled for this shop.

3. **A short history that earns the strap.** Boat ribs and Windsor bows first, in a few lines. Thonet: veneer packets, then solid + steam + band, factory in the beech, crate of parts, the numbers. One paragraph on what he is not. One paragraph on laminate so we never confuse Aalto with this. One paragraph on Warren: furniture *stock*, 1901, our islands, why a curve still belongs here.

4. **The box.** Atmospheric steam. 200–212 °F. Time as rhyme and as FPL. Working window. Door manners.

5. **The bend.** Strap, stops, form, overbend, kink, second body, back in the box.

6. **The wait.** Strap off at an hour. Oak under a blanket. Meter, not a calendar. What MC does to a finished curve later.

7. **Failures, in a list a person can use with gloves on.**

8. **Close.** We have not run a Viennese line. We have the woods and the climate and the refusal to fake a number. The next twenty sticks are the rest of the article. Keep the breaks.

## Things that do not get their own chapter

- Microwave bending (FPL allows it; we do not have the jig notes).

- A full Windsor how-to.
- A Moro island SKU list (one mention is enough).
- Personal invented memoir.

## Length

Long form: about 3,000 words.  
Wall card: the box, the times, the strap, the fail list.
