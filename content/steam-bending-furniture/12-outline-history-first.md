# Outline A — history first

Tried because the brief named history before shop. Rejected as the spine. Kept so we remember why.

1. **Lede:** Thonet, 1859, No. 14, fifty million.
2. **Back up:** he did not invent steam; boats and Windsors.
3. **The factory:** Koritschan, strap, catalog, crate.
4. **The fork:** laminate modernism vs solid steam.
5. **Warren:** mills, furniture stock, BBF case goods.
6. **Then, at last, the box:** MC, time, strap, dry.

## Why it loses

The reader who needs to bend a stretcher has to walk through Vienna to get to a kettle. History becomes a school assignment stapled to a how-to. BBF becomes a cameo in someone else’s museum.

## What it donates to the chosen outline

Thonet still gets a clean run, just not the first chair in the room. The “he did not invent steam” correction stays. The crate of parts stays as a commercial lesson: a curve that cannot ship is a hobby.
