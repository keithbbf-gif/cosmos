# Voice

How this shop talks when it is not selling a mocha island to a marketplace algorithm.

## Sound

- First person plural: *we*. Not “one should.” Not “craftspeople throughout history.”
- Short sentences. Then a longer one when the thought actually has a hinge.
- Specific nouns: strap, end stop, white oak, Pennington, condensate, pith.
- Verbs that happen in a room: pull, wrinkle, meter, cover, scrape, break.
- A little stubborn. Not cute. Not folksy-for-hire. We are in south Arkansas. We do not have to announce it every third line with “y’all” and a biscuit.

## What Keith’s other writing already sounds like

The COSMOS pages are not furniture pages, but the mouth is the same man: numbered when it helps, allergic to a claim without an artifact, willing to say “do not conflate,” willing to leave a scar in the paragraph so the next person does not pay the same tuition. Steal that spine. Leave the software words here.

## Person

We are a hardwood shop that has shipped case goods and boards. We are learning or restating a curve process in public, with lab numbers and old factories in the room. We are not pretending to be Fortune. We are not pretending to be Thonet. We are not writing “as a small-batch artisan brand.”

## Address the reader

Talk to the person who will pick up the stick. Sometimes that is us. Sometimes that is a reader who found the piece. Do not split those into “pro tips” and “for beginners.” Same box.

## Rhythm we want

> Wood will crumple. It will not stretch. Everything else is furniture.

> The door dumps a cloud that does not look like a cloud until it hits the cooler air in front of your mouth. Stand to the side of that.

> Thonet’s trick was not steam. It was a strap and a factory that could do the same back on Tuesday.

## Rhythm we do not want

> In the world of fine furniture, steam bending has long been a time-honored technique that allows artisans to unlock organic curves.

> Whether you are a weekend warrior or a seasoned pro, moisture content is key.

> Join us as we delve into the rich tapestry of bentwood.

If a sentence could run under a stock photo of a white workshop, kill it.

## Contractions

Yes. *Don’t, isn’t, we’ll, that’s.* This is speech that got written down.

## Humor

Dry. Rare. Never a punchline after a burn risk.

## How we say I don’t know

“We have not bent twenty gum bows and kept the breaks. Until we do, gum is on the lab list and off the first customer part.”

Not: “Further research is needed.”
