# Card — the box

- Long enough for the longest blank. A little air around the stick.
- Slope to a **drain**. Condensate is not a bath.
- **Racks or dowels.** Work in the weather, not in a puddle.
- Steam **in low**. **Vent** at the far end. Do not cap the vent.
- Thermometer you can read from the lane.
- Plywood is fine. Insulation is fine. **Pressure is not.**
- Burner / kettle **out from under** the lid. The water scalds as dead as the vapor.
- Target in the chamber: **200–210 °F**. 180 °F is lunch.

## Time

| Starting MC | Lab time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20–25% | ~38 min per inch | Heat without drying it out |
| drier | ~75 min per inch | You are putting water back |
| shop rhyme | 1 hour per inch | Learn it, don’t worship it |
| 1" part, 3 hours | too long | Inside wrinkles |

**House, 1" air-dried oak at 15–18%:** 1 hour. Pull. Fights? +20 minutes. Not 3 hours.

Write **time-in** on the end grain. A 1" part has ~10 minutes of useful heat after the door. Do not load more than you can bend.

## Door

Gloves. Face to the side. Open, take, close.

Sources: FPL ch. 19 (times, atmospheric steam); Fortune FWW 238 (200–210 °F, 1–2 h/in, 10-minute window, wrinkle warning).
