# Section try — the pull

You have maybe ten minutes. That is not a reason to rush. It is a reason to have the form, the strap, the clamps, and the extra body already in the room.

## Strap

Steel on the convex face. End stops so the blank cannot get longer. The stretch, such as it is, lives in the steel. The wood is asked to crumple on the inside.

Fortune’s feel: snug the adjustable stop until the strap and the stick take a slight arc, then stop. Too loose, the outside tears. Too tight, the inside wrinkles. A couple of clamps keep a thin part from walking off the strap. Take them off one at a time as you pull.

Leave a steel strap on a tannin wood and you will decorate it blue-black. That comes later. For the pull, the strap is the job.

## Form

Stacked particleboard or plywood, cut to the curve you want plus a little more, because the piece will try to open when it is free. We do not have a universal springback percent in this folder. Overbend a little. Write on the form what that species and thickness did. The next blank is cheaper if the form tells the truth.

The face of the form does not need to be furniture. It needs to be fair. A lump in the form becomes a lump in the work.

Anchor one end of the strap to the form. Pull. If you are over an inch and a half, do not be proud. Get a second pair of hands or a block and tackle.

## Kink

If a kink starts, stop pulling with the lever. Clamp the strap and the work to the form just behind the kink and let the clamp do that inch. Then go on. Muscling through a kink is how you write a failure in a wood that would have forgiven you.

## After it is down

Clamp it as if you mean it. The whole face, not three heroic points. Then breathe. The stick is still plastic. You can ruin it with a clamp pad that leaves a dent you will sand for an hour.

One hour later, the strap comes off. Fortune’s stain clock. If you are running duplicates, move the piece to a drying rack that holds the same curve so the form is free. If you are running one, it can stay on the form.

Oak and the other ring-porous woods do not get a fan in the face on day one. Cover them. Tomorrow you can let them breathe. Walnut and the rest can take air sooner.

If it fought you and you are still inside the window, you already know you can put it back in the box. If you are outside the window, do that anyway rather than invent a new radius with a hammer.
