# Card — safety

Steam is not a personality. It is a burn you do not always see.

1. **Atmospheric box only.** No homemade pressure vessel. Vent open.
2. **Drain.** Hot water on the floor is a slip and a scald.
3. **Burner out from under the plywood.** Watch the pot. Dry-fire is how you meet the fire department.
4. **Gloves.** Face to the **side** of the door. Glasses help until they fog; don’t lean in to see.
5. **Lane clear.** No coffee tour through a cycle. No kids.
6. **Hot wood stays hot.** A 1" part is still plastic, and still a brand, for minutes.
7. **Two people** on thick stock. Pride is not a clamp.
8. **Write time-in.** Oversteam is a quality failure and a longer exposure.
9. **Strap edges are steel.** They cut.
10. If you are hurt, the piece can go back in the box. You cannot.

This card does not replace a shop’s own rules. It is the minimum for this process.
