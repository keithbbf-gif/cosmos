# Section try — the box

A steam box is a wet oven that must not become a bomb.

## What it is

A long chamber, a little bigger than the longest blank, sloped so condensate runs to a drain and not onto the work. Racks or dowels so the sticks are in the weather, not sitting in a puddle. Steam in low. A vent at the far end. A hole for a thermometer you can actually read. Insulation if you are not trying to heat the county.

Plywood is fine. Fortune has used the same cheap box since 1976. A liner is nice. Pressure is not. Atmospheric steam. If someone wants to weld a vessel and hang a gauge on it, they can go work for a factory that already has the insurance.

The kettle can be a wallpaper steamer, a dedicated wood steamer, or a pot on a burner that you do not forget. The water in that pot will scald you as dead as the vapor. Keep the hose short enough that you do not trip on it and long enough that the burner is not under the plywood.

## Heat

Two hundred to two hundred ten Fahrenheit in the box. Fortune’s numbers. Boiling water at our elevation is close enough to 212 that we do not need a philosophy. If the thermometer says 180, you are not steaming. You are warming lunch.

## Time

The rhyme is one hour per inch. Learn it, then do not worship it.

The Forest Service: about thirty-eight minutes per inch if the stick is already at twenty to twenty-five percent moisture; about seventy-five minutes per inch if it is drier and you are putting water back in. Fortune: one to two hours per inch at 200–210 °F, and a third hour on a one-inch part is how the inside of the curve wrinkles instead of slips.

Our box is not Thonet’s boiler. They will cook a beech rod for six hours under pressure. That number does not travel.

Do not load more sticks than you can bend inside the working window. A one-inch part stays useful for about ten minutes after it leaves the door. A two-and-a-half-inch part, about twenty. Write the time-in on the end grain. The last stick in a crowded box is how you oversteam without meaning to.

If a part cools and fights you, put it back. That is allowed. Macho is not a process.

## The door

Stand to the side. The cloud is not always visible until it hits cooler air, which may be your glasses. Gloves. No face in the opening. No one walking through the lane with a coffee.

Open, take the piece, close. The box is not a conversation pit.
