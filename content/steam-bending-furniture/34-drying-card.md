# Card — dry

The pull is loud. This is what keeps it.

- Stay in the curve until the piece is as dry as the **room it will live in**.
- **Meter.** A week is a guess. Fortune’s 7–8% is a northern furniture number. August in Warren may not be that.
- **Oak and other ring-porous:** blanket day one. Then air. A fan on a wet oak face is how you buy checks.
- Other woods: air sooner. Fan is a tool.
- **Strap off ~1 hour** after the pull (stain), then stays or rack for the rest of the dry.
- After it is free:
  - wetter later → curve **opens**
  - drier later → curve **tightens**
  - wet/dry cycling → slow **straightening**
- Do not hang a bent rail over a stew pot.
- Cut joinery after it has sat in the finish room.
- A bent piece is a little weaker than the same piece left straight. Design the section for the lean.

Sources: FPL ch. 19 (fixing the bend; moisture after; strength); Fortune FWW 238 (7–8%, week, oak blanket, strap clock).
