# Section try — how it fails

Write the failure down on the form. The next blank is a cheaper teacher if the last one left a note.

**Broke on the outside, long splinters.**  
The outside was asked to stretch. No strap, or a strap that was theater. Or the grain ran out. Or the stick was kiln-dried and proud. Or the radius was a wish.

**Broke on the outside, short and brash.**  
Bad wood. Pith, brittle, a knot you talked yourself into.

**Wrinkles on the inside, like a wet rope.**  
Oversteamed, or the strap was cranked down like a vise, or the stick was too green and the water in the cells had nowhere to go. Hydrostatic crush. The lab warned us. We do not get to be surprised.

**Checks, especially on oak.**  
You dried the skin too fast. Fan on a wet ring-porous face. Or the blank was already checked and the steam opened it.

**Blue-black face.**  
Steel and tannin. Strap stayed the night. Scrape what you can. Next time, one hour.

**It came off the form and opened.**  
It was still wet. Or you did not overbend. Or the room is wetter than the room you dried it in.

**It came off the form and tightened.**  
It kept drying. You targeted a number the house will never see.

**It slowly straightened over months.**  
Wet, dry, wet, dry. The wrinkled face keeps arguing. The handbook said this. A finish helps. A steam cycle over a dishwasher vent does not.

**It would not start around the form.**  
Cold. Understeamed. Or you stood around talking for twelve minutes with a three-quarter-inch stick in your hand. Put it back in the box. Do not invent a longer lever yet.

**It started, then a kink.**  
You kept pulling. Clamp behind the kink next time.

**Three in a row.**  
Stop changing three variables. Change the blank or the time or the radius. Not all three. Write it down.

We have not bent twenty gum bows in this shop and kept a scrap tally. Until we do, a gum failure is a note, not a legend. Same for any species we have only met as a cutting board. The fail list above is borrowed from the lab, from Fortune, and from every boat shop that already paid. Our own tally comes later. Keep the breaks. Do not throw them in the burn pile before you know what they said.
