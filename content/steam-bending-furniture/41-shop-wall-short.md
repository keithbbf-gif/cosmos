# Steam — wall card

Bradley Brand Furniture. Hang by the box. Big type when you print it.

**Wood crumples. It does not stretch. Strap the outside.**

## Blank
- Oak, hickory, ash, elm, walnut first. Gum later. Pine = mild only.
- Grain 1:15 or better. No pith, shake, rot, knot in the bend.
- Meter **15–18%** for a form. Write species / thickness / MC on the end.

## Box
- Atmospheric. Drain. Racks. Steam in low. Vent open. No pressure.
- **200–210 °F.** Gloves. Face out of the door.
- Rhyme: 1 hour per inch.
- **House, 1" air-dried oak 15–18%:** 1 hour. Fights? +20 min. Not 3 hours.
- ~10 min of heat after the door on 1". Time-in on the end.

## Pull
- Strap on the convex face. End stops. Slight arc. Stop.
- Form = finished curve plus a little. Write what the last stick did.
- Kink: clamp behind it. Do not muscle through.
- Thick: second body.
- **Strap off at 1 hour.**

## Dry
- Stay in the curve. Meter, not a calendar.
- Oak: blanket day one.
- Off wet = opens. Fan on wet oak = checks.

## Fail, in one line
Outside break = stretch or runout. Wrinkles = oversteam / green crush. Blue = steel stayed. Three in a row = change **one** thing.

Keep the breaks. Do not start a customer part on a first kiln stick.
