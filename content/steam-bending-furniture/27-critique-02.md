# Critique of draft 02

Closer. Still a little proud, still a little even. A good piece has a change of speed.

## Working

- Headings help. A thumb can find the box.
- House rule on time is the first sentence that sounds like this shop and not a summary of better shops.
- Warren is one movement now.
- Fail run is a paragraph a person can use.

## Not working

1. **The lede repeats itself.** “Wood will crumple” as title and first line is a drum we don’t need twice. Title can stay. First line should take a step.

2. **Fortune’s name is doing too much host work.** Once, with the magazine. After that, “a chairmaker who has been at this for decades” or just the rule. We are not his student essay.

3. **“The old work” is still a hallway of plaques.** Boat, Windsor, Thonet, Warren — four nods, each polite. Let Thonet have the middle of that section. Let Warren have the last word. Boat and Windsor can share a breath at the top.

4. **Numbers cluster.** 12–16, 15–20, 12–20, 18, 38, 75, 200–210. The reader glazes. Keep the house numbers in the clear and put the lab range in one sentence, not three.

5. **“Not a hoop” / “only the hoop”** — hoop is earning its keep. Do not wear it out.

6. **Safety is still thin.** One more concrete line: slope, drain, no cap on the vent, pot off the plywood.

7. **The pull is starved.** Draft 01 had the second body and the drying rack. Bring them back. That is shop, not decoration.

8. **Close is almost right.** “Keep the breaks” stays. Cut “the rest of this article.” We are not narrating the folder.

9. **Title / subhead.** Subhead is honest. A little stiff. Try: *History and shop practice for Bradley Brand Furniture.* Or no subhead.

## Cut list for draft 03

- Second “will crumple” if the title already said it.
- One Fortune mention.
- One “hoop.”
- The Crystal Palace “curious” if we need the line-inches. (Keep if the paragraph still sings.)
- “Different job” after laminate — already clear.
- Any sentence that only announces the next sentence.

## Add

- Drying rack / second body.
- One line on overbend as a note on the form, not a theory.
- The 3 percent rule, in English: if the inside and outside have to become different lengths by more than a little, you need the strap. Not as a formula.
