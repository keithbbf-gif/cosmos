# Card — strap and form

## Strap

- Steel on the **convex** face.
- **End stops** so the blank cannot get longer.
- Snug to a **slight arc**. Then stop.
  - Too loose → outside tears.
  - Too tight → inside wrinkles.
- If inside and outside would differ by more than **about 3%** in length, you need this. Lab rule. Not a nicety.
- **Off at ~1 hour.** Tannin + iron = ink.
- Thin parts: a couple of clamps so they don’t walk off the strap. Remove as you pull.

## Form

- Fair curve. A lump in the form is a lump in the work.
- Cut **past** the finished curve. The piece will open.
- No universal springback percent in this shop yet. **Write on the form** what that species and thickness did.
- Anchor one end of the strap. Pull.
- One person to ~1½". After that: second body or block and tackle.
- **Kink:** stop the lever. Clamp behind the kink. Then go on.
- Duplicates: move to a **drying rack** that holds the same curve.

Sources: FPL ch. 19 (3% / end pressure, 25–30% compression vs 1–2% tension); Fortune FWW 238 (feel, solo thickness, kink, stain clock).
