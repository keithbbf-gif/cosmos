# Rejected openings

Tried out loud. None of these lead the piece. Kept so we do not wander back.

---

**1.** In today’s fast-paced world, homeowners are seeking organic silhouettes…

Dead on arrival. Sounds like a marketplace description tag.

**2.** Michael Thonet changed furniture forever in 1859 when he released Chair No. 14.

True-ish, and it makes the essay a museum wall label. We are not a museum. Thonet can come in on page two.

**3.** Steam bending is the process of using heat and moisture to make wood pliable.

Definition first is how a textbook starts. A shop starts with a stick that broke, or with a stick that didn’t.

**4.** Down here in Bradley County, we still remember when the mill whistle…

Too much porch. We can say Warren without a fiddle.

**5.** I still think about the first bow I broke.

We did not agree to invent a memoir. If Keith wants that sentence later, he can write it with a date and a species.

**6.** There are three ways to make a curve in solid wood.

Fortune’s opening. Honest. Also his. We can say the same fact later, in our order, without lifting his first step.

**7.** Lignin is a thermoplastic polymer with a glass transition temperature of approximately 170 °C.

That is a lab door. The reader has not earned the chemistry yet, and the number without water is misleading anyway.

**8.** Forget everything you know about woodworking.

No.

**9.** A Moro island is a box of rectangles. That was the job. The next job is a curve that still belongs on a kitchen floor.

Close. A little ad-copy. The contrast is useful. Park it. Maybe a later section, not the first breath.

**10.** Wood does not want to be a curve.

This one has a pulse. Hold it. It might be the start after we stop performing.

**11.** The Forest Products Laboratory in Madison has already done the expensive part of this argument.

True, and smug. The lab comes in when we need a number, not as a flex.

**12.** If you can boil water and tell the truth about grain, you can bend a piece of oak.

Almost. Sounds like a YouTube title. The truth about grain is the hard part, so the sentence lies by being catchy.

---

**Kept in the pocket:** #10, maybe a cleaned #9 as a turn, never as the lede.
