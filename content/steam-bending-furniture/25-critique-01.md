# Critique of draft 01

What is working. What is not. No compliments that are not also a cut.

## Working

- The lede is the right lede. Physics you can feel. Not Vienna, not a definition.
- BBF is in the room without a brand paragraph at the top.
- The dates are not flattened. 1901 / 1903 / 2008 still distinct.
- Laminate is named and put down without a sneer.
- Failure list is usable.
- Numbers have parents.

## Not working

1. **History is a brick in the middle.** You are in the blank, then you are in a boat, then you get a full Thonet biography, then Warren, then finally the box. A reader with a kettle is punished. A reader who wanted history already got a taste in the lede and then had to wait.

2. **Thonet is too long for this pass and still not sharp enough.** The Crystal Palace sentence can live. The lifetime itinerary does not all have to. Keep: veneer first, strap, factory, crate, the production numbers, Frankenberg is not our box. Cut: some of the travelogue.

3. **Warren and the islands appear twice.** Once as “we made rectangles,” once as the mill paragraph. Merge. One pass through the county.

4. **The chemistry paragraph is proud of itself.** “Tubes and glue” is good. 170 °C wants a faster landing on 212 °F. Do not linger in Madison.

5. **“Not romance. Continuous grain.”** Keep once. It shows up in spirit three times.

6. **Ash borer** is a side door. One sentence is enough. Do not let it become a news brief.

7. **The fail list at the end is a dump.** Either it becomes a tight table-in-prose after the wait, or it becomes the wall card and the long piece only names the two or three failures that teach.

8. **No titles, no breathing.** Draft 01 is a march. The finished piece needs a few headings a person can find with a thumb: the blank, the old work, the box, the pull, the wait.

9. **“Cheap way to fake”** is a sneer I said we would not make. Change *fake*. The laminate sentence already does the work.

10. **Missing: one clear house rule on time.** The reader should leave knowing what *we* will do on a 1-inch air-dried oak part, not only what three authorities would do. Pick it: an hour, check, another twenty minutes if it fights, never three.

11. **Missing: safety as a paragraph, not a clause.** Door, drain, no pressure, pot water. Then move on.

12. **Close is good.** Do not decorate it.
