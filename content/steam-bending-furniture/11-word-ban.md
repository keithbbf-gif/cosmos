## Punctuation habits

- Do not hang every sentence on an em dash.
- Do not end three paragraphs in a row with a one-word punch.

## Allowed ugly words

Strap. Condensate. Pith. Shake. Tannin. Scrap. Wrinkle. Meter. Form. End stop. Hydrostatic. These are not pretty. They are the job.
