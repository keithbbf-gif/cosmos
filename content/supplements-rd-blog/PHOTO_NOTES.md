# Photo notes

No fake lab. No white-coat stock presented as our facility. No COA screenshot with a real lot number from a supplier you do not have the right to show.

These drafts are text-first. Images, if any, are diagrams or clearly labeled stock.

Editorial SVGs now ship for **all 40 slugs** (`assets/<slug>/`, `GRAPHICS_INDEX.md`). Wave 1 (01–16) came from PR #240. Wave 2 (17–40) extends the same palette and helpers. Captions say illustrative/qualitative. Do not add fake effect-size bars.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or clearly licensed scientific diagrams: chemical structures (NMN, cholecalciferol, creatine), a simple GI-tract schematic, a label callout diagram you drew.
- Wikimedia Commons / NIH / CDC / FDA public-domain figures. Keep the license line under the image.
- Neutral pack-shot style later, shot in-house, of *your* bottles — after SKUs exist.
- Tables and timelines set in type. Those carry more information than a hero image.

## Banned

- AI-generated "our laboratory in [city]."
- Unsplash scientists as implied staff.
- Amazon listing screenshots that include other brands' faces or claims.
- Before/after bodies.
- Kids with gummies.
- Cannabis leaf on a piece that is about *not* selling CBD as a supplement.

## Alt text

Describe the figure, not the vibe. "Flowchart: finished-product COA rows for identity, assay, heavy metals, microbes, residual solvents" — not "quality you can trust."
