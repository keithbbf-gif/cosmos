# Editorial calendar — supplement R&D, Jan 2020 → 2026

Sixteen drafts. One opener, then the fifteen assigned themes. Cadence below is a suggestion for a later WordPress staging site, not a live-publish order.

All files live under `content/supplements-rd-blog/`. Status: `draft`. Nothing here is cleared for a public product page.

## Cadence (suggested)

Ship one piece every 10–14 days. That is a six-to-eight-month shelf, which matches how slowly this evidence actually moves. Do not dump sixteen posts in a week. A science/QA reviewer should see each piece before it leaves staging.

Cluster:

| Wave | Weeks | Pieces | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — frame + scars | 1–6 | 01, 02, 12, 10 | Reader learns the rules (DSHEA, letters, testing) before ingredients |
| 2 — the three nutrients everyone Googled | 7–12 | 03, 04, 11 | Immune boom, D/zinc/omega-3, then the 2020–22 sourcing mess that fed it |
| 3 — forms and strains | 13–20 | 05, 08, 09, 07 | Probiotics, NAD/NMN, delivery tech, adaptogens — high hype, high QA load |
| 4 — operator kit | 21–30 | 06, 15, 13, 14, 16 | Sports, women's health, tests, CBD-adjacent law, COA literacy |

Hold any piece that still carries `[CITE NEEDED]` on a claim you would print on a label.
