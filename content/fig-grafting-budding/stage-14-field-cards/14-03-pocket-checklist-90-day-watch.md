# Pocket checklist — 90-day watch

The graft is not a Saturday. It is the next three
months. This is the walk-by card.

**Week 1–3.** Bags still on if they need to be?
Shade still honest on the west? Stock drinking?
Scions plump, not raisins? Soft suckers rubbed —
not next week, today? No daily peeking that dries
the bag. After a frost or a heat spike: wait, then
scratch, do not hold a funeral on the same afternoon.

**Week 3–6.** Vent before you strip bags. Tape stays
until the knit is real or the film is strangling.
Chip-buds may still be quiet and green. Quiet and
green is not dead. Brown and dry is dead — cut it
off so it is not a wick. Stake anything that has
started to run. Birds and dogs still exist.

**Week 6–12.** The bully shows. Pinch. The polite
head needs light. Fruit on a first-year wand comes
off unless the plant is already a horse. Ties
loosened before they girdle. White paint on stubs
still doing a job? Cloth coming off in steps, not
in one blinding noon.

**Always.** Know the union. If you cannot see it,
you will spare a sucker. Walk after you irrigate.
Walk after a storm. A hinge plus a storm is a
broken flush. A broken flush above a live union can
reshoot. A break through the union is a recut.

**The note.** Date, weather, what you saw, what you
did. Three lines. This is how you stop grafting by
myth next year.

**The end of 90 days.** You should know who lived.
You should have a stake on the living. You should
have a plan for the first winter: mulch, maybe a
wrap, scions of the new head if the wood is long
enough. If you do not have those, you do not have
a finished graft. You have a plant you have been
meaning to get to.

I lose unions in this window more often than on cut
day. Cut day is exciting. Day 40 is a Thursday. The
sucker does not care that you were busy on Thursday.
This card is for Thursday.

Put the three trees you grafted on the same list as
the tomatoes if you have to. If it is not on a walk
you already take, it will not happen. I clip a
bright tag at path height so Thursday-me cannot
honestly say he did not see it.

At day 90, throw the dead wood away and seal the
stubs. Do not leave a museum of tan sticks "as a
reminder." The reminder is the note. The tan stick
is a wick. Then write one sentence about next
year's timing. That sentence is the only part of
this card that compounds.
