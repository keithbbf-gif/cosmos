# Side veneer when you cannot head

Sometimes you cannot cut the top off. The tree is the only shade
on that side of the house. The variety on top is still feeding
you. You just want a new branch of something else, or you want
a first union *before* you commit to a heading next year.

Side veneer: a shallow downward flap or a long shallow slot on
the side of a live stem, a matching face on the scion, scion
set in, wrap, leave the top of the stock on as a sap-drawer.
When the scion takes and starts to pull, you shorten the top.
Later you can remove it.

On figs this is also how you add a variety to a cocktail tree
without making a bald stump in April. You pick a scaffold, add
a side union, and let the scaffold keep working. If the union
fails, you still have fruit.

The cut wants to be long and shallow, not a deep notch into
pith. You are looking for a rectangle of cambium. The scion
is a one- or two-bud piece with a matching face. Wrap so the
scion cannot kick out when the stock sways. Figs sway.

I do these when the stock is moving. A fully dormant thick
stem does not want to knit a side wound as kindly. Chip-bud
is the simpler side method if you only have one bud. Veneer
is for when you have a little stick and you want a branch,
not a single eye.

Aftercare is the part people skip. The stock top will outgrow
the new scion. You have to start tipping the top once the
scion is clearly alive, or the scion remains a decorative
nub. Do it in steps. One hard heading the week the scion
breaks can stall a tree that was not ready to lose that
leaf area.

In 8a sun, a side wound on the southwest face cooks. Put
veneers on the east or north of the stem, or shade them.
I learned that on a *Brown Turkey* trunk that looked like
a lizard on the west side by June.

If you are nervous about heading a big tree, this is the
rehearsal. Get a take on the side. Learn that tree's
timing. Head it next March with information instead of
adrenaline.

Place the veneer where you can reach it without a circus.
A union you cannot inspect will become a union you forget
to sucker-check. I have a veneer on the back of a *Turkey*
that took and then starved because I never walked that
side with the hose. The front of the tree looked fine. The
scion did not.

Tie a bright bit of tape on the scaffold at eye level, not
only at the union. You need a flag from the path. Figs
leaf out and hide their own surgery.
