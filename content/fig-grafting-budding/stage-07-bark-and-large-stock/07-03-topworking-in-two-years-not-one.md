# Topwork in two years, not one

The fantasy is Saturday: four scaffolds headed, four varieties
on, bags like party hats, done. The tree's fantasy is to live.
A large fig in 8a can take a full heading. It would rather
not. You get more living scions and less sunburn if you
convert half the tree this spring and the other half next.

Year one: pick two scaffolds. Side-veneer or bark-graft those.
Leave the rest to feed the roots and shade the trunk. Eat
the old variety one more summer. Learn whether you even like
the new one enough to finish the job.

Year two: head the remaining old wood. Graft it. By now you
have a year of timing on that specific tree — when it slipped,
how hard it suckered, whether the west face cooks. You are
not guessing with a whole canopy on the line.

This also lets winter vote. If January deletes a tender new
head, you still have the old variety making fruit while you
re-graft the hole. A one-weekend cocktail tree that loses two
heads is a sad stick collection.

Exceptions. A freeze-killed top has already done the heading.
You are not choosing two years. You are choosing live wood
and a sap-drawer if any exists. A small pot is a one-morning
job. Two-year topwork is for a tree whose shade you use and
whose stump you do not want to stare at all summer.

Keep notes on which scaffold is which. Wire labels. A map on
an index card in the shed. I have a tree that is "the purple
one on the left unless I rotated in my head." I did rotate.
I grafted the same variety twice and wondered why the ripening
never staggered.

Two years feels slow when you are holding scions. It is not
slow compared to digging a mature fig and starting over. The
roots are the expensive part. Let them keep a shirt on while
you change the hat.

Year-one work should be the scaffolds you can reach without
a ladder if you can arrange it. Learn the tree at eye level.
Year two you can go higher with information and with a
stake already on the property. I did year one on the high
west arm once because it was the sour one I hated. I could
not inspect it without a circus. The unions dried while the
easy arms made fruit I still did not want.

Tell anyone who waters the yard which arms are the new ones.
A well-meaning soak that blasts a bag off a stub is a
friendly failure. Point. Label. Do not assume the tree is
self-explanatory. It is a fig. It looks like a fig.
