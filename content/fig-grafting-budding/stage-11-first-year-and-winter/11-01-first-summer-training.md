# First-summer training

A take is not a tree. The first summer you are choosing a
shape while the plant is still deciding whether it believes
you.

Let the scion run until you have some length, then pinch
or head it to force laterals if you want a bush. If you
want a standard, pick one wand, stake it, and remove the
side noise on the scion until it has a trunk you can live
with. Do not do both in your head and neither on the
plant.

Fruit. First-year unions will try to make figs. I pick
them off unless the plant is already strong and I am only
curious. A syconium on a two-foot scion is a cute way to
slow a union you wanted to thicken. Taste next year.

Water. 8a summers are not gentle. New canopies on old
roots can actually be fine — the roots were already there.
New canopies on young pots are not fine. Do not teach a
bench graft about drought in July.

Fertilizer. I do not pour nitrogen on a fresh union. A
little compost, a steady water habit, and the vigor the
variety already has. Yellow hungry leaves on an otherwise
taking plant can get a light feed. Soft, dark, endless
growth in September is how you grow winter-tender wood.

Sun. Once the bags are off and the shoot is moving, take
the heavy shade away in steps. A fig that lives under
cloth all summer will bleach when you finally remember
it. Three days of half cloth, then out.

On a cocktail tree, this is the summer you meet the bully.
Pinch it. Give the polite variety light. If you cannot
see the polite variety from the path, you already lost
the argument.

Do not prune "to look like the catalog" in August. Heavy
late cuts push new wood into the fall. In 8a that wood
is the first to burn in January. Shape in late spring and
early summer. Then stop.

Write what you did. Future-you in March will want to know
whether this head is the scion or a sucker you spared
because it had fruit. Ink is cheaper than a wrong cut.

A first summer is also when you decide if the variety
deserves the rest of the tree. Taste if there is fruit
you did not pick off. Watch rust. Watch vigor. If you
hate the manners, you do not owe it year-two scaffolds.
Leave it as a one-head experiment and spend next March
on something you will eat.

Do not train with wire in July sun. Soft bark plus hot
wire is a cut. Soft tape, morning work, and a second
look after a growth spurt. That is the whole training
kit.
