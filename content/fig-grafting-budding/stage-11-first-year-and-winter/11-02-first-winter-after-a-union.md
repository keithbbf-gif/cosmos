# First winter after a union

A six-month-old graft is not as hardy as the catalog
variety on ten-year roots. The wood is new. The union is
a knuckle. A 12°F night that an old *Celeste* shrugs will
still tan a first-year head, especially if you fed it
late and it went into December soft.

Mulch the stool. Four to six inches over the root zone,
not a volcano piled against the bark all winter without
a thought — keep the collar able to breathe, especially
on young pots you sank in the ground. In a frost pocket
I add a wrap on the lower trunk after the first hard
frost: burlap, not plastic. Plastic sweats and then
freezes the sweat.

Pots come against the house or into an unheated garage
for the ugly nights. A bench graft you made in February
is still a pot plant in January even if it looks like a
tree. The roots in a black pot are colder than the
ground.

Collect scions of the new variety *before* the worst
nights if the wood is long enough. Insurance again. If
January takes the head and you have no wood, you have a
stool. If you have wood, you have a plan for April.

Do not prune in November to "clean up." You will cut
into wood the plant wanted as insulation and you will
wake buds that should stay asleep. Clean dead wood in
spring when you can see what lived.

A union that looks cracked in February is not always
dead. Wait for swell. Scratch. I have had knuckles that
looked like a bad hinge and still pushed. I have had
smooth pretty unions that were tan underneath. Eyes
after a freeze are liars. The fingernail is not.

If you used hardy understock, the winter is allowed to
delete the top. That was the contract. Do not be shocked.
Be ready with the bag of wood and a date in March.

Zone 8a is the zone where people skip first-winter care
because most years they get away with it. Most years is
not the year you care about. The year you care about is
the one that hits 11°F on a night you were sure we were
done with winter. Mulch is cheap. Regret is a season.

Potted unions on a south wall still get a cold pot. The
wall helps the top. The black plastic loses heat to the
night sky. Wrap the pot or sink it in mulch for January.
I have babied a head and lost the roots because I stared
at the top.

If you wrap a trunk, take the wrap off when the worst
nights are done. A forgotten wrap in a wet 8a March is
a slug hotel and a bark problem. Set a reminder the same
day you wrap. Future-you will not remember the burlap
under the fig.
