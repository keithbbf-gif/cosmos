# Approach grafts and inarch repairs

Approach grafting is two living plants rubbed together until
they become one. You cut matching faces, tie them, wait,
then cut the scion from its old roots and/or the stock from
its old top. Both sides drink the whole time. That is why
difficult unions use it. Figs are not usually difficult.
I use approach when I cannot afford a miss: a last plant of
a variety, or a repair.

Inarch is the repair version. A young fig, or a rooted
cutting in a pot, gets its top face-tied onto a wounded
trunk — a girdle from a string, a sunburn strip, a rodent
line. The inarch becomes a living bypass. Bridge grafting
is the same idea with scion sticks above and below a wound.
On figs I have used a nearby sucker as the inarch because
the sucker was already paid for.

Do this when both pieces are growing, not in the dead of
January. You need cells that want to knit. Wrap so the
faces cannot creep. Shade the wound. Figs move in the
wind; a loose approach is two plants that used to be
touching.

Wait longer than you want. A month is a start. I like to
see swelling at the edges before I cut anyone free. Cut
the scion's old roots in steps if you are nervous — half,
then the rest. Same for removing a stock top.

8a summer will cook an exposed trunk wound. Paint and
cloth still apply. A girdled trunk in July is on a clock.
Do the inarch as soon as you have a nurse plant, not after
you think about it through a heat wave.

This is not how you mass-produce *Celeste*. This is how you
save a trunk you already like, or how you move a variety
off a pot onto a better stool without a hard cut. If your
yard has no wounds and no last-of-its-kind plants, you may
never need this chapter. Keep it anyway. The year you need
it, you will not want to learn on the only trunk that
matters.

String-trimmer girdles are the usual 8a reason I open this
file. A bush form with low unions is a target. If you
inarch a sucker over a trimmer ring, also move the trimmer
habit. A second ring next June makes the first repair look
clever and pointless.

Tie the approach so you can water the nurse pot without
wrestling the trunk. A pot that dries because it is jammed
in a crotch is a nurse that quit. Stake the pot. Ugly is
allowed for a month. Dead is not.
