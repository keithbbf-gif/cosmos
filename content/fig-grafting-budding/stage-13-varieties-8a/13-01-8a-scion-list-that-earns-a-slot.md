# An 8a scion list that earns a slot

A slot on a trunk is expensive. It costs a year and a
walk every May. I do not spend it on a name I have not
eaten or on a fig that needs a California summer and a
dry eye.

**Celeste.** The southern default. Closed eye, early,
hardy enough for most 8a winters on its own roots.
Honeyed, not huge. If I could only keep one scion
mother in a humid yard, this is in the argument. Some
strains are better than others. Taste yours before you
multiply a polite but dull tree. Taste the mother before
you tape a name onto a trunk.

**Chicago Hardy.** Insurance and a fine main-crop fig
in its own right. Not the richest bowl. The stool that
comes back. I graft it *as* understock more than I
graft it as a prize scion, unless I need a head that
can take a punch. Boring roots, interesting tops — that
is the usual 8a conversion.

**LSU Purple.** Heat and humidity were the breeding
brief. Vigorous. You will prune it. Fruit that actually
ripens in a Gulf-ish summer. On a cocktail tree it is
the bully unless you pinch. Worth a slot if you have
the hours.

**Texas Everbearing / Brown Turkey kin.** Long harvest,
common as dirt, open-eye risk in wet 8a. I keep one if
I want a workhorse for drying or cooking and I pick on
time. I do not give it four slots and then complain
about souring.

**Olympian / Violette de Bordeaux / Ronde de Bordeaux.**
Smaller, richer, often more polite. Good scion if you
will protect a tender year and you want flavor over
volume. They can be the heads January edits. Put them
on a hardy stool if your pocket is cold.

**LSU Gold.** Beautiful when it behaves. I treat it as
an 8b-leaning luxury in a cold 8a pocket and as a fair
bet on a warm wall. Not my first conversion on a
tree I cannot replace.

**Alma.** Late, rich, and a winter question. If your
10°F nights are real, this is a disposable top. If you
have a south courtyard that never sees 18, a slot is
reasonable. Collect wood before the ugly forecast.

I skip Smyrna types that need a caprifig and a wasp
unless I am doing that whole religion. I skip unnamed
seedlings as scions. I skip anything I have not seen
leaf through an August here.

A slot is not a trophy case. It is a ripening week and
a winter risk. If the name does not earn both, it stays
in a pot until it does.
