# When to unwrap and when to wait

Unwrap too early and the wind finishes a job that was
almost done. Unwrap too late and you girdle a flush or
you hide a dead scion that is rotting into the stub.
The date is not "three weeks" as a religion. The date is
what you see.

I vent bags before I remove them. A day or two of extra
air, still in shade. Then the bag comes off and the tape
stays. The tape comes off when the union looks knitted
and the scion is either pushing or still green and I need
to cut a film that is getting tight.

Chip-buds are slower to read. A March chip may sit until
May. An August chip may sit until next March. I do not
unwrap a quiet green chip because I am bored. I unwrap
when the film is strangling or when I need to recut a
dead one. Green and quiet stays dressed.

A scion that has pushed a foot of growth still needs the
union supported. I may slit the tape instead of stripping
it. Fig flushes are brittle at the seam. The stake starts
before the unwrap finishes.

Signs you waited too long: a pinched ring, a swollen
above and a thin below, film embedded in bark. Cut it
carefully. Do not rip. I have peeled bark off a take
because I was impatient with plastic.

Signs you were early: the scion wrinkles the afternoon
the bag comes off. If that happens, bag it again, add
shade, and feel foolish. Foolish is cheaper than dead.

In 8a I would rather be a week late on unwrap than a
week early in a dry north wind. The wind here in April
can be a hair dryer. Believe it.

Write the graft date on the label. Future-you will not
remember whether this bag is two weeks or six. Future-you
is the person who girdles things.

When in doubt, scratch, do not strip. Green: wait or
vent. Brown: cut off, seal the wound, try again on fresh
wood. Waiting on a corpse is how stubs go hollow.

A 8a habit that has saved me: unwrap in the morning of a
cloudy day, not at 2 p.m. on a blower-wind afternoon. The
scion can learn air without learning a hair dryer in the
same hour. If the only free hour you have is a hot one,
wait a day. The tape will not mind.

Chip-buds I marked "next spring" in August get a March
walk, not a November fidget. Winter unwraps on a quiet
chip are how you give a bud to the north wind as a gift.
Leave the shirt on until swell or until the film is
clearly a problem.
