## B. Chambers on the record

[Interview With A Fig Grower: Keith and Jack Chambers](https://www.thefigjam.co/p/interview-with-a-fig-grower-keith) — The Fig Jam, 25 Jul 2025.

Used: South Arkansas **8a**; 300–350 varieties / ~650 trees; most in #3 / #5; ~25–30 in-ground after 2024–25; fertigation; Keith favorites (Nuestra Señora del Carmen, Maryland Berry, Navid’s Unk, BGM Unk, Melanzana Merdoscola, Labrijta, Red Sicilian, Red Lebanese Bekaa Valley, Negra d’Agde, Black Madeira, Adriatic JH); Keith **not a fan of Brown Turkey or Celeste so far**; Verte splitting; long trial / graft-over; Jack’s plate list (Red Sicilian, Navid’s Unk Dark Greek, Syrian Dark #2, Jack Lilly, NSDC splits in rain, Negra d’Agde); looking-forward names (Yellow Long Neck, White Madeira #1, Colonel Littman’s Black Cross, Castel Trosino, I-258, and others) are **not plated in that interview**.

Forum handles (context only): PapaFig / AAAJacks. YouTube: fruitaholics.
