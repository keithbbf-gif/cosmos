Ronde means round. Bordeaux is a family word that also sits on Violette and on Jack’s Red Sicilian card. Do not merge them. Ronde de Bordeaux is the small, early, dark fig people plant when their season is short or their patience is.

Grower consensus — and I will mark it as consensus, not as our signed plate — is berry, productive, tight eye, early. I did not name RdB on [The Fig Jam](https://www.thefigjam.co/p/interview-with-a-fig-grower-keith). Jack’s small darks were Syrian Dark #2 and the Unk Greek. Same job class. Different names.

Common type. No wasp.

## The name pile

Ronde de Bordeaux is not Violette de Bordeaux. VdB is the small black, deep-red, acid-berry fight. RdB is the early round dark bowl. Red Sicilian is Bordeaux family on our [Fig Reviews](https://figroots.com/fig_reviews_archive/) card — medium, sweet plus berry, productive, and Jack’s first rank. Negra d’Agde is its own card. Malta Black is the clone in the folder. Do not pour four dark names into one pot because a city in France printed on the tag.

Alabama [ANR-1145](https://www.aces.edu/blog/topics/crop-production/fig-production-guide/) does not give RdB its own paragraph the way it gives Bordeaux / Negronne / VdB a paragraph. That is useful. It means I will not cite Auburn for a flavor I have not signed. Forum average is not a homestead score. [VERIFY] on our plate when it repeats.

If your “RdB” is large, late, and open-eyed, you do not have the clock. If it is small, black, deep red, and acid, you may have wandered into VdB. If it is copper and Turkey-shaped, write a Turkey type. The [introduction page](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/) already said 1–3 years to prove type.

Pastilière is the other early dark people mention. Different plant, its own draft. Do not merge early darks to feel organized.

## Why small and early is a climate tool

Scott’s Black wants a long year. Black Madeira wants a long year and a dry hang. Col de Dame Noire wants a long year. RdB wants to be done before the worst of August, or at least to be feeding you while the celebrities are still hard.

In a resprout year after a freeze, early figs on new wood are food. Late figs on new wood are leaves. If Chicago Hardy is your winter story, RdB is a summer partner. [Our winter page](https://figroots.com/winter-protection/) already named Chicago Hardy and Smith for the cold conversation. It did not name RdB as hardy theater. It named a clock you can eat.

[Jack’s type page](https://figroots.com/2025/08/17/types-of-figs/) already said common figs set without a wasp. RdB is that type. Do not buy pollen. Do not buy a San Pedro brochure. Desert King wants last year’s wood. RdB wants this year’s wood and a short calendar.

Birds: dark and early. They will find the first bowl. Net or pick. A green fig lasts longer. This is not a green fig. UAEX said daily picking may be needed when a tree is in production. An early dark that loads a shoot is a harvest job, not a weekend hobby.

Split: small is not immune. Drought then flood. Overripe hang. Even water. Morning pick. Alabama said the riper the fig, the more it splits and sours. UAEX’s souring sentence still applies: water in the eye plus heat after rain.

Eye: the reputation is tight. [VERIFY] yours. If your “RdB” is open and souring, you have a different small dark. Tight is a door, not a vault. Photograph the ostiole after rain. Do not print “tight eye” in a caption unless the still shows it. That is already on the photo brief.

I will not invent a ripening date for Saline County. Consensus says early. Early is a class. Write the first soften on the tag when you have it.

## Flavor

Berry. Not huge. Not a Madeira intensity contest. A handful. That is the point. People who only want large figs will call it a waste of a hole. People who want to eat in July will not.

I will not invent a score. When our plate repeats I will write it. Until then I will plant it as a clock, not as a trophy. Jack ranked Syrian Dark #2 third — extremely tight eye, small dark. He ranked Negra sixth — berry over sugar. He ranked Red Sicilian first — sweet plus berry, medium, productive. RdB is in that craving without a signed number. Leave it that way.

I am not a fan of Brown Turkey or Celeste so far. An early dark berry bowl is closer to why we stayed in this crop than those two tools. That is a craving. It is not a tasting card.

## How I run it in 8a

Sun: **6–8 hours**. UAEX’s minimum. Afternoon cloth in July. East wall if you have one. The [Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) page already unpacked South shade plus water and tight eye as a humidity judge. A small dark fig in deep shade is a late small dark fig, which defeats the hire.

Pots: one **#3** for the early dark bowl, potted up before June. Fertigate once it is a plant. Shuffle it. Winter page: barely moist, not a heated den. Early fruit does not make the pot hardy. Container roots still freeze at the rim around the 15°F talk.

Wood: **6–8 inches**, three nodes, off a fruiting early tree. Lignified if you can. Do not mail a June leaf as RdB. Do not mail a late open-eye dark as RdB because it is small.

In-ground: a manageable bush if you do not feed it like a shade tree. Leave crop wood. It fruits on this year’s growth like most common figs; it still does not want a hat-rack for no reason. Dead wood. Crossing wood. TAMU’s five-or-six-trunk chapter if a freeze takes the top — then you still want the early clock more than ever.

Water: even. An early loader that droughts then floods will split the first bowl you waited for. Morning pick. Neck wilt. Give. Soft.

Rain: walk it before the storm if fruit is already giving. A day-early handful in the fridge at about **40°F** is still a bowl. A split handful on the ground is a beetle farm. Dried-fruit beetles want a door. Tight reputation or not, pick.

Late nitrogen: stop so September wood can lignify. Green shoots die first. An early fig can still grow tender late wood if you keep feeding it like a tomato.

Nematodes: dirty ground stays in a pot. RdB is not a nematode solution.

## How I would run it

One plant in a **#3** for the early dark bowl. Wood from a fruiting early tree. **6–8 inches**, three nodes. Pot up before June.

If you already have Syrian Dark #2 and it is feeding you, you may not need RdB. If you have only late berries — Madeira, Scott’s Black, Col de Dame Noire — you do. Pair it with Chicago Hardy if winter is your other problem. Pair it with Alma if you wanted a closed-eye brown tool for the rain weeks. Do not make NSDC your only berry. Jack already said NSDC splits if there is much rain. Small early darks pay rent in bad weather.

Write Ronde de Bordeaux on the pot when a small round dark early fig with a tight eye repeats. Write unknown-early-dark if the clock is all you have measured. The clock is already worth a label.
