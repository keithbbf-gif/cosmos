Col de Dame means the lady’s neck. Noire is the dark one. Blanc is the green one. Mutante is the sport Keith said he was looking forward to tasting — which means as of July 2025 I had **not** plated Mutante. This page is Noire.

Collector consensus: dark, neck, berry, late, wants heat, a fig people compare to the serious French plates. Eye not Celeste-tight. I did not put CDDN on the Fig Jam favorite list. I put Black Madeira and Negra. Same craving, different clocks.

Common type. No wasp. The type is not the problem. The calendar is.

Jack’s [Types of Figs](https://figroots.com/2025/08/17/types-of-figs/) primer: common figs persist without pollen. Col de Dame Noire sits in that bucket in the accounts I use. You do not buy a wasp. You do not brush an ostiole. If your CDDN drops a main crop at marble size every year, look at water and age first, then type. [VERIFY]. A late common fig that never sets is usually a GDD problem, not a Smyrna problem. A Smyrna without a wasp is a leaf bush. This plant should not be that machine.

San Pedro is the other confusion. Desert King needs pollen for the main crop. CDDN is not sold as San Pedro. Do not give it a wasp yard because the neck looked serious in a photo.

Condit’s 1955 monograph has French necked figs and Col de Dame talk in the old piles. I will not pretend I matched our stick to a 1955 plate. The family is old. The marketplace suffixes — CDDN, CDD Noire, Col de Dame Black — are collector shorthand. The fruit still has to finish.

## Late is a trait

Scott’s Black is late. Gold is late. Madeira is late. CDDN is late. Stacking four late figs is how a collection looks full in August and empty in a cool September.

[Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) already said growing degree days are the first filter. Sometimes a listing prints a GDD number. More often it prints a Mediterranean sunset. Count the days you actually have. I will not invent a Saline County ripen date. Write the first soften, if you get one. Write the last hard bead in October. Both numbers are data.

Head start in the hoop. Black pot against an east wall. Do not feed for leaves in September. None of that turns a late fig into Ronde de Bordeaux. It might finish a late fig.

If two years end in hard fruit, believe it. Keep the plant as a pot you start early or graft something earlier on the hole. We graft. We do not stare at green beads to prove we paid French prices.

Breba: UAEX said breba rarely overwinters in Arkansas. A necked French fig may throw a spring fig after a civil winter on protected wood. That is a bonus. That is not the crop. Main crop on current wood is the crop you can plan, and even that crop is late. Do not hat-rack it in January. You will not get an early fig by chopping a late one. You will get a later fig on shorter wood.

Hardiness: I will not call CDDN Chicago-hardy. ANR-1145’s 15–20°F conversation is Celeste, English Brown Turkey, Alma, Hardy Chicago. A late French dark in 8a is a pot-honest trial. A #3 still freezes at the rim. Shuffle it.

## Flavor I will not fake

When it finishes in a dry week, people use words like jam and berry and “finally.” When it does not finish, people use words like waste. Both are weather.

Consensus. Not a homestead score. I did not put Col de Dame Noire on the 25 July 2025 favorite pile. I put Black Madeira, Negra d’Agde, Red Sicilian, NSDC, Adriatic JH. I named CDD Mutante only as looking-forward — untried. Noire is not Mutante. Blanc is not Noire. Grise in the Col de Dame family is another pot. Do not merge four necks because the lady is the same lady.

[VERIFY] our plate before anyone writes a score on FigRoots. Until then this is a climate warning with a famous neck.

Eye: not Celeste-tight. [VERIFY] ours after rain. UAEX: souring when water enters the eye, worse after rain plus heat. Late storms hit late fruit. Large-enough fruit. Pick what softened. A day-early necked fig in the fridge at about **40°F** is still a fig. A split fig on the ground is a beetle farm.

Birds: dark, late, fewer fruit — they will take each one. Net. A green sister hides a little. This one does not.

Split: late storms. Drought then flood. Overripe hang on a fig you waited all year for. Even water. Pride is how you lose the only ripe one to a Tuesday rain.

Sister slugs: Violette de Bordeaux / Negronne is a small black with deep red pulp. Noire de Caromb is a working dark, productive talk, not a trophy neck. Pastilière is early and drops. Ronde de Bordeaux is small and early. Black Madeira is the late intensity I already named. Mission is the California dark that Alabama called disappointing here. Do not merge a necked late French fig into any of those because the photo was black.

Yellow Long Neck was looking-forward in that interview. A long neck is not a cultivar. Hunt’s long stem is a Georgia peduncle on a small brown. Different job.

## How I would run it

One pot. Wood from a fruiting necked dark fig, not a listing with a French filter. **6–8 inches**, three nodes. Pot up before June. Hoop in spring if we have the house. Fertigate once it is a plant.

**6–8 hours** of sun. East wall. Afternoon cloth in July — South shade plus water is already on Pick The Right Fig. Nematodes: pots.

Do not row it. Do not tell a beginner this is the fig. Tell them Negra and Jack Lily and a tool tree, then CDDN if they still have a hole and a long year.

Pair it with an early fig. Ronde, Pastilière, a Celeste-class brown, Jack Lily. The early one is dinner in a short year. The late one is the maybe.

A neck is also a pick cue. When it wilts and the body gives, you are late enough. When the neck is still a stiff hose and the body is a bead, you are early. Do not wait for a Portuguese postcard black in a Gulf steam week. The introduction page already said ripe is a feel.

If it finishes and the plate is the berry people promised, keep it and take wood. If it finishes and you wanted Negra, you planted the wrong dark. If it does not finish, graft. The hole still has a root system. Fame is not a reason to keep a leaf bush.

Write Col de Dame Noire on the pot when a necked dark late fig repeats and finishes. Write unknown-late-dark-neck if it never quite does. Honesty is a cultivar too.
