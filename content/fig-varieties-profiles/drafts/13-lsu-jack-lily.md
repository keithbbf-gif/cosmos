Jack spelled it Lilly on [The Fig Jam](https://www.thefigjam.co/p/interview-with-a-fig-grower-keith). The site review spells it Lily. The plant does not care. I will use both so a search finds the tree.

He ranked it fourth on his plate list: sweet, moderate berry, very nice balance, very tight eye, small fig. The FigRoots review files it in the Adriatic family, berry type, prolate spheroid, flavor 4.5, sweetness 4, meat 4.5 — October 1, 2024 on that card. One season. One plate. That is more than most leak figs get in public.

It is not an official LSU release. Lazy Dog filed it with the unofficial greens. Same rule as [Hollier](lsu-hollier): say unofficial when you share wood.

## Official six, then this boy’s name

Polozola and Stagg, 2025: [O’Rourke](lsu-orourke), [Champagne](lsu-champagne), [Tiger](lsu-tiger), [LSU Gold](lsu-gold), [LSU Purple](lsu-purple), [Scott’s Black](lsu-scotts-black). Those are the releases. The same article says unnamed program figs left with enthusiasts, and that many circulating “LSU” names have no connection to the breeding. Jack Lily lives in that second sentence. Dean Lee is trying to preserve the real set. Do not help a seller steal a diploma.

Common type. No wasp. Jack already wrote the type primer on [Types of Figs](https://figroots.com/2025/08/17/types-of-figs/). This is not a Smyrna. If your “Jack Lily” drops like [Calimyrna](calimyrna), you have the wrong plant.

## Do not confuse the small green pile

[Celeste](celeste) is also small and tight. I am not a fan of Celeste so far. I said that on The Fig Jam. Jack Lily is the small fig Jack will actually talk about. That difference is the review. Celeste is brown-to-violet sugar. Jack Lily is green skin, berry-strawberry pulp, Adriatic family on the site card.

[Adriatic](adriatic) the California commercial name is a different pile — Condit’s five, white/green, another climate. Adriatic *family* on a FigRoots card means flavor talk: green skin, berry-strawberry pulp, not a dark Bordeaux. Do not upgrade Jack Lily to a numbered Adriatic or to Champagne because someone said “Adriatic.” Families are flavor talk. Cultivars are plates that repeat.

If your “Jack Lily” is purple, you have the wrong stick. If it is yellow-honey with no berry, same. If it is large and late and gold, look at [LSU Gold](lsu-gold). If it is medium yellow, look at Champagne. If it is a leak yellow with pink-red pulp, look at Hollier.

[Verte](verte) / Green Ischia is the other green fig I already said splits on us. Do not merge them to tidy a spreadsheet.

## What the sheets actually wrote

Alabama ANR-1145 does not list Jack Lily. Florida HS27 does not. Condit did not. UAEX will not hand you this name. The university sheets you can cite are the 2025 history’s warning about unofficial LSU stickers, and the older Stagg clip about unnamed selections leaving Hammond and Port Sulphur.

What you *can* cite that is ours: Jack’s Fig Jam ranking, and the [Fig Reviews](https://figroots.com/fig_reviews_archive/) card dated October 1, 2024 — Adriatic family, berry, prolate spheroid, those scores. One season. Do not recycle 2024 as climate.

[Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) still owns GDD, tight eye, birds, South shade and water, nematodes to pots. The [introduction](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/) still owns trusted sellers and 1–3 years to prove type. This page does not restage those essays. It names the small green fig Jack already scored.

## Why a small fig matters here

Humidity judges size. A small tight-eye fig finishes, sheds less storm, and sours less than a Madeira you bragged about. Jack’s whole top of the list is that shape of thought: Red Sicilian, Navid’s Unk, Syrian Dark #2, Jack Lily. Productive. Tight or tight-enough. Berry. Not a dinner-plate fig for a photo.

I will stand on his words. Sweet. Moderate berry. Very nice balance. Very tight eye. Small. I will not add a chocolate-berry speech he did not give.

## The 8a week

Pick: morning. Soft. Small figs hide. Walk the tree. Neck wilt. Give. October 1 on a 2024 card is late for a “small early fig” story. [VERIFY] against this year’s notes. A late small fig is still useful if it hangs through a storm. A late small fig that never softens is a cup of leaves. Write dates. I will not invent a Saline County number, and I will not promote 2024 into a county calendar.

Rain: tight eye is why this plant belongs in 8a talk. Tight is a screen, not a vault. Souring is water in the eye plus heat. Split: small figs still split if you water like a hose after a drought. Even. Not heroic.

Birds: green helps. Tight eye helps. Neither is a net. A ripe fig still smells like lunch.

Rust: rake litter. Yellow then reddish spots, blisters under the leaf. No official rust sentence. Do not write one because LSU is in the rumor of origin.

Sun: **6–8 hours**. East wall. Afternoon cloth in July. A small green fig in deep shade is still shade.

Pots versus ground: we run hundreds of pots, about 25–30 in-ground. We already run this one. Keep the plant. A **#3** you pot up before June is how extras get made. Fertigate once it is a plant. Do not push nitrogen into September. In-ground if the plate keeps matching the card.

Wood: **6–8 inches**, three nodes, off the tree that matched the card, not off a neighbor’s maybe. Unofficial LSU numbers moved around Hammond and Port Sulphur and then around the country. Two Lilies can disagree. Write the source.

## Name and source

Jack Lily, Jack Lilly, LSU Jack Lily, Jack’s Lily. Spell it like the tag you ate. If the wood came through our own fruit, say so. If it came from a forum trade, say so.

Do not pay celebrity prices for a small green fig with an LSU sticker. Ask for fruit photos: size, eye, cut. Ask unofficial. If the seller bristles, keep your money. The introduction page already said that.

## Culinary, honest

Fresh: this is the job. Jack’s plate. The site scores. Small, balanced berry-honey, tight eye. A conversation fig, not a prize pumpkin. Eat it tree-ripe. A small fig picked early is a grassy waste of a ranking.

Jam: you will not fill a winter pantry off a small fig unless you have a hedge. We do not have a Jack Lily hedge. If you get extras, you can put them up. Do not plant this as the kettle tree. Plant Kadota or a loading LSU if you wanted jars. Plant this if you wanted a bowl you will actually talk about.

Kid bowl: a child who likes berry will eat these. A child who wanted a big yellow Gold will call them small. Both can be telling the truth. Give them two. They disappear.

I did not put Jack Lily on *my* Fig Jam favorite list. Jack did. That is enough. I will not steal his ranking and pretend it is mine.
