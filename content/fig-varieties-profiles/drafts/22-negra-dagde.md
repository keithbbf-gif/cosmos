Jack put Negra d’Agde sixth on his list and then said the thing I care about: not very sweet, but more berry flavor than any other fig on the list. Tight eye. Moderate size. Very productive.

The FigRoots card: Dark Berry family, berry type, oblate ovoid, August 15, 2024 on that card, flavor 5, sweetness 3.5, meat 4.5. The sweetness number is the point. People who only chase brix will walk past this tree. People who want a fig that tastes like a berry will stop.

I named it too, on the same interview, in the pile with NSDC and Black Madeira and Red Sicilian. This is not a leak we are guessing at. This is a plate we already put on the internet.

## The name pile

Negra d’Agde, Negra d'Agde, Agde. A French dark. Not Violette de Bordeaux. VdB is the small black, deep-red, acid fight Alabama folded toward Beer’s Black. I will not flatten those. Not Malta Black — that is the clone in `D:\FIGS\Malta_Black_Fig`, captioned as that clone. Not Ronde de Bordeaux, which is the early round clock. Not Red Sicilian, which is Bordeaux family on the same [Fig Reviews](https://figroots.com/fig_reviews_archive/) page and Jack’s first rank.

If a seller writes “Negra” and the fruit is a Madeira monster, you paid a Madeira price for a sticker. If the fruit is small, early, and open-eyed, you may have a different small dark. If it is large-eyed and splits in every rain, you may have wandered toward NSDC. The card is the map: moderate, tight, berry over sugar.

Alabama [ANR-1145](https://www.aces.edu/blog/topics/crop-production/fig-production-guide/) does not give Negra its own paragraph. I will not invent Auburn language to decorate a plate we already scored. Forum average is not a second score. The [Fig Jam](https://www.thefigjam.co/p/interview-with-a-fig-grower-keith) list and the FigRoots card are the on-record numbers. I will not add a homestead score on top.

The [introduction page](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/) already said trusted sellers and 1–3 years to prove type. Ask for fruit photos that look like a moderate dark fig with a tight eye. Ask for a source.

## What the plant is

Common type. Persistent. No wasp. [Jack’s type page](https://figroots.com/2025/08/17/types-of-figs/) already drew that line. Dark. Tight eye — Jack said it, the humidity judge likes it. Productive enough that picking is a job. August 15 on a 2024 card is an earlier clock than Jack Lily’s October 1 card. [VERIFY] this year. Two dates are not a climate model, but they are better than a catalog’s “midseason.” I will not invent a Saline County start date to sit beside those cards.

Size: moderate. Not NSDC. Not Gold. A handful. Productive plus moderate is how you fill a bowl without waiting for dinner-plate figs that split.

Birds: dark. Net or pick. Tight eye does not hide color. UAEX said daily picking may be needed when a tree is in production. This is that tree.

Split: better than NSDC. Jack said NSDC splits if there is much rain. He did not hang that sentence on Negra. Still pick before a storm if the fruit is already soft. UAEX’s souring sentence still applies: water in the eye plus heat after rain. Tight is a door, not a vault. Alabama said the riper the fig, the more it splits and sours. Morning pick.

I am not a fan of Brown Turkey or Celeste so far. Negra is one of the reasons. Once you have this plate, the tool figs taste like tools.

## Flavor without the sugar sermon

I used to think I did not like figs because I had not had a good one. That line is already on the [introduction page](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/). Negra is the correction for people who met a grocery Mission and a sugar Celeste and quit.

It will not taste like a cookie. It will taste like a dark berry with a fig attached. If you want syrup, eat Champagne. If you want both, eat Red Sicilian, which Jack called very productive and sweet with a nice berry.

The sweetness 3.5 next to flavor 5 is the whole argument. I will not “fix” the card by talking the sweetness up. People who want cookie figs should plant something else and leave this hole alone.

Maryland Berry is in my pile on the same interview. Different plant. Same craving class. Black Madeira is in that pile too — late, famous, a long-year fig. Negra is the one that showed up on August 15, 2024 on our card. [VERIFY] the clock this year. Do not make Madeira the only dark berry.

## How I run it in 8a

Sun: **6–8 hours**. UAEX’s minimum. Afternoon cloth in July. East wall if you have one. The [Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) page already unpacked South shade plus water and tight eye. A berry fig in deep shade is a late berry fig.

Pots: we already run it. **#3** / **#5**, potted up before June. Fertigate once it is a plant. Extra **#3**s before we mail anything. Shuffle it. [Our winter page](https://figroots.com/winter-protection/) already said barely moist, not a heated den. Keeper wood does not warm the mix. Container roots still freeze at the rim around the 15°F talk.

Wood: **6–8 inches**, three nodes, off the tree that matched the card. Lignified if you can. Do not mail a look-alike. Do not mail Malta Black as Negra. Do not mail VdB as Negra.

In-ground when it earns the hole. East wall. Even water. This is a keeper class, not a trial you forget in a wagon. TAMU’s five-or-six-trunk chapter if a freeze takes the top. Leave crop wood if the top lived. Dead wood. Crossing wood. Do not hat-rack a berry fig in January unless the plant proved it is a Turkey.

Water: even. Drought then flood splits even a tight-eye fig. Morning pick. Neck wilt. Give. Soft. A day-early fig in the fridge at about **40°F** is still a fig.

Rain: walk it. Tight eye is why we keep it through August. Photograph the ostiole after rain anyway. If a second plant is sweeter and looser-eyed, it is not this. Relabel.

Late nitrogen: stop so September wood can lignify. Green shoots die first.

Nematodes: dirty ground stays in a pot. Negra is not a nematode solution.

## How I would run it

We already run it. Keep the mother tree. Wood off the tree that matched the card. **6–8 inches**, three nodes. Extra **#3**s before we mail anything.

If you do not have it, ask for fruit photos that look like a moderate dark fig with a tight eye, not a Madeira monster. Ask for a source. Do not pay Black Madeira prices for a Negra sticker.

If you have room for two berry figs, keep Negra and Red Sicilian. They teach different mouths. If you have room for three, add NSDC and pick it like a job in a rain week. If you have only late berries, pair Negra with Ronde or Jack Lily so a short year still feeds you.

Write Negra d’Agde on the pot that matches the review. If a second plant is close but sweeter and looser-eyed, it is not this. Relabel. The berry-first plate is the cultivar.
