Jack’s number one: very productive, sweet, nice berry, medium size.

The FigRoots card: Bordeaux family, berry type, oblate spheroid, October 1, 2024 on that card, flavor 5, sweetness 4, meat 4.5. I named Red Sicilian in my own pile on the same interview. This is not a rumor from a marketplace.

A kid who ranks figs will rank the one that shows up. That is not a lesser reason. A rare fig you eat twice is a story. A medium fig you eat for three weeks is a crop.

## Sicily is a word

Red Sicilian, Sicilian Red, Palermo something, a neighbor’s “the red one.” Our inventory name is the tree that matched the review. Wood off that tree. If a second Red Sicilian arrives and does not match, it is not a debate. It is two plants. Unknown-red-sicilian-B until it repeats.

I will not write a history of Sicilian figs to decorate this page. Condit would need a chapter. We need a bowl.

Bordeaux family on our [Fig Reviews](https://figroots.com/fig_reviews_archive/) card is a flavor-family word, not a merge with Violette de Bordeaux or Ronde de Bordeaux. VdB is the small black, deep-red, acid fight. RdB is the early round clock. Negra d’Agde is Dark Berry family on the same archive — berry first, sweetness 3.5. Keep the cards apart. Malta Black is the clone in the folder. Do not pour a Sicilian sticker onto that drive name.

Alabama [ANR-1145](https://www.aces.edu/blog/topics/crop-production/fig-production-guide/) does not give Red Sicilian a paragraph. I will not invent Auburn language. The [Fig Jam](https://www.thefigjam.co/p/interview-with-a-fig-grower-keith) list and the FigRoots card are the on-record numbers. I will not add a homestead score on top. Forum average is not a second card.

The [introduction page](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/) already said trusted sellers and 1–3 years to prove type. A marketplace “Red Sicilian” without a cut photo is a color word.

## What we are holding

Common type. Persistent. No wasp. [Jack’s type page](https://figroots.com/2025/08/17/types-of-figs/) already drew that line. Bordeaux family on our card — dark berry clan, not a honey Adriatic. Medium. Sweet enough that Jack said sweet before he said berry. Negra is the opposite order. Keep both if you can. They teach different mouths.

October 1, 2024 versus Negra’s August 15, 2024. [VERIFY] this year. If Red Sicilian is later, it is a late bowl, not a replacement for the early darks. Pair it. Do not make it the only berry. Jack Lily’s card is also October 1, 2024 — small, tight, Adriatic family. Different shape, different job. Ronde de Bordeaux is the early dark if you wanted food while this one is still hard.

Eye: the card does not brag tight the way Jack bragged Syrian Dark #2 and Jack Lily. Look at ours after rain. [VERIFY]. Productive plus a loose eye is a souring job. Productive plus a decent eye is a gift. UAEX’s souring sentence still applies: water in the eye plus heat after rain. Alabama said the riper the fig, the more it splits and sours. Morning pick.

Birds: dark, medium, lots of them. Net. Or pick like it is a job, because it is. UAEX said daily picking may be needed when a tree is in production. Productivity is why Jack ranked it first. Productivity is also why the birds learn the address in a week.

Split: not the NSDC warning. Jack hung the rain-split sentence on Nuestra Señora del Carmen, not on this one. Still a storm week. Morning. A day-early fig in the fridge at about **40°F** is still a fig. A split fig on the ground is a beetle farm.

I am not a fan of Brown Turkey or Celeste so far. Red Sicilian is in my pile for a reason. Once you have a sweet-plus-berry bowl that keeps coming, the tool figs taste like tools.

## Flavor is a loaded shoot

Jack said very productive, sweet, nice berry, medium size. The card said flavor 5, sweetness 4, meat 4.5. That is the balance. I will not talk the sweetness down to make it sound more like Negra. I will not talk the berry down to make it sound like Champagne. The bowl is the review.

Navid’s Unk Dark Greek sat second on Jack’s list. Syrian Dark #2 sat third. Jack Lilly fourth. NSDC fifth. Negra sixth. Red Sicilian sat first because it showed up and ate well. I will not rearrange his list to make a rarer name look serious.

If the fruit on a new stick is berry-first and not sweet, you may have drifted toward Negra. If it is sweet and brown and small, you may have drifted toward a Turkey. If it is large-eyed and unzips in rain, you may have drifted toward NSDC. The card is the map.

## How I run it in 8a

Sun: **6–8 hours**. UAEX’s minimum. Afternoon cloth in July. East wall if you have one. The [Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) page already unpacked South shade plus water. A productive fig in deep shade is a shy fig you will then blame.

Pots: mother tree stays. Extra **#3** / **#5**, potted up before June. Fertigate once it is a plant. Do not starve a producer and then call it shy. Shuffle it. [Our winter page](https://figroots.com/winter-protection/) already said barely moist, not a heated den. Container roots still freeze at the rim around the 15°F talk. Chicago Hardy and Smith are the colder names on that page. This fig still has to come inside.

Wood: **6–8 inches**, three nodes, off the review plant. Lignified if you can. This is a share fig. Productive wood is how a collection stays friendly. Do not mail a look-alike. Do not mail Malta Black as Red Sicilian.

In-ground when it earns it. East wall. Even water. TAMU’s five-or-six-trunk chapter if a freeze takes the top. Leave crop wood if the top lived. Dead wood. Crossing wood. Do not hat-rack a berry fig in January unless the plant proved it is a Turkey. UAEX’s Celeste prune warning is the Southern sentence I keep in my pocket.

Water: even. Drought then flood splits a loaded shoot. Morning pick. Neck wilt. Give. Soft. Not a calendar I will invent beside the 2024 card. [VERIFY] this year’s first soften and write it on the tag.

Late nitrogen: stop so September wood can lignify. Green shoots die first. A late bowl still needs lignified wood if you want the plant next year.

Nematodes: dirty ground stays in a pot. A first-rank fig is not a nematode solution.

## How I would run it

Mother tree stays. Cuttings **6–8 inches**, three nodes, off the review plant. Extra pots. This is a share fig.

If you do not have it, this is a better first “berry fig” purchase than Black Madeira. Madeira is late and famous. Red Sicilian is the one Jack ate enough of to rank first.

Pair it with Negra if you wanted the berry-first lesson. Pair it with an early dark if October 1 was the 2024 clock and you did not want to wait. Pair it with Jack Lily if you wanted a tight-eye small green in the same late window. Do not make NSDC the only large berry in a rain year.

Write Red Sicilian on the pot that matches the card.
