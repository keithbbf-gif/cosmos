Jack’s third: very productive, extremely tight eye, strong berry, moderately sweet, small fig.

That is a better cultivar description than most official releases get. I did not need to add a French village.

Common type. Persistent. No wasp. Jack already defined that on [Types of Figs](https://figroots.com/2025/08/17/types-of-figs/). A numbered dark fig from a Syrian group that collectors split because the first one and the second one were not the same plate. The **#2** is the inventory. Do not drop it to sound elegant.

I named other darks on [The Fig Jam](https://www.thefigjam.co/p/interview-with-a-fig-grower-keith) — Negra, Black Madeira, Red Sicilian, NSDC. Jack put this one on his short list. That is the on-record sentence. I am not a fan of Brown Turkey or Celeste so far. A small tight berry fig is one of the reasons.

## Why this plant is an 8a lecture

Extremely tight eye. Small. Productive. Berry. That is the humidity syllabus on [Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) made into a tree.

NSDC is large and open and splits. Jack said it splits if there is much rain. Madeira is late and famous. Syrian Dark #2 is the fig you eat while those two are having a weather problem.

Moderately sweet. Strong berry. Same family of mouth as Negra, with more production talk and a smaller body. Keep both if you can. They are not duplicates. [Fig Reviews](https://figroots.com/fig_reviews_archive/) already scored Negra and Red Sicilian. This page does not get a fake card. Jack’s microphone is the card.

TAMU’s [disease handbook](https://plantdiseasehandbook.tamu.edu/food-crops/fruit-crops/fig/) puts Celeste, Texas Everbearing, and Alma in the closed-eye resistant column and Magnolia and Kadota in the open-eye souring column. Syrian Dark #2 is not on that Texas table. Jack’s “extremely tight” is the closest sentence we have. Photograph the ostiole. Get the lens close. If the eye is not extremely tight, it is not Jack’s #3.

UAEX’s souring sentence is still water in the eye plus heat after rain. Tight is a screen, not a vault. Physics can still split a balloon. A week of rain plus heat can still put water where you did not invite it. Even water anyway. A drought then a flood will unzip a small fig if you let it hang for a prettier dark.

Morning pick. Neck wilt. Give. Soft. A day-early small berry fig in the fridge at about **40°F** is still a fig. A split fig on the ground is a waste of a productive tree.

Birds: small and dark and many. They will graze. Net or pick a lot. A green fig lasts longer. This is not a green fig. Productivity is why a twelve-year-old ranked productive figs first and third. A rare fig you eat twice is a story. A small fig you eat for three weeks is a crop.

## Numbers and neighbors

Syrian Dark, Syrian Dark #1, #2, a neighbor’s “Syrian.” If the eye is not extremely tight, it is not Jack’s #3. Relabel.

Navid’s Unk Dark Greek sat second on Jack’s list — similar to Red Sicilian, a little tighter, a little smaller. Another small dark. Do not merge Greek and Syrian because both are small and dark. The Unk name is honest. Keep it honest.

Red Sicilian is Jack’s first: very productive, sweet, nice berry, medium. Bordeaux family on our review card. Different size. Different sugar. Keep both.

Negra d’Agde is sixth on Jack’s list: not very sweet, more berry than any other fig on that list, tight eye, moderate size, very productive. Berry-first. This one is strong berry and moderately sweet. The order of the words matters.

Ronde de Bordeaux is the small early dark people plant for a short year. I did not name RdB on The Fig Jam. Same job class. Different name. Do not merge them on a label to feel organized.

Chicago Hardy is the small dark resprout story on our [winter protection](https://figroots.com/winter-protection/) page. Winter is that plant’s job. Tight productive berry is this one’s job.

[An Introduction to Figs](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/) already said it takes **1–3 years** to prove type. A first-year pot can lie about volume. A first-year cup can lie about the eye. Wait for a loaded shoot and a wet week. Both are the type test.

I will not invent a Saline County soften date. Write the first soften on the tag. [VERIFY]. Small plus productive often means an earlier bowl than Madeira. Often is not a calendar I will print.

## Rust, wood, winter

Rust is the Gulf leaf tax. Yellow-green flecks, then yellowish-brown, blisters under the leaf, defoliation while you are still picking. A productive small fig that drops its leaves in August will still try to finish what is already soft. It will not finish the last beads. Rake. Do not leave a wet skirt of last year’s leaves. I will not advertise rust-proof.

Mosaic mottles leaves on a lot of dark collector figs. Mottled is not a reason to discard a tree that fruits. Dwarfed fruit is a reason to take cleaner wood off the tree Jack ranked.

Nematodes: pots if the ground is dirty. This is not Alma and it is not the LSU Purple rumor.

Cold: Alabama ANR-1145’s 15–20°F talk names Celeste, English Brown Turkey, Alma, and Hardy Chicago. Syrian Dark #2 is not on that list. Treat in-ground as a trial that has to earn the hole. Treat a **#3** as a pot you still shuffle. Barely moist in the shop. Not a heated den.

Do not hat-rack a small productive berry fig to make a standard. You will eat less. Crop wood is the plan. If it dies to the crown, five or six trunks at two feet, TAMU’s chapter, and know that a resprout year still wants an early partner. Small plus productive is how you get food on new wood if the year is long enough. [VERIFY] after a bite.

Do not fertilize late. Succulent September wood is how a humidity fig writes a tender will.

## How I would run it

Keeper. Wood off the tree Jack ranked, **6–8 inches**, three nodes. Alabama likes 3–6 inch pencil wood. We run a little longer. Extra pots. This is a share fig for people who live in steam. Pot up to a **#3** before June. Fertigate once it is a plant, not while it is a stick. Do not starve a producer and then call it shy.

Sun: **6–8 hours**. Alabama wants a full eight so fruit dries after rain. We give morning sun and afternoon cloth in July. A small dark fig in deep shade is still shade. A small dark fig in a west fry on black plastic will cook and then split when you water it.

In-ground when it earns it. East wall if you have one, clay that drains, mulch over shallow roots. Do not plant it in last year’s tomato hole. Do not make it the only fig if you also wanted a large dry-week conversation — plant NSDC for that week and pick this one when it rains.

If a second “Syrian Dark #2” arrives and the eye is loose, it is not a debate. It is two plants. Unknown-syrian-dark-B until it repeats. If you drop the #2, you are starting a new pile. I have enough piles.

Write Syrian Dark #2 on the pot that matches an extremely tight, small, productive berry fig.
