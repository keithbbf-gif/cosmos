A small black fig with pulp the color of a wine reduction is why people tolerate the name pile. Alabama’s sheet bolded **Bordeaux** and then listed Beer’s Black, Negronne, Violette de Bordeaux as if that were settled. Condit and a century of French yards treat Negronne / Violette de Bordeaux as the same small fig. Beer’s Black is the Mission-adjacent argument I will not flatten to tidy a paragraph.

If your plant is large, Mission-shaped, and pale inside, you do not have VdB. If it is small, almost black, deep red, a little acid in the berry, you might.

## The name pile

Violette de Bordeaux, Negronne, Bordeaux, Figue de Bordeaux. Petite Negri. Beer’s Black if a seller is in a mood. Black Jack if the seller is in a worse mood. [ANR-1145](https://www.aces.edu/blog/topics/crop-production/fig-production-guide/) put Beer’s Black on the Bordeaux line and called the fruit large, almost black, deep-red pulp, distinctive but agreeable acid, medium eye. That sheet is useful for the acid and the eye. It is not a license to pour a Mission-sized plant into a small-black hole.

Petite Negri is Mike McConkey’s 1980s introduction. Alabama thinks it is Bordeaux or a sport and they could not fruit it. Another name. Same advice: plate.

We have Malta Black in its own folder on the drive. Caption *that* clone as that clone. Do not promote it to VdB because both are dark. Condit’s Malta is Celeste — a small brown sugar fig. Three uses of an island word. Three plants if the fruit says so.

Ronde de Bordeaux shares the city and not the plant. Red Sicilian sits in the Bordeaux family on our [Fig Reviews](https://figroots.com/fig_reviews_archive/) card. Negra d’Agde is its own card. Do not pour them into VdB to tidy INDEX. The word Bordeaux is a family argument. It is not a cultivar.

Write the name you can defend with a photo of a small black fig and a deep red cut. If you cannot, write unknown-small-black. The [introduction page](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/) already gave you 1–3 years to prove type. Use them.

## What the plant is

Common type. Persistent. No wasp. [Jack’s type page](https://figroots.com/2025/08/17/types-of-figs/) already drew that line. Brebas pyriform with thick necks when the wood lives; main crop variable, often without necks. Medium eye in Alabama’s sentence. Excellent fresh or dried in a climate that can dry. Fairly hardy. Well adapted in the South and Southwest on that sheet — which is a kinder Southern sentence than Mission got. Mission disappointed Alabama on hardiness. VdB did not get that slap.

Flavor: berry with a pleasant acid. Not sugar-Celeste. Not honey-Kadota. This is the family Jack is ranking when he talks Negra d’Agde and Red Sicilian — dark berry, not syrup. VdB itself was not on his short list on [The Fig Jam](https://www.thefigjam.co/p/interview-with-a-fig-grower-keith). Negra was. Different plants. Same craving. I will not invent a homestead score that puts VdB next to Negra so the page looks finished. When our plate repeats I will write it.

Eye: medium is not tight. Wet August is a test. Small fruit helps. Morning pick helps. UAEX’s souring sentence still applies: water in the eye plus heat after rain. Tight is a door. Medium is a wider door. A “dried fig” plan in our humidity is a dehydrator, not a roof. Alabama’s “excellent dried” is a California and Southwest sentence living in a steam bath.

## Cold and wood

Fairly hardy on the Southern sheets. Not Chicago Hardy as a brand. Alabama’s hardy group for 15–20°F talk is Celeste, English Brown Turkey, Alma, Hardy Chicago. VdB is “fairly hardy,” which is a softer word. In 8a it is a reasonable in-ground trial if you like small black figs. Breba still needs last year’s wood and a civil spring. UAEX: breba rarely overwinters. Main crop is the crop. Do not buy VdB as a two-crop machine. That is Desert King’s brochure problem, and Desert King is a San Pedro.

Prune like you wanted fruit, not like you wanted a hat rack. Dead wood. Crossing wood. Leave the rest. A January saw on last year’s wood deletes breba and can delete a lot of main-crop wood if you treat every fig like a Turkey. UAEX already wrote the Celeste version of that warning. I treat VdB that way until the plant proves it is a resprout Turkey.

Pots: it stays small enough to be a honest **#3** / **#5** fig. That is a gift. Shuffle it. Do not let a “hardy French fig” sit in a wind tunnel in January. [Our winter page](https://figroots.com/winter-protection/) already said barely moist, not a heated den. Container roots still freeze at the rim around the 15°F talk. Fairly hardy tops do not warm the mix.

## How I run it in 8a

Sun: **6–8 hours**. UAEX’s minimum if you hope to harvest. Afternoon cloth in July. East wall if you have one. The [Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) page already unpacked South shade plus water.

Pots: pot up to a **#3** before June. Fertigate once it is a plant. Most of our collection lives there. A small black fig that stays small is how a wagon stays honest.

Wood: **6–8 inches**, three nodes, off a fruiting small-black tree. Lignified if you can. Do not mail a June leaf. Do not mail Beer’s Black as VdB because both photographs are dark.

Water: even. Drought then a flood is how fruit splits. Alabama said the riper the fig, the more it splits and sours. Morning pick. Neck wilt. Give. Soft. Not a calendar date I will invent for Saline County. [VERIFY] your first soften and write it on the tag.

Rain: medium eye. Walk the tree before the storm if fruit is already giving. A day-early fig in the fridge at about **40°F** is still a fig. Photograph the ostiole after rain. If your “VdB” sours like an open-eye celebrity, [VERIFY] type. You may have the Mission-adjacent plant Alabama folded in.

Birds: small and black is still a billboard. Net or pick. A green fig lasts longer. This is not a green fig. One neglected bowl teaches the address.

Late nitrogen: stop the feed so September wood can lignify. Green shoots die first. A late freeze after budbreak is cruel to a plant that wanted to move. Cover or roll.

Nematodes: dirty ground goes in a pot. VdB is not a nematode solution.

## How I would run it

One plant for the berry craving if you do not already have Negra d’Agde. If you have Negra, you may not need this hole. If you have neither, Negra is the one Jack already scored. VdB is the French name people recognize. Red Sicilian is the one he ranked first — sweet plus berry, productive. Different mouth. Keep two if you have room. Do not keep five dark names you have not plated.

Wood from a fruiting small-black tree. **6–8 inches**, three nodes. Pot up before June.

If the fruit is pale inside two years running, change the label and keep the hole for a real berry fig. If the fruit is deep red and you like it, take wood. Share it with the name you proved.

I am not a fan of Brown Turkey or Celeste so far. A small black acid berry is closer to why I stayed in this crop. That is a craving. It is not a score I will hang on VdB until the plate repeats.

Write Violette de Bordeaux / Negronne when the small black, deep-red plate repeats. Write Beer’s Black only if you mean that fight, in a different sentence.
