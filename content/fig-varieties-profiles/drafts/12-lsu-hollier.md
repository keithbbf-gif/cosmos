LSU named six figs. The internet named twenty. Hollier is one of the ones that walked off the research ground in a cutting bundle and grew a life of its own.

Stagg said it in the older news clip: besides the six, unnamed figs from the program have been grown and distributed by enthusiasts. The 2025 history says the same thing a second time, and adds that many varieties circulate as LSU releases with no connection to the breeding. Both sentences can be true about different sticks.

Hollier, by grower consensus, is a yellow-skinned fig with pink to red pulp and a plate people call complex. Lazy Dog planted it in 2023 and put it among the most complex they had tasted — their plate, their year. I did not name Hollier on [The Fig Jam](https://www.thefigjam.co/p/interview-with-a-fig-grower-keith). I will not steal their adjective and tattoo it on our tree.

## Unofficial is not a slur

Unofficial means the university did not put a brochure behind it. The wood can still be good. [Jack Lily](lsu-jack-lily) is unofficial and Jack put it on his short list. Official is a paper. Flavor is a plate.

The official six are [O’Rourke](lsu-orourke), [Champagne](lsu-champagne), [Tiger](lsu-tiger), [LSU Gold](lsu-gold), [LSU Purple](lsu-purple), [Scott’s Black](lsu-scotts-black). Say those names when you mean releases. Say Hollier when you mean Hollier. Do not upgrade a leak to a diploma because the letters L-S-U sell a cutting.

What unofficial *does* mean: the name is unstable. You will see Hollier, LSU Hollier, and “that yellow LSU with red pulp.” You will see sellers upgrade it to a release. You will see two Holliers that do not match. Write the source on the pot. Write the year you ate it. Unknown is cleaner than a confident wrong name.

Common type in every account I trust. No wasp. If a Hollier drops its crop like a Smyrna, you do not have Hollier. Jack already defined the types on [Types of Figs](https://figroots.com/2025/08/17/types-of-figs/). Caducous is Calimyrna’s job, not this one.

## Do not confuse the yellow LSU pile

[Champagne](lsu-champagne) is the official medium yellow, tested as Golden Celeste, amber-to-honey talk, Celeste-class calendar. If your yellow fig cuts amber with no pink, you may have Champagne with a story.

[LSU Gold](lsu-gold) is the official large yellow-to-gold, later clock, upright hog. Alabama’s stale Gold note said strawberry pulp and a honeydew leak. Florida said pink-to-red pulp on Gold. The sheets disagree. A yellow fig with red pulp is not automatically Hollier, and it is not automatically Gold. [VERIFY] size, calendar, and the cut on the same bench.

[Kadota](kadota) is Dottato, canning, open-enough eye. [Calimyrna](calimyrna) is a Smyrna. [Peter’s Honey](peters-honey) is the Lattarula argument. None of those get an LSU sticker because the skin is yellow.

Scott’s Yellow is a different leak. Do not merge Scott and Hollier because both walked off without a press release.

## What the sheets actually wrote

Polozola and Stagg, 2025: six named releases. Unnamed program figs distributed by enthusiasts. Many circulating “LSU” names with no connection to the original breeding. Dean Lee received a donation of most of the LSU cultivars and other promising varieties, with the aim of preserving them and sharing cuttings. That last sentence is the university trying to put the real set back on a bench. It is not a Hollier release note.

The older “continues fig research” clip: same six, plus unnamed figs grown and distributed by enthusiasts. Hammond and Port Sulphur held elite wood. Numbers left.

Alabama ANR-1145 does not list Hollier. Florida HS27 does not list Hollier. Condit never saw this name. UAEX will not recommend it by cultivar. [Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) still applies — GDD, tight eye, pots if nematodes, South shade and water. The [introduction](https://figroots.com/an-introduction-to-figs/) still applies — trusted sellers, 1–3 years to prove type.

I will not invent a county-agent flavor paragraph that does not exist. Grower consensus is yellow skin, pink-to-red pulp, “complex.” Mark the limit. Their plate is not our score.

## The 8a week

Pick: morning. Soft. Neck wilt. I will not invent a season. Watch it against Champagne and Gold on the same bench. Earlier than Gold and redder inside is the rumor. Rumors are not dates. [VERIFY] your first soften and write it on the tag. I will not invent a Saline County number.

Rain: assume Gulf humidity breeding, then [VERIFY] the eye. Yellow figs still split. Souring is water in the eye plus heat. Pick before you hang it for a prettier photograph.

Birds: yellow skin helps a little. Ripe fruit still smells like lunch. Net or pick.

Rust: rake litter. Yellow then reddish spots, blisters under the leaf. No official rust sentence. Do not write one.

Sun: **6–8 hours**. East wall. Afternoon cloth in July.

Pots versus ground: we run hundreds of pots, about 25–30 in-ground. I said that on The Fig Jam. Like any named stick that did not come with a pedigree: one plant. **#3**. Pot up before June. Fertigate once it is a plant. Do not build a row. Do not mail cuttings as “LSU official” to a first-year grower. In-ground only after the fruit repeats and you still want the hole.

Wood: **6–8 inches**, three nodes, off the plant that matched, not off a blender listing. If the fruit is ordinary, you lost a #3 and a year. That is the collection. Dead-outs sting. I said that on the record. Hollier is not a promise.

## The ethics of the name

If you sell cuttings, say unofficial. If you post a photo, say unofficial. The AgCenter is trying to preserve the real set at Dean Lee and share wood. Muddying the six helps no one, including Hollier. The leak figs deserve their own honest names. They do not deserve a stolen diploma.

If the fruit is excellent, keep the tree. Graft it onto a boring hole. Share it as Hollier-from-[source], fruiting 20XX. That is how collector names stay useful.

## Culinary, honest

Fresh: that is the only reason to keep a leak. Pink-to-red pulp on a yellow skin is why people talk. “Complex” is Lazy Dog’s word for their year. I will eat ours when it repeats and say what it is. Until then I will not write a flavor wheel.

Jam: I will not promise a kettle from a fig I have not loaded. If you get a pile of ripe yellow-red cuts, you can put them up like any honey-berry middle fig. If you get six fruit, eat them.

Kid bowl: a yellow fig a child can see. Red pulp is a surprise if they expected Kadota honey. Do not tell them it is an official LSU release. Tell them it is a good fig if it is a good fig.

## How I would run it

One plant. Prove it. Compare it to Champagne and Gold on the same week. Keep the better yellow. Relabel the rest.

Write Hollier on the pot when a yellow fig with pink-red pulp repeats and your source was not a blender listing. Write LSU only as the neighborhood the stick came from. The neighborhood is crowded.
