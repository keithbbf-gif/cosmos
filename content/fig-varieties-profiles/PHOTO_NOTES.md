## Rules that do not bend

1. **Ours first.** Hunt the folders below before Commons or USDA.
2. **Named folders are not proof of type.** `Malta_Black_Fig` and `El Dorado Fig` are stills of *those trees*. Caption the clone we have.
3. **No success-rate banners.** No “best fig 2026.”
4. **PD fills** need URL + license in the media caption, and the sentence “not our tree.”
5. **WordPress stays Draft.**
6. If a variety-named folder exists on the live drive and is not listed here, use it and [VERIFY] the spelling. Do not invent a folder in git.
