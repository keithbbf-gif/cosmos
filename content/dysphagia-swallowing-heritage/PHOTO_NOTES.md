# Photo Notes — Art Direction (Dysphagia / Swallowing Series)

Staged guidance for a later SLPWOW.com editor. No live CMS writes. **Cleared PD/CC portraits** live in `assets/portraits/`; see `RIGHTS.md`.

## What we are making

A magazine series, not a textbook plate section. Each profile should have **one** lead aim if rights allow: a portrait, or, for the instrument people, one object (a rigid esophagoscope, a 1898 bismuth plate, a 1983 book spine). Essays may use one historical object — never a collage of uncredited faces.

## Tone

- Warm paper, not neon clinic.
- Full names and life dates in the caption, then a short credit line in smaller type.
- Do not put a modern stock “therapist thickening a cup” on an 1898 essay.
- Do not colorize historical photographs for the first run.
- Do not run a still from a living patient’s swallow study. Ever.

## Caption formula

```
[Full name], [year of photograph if known].
Credit: [photographer or “photographer unknown”], [collection], [license].
```

Object caption:

```
[Object], [year].
Credit: [collection], [license].
```

## Placeholder (required when rights fail)

Use this block, unaltered, under the title or in the lead slot:

> **Portrait placeholder.** No public-domain or clearly licensed portrait located as of the pack date. See `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` for hunt status. Do not invent or generate a substitute likeness.

A gray frame with the person’s name and dates is acceptable in WordPress. A generated “historical” face is not.

## Hunt order (next editor / graphics pass)

1. Wikimedia Commons — confirm the **file page** license, not a Google thumbnail.
2. Library of Congress, Smithsonian NPG, Wellcome Collection, Gallica, Deutsche Fotothek, NLM Images from the History of Medicine, BIU Santé (Licence Ouverte).
3. University archives (Harvard Countway; Jefferson / Temple for Jackson; Northwestern for Logemann; Johns Hopkins for Donner and Jones; NIH for Bosma; Medical College of Wisconsin for Dodds).
4. ASHA Archives. Many photographs are viewable; **redistribution rights are not automatically granted**.
5. Family or estate — last, and only with a written license.

Stop the hunt when a file is PD, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, Licence Ouverte, or a museum open-access equivalent. Do not download “fair use” press photos into this repo.

## Objects that beat a weak portrait

- Cannon-era bismuth radiograph (if a PD plate can be found).
- A Jackson foreign-body card or safety-pin display (Mutter / College of Physicians of Philadelphia — ask).
- 1983 Logemann book spine (cover art is probably still in copyright; photograph the physical book only with permission).
- *Dysphagia* volume 1, number 1 (1986) table of contents — typeset, not a face.
