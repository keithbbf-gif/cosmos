# Julie Cichero

*Educational history for SLPWOW. Not a treatment plan and not a substitute for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

“Honey-thick” does not survive translation. Julie A. Y. Cichero’s public work — University of Queensland, the International Dysphagia Diet Standardisation Initiative from 2013, the framework papers in *Dysphagia* and allied journals, 2016–17 — is a translator’s career with a laboratory behind it. She was living when this pack was staged. Published papers and IDDSI documents only.

## A dialect problem with an industry attached

The American National Dysphagia Diet of 2002 tried to officialize nectar and honey. Kitchens still improvised. Thickener companies still sold a local faith. Cichero’s group and the international committee treated texture as something you could test without asking a liquid to taste like a flower. Levels instead of poetry. The poetry had been doing real harm in research: papers that could not be compared.

This magazine will not print the tests as a household checklist. The historical fact is that an Australian SLP became one of the names on a global vocabulary. Steele’s Toronto instrumentation is the other name this pack puts beside hers. Two labs, one dialect problem.

Queensland as an address matters because IDDSI is easy to misfile as a North American committee product. It was international on purpose. Australian texture research had already been arguing with British and Japanese vocabularies. Cichero’s name on the framework papers is a record of that argument, not a personal brand. The initiative’s own site will give you the committee list. Read the list. Founder myths are a kind of nectar — local, sweet, untranslatable. The committee list is drier, and truer. Read it before you credit a single postcode with a global vocabulary. Queensland is one address on that list, not the list.

## What later people kept

A number on a tray ticket that can travel. A research language.

What they had to argue with: pleasure, culture, the pantry, and the ethics literature that treats a thickened cup as a moral object, not only a flow object.

Living — no family, no home. Portrait: placeholder.

A framework that travels still has to land in a kitchen that did not sit on the committee. That landing is someone else’s job. Cichero’s job, in this pack, is the 2013 convening and the papers that made nectar look provincial.

## Residue

A 2013 convening and a 2016 framework. The residue is a level that still has to be attached to a person by someone licensed to attach it.
