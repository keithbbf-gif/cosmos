# Bibliography — Dysphagia & Swallowing Heritage

Sources used or checked while staging this pack (14 September 2026). This is a working shelf, not a complete dysphagia library. Prefer the primary paper or the institutional necrology over a blog.

## Physiology and early imaging

- Magendie, F. *Précis élémentaire de physiologie.* Paris: Méquignon-Marvis, successive editions from 1816–17. Deglutition sits inside the alimentary chapters.
- Cannon, W. B. “The Movements of the Stomach Studied by Means of the Röntgen Rays.” *American Journal of Physiology* 1 (1898): 359–382.
- Cannon, W. B., and A. Moser. “The Movements of the Food in the Esophagus.” *American Journal of Physiology* 1 (1898): 435–444.
- Cannon, W. B. *The Mechanical Factors of Digestion.* London: Edward Arnold, 1911.

## Endoscopy and the esophagus

- Killian, G. Contemporary reports of the 1897 Freiburg foreign-body bronchoscopy; later textbook traces in Killian’s own lectures. Confirm a citation from the German primary before printing a minute-by-minute scene.
- Jackson, C. *Bronchoscopy and Esophagoscopy: A Manual of Peroral Endoscopy and Laryngeal Surgery.* Philadelphia: W. B. Saunders, successive editions (1922 onward).
- Jackson, C. *The Life of Chevalier Jackson: An Autobiography.* New York: Macmillan, 1938.
- Mosher, H. P. “The Lower End of the Oesophagus at Birth and in the Adult.” Semon Lecture, 1929; journal version in the British laryngology literature of that year.
- Hill, F. “Harris Peyton Mosher, M.D., D.Sc., LL.D. 1867–1954.” *Annals of Otology, Rhinology & Laryngology* 63 (1954).
- Hurst, A. F. Papers on cardiospasm / achalasia and barium interpretation from Guy’s Hospital, 1910s–1930s. See also *A Twentieth Century Physician* (autobiographical, 1949 posthumous).

## Infant swallow and Hopkins

- Bosma, J. F. *Anatomy of the Infant Head.* Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1986.
- Bosma, J. F., H. M. Truby, and J. Lind. Cry and neonatal-transition cine papers, *Acta Paediatrica* supplement 163 (1965).
- “Dr. James F. Bosma, 85, led swallowing research.” Baltimore *Sun*, 28 June 2001.
- “Martin W. Donner.” Baltimore *Sun*, 15 April 1992 (death 13 April 1992).
- Donner memorials in *Dysphagia* 7 (1992), including Bosma’s note.
- Jones, B., and M. W. Donner, eds. *Normal and Abnormal Swallowing: Imaging in Diagnosis and Therapy.* New York: Springer, 1991 (and later edition).

## The SLP turn and the 1983 book

- Logemann, J. A. *Evaluation and Treatment of Swallowing Disorders.* San Diego: College-Hill, 1983. Second edition, Austin: Pro-Ed, 1998.
- Northwestern Now. “Jerilyn Logemann, Pioneer in Swallowing Disorders, Dies at 72.” June 2014. Death date 19 June 2014.
- Feinberg News Center obituary, 23 June 2014 (Ph.D. 1968; faculty from 1978).
- Groher, M. E., ed. *Dysphagia: Diagnosis and Management.* successive editions from the mid-1980s (Butterworth / later Elsevier with Crary).
- Early VA and head-and-neck SLP papers of the 1970s (Larsen and others) — cite the specific paper in the essay, not a textbook rumor.

## Endoscopy of swallowing

- Langmore, S. E., K. Schatz, and N. Olsen. “Fiberoptic Endoscopic Examination of Swallowing Safety: A New Procedure.” *Dysphagia* 2, no. 4 (1988): 216–219.
- Langmore, S. E. *Endoscopic Evaluation and Treatment of Swallowing Disorders.* New York: Thieme, 2001.
- Silberman, H. D., H. Wilf, and J. A. Tucker. “Flexible Fiberoptic Nasopharyngolaryngoscope.” *Annals of Otology, Rhinology & Laryngology* 85 (1976): 640–645. (Instrument, not yet a swallow protocol.)

## Milwaukee physiology

- Society of Abdominal Radiology. Wylie J. Dodds Research Award page (memorial facts: died 1992, age 58; MCW; manometry + visual dynamics).
- Dodds, W. J., J. A. Logemann, and P. J. Stewart. Physiology-of-swallowing papers in *AJR*, *Dysphagia*, and *Gastroenterology*, 1980s–early 1990s. Cite the specific title in the article when a claim is sharp.
- Shaker, R., et al. Head-lift / UES papers of the 1990s — history of a *publication*, not a home program.

## Screening, texture, ethics, pediatric texts

- Daniels, S. K., and colleagues. Clinical-indicators papers after stroke, 1990s–2000s (cite the year used in the essay).
- Martino, R., et al. TOR-BSST literature, *Stroke* and later validation papers.
- National Dysphagia Diet Task Force. *National Dysphagia Diet: Standardization for Optimal Care.* Chicago: American Dietetic Association, 2002.
- Cichero, J. A. Y., et al. IDDSI framework papers, 2016–17 (*Dysphagia* and allied journals). International Dysphagia Diet Standardisation Initiative, founded 2013.
- Steele, C. M., and colleagues. Thin-liquid and IDDSI instrumentation papers from Toronto.
- Arvedson, J. C., and L. Brodsky. *Pediatric Swallowing and Feeding.* successive editions (Singular / Plural / later with Lefton-Greif).
- Lefton-Greif, M. A. Hopkins pediatric dysphagia reviews.
- Leslie, P., and colleagues. Ethics-of-feeding papers (end of life, risk feeding) — cite the specific essay used.
- Martin-Harris, B., et al. MBSImP papers, *Dysphagia* 2008 onward.

## Institutions and later residue

- *Dysphagia* journal, Springer, vol. 1 (1986), Donner as founding editor.
- Dysphagia Research Society, founded 1992; Dodds–Donner lecture lineage on the society awards pages.
- ASHA practice portal pages on adult dysphagia and FEES — institutional position statements, not how-to manuals for this pack.
