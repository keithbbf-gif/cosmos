# Claims Guardrails — Dysphagia & Swallowing Heritage

Educational history for SLPWOW. Fail a draft that forgets this file.

## What a reader may take away

- Who looked at a living swallow, when, and with what meal or light.
- Which books, films, journals, and meetings still sit in training programs.
- Where radiology, ENT, GI, nursing, and speech-language pathology shared a hallway — and where they refused to.

## What a reader may not take away

- A home program.
- A diagnosis.
- A reason to delay an ENT, GI, or SLP visit.
- A founder fantasy (“Logemann invented swallowing”; “Langmore invented the nose”).

If a paragraph can be copied into a clinic handout without rewriting, cut it.

## Uncertainty is a claim

Print the disagreement. Examples already in the copy:

- Donner memorials call the 1981 Hopkins unit the first swallowing center “in the world.” That is a colleague’s sentence, not a census of every GI fluoroscopy service.
- FEES began in print as FEESS. Later branding dropped a letter. The 1988 paper is the date; the acronym is not a sacrament.
- Donner’s birth day is not forced in this pack. The Baltimore *Sun* gives age 71 at death on 13 April 1992.
- Dodds’s birth year is inferred from “died 1992 at age 58” in Society of Abdominal Radiology memorial copy. The article says so.

A clean false date is worse than an ugly “not found.”
