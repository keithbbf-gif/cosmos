# Style guide — architectural millwork and molding history (BBF lane)

- Voice: shop floor talking to a finish carpenter, not a catalog. Short sentences, named books, named machines, dated anchors. Arkansas hardwood country is the home shop, not a slogan.
- Human voice only. Ban: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, tapestry, underscore, ever-evolving, “it’s important to note,” “Whether you’re…,” corporate triads, fake intimacy, “elevate your space.”
- Feature length: **≥700 body words** (front matter excluded); target 750–1100.
- Set `voice_check: human` when prose is first filled; set `voice_check: edited` after a magazine-floor editor pass. Keep `status: draft` until Keith clears publish. Lane is `bbf-millwork`.
- Use `era_focus` in front matter; mark uncertain dates `[VERIFY]` in prose. Soft identifications stay **soft**.
- Citations array in YAML — writer fills; graphics script does not invent references.
- Embed figures immediately after the H2 they support (`<!-- graphics-pack:v1 -->` block). Do not reorder those first two H2s, captions, or SVG paths.
- Front matter `figures:` lists relative paths from the article file. Keep the graphics-pack order: timeline first, shop/profile plate second.
- This is staged Website-GC copy. Not a live bid, not a construction spec, not architectural or engineering advice.
