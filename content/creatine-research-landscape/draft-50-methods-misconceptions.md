# CRL-50 — Misconceptions that are research-methods errors

> **Educational landscape only.** Folklore as broken methods, not
> as medical myth-busting. Not advice, not a product defense, and
> not a recommendation to use any product. Does not claim that
> creatine diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents any disease.

## Learning aims

- Replace five loud sentences with five methods sentences.
- Notice when a misconception is really a unit error.
- Leave medical folklore to clinicians; we only do assay folklore.

## "Creatine is a steroid / a protein / an amino-acid stack"

**Error class:** identity. Creatine is a guanidino compound
related to amino-acid metabolism and not inserted into proteins
(CRL-05). Steroid is a different skeleton. Stack is a marketing
word.

## "If the scale went up, they grew muscle"

**Error class:** time and compartment. Early mass is mostly
told as water (CRL-24). DXA lean mass includes that water.
Hypertrophy claims need a clock and a better tissue sentence
(CRL-28).

## "Creatinine went up, so the protocol damaged an organ"

**Error class:** downstream analyte. A larger pool leaks more
creatinine (CRL-16, CRL-17, CRL-43). This series will not
interpret a person's organs. It will say the folk leap skips
the chemistry.

## "They didn't respond, so creatine does nothing"

**Error class:** missing Layer A, missing baseline, missing
task class (CRL-23, CRL-26). A high starting TCr, a long
aerobic test, or a missed dose can all look like "nothing."

## "You have to load or it doesn't work"

**Error class:** forgotten arm. Hultman 1996's 3 g × 28 day
path arrived at a similar muscle number (CRL-19). Arrival
time is the measured difference.

## "This new salt is more bioavailable"

**Error class:** kitchen property as physiology. Solubility
is not a biopsy (CRL-39, CRL-40). Demand equimolar creatine
and a pool assay.

## "The meta-analysis says it works"

**Error class:** mixture. Ask the inclusion table (CRL-30).
A diamond that mixes unlike tasks is a polite average.

## "ISSN said so"

**Error class:** secondary as primary (CRL-32). Use the stand
as a bibliography.

## "It's not on the WADA list, so the tub is fine"

**Error class:** wrong axis (CRL-41, CRL-42). Listing is not
an assay of the scoop.

## What we will not "bust" here

Hair, skin, and clinic-shaped folklore often appear in Q&A
reviews. Some of that folklore is medical or cosmetic. This
series does not run a clinic and does not run a salon. If a
misconception requires a disease word to state, we do not
state it.

## What this draft does not claim

- That every folk sentence is false in every person. Some are
  un-asked.
- That correcting methods folklore is a product endorsement.
- That a reader should argue with a clinician using this page.
