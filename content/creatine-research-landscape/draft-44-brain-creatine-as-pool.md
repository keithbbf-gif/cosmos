# CRL-44 — Brain creatine as a measured pool, not a treatment chapter

> **Educational landscape only.** A compartment and an assay.
> Not a cognitive product, not medical advice, and not a
> recommendation to use any product. Does not claim that creatine
> diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents any disease.

## Learning aims

- Place brain creatine as a small, real compartment next to the
  muscle majority (CRL-11).
- Read 1H-MRS as a voxel estimate, not as a biopsy.
- Keep oral schedules that move muscle in days from automatically
  writing the same percentage onto a brain voxel.

## The measurement that opened a file

Dechent, Pouwels, Wilken, Hanefeld, and Frahm (1999) used
quantitative localized proton MRS in healthy young volunteers
who took 4 × 5 g monohydrate per day for 4 weeks. Mean total
creatine across brain regions rose about **8.7%** (their figure),
with regional unevenness (they reported a larger thalamic
percentage than gray-matter cortex) and with inter-subject spread.
Control spectra months later were back. Other metabolites were
discussed; read the paper for those secondary marks.

That is a **pool paper for a different organ**. It is not a
crossword-test paper. It is not a clinical paper in this series.

## Why the percentage is smaller and slower-looking than muscle

Muscle is built to store a large TCr and is fed by a highly
expressed transporter (CRL-12). Brain voxels are a different
tissue, behind a different set of barriers, with different
transporter density and a different synthesis story. A 5-day
muscle-loading week is not a 5-day brain-loading week by
assumption. Dechent's schedule was weeks, and the rise was
single-digit to low-teen percentages, not Harris's muscle
twenties.

If a later cognitive-task paper uses 5 days and never measures
a voxel, Layer A in brain is unanchored (CRL-45).

## What this series will not do with the neighbor

Reviews of brain creatine often walk into injury and clinic
language. We will not follow. We will not name conditions. We
will not quote clinical effect sizes. We will not treat MRS as
a medical test.

## How a careful paper talks

Field strength, voxel placement, quantification assumptions,
the oral schedule, and whether muscle TCr was measured in the
same people (rare, and useful when present). "Brain creatine
increased" without a method is a vibe.

## What this draft does not claim

- That a raised brain voxel improves thinking.
- That readers should take large multi-week doses "for the
  brain."
- That a 9% mean is your head.
