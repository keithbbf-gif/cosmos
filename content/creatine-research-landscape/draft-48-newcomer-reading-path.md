# CRL-48 — A staged reading path for a newcomer

> **Educational landscape only.** An order of reading. Not a
> certification, not medical advice, and not a recommendation to
> use any product. Does not claim that creatine diagnoses, treats,
> cures, or prevents any disease.

## Learning aims

- Walk the folder in an order that builds verbs before adjectives.
- Know which three primaries to open in a library after the
  drafts.
- Leave with a worksheet habit, not with a scoop habit.

## Path A — one evening (about six drafts)

1. CRL-02 guardrails (the fence).
2. CRL-01 map (the provinces).
3. CRL-05 chemistry (the thing).
4. CRL-09 1990s inflection (the hinge).
5. CRL-03 how to read a paper (the worksheet).
6. CRL-52 one-page summary (what you may say aloud).

Then stop. If you can complete the CRL-03 worksheet on Harris
1992 from memory, you are already ahead of most comment threads.

## Path B — a short course (one draft-stage per sitting)

- Sitting 1: Stage 0 (CRL-01–04).
- Sitting 2: Stage 1 (CRL-05–10). Open Wyss & Kaddurah-Daouk
  2000 as the long companion, even if you only read the
  synthesis and distribution sections.
- Sitting 3: Stage 2 (CRL-11–17). Open Harris 1992.
- Sitting 4: Stage 3 (CRL-18–25). Open Hultman 1996.
- Sitting 5: Stage 4 (CRL-26–32). Open Greenhaff 1993 and one
  meta-analysis inclusion table.
- Sitting 6: Stage 5 (CRL-33–38). Open Burke 2003 or Watt 2004.
- Sitting 7: Stage 6 (CRL-39–43). Open Jäger 2011 as inventory.
- Sitting 8: Stage 7 (CRL-44–46). Open Dechent 1999. Do not
  open clinical neighbors.
- Sitting 9: Stage 8 (CRL-47–52). Write your own one-page
  summary and compare it to CRL-52. If you have added a disease
  verb, start over at CRL-02.

## Path C — if you only care about exercise tasks

CRL-02, CRL-10, CRL-19, CRL-26, CRL-27, CRL-28, CRL-29,
CRL-30, CRL-31. Then one primary in your sport's task class.
Skip brain and occupational corners until the worksheet is
easy.

## Path D — if you only care about why the folklore is loud

CRL-02, CRL-16, CRL-23, CRL-24, CRL-31, CRL-39, CRL-41,
CRL-50, CRL-51.

## Three primaries that repay a full read

1. Harris, Söderlund, Hultman 1992 (*Clin Sci*).
2. Hultman et al. 1996 (*J Appl Physiol*).
3. Greenhaff et al. 1993 (*Clin Sci*) or Greenhaff et al. 1994
   (*Am J Physiol*) — pick task or PCr kinetics.

Everything else in this folder is a map of those rooms and of
the later houses built beside them.

## What this draft does not claim

- That finishing the path makes you a trialist.
- That skipping a stage is a moral failure. It is just a hole.
- That the path is a use protocol.
