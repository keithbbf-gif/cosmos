# Creatine research landscape

Educational drafts that map **how creatine has been studied** — chemistry, human
physiology, trial methods, exercise literature, and the habits of a careful
reader. The series is staged. Read in order if you are new; jump by draft id if
you already know a corner of the map.

This is **not** a supplement guide, a dosing protocol for personal use, or a
medical document. It does **not** claim that creatine diagnoses, treats, cures,
or prevents any disease.

## How the folder is staged

| Stage | Theme | Drafts | What you leave with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Orientation | 01–04 | Scope, claim ban, how to read a paper, glossary |
| 1 | Molecule and history | 05–10 | What creatine is, where it comes from, who opened the modern file |
| 2 | Distribution and measurement | 11–17 | Pools, transporters, biopsy, MRS, creatinine as a signal |
| 3 | Trial methods | 18–25 | What protocols *measure*, not what anyone should take |
| 4 | Exercise literature map | 26–32 | Task types, meta-analysis limits, position stands as secondary lit |
| 5 | Population modifiers | 33–38 | Diet, sex, age band, training status, environment, caffeine |
| 6 | Forms, quality, regulation | 39–43 | Salts, stability, labels, statute category, healthy-volunteer safety lit |
| 7 | Brain and occupation (methods) | 44–46 | Brain creatine as a measured pool; cognitive and occupational designs |
| 8 | Synthesis | 47–52 | Open questions, reading path, timeline, misconceptions, one-page summary |

## House rules (every draft)

- Educational landscape only. No disease claims. No personal prescriptions.
- Landmark papers are cited so you can open them. Citations are pointers, not
  copied text.
- Numbers are given as **reported in named studies** or as **commonly quoted
  ranges in reviews**, never as universal human constants.
- When the literature is thin, mixed, or industry-adjacent, the draft says so.
- `verify_landscape.py` checks draft count, required sections, and a banned-claim
  phrase list. It does not certify scientific completeness.
