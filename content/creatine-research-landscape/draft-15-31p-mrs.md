# CRL-15 — 31P-MRS: watching phosphocreatine without a needle

> **Educational landscape only.** A measurement-method chapter. Not a
> radiology manual, not medical advice, and not a recommendation to use
> any product. Does not claim that creatine diagnoses, treats, cures, or
> prevents any disease.

## Learning aims

- State what a phosphorus spectrum can see in a limb voxel.
- Contrast MRS kinetics with biopsy stoichiometry.
- Read PCr recovery as a protocol-bound curve, not as a wellness score.

## What the method is for

Phosphorus-31 magnetic resonance spectroscopy can resolve inorganic
phosphate, phosphocreatine, and ATP phosphates in a volume of muscle
(or other tissue) while a person rests, contracts, and recovers. The
draw is obvious: repeated measures, no needle, a time course instead of
two sticks.

Greenhaff 1994 sits near this tradition even where biopsy was used:
the scientific appetite is for **PCr as a function of time**. Later
groups leaned harder on MRS.

## What a voxel is not

A voxel is a blob of mixed fibers, extracellular space, and whatever
the coil sees. Absolute millimolar concentrations require assumptions
or calibration; many papers report **relative** PCr, or PCr as a
fraction of PCr+Pi, or time constants of recovery. Those are not
automatically the same number as "mmol/kg dry mass" from a freeze-dried
biopsy. Meta-analysts who dump both into one column are mixing
denominators.

PCr recovery after a contraction is often interpreted as a window on
oxidative ATP synthesis, because in many protocols the rebuild of PCr
is paid for by mitochondria. That interpretation is model-dependent
(pH, H+ handling, the size of the remaining PCr dip). A faster recovery
after a feeding week could mean several things. A paper that reports
only a prettier curve without a model paragraph is showing you a curve.

## Feeding studies and MRS

If oral creatine raises muscle TCr, resting PCr *may* rise, and the
kinetics of depletion/repletion *may* change. "May" is the word until
the paper shows the spectrum. Some studies find clearer rest PCr
changes than others. MRS is less surgical than biopsy and also less
chemically specific about total creatine (proton MRS is the cousin used
in brain work; CRL-44).

A negative MRS study is not automatically a negative pool study. A
positive kinetic change is not automatically a bigger TCr. Layer A and
Layer B still want their own sentences (CRL-03).

## Practical leaks

- **Coil placement and voxel drift** between visits.
- **Exercise hardware in the magnet** that does not match the sport
  task the introduction promised.
- **Partial volume** with fat or inactive muscle.
- **pH** from the Pi chemical shift, which changes the CK equilibrium
  story.

None of these leaks make MRS unserious. They make it an instrument.

## What this draft does not claim

- That MRS replaces biopsy for every question. It replaces it for some.
- That a PCr recovery constant is a medical test in this series. It is
  a research readout.
- That readers should seek a spectroscopy session.
