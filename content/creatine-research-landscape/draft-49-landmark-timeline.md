# CRL-49 — Annotated timeline of landmark papers

> **Educational landscape only.** Dates and why they sit on the
> map. Not a hall of fame, not medical advice, and not a
> recommendation to use any product. Does not claim that creatine
> diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents any disease.

## Learning aims

- Attach a year to the isolation, the phosphagen, the biopsy
  hinge, and the schedule fork.
- See later documents as secondary until they earn a new assay.
- Use the timeline as a reading order, not as a worship order.

## Before the oral file

**1832 — Chevreul.** Isolation and naming from meat extract.
Identity, not sport (CRL-08).

**Mid-1800s — Liebig and contemporaries.** Creatine as a muscle
constituent. Still chemistry.

**Early 1900s — Folin line.** Creatinine methods; diet and
excretion as measurable human facts (CRL-16).

**Mid-1900s — phosphagens and CK.** The reaction and the
isoforms; Wallimann 1992 is a modern review of the spatial
story (CRL-10, CRL-13).

**1962 / 1974 — Bergström needle; Harris, Hultman, Nordesjö.**
Human muscle metabolites become routine, with published
variance (CRL-14).

**1979 — Walker.** Long synthesis review (CRL-06).

**2000 — Wyss & Kaddurah-Daouk.** The physiology-review
anchor for metabolism and creatinine.

## The hinge years

**1992 — Harris, Söderlund, Hultman.** Oral creatine raises
muscle creatine in healthy people. Baseline matters.

**1993 — Greenhaff et al.** Repeated maximal torque.

**1994 — Greenhaff, Bodin, Söderlund, Hultman.** PCr
resynthesis.

**1996 — Hultman et al.** Fast path, slow path, hold, washout,
creatinine.

**1996 — Green et al.** Carbohydrate drink and retention.

**1996 — Vandenberghe et al.** Caffeine plus load; Layer B
surprise (CRL-38).

**1996 — Casey et al.** Maximal exercise metabolism and
performance in one design (CRL-27).

## Expansion, not replacement

**1999 — Dechent et al.** Brain voxel TCr after weeks
(CRL-44).

**2000 — Steenge, Simpson, Greenhaff.** Protein plus
carbohydrate and retention (CRL-21).

**2003 — Burke et al.; Branch meta-analysis; van Loon et al.;
Rae et al.; Snow & Murphy review.** Diet, pooling, mixed
tasks, a cognitive battery, uptake factors — a year the file
branched.

**2004 — Watt, Garnham, Snow.** Vegetarians, TCr, transporter
expression.

**2007 / 2017 — ISSN position stands.** Secondary (CRL-32).

**2009 / 2011 / 2012 — Spillane; Jäger; Jagim.** Forms and
the demand for equimolar proof (CRL-39).

**2015 / 2017 — Lanhers et al.** Narrower strength
meta-analyses (CRL-30).

**2021 — Antonio et al. misconceptions review.** Folklore
list; still secondary; still fenced.

## How to use the list

If a popular article has no date before 2010 and no Harris or
Hultman, it is probably telling you a shop story. If a methods
section cites only a position stand, ask for the primary.

## What this draft does not claim

- That later papers are lesser. Some are better designed.
- That this list is complete.
- That a landmark is an indication.
