# CRL-40 — Stability: creatine in a bottle becomes creatinine in a glass

> **Educational landscape only.** Material behavior. Not storage
> advice as a product warranty, not medical advice, and not a
> recommendation to use any product. Does not claim that creatine
> diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents any disease.

## Learning aims

- Separate dry monohydrate, a fresh solution, and a warm aged
  solution.
- Treat creatinine in a liquid product as a degradation signal.
- Connect bench chemistry to why "ready-to-drink creatine" is a
  hard methods object.

## The reaction is not mysterious

Creatine cyclizes to creatinine in a pH- and temperature-dependent
way. Acid and heat hurry it. Dry, cool, crystalline monohydrate is
a relatively dull solid. A solution sitting on a bench is not dull.
Wyss and Kaddurah-Daouk (2000) review the chemistry; food-science
and pharmaceutical-adjacent papers measure rates in buffers.

This is why a methods line that says "creatine dissolved the night
before" is not the same line as "creatine weighed and mixed at
the visit."

## What a liquid product has to prove

If the label says creatine and the assay says creatinine, the
inlet is not the inlet the 1990s cluster used. Jäger et al. (2011)
and later quality surveys sometimes report this failure mode. A
landscape reader treats an unassayed liquid as a **different
intervention**, or as none.

## Powder is not immortal

Moisture in a tub, a wet scoop, and a bathroom cabinet are how a
dry solid becomes a damp solid. Degradation is slower than in a
beaker and still not zero. Research groups that assay each batch
are not being precious. They are keeping Layer A attached to the
correct molecule.

## What this does not say about a body

Conversion to creatinine also happens in the body; that is the
leak (CRL-17). Bench instability is not a proof that oral
monohydrate "doesn't survive digestion." The biopsy file already
answered that for the dry solid swallowed promptly. Do not import
a bottle problem into a gut myth, or a gut myth into a bottle
problem.

## How a careful paper talks

When the drink was mixed, the pH if they buffered it, the storage
of the powder, and an assay if the form is unusual. "Participants
were given a week's worth of premixed bottles" should trigger a
stability sentence.

## What this draft does not claim

- That every liquid on a shelf is fully degraded. Some may be
  formulated to slow the reaction; they still need an assay.
- That readers must refrigerate anything. This is not a storage
  manual.
- That creatinine in a glass is a health product.
