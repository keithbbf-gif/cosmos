# CRL-13 — The creatine kinase shuttle as a working hypothesis

> **Educational landscape only.** A spatial-bioenergetics model. Not
> medical advice, not a supplement label, and not a recommendation to
> use any product. Does not claim that creatine diagnoses, treats,
> cures, or prevents any disease.

## Learning aims

- State the shuttle idea in one paragraph that a methods reader can use.
- Separate "CK is near-equilibrium in the cytosol" from "PCr is a
  courier between mitochondria and myofibrils."
- Know why a feeding trial does not prove the shuttle.

## Two pictures that get blended

**Picture 1 — buffer.** PCr is a local reservoir. When ATP drops and ADP
and H+ rise, CK runs toward ATP. This picture needs no special
geography. It is enough to explain why extra TCr *might* matter in a
short, hard contraction.

**Picture 2 — shuttle (or circuit).** Mitochondrial CK uses newly made
ATP to rebuild PCr at the outer mitochondrial compartment; PCr diffuses
to cytosolic CK at sites of ATP use (myosin, ion pumps); creatine
diffuses back. Wallimann et al. (1992) is the review most people mean.
PCr/Cr become a **spatial energy currency** that is easier to move than
ADP/ATP in a crowded myofibril.

Both pictures can be true in some tissues and some timescales. They are
not the same claim. A paper can measure a bigger TCr pool (Picture 1
gets more reservoir) without testing whether diffusion paths changed
(Picture 2).

## What would count as evidence for the shuttle

Isoform localization, isolated-mitochondria kinetics, diffusion
modeling, and some genetic or pharmacological disruptions of CK
isoforms in animals. That is a different literature from "we gave
undergraduates 20 g of monohydrate and timed a sprint."

If an exercise paper cites Wallimann in sentence one and never returns
to localization, the citation is atmosphere.

## Why feeding papers still mention it

Authors want a reason that extra creatine would matter *inside* a cell
that already has a lot of creatine. The shuttle offers a reason that is
about **where** the extra Cr/PCr sits, not only about how much. It is a
legitimate "why we cared" sentence. It is not a substitute for the
sprint clock or the biopsy.

MRS papers that fit PCr recovery after a contraction (CRL-15) are closer
to Picture 1 (a kinetic of the whole voxel) than to a proof of
Picture 2 (a path between named compartments). Recovery half-times are
still useful. They are just not electron-microscope evidence.

## How to write about it without over-claiming

Allowed: "The authors invoke the CK-shuttle model as a rationale."
Allowed: "Wallimann et al. summarize isoform compartmentation."
Not allowed in this series: "Creatine supplementation works because it
fixes mitochondrial shuttling in [anyone]."
Not allowed: using the shuttle as a bridge into clinical tissue stories.

## What this draft does not claim

- That the shuttle is false. It is a serious, argued model.
- That extra oral creatine has been shown to remodel CK localization in
  ordinary sport trials. Those trials almost never look.
- That ATP diffusion is "impossible" without PCr. Cells are more than
  one slogan.
