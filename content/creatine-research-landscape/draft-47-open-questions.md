# CRL-47 — Open questions and underpowered corners

> **Educational landscape only.** A list of unfinished measurements.
> Not a grant pitch in disguise, not medical advice, and not a
> recommendation to use any product. Does not claim that creatine
> diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents any disease.

## Learning aims

- Name corners where volume of talk exceeds volume of assay.
- Prefer questions that a methods table could close.
- Keep "unknown" from turning into "therefore take it" or
  "therefore ban it."

## Questions that are still measurement questions

**Women, with biopsies.** Repeat Hultman-shaped work with
disaggregated TCr, not only mixed-group 1RMs (CRL-34).

**Diet × training × TCr in one design.** Burke 2003 is a start.
Most papers still skip diet (CRL-33).

**Equimolar form comparisons with a pool assay.** The novel-salt
shelf is louder than its biopsies (CRL-39).

**Caffeine, copied carefully.** Vandenberghe 1996 vs later mixed
designs still do not share a protocol (CRL-38).

**Brain voxel and a cognitive primary, together.** Dechent plus
Rae in the same people, pre-registered, adequately powered
(CRL-44, CRL-45).

**Washout clocks outside young men.** Thirty days is one paper's
group statement (CRL-22).

**What DXA lean mass is made of after week one.** Water vs
protein remains a composition problem (CRL-24, CRL-28).

**Adherence biology vs adherence behavior.** Transporter
measurements are rare (CRL-12, CRL-23).

## Questions this series will not pose

Anything of the form "does creatine treat [a condition]."
Anything of the form "what dose should a person take."
Anything of the form "is it safe for [a medical history]."
Those are not unfinished educational measurements here. They
are out of scope.

## Why "more papers" has not closed the list

The easy experiment (college men, 20 g, a sprint, no biopsy)
is cheap and publishable. The expensive experiment (sex-
balanced, diet-stratified, biopsied or MRS'd, long washout,
assayed powder, registered primary) is still scarce. Volume
is not the same as coverage.

## How a careful reader uses this list

Pick one corner. Open the two or three primaries. Write the
worksheet from CRL-03. If the worksheet is empty, the corner
is still open even if a review sounds finished.

## What this draft does not claim

- That these are the only open questions.
- That closing them would create an indication.
- That an open question is a consumer mystery to be solved by
  a brand.
