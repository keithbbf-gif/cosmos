# Photo notes — SLPWOW SLP News

This pack is **SVG-only** for the image+SEO wave on PR #453.

## Allowed

- Original editorial schematics (cards, timelines, buckets, flows).
- Later: cropped **public-domain or CC-licensed** equipment engravings with a `RIGHTS.md` row and bibliography entry.

## Not allowed

- AI-generated faces or “diverse clinician” stock renders.
- Classroom photos, therapy sessions, or any image with identifiable children.
- Patient photography from clinics or hospitals.
- Government or vendor logos presented as if SLPWOW is endorsed.

## If adding a PD/CC raster later

1. Confirm license on the file’s source page (not a Pinterest mirror).
2. Add a row to `RIGHTS.md` with credit line for publish.
3. Prefer `loading="lazy"`, width/height, and the same `<figure>` / `ImageObject` pattern as SVG embeds.
