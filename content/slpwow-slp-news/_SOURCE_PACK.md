## ASLP-IC

- Official: https://aslpcompact.com/
- Map: https://aslpcompact.com/compact-map/
- CompactConnect apply: https://app.compactconnect.org/Dashboard
- Homepage (as of fetch 2026-09-14): Tennessee issuing as of 28 May 2026. Registration open for licensed practitioners in Louisiana, Ohio, Tennessee, West Virginia.
- 37 jurisdictions have enacted (36 states + 1 territory). Map buckets: enacted-not-issuing 33; issuing 4; active legislation 4; no active legislation 14.
- Privilege is equivalent to a license in the remote member state. Practice occurs in the state where the patient/client is located at the encounter.
- ASHA news 28 Oct 2025: CompactConnect launched; LA and WV first issuing states that day. Practitioners cannot register until home state onboards. https://www.asha.org/news/2025/interstate-compact-begins-issuing-compact-privileges/
- ASHA news 30 May 2025: CompactConnect then anticipated 30 Sep 2025; Arizona signed 13 May 2025 (36th jurisdiction at that time). https://www.asha.org/news/2025/interstate-compact-moves-closer-to-rollout-what-audiologists-and-slps-need-to-know/
- Nov 2025 newsletter: 28 Oct 2025 LA/WV operationalized. https://aslpcompact.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/November-2025-Compact-Newsletter-.pdf
- Feb 2026 newsletter: $50 Commission administrative fee per privilege; home-state FBI CBC rule amendment + legal-impossibility exceptions; MI and PA legislation carried over; NJ and NM reintroduced. https://aslpcompact.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/February-2026-Compact-Newsletter-3.pdf
- Ohio OTPTAT / SHP page: Ohio processing applications 9 Feb 2026. As of early 2026, WV and LA operational for Ohio licensees. 37 enacted including 1 territory. https://shp.ohio.gov/about/interstate-compact
- Benefits listed by Commission: continuity of care; military-spouse portability; access; choice; telehealth facilitation; simpler process.
- State fees and jurisprudence vary; Commission points to its fee page. Do not invent state-by-state fees.

## Medicare / CMS / ASHA public

- CMS Therapy Services: https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/therapy-services
  - CY 2026 KX: $2,480 PT+SLP combined; $2,480 OT. Targeted MR $3,000 until 2028 then MEI-indexed.
  - CAA 2026 §6209: PTs, OTs, SLPs may furnish telehealth including 98966–98968 through 31 Dec 2027.
  - New sometimes-therapy RTM codes 98979, 98984, 98985; descriptors revised for 98976, 98977 (CR 14250).
  - MPPR: 50% on PE of second and subsequent always-therapy services same day (since 1 Apr 2013). MPPR file updated 24 Feb 2026.
- CMS MM14315 (CY 2026 MPFS summary): KX $2,480; originating-site Q3014 80% of lesser of charge or $31.85 for 2026 (MEI 2.7%). https://www.cms.gov/files/document/mm14315-medicare-physician-fee-schedule-final-rule-summary-cy-2026.pdf
- CMS telehealth FAQ updated 26 Feb 2026: through 31 Dec 2027 extended practitioners may bill telehealth; starting 1 Jan 2028 PTs, OTs, SLPs, audiologists can no longer furnish Medicare telehealth. Beneficiaries anywhere in US/territories through 2027; 2028 rural/medical-facility rules return except behavioral health. https://www.cms.gov/files/document/telehealth-faq-updated-02-26-2026.pdf
- CMS MLN901705: home telehealth paid at non-facility PFS; hospital outpatient therapy telehealth uses modifier 95; Q3014 amounts as above.
- ASHA public news 13/19 Nov 2025: CF $33.57 APM / $33.40 others; 2.5% one-time congressional increase; ASHA estimated SLP cuts could approach ~4% without more legislation; PE rebalancing (non-facility up, facility down); 2.5% efficiency adjustment on select non-time-based codes; 92507 and 92557 exempt because on telehealth list; KX $2,480; MR $3,000; MPPR and targeted review maintained; most SLPs exempt from MIPS. CR then had telehealth/GPCI only through 30 Jan 2026 — later superseded by CAA 2026 to 31 Dec 2027. https://www.asha.org/news/2025/medicare-finalizes-2026-medicare-fee-schedule-modest-updates-but-continued-cuts/
- ASHA public telehealth page, updated 3 Feb 2026: CAA 2026 / H.R. 7148 extends audiologist and SLP Medicare telehealth authority through 31 Dec 2027. Services on the list since 2020 are permanently on the telehealth services list as of 1 Jan 2026. Traditional FFS only; MA/Medicaid vary. HIPAA enforcement resumed after PHE. If Congress later lets authority lapse, cash-pay for those listed services may be allowed until a new extension forces claims again. https://www.asha.org/practice/reimbursement/medicare/providing-telehealth-services-under-medicare/
- ASHA public 2026 SLP fee-schedule PDF (siteassets, first ed. 13 Nov 2025): same CF figures; 92507 national rate +~2%; 96112 −1%; SLP services paid at non-facility rate by law regardless of setting; efficiency adjustment may recur every 3 years. Summarize; do not reprint the rate tables. https://www.asha.org/siteassets/reimbursement/2026-medicare-fee-schedule-for-speech-language-pathologists.pdf

## Workforce / licensure

- BLS OOH SLP: 2024 median pay $95,410; 187,400 jobs in 2024; 215,500 projected 2034; +15%; +28,200; ~13,300 openings/year. https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/speech-language-pathologists.htm
- BLS MLR 2026: offices of PT/OT/speech therapists and audiologists +23.3% industry growth 2024–34. https://www.bls.gov/opub/mlr/2026/article/industry-and-occupational-employment-projections-overview.htm
- ASHA 2024 Schools Survey SLP Workforce PDF (public siteassets): 87% FT; 86% salaried; paperwork #1 of 13 challenges every facility type; high workload/caseload #2 most types; 34% considering setting change; 27% considering leaving profession due to burnout; 50% top-two retention idea = separate salary schedule from teachers; 12% supervise an SLPA; 63% union available; 79% more openings than seekers (92% South Atlantic; 59% Middle Atlantic). https://www.asha.org/siteassets/surveys/2024-schools-survey-slp-workforce.pdf
- Bridge Michigan (Eastpointe / statewide virtual SLP): Optimise task force listed 253 school SLP openings; MDE does not know how many sped posts are filled virtually; EPIC’s Tara Kilbride on turnover. Quote only if taken from the story. https://bridgemi.com/talent-education/short-of-staff-michigan-schools-try-virtual-special-education-speech-therapy/
- Michigan News Source 27 Feb 2026: Eastpointe 149 students (37 preschool) with 4 virtual + 2 on-site SLPs; Gibson: “This is not an ideal situation.” “I think the best speech services are delivered face-to-face.” 250+ openings; MI 44th starting teacher salary; 215,000+ students on IEPs. https://www.michigannewssource.com/2026/02/screen-time-for-speech-time-districts-adapt-to-hiring-woes/
- WYPR 21 May 2025: BCPS SLPs; union/survey: >62% caseloads 61–100; ASHA national average caseload ~50 cited in story; Supt. Myriam Rogers: no SLP budget cuts FY current or FY26; ~40 of 200 SLPs booked workload meetings. https://www.wypr.org/wypr-news/2025-05-21/baltimore-county-school-speech-therapists-say-workload-is-simply-unsustainable
- Florida SB/HB 471 (2026 filed text): DOE statewide strategic plan for SLP recruitment/retention; caseload/compensation comparison; weighted caseload/workload methodology with time for listed duties. https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026/471/BillText/Filed/PDF
- Utah HB 374 enrolled: effective 6 May 2026; repeals USBE licensure exemption; DOPL license required. https://le.utah.gov/Session/2026/bills/enrolled/HB0374.pdf
- Utah DOPL audiology page: HB 374 transition — standard application + copy of USBE credential; no transcripts/exam scores required for that path; USBE credentials remain valid until printed expiration. https://commerce.utah.gov/dopl/audiology/
- USBE June 2026 memo: HB 374 transferred licensing to DOPL; USBE license areas expire over next five years.
- NY OP: title protection; school/exempt settings exist; 36 weeks supervised experience after degree. https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/speech-language-pathology/license-requirements
- SCDE educator cert for SLP (page dated 11 Jun 2026): SBE approved SLP educator certificates 1 Jul 2020; renew educator cert with current LLR SLP license valid past June 30 of expiry year. https://ed.sc.gov/educators/certification/slp/
- RI: full SLP license two years, expire 1 Jul even years (R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-48.2-6); RIDE support professional certificate area 13004 PK-12. Confirm on RIDOH/RIDE, not third-party blogs as primary.

## Research (paraphrase only; cite DOI)

- Light, Fager, Gormley, Watson Hyatt, Jakobs (2025). *AAC*, 41(3), 230–244. DOI 10.1080/07434618.2025.2508490. OA CC BY-NC-ND. Societal barriers; lived experience + research agenda (attitudes, policy, inequitable services, population data, training).
- Götze et al. (2025). JMIR Med Educ 11:e63392. DOI 10.2196/63392. Free-access AAC e-learning tools for SLP/phoniatrics; formats/content/learning styles; PRISMA-style 2023 search.
- Babb & Caron (2026). *AJSLP* 35(2), 431–447. DOI 10.1044/2025_AJSLP-25-00218. ALL app; 4 children using high-tech AAC; parent-delivered; gains in blending, decoding, HFW; 3/4 generalized blending/decoding.
- Light, Holyfield, McNaughton, Nieder, Preece (2025). *AAC* 41(3), 283–294. DOI 10.1080/07434618.2025.2502032. Literacy barriers for people who need/use AAC.
- Duboisdindien, Lavoie, Laforce, Macoir (2025). *Front. Hum. Neurosci.* DOI 10.3389/fnhum.2025.1724345. Published 19 Dec 2025. Teletherapy phonological STM training in lvPPA; trained-item gains; limited functional generalization.
- *Brain Sci.* 2026, 16, 137. DOI 10.3390/brainsci16020137. Published 28 Jan 2026. Computerized naming + cerebellar tDCS in Cantonese-speaking people with aphasia. Cite as early-stage / mixed literature; do not overclaim clinical readiness.
- *Front. Hum. Neurosci.* 2026. DOI 10.3389/fnhum.2026.1810169. Double-blind RCT; fNIRS-guided neuronavigated rTMS + intensive SLT vs sham; subacute post-stroke aphasia; naming (Chinese BNT). Adjunct research, not a practice standard.
