# Style guide — SLPWOW SLP News

## Reader

Working clinicians, informed parents, and teachers who encounter SLP stories in the feed. Magazine clarity, not alarm.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Name the document, not the outrage.
- Ban SEO slop: *delve, leverage, game-changer, in conclusion, whether you're a…*
- `voice_check: human` on every draft.

## SEO figures

- One primary `<figure>` per brief with `itemscope itemtype="https://schema.org/ImageObject"`.
- `alt` describes the schematic for screen readers and image search.
- `<figcaption>` states figure number, schematic intent, and credit line.
- Prefer `loading="lazy"` and explicit `width` / `height`.

## Graphics

- News cards, timelines, and infographics only — **no AI faces or child likenesses**.
