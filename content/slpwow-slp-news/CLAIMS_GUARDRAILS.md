# Claims guardrails — SLPWOW SLP News

## Never say

- That a news item diagnoses a child or replaces a screen/evaluation.
- That a headline alone justifies billing, coding, or caseload changes.
- That ASHA, CMS, CDC, or a state board said something they did not (quote or link).
- Vendor marketing language presented as COSMOS/SLPWOW endorsement.

## Always do

- Name the **primary source** readers should open.
- Separate **headline heat** from **document text**.
- Mark figures as **schematic** in caption and `alt` text.
- Keep `status: draft` until a human editor clears import.
