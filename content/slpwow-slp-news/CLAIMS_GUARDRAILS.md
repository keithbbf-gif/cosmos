# CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS — SLP News (SLPWOW)

House rules for every draft in this folder. These are editorial constraints, not clinical protocols.

## Never

- **No diagnosis, treatment, or individualized clinical advice.** Describe policy, research findings, and practice conditions. Do not tell a reader what to do with a named patient, student, or family. Do not imply that a study result is a prescription.
- **No PHI.** No real student, patient, or family identifiers. No reconstructed case notes. If a public source names a superintendent or clinician speaking on the record, that is news attribution, not PHI.
- **No fabricated quotes.** Quote only language that appears in a cited public source. If the source paraphrase is all that exists, paraphrase and attribute. Do not invent board testimony, ASHA staff remarks, or “clinicians say” composite quotes.
- **No ASHA member-only paste.** Public ASHA news pages, public reimbursement explainers, and the public 2026 fee-schedule PDF on `asha.org/siteassets` may be summarized. Do not reconstruct member-only Leader articles, paywalled CE, or closed-listserv text. Do not reprint ASHA fee tables line-for-line.
- **No other-blog clones.** Do not rewrite ProTherapy Staffing, SpeechPathology.org, MedSol, or similar roundups. Use primary pages (ASLP-IC, CMS, state legislatures, BLS, journals) and original newsroom copy.
- **No invented compact or fee numbers.** If a figure is not in the source list for that article, omit it or mark it as unverified and cut it before import.
- **No “this is covered” billing guarantees.** Medicare rates, modifiers, and telehealth clocks change. Point clinicians to their MAC and the current CMS page.

## Always

- Front matter: `status: draft`, `category: slp-news`.
- A `Sources` section with title, publisher, date, and URL.
- Distinguish **proposed**, **enacted**, **issuing**, and **final** legal states. Compact membership is not privilege issuance. A CMS proposed rule is not the final rule.
- Label research as research. Sample size, design (RCT, multiple-case, survey), and journal/DOI belong in the story, not only the bibliography.
- Educational news voice: what changed, who said so, what a working SLP should *verify*, not what they should *do* to a client.

## Voice

Human newsroom. Short ledes. Specific dates. No “in today’s rapidly evolving landscape,” no “it’s important to note,” no “as an SLP, you may be wondering.” Prefer “The Commission said” over “stakeholders are excited.”
