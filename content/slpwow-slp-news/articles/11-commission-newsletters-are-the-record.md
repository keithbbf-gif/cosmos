# The Commission’s newsletters are the dated public record. Open the PDFs.

WASHINGTON — Sept. 14, 2026 — If a clinician needs to know *when* the Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology Interstate Compact Commission said something, the homepage newsletter stack is the public trail. The letters are dated, signed, and posted as PDFs. Recruiter decks and association roundups are not.

As of a 14 September 2026 fetch, the Commission home page lists Volume 1 issues from 2025 and Volume 2 through Edition 2, August 2026. This desk opened the 2025 letters we could retrieve and the February 2026 letter (Volume 2, Issue 1, dated 4 February). We did not fetch the August 2026 PDF. Readers who need that edition should open it from the homepage rather than trust a paraphrase that does not exist here.

## What the letters we did open actually recorded

The November 2025 letter (Volume 1, Edition 4, dated 4 November) put a legal timestamp on launch: Louisiana and West Virginia “operationalized” on 28 October 2025. The February 2026 letter recorded the $50 Commission administrative fee per privilege, a home-state FBI background-check rule amendment with revised timelines and “legal impossibility” exceptions, carryover bills in Michigan and Pennsylvania, and reintroductions in New Jersey and New Mexico. The August 2025 letter (Volume 1, Edition 3, dated 1 August) described the fee rule and the criminal-background-check amendment before CompactConnect went live.

Those are Commission documents. They are not ASHA membership mail and they are not this desk’s reporting notes. When a later homepage banner — Tennessee issuing as of 28 May 2026 — updates an operational fact, the letter that predates it does not become false. It becomes older.

## How to use them

Read the issue date before you quote a fee, a bill name, or an issuing count. Volume 2, Edition 2, August 2026 is listed on the homepage; this article does not summarize that file. If a claim about a 2026 rule, a new issuing state, or a fee change first appears in that PDF, the PDF is the source. The Compact Map and the home page remain the place to check whether a member is issuing today.

This article does not offer clinical advice, billing guarantees, or diagnosis guidance.
