# South Carolina renews the SLP educator certificate against a current LLR license

COLUMBIA, S.C. — June 11, 2026 — South Carolina’s educator certificate for speech-language pathologists is a State Board of Education credential, not the practice license. The South Carolina Department of Education’s certification page, dated June 11, 2026, says the board approved issuance of educator certificates in the SLP field effective July 1, 2020. SCDE no longer issues educator certificates in the older field of speech-language therapist.

People who still hold the therapist field may maintain and renew that credential and keep serving in their current roles. An SLP who wants the newer field can request an evaluation through My SC Educator Portal and upload a valid SLP license from the Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation.

## How renewal works when SLP is the only field

An individual whose educator certificate is only in the instructional-service field of speech-language pathologist renews by submitting a current copy of a valid LLR SLP license. SCDE says the SLP may request renewal after January 1 of the year the educator certificate expires. The LLR license submitted must be valid beyond June 30 of that expiry year. The department’s own example: if the educator certificate expires June 30, 2025, the LLR license must be valid beyond June 30, 2025.

That is a date check, not a CE audit, for the single-field SLP certificate.

## Extra fields change the math

An educator who holds SLP certification and additional fields — classroom teaching, leadership, or others — must earn 120 renewal credits every five years. Credits earned to renew the LLR license may be applied to those 120. Public middle- and high-school educators still have to meet the Jason Flatt Act suicide-awareness training and any other legislative renewal requirements that attach to their current certification and employment.

District employees submit licenses and transcripts to the district renewal coordinator. SCDE does not process renewal for people employed by a South Carolina public-school district. People employed elsewhere upload to the portal themselves.

LLR’s practice license is a separate biennial card that expires March 31 of the second year. Keep both calendars visible.
