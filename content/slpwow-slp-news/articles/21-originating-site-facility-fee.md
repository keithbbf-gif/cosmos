# Medicare’s 2026 originating-site facility fee is $31.85 before coinsurance

BALTIMORE — Jan. 5, 2026 — The originating-site facility fee for Medicare telehealth in calendar year 2026 is a small, specific number. HCPCS Q3014 pays 80 percent of the lesser of the facility’s actual charge or $31.85. The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services published that amount in MM14315, the CY 2026 Physician Fee Schedule summary, and repeated it in MLN901705, the December 2025 telehealth and remote-monitoring booklet. The Medicare Economic Index increase behind the update is 2.7 percent.

The patient remains responsible for any unmet Part B deductible and for coinsurance. Eighty percent of the lesser of charge or $31.85 is the Medicare payment math, not a $31.85 check to the clinic and not a $0 bill to the beneficiary.

## A facility fee, not an SLP professional fee

Q3014 is the originating site’s separately billable Part B facility fee — the site where the patient is located while a distant-site clinician furnishes a telehealth service. MLN901705 tells that site to bill the Medicare Administrative Contractor for Q3014. It is not 92507. It is not modifier 95. It is not the SLP’s conversion-factor payment.

MLN901705 also states that Medicare pays for telehealth furnished to patients in their homes at the non-facility Physician Fee Schedule rate, and that hospital outpatient therapy telehealth billed by qualified therapists employed by hospitals uses modifier 95. Those are professional or institutional therapy instructions. They do not turn Q3014 into a speech-language pathology code.

Through December 31, 2027, CMS’s February 26, 2026, FAQ says beneficiaries can receive Medicare telehealth anywhere in the United States and territories. That people-and-place window is a different rule from the Q3014 fee. A home originating site and a hospital originating site do not automatically produce the same facility-fee line. The MAC’s billing instructions decide whether Q3014 is even payable for that place of service.

## What SLPs should verify

Do not price an SLP telehealth visit off $31.85. If the practice or the host facility bills Q3014, confirm with the MAC that the site is an originating site for that date of service and that the charge-versus-$31.85 comparison is being applied. Tell the financial counselor the beneficiary still has deductible and coinsurance on the fee. Read MM14315 and MLN901705 alongside the current telehealth FAQ; a December MLN booklet can lag a February statute.
