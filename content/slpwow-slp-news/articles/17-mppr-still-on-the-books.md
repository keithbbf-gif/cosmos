# Medicare’s 50 percent therapy MPPR is still on the books for 2026

BALTIMORE — Feb. 24, 2026 — The multiple procedure payment reduction for always-therapy services did not sunset with the old therapy caps and did not sunset with the 2026 fee schedule. The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services still applies a 50 percent cut to the practice-expense portion of the second and subsequent always-therapy services furnished to the same patient on the same day. The policy has run at that 50 percent rate since April 1, 2013, in both practitioner/office and institutional settings.

CMS’s Therapy Services page is the primary citation. The service with the highest practice-expense relative value unit is paid at 100 percent of that PE component. The remaining always-therapy services that day are paid at 50 percent of PE. Work and malpractice pieces are not the target of this particular reduction. The “MPPR Rate File” for calendar year 2026 sits in that page’s Downloads section. CMS last updated the file on February 24, 2026, to include code 97026.

## Same day, all three therapy disciplines

The reduction is a per-day policy across physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech-language pathology in the same facility. ASHA’s public 2026 SLP PDF says so, and says SLP codes are often less exposed than timed PT and OT codes because many SLP services are untimed and carry higher values. That is an association observation, not an exemption. If two always-therapy codes land on one day, the file and the PE ranks decide the math.

ASHA’s November 2025 public news item listed MPPR among the policies CMS is maintaining in 2026, alongside targeted manual medical review. Maintenance is the news. There is no 2026 holiday from the 50 percent PE haircut.

The reduction is not a medical-necessity denial, not the KX threshold, and not a reason to omit a medically necessary second service. It is also not a national dollar amount this desk can quote for a given pair of codes. Locality still applies. The MAC still prices the claim.

## What SLPs should verify

Download the February 24, 2026, MPPR rate file from the CMS Therapy Services page rather than last year’s spreadsheet. Check whether each code on the day’s claim is flagged always-therapy. If PT or OT billed the same beneficiary the same day in the same facility, assume the PE rank will be compared across disciplines. Confirm the MAC’s line-level output against the file. ASHA’s public MPPR explainer is a teaching page, not a contractor remit.
