# February’s compact newsletter named four pending bills. The map will not freeze them.

WASHINGTON — Feb. 4, 2026 — The Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology Interstate Compact Commission’s February 2026 newsletter — Volume 2, Issue 1, dated 4 February — named four non-member states still in a bill fight. Legislation had carried over in Michigan and Pennsylvania. Legislation had been reintroduced in New Jersey and New Mexico.

That is a dated roster, not a prediction. Susan Adams, the Commission’s legislative liaison, wrote that the Commission awaited additional introductions in 2026 and was still doing outreach to non-members. She pointed readers to the Compact Map for the current member list.

## What the map showed later

Fetched on 14 September 2026, the Compact Map reports four jurisdictions with active legislation and 14 with none. It does not print the bill numbers on the bucket labels. It does not promise that February’s four names are still September’s four names.

Bills die in committee. Carryovers lapse. A reintroduction can stall. A state that sat in the “no active legislation” column in February can file in March. This desk is not treating the February letter as a live whip count and is not naming which of today’s four active files belong to which capitol.

ASHA’s 30 May 2025 public note had listed a longer 2025 introduction class, including Michigan, New Jersey, New Mexico, and Pennsylvania among others. By February 2026 the Commission’s own letter had narrowed the named leftover work to those four. Use the letter for what it is: a mid-winter status from the liaison. Use the map the week a move, a hire, or a telepractice contract depends on whether a state has joined.

## What a pending bill is not

A pending bill is not enactment. Enactment is not issuance. The Commission’s home page still lists four issuing states — Louisiana, Ohio, Tennessee, and West Virginia — and 37 enacted jurisdictions. A clinician in a non-member cannot buy a privilege because a neighboring legislature has a bill on the calendar.

Recheck the Compact Map before you treat any of these names as current. If the work is already in an issuing member, the privilege question is onboarding and CompactConnect, not a hearing notice in Lansing, Harrisburg, Trenton, or Santa Fe.

This article does not offer clinical advice, billing guarantees, or diagnosis guidance.
