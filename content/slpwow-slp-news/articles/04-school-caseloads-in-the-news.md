**Disclaimer.** Not legal or union advice.

State ratio bills, hiring freezes, and service-minute lawsuits often share a headline word — **caseload** — but they are different levers. A card that names the claim, the statute, and the local vacancy picture is harder to misread than a single angry number.

Verify whether the story is about maximum ratios, evaluation backlogs, or compensatory services before you forward it to your team chat.
