**Disclaimer.** Editorial map for the SLPWOW news desk — not a site map.

SLPWOW runs parallel packs: parent milestones, professional history, and this news desk. They share a verify-on-primary-source habit but serve different readers.

Use the series map when you wonder whether a pediatric CDC story belongs in News or in the milestones pack — we cross-link, we do not duplicate outrage.
