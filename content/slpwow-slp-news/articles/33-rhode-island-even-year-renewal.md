# Rhode Island SLP licenses expire July 1 in even years. The school certificate is separate.

PROVIDENCE — Sept. 14, 2026 — A full Rhode Island speech-language pathologist license is a two-year card. R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-48.2-6 says the Department of Health issues the license, or a renewal, for two years. Unless it is suspended or revoked sooner, it expires on the first day of July, two years after issuance, and may be renewed biennially in even years if the applicant still meets the chapter.

Renewal, the statute says, is the renewal application plus the renewal fee set in the Department of Health’s fee-structure regulation, payable before July 1 of even years. A licensee who lets the license lapse by failing to renew on or before June 30 of an even year may still renew by sending the application, the renewal fee, and an additional late fee from that same fee structure.

This desk is not printing a dollar amount. RIDOH’s licensing page is where the fee link lives, and the department posted a 2026 amendment to the fee-structure rule. Confirm the live RIDOH page and 216-RICR-10-05-2 before paying a renewal fee.

The health regulation that implements the statute, 216-RICR-40-05-33, repeats the even-year July 1 clock. RIDOH says it mails renewal notices 60 days before expiration. The notice is a courtesy. The statute still runs on July 1.

Continuing education sits on the same even-year calendar. R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-48.2-11 requires at least 20 clock hours in the preceding two years, completed on or before June 30 of even years, with evidence available to the department. People first licensed by examination after a July 1 renewal date are exempt until the next cycle. Confirm the current CE rule on the statute and RIDOH pages; do not take a blog’s hour count as the last word.

## The school assignment is a RIDE field

A RIDOH license is not a public-school assignment. The Rhode Island Department of Education issues a support-professional certificate valid for assignment as a Speech and Language Pathologist in grades PK–12. RIDE’s required-tests list codes that area 13004. The content test on that list is Praxis 5331 with a passing score of 162.

Hold both documents if the job is a Rhode Island public school. The even-year health renewal does not renew 13004, and 13004 does not keep the RIDOH license alive past July 1.

RIDOH’s compact note on the same licensing page said the state had enacted ASLP-IC legislation and that the compact was not currently operational there. A privilege, when Rhode Island is issuing, would still not replace the RIDE certificate.
