A west window in an 8a August house is a greenhouse with bad airflow. Fruit there does not ripen. Fruit there cooks, weeps, and grows a science project on the shoulder.

I want a table away from glass. One layer. Room air, not a sunbeam. If the house is 82°F, that is already enough warmth for the little softening a near-ripe fig will do. It does not need a solar assist.

Paper bags are for pears and for people who like mysteries. A bagged fig in our humidity is a steam room. I have opened that bag. I will not write a second review of the smell.

An apple in the bag adds ethylene. Figs can notice ethylene. They also notice wet air and bruises. I will not run a fruit salad experiment to chase a slogan. If the fig was almost, it will move on a plate. If it was not, the apple will not hire it a sugar factory.

Lights over a shop bench are for people, not for syconia. I have seen a tray left under a shop LED overnight because someone wanted “a little help.” The help was a warm top and a confused morning.

The receiving dock is boring: dining table, towel if you need to stop rolling, stem orientation if you are fussy (I am fussy when the tray is for tomorrow; I am not fussy when I am eating now). Fan in the room is fine. Fan on the fruit is how you shrivel a skin that was already thin.

If the house is hotter than the shade outside, use the shade outside for an hour and then come in. Do not bounce them in and out all day like a dog. One move.

Kids will put figs on the windowsill because tomatoes go there in movies. Tomatoes are a different religion. Walk them to the table. Make it a rule like the latex rule.

A car dashboard is not a ripening room. A car dashboard is how you write a smell that lives in the upholstery. I have done that with a forgotten tray. The car still remembers.

Zone 9 dry air can get away with a sill. Our air is wet. Wet plus sun plus sugar is vinegar. Use a table.

A radiator in a Zone 7 April is a different windowsill. People copy that photo into our August and then they have jam on the paint. Our paint does not need jam. Our table needs a tray.

I have used the shop bench under a north window because the light was pretty. Pretty heated the top skins. I moved the tray two feet into the room and the pretty stopped being a method. Light for a photo, shade for a hold. Do not hire one job to do the other.

If flies are bad, a cloth tent that can breathe is allowed. A sealed cake dome is a sauna. I have opened the dome. I will not open it twice for the same reason.

A dehydrator is not a ripening room either. I have seen someone try to “finish” hard figs on low. They made warm marbles with tough skins. The box is for fruit that was already food.

A cloche or a glass bowl in sun is a bug cooker. I have seen that photo captioned as “ripening.” It is a greenhouse the size of a salad. Our air plus that glass is vinegar. Use a plate in the room.

If you need a “room,” you needed a tree. The house is a hallway between the plant and the mouth. Do not decorate the hallway with glass and hope.
