People ask what “almost” means and then they want a photo. A photo is a liar. A hand is not.

Marble: hard as the tip of your nose. Neck standing like a soldier. Skin glossy, tight, no give at the equator. Color may already be “right.” Leave it. This is not a save. This is a plant still at work.

Almost: the equator gives a little, like a peach you would not serve yet. Neck not fully wilted, maybe a hint of tired. Twist is possible but not eager. Color is there. Eye is whatever that name is, not leaking vinegar. This is a bird-save, a rain-save, a frost-save, a kitchen-firm if you are closer to cook than to dessert. This is not a grocery of marbles.

Ready: neck nodded, give like a ripe peach, twist says yes, stem comes, mouth already knows if you tasted one. This is the farm.

Late: balloon, weep, ants, wasps, a drop, a smell. Eat standing or leave it. Do not fridge a late fig and pretend you bought time.

I make new hands do the line. Three fruit. Eyes closed for the middle one. If they cannot feel the difference between marble and almost, they do not pick that plant unsupervised. Pride is how a tray fills with homework.

Drought will fake a neck wilt on a marble. The equator is still a nose. Water, wait, do not be the hero of a dry Tuesday.

Heat will fake a ready hang on an almost. Morning is how you catch the fake.

A green-skinned name fools eyes. It does not fool a thumb if the thumb is paying attention. Press near the neck, not a death grip on the belly. A death grip is a bruise you will blame on “delicate.”

I do not weigh fruit to decide. Weight helps on a known name — a ready Celeste is heavier than it looks — but a new name will send you to numerology. Thumb, twist, mouth.

If I am unsure, I leave it. Unsure is a valid harvest result. The plant will not be insulted. The bird might be grateful. I can walk again tomorrow.

Teach a child the line with fruit you can afford. If they smash the ready one, that is tuition.

Zone 9 “almost” can hang another week. Our almost in a wet August can be a day. Translate.

A name you just got in a trade will not match the line you learned on Celeste. Spend three mornings on the new plant before you strip it. Tuition. I have paid it in a bowl of polite nothing because I assumed the thumb was portable on day one.

If your hands are hot, run them under the hose. A hot thumb lies toward soft. Morning helps. Clean cool skin helps more than a YouTube.

Gloves change the feel. If you have to glove for latex, practice the line with gloves on. A bare-hand memory will lie through nitrile. I learned that on a tray of “almosts” that were ready and I was numb.

A swollen ostiole that is wet and brown is not “almost.” That is late or weather. Do not put it in the almost line. The line is for clean fruit. Dirty fruit has its own pile and its own clock.

I do the line again after a rain, because rain changes the equator. What felt like almost yesterday can feel like soup today and still be empty. Reset the lesson. Do not trust Monday’s thumb on Wednesday’s weather.

Write it on a card if you have to: nose / peach-not-yet / peach / balloon. Tape it in the shop. Then throw the card away when your thumb knows. The card is training wheels. The hand is the work.
