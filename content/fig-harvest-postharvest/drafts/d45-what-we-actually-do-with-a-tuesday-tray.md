Tuesday, August, South Arkansas. I walked at 7. The tray is not a magazine. It is a mix, because the yard is a mix.

On the table, away from the window: I eat the wounded standing up. I put the honest dessert in a small bowl for lunch and supper. I put the intact extras in a shallow container, towel, crisper, if and only if we will see them by Thursday. I put the honest almosts that I picked as a rain-save on a plate overnight. I put the kitchen-firm in a second bowl if I will cook, or I freeze them tonight if I will not. I put the splits that still smell like fig in a now pile. I put the sour in a hole.

That is the whole religion when I am not performing.

If the tray is a glut, the box gets plugged in after supper or the sheet pan goes in the freezer before I sit down. I have sat down first. I have regretted sitting down first.

Jack sorted with me when the names mattered. Most Tuesdays I do not score. I sort. Scoring is a review day. Tuesday is food.

A neighbor gets a bowl if the bowl is dessert and I am walking past their gate. A neighbor does not get my now pile unless they asked for jam fruit and they know what that means.

Kids get the first good one. Then they get a job: stems the same way, or space the freeze pan. Then they get told no about the sour. No is a harvest skill.

I write nothing unless the name is one we are reviewing. Then I write the date like we did on the plates — August 15, October 1 — and I do not invent a prettier date.

The tray is empty before I go to bed, or it has become a plate, a container, a bag, a box. An overnight tray in the shop is how ants clock in for Wednesday.

This is 8a. This is not a California dry yard and it is not a Zone 7 panic. It is a humid August with enough heat to finish good names and enough weather to ruin a delay.

If you wanted a system with twelve products and a label maker, you came to the wrong shop. We have two pens, a crisper, a box, a freezer, a hole, and a morning. Use them in that order. Leave the slogans on the forum.

If Tuesday is a storm and a meeting, the tray is smaller on purpose. I pick closer Monday. I do not hero Tuesday. Hero Tuesdays are how this set gets ignored.

The hole, the hens, the box, the bag, the crisper, the mouth. That order has kept me liking figs in a month that can make you hate them. I still like them. That is the only proof I will offer.

If I am alone in the shop, I still say the jobs out loud: eat, keep, save, cook, hole. Out loud is how the sour does not slide into keep because I was thinking about email. Email can wait. The tray cannot.

If the tray is three figs, I skip the theater. Eat them. The system is for a glut. A system on three figs is how you make a hobby look like a job and then you quit the hobby.

The Tuesday tray is the test of the whole set. Timing got you fruit. Picking got it off. Off-tree did not turn rocks into jam. Drying and cold and the bag took the rest. If Tuesday still ends in a smell, reread the daily piece and set the alarm.
