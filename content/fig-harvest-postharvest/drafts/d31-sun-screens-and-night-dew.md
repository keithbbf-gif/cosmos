If you sun-dry here, you are in a negotiation with dew. Dew wins if you forget.

A screen that can walk is the only screen I respect. Hardware cloth or a clean window screen on a frame I can carry. Fruit in one layer, not touching if I can help it. Up off the table so air moves under. Cheesecloth on top if the flies clock in. Flies will clock in.

Morning sun after the dew burns off. Not 6 a.m. wet fruit steaming under cloth. Mid-morning to late afternoon if the day is actually dry. Then the whole frame comes in before dusk. Overnight outside is how you undo the day and add slug opinions.

A pop-up storm is not a vibe. It is a run. I have run. I have also lost a tray to a fifteen-minute rain because I was “just finishing something.” The something was not worth the tray.

Cars and roofs I already insulted. I will insult them again if the fruit is on a dark metal roof. That is cooking. Cooking is a jam set.

I turn the fruit once. I do not build a ceremony.

Three to five days is the postcard. Here it can be five to never if the air stays wet. When the air stays wet, I finish in the box. I do not “give it one more day” until I have pale mold on a shoulder. Mold is a decision the dew made.

Animals like screens. A shop with a door is better than a picnic table with a story.

Kids will poke the cloth. Poke is a hole. Hole is a fly.

If I only have a little fruit and a pretty September, I will sun-dry for the pleasure of it. If I have a glut, I plug in. Pleasure is not a plan for Tuesday.

Salt or sulfur or campfire smoke — those are old commercial tricks. I do not run a sulfur house. I run a fan. If you want a chemistry set, that is your yard. This set is food on a screen that comes inside.

Zone 9 night air can be dry enough to leave fruit out. Our night air is a drink. Bring the drink inside, or do not start.

A screened porch is better than a picnic table and worse than a box. I will use the porch for a pretty September Saturday. I will not use it as my August plan. Dew still walks through a screen. Flies still walk through a hole you did not see.

I weigh a screen in my hands before I set it down. If I cannot carry it loaded, I did not build a walking screen. I built a table that pretends. Pretend tables stay out and take the rain.

A black plastic tray in the sun cooks the bottoms. I want mesh and air. If all I have is black plastic, I am in box territory. Do not invent a sun method on cookware.

I do not sun-dry under a pecan that is dripping honeydew. Sticky rain from above is not a method. Move the screen or plug in the box. I have cleaned that mess. I still talk about it like a scar.

I clip the cloth so a wind does not make a sail. A sail dumps fruit into the clay and then you have a drop decision you did not need. Two clips. I have used clothespins. Ugly works. A sail does not.

Sun is free. Dew is also free. Only one of them is on your side. Treat the screen like a pot you can walk. If you cannot walk it, you do not have a sun-dry. You have an offering.
