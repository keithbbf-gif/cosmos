There is a keep pile and there is a now pile. Sour does not get a third pile called hope.

Souring is a smell. Vinegar, dirty wine, a sweetness that went wrong. If you have to ask, it is sour. Do not store it. Do not dry it. Do not freeze it “for later baking” like later you will be a different person. Later you will still have a nose.

A fresh split that still smells like fig can be now food — mouth, pot, a fast dry if you cut it and you are honest. A split that already has tenants is not food. Tenants do not rinse off.

One sour fig in a closed container is a teacher. It teaches the others a smell. I have opened that container. I now sort like a customs agent.

The keep pile is stem-on, intact, morning-picked, tasted as a type that morning. That is a short list on purpose.

People will hide a questionable fig under a good one because they hate waste. Waste is also a ruined tray. The hole and the hens are allowed to win.

I do not “cut the bad part out” of a sour fig and keep the rest for storage. A sour chamber is a room. The whole fruit was in the room.

Kids will try to save every fig because they picked it. Saving is for almosts that are still clean. Saving is not for vinegar.

If a whole plant is souring, that is weather plus eye plus delay. Storage will not fix a plant. Harvest closer. Water even. Next year, a tighter eye if you are tired.

Zone 9 dry splits can still be food longer. Our splits clock in. Translate.

A “just a little tangy” is how people talk themselves into a stomach night. Tangy is lemon. Souring is spoiled. If you have to sell it to yourself, it is not keep.

I have tried to dry a tray of splits because the box was already on. The jar tasted like a storm drain with sugar. I threw it out and I still remember the waste. The waste was the moment I put them in, not the moment I tossed the jar.

Two trays. I will buy you the second plate if that is what it takes. I cannot buy you the nose. Use the nose.

I smell the keep container the next morning even if I sorted well. One late sour is enough. If the container smells like a cidery, I dump it and I start over with what is still on the plant. Pride in yesterday’s sort is how you serve a problem.

A fruit fly in the keep container is a vote. I do not outvote a fruit fly with hope. I empty, wash, start over. Hope is how a kitchen gets a week of flies and a story about “the figs.”

I do not bury sour in a smoothie and call it hidden. Hidden is how you teach a kid that figs taste like that. If it is not food, it is not a smoothie. The hens and the hole do not need a recipe.

A keep pile that needs a story is not a keep pile. If I am explaining the fruit, I have already lost the argument. Put it down. Walk away. The now pile will take it if it is still honest.

If I am tired, I still sort. Tired is when the sour slides. Sit down. Sort. Then sit down again.

Keep and now. Two trays. If you only have one tray, you will lie to yourself. Get a second. It can be a plate. It cannot be a wish.
