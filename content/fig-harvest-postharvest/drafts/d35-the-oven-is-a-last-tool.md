An oven will dry figs. An oven also wants to bake dinner and heat a house that is already too hot. I use it when the box is full, the glut is honest, and I am not willing to lose the tray.

Lowest heat I can get. Door cracked with a wooden spoon so the wet air can leave. Fan on if it is a convection box. One layer. Watch it like it is a child near a pond. Ovens lie at “170” and then they spike.

I have cooked a tray into fig chips because I walked away. Chips are still food. They are not what I wanted. I wanted leather.

Do not stack sheet pans and hope. The top pan is a lid. The bottom pan is a skillet.

A gas oven with a pilot is a different animal than a tight electric. I will not write your brand a love letter. I will write: stay in the room.

The oven is also how you finish a sun tray that got rained on and is still honest. Short time. Check the center. Do not try to do a twelve-hour oven dry while you sleep unless you like waking up to a smell that is not supper.

I would rather freeze the overflow than run the oven for six hours in August and hate the house. Freezing is a tool. The oven is a stubborn tool. Stubborn has a place. Stubborn should not be the plan.

Microwave leather experiments are a forum sport. I have not made a winter jar I liked that way. I will not tell you to.

If you have no box and no winter patience, make jam or freeze. Those jobs match a kitchen. Long dry in an oven is a shop job wearing an apron.

Zone 9 people can laugh and point at the sun. Zone 7 people can run an oven in September and the house says thank you. Our August house says please stop.

If I run the oven, I set a timer I will actually hear. Not a phone in another room. The shop radio or a cheap wind-up. I have lost a tray to a conversation on the porch. The conversation was good. The tray was carbon.

A convection roast setting is not a dehydrator. It is a browning machine. I have used it by accident. The fruit looked like a commercial bag and chewed like a shoe. I still ate it chopped into yogurt because I am not a hero of waste. I did not call it a method.

When the glut is bigger than oven plus box, freeze. I will say it until it sticks. The oven is for the last honest tray, not for the whole farm. If you find yourself lining every rack and the cookie sheets and the pizza stone, you are in a mood, and moods burn sugar.

If the house has guests, I do not run the oven-dry. I freeze. Guests plus a cracked door plus fig steam is a party I did not mean to throw. The bag does not ask anyone to sweat.

I do not broil “just to finish the tops.” Broil is how you invent charcoal. If the tops are wet, you needed more time and air, not a flame from above.

I put a note on the oven door that says FIGS so a helpful person does not preheat for chicken. Helpful is how you invent charcoal and a ruined supper. The note is ugly. The surprise is worse.

Last tool means last. Buy a used box if this is your every year. The oven is how you finish a season, not how you design one.
