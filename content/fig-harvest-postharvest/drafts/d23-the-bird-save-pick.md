A bird-save is a specific fruit. It is not a mood.

The plant is being hammered. The forecast is a two-inch rain on an open eye. Thursday night is frost. Those are saves. The fruit has to already be in the conversation: color of that name, a hint of neck or a real give, a twist that is not a saw. Then you may take it and give it one night, one layer, stem up, out of the sun.

A marble is not a save. A marble is a surrender.

I put saves on a plate, not in a bowl. Bowls hide the ones that go bad first. A plate shows you who moved by morning. The ones that softened and smell like themselves get eaten or chilled. The ones that stayed stubborn get cooked that day or they get honest compost. I do not give them a second night as a reward for being difficult.

Rain-save on an open eye happens at first honest give, not at first blush. Blush is still a tree job. Give is the door.

Frost-save can be a little earlier than I like because the night will not negotiate. I still refuse the hard green. I have filled a dehydrator with close fruit before a cold night and been glad. I have filled a tray with marbles and been a fool.

Bird-save on a dark name happens earlier in the color than on a green-skinned name, because the birds can see the dark ones. That is not science. That is a mockingbird.

I label the plate “save” so a helpful person does not fridge the whole thing “to keep.” The fridge will stop the little softening you were hoping for. Saves need the counter first, then the cold if they made it.

A save is not a review fruit. Do not score a bird-save and decide the variety is 3. I have watched that happen. The plant was not ready. You were in a fight with wildlife.

If the same plant needs saving every morning, change the plant’s weather. Walk the pot. Net if you can do it without building a snare. Pick the ripe ones earlier in the day. A daily save is a system failure, not a technique.

Zone 7 lives in save season. I will steal their lack of romance when I need it. I will not live there in the first week of August because I am bored.

I have picked a whole plant as saves because I was leaving for two days. That is a real save. I have also picked a whole plant as saves because I was impatient on a Sunday. That is a fake. The plate will tell you in the morning. The fake plate is still mostly homework. Eat what moved, cook what did not, and do not schedule a fake again.

A net that is already up changes the save. If the birds cannot get in, you can let the neck finish. The save is for the hole in the system, not for the feeling that you should be doing something with your hands.

A save plate on a Tuesday should be empty by Wednesday lunch. If it is still there Thursday, you were not saving. You were hoarding homework. Homework goes to a pot or a hole.

A save is easier to justify than to stop. When the birds move on, go back to the neck. I have stayed in save mode for a week out of habit. Habit picked a lot of homework.

The word save is earned. If you cannot say what you are saving *from*, you are just picking early. Early has another name. It is called a bad bowl.
