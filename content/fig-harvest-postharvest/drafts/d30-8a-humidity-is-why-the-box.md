I would sun-dry if I lived in a postcard. I live in South Arkansas. August air is a wet blanket. Fruit on a screen outside will gather dew at night and second thoughts at noon.

A dehydrator is not fancy. It is how you make a dry climate in a cube. Fan, heat, time. The cube does not care that the yard is 80% humidity. The cube has a job.

I still like a screen in a dry September stretch for a small tray, and I still bring it in at night. That is a hobby. The box is the method. If you only have one tool, have the box.

People will tell you “just use the car” or “just use the shop roof.” I have a shop roof. I also have flies, a pop-up storm, and a possum with a business plan. The box closes.

Run it where you can stand the heat and the smell. A small kitchen in August plus a box is a second stove. The shop bench is better if the dust is not a blizzard. Do not run it in a closed closet. The moisture has to go somewhere.

I do not chase a 0% humidity fantasy. I chase fruit that bends and does not weep. Paper will say finish near 17% moisture. I do not weigh chemistry every batch. I cut one. If the center is jam-wet, it goes back in. If the center is leather, it comes out. If it shatters like glass, I overshot and I will still eat it chopped.

135°F is the fruit preset on most home boxes. Davis talks 140°F / 60°C. Close enough that I will not start a war. I will start a war with “high” and a forgotten tray. High cooks the outside and leaves a swamp in the middle.

If the week is a steam bath, the box runs longer. That is not a broken machine. That is 8a leaking into the cube every time you open it to peek. Peek less.

Sun is allowed as a pre-wilt on a dry morning — fruit on a screen for a few hours, then into the box. I will not pretend that is “sun-dried” for a label. That is a head start.

Zone 9 can laugh at a box. Zone 7 can skip a box. We are the humid middle. The box is how August becomes a jar instead of a smell.

A box with a dead fan is an oven. I listen for the fan before I walk away. I have come back to silent heat and a tray of cooked shoulders. The heating element is not the method. The air moving is the method. If the fan dies, you are in oven-last-tool territory, and I already told you how I feel about that in August.

I empty the drip tray if the box has one. People forget and then they have a swamp under a fan. The swamp is 8a in miniature. Dump it. That is the whole extra rule.

A box in a bathroom with a shower habit is a comedy. Moisture lives there. The shop or a laundry room with a door you can leave open is better. I have tried the bathroom. The fruit tasted like a towel.

I bought a box because I got tired of losing a glut to pride. Pride says the old ways are enough. The old ways were a dry wind. We do not have their wind. We have a fan we can plug in. Plug it in.
