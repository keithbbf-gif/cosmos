# Staging sheet

Every file in `drafts/` carries YAML:

```yaml
status: staged
stage: time | pick | ripen | dry | store
focus: 8a
zones: [7, 8a, 8b, 9]
author: Jack Chambers | PapaFig
cluster: timing | picking | off-tree | drying | storage
voice: human
voice_check: draft   # writer pass. Editor sets edited after voice pass
```

`status: staged` means the piece is a finished writer draft in the house
voice, sitting for a human editor. It is not `published`. It is not `scratch`.

## Read order (if you want fruit in a bowl, not a theory)

1. `d01` through `d10` — when it is actually time
2. `d11` through `d20` — how you take it
3. `d21` through `d28` — what the counter will and will not do
4. `d29` through `d36` — dry what was already good
5. `d37` through `d45` — keep it a few days, or change its job

## Required topics (coverage)

- Timing: August crop, breba as bonus, neck bend, eye/ostiole, heat stall, frost
- Picking: twist + stem, morning, latex, shallow tray, birds, wasps, pots
- Off-tree: sugar is mostly done, softening ≠ ripe, bird-save, no fridge-to-ripe
- Drying: ripe only, 8a humidity, sun+dew, ~135°F, wet center, condition the jar
- Storage: days not a week, crisper 32–40°F, do not wash first, freeze the glut

If a later editor cuts the set down, keep at least one draft from each stage and
do not cut `d01` (August crop), `d05` (neck bend), `d21` (not a banana),
`d30` (humidity / dehydrator), or `d37` (days, not a week).
