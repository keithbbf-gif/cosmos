## A patient’s name on a method

Lee Silverman is the ethical fact in the branding. Methods named for doctors are ordinary. Methods named for patients are rarer and easier to empty of the patient. A heritage page that never says the name is doing the emptying.

The late-1980s papers are the start of a literature. The company is the afterlife. Essay 14 already named Boone’s older observation and the crowded quieter programs. This profile exists so Ramig is not only an acronym on a slide.

Dosage debates in the later trials are the grown-up form of Boone’s corridor observation. This page will not reprint a dose. It will say the debate exists and that a magazine is the wrong place to settle it.
