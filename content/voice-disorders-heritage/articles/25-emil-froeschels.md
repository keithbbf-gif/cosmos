# Emil Fröschels

*Educational history for SLPWOW. Not a treatment plan and not a substitute for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

> **Portrait placeholder.** No public-domain or clearly licensed portrait located as of the pack date. See `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` for hunt status. Do not invent or generate a substitute likeness.

Emil Fröschels coined a word for the therapy side of the work and then had to carry the word out of the city that made it.

He was born in Vienna on 24 August 1884 and died in New York on 3 January 1972. The M.D. is Vienna, 1907. Some web pages still send him to Jena for the degree. Vienna is the better-sourced one; say so if you reprint a CV that disagrees. *Lehrbuch der Sprachheilkunde (Logopädie)* appeared in 1913 (later editions 1925, 1931). In 1924 he called the meeting that became the International Association of Logopedics and Phoniatrics. The sibling pack tells the association and the American academy’s parallel. This profile stays with the voice methods and the suitcase.

## Chewing, pushing, exile

The *Kaumethode* assumed a tight voice might borrow ease from a vegetative movement. The pushing work assumed a weak or paretic voice might borrow closure from an effortful gesture. Essay 07 refuses the step list. The life has to name the theory: a Viennese physician who thought a mundane movement could treat a skilled organ. Brodnitz, in New York, still thought so in 1954. Later resonant-voice and easy-onset dialects are relatives, not reprints.

He fled in 1938. St. Louis and New York were the American rooms. The IALP word traveled farther than he did. European therapists still call themselves logopedists; Americans do not. The split is a passport story as much as a terminology story.

## How to read him now

Read the 1913 textbook as a physician writing for teachers and doctors at once — Gutzmann’s double claim in an Austrian key. Read the chewing paper as an exile document. Read the American afterlife as a few stubborn textbook paragraphs and a New York clinic, not as a modern efficacy trial. Do not resurrect a 1930s gesture from a heritage page.

The sibling pack’s Fröschels profile covers the association politics and the stuttering side. Use both. Do not merge them into one “pioneer” blur.

## 1924 and 1938 are different years

The IALP meeting is a triumph story. 1938 is not. A profile that only prints the word *logopedics* and the chewing method has written a CV. The suitcase is the American voice clinic’s European accent (essay 08). Fröschels in New York is not Fröschels in Vienna with better coffee. The patients changed, the language changed, the methods were explained to people who had already read Van Riper.

Use the sibling pack for the association minutes. Use this page for the larynx-adjacent methods and the fact of flight.
