# Gottfried E. Arnold (Godfrey)

*Educational history for SLPWOW. Not a treatment plan and not a substitute for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

> **Portrait placeholder.** No public-domain or clearly licensed portrait located as of the pack date. See `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` for hunt status. Do not invent or generate a substitute likeness.

Gottfried Eduard Arnold was born in Olmütz, then Austria-Hungary, on 6 January 1914, and died in Vienna on 5 July 1989. In the United States he published as Godfrey E. Arnold. First mention in this pack carries both. He was the son of Anton Arnold, a tenor at the Vienna Court Opera. He trained as a physician at the University of Vienna and as a pianist at the State Academy. Internships with Hermann Gutzmann Jr. in Berlin sit in the CV. The Vienna ear clinic’s voice-and-speech ambulance gave him the case material for *Die traumatischen und konstitutionellen Störungen der Stimme und Sprache* (1948) and, with Richard Luchsinger, the 1949 textbook.

## Injection, and a word

In 1962 he described Teflon injection into a vocal fold. The next generation would spend years regretting the material and pulling it out. History is allowed to say that. In the early 1960s he and Hans von Leden put *phonosurgery* into English — operations meant to change a voice, not only to save a life. Essay 15 is the wider knife. This profile is the man who wanted a name for a hopeful, sometimes reckless, middle.

He married Isolde Reuter, an opera singer, in 1941. That fact is in the public biographical record and belongs here only because the household was the European version of Sataloff’s later double life: medicine and the stage in one family. After retirement he returned to Vienna and is buried at the Zentralfriedhof.

## How to read him

Read the textbook with Luchsinger as the map (profile 27). Read the Teflon year as a date, not a recommendation. Read “phonosurgery” as a 1960s optimism that Isshiki’s framework surgery would later discipline. He is Nadoleczny’s student in the Munich stories and Gutzmann Jr.’s intern in the Berlin ones. The lineage is the European medical voice, Americanized, then sent home to die.

## Two first names, one shelf

Catalogers who do not cross Gottfried and Godfrey will split a man. This pack refuses the split. The Teflon year and the phonosurgery word are the American sentences. The 1948 Vienna monograph and the 1949 textbook are the European ones. Isshiki later made the hopeful middle more precise. Arnold’s generation had to name the middle first, and they named it with a material the next generation hated.

Zentralfriedhof is a Viennese ending for an Olmütz birth. The empire of his childhood did not outlive his childhood. The textbook did.
