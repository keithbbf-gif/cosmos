# Hans von Leden

*Educational history for SLPWOW. Not a treatment plan and not a substitute for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

> **Portrait placeholder.** No public-domain or clearly licensed portrait located as of the pack date. See `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` for hunt status. Do not invent or generate a substitute likeness.

Hans Victor von Leden died at home in Los Angeles on 5 March 2014, of pneumonia, at ninety-five. He had been born in Breslau, Silesia — now Wrocław — on 20 November 1918, and had followed a father who left when the 1930s became readable. The memorial essays call him a founder of phonosurgery and of CoMeT. Both claims are dated and checkable.

## Films, a word, a collegium

With G. Paul Moore at Northwestern he made the 1956 high-speed pictures that essay 09 treats as the moment vibration became public. With Godfrey Arnold in the early 1960s he put *phonosurgery* into English. On 14 August 1969, at the ninth International Congress of Otorhinolaryngology in Mexico City, he and ten laryngologists founded the Collegium Medicorum Theatri. He served years as secretary-coordinator. He also pushed a Committee on the Care of the Voice inside IFOS. Eighteen societies made him an honorary member, the memorialists say, and they count thirty-six awards the way those documents do.

The Institute of Laryngology and Voice Disorders, and the Los Angeles years, are the American address. CoMeT is the international one. The Voice Foundation’s 1982 G. Paul Moore Lecture was his. The life is a man who wanted the artistic voice to have a medical guild that was not only a gala.

## Residue

A word still used in OR schedules. A membership still harder to get than a symposium badge. Thirteen scientific films, if the memorial count holds. A childhood city that changed its name. Do not generate a face for a man who sat for a century of real cameras. Ask CoMeT and the Foundation before ingesting a gala shot.

## IFOS and a Mexican August

The Committee on the Care of the Voice inside IFOS is the unglamorous twin of CoMeT. One is a membership. One is a committee in a larger ENT federation. von Leden wanted both. The 14 August 1969 date is specific enough to keep; Mexico City during a world congress is how international specialties actually start — not in a brand office.

Breslau / Wrocław is not a trivia line. It is why a Los Angeles phonosurgeon’s childhood city is now spelled differently on maps. The memorialists who count awards are doing their genre. This pack keeps the films, the word, and the collegium.
