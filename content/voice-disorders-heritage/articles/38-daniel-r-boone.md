# Daniel R. Boone

*Educational history for SLPWOW. Not a treatment plan and not a substitute for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

> **Portrait placeholder.** No public-domain or clearly licensed portrait located as of the pack date. See `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` for hunt status. Do not invent or generate a substitute likeness.

Daniel R. Boone wrote the American student book and then kept showing up to teach it.

He was born in Chicago on 30 October 1927, son of Claude B. and Pearl L. Boone, and died on 26 or 27 October 2018. The University of Arizona notice prints both days on the same page — Friday the 26th in the lead, the 27th in the narrative, three days short of ninety-one. Parkinson Voice Project, which hosted him that week in Richardson, Texas, says the early morning of Saturday the 27th at Methodist Richardson Medical Center. This pack prints the disagreement.

## VA rooms, then a title

Army of occupation, Korea, 1946–47. B.A., University of Redlands, 1951, speech pathology after a start in radio-television. Long Beach VA, 1951–53, aphasia from two wars. M.A. 1954 and Ph.D. 1958, Western Reserve. Western Reserve faculty, Kansas Medical Center, University of Denver, then Arizona from 1973 to 1999. The first twenty professional years, Arizona’s page says, were neurogenic. *The Voice and Voice Therapy* (1971) turned the later forty toward voice. ASHA presidency, Fellowship, Honors (1984). Eighteen books, if the department count holds; *Trouble Talking* is the late narrative of forty patients.

He noticed, early, that people with Parkinson disease could be louder on purpose. Essay 14 is the intensity story. The Texas program that hosted his last workshop built a branded line on “speak with intent” and thanked him. The historical fact is the VA observation, not the brand.

Mary Mosenthal Boone, sixty-three years; four children. The Arizona obituary names them. That is enough family.

## How to read the 1971 book

As a menu of facilitating techniques, later criticized for being a menu. As the date American SLP training had a voice text that was not a laryngologist’s paperback. As a document, not a protocol. Later editions exist; cite the one you hold.

## Redlands to Richardson

The radio-television start is in the Arizona obituary and belongs here as a fork, not a cute origin. He chose speech pathology. The VA years chose him back. ASHA’s presidency is the guild honor. The 1971 title is the shelf honor. The last workshop is the death date’s argument.

*Trouble Talking* as a late book of forty patients is a narrative choice a heritage series can name without quoting a case. Do not mine it for stories. The observation about Parkinson intensity is already in essay 14.
