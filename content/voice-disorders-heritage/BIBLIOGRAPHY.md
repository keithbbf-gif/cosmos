# Bibliography — SLPWOW Voice Disorders Heritage Pack

Sources consulted while writing the 44 staged articles. Editorial working list, not a complete historiography. Prefer the primary paper or the institutional memorial over a secondary retelling when they disagree.

Articles cite contested facts in the text. This file holds the warrant.

## Instruments and the mirror

- García, Manuel. “Observations on the Human Voice.” *Proceedings of the Royal Society of London* 7 (1855): 399–410. Communication 24 May 1855.
- Czermak, Johann Nepomuk. *Der Kehlkopfspiegel und seine Verwerthung für Physiologie und Medizin.* Leipzig, 1860.
- Babington, Benjamin Guy. Hunterian Society demonstration of a “glottiscope,” 18 March 1829. Later historical reconstructions: papers in the laryngology-history literature; do not treat a modern drawing as Babington’s own plate.
- Bozzini, Philipp. *Lichtleiter* descriptions, 1806. Later museum and endoscopy-history essays; reconstructions vary — articles refuse a fake successful glottis view.
- Green, Horace. *A Treatise on Diseases of the Air Passages.* New York, 1846. Contemporary accusations of fraud belong with the claim.

## London laryngology

- Mackenzie, Morell. *The Hygiene of the Vocal Organs.* London, 1886.
- Mackenzie, Morell. *The Fatal Illness of Frederick the Noble.* London, 1888. Pamphlet war, not a slide review.
- Hospital for Diseases of the Throat, Golden Square: institutional histories print 1865 (some secondary lists 1863 for an earlier outpatient start). Articles flag the wobble.
- Semon, Felix. Contemporary London laryngology; “Semon’s law” as later teaching object.

- Gutzmann, Hermann. *Des Kindes Sprache und Sprachfehler.* 1894.

- Gutzmann, Hermann. *Sprachheilkunde.* 1912.

- Nadoleczny obituary. *Practica Oto-Rhino-Laryngologica* 3 (1940): 376–379. https://doi.org/10.1159/000273277 (16 March 1874 – 6 November 1940).

- Tarneaud, Jean. French *phoniatrie* (term pushed 1932); Société Française de Phoniatrie; *Folia Phoniatrica* founding, 1947, with Richard Luchsinger and Miroslav Seeman.

- Arnold, Gottfried E. *Die traumatischen und konstitutionellen Störungen der Stimme und Sprache.* Vienna, 1948.
- Arnold, G. E. Teflon injection description, 1962; “phonosurgery” with Hans von Leden, early 1960s (see von Leden, “The History of Phonosurgery,” in Ford & Bless, *Phonosurgery*, Raven Press, 1991).

## Seeing, measuring, layers

- Oertel, Max. Laryngeal stroboscopy: teaching dates cluster on 1878 attempts and a usable 1890s instrument. Articles refuse a fake precise day.
- Moore, G. Paul. “A Stroboscopic Study of Vocal Fold Movement.” Ph.D. diss., Northwestern University, 1936.
- Northwestern University Archives. G. Paul Moore (1907–2008) finding aid. Born 2 November 1907; died January 2008, Gainesville.
- Voice Foundation. G. Paul Moore Lecturers page (death 17 January 2008; lecture founded 1981).
- Moore, G. Paul, and Hans von Leden. *The Larynx and Voice* films, 1956 (normal function; daily stress; pathologic companion). Voice Foundation historical video archive.
- van den Berg, Janwillem. *Physica van de stemvorming, met toepassingen.* Dissertation, Rijksuniversiteit Groningen, 1953.
- van den Berg, Janwillem. “Sur les théories myo-elastique et neuro-chronaxique de la phonation.” *Revue de laryngologie* (1954).
- van den Berg, Janwillem. “Myoelastic-Aerodynamic Theory of Voice Production.” *Journal of Speech and Hearing Research* 1 (1958): 227–244. https://doi.org/10.1044/jshr.0103.227
- Gould Award 1960: *Archives of Otolaryngology* announcement, 1961.
- National Center for Voice and Speech. Hall of Fame: Janwillem van den Berg; Wilbur James Gould. https://ncvs.org/
- Hirano, Minoru. “Morphological Structure of the Vocal Cord as a Vibrator and its Variations.” *Folia Phoniatrica* 26 (1974): 89–94. https://doi.org/10.1159/000263771
- Hirano, Minoru. *Clinical Examination of Voice.* 1981.
- American Laryngological Association. Obituary: Minoru Hirano, died 19 December 2017.
- Titze, Ingo R. *Principles of Voice Production.* Englewood Cliffs, NJ: Prentice Hall, 1994.

## Professional voice and the knife

- The Voice Foundation. Mission and History: founded 1969; first symposium 1972; gala 1973; Sataloff chairmanship 1989. https://voicefoundation.org/about/history-and-mission/
- Sataloff memorial: “In memory of Wilbur James Gould, M.D.” *Journal of Voice* (1994). Death 7 February 1994, Lenox Hill, age 74.
- Collegium Medicorum Theatri (CoMeT): founded 14 August 1969, Mexico City (von Leden memorials).
- “In Memoriam: Hans von Leden 1918–2014.” Born 20 November 1918, Breslau; died 5 March 2014, Los Angeles.
- Isshiki, Nobuhiko. *Phonosurgery: Theory and Practice.* 1989. Framework-surgery papers from the 1970s.
- British Laryngological Association. “In Memoriam: Nobuhiko Isshiki 1930–2022.” Died 14 August 2022.
