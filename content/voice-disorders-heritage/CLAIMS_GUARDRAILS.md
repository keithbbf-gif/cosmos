# Claims Guardrails — Voice Disorders Heritage

Educational history for SLPWOW. Fail a draft that forgets this file.

## What a reader may take away

- Who looked at a living glottis, when, and with what light.
- Which books, scales, films, and meetings still sit in training programs.
- Where medicine, singing pedagogy, and speech-language pathology shared a hallway — and where they refused to.

## What a reader may not take away

- A home program.
- A diagnosis.
- A reason to delay an ENT or SLP visit.
- A celebrity-medicine fantasy (“Garcia invented the voice clinic”).

If a paragraph can be copied into a clinic handout without rewriting, cut it.

## Hard refusals

| Topic | Allowed | Refused |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Chewing / pushing / accent / resonant / VFE / LSVT | Who published it, year, the argument around it | Step lists, cue cards, dosage, “do this daily” |
| Botulinum for spasmodic dysphonia | Dedo, Blitzer, Ludlow; the 1980s turn | Units, sites, intervals, brand names as advice |
| Thyroplasty / injection | Isshiki types as *history*; Arnold’s Teflon era | Surgical indication charts |
| Gender-affirming voice | That the work exists; that it is recent and contested | Protocols, pitch targets, “how to sound…” |
| Reflux / hygiene | That hygiene lectures were a mid-century genre | Diet lists, steam recipes |
| Named living patients | Never | Never |
| Named dead public figures | Frederick III; published performer memoirs | Gossip, invented dressing-room scenes |

## Living people in this pack

No family detail that is not in a citable professional bio. No home address. Portrait: placeholder unless a rights-clear, non-generated image is logged.

## Sibling pack

Do not retell the Hotel McAlpin, the Iowa stuttering school, or ASHA’s four names as if they were voice-clinic origin stories. Point to `content/slpwow-speech-pathology-history/` and stay on the larynx.

## Uncertainty is a claim

Print the disagreement. Examples already in the copy:

- Türck and Czermak both used a mirror in 1858; priority is a Viennese fight, not a solved riddle.
- Duchan says Brodnitz studied with Gutzmann Sr.; UEP says Gutzmann Jr. and Flatau. Sr. died in 1922; Brodnitz’s Berlin medical year is 1924. The article says so.
- Boone’s Arizona notice prints 26 and 27 October 2018 on the same page.

A clean false date is worse than an ugly “not found.”
