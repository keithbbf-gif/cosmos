- American Speech-Language-Hearing Association. (2024). *2024 Schools Survey report: SLP workforce and work conditions.* <https://www.asha.org/siteassets/surveys/2024-schools-survey-slp-workforce.pdf>
- American Speech-Language-Hearing Association. (2022). *2022 Schools Survey: SLP caseload and workload characteristics.* (Median 48 — cite only when contrasting years.) <https://www.asha.org/siteassets/surveys/2022-schools-survey-slp-caseload.pdf>

Key 2024 caseload figures used in this pack (do not “round for SEO”):

- Median actual monthly caseload, full-time clinical providers: **50** (range 4–351; n = 2,815).
- Median manageable: **40** (n = 2,776).
- Facility actual medians: day/residential **25**; preschool **40** actual / **35** manageable `[VERIFY preschool pair against Figure 1 if quoting both]`; elementary **51** / **45**; secondary **50** / **45**; telepractice office **52** / **45**; combination **50** / **45**.
- State actual medians with n ≥ 25 include Indiana **78**, Texas **65**, Tennessee **63**, Florida/Kentucky/Louisiana **60**, New York **32**, Connecticut **39**, Arkansas **40**, Massachusetts **40**.
- Barriers: no barrier 24%; SLP shortage 26%; lack of admin support 12%; dismissal difficulty 10%; district/state policy 10%; parent resistance 4%; shortage of assistants 1%; other 15%.
- Weekly hours (mean, list capped at 55, 89% of respondents, n = 2,347): total **39.3**; direct **22.6**; documentation **6.0**; diagnostics **4.0**; consultation **2.5**; supervision **1.1**; tech checks **0.7**; other **2.4**.
- Missed sessions: 42% make up only in a few circumstances; 37% always/almost always; 20% never/almost never.
- Areas of intervention (percent who regularly serve / mean n among those who do): ASD 93.8% / 14.2; language (semantics/morphology/syntax) 90.2% / 23.0; speech sound 89.1% / 20.1; pragmatics 86.7% / 14.7; AAC 74.5% / 7.2; fluency 67.6% / 2.6; CAS 60.1% / 3.0; literacy 28.0% / 14.8.

Key 2024 workforce figures:

- 87% full time; 86% salaried; 12% contractors; 1% self-employed.
- Paperwork #1 challenge in every facility type; workload/caseload #2 in most.
- 34% considering changing setting; 27% considering leaving the profession because of burnout.
- 50% put a separate salary schedule from teachers in their top two retention suggestions.
- 12% supervised an SLPA; 63% had union representation available; 79% said more openings than seekers.
