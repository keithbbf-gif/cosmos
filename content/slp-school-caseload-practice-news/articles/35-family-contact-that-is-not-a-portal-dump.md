This series does not use student records or any protected health information (PHI). Local IDEA, FERPA, Medicaid, and district rules govern practice. Talk with your district, a licensed speech-language pathologist, or counsel before changing services.

IDEA already put families at the table. A portal notification is not a table.

ASHA’s 2024 workforce list included limited family/caregiver involvement and support among the 13 challenges. It did not win first place. Paperwork did. That ranking is easy to misread as “families are fine.” It is easier to misread as “families are the problem.” Four percent named parent resistance as their top *caseload* barrier (essay 14). Most families are not that checkbox.

A **portal dump** is a progress report uploaded at 11 p.m. with no conversation, a 12-page IEP draft the night before the meeting, a therapy calendar that assumes someone can leave work for a 1:15. It satisfies a system. It does not satisfy a partner.

This essay will not write a script. It will name the contact that is actually the job.

## What families are owed

Prior written notice when the district proposes or refuses a change. Access to the education record (essay 33). An interpreter if the meeting is not in a language they can use (essay 36). A progress report on the schedule the IEP promised (essay 29). A service page they can understand (essay 24).

They are owed the difference between MTSS, 504, and IEP (essay 19) in human words. They are owed “we are not diagnosing from this blog, and we are not diagnosing from a milestone chart.”

They are not owed another student’s comparison. They are not owed a dump of closet data they cannot picture in a kitchen.

## What SLPs need and rarely schedule

A description of communication at home *in the family’s words*. A yes or no on whether the device came back charged. A question they actually have. Consent that is specific — evaluation, services, public benefits — not a blur (essay 32).

Preschool SLPs live in this hour more than secondary SLPs. The facility tables already hinted that family involvement ranks differently by setting. Do not staff a high school like a preschool and then call families “uninvolved.”

## Workload, not sainthood

Family contact sits in the indirect clusters. It is not extra virtue. It is how *Endrew F.*’s “circumstances” get into the room. It is also how you avoid a hearing that began as a missed email.

Large caseloads shrink the hour. The portal said so. Name it (essay 43). Do not “solve” it with a mass message that includes the wrong student.

## Language that is not a dump

*Here is what we are working on in the classroom this month, in one paragraph. Here is when the next report comes. Here is how to tell us the plan is nonsense. Here is the interpreter line if you need it.*

No protocol for home practice. ASHA’s public pages already have interaction tips; this pack will not morph them into homework.

You can refuse a diagnosis. You can refuse a named child.

IDEA already seated families at the table; a timestamp is not a chair. Limited family involvement ranked among 2024’s thirteen challenges; it did not win, and it does not license a dump. Contact is a workload cluster, not leftover virtue. If the family needs an interpreter and the meeting proceeds without one, you have a civil-rights problem wearing a calendar. If the family needs a paper copy and you only have a portal, you have an access problem wearing a platform.

A year of dumps is not “they never engage.” It is a pipe that never opened.

The portal is a pipe. The family is a partner. Do not confuse the pipe for the partner.
