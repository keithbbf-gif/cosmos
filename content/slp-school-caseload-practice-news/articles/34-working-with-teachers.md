“Limited time for collaboration” ranked **sixth** among 13 challenges in ASHA’s 2024 workforce report. In elementary it sat fifth. In day/residential and preschool it sat tenth. The national activity list put consultation at **2.5 hours** a week. Those two numbers are the same story told twice: the job requires other adults, and the calendar does not.

A **therapy dump** is the opposite of that job. It is a folder of closet techniques left on a teacher’s desk with a smile. It is a 20-slide deck in an August in-service and silence until May. It is “just remind him to use his words” as if that were a present level.

ASHA’s 2010 roles document wanted interprofessional practice. The caseload portal says large caseloads steal the time. This essay will not write a collaboration protocol. It will name what a teacher can actually use.

## What a teacher can use

A present level that sounds like their room (essay 22). A goal they would notice if it moved (essay 23). Two sentences about what they are already doing that *is* language instruction. One ask that fits the unit, not a second curriculum. A time to talk that is on a calendar — even fifteen minutes in a 3:1 week (essay 26).

They can use honesty about LRE: why this student leaves, or why you are coming in (essay 24). They can use a warning before you pull a student from the only writing block.

They cannot use a diagnosis. They cannot use another student’s comparison. They cannot use a dump.

## What SLPs need from teachers and rarely get in writing

The language of the next unit. Who talks in the group and who does not. Whether the device is charged. Whether the accommodation on the 504 or IEP is actually happening. A work sample.

That list is data (essay 30). It is also workload for the teacher. If you want it, trade something they value: a language routine that saves *them* a reteach, not an extra program.

## Role understanding is the cousin ranking

“Limited understanding of my role by others” ranked seventh nationally and fourth in secondary (essay 11). Collaboration fails when the teacher thinks you are the baby-talk person or the reading teacher or the behavior person. A one-page roles summary in August is not enough. A shared unit in October is better.

Science of reading (essay 37) is a live wire in this conversation. Stay in your lane without abandoning language. The dump that says “you do phonics, I do /r/” is as unhelpful as the dump that says “I will now run your literacy block.”

## How to put it on a principal page

Consultation hours calendared versus the 2.5 mean. Number of teachers you are supposed to be a partner to. Number of buildings. No student names.

If collaboration time is zero, classroom-based service (essay 28) is a slogan.

## Language that is not a dump

*Here is the one language demand in next week’s unit that matches this goal. Here is what I will do. Here is a sentence you can try if you want. Here is when I will come back to hear whether it was nonsense.*

You can refuse a home program. You can refuse a student story.

If the only collaboration time is a passing-period apology, you do not have a partner. You have a hallway. Put fifteen minutes on a calendar or stop calling the dump a program.

The teacher has the year. You have a slice. Talk like that is true, because it is.
