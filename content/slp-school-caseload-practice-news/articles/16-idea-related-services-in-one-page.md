**Disclaimer.** Educational only. Not legal, billing, union, or clinical advice. Not a diagnosis and not a treatment protocol. This series does not use student records or any protected health information (PHI). Local IDEA, FERPA, Medicaid, and district rules govern practice. Talk with your district, a licensed speech-language pathologist, or counsel before changing services.

IDEA’s public site is the door. The statute and 34 C.F.R. Part 300 are the house. This page is a hallway map, not a lease.

A **related service** is transportation and such developmental, corrective, and other supportive services as are required to assist a child with a disability to benefit from special education. Speech-language pathology is on the federal list. So are audiology, interpreting, psychological services, physical and occupational therapy, and others. The list is not a buffet a parent orders from a blog.

Two structures matter for SLPs. Sometimes speech-language impairment is the **primary disability category** (essay 17). Sometimes the primary category is something else and speech-language is written as a related service. ASHA’s caseload definition counts both, plus 504 and, in some districts, MTSS. If your data system only prints the first structure, your integer is a different object from the federal one.

## What the IEP has to carry

Federal IEP contents include present levels of academic achievement and functional performance, measurable annual goals, and a description of how progress will be measured and reported. Special education, related services, supplementary aids and services, and program modifications are stated so the team — and a later reader — know what FAPE was supposed to look like this year.

*Endrew F. v. Douglas County* (2017) added a sentence the Department of Education later put in a Q&A: the IEP must be reasonably calculated to enable the child to make progress appropriate in light of the child’s circumstances. The Court rejected “merely more than de minimis.” It did not print a minute count. It did not name speech. It named the program.

ED’s 2017 Q&A told teams to take present levels, goals, and progress measurement seriously enough that the standard can be met for each child. That is why essay 22 cares about a teacher-readable present level and essay 29 cares about progress that is not a secret.

## What “related” does not mean

It does not mean optional. If the team determines the service is required for FAPE, it is required.

It does not mean “less than special education.” Related services can be the difference between access and a seat.

It does not mean the SLP works only on a medical problem that happens to occur at school. Educational relevance (essay 18) is the filter. A clinical finding that does not affect the educational program may belong in a clinic, a 504 conversation, or a “we considered and declined” note — not in a blog diagnosis.

It does not mean the parent consented to Medicaid billing. Consent to evaluate, consent to initial services, and consent to access public benefits are different signatures (essay 32). Do not flatten them.

## Timelines are floors

Initial evaluation timelines and IEP-meeting timelines are federal floors. States may be tighter. This pack will not print a number that might be wrong in your state. Open your state’s special education regulations. Essay 20 stays with the *idea* of a clock without promising eligibility at the end of it.

## LRE is a placement principle, not a closet preference

To the maximum extent appropriate, children with disabilities are educated with children who are not disabled. Removal happens when education in regular classes with supplementary aids and services cannot be achieved satisfactorily. Pull-out speech can be that removal for a slice of the day. It has to be justified as a service location, not as a habit (essay 24).

## How this page is used in a caseload fight

A related-service student is still a student on the caseload. A ratio law that only counts primary SLI will under-count the week (essay 05). A vacancy that “only” hits related-service minutes still hits FAPE (essay 08). A make-up policy that treats related services as the first thing to cancel has made a legal judgment it may not have the authority to make (essay 07).

You can print IDEA’s related-services definition and the *Endrew F.* Q&A cover page and take both to a meeting. You can refuse to say a particular child is eligible. You can refuse a protocol.

The statute is a floor. The IEP is the room. The blog is neither.
