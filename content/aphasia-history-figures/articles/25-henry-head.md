This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Henry Head was born in 1861 and died in 1940. Before the war that made his aphasia book, he had already made himself a subject: the collaboration with W. H. R. Rivers on the nerves of his own arm, the notes on sensation that made him famous in a different corridor. After 1914 he sat with wounded men whose language had been torn by metal. *Aphasia and Kindred Disorders of Speech* (Cambridge University Press, 1926) is two volumes of a person in a chair.

He sorted patients into verbal, syntactic, nominal, and semantic groups that later batteries did not keep. He attacked the “diagram-makers” by temperament as much as by evidence. He wrote the 1908 Paris debate into English as a story of men who could not agree on the clinical features and therefore could not agree on the anatomy.

## Presence and its limit

The 1926 book is one of the few classics a clinician can still read as if a human wrote it. That is the method. It is also the limit. Presence does not scale to a Minneapolis caseload. Tests would come from people who had less time and more need to compare Tuesday to November. Head would have disliked some of those people. Some of those people still needed him.

He was not always fair to Lichtheim. He was not always clear. His categories leaked. Later cognitive work would reuse “semantic” and mean something he did not. That is ordinary. The usable Head is the man who would not let a sketch finish the visit.

He wrote as a knighted London physician, with the confidence of Queen Square. Working-class patients enter as cases. Their return to a dock or a shop rarely does. British speech therapy’s women are not his footnotes. Keep the class of the voice in the vita.

## The arm and the war

Head had already made himself a subject — the Rivers collaboration, the nerves of his own arm — before he sat with wounded men. The 1926 book’s presence is a method learned on himself and then spent on others. Presence does not scale to Minneapolis. Tests came from people he would have disliked. Some of those people still needed the 1926 refusal to let a sketch finish the visit.

His categories leaked. Later cognitive work reused “semantic” and meant something else. Ordinary. The usable Head is the man who wrote Paris 1908 as a failure to agree on the picture. The class of the voice is also usable: knighted London, cases from below.


Cambridge printed two volumes in 1926 because Head would not compress the chairs into a table. Compression came later, from other people, for other caseloads. Both facts can be respected. The wounded men are not a style. They are why the book exists. The diagram-makers are not cartoon villains. They are why he had something to refuse.
