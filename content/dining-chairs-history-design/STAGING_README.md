# Staged for BBF SEO — dining-chairs-history-design

**Status:** staged only. Every article is `status: draft`, `voice_check: edited` (editor pass 2026-09-14; see `EDITOR_REPORT.md`). Do not set WordPress publish dates. Do not paste these files onto a live domain until a photograph plan exists.

## What this pack is

Forty-six magazine essays on dining chairs: the history of the form, and the sitting that dinner actually asks for. They are a chair-pillar research spine for Bradley Brand / BBF. They teach forms, joints, seats, measurements, labor, and rooms. They do not sell SKUs.

Sibling: `content/american-dining-room-history/` is the room-and-table spine. This pack does not rewrite that splat-to-ladder survey, the Hitchcock mill essay, the Eames/Saarinen table essay, or the IKEA table essay. It goes into the chair and into the body at the meal.

## SEO rules

- One slug owns one `primary_keyword`.
- `meta_description` is a search snippet, not a slogan.
- `seo_intent` is informational.
- Cross-link siblings inside this pack and, where honest, the dining-room pack. Do not cannibalize `dining-chairs-splat-to-ladder` with a second “history of the dining chair” lede. The opener here is the chair at dinner, not the room.
- No therapy, supplements, AI, COSMOS, figs, or office-task-chair funnels.
- Do not treat BIFMA computer-use numbers as dining law.

## Brand

Bradley Brand may appear where Arkansas or southern hardwoods are the subject (`arkansas-oak-side-chair`, and a sentence in woods or shop hours). No product names. No commission CTA in the body.

## Publish posture

Furniture-history cadence is slow: one URL a month unless a piece is already photographed. This pack is the research spine, not a flood. Ergonomics pieces wait on shop measurements and a table in the photograph, not on a stock “family dinner” image.
