# Claims Guardrails — Stuttering & Fluency History Pack

Staged. Educational history only.

## Hard stops

- No treatment protocols, home programs, or “try this if your child stutters…” language.
- No living-patient stories, even anonymized composites.
- No invented quotations.
- No generated faces; no stock “smiling child with therapist” on an 1841 or 1939 essay.
- No claim that SLPWOW or WOW Therapies invented, owns, or uniquely practices a historical method.
- No “gold-standard treatment,” “cure,” or “stuttering solved” language as endorsement. Published method names are historical objects. Commercial book titles may be quoted as titles.
- No DIY delayed auditory feedback, no home metronome program, no parent-administered Lidcombe how-to.

## Historical subjects who were not public figures by choice

The twenty-two children in Mary Tudor’s 1939 Iowa orphanage thesis are already in the public scientific and legal record. Name the *study* and the *setting*. Do not invent the children’s inner lives. Do not reprint a full roster of their names as entertainment. The 2001 newspaper series and the 2007 settlement are public facts; the wards are not a magazine feature.

Demosthenes is a public ancient figure. The pebble story is later anecdote (Plutarch and the tradition after him). Say so. Do not write it as a clinic demonstration.

## Living figures

Ehud Yairi, C. Woodruff Starkweather, Barry Guitar, Mark Onslow, Ronald L. Webster, and Gavin Andrews (and any other living subject) are treated as public scientific figures. Stick to published books, tests, papers, and institutional pages. No private biography. Portraits: hunt only; not public domain.

## Uncertainty

If a death year, first-edition year, or “who coined it” is contested, the article says so. A missing date in `INDEX.md` is better than a guessed one.

Examples already in this pack:

- Joseph Sheehan’s death is 13 November 1983 in the UCLA faculty memoir and 14 November 1983 in UPI and Contemporary Authors. The profile states both.
- Dean E. Williams’s birth year 1924 appears in FamilySearch, not in the Conture remembrance used for the 31 July 1994 death. The profile flags the difference.
- Ehud Yairi’s birth year was not independently confirmed from a CV header or necrology at pack date. Use “living; birth year not found.”

## Relationship to the profession pack

This series may retell Iowa, Johnson, Van Riper, and Travis because they are fluency’s public events. It must not copy the sibling pack’s sentences. New scene, new residue, deeper argument.

## Words that smuggle a cure

*Balbuties*, *psellismus*, *stammering*, *stuttering*, *dysphemia*, *spasmophemia* — each word carries a theory. Do not collapse them into “they didn’t have a word yet.” Do not use *speech impediment* as the house noun for a person. Historical sources may. We do not, except inside a quotation.

British *stammer* and American *stutter* are regional names for a related public fact. Use the word the source used; then say so once.

## Eugenics, race science, orphanages, knives

Hunt’s anthropology, Dieffenbach’s tongue, Johnson’s orphanage design, and the later commercial “solved” books belong in the record. Do not sanitize. Do not sermonize. Put the primary act on the page, then the later judgment.
