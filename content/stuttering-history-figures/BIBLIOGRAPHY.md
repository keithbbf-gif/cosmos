# Bibliography — Stuttering & Fluency History Pack

Sources consulted for staged copy, 14 September 2026. Prefer the primary paper over a textbook paraphrase.

## Primary and near-primary

- Plutarch. *Life of Demosthenes.* (pebble and seashore anecdotes — later tradition, not a medical note.)
- Itard, J.-M. G. Reports and notes on *bégaiement* in the Paris medical press of the 1810s–1820s; later Académie review context for Colombat (1831 edition front matter).
- Colombat de l’Isère, M. *Du bégaiement et de tous les autres vices de la parole traités par de nouvelles méthodes.* Paris: Mansut fils, 1830; 2nd ed. 1831.
- Colombat de l’Isère, M. *Traité de tous les vices de la parole et en particulier du bégaiement.* Paris: Béchet et Labé, 1840.
- Dieffenbach, J. F. *Die Heilung des Stotterns durch eine neue chirurgische Operation.* Berlin: Albert Förstner, 1841. French letter to the Institut de France, same year.
- Hunt, J. *Stammering and Stuttering, Their Nature and Treatment.* London, 1861 (and later printings).
- Bluemel, C. S. *Stammering and Cognate Defects of Speech.* New York: G. E. Stechert, 1913.
- Bluemel, C. S. *The Riddle of Stuttering.* Danville, Ill.: Interstate, 1957.
- Travis, L. E. *Speech Pathology.* New York: D. Appleton, 1931.
- Tudor, M. “An Experimental Study of the Effect of Evaluative Labeling upon Speech Fluency.” M.A. thesis, University of Iowa, 1939.
- Van Riper, C. *Speech Correction: Principles and Methods.* New York: Prentice-Hall, 1939. (later editions through the textbook century.)
- Johnson, W. *People in Quandaries.* New York: Harper, 1946.
- Johnson, W., and associates. *The Onset of Stuttering.* Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1959.
- Sheehan, J. G. “Theory and treatment of stuttering as an approach-avoidance conflict.” *Journal of Psychology* 36 (1953): 27–49.
- Sheehan, J. G. *Stuttering: Research and Therapy.* New York: Harper & Row, 1970.
- Wingate, M. E. “A Standard Definition of Stuttering.” *Journal of Speech and Hearing Disorders* 29 (1964): 484–489.
- Andrews, G., and M. Harris, et al. *The Syndrome of Stuttering.* London: Spastics Society / Heinemann, 1964.
- Goldiamond, I. “Stuttering and fluency as manipulable operant response classes.” In L. Krasner and L. P. Ullmann, eds., *Research in Behavior Modification.* New York: Holt, Rinehart and Winston, 1965.
- Bloodstein, O. *A Handbook on Stuttering.* First ed. 1969; later editions through the sixth (2008, with N. Bernstein Ratner) and after.
- Perkins, W. H., J. Rudas, L. Johnson, and J. Bell. “Stuttering: Discoordination of Phonation with Articulation and Respiration.” *Journal of Speech and Hearing Research* 19 (1976): 509–522.
- Howie, P. M. “Concordance for stuttering in monozygotic and dizygotic twin pairs.” *Journal of Speech and Hearing Research* 24 (1981): 317–321.
- Boberg, E., and D. Kully. *Comprehensive Stuttering Program.* San Diego: College-Hill, 1985.
- Onslow, M., L. Costa, and S. Rue. “Speech outcomes of early intervention for stuttering.” Early Lidcombe-era papers from 1990 onward; later RCTs in the 2000s.
- Fox, P. T., R. J. Ingham, J. C. Ingham, et al. “A PET study of the neural systems of stuttering.” *Nature* 382 (1996): 158–161.
- Yairi, E., and N. G. Ambrose. *Early Childhood Stuttering.* Austin: Pro-Ed, 2005.
- World Health Organization. *International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health.* Geneva: WHO, 2001.
