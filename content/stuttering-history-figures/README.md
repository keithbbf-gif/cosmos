# SLPWOW — Stuttering & Fluency History & Major Figures (staged)

Magazine series for a later import to **SLPWOW.com**. This folder is the pack. It is not a live-site edit, not COSMOS core, and not a merge with the general speech-pathology history pack or the aphasia deep series.

**Start here:** [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md) (calendar + roster). Voice: [`STYLE_GUIDE.md`](STYLE_GUIDE.md). Claims: [`CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`](CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md). Portraits: [`PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`](PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md) (notes only). WordPress: [`WP_IMPORT.md`](WP_IMPORT.md).

Forty-five Markdown drafts live in `articles/` (16 era essays, 29 profiles). **Five** cleared public-domain or open-license portraits ship in `assets/portraits/`; the rest stay placeholder-only until `RIGHTS.md` gains a row.

## Portrait policy

Never generate or embed synthetic historical faces. Cleared portraits are Commons / BIU Santé / museum open-access files logged in `RIGHTS.md`. Profiles with a cleared file use a lead `<figure>` with SEO `figcaption` (see `PHOTO_NOTES.md`). Until a row exists in `RIGHTS.md`, profiles carry the placeholder block in `STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

## Sibling packs

- `content/slpwow-speech-pathology-history/` — the profession (ASHA, Iowa as a school, the textbook century). Johnson and Van Riper appear there as profession ancestors. This pack is the **fluency** deep series: knives, orphanages, icebergs, parent chairs. New scene, new residue. Do not copy those sentences.
- `content/aphasia-history-figures/` — lesions and wards. Do not merge the trees. Cross-link after import if a reader needs Broca for a different reason.

## What this pack will not do

- No merge into furniture, figroots, COSMOS modules, or clinic booking pages.
- No clinical protocols as advice. No “try this if your child…”
- No live `slpwow.com` or WOW Therapies CMS writes from this folder.
- No generated historical faces.
