This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Hermann Gutzmann Sr. (1865–1922) lectured in Berlin as if speech were a medical organ system. *Phoniatrie* is his family’s word as much as anyone’s: the father Albert had already treated voice and speech; the son made an academic trade of it. Stuttering sat in that trade beside cleft, voice, and the deaf child’s articulation. The clinic was a hospital room, not a drawing-room. The pupil was a patient.

Vienna, a generation later, gave the work another name. Emil Fröschels (1884–1972) used *Logopädie* and helped found, in 1924, the international association now remembered as IALP. He also published a chewing method for stuttering — *Kaumethode* — that later American textbooks reduced to a cartoon of people pretending to chew while they talked. The cartoon is lazy. The historical fact is a Viennese physician trying to break a feared word by changing the act of speaking into something too ordinary to guard.

## What the decades thought they were solving

Berlin thought it was solving a neurology of the speaking apparatus. Gutzmann wrote as a physician among physicians. Stuttering was a *Sprachstörung* with a chart. The charts are dated. The permission is not: a stammer can be examined without first being a joke.

Vienna thought it was solving a whole person who spoke. Fröschels moved among psychiatry, voice, and the new therapy trade. The chewing papers are one tool. The larger move is to treat fluency as a learned struggle, not a short tongue. American visitors took notes. After 1938 they would not have to travel. The notes came to them in exile.

## 1924 and 1938

1924 is a congress year. Logopedics gets an international noun. The sibling profession pack tells that story as the birth of a field. This pack tells it as a fluency address. A stammerer in Vienna in 1924 could be sent to a named specialty. A stammerer in many American towns could still be sent to an elocutionist or a surgeon leftover.

1938 is an emptying. Fröschels was Jewish. He left. The association he had helped to build had to live with the fact that its city had become unsafe for him. American speech correction received European talent as a refugee fact, not as a faculty exchange. A history that prints only the chewing method and not the exit visa is a demonstration tape.

## Who was left out

Women built the daily logopedic work and rarely got the chair. Patients who could not pay for a university clinic used folk methods or silence. The Nazi years did not only exile Jewish physicians. They also applied eugenic law to disabled people, including people whose speech marked them. This pack will not invent a particular murdered stammerer to decorate the paragraph. The legal climate is documented. The missing names are the point.

## Residue

A modern European clinic that says *Logopädie* or *Phoniatrie* is still walking around in these decades’ clothes. The American clinic says *fluency* and *SLP* and pretends the clothes were always Midwestern. They were not. Gutzmann’s chart and Fröschels’s chew and the 1938 suitcase are part of why a stuttering evaluation in 2026 can look like medicine and like teaching at once.

The chewing method is not a homework assignment in this series. It is a dated attempt to make speech too dull to fear. Later clinicians kept the fear analysis and put the chew down. That is how a craft is supposed to behave.
