This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Mark Onslow’s name is on the Lidcombe Program the way a city is on a train: the work happened at a health-service address in Lidcombe, New South Wales, and then at the Australian Stuttering Research Centre. Early outcome papers with colleagues including Louise Costa and Sandhya Rue appear around 1990. Randomized trials in the 2000s made the argument international. Ann Packman is a co-author, not a helper. This profile will not teach the program. Training exists for people who intend to use it. A magazine that extracts verbal contingencies and severity ratings is a pirate manual.

Living scholar. Published papers, books, and institutional pages only. No private biography. He has written as a researcher of stuttering treatment, not as SLPWOW’s clinician and not as a household brand.

## The argument

Can you treat a preschooler early without becoming Mary Tudor? Lidcombe’s published answer is: treat the speech with a parent as daily clinician, measure, maintain, put the work through trials. Critics’ answer is: natural recovery (Yairi, Ambrose) complicates every success story, and a contingency on a toddler’s mouth is still a contingency. Both answers belong in the same library. Curlee asked the design question from Arizona. Onslow answered with Australian trials. The conversation is not a morality play.

Operant years (essay 11) are the family resemblance. Rustin’s London centre is the cousin who is not the same child. Do not file Palin under Lidcombe.

## What later people kept

A fidelity culture. A parent in the chair as a named role. What they abused: worksheets downloaded from unlicensed blogs. What they still argue about: age, dose, who recovers anyway, what the child learns about a mouth that is always being rated.

Living subject. No guessed dates. No generated face. No how-to.

The Australian Stuttering Research Centre is the later institutional name a student meets in a citation. Lidcombe the suburb is the earlier geography. Keep both. A program named for a place can be mistaken for a franchise. It is a research-and-training culture with papers, trials, and people who will tell you that a downloaded worksheet is not the program. SLPWOW will not host the worksheet. It will host this history: a parent in a chair, a trial in a journal, an argument with Illinois recovery numbers that has not ended, and a hard stop at the line where a magazine becomes a manual.
