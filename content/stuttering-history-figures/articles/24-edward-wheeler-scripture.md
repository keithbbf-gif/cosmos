Edward Wheeler Scripture wanted a number where a master had wanted a gentleman. Yale’s psychological laboratory, in the 1890s and after, was one of the rooms where American experimental psychology learned to time and record. Scripture put speech into that room. Stuttering was one of the speech problems that could be made to leave a trace on smoked paper and, later, on film.

He was born in 1864 and died in 1945. The sibling profession pack already warns that his stations after about 1915 conflict in secondary lists. This profile will not invent a tidy itinerary. He worked in the United States, including a speech clinic associated with Vanderbilt at Columbia, and later in London. The London years matter here: a laboratory man running a stuttering practice in the city of Hunt’s ghost.

## The job that mattered

Measurement as a moral. If you can record a block, you do not have to take the elocutionist’s word. Scripture wrote on phonetics, on the speech of people with neurologic disease, and on stammering. The books are mixed in quality and mixed in kindness. Early experimentalists were not automatically humane. They were automatically sure that a device beat a vibe.

American speech correction needed him and then outgrew his particular devices. Iowa would make a louder laboratory. Van Riper would make a louder clinic. Scripture is the hinge from the drawing-room to the smoked drum.

## What later people kept

The permission to record. The suspicion that a master’s testimonial is not data. What they discarded: some of the neurology, some of the personality talk, some of the London clinic’s certainty. Career dates after 1915 should be footnoted in any later encyclopedia entry; this magazine will only say that the lists disagree.

He is not an SLP. He is a psychologist who treated speech as an experiment. The experiment is the residue.

London after the American years is easy to skip in a U.S. syllabus. Do not skip it. Hunt’s city received a man with a laboratory habit and a clinic sign. The combination is the twentieth century in miniature: measurement plus a paying stammerer, still not a profession with a certificate, already not a drawing-room. The smoked drum and the fee shared a week.

The sibling pack has a Scripture profile for the profession. This one is the fluency hinge: Hunt’s city, Yale’s drum, a stammer as a trace. New sentences.
