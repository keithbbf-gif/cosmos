This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Hugo Harris Gregory Jr. spent a career at Northwestern University trying to end a civil war. Modification versus fluency shaping had become a loyalty test. Gregory said, in public and in workshops from 1985 to 2001, that a clinician could evaluate a speaker and borrow from both benches. The *Daily Northwestern* obituary (8 November 2004) called him one of the first to advocate merging the two main approaches. That is a colleague’s sentence, not a randomized trial. It is why he is in this pack.

He was born 11 July 1928 in Texarkana, Texas, grew up in Portland, Arkansas, and stuttered badly enough that his parents sent him by train, at fourteen, to a summer camp for stutterers in Bristol, Rhode Island, for three summers. The Ashley County Ledger obituary text is the warrant for that geography. University of Arkansas for two years; then three Northwestern degrees, Ph.D. 1959. He met Carolyn Booth, a speech pathologist, and they married into a lifelong clinical partnership. Faculty at Northwestern 1962–1993; emeritus after. He died 11 October 2004 at Johns Hopkins Hospital of a stroke and meningitis after surgery. He was seventy-six.

## The work

A 1969 assessment of therapy results (the ERIC document still circulates). Later *Stuttering Therapy: Rationale and Procedures*, with Carolyn Gregory, June H. Campbell, and Diane G. Hill. International workshops; an IFA presence; a 1989 World Congress session on relapse with Carolyn. Fifteen doctoral dissertations, the campus paper said. The workshop, not the hemisphere, is the residue.

Portland, Arkansas, is not a trivia tag. SLPWOW’s household knows that part of the map. Gregory left the state to become a Northwestern name and did not pretend the train had been a protocol.

## What later people kept

Permission to stop choosing a church. Guitar’s later “integrated” textbook is a cousin, not a copy. What they discarded: the idea that a merger is easy. Relapse, which Gregory was willing to put on a congress program, is the part of the merger that still fails in month fourteen.

He stuttered and did not make the childhood the whole vita. The camp is in the obituary because it explains the vocation. It is not a warrant for sending a 2026 fourteen-year-old to a copy of 1942. Carolyn Gregory’s name on the later book is the other half of the workshop. A vita that files her as “his wife” is doing the 1950s in a 2004 sentence. She was a clinician-author. The marriage is a fact. The authorship is the other fact.
