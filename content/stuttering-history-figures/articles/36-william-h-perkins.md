This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

William H. Perkins ran a stuttering clinic at the University of Southern California and spent the middle of his career trying to make fluency a coordination problem: phonation, articulation, respiration. The 1976 *Journal of Speech and Hearing Research* paper with Joanna Rudas, Linda Johnson, and Jody Bell is the laboratory object — voiced, whispered, silent articulation, and a claim that complexity of those pairings tracks the block. This page will not reconstruct the conditions as a home experiment.

USC Annenberg’s notice says he died 6 October 2008 from complications of a fall in Baldwin Hills, California, age eighty-five. Age eighty-five in October 2008 implies a birth year around 1923. This article has not found a separate birth announcement and will not invent a day. Distinguished emeritus, 1991; speech pathology and otolaryngology in the Keck School, speech communication in Annenberg. Richard Curlee took master’s and doctoral degrees from USC in the 1960s; the memorialists later tied that training to treatment-research design.

## The turn

Perkins also wrote, in the 1980s and after, about failure. “Learning from negative outcomes in stuttering therapy” is not a title a hotel seminar buys. He became willing to say that impressive first weeks were not lives. Later papers on the “lost voice” of the person who stutters — the inner experience the graph cannot hear — sit in the same vita as the 1976 coordination claim. A man who had believed in a mechanism allowed the speaker back in.

That turn is why he is not only a fluency-shaping ancestor. He is also a critic of his own shop.

## What later people kept

A serious attempt to name a motor coordination. A public honesty about relapse. What they discarded: any single-mechanism promise. What they still argue about: whether the whisper studies say what the 1976 abstract said.

Call him Dr. Perkins. Do not assign whispered homework. Do not use the Baldwin Hills fall as color beyond the death fact.

USC’s stuttering clinic, in his years, sat inside a medical-school and communication-school knot that later universities would call interdisciplinary and then forget to fund. Curlee carried the design habit to Arizona. The habit is the lineage, not a whispered condition. A magazine that reduces Perkins to 1976 is stealing the later essays in which he admitted the first week had lied.
