This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

The 1831 title page is crowded. *Du bégaiement et de tous les autres vices de la parole*, second edition, Paris, Mansut, with a report to the Académie de médecine. Marc Colombat de l’Isère had opened an Institut orthophonique the year before. He wanted stammering to be a medical house, not a parlor trick and not, yet, a surgical fashion.

He was born in 1797, took the territorial name de l’Isère, and died in Paris on 10 June 1851. He wrote on women’s surgery, on instruments, on ventriloquism, and repeatedly on speech. The stammering books are the reason he is in this pack. The rest of the catalog is a reminder that a nineteenth-century medical entrepreneur did not have a single specialty board.

## The house and the books

1830: the institute and the first *Du bégaiement*. 1831: the second edition, with Itard, Esquirol, and Hervez de Chégoin on the report. 1840: *Traité de tous les vices de la parole et en particulier du bégaiement* (Béchet et Labé). Later printings and a German translation made him a European name. Later advertisements inflated an 1833 prize from the Académie des sciences. The books are checkable. The prize-as-miracle is a sales object. Say both.

His method, as later textbooks summarize it, leaned on rhythm, a measured attack on consonants (the *muta-consonne* nickname), and a practiced breathing that did not require a wedge from the tongue. This profile will not reconstruct the drills. It will say that Colombat offered a non-operative product in the same market Dieffenbach would enter with steel in 1841. Colombat’s 1844 reply to Becquerel is the sound of that market arguing.

## What later people kept

The word *orthophonie*, which Belgium and France would later professionalize into something Colombat would not recognize. The habit of a private address for fluency. The idea that cadence can change a block — an idea that will be rediscovered with metronomes, with delayed feedback, and with prolonged speech. Rediscovery is not lineage. Do not draw a straight line from 1830 to Lidcombe.

What had to be put down: “vice of speech” as a moral noun. The paying-pupil model as the whole of the science. The inflated prize.

He is not an SLP. He is a physician-author with a house. The house is the residue. So is the 1831 title page, which a later graphics pass may photograph under a Public Domain Mark (Wellcome has held copies). Do not pirate a modern reprint’s typesetting if the file is not clearly free.
