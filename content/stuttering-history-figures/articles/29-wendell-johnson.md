This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

The theory had a clean sentence: stuttering begins when a listener puts a bad name on an ordinary bump. Wendell Johnson spent a career making that sentence sound like kindness, and in 1939 the sentence walked into an orphanage. The sibling profession pack has the ASHA presidency (1950), the journal editorship, the honors, *People in Quandaries* (1946). This profile keeps the cause and the thesis he advised.

He was born 16 August 1906 in Roxbury, Kansas. He came to Iowa City in 1926 to study English, already stuttering, and stayed as a psychologist and speech pathologist until a heart attack in 1955 stripped titles and a second crisis killed him on 29 August 1965, age fifty-nine.

B.A. 1928, M.A. 1929, Ph.D. 1931, all Iowa, Travis advising on *The Influence of Stuttering on the Personality*. Full professor and clinic director in 1943. The vita is a single campus. That is not a small thing. It is also a conflict of interest dressed as loyalty.

## The theory and the porch

Diagnosogenic theory said stuttering begins when a listener applies a bad name to ordinary disfluency. It is a humane idea if you meet it as a warning about panic. It is a dangerous idea if you meet it as a license to test labeling on children who cannot leave. Mary Tudor’s 1939 master’s thesis at the Davenport orphans’ home is that license used. Johnson advised. The 2001 newspaper series and the 2007 settlement are public facts. This profile will not reprint the children’s names. It will not let the semantics book wash the porch.

*The Onset of Stuttering* (1959), with associates, is the later, thicker attempt to make the listener’s adjective a research object without another orphanage. Yairi and Ambrose’s Illinois work would later argue that early stuttering is not only a parental hallucination. Both can be in the library. Only one of them used a captive ward.

## What later people kept

A suspicion of casual labeling. A general-semantics habit of noticing what words do. A clinic culture in which people who stutter become scientists. What they had to apologize for: 1939. What they discarded, partly: the claim that the mouth is innocent until the adjective arrives.

The sibling pack’s Johnson profile opens on the Kansas-to-Iowa arrival. This one opens on the theory and the home. The man is the same. The sentences are not copied.
