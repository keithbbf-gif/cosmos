This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Barry Guitar’s *Stuttering: An Integrated Approach to Its Nature and Treatment* is the late-century undergraduate object. Lippincott and later Wolters Kluwer kept reprinting it; editions stacked; students underlined the chapters that tried to put Van Riper’s furniture and the shaping machines in one house. Hugo Gregory had already said the war was optional. Guitar’s book made the option a syllabus. That is influence. It is not a peace treaty signed by speakers.

This profile treats a living scholar at the University of Vermont. Published textbooks, papers, and institutional pages only. No private biography. He has written, in public professional contexts, as a person who stutters; this article will not embroider that remark into a childhood. Vermont’s fluency work — including later collaborations and a clinic culture students remember — is the address. ASHA Fellow and other honors sit on the usual vita pages; this magazine does not need the full medal list.

## The job that mattered

A readable synthesis. Bloodstein’s handbook is the reference shelf. Guitar’s book is the course. Between them a student can meet onset data, theories, and two treatment families without joining a church. The risk of a synthesis is smoothness. Stuttering is not smooth. The better editions keep the arguments visible: recovery numbers that embarrass early bragging, shame that embarrasses a clean count, devices that embarrass a craft.

Do not extract chapter exercises as a parent blog. The book is for sale from a publisher. SLPWOW is not a pirate.

## What later people kept

The word *integrated* as a respectable adjective. A generation of clinicians who can name both benches. What they still argue about: whether integration is a third method or a refusal to choose. Gregory’s workshops are the ancestor. Guitar’s boards are the distribution system.

Living subject. No guessed dates. No generated face. No protocol.

Vermont is a small-state address with an outsized syllabus. Students who never saw Iowa or Hollins still met both in Guitar’s chapters. That distribution is a historical event: the civil war became a course unit. A course unit can flatten a speaker. It can also stop a new clinician from joining a church in week three of the semester. The better teaching keeps the first-week tape and the fourteenth month in the same lecture. The worse teaching prints “integrated” on a slide and then assigns only one bench’s homework. This magazine is not that homework. It is a note that the table of contents had a politics.
