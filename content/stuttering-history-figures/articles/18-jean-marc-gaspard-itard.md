This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

He is in every disability history for a boy found in the Aveyron woods. He is in this pack for a thinner reason: Paris asked a serious physician to look at *bégaiement*, and Itard’s name later sat on the 1831 Académie de médecine report printed with Colombat’s second edition. That is not a founding. It is a permission. Stammering could be a medical object without first being a joke.

Jean-Marc Gaspard Itard was born 24 April 1774 at Oraison in Provence and died 5 July 1838 in Paris. He trained as a physician, worked with the abbé Sicard’s deaf students, and became the teacher-clinician of Victor. The wild-boy books are why the face is still engraved. The stammering notes are why a fluency magazine borrows the engraving.

## The job that mattered

Itard believed some speech and hearing problems could be described, trained, and at least partly moved. He also believed some could not. Victor’s story is a record of both beliefs. Later oralist histories over-claim him; later anti-oralist histories under-claim the patience. This pack will not retry Victor. It will say that a man who had already sat with a child who had no conventional language was a reasonable person to ask about a child who had language and could not start it.

The Académie report in Colombat 1831 is a committee object (Itard, Esquirol, Hervez de Chégoin). Do not make Itard the author of Colombat’s method. Do not make Colombat Itard’s student. Paris was a small town of letters. Names share title pages.

## What fluency actually inherited

A habit of looking. Not a protocol. Not a pebble. Not a knife. Looking is easy to romanticize and hard to do when the paying father wants a miracle. Itard’s larger career includes the medicalization of deaf education, which later Deaf history has had to argue with. Keep that argument in the vita. A fluency profile that prints only kindness is doing public relations.

He is not an SLP. The credential does not exist. He is a physician who wrote. The writing is the inheritance.
