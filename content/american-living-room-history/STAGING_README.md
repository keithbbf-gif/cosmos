Forty-four magazine essays on American living-room furniture from about 1650 to the present. They are the history-pillar research spine for Bradley Brand / BBF. They teach forms, shops, woods, labor, and rooms. They do not sell SKUs.

This pack is the living-room sibling of `content/american-dining-room-history/`. Do not merge the two into one hub page.

## SEO rules

- One slug owns one `primary_keyword`.
- `meta_description` is a search snippet, not a slogan.
- `seo_intent` is informational unless a later editor changes it.
- Cross-link siblings. Do not cannibalize `parlor-becomes-living-room` with a second “history of the living room” lede.
- History hub month (pillar map): 2027-05. Factory-vs-one-off: 2027-09. Supporting slugs wait.
- No therapy, supplements, AI, or COSMOS copy.

## Brand

Bradley Brand may appear where Arkansas or southern hardwoods are the subject (`arkansas-southern-hardwood-living`, and a sentence in vernacular or woods hours). No product names. No commission CTA in the body.
