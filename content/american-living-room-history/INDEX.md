# American Living Room Furniture, 1650–Now

A series for buyers, designers, restorers, and anyone who inherited a sofa that does not match the room. One form, shop, region, or hour per article. History is footed to named work. Gaps are marked `[CITE NEEDED]`.

Bradley Brand works Arkansas hardwoods. These drafts teach how American living-room furniture was made and used. They do not sell sofas.
