E. V. McCollum had a nose for the thing that was not protein.

At Wisconsin, then later at Johns Hopkins, he and Marguerite Davis ran rats on diets that looked adequate if you trusted the nineteenth century. When they used certain fats, the animals grew. When they used others, they did not. Butterfat carried something lard did not. Egg yolk carried it. The factor dissolved with the fat. They called it fat-soluble A. The water-soluble leftover of the old "vitamine" fight became B. The alphabet had a landlord.

Osborne and Mendel, in New Haven, were on the same scent at the same time. Simultaneous discovery is the rule in this field, not the exception. The polite histories split the credit. The impolite ones pick a campus. What matters for the commercial story is the *split itself*: once A is fat and B is water, you can fractionate. Fractionation is how a packing plant, a cod-liver oil house, and later a solvent extractor all get into the vitamin business without calling themselves doctors.

Fat-soluble A would later come apart in the hands of other people. The eye-and-skin factor and the bone factor were not the same passenger. D gets its own letter and its own lamp. A keeps the butter and the carrot (or rather the carotene that animals can cut down). If you flatten that into "vitamin A from carrots," you have a children's book. The children's book is not false, but it hides the years when A was a bucket.

McCollum was a public explainer as well as a rat man. He wrote for audiences that wanted rules. Rules travel faster than fractions. By the 1920s an American shopper could meet "A" on a food advertisement that had never seen the inside of his animal room. That is the leak from bench to copy, and it starts early.

I am staying with the bench. Two fats. One rat colony. A letter assigned because "accessory factor that dissolves in ether" is a rotten thing to print on a crate. The letter is a filing tab. It is not a promise.

Lard and butter are still groceries. They are not protocols. McCollum's rats were arguing about an unidentified passenger in the cream. The passenger got a name before it got a structure. Karrer will draw the structure later. Commerce will not wait for Karrer.


Wisconsin's dairy weather is in the experiment. Butterfat as a hero reagent is a land-grant sentence. A plains campus will find the factor in cream before it finds it in a tropical oil. Geography writes the first fractions. Later, when A is a solvent extract from a drum, the cream will have become nostalgia. McCollum's rats were arguing in a dairy state. Keep the state in the caption.
