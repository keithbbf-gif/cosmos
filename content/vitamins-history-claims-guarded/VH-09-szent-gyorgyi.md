Albert Szent-Györgyi liked a good story about himself, and for once the story is better when you leave the jokes in.

He was after a reducing substance in the adrenal cortex. He found an acid he did not yet want to baptize as a vitamin. Hexuronic acid was the cautious name — six carbons, a sugar cousin. He got some from adrenals, some from oranges. The yields were miserable. Then, the part everyone retells: he is in Szeged, Hungary, the paprika capital, and he realizes the pepper on the dinner table is a warehouse. From paprika he pulls grams instead of milligrams. Grams change a career. Grams let you send samples to other people. Grams let Haworth in Birmingham chew on the structure.

In 1932 the identification landed: hexuronic acid was the antiscorbutic factor the guinea pigs had been voting for. Szent-Györgyi and King (in Pittsburgh) stepped on each other in the journals. Priority smoke. The substance did not care.

He took the 1937 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. Haworth shared the chemistry prize that same year for the carbohydrate work, including the ascorbic structure. A substance had become famous enough to split across two prize rooms.

What I want from this draft is the *texture* of isolation. Adrenals from slaughterhouses. Orange juice that fights you. A national vegetable that turns out to be a reagent. This is not a wellness kitchen. It is a man in a provincial university using the thing his town already grew.

Commerce is already leaning in the doorway. Once you can get grams, a firm can ask about kilos. Roche will not be far. Reichstein's process will make the paprika barrel optional. That substitution — factory sugar chemistry for a Hungarian pepper — is the commercial history in one move. Food as source, then food as memory, then a synthetic lot number.

Szent-Györgyi later became a public scientist of a more wandering kind, and people still try to recruit him into megadose arguments he does not belong in on this page. His 1930s work is a structure and a source. It is not a serving suggestion.

If you cook with paprika tonight, that is supper. It is not compliance with a protocol. The pepper's job in 1932 was to sit in a flask.


He sent samples. That is the unglamorous sentence under the paprika story. A factor that cannot be mailed is a local rumor. A factor that can be mailed becomes a structure in someone else's town. Haworth would not have had a year without grams in a jar. Roche would not have had a process to buy. The pepper is famous. The post is the machine.
