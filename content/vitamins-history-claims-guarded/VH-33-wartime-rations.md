War turns a crystal into a line item.

The first Recommended Dietary Allowances — 1941, Food and Nutrition Board — were not a drugstore poster. They were a planning tool. How much of which factor does a population need if you are feeding camps, ships, and factories. A number on a board is how a government buys. Once the Board publishes a number, a contractor can bid against it.

Roche, Merck, the British houses, the Japanese plants that still could run — the 1940s vitamin business is a procurement business. Ascorbic acid in a packet. B letters in a chocolate bar that does not want to melt. Cod-liver oil in a school. The objects are ordinary. The volumes are not.

I have read quartermaster specifications the way some people read menus. They are menus. They are also industrial policy. If the spec says a ration must contain so many milligrams of C, someone must make those milligrams. The factory that can sign the spec gets the check. After the war that factory still exists. It looks for a civilian aisle.

This is the hinge a lot of "wellness" history skips. The civilian multivitamin boom does not descend from a guru. It descends from surplus capacity and a public trained, by posters, to think in letters. Posters are claims in pictures. We will not reprint their promises. We will name their existence.

The RDA itself is a committee compromise. It is not a personal protocol. Later it becomes a percent on a label ("% DV"), which is a different political object. People fight about the number because the number looks like a verdict. It is a planning staple that leaked into marketing.

Soldiers are not testimonials. A man who ate a packet in a hole in 1944 is not a reason to sell you a gummy. The packet was a spec. The gummy is a later retail mood.

If this draft has a moral, it is bureaucratic. Letters became purchasable because a board wrote numbers and a war paid invoices. The invoices are the commercial history. The medals and the paprika were the prologue.


After 1945 the plants did not go back to being small. Civilian multivitamins are, in that sense, demobilized equipment. So are a lot of American foods. The Board's numbers stayed in print and learned to live on a new box. If you want a cleaner origin myth — a guru, a beach, a revelation — you can find one. It will be a later sticker on a wartime machine. Peel the sticker. The invoice is still under it.
