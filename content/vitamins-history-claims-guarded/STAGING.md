# Staging

These files are **staged**. That is a status, not a compliment.

| Stage | Meaning | Who moves it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `claims-guarded` / `status: staged` | Written, linted, sitting here. Not a label. Not an ad. Not live on a store. | Writer |
| human-revise | A person with the pen cuts, facts, and voice. | Human editor |
| counsel-look | Optional. Statute questions only. | Counsel, if asked |
| ready | Still not a product. May be published as *education*. | Human |
| refuse | Died on a claim, a false date, or a brochure mouth. | Any reviewer |

Promotion is a later act. Do not "just ship" a staged file onto packaging, Amazon copy, a landing page, or a script that tells someone to take a pill.

A draft can stay staged forever. That is cheaper than a warning letter.
