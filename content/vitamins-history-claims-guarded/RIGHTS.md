## Caption and claims rules

- Keep `<figure>`, descriptive `alt`, and `<figcaption>` together when embedding (see `staged/FIGURE_EMBEDS.md`).
- Captions describe **history and documents** — not product benefits, intakes, or disease outcomes.
- Do not crop out credit lines required by CC BY licenses.
- Do not upscale or “enhance” archive files with generative tools.

## Banned without a new row in this file

- AI portraits or “historical reenactment” faces.
- Stock scientists in lab coats implying brand staff.
- Supplement labels, Supplement Facts panels, or ad copy presented as editorial proof.
- Hotlinked museum URLs in production without a local file and rights row.
