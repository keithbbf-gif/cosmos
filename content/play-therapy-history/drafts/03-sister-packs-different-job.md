Three folders now sit in the same house, and they will steal from each other if nobody names the job. This page is a map of the theft we are refusing.

`content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` is the counseling and clinical-heritage calendar: Pinel to Berg, asylums to third-wave nicknames, Freud and Rogers and Linehan as full figures. It already has essays on Anna Freud, Melanie Klein, Donald Winnicott, and Carl Rogers. Those essays have to cover a life. They cannot also be a history of the playroom.

`content/play-therapy-history/` — this folder — is the play-technique, play-profession, play-object calendar. It starts when play is asked to do clinical work and stays there. It does not retell the Hampstead War Nurseries as a biography of Anna Freud. It does not retell Paddington Green as a biography of Winnicott. It takes the *play* out of those lives and writes the quarrel that the sister pack had to leave as a paragraph.

The SLPWOW speech-pathology lane is a third object. Van Riper, fluency, school SLP history, dysphagia: different profession, different license, different graphics. A child who stutters may end up in a playroom. That does not make Charles Van Riper a play therapist, and it does not make Axline a fluency researcher. Do not merge the calendars to save a URL.

## What this folder will borrow, and how

When I need Rogers, I will name the 1957 paper and send the reader to the sister slug `carl-rogers`. Axline was a student in that weather. The weather is not the playroom.

When I need Klein or Anna Freud, I will name the 1932 book and the 1927 technique book and send the reader to `melanie-klein` and `anna-freud` for the rest of the life. Here the question is narrower: could play stand in for an adult's free association, and who said no?

When I need Winnicott, I will stay with "Transitional Objects" (1953), the squiggle as a *named* practice he wrote about, and *Playing and Reality* (1971). The good-enough sentence already has a home next door. I will not steal it and stretch it into a parenting brand.

When I need Bowlby, I will mostly not. Attachment language leaked into Theraplay, into some filial rooms, and into late-century trauma play. That leak is a historical fact for later essays. The WHO monograph and the Strange Situation belong to the sister pack.

## What this folder will not do for the live site

WOW Therapies, as the homepage last presented itself, is a Southeast Arkansas practice talking about occupational, physical, and speech services. Hosting a play-therapy *history* does not quietly add a fourth clinical department. If the practice later offers a named child-mental-health service, that is a service-page decision with a license on the wall. It is not this folder's job to warm the oven.

The ACT / mindfulness pack — `content/act-mindfulness-therapy-history/` — is a cousin, not a parent. Linehan appears there as a mindfulness staffing pattern. She does not appear here. Sandplay is not MBSR. A cushion is not a tray.

CBT deepen (`content/cbt-history-deep/`) owns thought records and East 65th Street. A child CBT protocol that uses play as a delivery device is a different historical object from Axline's 1947 book. If a later writer wants that object, they can open a new folder. They should not smuggle a worksheet into this one.

## How to use the cross-links

Internal links in this pack should stay inside the forty-five slugs unless the sentence truly needs the sister figure. Then the link is "see also," not a paste. Do not import the Freud essay. Do not import the Van Riper essay. A hub page may list both calendars. A single URL should not pretend to be both.

Graphics must not reuse SLP oral-mechanism diagrams as "play" decoration, and must not reuse a Freud 1909 Halberstadt as if he ran a playroom. Period objects for this pack are title pages, PD toy catalogs, and type. See `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`.

## A practical test

If you can delete the word *play* from the essay and the essay still works as a general therapy-history piece, it belongs next door. If you can delete the word *child* and the essay becomes a sand-tray mysticism tract, it has failed the fence. If you can delete the claims box and nothing feels missing, the box was never doing its job.

This folder exists because the sister pack was honest about its own limits. A survey of psychotherapy cannot also be a history of APT dues. A history of APT dues cannot also be a life of Freud. The reader who wants both can walk. We will not duct-tape the walk into one post.
