**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Play therapy spent the second half of the twentieth century collecting stories (*Dibs*, 1964) and the first years of the twenty-first collecting graphs (Bratton, Ray, Rhine, and Jones, 2005). Both collections are historical. The fight is about which collection gets to speak in a room that pays. This essay is about the fight, and about the moment the fight turns into a worksheet.

It will not declare a winner. It will not reprint an effect size as a promise. It will name the verbs that leak.

## EST lists, and a door that was not built for a playroom

The Society of Clinical Psychology's empirically supported treatment lists in the 1990s (Chambless and colleagues; sister-pack objects) taught a generation of clinics to ask for manuals, fidelity, and a diagnosis that would sit still. Child-centered play therapy is a stance, not a twelve-session packet. Filial work has formats with counts. Theraplay has a structure. Sandplay has a process that resists a session number. An umbrella word — play therapy — cannot honestly occupy a single EST cell. People tried anyway. Trying is the history.

IAPT in Britain and managed care in the United States are different payers with the same appetite: short, named, countable. A method that refuses to be countable will be called unscientific. A method that becomes countable will be called a protocol. This series is willing to be called unhelpful before it becomes a protocol.

## What "more research is needed" is doing

The sentence is often true. It is also a way to keep selling weekends while waiting for a perfect trial. A perfect trial of "play therapy" is a bad object, because the umbrella is too wide. A trial of a *named* school, with a sample, a comparison, and a duration, is a better object. It is still not a homework assignment.

Later school-based CCPT studies tried to be the better object. Critics asked about sample size, control conditions, and allegiance. Those questions are the profession being a profession. A brochure that skips them is not being a profession.

## The worksheet temptation

The leak happens in the last paragraph. A writer has stayed historical for two thousand words and then offers "five ways to try play at home." The five ways are the article. Everything else was bait.

This series's first essay promised not to take the bait. The temptation will return in the graphics pass, in the SEO pass, in the "related services" plugin. Watch the plugin.

A milder leak is the thought record for parents, the feelings chart, the "ask your child these questions." Milder is still a session. We will not.

## What not to do with an evidence essay

Do not write "studies prove." Do not write "gold-standard." Do not pair a graph with a diagnosis keyword and a booking button. Do not sneer at clinicians who still assign *Dibs*. Stories and graphs are different objects. A field can hold both if it keeps the verbs honest.
