**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

A pack of forty-five drafts can sound like a library. It is not a library. It is a set of title pages a writer was willing to stand next to, plus a set of trails marked `[VERIFY]`. This essay is the honesty list, in prose, so a later editor does not have to infer what we did not open.

## Objects we named as dated

Hug-Hellmuth 1921. Anna Freud 1927. Klein 1932. Lowenfeld 1928/1935. Levy 1938. Axline 1947 and 1964. Moustakas 1953, 1959. Winnicott 1949, 1953, 1971. Guerney 1964. Erikson 1950, 1977. Oaklander 1978. Jernberg 1979. Kalff 1980. APT 1982. Handbook 1983. Allan 1988. Landreth 1991 and UNT Center 1988. Gil 1991, 1994. *IJPT* 1992. BAPT 1992. O'Connor *Primer* 1991. Kottman 1995. Bratton, Ray, Rhine, and Jones 2005. Schaefer's death notice 2020. Landreth's death notice 11 June 2026.

Those dates are the spine. If a later pass moves a year, it should move it in `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` first.

## Trails we marked thin

Levy's exact 1938 pagination. Axline's *Progressive Education* 1950 article. Jernberg's death year. Bernard Guerney's death year. Louise Guerney's vital status. First-issue *IJPT* editor names until a masthead is in hand. PTUK/PTI as a live UK quarrel. Arkansas statute language if anyone ever writes a service line. Landreth's birth year if the obituary is the only print source.

Thin is not fake. Thin is a refusal to sand a date.

## What we did not open

Hug-Hellmuth's full German corpus. The entire King and Steiner volume line by line. Every 1960s school-counselor article in the cluster. Every later edition of Landreth. Child-life society minute books. Sandplay society training manuals. APT credential PDFs for every revision year.

Wikipedia was a lead. It was not a source. Where a draft leaned on a later survey for a 1960s name cluster, the draft said to open the paper.

## Why an honesty list belongs in the pack

Because the worst therapy-history sentence is the clean one. Because a claims fence that only watches for worksheets will miss a fabricated year. Because a later graphics agent will want a face for every name, and the bibliography is how we say: the name is enough.

The next essay is the face we will not make.

## How to move a year without faking a shelf

If a later pass opens King and Steiner, or a 1960s school article, or an RPT manual cover year, add the object to `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` and then to the draft's YAML. Do not silently polish a `[VERIFY]` into a clean number you did not see. The worst therapy-history sentence is the clean one. This list exists so a graphics agent cannot infer that we held every box we named.

## Wikipedia was a lead

It was not a source. Where a draft leaned on a later survey for a 1960s name cluster, the draft said to open the paper. That sentence is part of the honesty list. A later editor who opens the paper should upgrade the YAML. Until then, the cluster stays a cluster. Clean falsehoods are the enemy. Ugly `[VERIFY]` is the friend.
