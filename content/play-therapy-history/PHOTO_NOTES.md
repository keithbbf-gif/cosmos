# Photo notes — play therapy history series

Text is the product. Images are evidence or atmosphere, never a fabricated child.

Companion: `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` (the license table). Graphics agents start there.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or Creative Commons portraits listed as `pd` or `cc` in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`. Almost none exist for this pack. That is correct.
- Period objects: title pages (Axline 1947 if a PD scan of the *book page* exists — jackets are often still ©), PD toy catalogs, PD hospital plans, PD engravings of nurseries.
- Original diagrams drawn for this series: a dated timeline, a map of rooms (Vienna, Berlin, London, Columbus, Denton, Fresno), a family-tree of *schools* (not of children).
- Type treatments: name, dates, one short epithet.

## Banned

- AI-generated faces, AI children, "historical photographs" that are not photographs, or "what X would look like."
- Scraped workshop / TED / university headshots.
- Any photograph of a child in a playroom, sand tray, or hospital bed used as decoration.
- *Dibs* cover art treated as a free illustration of a real child. The book is a public object; the child is not clip art.
- Patient artwork from play-therapy collections presented as free decoration.
- Stock "therapist on the floor with smiling child."
- Before/after faces.
- Atrocity or neglect photographs as headers.
- Anything that implies a WOW Therapies playroom you have not photographed with consent.

## Alt text

Name what is in the frame. "Title page of Virginia M. Axline, Play Therapy, Houghton Mifflin, 1947." Not "a child finds the self through play."

For type-only posts: no empty `<img>`. The heading is the image.

## WordPress featured image

If `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` says `no-portrait` or `confirm`, the featured image is a **series template** (wordmark + essay title), not a guessed face and not a stock child. Build one template; reuse it.

On upload day, open the Commons or museum URL again. Paste the credit block from `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` into the media caption **and** under the image in the post.

## Color and crop

Do not colorize PD photographs and call them contemporary. Do not crop a group so that a bystander becomes "the" portrait. Do not flip a face for layout.

## SLP lane

Do not reuse SLPWOW speech-pathology graphics here (oral-mechanism diagrams, fluency waveforms) as decoration. Different pack, different eye.

## If the graphics agent and the writer disagree

The license table wins. A beautiful unlicensed face — or a beautiful unlicensed child — does not ship.
