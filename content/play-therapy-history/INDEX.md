# Editorial calendar — WOW Therapies, play therapy history

Forty-five **draft** essays in seven stages. Status in YAML is `draft` (writer-cleared for **staging** review). Nothing in this folder is cleared to write itself onto the live wowtherapies.com sitemap.

All files live under `content/play-therapy-history/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`.

This pack is the play-therapy heritage lane. It is complementary to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` and to the SLPWOW speech-pathology history pack. Do not merge the calendars.

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-five essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):

| Wave | Suggested order | Pieces | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — fence | 1–2 weeks | 01, 02, 03 | How to read before any founder |
| 2 — child analysis | 3–8 | 04–12 | Hug-Hellmuth to Erikson, play as a *technique* |
| 3 — rooms and objects | 9–12 | 13–17 | Lowenfeld, Kalff, hospital, architecture |
| 4 — American nondirective | 13–18 | 18–22 | Axline, Dibs, Moustakas, Landreth, CCPT as a school |
| 5 — other schools | 19–24 | 23–29 | Filial, Gestalt, Theraplay, Adlerian, Jungian, Schaefer, O'Connor |
| 6 — profession | 25–30 | 30–36 | APT, journal, BAPT, schools, meta-analysis, RPT, Gil |
| 7 — critiques and tools | 31–36 | 37–45 | Who was left out, toys, gender, evidence, living people, map |

Hold any piece that still carries `[CITE NEEDED]` or `[VERIFY]` on a date you would print.

`check_pack.py` fails an era under 1,200 words, a figure under 1,000, or a reader essay under 800. Deepen with sources, not recap.
