# Bibliography — WOW Therapies, play therapy history series

Works cited across the pack. Article YAML `citations` lists the subset used in that piece. Prefer the edition named here. Wikipedia is a lead, not a source.

Last pass: 2026-09-14.

## General and profession

- Association for Play Therapy. Society history pages and director-emeritus list (Schaefer, O'Connor). Established 1982. [a4pt.org](https://www.a4pt.org/).
- Schaefer, Charles E., and Kevin J. O'Connor, eds. *Handbook of Play Therapy*. New York: Wiley, 1983.
- O'Connor, Kevin J., and Charles E. Schaefer, eds. *Handbook of Play Therapy*, vol. 2. New York: Wiley, 1994.
- *International Journal of Play Therapy*. Association for Play Therapy. First volume 1992.
- Landreth, Garry L. *Play Therapy: The Art of the Relationship*. Muncie, IN: Accelerated Development, 1991. Later eds. 2002, 2012 (Routledge).
- Center for Play Therapy, University of North Texas. "Our Story." Center established 1988 under Landreth; departmental vote 1987; first international conference on the UNT campus 1973. [cpt.unt.edu/about-us/our-story](https://cpt.unt.edu/about-us/our-story).
- University of North Texas. Garry Lee Landreth obituary notice. Died 11 June 2026. [northtexan.unt.edu](https://northtexan.unt.edu/obituaries/garry_lee_landreth.html).
- British Association of Play Therapists. Association history (BAPT, 1992). Use the society's own dated pages; do not flatten UK registration into APT credentials.
- Homeyer, Linda E., and Mary O. Morrison. "Play Therapy: Practice, Issues, and Trends." *American Journal of Play* 1, no. 2 (2008): 210–228.
- Bratton, Sue C., Dee Ray, Tammy Rhine, and Leslie Jones. "The Efficacy of Play Therapy with Children: A Meta-Analytic Review of Treatment Outcomes." *Professional Psychology: Research and Practice* 36, no. 4 (2005): 376–390.
