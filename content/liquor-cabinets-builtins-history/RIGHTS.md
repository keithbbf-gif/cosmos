# Rights and attribution

## Policy

- **No synthetic or AI-generated photographs.** Every raster image in `assets/` is a licensed photograph from Wikimedia Commons, a U.S. federal work (HABS / NPS / court exhibit), or a museum Open Access file hosted on Commons (Metropolitan Museum of Art CC0, Cleveland Museum of Art CC0, Dayton Art Institute CC0, and similar).
- **Schematics** (`schematic-*.svg`) are original line art for this channel pack, dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Redistribution:** JPEG/PNG files may be redistributed under their respective licenses when attribution and share-alike terms are preserved. SVG schematics may be reused without restriction.
- **Trademarks:** Museum names and historic site names appear only in factual captions. No endorsement is implied.

## Caption credit line (HTML)

For web or WXR export, each `<figure>` includes a `<figcaption>` with a human-readable caption. Where required by license, the figcaption ends with a short credit, for example: `Photo: Daderot / public domain (Wikimedia Commons).`

Full provenance table: `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`.

## DMCA / corrections

If you believe an asset is mis-attributed or not licensed for this use, remove the file from `assets/`, replace the embed with the remote URL noted in `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`, and file a correction in the content queue. Do not substitute unverified stock or generated images.
