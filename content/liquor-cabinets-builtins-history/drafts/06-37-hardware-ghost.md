Hardware is where a bar becomes honest or becomes a toy.

A drop leaf that holds two bottles and an elbow is a table. It needs stays that do not creep. It needs a hinge that does not rely on a skin of wood. It needs a rail or a knowing edge so a glass does not walk. If I use jewelry hinges on a working leaf, the leaf will teach me.

A slide — a tray that comes out — is the 1930s machine. Full-extension slides that are rated for the weight. Soft-close if the tray has glass on it. A stop so the tray does not come out of the case into a guest. The working face of the slide is a finish. It will see citrus. Do not give me a raw maple tray and a story about honesty. Honesty is a film that can be wiped.

Hinges on a glass door are not hinges on a linen door. Weight. Slam. A European cup hinge can be right. A butt hinge can be right. A pivot can be right and unforgiving. I will not pick one on camera for every piece. I will say: the hinge has to take the door and the habit. Soft-close on glass is not a luxury.

Locks we already did as history. As hardware: a good lock, a strike that meets, a key that is not taped inside, no key point toward a pane. A lock and a magnet catch can fight. Pick the boss.

The ghost of 1925 is the trick latch. The pressure catch behind a false spine. The spring drawer. I will build a quiet pressure catch on a door that is supposed to read as a panel. That is manners. I will not build a maze. Mazes get old. They also get forced by a guest who has had two drinks, and then the maze is a repair.

Pulls. A lip pull on a mid-century cousin. A ring on an Empire cousin. A nothing pull on a contemporary door that is all fingerprint. If the household cooks, they will touch the door with a bad hand. A pull that can be worked with a knuckle is kindness.

Soft-close and a felt bumper are not the same. I use both on a glass piece. The bumper is the last quiet. The slide is the travel.

Levelers. A bar that pours on a floor that is out will pour in a curve. Adjustable feet. A plinth that can hide them. If the piece is a built-in, the scribes hide the sins and also hide the fact that a fridge is already fighting the floor. Level the fridge on its own feet. Do not make the wood do the fridge's job.

I buy hardware from people who will still sell me a replacement in ten years. A boutique hinge that is a sculpture is a future problem. This shop has to answer the phone.

The Georgian brass hoop was hardware. The caster was hardware. The plug was hardware. We are in the same business. If the hardware is cute and the leaf droops, the piece is a costume.

I keep a small hardware board in the shop with the locks and slides I will still be able to buy. If a designer brings a sculpture hinge from a fair, we talk about the spare. A bar that cannot get a spare hinge in a decade is a bar that will live with a coat hanger. I have seen the coat hanger. It is honest and it is sad.

Magnetic catches on a tall pair of doors will drift as the wood moves. A good catch at the top and a good catch at the bottom is not redundancy. It is climate. One catch in the middle of a tall door is how you get a grin.

If the piece has a key, the escutcheon should not be sharper than the door. I have watched a cheap keyhole chew a sleeve. Hardware meets bodies. Bodies win the argument later, on a phone call.
