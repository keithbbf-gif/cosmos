# Staging sheet

Every file in `drafts/` carries YAML:

```yaml
status: staged
stage: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6
stage_name: foundation | minerals | machines | surfaces | finish | shop
voice: human
audience: shop
safety: shop-safe
```

`status: staged` means the piece has had a voice pass and is waiting
on a human. It is not `published`. It is not `scratch`.

`stage` is the syllabus sequence, not a CMS workflow. Do not conflate
them. A piece can be stage 4 (surfaces) and still `status: staged`.

## Read order (if you want a surface, not a pile)

1. `01` through `08` — what a scratch is, and the fence
2. `09` through `16` — what is on the paper
3. `17` through `24` — what writes the pattern
4. `25` through `32` — the surface that is not a flat field
5. `33` through `40` — the last grit and the first coat
6. `41` through `46` — lungs, heat, a table, a client, the last look

## Required topics (coverage)

- Grit scales and progression (P vs CAMI, erase-the-last)
- Inspection (raking light, pencil, the lying thumb)
- Minerals (garnet, AO, SiC, ceramic/zirconia, stearate, mesh, nonwoven, steel wool)
- Machines and hands (ROS, belt, wide-belt, pads, H&L, cork, scraper, profiles)
- Problem surfaces (veneer, end grain, edges, carving, plywood, old paint, glue, softwood)
- Into the finish (last grit, water pop, between coats, wet sand, rub-out, stain, cross-grain)
- Shop (dust, loading, heat, schedule, client speech)

If a later editor cuts the set down, keep at least one draft from each
stage and do not cut `04` (erase the last), `08` (fence), `25` (veneer),
or `41` (dust).
