# The last pass is the one you inspect

I used to think the last pass was the last disc. It is
the last look. Raking light. Edges. Joints. Ends. Glue.
Heat. The skip you hoped 220 would hide. Then you stop.

Stopping is a skill. The extra minute is how dishes are
born and edges die.

## The walk

Light low, long side, short side. Across, not down.
Pencil if I do not trust my eyes on a dark wood. Edges
with a finger for ridges and an eye for sausages. Joints
for cross-grain (draft 39) and for glue rivers (draft
31). Ends for a coarser tooth (draft 26). Veneer edges
for core (draft 25). A sniff for toast (draft 43).

If I find a defect, I start as coarse as that defect
*locally*, then I bring the neighborhood back, then I
walk again. I do not "blend from 220" on the whole top
because one corner spoke up.

## The lies I still believe

The fluorescent is enough. The thumb is enough. The
client will not look across. Friday is a grit. A finer
disc will fix a skip. I have believed all of these after
writing this pack. The walk is how I catch me.

If I cannot see, I wait for a better light or a better
hour. A top can sit. A shipped swirl cannot.

## Dust before the can

Vacuum. The dust on the surface is a layer. It becomes
a release and a nib. Clean hands. The fingerprint is
always mine. If the next finish is waterborne, I treat
stearate as a suspect (draft 13). If it is oil, I still
do not wipe with the sandwich rag.

This is not the finish schedule. This is the last
sanding act: do not hand the finisher a lie.

## What "done" means

The previous grit is gone. The last grit is even. The
edges match the field or match the drawing. The joints
do not have a foreign grain. The photograph is one the
binder can live with. The fence was not hopped.

Done is not "I am tired." Tired is a pause (draft 06).

## After this pack

An Images pass still owes photographs. A human still
owes a publish mark. A board still owes you an argument.
If a later editor cuts the set, keep 04, 08, 25, and
41. If they keep this one too, it is only a checklist
for a walk you already knew and still skip.

I get my eye down. I look across. I stop. The can can
wait one more minute. The dining room will not give it
back.
