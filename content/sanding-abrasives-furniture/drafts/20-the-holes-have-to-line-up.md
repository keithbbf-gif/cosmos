# The holes have to line up

I ran a five-hole disc on an eight-hole pad for a morning
because they both said 5-inch. The vacuum sounded busy. The
disc loaded in a ring. The pad got hot. I had invented a closed
coat with a premium label.

Dust extraction is plumbing. The holes in the disc, the pad,
the interface foam, and the hose have to be a path. If they
are not, you are sanding with a dust cake and calling it 180.

## Disc and pad

Match the pattern, or use a disc that is honestly multi-hole /
mesh so the pad's holes can find air. A pretty disc with the
wrong constellation is a lid.

Some pads are "universal" in the catalog sense. Test with the
vacuum on: put a hand near the pad edge and see if air is
moving through the face, not just making noise at the motor.
Noise is not flow.

## Interface pads

Foam between pad and disc is for curves and for a softer cut.
It also closes holes if the foam was not punched to match, or
if it has collapsed. A closed interface is a heater. A thick
interface on a tabletop is how you round edges and lose flat.

I use interface on a chair seat, a carved apron, a slight
crown I want to follow. I do not use it on a dining field
unless I am scuffing a film and I like the forgiveness. Even
then, holes.

## Soft is not kind

A soft pad plus pressure plus a fine disc is a swirl and a
dish. People add foam because the last pass felt harsh. Harsh
was often a dull mineral or a skip. Foam hides the skip longer.
The light still finds it.

## Hose and bag

A clogged hose is a closed coat. A full bag is a closed coat.
A shop vac on a ROS with no baffle, dumping fine dust back
toward the motor, is a fence issue (draft 08). Empty the thing.
If the machine is blowing more than it sucks, stop and fix the
path before you "just finish this grit."

## Shop consequence

Treat hole alignment as part of choosing a disc, not as
packaging. If the disc loads in a pattern that matches the
pad, you have a plumbing problem, not a wood problem.

Mesh (draft 14) forgives some mismatch because the whole face
is hole. Paper forgives nothing. I still check. Forgiveness is
how I got lazy last time.
