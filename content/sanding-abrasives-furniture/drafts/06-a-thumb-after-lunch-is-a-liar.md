# A thumb after lunch is a liar

The top felt smooth. I said so out loud, which is how you know I
was already defending myself. The sun, at four, said the 80-grit
arcs were still there, filled with finish, shinier than the wood
around them. My thumb had closed the argument my eyes still owed.

A thumb after lunch is a liar. Skin oils fill a scratch. Fatigue
makes "good enough" feel like a measurement. Heat from the last
pass makes the surface slick. You are reading your hand, not the
board.

## What feel is good for

Feel finds a ridge. A glue line proud of the field. A dent. A fuzz
after grain raise. An edge that is still sharp enough to cut a
cloth. Feel is a local tool. It is how you decide whether the
scraper has more work, or whether an arris needs a pass.

Feel is bad at pattern. Pattern is repeating. Skin averages. A
uniform leftover 120 can feel like 180 if your hand just came off
a belt-sander job. Relative feel is a trick the shop plays all
afternoon.

If you want to use your hand, use the back of a fingernail lightly
across the grain, not a pad of thumb along it. You will feel a
ridge sooner. You will still not see a swirl. See the swirl.

## Oil, sweat, the lying film

Hands leave oil. Oil fills. A filled scratch feels refined. Then
you stain and the oil resists, or you oil-finish and the handprint
is a ghost. Wash. Or glove the last inspection if you are a person
whose hands write their name on maple.

Lunch makes it worse. Salt, bread, the sandwich that was a mistake.
I have stained a fingerprint into a top with oak dust on a wet oil
cloth. The fingerprint was always mine. The thumb that passed the
top was the same thumb.

## Fatigue is a grit change

Late in the day you linger. Lingering on a random-orbit dishes.
You also press harder because tired arms think pressure is
progress. Pressure heats. Heat burnishes. Burnish feels smooth.
Burnish under stain is blotch.

When I am tired I do not "just finish the 180." I stop on a grit
change, or I switch to the raking light and the pencil and I let
those tools overrule me. If I cannot see, I stop. A top can sit
overnight. A shipped swirl cannot.

## The rule I write on the door

Do not pass a show surface by thumb alone. Feel the ridge. See
the pattern. If the two disagree, the light wins.

If you are late, you will believe the thumb. That is why this
draft exists as its own piece and not as a sentence inside the
light essay. The liar is not the hand. The liar is the hour.
