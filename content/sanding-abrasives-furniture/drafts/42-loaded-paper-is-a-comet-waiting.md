# Loaded paper is a comet waiting

The sheet went slick, then a clump let go, and a 60-grit
comet ran across a field I had already called almost done.
A loaded face is finer than the number — a burnisher —
until a chunk of waste comes off and you have a coarse
scratch with a tail.

Clean the sheet, or throw it. The comet is always cheaper
to prevent than to recut.

## Why it loads

Softwoods, resins, paint films, stearate-less closed coat,
no extraction, heat, pine (draft 32), glue smear (draft
31). The waste has nowhere to go, so it becomes the face.

Open coat and mesh delay it (draft 07, 14). They do not
repeal it. A vacuum that is actually a path (draft 20)
delays it. Pressing harder accelerates it.

## What to do in the minute

Knock the disc. A cleaning stick on hook-and-loop. A
brush. If it shines, it is already late. Change it. I
have "just finished this corner" on a loaded sheet and
paid with a tail that crossed the corner I had finished.

Do not wash a disc in solvent as a hobby. That is a fence-
adjacent move (draft 08) and a way to wreck the bond.
Throw it away.

## The recut

A comet is a new defect. Start as coarse as that defect
locally, then bring the neighborhood back through the
sequence so you do not have a polished trench. A ROS on
the whole top to "blend the comet" is how you make a
bowl with a scar in it.

If the comet is in veneer, you may be in a hole (draft
25). Stop and look before you chase.

## Shop habit

Change paper sooner than your thrift. Keep open coat for
the woods that load. Do not sand paint and then wood with
the same disc. The paint cake is the comet's favorite
food.

I write this like I always change paper. I do not. I
change paper after I remember the last comet. That is
why the draft is here, not because I am a model.

## Shop consequence

If the surface suddenly got a deep scratch in a pass that
was supposed to be 180, look at the paper before you look
at the wood. The wood did not grow a 60. You dropped one.

Loaded paper is a finer grit until it isn't. The second
half of that sentence is the one that shows in raking
light.
