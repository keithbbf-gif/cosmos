# Rubbing out has its own numbers

400 and beyond on a film is rubbing out, not sanding wood.
Compounds, papers, and pads are a sheen language. It is easy
to cut an arris into a raw line. The stain, if there was
one, is already under the film. The raw line is wood in a
sea of polish.

This is not a between-coat nib pass. This is a finished
build you have decided to level and to bring to a sheen
the can did not leave.

## When the film is ready

Not tacky. Not a fingernail print. Not "dry to the touch"
on a Tuesday after a Monday coat of oil-modified poly.
Cure is a clock the can and the shop temperature own. If
you rub early, you smear. If you wait, you cut cleaner.

I do not invent a day-count here that pretends to be
every brand `[VERIFY]`. I invent an offcut that got the
same schedule.

## The language

Papers in the high numbers, or micron films, or compounds
on a pad — they are cutting a coating. Each step must
erase the last scratch in the *film*. The same skip law
(draft 04) applies, only now the skip is a haze you will
see in a showroom light.

Satin from the can and satin from a rub are not the same
satin. Rubbed satin is often a finer, deeper quiet. Can
satin is flatteners in the film. If you rub a flatted
satin you can go glossy in spots where you cut the
flatteners off. Then you have a map.

## Edges, always

The film is thinnest on the arris. Rubbing loves edges
the way a ROS does. Tape, or a lighter hand, or accept
a slightly lower sheen on the break. A raw arris after a
rub is a recut of the whole neighborhood.

## Compounds

Commercial rubbing compounds exist for this. Use them as
products. Do not mix a household powder into a slurry
because a forum said it was "what they used in the old
shops." Old shops also had different films and different
lungs.

Oil as a lubricant on some traditional rubs — know the
film. Water on some. The wrong liquid blushes or lifts.
Test.

## Shop consequence

If you wanted the sheen in the can, stop at a clean last
coat and a nib pass. If you wanted a rubbed sheen, build
enough film to survive the rub, then treat rubbing as its
own sequence with its own inspection.

A dining table that will be used as a dining table will
get marked again. A piano rub on a mustard-using family
table is a beautiful week and a sad month. Choose the
sheen for the room, not for the photograph of the shop.
