## What not to stage

- Reenactment lynching imagery.
- Uncredited “old mill town” stock from a Midwest catalog.
- Jack or any minor in a heritage hero shot.
- A generated sepia of Beauvoir. Foundation lines, or nothing.

## Voice to the photographer

Stand where the railroad was. Include the empty. Do not ask anyone to smile for 1899.
