## Rules

- Wikimedia and LOC files: local copies live under `assets/photos/`; `registry.json` holds Commons titles and URLs. Record the exact Commons filename in the article figcaption credit line.
- Encyclopedia of Arkansas images: request or link. Do not hotlink their CDN into a commercial theme.
- Family albums and shop photographs: `photo_slot` only until a person who owns the negative says yes.
- No AI “old-timey mill” renderings in the figure list.
