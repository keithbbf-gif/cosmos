## Rail and industrial

- Darling and Bragg, as above (W&SVR; Gates mill closure 1924 in their note; equipment later to Crossett).
- Union Pacific short-line note, Warren & Saline River Railroad (WSR). Secondary.
- Wikipedia, “Warren and Saline River Railroad,” and ICC valuation notes for Warren, Johnsville & Saline River Railroad (incorporated August 7, 1905; reorganized 1920). Use for dates, not color.
- University of Wisconsin–La Crosse, Murphy Library. Historic Steamboat Photographs. *Gate City* (Packet, 1900–1913), Ways 2208. Prints a June 15, 1913, Saline wreck date against Woodard’s October 14, 1913. Keep the seam until a wreck report is found.
- U.S. Census Bureau. Decennial counts as printed in Encyclopedia of Arkansas town and county tables.
