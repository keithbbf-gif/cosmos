## Prose

Articles are original magazine copy grounded in Encyclopedia of Arkansas and named primary sources. `voice_check: edited` marks a human editor pass on prose and figures. Not legal advice. Not construction or medical guidance.

## Excluded (see `PHOTO_NOTES.md`)

- AI sepia mills, generated faces, reenactment lynching imagery.
- Encyclopedia of Arkansas hotlinked photographs without permission.
- Family albums and shop interiors until the rights holder approves (`photo_slot` comments in articles).

## Corrections

If an asset is mis-attributed, remove the local file, update `registry.json` and `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`, and replace the `<figure>` with a corrected plate or a schematic-only figure. Do not substitute unverified stock.
