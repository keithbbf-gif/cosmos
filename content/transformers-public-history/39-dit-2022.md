# Diffusion Transformer (19 December 2022)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 19 December 2022 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Peebles and Xie, *Scalable Diffusion Models with Transformers*,
arXiv:2212.09748.

## The claim

DiT replaces the U-Net backbone in latent diffusion with a **ViT-like Transformer** operating
on latent patches, conditioned on diffusion timestep and class (or text, in later
descendants). The 2017/2020 encoder, plus adaLN-style conditioning, becomes a generative
image architecture, not only a classifier.

This is the public type-case for "the backbone of a diffusion model can be a Transformer."
Later Sora-class and Stable Diffusion 3-class discussions sit downstream; those products'
unpublished internals are not this paper.

## What the artifact specified

DiT-S/B/L/XL sizes, patchify of a VAE latent, adaptive layer norm conditioning, and scaling
curves vs U-Nets at matched compute on ImageNet latents. Author-reported FID numbers in the
paper. No language model is required for the class-conditional setting.

## What it displaced

The assumption that diffusion *was* a U-Net. After DiT, backbone choice is a knob. U-Nets
did not vanish.

The paper's scaling plots matter more than any single FID: they argue that **Transformer
scaling laws, already public for language, also describe diffusion backbones**. A reader
who only remembers "DiT is a ViT in a diffusion loop" misses why the paper is in an
architecture history rather than an applications list. The adaLN-Zero conditioning is the
other load-bearing choice — timestep and class (or later, text) modulate the residual
stream without concatenating a second sequence of tokens. That is a different conditioning
geometry from cross-attention-to-a-text-encoder (the Imagen / SD1 story) and from
MM-DiT's later dual-stream mix.

## Immediate lineage

PixArt-α, SD3's MMDiT (public tech report later), and a 2024–2025 pile of diffusion
transformers for image and video. Date those by their own artifacts. This stamp stays
19 December 2022.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not reconstruct any closed video generator. It does not claim DiT invented
diffusion (Ho et al. 2020, arXiv:2006.11239; Rombach et al. latent diffusion, 2021-12-20,
arXiv:2112.10752). Those papers date the *generative process*. This card dates the
*Transformer backbone inside that process*.

A 2024–2026 "Sora-class" or "movie-gen" system that withholds its backbone sits under
`tph-49`, not under a guessed DiT diagram.
