# DETR: set prediction with encoder–decoder attention (26 May 2020)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 26 May 2020 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Carion, Massa, Synnaeve, Usunier, Kirillov, Zagoruyko, *End-to-End Object
Detection with Transformers*, arXiv:2005.12872.

## The claim

DETR uses the 2017 **encoder–decoder** as a set predictor: a CNN backbone produces a
feature map, a Transformer encoder reasons globally over flattened features, and a
Transformer decoder attends from a **fixed set of learned object queries** to those
features. Bipartite matching (Hungarian loss) aligns predictions to ground-truth boxes
so the model outputs a set, not a sequence that needs NMS as the primary design.

This is two days before GPT-3's arXiv stamp and six months before ViT. Vision Transformers
did not "then invent DETR." DETR is first in this pack's vision-decoder story.

## What the artifact specified

ResNet backbone (in the original), 6+6 Transformer layers in the type-case, 100 object
queries, auxiliary decoding losses. Cross-attention is load-bearing: queries are the
decoder side; image features are the encoder memory. That is Figure 1 of 2017, pointed at
boxes and classes.

Training is famously slow to converge relative to contemporary detectors; later Deformable
DETR and many descendants exist to fix that. Those descendants are not this date.

## What it displaced

The idea that Transformer seq2seq was for tokens of language. After DETR, "query tokens
plus a memory" is a detection and segmentation pattern (and, later, a multimodal pattern:
Flamingo's cross-attention to visual tokens is a cousin, 29 April 2022).

## Immediate lineage

Deformable DETR, DINO-detection, Mask2Former, and the query-based segmentation family.
Perceiver (4 March 2021) generalizes "cross-attend into a small latent" as a perception
backbone. Do not date Perceiver at DETR; do notice the shared query/memory idea.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not claim DETR retired Faster R-CNN in production. It did not. It claims DETR is
the type-case of **2017 cross-attention as object queries**. No private detector is
inferred.
