# BART: denoising seq2seq pre-training (29 October 2019)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 29 October 2019 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Lewis, Liu, Goyal, Ghazvininejad, Mohamed, Levy, Stoyanov, Zettlemoyer,
*BART: Denoising Sequence-to-Sequence Pre-training for Natural Language Generation,
Translation, and Comprehension*, arXiv:1910.13461.

## The claim

BART is an encoder–decoder Transformer pretrained as a **denoising autoencoder**: corrupt the
text with one or more noisers (masking, deletion, sentence permutation, document rotation,
text infilling), then generate the original. It is six days after T5's arXiv stamp and should
not be dated "2020" because of ACL.

Where BERT is encoder-only MLM and GPT is decoder-only LM, BART is the 2017 diagram with a
BERT-like noiser on the encoder side and a GPT-like decoder.

## What the artifact specified

Architecture "closely following" RoBERTa-base / large for the encoder and a matching decoder,
with GeLU and some BERT/GPT implementation alignments the paper lists. The interesting
specification is the **noiser set**, especially **text infilling** (spans replaced by a single
mask token) and sentence shuffling. Generation tasks (summarization, translation, dialogue)
are first-class evaluation, not an afterthought glued onto `[CLS]`.

A common secondary-source error (caught while gathering stamps for this pack) is citing
arXiv:1912.09713 as BART. That ID is a different paper. BART is **1910.13461**.

## What it displaced

The claim that encoder-only pre-training plus a separately trained decoder is the only way to
get generation out of the BERT family. After BART (and T5), seq2seq pre-training is normal.

## Immediate lineage

mBART for multilingual generation. A long tail of summarization fine-tunes. The *corruption
then reconstruct* idea reappears in vision (BEiT, MAE) with different masks; those are 2021
and later and must not steal this date.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not claim BART invented denoising autoencoders (Vincent et al. 2008 is the deep-DAE
ancestor). It claims BART is the widely cited *Transformer* DAE for text generation. It does
not treat later chat models as BART variants without a paper.
