# RoPE: rotary position embeddings (20 April 2021)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 20 April 2021 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Su, Lu, Pan, Murtadha, Wen, Liu, *RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with
Rotary Position Embedding*, arXiv:2104.09864.

## The claim

RoPE injects position by **rotating** pairs of dimensions of \(Q\) and \(K\) with an
angle linear in the token index. The dot product \(q_m^\top k_n\) then depends on
\(m - n\), i.e. on **relative** offset, without adding a Shaw vector or a T5 bias table
to the residual stream. Absolute index still enters (the angle is \(m\theta\)), but the
score is relative.

This becomes the default position mechanism of the open decoder-only wave (LLaMA and
almost everything after). Dating RoPE at LLaMA (2023) is the same class of error as dating
sliding windows at Mistral.

Su described the construction on a Chinese blog before the arXiv stamp. That blog was not
successfully re-fetched in this pass. The series uses **20 April 2021** and says so.

## What the artifact specified

2×2 rotation blocks (or complex multiply) on even/odd dimension pairs, \(\theta_i =
10000^{-2i/d}\), applied to \(Q\) and \(K\) only (not \(V\), in the standard form).
RoFormer is the paper's own encoder/application vehicle; the exportable object is the
rotation.

Because the rotation lives on \(Q\) and \(K\), it **interacts with later KV compression**.
DeepSeek-V2's MLA paper is explicit that a low-rank shared KV and RoPE conflict, which is
why they keep a **decoupled RoPE channel**. That 2024 fact is downstream of this 2021
geometry.

## What it displaced

Learned absolute embeddings as the unexamined default for new decoder-only LMs. After
LLaMA, a paper that uses absolute learned PE is making a choice that needs a sentence.
ALiBi (27 August 2021) is the other 2021 public alternative: no rotation, a linear
penalty on logits.

## Immediate lineage

Position Interpolation (27 June 2023), NTK-aware scaled RoPE (community, on or before
30 June 2023, weaker pin), YaRN (31 August 2023), LongRoPE (21 February 2024), and the
2025 DroPE line (drop PE after some training). All of those cards are "RoPE has a wall;
here is a retrofit." They are not new 2017 sinusoids.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not claim RoPE extrapolates unboundedly. The 2023 retrofit wave exists because it
did not, in practice, at the factors people wanted. It does not claim every closed model
uses RoPE.
