# GPT-2: unsupervised multitask in a decoder (14 February 2019)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 14 February 2019 (`official-blog-and-report`).
**Primary source:** Radford et al., *Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners*,
OpenAI; blog *Better language models and their implications*.

## The claim

GPT-2 is the same decoder-only topology as GPT-1, scaled, trained on WebText, and **not**
primarily sold as a fine-tune recipe. The report's title is the claim: a language model
trained to predict the next token can be pointed at other tasks by the prompt, without a
task-specific architecture. Zero-shot transfer is the public event. The 1.5B "XL" model is
the largest published size in that report.

Date kind is official report plus blog, 14 February 2019. Weights were released in stages
after a debate about misuse; the staged release is a *distribution* event, not a second
architecture date.

## What the artifact specified

Four published sizes, all decoder-only Transformers with learned absolute positions and a
1024-token context:

- 117M (GPT-1-sized), 345M, 762M, 1.5B.

The 1.5B model is 48 layers. Layer normalization is applied in the **pre-LN** style (the
report's Figure 2 / text describe moving LN), which is a real architectural delta from the
2017 post-LN default and from GPT-1. Byte-level BPE expands the vocabulary and avoids
UNKs. Residual scaling at initialization is mentioned for the deeper stacks.

WebText is the data claim: outbound Reddit links with a karma threshold, excluding Wikipedia
overlaps they could detect. That is a **data-architecture** move as much as a layer move —
the model is still a decoder, but the corpus is broader and dirtier than BooksCorpus.

## What it displaced

The assumption that zero-shot task performance required either supervised multi-task training
or a task-specific head invented at pre-train time. GPT-2's public numbers on LAMBADA, CBT,
Winograd, and translation-as-LM are uneven and author-reported; the historical force is that
they were **non-zero without fine-tuning**.

It also displaced, for a year, the idea that 100M-parameter BERT-class models were "large."
They were not, once 1.5B was on the table. That psychological shift is not an architecture,
but it changes which architectures get trained.

## Immediate lineage

GPT-3 will take the zero-shot claim, add few-shot in-context examples, and jump to 175B.
GPT-Neo / GPT-J / GPT-NeoX will later re-implement decoder-only GPT-2/3-class stacks in public
code (2021–2022). Those are systems and license events as much as architecture events.

Pre-LN as a default for deep decoders is on the path to `tph-32`.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not treat the partial weight release as evidence of a different block. It does not
claim WebText is fully specified as a recoverable dataset (later reproductions are
approximate). It does not claim GPT-2 "is GPT-3 but smaller" — GPT-3's few-shot framing and
dense scaling are a 2020 paper.

No private successor diagram is inferred from this report.
