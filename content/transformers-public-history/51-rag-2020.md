# RAG: retrieval as a first-class Transformer path (22 May 2020)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 22 May 2020 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Lewis, Perez, Piktus, Petroni, Karpukhin, Goyal, Küttler, Lewis, Yih,
Rocktäschel, Riedel, Kiela, *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP
Tasks*, arXiv:2005.11401.

## The claim

RAG is architecture because it adds a **non-parametric memory** to a seq2seq Transformer:
a query encoder retrieves documents from a differentiable (or approximately differentiable)
index; a generator conditions on those documents. The 2017 block does not grow a 100B
parameter knowledge table. It **reads**.

REALM (Guu et al., 2020) and DPR (Karpukhin et al., 2020) are sibling public retrieval
papers in the same season. This card uses RAG as the type-case that named the *generation*
combo. RETRO (8 December 2021, arXiv:2112.04426) later retrieves at chunked scale for a
decoder-only LM. Those are different wirings; do not collapse them.

Six days later, GPT-3's arXiv stamp will argue that a big parametric model can *also* do
knowledge-ish tasks few-shot. The field then spends years running both bets at once.

## What the artifact specified

RAG-Sequence vs RAG-Token (how documents are marginalized), a BART generator in the
type-case, a DPR-class retriever, Wikipedia as the non-parametric store in the main
experiments, and end-to-end fine-tunes where the retriever and generator can both move.
Author-reported 2020 open-domain QA numbers.

The index is part of the architecture. A paper that says "we added RAG" without saying
what is indexed, how it is encoded, and how often it is refreshed has underspecified the
system.

## What it displaced

The idea that the only way to update a Transformer's knowledge was to retrain weights.
After RAG, "swap the corpus" is a legal sentence. It did not make retrieval free (latency,
stale indices, citation errors).

## Immediate lineage

RETRO, Atlas, FiD (Izacard and Grave, 2020 — Fusion-in-Decoder is another public wiring),
and the 2023–2026 "tool-using LM" stack where the retriever is sometimes just a web search
API. Tool use is a *later* interface; RAG is the 2020 Transformer-internal type-case.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not claim RAG invented document retrieval. It claims RAG is the widely cited
*Transformer generator + learned retriever* paper. It does not treat any private search
pipeline as this architecture.
