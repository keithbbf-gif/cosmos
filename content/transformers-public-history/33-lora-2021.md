# LoRA: low-rank adapters as a frozen-backbone architecture (17 June 2021)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 17 June 2021 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Hu, Shen, Wallis, Allen-Zhu, Li, Wang, Wang, Chen, *LoRA: Low-Rank
Adaptation of Large Language Models*, arXiv:2106.09685.

## The claim

LoRA is architecture because it changes **which tensors are the model at fine-tune time**.
Keep the pretrained weights \(W_0\) frozen; learn two skinny matrices \(B A\) whose product
is added to selected projections (typically \(W_Q, W_V\)). Rank \(r \ll d\) is the new
hyperparameter. At serve time you can merge \(W_0 + BA\) and pay no extra latency.

Adapter-BERT and prefix / prompt tuning are public cousins (2020–2021). LoRA is the one
that became the default PEFT noun in the 2023 open-weight wave.

## What the artifact specified

Injection points (attention projections in the type-case), rank, scaling \(\alpha / r\),
no extra inference latency if merged. Experiments on GPT-2 / GPT-3-class and other
Transformers, with quality close to full fine-tunes at far smaller trainable-parameter
counts. Author-reported 2021 figures.

This is not a new attention score. A history that only lists score functions will miss why
2023 "fine-tunes" were often 8–64 rank updates.

The reparameterization \(W = W_0 + \frac{\alpha}{r} BA\) is the whole block. \(A\) is
usually initialized Gaussian and \(B\) at zero so the adapter starts as the identity.
Which matrices get adapters is a real architectural choice: attention-only vs attention
plus FFN vs every linear. The 2021 paper's type-case is attention projections. Later
"LoRA on MoE experts only" recipes are 2024-public and must not steal this date.

Mergeability is why LoRA beat some older adapter designs for *serving*: after training you
can fold \(BA\) into \(W_0\) and ship one matrix. Multi-tenant serving that keeps adapters
*unmerged* (many customers, one base) is a different systems story and is not specified
here.

## What it displaced

Full-parameter fine-tunes as the only respectable specialization, at least for many
decoder-only adaptations. It also displaced some "we must distill to a small model to
ship a variant" arguments: you can ship many LoRA deltas against one base.

## Immediate lineage

QLoRA (2023) is quantization plus LoRA, a later paper. (IA)^3, AdaLoRA, DoRA, and a forest
of PEFT methods sit here. Prefix-tuning and adapters remain alternatives, not subsumed.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not claim LoRA is free vs full FT on every task. It does not claim merged LoRA
equals a from-scratch model of the same effective rank. No private adapter stack is
described.
