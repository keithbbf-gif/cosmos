## Caption and SEO rules

- Keep `<figure>`, descriptive `alt`, and `<figcaption>` together when embedding (see `staged/FIGURE_EMBEDS.md` and inline blocks in numbered drafts).
- Captions describe **public architecture history** — dates, topology, and mechanism names from cited artifacts. Not product recommendations, benchmark bragging, or unreleased-block speculation.
- Do not imply affiliation with any lab, cloud vendor, or closed product.
- Do not hotlink arXiv PDF figure crops in production without a new row in `image_sources.json` and an update here.

## Banned without a new row in this file

- AI-generated diagrams, “paper figure enhancements,” or photorealistic renders.
- Faces or likenesses of researchers, executives, or mascots.
- Private system names, internal codenames, or unreconstructed withheld blocks (see `00-reading-rules.md` fence).
- Trademarked logos presented as editorial proof.
