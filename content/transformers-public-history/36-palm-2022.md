# PaLM: 540B dense decoder with MQA (4–5 April 2022)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 4 April 2022 (`official-blog`); paper 5 April 2022 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Chowdhery et al., *PaLM: Scaling Language Modeling with Pathways*,
arXiv:2204.02311.

## The claim

PaLM is a **540 billion** parameter dense decoder-only Transformer trained with the Pathways
system, and it is a prominent public **MQA** adopter at scale. The paper also documents
SwiGLU, parallel attention/FFN in a variant, RoPE in later PaLM-2 (not this paper's every
detail — do not collapse PaLM and PaLM 2). This card is PaLM the 2022 paper.

Few-shot numbers on BIG-bench, reasoning, and code are the evaluation event. Architecturally
the interesting published choices are scale, MQA, and the systems story (Pathways), not a
new score function.

PaLM is also a public SwiGLU + (in the 540B) standard decoder stack at a size only GPT-3
had previously put on a few-shot table. The paper discusses discontinuous "breakthrough"
behavior on some BIG-bench tasks as scale increases. That discussion is an *evaluation
claim*, not a new layer. It later gets entangled with the "emergent abilities" debate
(Wei et al. and subsequent critiques). Keep those papers off this stamp; they are not
PaLM's architecture.

## What the artifact specified

Tabled depths/widths for 8B / 62B / 540B. MQA for decode efficiency. Training on a large
multilingual/code-heavy mix (author-described). The Pathways paper/system is the compute
vehicle; this series does not treat Pathways as a Transformer block.

The 540B configuration is the one later slides mean by "PaLM." Layer count, width, and
head count belong to Table 1 of the PDF (draft debt: quote those cells). The *choice*
that belongs on this card without guessing the integers is already public: MQA at
hundreds-of-billions scale is how Shazeer 2019 finally shows up in a flagship dense LM.

PaLM 2 (technical report 10 May 2023) is a different public artifact and must not steal
this date.

## What it displaced

A lingering sense that only one lab (the GPT-3 line) would publish 100B+ dense decoders
with few-shot tables. Chinchilla (a week earlier) and PaLM (a week later) make March–April
2022 the month scale became a two-lab public conversation again.

## Immediate lineage

Gemini reports (6 December 2023 family paper) are later and more multimodal; they withhold
many block details. U-PaLM / Flan-PaLM are instruction-tuned descendants. The MQA choice
feeds GQA's 2023 motivation: PaLM-scale decode made shared KV heads a default conversation.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not reconstruct unpublished Gemini blocks from PaLM. It does not treat Pathways as
open infrastructure. Author-reported 540B is the paper's number.
