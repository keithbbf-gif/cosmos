# ALiBi: linear biases instead of position embeddings (27 August 2021)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 27 August 2021 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Press, Smith, Lewis, *Train Short, Test Long: Attention with Linear
Biases Enables Input Length Extrapolation*, arXiv:2108.12409.

## The claim

ALiBi throws away added position embeddings and adds a **head-specific linear penalty** to
attention logits, proportional to the distance between query and key. The paper's promise is
in the title: train on short sequences, evaluate on longer ones, without a rotary retrofit.
The mechanism is a few slopes, not a \(d_{\mathrm{model}}\)-wide PE table.

This is four months after RoPE's arXiv stamp. 2021 therefore contains *two* public answers
to "absolute PE does not extrapolate," and they are not the same answer.

## What the artifact specified

For each head \(h\), a slope \(m_h\) (they use a geometric sequence of slopes). Logits get
\(-m_h \cdot |i - j|\) (causal: \(j \le i\)). No PE is added to token embeddings. Experiments
are on decoder-only language models; they report perplexity that degrades more slowly than
sinusoidal / rotary baselines when the test length exceeds the train length, in their setup.

MPT (MosaicML, 2023) is a later public adopter. BLOOM used ALiBi (BLOOM paper 9 November
2022). Those are adoption dates.

## What it displaced

The assumption that extrapolation required a clever basis function on the embeddings. ALiBi
says the score function can carry a **distance prior** instead. It did not displace RoPE as
the 2023 open-weight default; LLaMA chose RoPE, and the retrofit industry (PI, YaRN) grew
around that choice.

## Immediate lineage

NoPE (Kazemnejad et al., 31 May 2023) asks whether causal decoders can skip explicit PE
entirely. StreamingLLM / attention sinks (29 September 2023) are a different length story
(softmax mass on early tokens). Do not file sinks as ALiBi.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not claim ALiBi won a 2026 bake-off. It does not claim slopes generalize to every
downstream task. It does not assign ALiBi to closed models.
