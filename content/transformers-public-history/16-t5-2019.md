# T5: text-to-text encoder–decoder and relative bias (23 October 2019)

**Status:** staged draft. Not a publication.
**Lane:** public transformer architecture history.
**First public appearance:** 23 October 2019 (`arxiv-v1`).
**Primary source:** Raffel, Shazeer, Roberts, Lee, Narang, Matena, Zhou, Li, Liu, *Exploring
the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer*, arXiv:1910.10683.

## The claim

T5 puts the 2017 **encoder–decoder** back in the center, casts every text problem as
text-in / text-out, and then actually ablates the choices the 2018 fork had been making by
folklore. The architecture deltas that belong on this card (as opposed to the C4 data card
someone should write later) are:

- a return to encoder–decoder rather than BERT or GPT;
- **relative position scalar biases** with logarithmic buckets, not 2017 sinusoids and not
  Shaw's vector embeddings;
- **no \(1/\sqrt{d_k}\) scaling** on the attention logits;
- span-corruption as the default denoising objective.

JMLR 2020 is the journal stamp. The public date is 23 October 2019.

## What the artifact specified

Model sizes from Small through 11B. Encoder and decoder depths match in the default
configurations. C4 (Colossal Clean Crawled Corpus) is the data contribution: a cleaned Common
Crawl derivative they release enough description of to make the experiment roughly repeatable.

The paper is long because the ablations are the point: encoder-only vs decoder-only vs
encoder–decoder, different denoisers, different position schemes, different fine-tune
schedules. A reader who cites T5 only as "that 11B model" has missed why it is in an
*architecture* history.

Relative bias: each head learns a scalar \(b\) per bucketed offset. Cheap, exportable, and
the production-simple descendant of Shaw. Many later encoder–decoder systems keep it; the
decoder-only LLM wave generally does not (they go RoPE).

## What it displaced

The idea that BERT-style classification heads are the natural interface to a pretrained
Transformer. After T5, "just generate the answer as text" is a respectable default, including
for tasks that used to be label IDs. That interface choice is why instruction-tuning later
looks like more T5, even when the backbone is a decoder.

## Immediate lineage

BART (six days later) is a different denoising encoder–decoder. mT5, ByT5, T0, Flan-T5, and
UL2 are the family. Prefix-LM and "pack the task name into the input" are T5 habits that
survive in models that are no longer encoder–decoders.

## What this draft does not claim

It does not claim C4 is a complete dump of the web. It does not claim relative bias is
strictly better than RoPE; they were evaluated in different eras on different topologies. It
does not treat Flan-T5 (20 October 2022) as this paper.
