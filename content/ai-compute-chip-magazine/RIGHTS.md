# Rights and image policy

This issue ships **original SVG schematics** only. No third-party product photography, no trademark logos, and **no AI-generated images**.

## Editorial rules

1. **No AI-generated images** — including faux die shots, synthetic board photos, or invented press events.
2. **Diagrams are explanatory**, not datasheets; verify numbers in each article's Sources block.
3. **`<figure>` blocks** use descriptive `alt` text, visible `<figcaption>`, and `loading="lazy"` where supported.
4. **Do not embed** vendor logos or trademarked board art.
