## What this pack is

A public-source history of **speech-language work inside American public schools**, and of the **statutes and cases** that turned a city itinerant job into a related service with a federal file. Readers are clinicians, students, caregivers, and curious people. The host brand is SLPWOW (WOW Therapies household). Articles stand alone. No booking close.

## What this pack will not do

- No clinical protocols, cue hierarchies, or “try this if your child…”
- No merge into furniture, figroots, COSMOS modules, or clinic booking pages.
- No live `slpwow.com` or WOW Therapies CMS writes from this folder.
- No generated historical faces.
- No copy-paste of the sibling profession pack’s McAlpin / Iowa / Wisconsin sentences. Schools are the subject here.
