This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

On 7–8 October 1971, the Eastern District of Pennsylvania entered a consent agreement in *Pennsylvania Association for Retarded Children v. Commonwealth of Pennsylvania*, 334 F. Supp. 1257. The caption still uses the association’s then name and the statute’s then noun. The order’s work was plainer: children labeled mentally retarded could not be shut out of a public program of education and training. Notice and a hearing before a change of assignment. A special master to find the children who had been denied. A deadline no later than 1 September 1972. A further opinion in May 1972, 343 F. Supp. 279, tidied the decree. The Pennsylvania Attorney General had already been made to construe the School Code so that “mental age of five” and compulsory-attendance exits could not be used, against a parent’s wish, to end the child’s access.

Thomas K. Gilhool was among the lawyers. The case is a parents’ association case. That matters. The door did not open because a speech bureau asked. It opened because families sued a commonwealth.

## Mills, a year later, a wider class

*Mills v. Board of Education of the District of Columbia*, 348 F. Supp. 866 (D.D.C. 1972), is Judge Joseph C. Waddy’s memorandum, judgment, and decree. Seven children, by their next friends. The labels in the complaint are the District’s: behavioral problems, mentally retarded, emotionally disturbed, hyperactive. Some had been refused admission. Some had been put out after admission. No alternative placement. No periodic review. The class was certified in December 1971.

Waddy ordered identification, contact, education, individual assessments, plans, and hearings. He refused the Board’s poverty as a defense. If the District had money for some children, it could not solve a budget by dropping the children who cost more. Later writers call that the sentence Congress copied into the spirit of 94-142. The copying is real even when the statute never names Waddy.

Mills is wider than PARC. PARC’s class was children with intellectual disability, in the language of the day. Mills reached other disability labels the District had used as keys to the sidewalk. The Department of Education’s IDEA history page treats the two cases together as the judicial predicate for the 1975 Act. Over a hundred later opinions cite them. That is a legal afterlife, not a romance.

## What a speech teacher could hear in the decrees

Neither decree is a speech case. Both are desk cases. The residue for this series is that “related services” in 1975 would only matter for children who were allowed in. A consent decree that says *education and training appropriate to the child’s capacity* is also a warning: capacity talk had been the old exclusion’s costume. The new orders kept the word and tried to strip the costume.

Due process in both decrees — notice, hearing, a chance to contest a label — is the ancestor of the IEP meeting’s least pleasant half. Child find, later a statutory duty, is already in the master’s assignment: go get the children you refused. Essay 30 is that duty after Congress adopted it. Here it is still a court-appointed person with a list.

## What the decrees did not do

They did not hire staff. They did not write a caseload number. They did not decide whether a lisp “adversely affects educational performance.” They did not desegregate a city. They did not make a basement closet into a classroom. A later school SLP who cites PARC as if it were an ASHA practice portal is mixing libraries.

They also did not rename the children. The captions are of their decade. A modern reader can hold two facts: the noun is obsolete and offensive in house style, and the order still has to be read in the noun it used, or you will not find it in the reporter.

Ford’s 1975 signature is the federalization of a door these two courts had already shoved. The speech bag walked through after the door moved. That sequence is the civic fact. The bag does not get to remember itself as the hinge.
