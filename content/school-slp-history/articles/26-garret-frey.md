This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Garret Frey was injured in a motorcycle accident when he was four. He was paralyzed from the neck down and ventilator-dependent. He was also a student in the Cedar Rapids Community School District, Iowa, who succeeded in school when the health supports were there. The district provided some services and refused to provide the continuous one-to-one nursing he needed to stay through the day. Cost, they said, and the medical-service exception.

*Cedar Rapids Community School District v. Garret F.*, 526 U.S. 66 (1999), Justice Stevens for a 7–2 Court, applied *Tatro*. Continuous nursing is not physician-only care. It is nursing. Therefore it can be a related service if it is necessary for the child to benefit from special education. Garret could not attend without it. The Act does not contain a cost exception to that logic. Justice Thomas dissented, worrying about a statute turned into an unfunded hospital.

## Why the caption matters in a speech pack

Iowa is already a sacred state in the sibling profession-history series — laboratories, Travis, Johnson. This caption is a different Iowa: a district arguing about a nurse, a child who uses a ventilator, a Supreme Court that will not let expense redraw Tatro’s line. School speech-language pathologists in Cedar Rapids and everywhere else inherited a clearer related-services neighborhood. If continuous nursing is in, the cheaper items on the 1975 list are not easier to drop by calling them inconvenient.

AAC, suctioning next door, the nurse who also hears the child’s device alarms — the modern complex-needs team is a Garret F. team even when no one says the name. Speech work for a child who needs that team is related in the original sense: supportive of the education the child is otherwise ready to do.

## What the opinion is not

It is not a nursing protocol. It is not a holding that every 1:1 adult is a related service. It is not a biography beyond the facts the Court needed: the accident, the ventilator, the school success, the refused nurse. Later public interviews exist; this pack does not treat them as extra facts the opinion required.

It is not a promise that districts will staff the holding. Unfunded mandates are the dissent’s theme and Ford’s 1975 theme. The Court read the statute Congress wrote, not the appropriation Congress wished it had written.

The residue is a bright line that survived fifteen years and a more expensive fact pattern. Related services remain a list about a child’s presence in the building. The speech closet is one chair in that presence. Garret’s caption is the chair that costs the most and still counts.
