# Curriculum brief — Word lists (`WL`)

**Format code:** `WL`
**Wave:** free clinician PDFs
**Read first:** `../CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md` · `../AGE_BANDS.md` · `../FORMAT_SCOPE.md` · `../STYLE_CLINICIAN_PDF.md`

A word list is a **clinician book page**. It is a short, original compilation of ordinary English, grouped so a session can start without a scavenger hunt.

It is not a student coloring sheet, not a publisher’s “complete program,” and not a diagnostic corpus.

## 1. Job, in one sentence

Hand the clinician a **small, honest** list with a grouping rule they can see, a register tag, and no claim that these words measure a disorder.

## 2. Why this brief exists

Word lists are where SLP products quietly steal from each other. A shuffled Super-Duper-style column is still a copy. SLPWOW lists are **compiled here**, from everyday language, with our grouping sentences.

If you opened a tab to “copy a list faster,” close the tab. Write ten good words you can picture. That is the product.

## 3. Anatomy of a list page

1. **Header** — SKU, grouping rule in plain English, register (`child` / `teen` / `adult` / `mixed`).
2. **How to use (4 lines)**
   - These are options. Cross out freely.
   - Do not read 40 words at a child in one sitting.
   - Derive a `WS` or table-card if the student should see items.
   - Not a screen. Not a normed sample.
3. **The list** — 12–24 words for wave 1 (not 200).
4. **Blank lines** — 4, labeled “clinician adds.”
5. **Footer** — canonical claims.

Phonetic notes stay on the clinician page: `/s/`, `initial`, `2-syllable`. No pasted IPA chart from a textbook scan.

## 4. Grouping rules we allow

Write the rule at the top **in a full sentence**.

Allowed rule types:

- sound position the clinician already cares about (`words that can show a final consonant`)
- syllable shape (`CVC you can picture`)
- everyday theme (`kitchen actions`, `hallway school`)
- sentence-role (`verbs you can mime`)
- register (`workplace objects`)

Disallowed rule types:

- “words that diagnose childhood apraxia”
- “below-average vocabulary bank”
- “the official /r/ hierarchy from [book]”
- “copied from the 2019 Teachers Pay Teachers pack”

A linguistic category (cluster, coda, regular past) is not anyone’s property. A **particular published sequence with their pictures and jokes** is.

## 5. Compilation rules (original)

- Prefer picturable, classroom-safe, non-gimmick words.
- Mix open-class words. Do not fill a page with rare medical nouns to look clever.
- Avoid food-only lists (allergies, feeding history). If a food word appears, it is one among many.
- Avoid slang that punches down.
- Teen lists: hallway, sports gear, bus, locker — not “pretty princess cupcake” unless the teen asked.
- Adult lists: bus pass, form, appointment, neighbor — not cartoon animals.
- No trademarked character names. No brand-name snacks as a gimmick.
- No “funny wrong pronunciation” spellings as the printed target.

**Density:** 12–24 words, set in 12–14 pt, two columns max, generous leading. If you need a magnifier, you wrote a dump, not a list.

## 6. Seed lists (original SLPWOW compilations)

These seeds are **ours**. Use them as the first `WL` pages or as quarries for `WS` items. Do not replace them with a web scrape. You may add clinician-blank lines; you may not paste a 100-word internet list beside them.

### Seed A — `SLPWOW-FR-WL-ART-MX-01`

**Grouping rule:** Short, picturable CVC-ish words a clinician might use when they already care about a **final consonant**. We do not name a mastery age.

**Register:** child / mixed; strike what does not fit.

`bike · book · bus · cake · cup · deer · door · duck · fan · feet · gate · hat · lake · leaf · map · moon · net · nose · pen · rain · sock · sun · tape · web`

### Seed B — `SLPWOW-FR-WL-ART-MX-02`

**Grouping rule:** Everyday words that *can* show an **initial** consonant the clinician names. The page does not choose the consonant for a diagnosis.

**Register:** child / mixed.

`bell · boat · coat · game · home · jump · kite · lamp · nest · park · ring · sand · tent · van · wind · zip · gate · leaf · map · pen`

(Writer: when you set type, **do not** subtitle this “the /b/ list” unless the clinician header is a blank: `Initial sound I am using today: ______`.)

### Seed C — `SLPWOW-FR-WL-VOC-EE-01`

**Grouping rule:** Verbs you can mime in a small room without a special toy kit.

**Register:** early elementary.

`open · close · push · pull · wait · look · find · give · take · stack · wipe · fold · point · share · stop · start`

### Seed D — `SLPWOW-FR-WL-VOC-AD-01`

**Grouping rule:** School-hallway and classroom nouns a middle/high student might actually say.

**Register:** adolescent.

`locker · badge · pass · binder · charger · bus · map · form · deadline · hallway · substitute · project · locker room · advisory · lab`

### Seed E — `SLPWOW-FR-WL-SYN-LE-01`

**Grouping rule:** Regular-past *frames*, not a morphology test. Clinician picks whether past tense is even the target.

`Yesterday I _____ the door.`  
`We _____ the box on the shelf.`  
`She _____ the light.`  
`They _____ the paper.`

Suggested verbs the clinician may drop in (original short set): `closed · stacked · wiped · folded · opened · pointed`

Do not print “Brown’s Stage” numbers.

### Seed F — `SLPWOW-FR-WL-NAR-MX-01`

**Grouping rule:** Neutral story-bones a clinician can point to while the student tells. Not a story-grammar test with cut scores.

`who · where · what happened · then · problem · fix · end · feeling`

These are **prompt words**, not a claim that missing one means a disorder.

## 7. Deriving student pages

A list is the **source**. A worksheet is a **selection**.

- Pick 8–10 words from one seed.
- Add pictures we own.
- Do not put the whole seed on an `EE` student page.
- Keep the same SKU family in the header (`WL` source: `…-WL-ART-MX-01`).

## 8. What never appears on a list page

- Percentiles, “grade level vocabulary,” “age of acquisition: 3;6”
- “If the student misses 6, refer”
- IPA dump from a photographed textbook
- Minimal-pair *characters* from a commercial deck
- A column titled “error / correct / disordered”

## 9. First-wave list SKUs

Ship Seeds A–F as the six SKUs named above. That is the wave. More lists after those six are in the wild and the originality review has been used at least once.

## 10. Reviewer questions (word lists)

1. Can the writer say, without a link, where each word came from? (Answer must be: ordinary English, compiled here.)
2. Is the grouping rule a sentence, not a stolen hierarchy name?
3. Would a teen cringe? If yes and the register is `teen`, rewrite.
4. Is there a blank line for the clinician’s own words?
5. Could a family mistake this PDF for a standardized vocabulary test? If yes, enlarge the “not a test” line and cut any grade-level language.

## 11. Claims and originality

- [ ] Compiled here. Not retyped from the web.
- [ ] 12–24 words (or a handful of frames), not a dump.
- [ ] Register tagged.
- [ ] Blanks present.
- [ ] Footer present.
- [ ] No acquisition ages, no referral rule.
