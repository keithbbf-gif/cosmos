# Curriculum brief — Printouts (`PO`)

**Format code:** `PO`
**Wave:** free clinician PDFs
**Read first:** `../CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md` · `../AGE_BANDS.md` · `../FORMAT_SCOPE.md` · `../STYLE_CLINICIAN_PDF.md`

A printout is a **reference or setup page**. It hangs, sits on the table, or goes home as an idea sheet. The user is not “completing a test form.”

If you added a 20-box grid the student must finish, you wrote a worksheet. Recode to `WS` or cut the grid.

## 1. Job, in one sentence

Give the clinician, partner, or caregiver a **quiet, original** page that supports a session they already planned — cues, choices, a board, a map — without turning the kitchen table into an evaluation.

## 2. Types in the free wave (only these)

| Type | Who holds it | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Clinician cue card | Clinician | Three cues they *may* use; none presented as the one true method. |
| Session map | Clinician + student | Greeting → try → close. Times are optional, not a compliance clock. |
| Partner / AAC board | Partner + person | Core-ish everyday words **we wrote**, plus blank cells. |
| Caregiver optional-try | Caregiver, after clinician send | Three ideas. No daily quota. No “if they fail, call us for a diagnosis.” |
| Room reminder | Clinic wall | One kind instruction (“wait,” “your turn”). Not a behavior rubric. |

Later, not now: color posters as a brand series, lanyard-sized decks, translated editions.

## 3. Age-band cuts (printout-specific)

Printouts often serve an **adult reader** even when the *session* is `EI` or `PK`. Put the band of the **session**, not the reader, in the SKU. Add “adult reader” in the clinician header when needed.

| Band | Printout feel |
|---|---|
| `EI` | One picture, two caregiver lines, huge type. No calendars. |
| `PK` | 3–4 pictures or 3 optional tries. |
| `EE` | Session map + short caregiver page OK. |
| `LE`/`AD` | Text-forward. No baby borders. |
| `AX` | Adult register. “Client,” not “kiddo.” |
| `MX` | Strikeable lines. |

## 4. Page inventory

1. Header — SKU, job, band, “printout — not a worksheet, not a test.”
2. The single object (board, map, cue list, optional-tries).
3. A “clinician chooses / skip any” line.
4. If the page can leave the building: **Send only if the treating clinician chose this.**
5. Canonical footer. Fluency/voice extra line when the domain needs it.

Do not add a scoring table “for completeness.”

## 5. Caregiver optional-try (the only home object in wave 1)

**Title shape:** “Three optional tries — if your clinician sent this.”

**Body shape (original copy — use this structure, not a scraped parenting blog):**

> This page is practice, not a test. Skip any try. Do not keep a score for a teacher unless your clinician asked you to.

> 1. Name it. Point to one picture. Say the word you usually say. Wait.
> 2. Choice. Hold two. Let them pick.
> 3. Your life. Use the same word at the sink or the door, once, if it fits.

**Do not write:**

- “10 minutes daily or they will fall behind”
- “If they miss this, request an evaluation”
- “Screen your child”

Pictures on a caregiver page must be **ours**. Three is enough.

## 6. Partner / AAC board rules

- Everyday words written by us. Example *starter set* (original short set, not a commercial core board clone):

  `want · more · stop · help · wait · my turn · all done · again`

- At least **two blank cells** with a pencil icon (line-art, original).
- A line: “Add the words this person already uses. This board does not measure thinking.”
- Do not title it “low-functioning board” or “nonverbal kit.”
- Do not claim it replaces a comprehensive AAC evaluation.

Layout: 3×3 or 2×4, cells ≥ 1.4 in. Photocopy-safe borders.

## 7. Clinician cue card (original copy)

Print three cues as **options**, not a protocol:

1. Wait. Count a slow three before you talk again.
2. Model. Say the word once. Do not demand a copy.
3. Choice. Offer two. Let them pick.

Add: “Other cues you already use are fine. This card is not a treatment package.”

Do not name a trademarked therapy program. Do not print oral-motor exercise steps.

## 8. Session map (original)

Boxes, not a maze:

```
hello / check-in     →     try some items     →     done
(student’s way)            (clinician’s target)     (student’s way)
```

Under the middle box: “Items live on the worksheet, if you brought one.”

Under the last box: “Done can be a word, a gesture, a device, or leaving.”

No token economy printed as shame (`−2 for stuttering`).

## 9. First-wave printout SKUs

| SKU | Job | Band |
|---|---|---|
| `SLPWOW-FR-PO-CG-EI-01` | One-picture optional try for caregivers | `EI` |
| `SLPWOW-FR-PO-CG-PK-01` | Three optional tries | `PK` |
| `SLPWOW-FR-PO-AAC-MX-01` | Partner board + blanks | `MX` |
| `SLPWOW-FR-PO-MIX-MX-01` | Session map | `MX` |
| `SLPWOW-FR-PO-ART-EE-01` | Clinician cue card for sound practice | `EE` |
| `SLPWOW-FR-PO-FLU-MX-01` | Partner reminder: wait, do not finish the word | `MX` |

`FLU` printout **must** carry the fluency sentence from `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md` §8. Title must not be “Smooth Speech Rules.”

## 10. Voice / hearing / swallow

- Swallow: **no printout** in the free wave.
- Hearing: no “this replaces an audiogram.”
- Voice: a single “kind voice habits” page may list water and shout-breaks as general education. Title: “Kind voice habits (not medical treatment).”

## 11. Claims and originality

- [ ] Not a scraped “homework calendar” from a marketplace.
- [ ] Not a redraw of a named core board product.
- [ ] No quota, no screen, no diagnosis.
- [ ] Footer present and readable.
- [ ] Home pages say the clinician sent them.

## 12. Common miss (reviewer shorthand)

| Looks like | Actually is | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 30-day initial the sun | `WS` + outcome pressure | Cut to three optional tries |
| Rubric 1–5 “social skills” | stealth instrument | Delete numbers |
| Poster “signs of apraxia” | diagnosis bait | Refuse |
| Mascot with a lisp | mockery | Refuse |
