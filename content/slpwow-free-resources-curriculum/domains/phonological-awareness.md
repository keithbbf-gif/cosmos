# Domain brief — Phonological awareness and early print (`PA`)

**Domain:** `PA`
**Formats:** `WS` · `PO` (caregiver optional sound-play) · not a reading-disability instrument
**Authority:** `../CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md` §8. Do not claim to diagnose dyslexia or “readiness.”

These pages are **sound-and-mark games**. A clinician may use them when they already care about sounds in words. A missed mark is not a reading diagnosis.

## 1. Job

The student marks, claps, or sorts **sounds they hear** (and sometimes letters the clinician already decided to show). The clinician tallies today. Nobody prints a readiness age.

## 2. What wave 1 will ship

One worksheet:

**`SLPWOW-FR-WS-PA-PK-01` — Same first sound?**

- 4–6 **picture pairs** we draw.
- Student marks yes / no (or points).
- Clinician says the words. The student should not have to decode a sentence to play.
- Data: `+` `−` `NR` for “matched the job I named” — usually “did they judge same/different the way I was listening for,” which is still **today’s judgment**, not a standard score.

Example pair *ideas* for art (ordinary English; original pairing, not a publisher sheet):

- `sun` / `sock`
- `map` / `moon`
- `hat` / `cup` (different)
- `door` / `duck`

Do not add a fifth pair that is a trick homophone joke.

Optional later (not wave 1): syllable clap, last-sound, blend-as-game. Do not ship a 12-skill “PA assessment.”

## 3. Caregiver printout (if we add one)

Title: “Optional sound play — only if your clinician sent this.”

Copy (original):

> Say two picture words. Ask if they start alike. Guessing is allowed. This is play, not a reading test. Do not keep a score for the school unless your clinician asked.

## 4. Letters

Wave 1 `PK` page is **picture + clinician voice**. Printed letters are optional on the clinician back (“if I already show letters, I might write ___”).

If the student must read `Which word starts with S?` without a picture, you left `PK` and you may have built a reading worksheet. Stop or reband to `EE` and say the job is print.

## 5. Claims fence

Forbidden on the PDF:

- “dyslexia risk”
- “not ready for kindergarten”
- “phonemic awareness deficit”
- cut scores
- copied Elkonin-box pages from a named curriculum (make our own boxes if we ever need them; wave 1 does not)

Allowed:

- “Sound play. Not a reading evaluation.”

## 6. Style

- Huge pictures, fat yes/no marks.
- No classroom-test look (no bubble sheets).
- Footer + “not a screen.”

## 7. Refuse list

- Any page titled “screener”
- A 20-item battery with a referral sentence
- Pasted kindergarten standards as a compliance claim
