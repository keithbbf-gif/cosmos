# Domain brief — Social communication and fluency-friendly pages (`SOC`, `FLU`, `AAC`)

**Domains:** `SOC` · `FLU` · `AAC` (partner support lives here when it is not a speech-sound board)
**Formats:** `WS` · `PO` · `RT` only as a blank snapshot, never a social “score”
**Authority:** `../CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md` §8. Read the fluency and AAC fences twice.

This domain is where free SLP products most often shame people. Our pages stay **optional, plural, and quiet**.

## 1. Shared rules

- Every prompt may be skipped.
- Speech, gesture, writing, and a device are all valid answers unless the clinician **privately** decided otherwise — the PDF does not punish a device.
- No personality rubrics. No “makes eye contact: 1–5.”
- No “smooth speech” prize bars.
- No “autistic social skills fixer” titles.
- Quiet students, multilingual students, and disabled students are not written as problems.

## 2. `SOC` — saying it another way

**SKU:** `SLPWOW-FR-WS-SOC-AD-01`

**Feel:** adolescent study handout. No stars.

**Job:** pick a situation, try a wording **or** refuse.

Original situation seeds (ours — not a scraped social-story pack):

1. You need a pencil and the box is at the other end of the table.
2. A group changed the meeting time and you just found out.
3. You did not hear a direction in a loud room.

For each: three blank lines labeled `one way I could try` · `another way` · `I pass on this one`.

Clinician box:

```
I named a communication job, if any: ________
I am not scoring personality.
```

`PK`/`EE` social pages are **not** in wave 1. Younger social work, if any, is a `PO` session map (`SLPWOW-FR-PO-MIX-MX-01`) plus clinician judgment — not a feelings test.

## 3. `FLU` — fluency-friendly

Fluency pages **do not treat or cure stuttering.** They do not coach “just slow down” as printed student text.

**Worksheet `SLPWOW-FR-WS-FLU-MX-01`**

- Student picks a topic from a short original list (`a game I know · a show I like · a place I go · I brought my own`).
- They talk, type, or show. Clinician tallies **turns taken / topics chosen**, not “stutter-free syllables.”
- Printed: “You may pause. You may start again. You may use your device.”

**Printout `SLPWOW-FR-PO-FLU-MX-01`**

Partner reminder (original):

> Wait. Do not finish the word. Do not say “slow down” unless the treating clinician asked you to use a specific strategy they already taught. This page does not treat stuttering.

**Required footer add-on** (also in guardrails):

> **Fluency note:** This page does not treat or cure stuttering. Do not coach a student to “just slow down” as a printed instruction. The treating clinician chooses any fluency strategy.

Forbidden titles: `Smooth Speech Goals` · `Stop Stuttering` · `Fluency Level 3`.

## 4. `AAC` — partner board

**SKU:** `SLPWOW-FR-PO-AAC-MX-01`

See `../briefs/printouts.md` §6. Starter words we own:

`want · more · stop · help · wait · my turn · all done · again`

Plus two blanks.

Printed:

> Add this person’s words. This board does not measure thinking. It is not an AAC evaluation.

Do not ship “core 100” clones of a commercial board. Do not title it for a diagnosis (`nonverbal autism board`).

## 5. Documentation

If a clinician wants notes, they use `SLPWOW-FR-RT-DOC-MX-01` and write observations. We will not ship a “pragmatic profile” with cut scores.
