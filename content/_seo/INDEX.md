## What a pillar map is for

A **hub** answers one durable question at the depth a first-time visitor needs, then sends them to a specific next page. A **cluster** answers one narrower question and sends them back to the hub (and, only if useful, to one sibling).

That is the whole internal-link design. Everything else is garnish.

Search does not need a spiderweb. A reader who just rooted a cutting does not need a link to Cato’s fig at Carthage *in the same paragraph as the watering schedule*. Put history on the history hub. Put the watering step on the grow page. Cross-link when the next action is real.

## House rules (all lanes)

1. **One primary hub per article.** The first contextual link in the body goes to that hub. Footer “related” is optional and short (two links, not eight).
2. **Hubs list clusters. Clusters do not list every other cluster.** A hub is a table of contents with a point of view. A cluster is a job.
3. **No reciprocal obligation.** If *Rooting methods* cites *Sanitizing cuttings*, the sanitizing page does not owe a matching “also see rooting” block. Link when the sentence needs it.
4. **Anchor text names the destination.** “the coco-coir + DE method”, “Arkansas ASLP-IC status”, “how we pick a hardwood for a tabletop”. Not “click here”, not the exact keyword five times, not the brand name as every anchor.
5. **Do not manufacture “also read” modules that only exist to pass PageRank.** If you cannot say in one spoken sentence why the reader would open the other page *now*, omit the link.
6. **External links are allowed and often better.** ASHA, county Extension, USDA APHIS, a primary paper, a forum thread Keith already trusts — those help the reader. Hiding them to keep dwell time is a tell.
7. **Rewrite before you spawn.** FigRoots and SLP WOW already have thin or leftover theme pages. Fix those before writing a third “intro to X”.
8. **Voice is the family’s.** Keith, Jack, Christina — first person, measured, specific. No “in today’s fast-paced world”, no keyword-stuffed H2s that a person would not say aloud.
9. **Staged here, published there.** This folder is the map. The live sites stay the only public URLs. Do not fork a second blog into COSMOS.

## Cross-lane linking (rare on purpose)

Lanes share a household. They do **not** share an audience most of the time.

| From → to | When it is honest | When it is spam |
|---|---|---|
| Figs → furniture | The glass-front woodshop is *how* potted figs overwinter; a build log that is actually about the bench or the hoop-house frame | “While you’re here, buy a table” on a spider-mite page |
| Furniture → figs | A shop note that the same shed holds trees in January | Every product page linking FigRoots |
| Therapy clinic → SLP WOW | A parent-facing page that says “Christina also writes for clinicians at …” once, in About | Reciprocal sitewide footer |
| SLP WOW → clinic | Never as a default. Only a *local Arkansas access* news item that names the desert of rural services, and even then no patient details | Directory-style “our clinic” on every news post |
| Any lane → AI public | A methods post that used a public model for *transcription of a public paper*, named | “Powered by AI” badges |
| Any lane → supplements | Botanical history of *Ficus* on the fig-history hub may cite the herbal-history hub **once** | Fig grow pages selling capsules |
| Supplements → therapy | Never | Never |
| AI public → COSMOS / cDeck / MOTIF / Crucible / patents | **Never** | **Never** |

If you are unsure, do not cross-link. Same-lane hub ↔ cluster is enough.

## Cadence the household can keep

Five sites at “three posts a week each” is how blogs die and get replaced by AI sludge. Default:

- **One shippable URL per lane per month** (new cluster *or* a real expansion of an existing hub).
- **A second piece** only in that lane’s busy season (see each file).
- **One hub rewrite per lane per quarter**, scheduled, not “when we feel like it”.
- **News-shaped lanes** (SLP WOW, AI industry) may ship short notes in a week that already has a long piece. Short ≠ thin: 400 honest words with a source beats 1,800 of paraphrase.

Rough load if Keith drafts figs / furniture / AI / supplements and Christina owns clinic + most of SLP WOW: **six to eight URLs a month across five properties**. That is the ceiling, not a quota. Skip a month rather than ship filler.

Calendars in the lane files run **October 2026 – September 2027**. Dates are publish *targets*, not contracts. Move a piece when the trees, the shop, or a fee schedule move.

## Shared “do not”

- Do not mention COSMOS, MOTIF, Crucible, the ledger, fencing tokens, or the patent docket on any public lane. The AI file restates this because that lane is the leak risk.
- Do not use patient names, school names, or session detail on therapy properties. HIPAA is not a style choice.
- Do not write disease-treatment claims for herbs or supplements. Structure/function only, with the DSHEA line, or stay in history/methods. See the supplements map.
- Do not scrape OurFigs / FigFanatics threads into “original” posts. Cite, or keep it in the first person from the yard.
- Do not buy links, swap “you link me I link you” with other nurseries, or add sitewide blogrolls of affiliates FigRoots already refused (the intro page says they take nothing from recommended sellers — keep that).
- Do not put Jack on the public internet beyond what he already authors and what the family has already said. He writes; he is not a mascot program.

## How to use a lane file

1. Read **Already live** so you do not rewrite a page that exists.
2. Pick the next row in that file’s month table (Oct 2026–Sep 2027).
3. Draft on the public CMS. Title and slug from the cluster table unless search data says the query is different — then change the table.
4. Add links using that file’s **Linking** section, then check this INDEX so you did not sneak a cross-lane footer.
5. When a hub’s cluster list is stale, update the hub *and* the map in the same sitting.

Proof of work is a live URL, not a green checkbox in this folder.
