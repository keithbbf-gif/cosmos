## Linking

**Inside SLP WOW:** news item → one related older news item *if the law changed*, plus the tools page if you updated a template. Research → tools only when a template changed. No “top 10 posts”.

**Inside WOW Therapies:** every service page → book/contact and the parent/adult “do we need this?” guide. Service pages do not all interlink. School-age does not need a swallow link.

**Between the two sites (tight):**

- Clinic About may name SLP WOW as Christina’s clinician-facing writing, one link.
- SLP WOW About (if you add one) may name the clinic as her private practice, one link.
- A *rural access* news piece on SLP WOW may link the clinic access page.
- No sitewide reciprocal footers. No “partner” badges.

**Never:** clinic → supplements; clinic → FigRoots; SLP WOW → furniture; either site → COSMOS or the AI blog unless the article is *about a public, cited tool* (e.g. a research-transcription method) and even then one link is plenty.

**HIPAA:** no before/after videos of kids, no “sweet 4-year-old with CAS” case studies, no school names. Composite hypotheticals must be labeled as such.
