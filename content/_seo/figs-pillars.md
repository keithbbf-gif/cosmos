# FigRoots — grow + fig history

**Map index:** [INDEX.md](INDEX.md) (linking rules for every lane).  
**Site:** [figroots.com](https://figroots.com/) — “An Informational Site on Growing Figs.”  
**Who writes:** PapaFig (Keith Chambers) and Jack Chambers. Jack already has bylines (types/terminology, 4-H demo).  
**Where else the same people are:** YouTube *fruitaholics*; PapaFig on OurFigs and FigFanatics; Facebook *PapaFig Fico*. Shop/build is a different lane ([furniture](furniture-pillars.md)).  
**Yard (public, 2025 interview):** rural South Arkansas, zone 8a; ~650 figs / 300–350 varieties; most in 3–5 gal pots; thirty-plus in-ground that survived 2024–25; 20×50 greenhouse in work; glass-front 65×15 woodshop used as winter overflow; family trees said to be a century old; UC Davis scions when public requests were still open.

Two pillars, one site. Growers who want a living tree and readers who want the plant’s past should both land on FigRoots and *not* be funneled into a sales pitch. The intro page already promises independence from sellers. Keep that.

## Pillar A — Grow (the how)

**Hub (canonical):** expand `/an-introduction-to-figs/` *or* add `/grow/` that the homepage tile hits, and make the intro the story version. Prefer **one** grow door. Recommendation: keep the intro as the emotional door; build `/grow/` as the practical hub with a 12-month zone-8a checklist and a link list. The intro already says “read more about methods here” — that target should be `/grow/` or `/rooting/`, not a third URL.

**Hub must cover, in this order:**

1. What a good fig actually tastes like (honey / berry / sugar / melon / peach-cherry-orange) — already started.
2. Cutting vs rooted plant vs big-box tree (scam warning stays; BFF/soil-move warning stays).
3. The year in zone 8a pots *and* in-ground (one table).
4. Links out: rooting hub, sanitize, potting mix, up-pot, fertigate, prune, winter, pests, pick-the-right-fig.

Do not put a 300-variety catalog on the hub. The collection is a fact (“we trial a lot”); the hub teaches *how to choose*, then a few named examples.
