# Bibliography

Primary and secondary works the series leans on. Articles cite the specific page or entry they used. Gaps stay marked `[CITE NEEDED]` in the draft.

## County and town

- CALS Encyclopedia of Arkansas, “Bradley County” (entry 750), Bill Norman; rev. David Sesser, 2022.
- CALS Encyclopedia of Arkansas, “Warren (Bradley County)” (entry 840), Jann Woodard.
- CALS Encyclopedia of Arkansas, “Timber Industry” (entry 2143), George W. Balogh; last updated 2026.
- Judge David A. Bradham, county sketch, 1951; reprinted in *Bradley County Family Histories*, *The Eagle Democrat*, mid-winter 1992–93; typed online by Billy R. Sharp, ARGenWeb.
- Rachel Silva, “Walks through History: Historic Downtown Warren,” Arkansas Historic Preservation Program, 14 July 2012.
- Robert Lynn Gatewood, *A Bicentennial History of Bradley County, Arkansas* (Warren and Bradley County Bicentennial Committee, 1976).
- *Bradley County Centennial Magazine* (Warren, 1936 / 1940).
- National Register nomination, Warren and Ouachita Valley Railway Station (BR0011), Arkansas Historic Preservation Program.
- National Register materials, Warren Commercial Historic District.

## Timber, mills, rail

- George W. Balogh, *Entrepreneurs in the Lumber Industry: Arkansas, 1881–1963* (Garland, 1995).
- Corlis C. Curry, “A History of the Timber Industry in Ashley, Bradley, and Drew Counties, Arkansas” (M.A. thesis, University of Arkansas, 1952).
- Kenneth L. Smith, *Sawmill: The Story of Cutting the Last Great Virgin Forest East of the Rockies* (University of Arkansas Press, 1986).
- Thomas D. Clark, *The Greening of the South* (University Press of Kentucky, 1984).
- O. H. “Doogie” Darling and Don C. Bragg, “The Early Mills, Railroads, and Logging Camps of the Crossett Lumber Company,” *Arkansas Historical Quarterly* 67 (Summer 2008).
- ARGenWeb Bradley County place pages: Bradley Lumber Company; Southern Lumber Company (postcard backs and plant notes).
- Rand Wood Products, “Bradley Mill & Millwork Companies History” (company history page; use as a later-owner narrative, not as 1901 proof).
- USDA Forest Products Laboratory, *Wood Handbook*, FPL-GTR-190 (2010) — species behavior only.

## Disaster, labor, civic

- “A Tragic Record: January 3, 1949,” *Eagle Democrat* / ARGenWeb Bradley tornado pages.
- “Thousands View Storm Area,” *Eagle Democrat*, 13 January 1949 (ARGenWeb).
- National Weather Service / Wikipedia compilation, “Tornado outbreak of January 3, 1949” (F4, Warren; death-toll range).
- Saline River News, “Remembering the 1949 Warren tornado 75 years later,” 2024 (later memory; check against 1949 paper).
- Arkansas Democrat-Gazette, “Hardwood sawmills in state feeling strain,” 30 November 2008 (Chambers mill closure).

## BBF / later shop (heritage only)

- Public CV extract, Keith Chambers / Bradley Brand Furniture, Warren — “Solid Hardwood Furniture since 1903”; millwright and tomato-farm work. Use as heritage, not as a catalog.
- Bradley Lumber Company / Bradley Brand Furniture lease (Robbins-Sykes / Pennington Road) — civic geography of a later shop in mill space. Do not quote dollar rent in the essays.
