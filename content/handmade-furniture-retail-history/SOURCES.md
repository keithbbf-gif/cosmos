# Sources and verify notes

Research notes for the staged drafts. Summaries only. Do not paste vendor copy into an episode.

Shop facts used in drafts are limited to what Keith has already said in public interviews or on keithfritz.com. Anything else is marked `hold` or written as industry history.

## Shop, public record

- Shop established 1999. Workshop: 1440 Main Street, Ferdinand, Indiana. Own trucks, made-to-order, showroom representation. [keithfritz.com/about](https://keithfritz.com/about/)
- First shop on Capitol Hill; cold-called designers; first piece at sixteen (Bombay Chippendale secretary, ~1,200 hours); lives above the shop; ~33,000 sq ft nineteenth-century factory; several hundred pieces a year. [John Rosselli artisan spotlight](https://www.johnrosselli.com/artist-spotlight/keith-fritz/)
- Son of a carpenter, raised in Siberia, Indiana; seminary and graduate school; Clinton dining table commission 2001; trade-only showrooms. [Aspire, Maker Monday, 2019](https://aspiremetro.com/keith-fritz/)
- 2011 William Thornton Award writeup: founded 1999; then ~12 craftsmen; ~40 standard pieces; showrooms in Chicago, New York, Boston, Atlanta, Dallas, Washington. [Dubois County Free Press](https://duboiscountyfreepress.com/william-thornton-award-for-excellence-in-craftsmanship-keith-fritz/)
- Public line on online buying: design should be a journey; people need to touch the work. Same Aspire interview.
- Showroom list is live and changes. Do not read a city list on camera without checking keithfritz.com that morning.

## Galleries and craft

- America House opened October 1940 at 7 East 54th Street as a cooperative retail outlet of the Handcraft Cooperative League of America (Aileen Osborn Webb). Later addresses include 485 Madison and 44 West 53rd. Closed 1971. Museum of Contemporary Crafts (now MAD) opened 1956 out of that teaching impulse. [American Craft Council history](https://craftcouncil.org/our-history/); [The Magazine Antiques on America House](https://www.themagazineantiques.com/article/america-house/)
- ACC Northeast craft fair lineage: Stowe 1966, then Mt. Snow, Bennington, Rhinebeck (1973), West Springfield (1984). First Baltimore Winter Market 1970s (300 craftspeople at the Civic Center). ACC history, same page.
- Studio furniture names the drafts may mention as public history, not as our clients: Wharton Esherick, George Nakashima, Sam Maloof, Wendell Castle. Moderne Gallery (Philadelphia, 1984) is a documented secondary-market door for that work.
- Pritam & Eames, East Hampton, opened as a gallery of original furniture; later a smaller functional-craft room. Maker-side color, not a claim we showed there.

## Showrooms and design centers

- Decoration & Design Building, 979 Third Avenue, in service as a design destination since 1965. To-the-trade: browse and buy are different acts. Shop the Building is a later consumer exception (trade price plus a fee). [ddbuilding.com/about-us](https://ddbuilding.com/about-us/); buyer explainer at The Design Lens.
- Same pattern in other cities: Merchandise Mart (Chicago), Design Center of the Americas (Dania Beach), ADAC (Atlanta), Washington Design Center. A design center is not a mall and not High Point.

## Markets

- First formal Southern Furniture Market in High Point: March 1–15, 1909. Southern Furniture Exposition Building opens 1921. Later names: International Home Furnishings Market (1989), High Point Market. Now April and October; trade, not public retail. [Furniture Today timeline](https://www.furnituretoday.com/business-news/the-evolution-of-the-high-point-market/); [NCpedia](https://www.ncpedia.org/international-home-furnishings-mark)

## Etsy era

- Etsy launched June 2005, Brooklyn. Handmade positioning. IPO April 21, 2015.
- October 2013: sellers may hire staff and apply to use manufacturers if they remain the author of the design and disclose the partnership. Contemporary coverage: AP, Wired (April 2014), company blog.
- Vintage bucket, craft-supplies bucket, handmade bucket — three doors, one storefront. 2015 10-K language is the clean public source.
- Etsy Wholesale (mid-2010s) tried to connect growing sellers to retailers. Treat as a chapter, not a current product claim. Verify live status before an update episode.

## Overstock / Wayfair wholesale past

- Overstock lineage: D2-Discounts Direct (Utah LLC, May 1997); site list posted 1998; transactional site March 1999; renamed Overstock.com October 1999; Patrick Byrne majority era 1999. Closeout inventory first; fulfillment-partner / dropship side grew until it overtook the owned-inventory side in the mid-2000s. SEC 424B4 (2002) and later Furniture Today supplier coverage.
- CSN Stores founded May 2002 (RacksAndStands.com, August 29, 2002). Hundreds of niche sites through 2011; consolidation into Wayfair.com and name change late 2011; IPO 2014. Supplier-direct dropship; High Point as a recruiting floor. Wayfair S-1 / S-1/A (2014); How I Built This interview with Shah and Conine.
- 2014 S-1 era claim: millions of products, thousands of suppliers, many of them small shops without a national brand. That is the educational point: the catalog was a network, not a warehouse.
