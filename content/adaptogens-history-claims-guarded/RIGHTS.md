## Banned without a new rights row

- Unredacted supplier COAs, clinic interiors presented as “our lab,” or stock “scientist” photos implying staff.
- AI-generated herbarium fakes passed off as archive scans.
- Hotlinking museum URLs in production without a row in this file.
