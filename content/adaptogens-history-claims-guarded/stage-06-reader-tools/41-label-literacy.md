A bottle is a document. Read it like one.

This is the most practical draft in the folder, and I will still not tell you what to buy. Buying is a person, a clinician, a budget, a taste. Reading is a habit you can take to any aisle, including the aisle you walk away from.

This draft does not treat, cure, or prevent any disease. It is not medical advice. It is a list of questions a historical reader can ask a label without finishing the seller's sentence.

**1. What is the Latin binomial, and is it actually there?**
*Rhodiola* without *rosea* is a mood. *Ginseng* without *Panax* is a theft risk. *Cordyceps* without a species and a method is a theater.

**2. What part?**
Root, rhizome, leaf, berry, seed, fruiting body, mycelium on grain, exudate. If the part is "herb" or "proprietary blend," you are in a fog. Traditional files are often part-specific. Fog is not a tradition.

**3. What form?**
Draft 40. Water, alcohol, powder, marker, dropper, soup. If the label says "ancient method" and shows a QR code, believe the QR code's century.

**4. Which noun is doing the selling?**
The plant, the part, the place, or the class? If ADAPTOGEN is larger than *Eleutherococcus senticosus* radix, the class is the product and the plant is a prop.

**5. Which time is being claimed?**
1947, 1969, 1994, "ancient," a dynasty, a village, a 1990s airport. Two times stacked ("ancient Andean adaptogen") is a red flag for category collapse.

**6. Which system language, if any, is being borrowed?**
*Qi*, rasayana, "stress response," "adrenal." If two systems are on one line, someone is translating without friction (draft 26).

**7. Is there a disease-shaped shadow?**
You will not always see a forbidden verb. You will see a condition-shaped photograph, a before-and-after, a wink. This folder's rule: if the shadow is the point, the document is not a history.

**8. Is identity testable?**
Lot number, species, a lab story that could in principle be checked. I am not certifying labs. I am saying a label with no way to be wrong is a label that cannot be right.

**9. What is the caution, and is it specific?**
"Consult a physician" is a fog caution. Licorice plus silence about a long-known textbook issue is a silence (draft 33). Pregnancy, medicines, blood pressure — if you have a body that is not a marketing demographic, you already know you need a clinician more than you need this list.

**10. Who is missing?**
The Nanai observer chain, the Andean district, the Korean gift box, the person who collected the wild root. Missing people are how a bottle becomes a myth.

You do not have to ask all ten in the store. You will look strange. Ask two. If the label fails the two, you have learned enough.

I want to distinguish this literacy from a certain internet habit of "exposing" every brand. Exposure culture is a cousin of wellness culture: it still wants a hero and a villain and an outcome. Label literacy can end in a shrug. Shrug is an adult result. Not every bottle is a scandal. Some are just thin.

A thin bottle can still be a legal supplement in a DSHEA climate. Legal thinness is allowed. Historical thinness is what we are naming.

If you write labels, these questions are not a gotcha. They are a request to let the document be a document. Put the Latin on it. Put the part on it. Put the form on it. Put the class-word in a smaller font, or leave it off and see whether the plant can stand. If the plant cannot stand, you were selling the class.

If you write essays, do not mock the shopper who has not asked these questions. The dialect (draft 37) was designed to be easy. Ease is a design. Designs can be redesigned by a slower eye.

I have stood in aisles with this list in my head and bought nothing, and I have also bought tea that admitted it was tea. Tea that admits it is tea is the gold standard of a claims-guarded life. Gold standard is a joke. I mean it anyway.

The list will not keep you safe. Only a person who knows your medicines and your history can talk about safe. The list will keep you from confusing a caption with a *bencao*. That confusion is the one this folder exists to interrupt.
