A bibliography is usually a list that pretends to be complete. This one is a list that pretends to be honest.

I have already written `CITATIONS.md` as the short, formal version. This draft is the same honesty in a human voice: what we opened, what we did not, what remains a quarrel, and why a claims-guarded series has to end on sources rather than on a plant that "wins."

This draft does not treat, cure, or prevent any disease. It is not medical advice. It is the file's memory.

**Opened and used as documents**

The EMA HMPC reflection paper (EMEA/HMPC/102655/2007, adopted 8 May 2008). Public PDF. We took its sorting of tonic / stimulant / adaptogen, its complaint about wandering clinical packages, and its refusal of the noun as EU authorisation terminology. We did not take it as a global ban or as a secret blessing.

Panossian and Wagner's 2011 *HerbalGram* review. We took the dating footnote (word not found before 1958 in their search; Lazarev 1957 as their introduction date; 1947 as a congress neighborhood around dibazol and resistance without the later brand-word). We left their efficacy enthusiasm on their page.

Brekhman and Dardymov, *Annual Review of Pharmacology* 9 (1969): 419–430, as the usual English handle for the late-1960s crossing. We noted that some bibliographies drift to 1968 / volume 8.

Selye's 1936 *Nature* note, as a document later writers could point at, not as a license.

DSHEA, 1994, as a climate, not as a section-by-section brief.

The *Shennong Bencao Jing* as a textual tradition, not as an autograph we held.

The Nanai / limonnik chain as a chain (Komarov, Arsenyev, later reviews), not as a field notebook.

Davydov and Krikorian 2000, as a "closer look" genre, not as a verdict we re-tried.

Wagner, Nörr, Winterhoff 1994, and Nörr's 1993 dissertation as listed by the EMA, as evidence that a European bench was willing to seat several plants for a school's exam.

**Not opened**

Vladivostok institute archives. Lazarev's 1947 congress page in the original. The full Russian-language pile that reviews call enormous. Individual trial reports under those reviews. WHO monograph volumes line by line. FDA docket rooms. USPTO files. A Nanai elder. A Korean court manuscript. A Peruvian district ledger. Your medical chart.

If a sentence in this folder would have required one of those, the sentence should have hedged or died. If you find a sentence that did not, mark it. Drafts are for marking.

**Quarrels we refused to flatten**

1947 versus 1957/1958 for the word.

1968 versus 1969 for the *Annual Review* handle.

Whether *adaptogen* is a coherent pharmacological class. The EMA said it is not accepted as ordinary EU authorisation terminology. Defenders keep writing reviews. We kept both facts.

Whether a given plant "meets the definition." Membership is the school's exam, not this folder's prize.

**What a later writer should add, if they have the desk for it**

A real look at the 1947 congress text.

Local-language work on Scandinavian rhodiola use that does not go through a Viking postcard.

A Korean-language *insam* institutional history that is not airport advertising.

A Quechua-and-Spanish maca file that is not a 1990s boom brochure.

A Nanai-language account, if one is offered on its own terms.

A pharmacognosy identity series with vouchers, which this folder is not.

**What we will not add**

A winner. A stack. A dose. A "true adaptogen" list. A disease. A testimonial. A lede about your office.

I want to end the series on a feeling that is not triumph. The feeling is: **the file is longer than the noun.** Chinese materia, Ayurvedic shelves, Andean food, a Soviet bench, a Brussels PDF, a U.S. statute, a dropper, a courtyard basil. The noun tried to be a suitcase for all of it. Suitcases crush. We unpacked.

If you only remember one citation, remember the 2008 reflection paper and the 2011 dating footnote. A refusal and a quarrel. That pair is enough to stop the flat explainer. Stopping the explainer is not as good as a cure. We did not have a cure to give. We had a fence, a voice, and forty-five drafts that tried to keep both.

The bibliography will go stale. Stale is allowed if the honesty does not. When a new document appears, add it by kind and by year, and do not let it finish a disease sentence. That is the whole method. It is not a school. It is a habit.

I am done selling you a method. I am done, almost, talking. The claims box is the last fence post. After that, the folder is yours to mark up, and not yours to turn into a label.
