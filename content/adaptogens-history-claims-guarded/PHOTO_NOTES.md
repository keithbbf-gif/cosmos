# Photo and figure notes

Text-first series. Figures support **history and vocabulary**, not product aisles.

## Three classes

1. **License-cleared rasters** — botanical plates and specimen photographs logged in `RIGHTS.md` / `_download_meta.json`.
2. **Original SVG schematics** — timelines, vocabulary maps, family trees. Always captioned *schematic; not to scale*.
3. **No third class yet** — no in-house orchard or herbarium stills in this pass.

## Caption discipline (claims-safe)

- Describe what the reader is looking at (plate, specimen, timeline anchor).
- Do not imply efficacy, safety, or “ancient proof” of a modern label claim.
- “After Jartoux, 1713” is honest; “photograph of a 1713 forest” is not.
- Alt text = figure content, not marketing (“supports healthy stress response” is banned).

## Embed format

Use captioned `<figure class="blog-figure">` blocks from `staged/FIGURE_EMBEDS.md`. Paths are relative to each draft file under `stage-*/`.
