# PHOTO_NOTES — `D:\FIGS` first

User stills on **KC-PC** live under `D:\FIGS`. Those folders are the first pick
for every slug in this pack. Public-domain fills are a last resort and only
after a miss. Do not scrape nursery catalogs. Do not use `.dtrash`. Prefer
stills over `Fig & Garden Videos` / `2025 Fig Videos`.

This file is the **shoot / pick brief**. A Commons greenhouse does not replace
our hoop. WordPress stays Draft. These notes are for the media library, not a
publish button.

## Rules that do not bend

1. **Ours first.** Hunt the folders below before Commons or USDA.
2. **Named folders are not proof of type.** Caption the clone we have. Do not
   upgrade the tag.
3. **No yield banners.** Do not watermark “5×” or “230 lb.” Those numbers stay
   in prose with a citation.
4. **PD fills** need URL + license in the media caption, and the sentence
   “not our house.”
5. **Do not invent a gothic kit, a roll-up crank, or a woodchip burial** if the
   folder does not have it. Shoot it or skip it.
