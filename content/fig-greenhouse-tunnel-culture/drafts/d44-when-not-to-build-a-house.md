A house is a tool. Tools have cases where the right move
is the drawer. I will name the drawer.

Do not build if you have never eaten a ripe fig off a
tree you kept alive outdoors. Learn the fruit. Learn the
water. Learn the name. A hoop will not teach you those
faster. It will teach you vents and a new way to fail.

Do not build if a south wall and Chicago Hardy or
Improved Celeste already finish in your 8a or 9a year.
You may still want a rain roof you can strip. You do
not want a gothic lifestyle. The wall is cheaper and it
does not rust in still air unless you invent a thicket.

Do not build if your only plants are pots you can
shuffle. FigRoots already gave you the room: garage,
shed, greenhouse above 40°F. That is a shop. A 20-foot
kit for three #3s is a place to trip.

Do not build if you will not be the crank. A sealed
house is a cooker. An unvented spring cover is a
funeral. A frost night you ignore is mush. If your year
is travel, plant outdoor names and ask a neighbor to
water. Do not ask a neighbor to dance a row cover.

Do not build on a lake. Do not build in a pecan shade.
Do not build with construction sheeting and a heater on
a cord. Do not build because a California photo made
you lonely.

Zone 7 is the zone most allowed to build, and still
should not if the winter method will not happen. A hoop
without a bury or a second cover is Zhi’s first night.
Zone 9 should almost default to the drawer. Build a
skeleton you can strip if rain is the thief. Do not
build a bag. 8a should build only for a named failure:
October green fruit on names you already like, or a
collection that is too many cans for the shop door, or
a short market test you will record. If you cannot name
the failure, you are building a hobby. Hobbies are
allowed. Call them that. Do not call them culture.

I have wanted a house because I wanted a project. The
fig did not ask. The fig asked for a hole that drains,
6–8 hours of sun, a name that finishes, and a winter
that is a season. Those are outdoor sentences. They
still apply under film. If they are not true in the
yard, they will not become true in a bag.

If you are on the fence, spend one year on a cattle
panel over one plant, or a blanket and a wall, or a
better shop door. Cheap experiments tell you whether
you are a crank person. Steel will not make you a
crank person. It will make you a person with steel.

The pack is not a dare. It is a map of a tool. Maps
include the edge. This is the edge. Stay in the yard
if the yard already feeds you. That is not a failure.
That is a fig doing the job without a roof.

If you are building to impress a forum, stop. A wall
fig that feeds you is more culture than a kit that
feeds a thread. I have wanted the thread. The fig did
not care. The fig cared about a hole and a name and a
night. Those are still free of steel.

A used kit you will not vent is still a no. Price does
not change the crank.

The drawer is allowed to be the last word. A fig on a wall is already culture. Steel is optional. Optional is a kindness. Take it.
