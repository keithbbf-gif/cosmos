The roof is a sour-killer if sour was rain in the eye.
That is a real sale. Open-eye figs in a humid August are
cups. A dry sky over a tight-eye Celeste is nicer. A dry
sky over an open-eye honey fig is still a cup if the
humidity is a gym and a beetle already opened the door.
I will not tell you plastic makes ostioles close.

Split is a swing. Dry, then wet, then the skin cannot
keep up. The soil pack already refused to cargo-cult
California 55% ETc onto an Arkansas #3. Under a roof the
swing is usually you. You forgot the pot, then you made
up for it. You shut the drip off to “ripen,” then you
panicked. The sky is innocent. Congratulations. You are
the weather.

Keep the cans even *before* the fruit goes soft. Do not
catch up the day it jams. Skip the sprinklers. Pick what
is ready. After a binge, do not binge again to apologize.
Zhi said fruit needs immediate pick. In a house that
sentence is the whole IPM program for sour: pick, do not
leave a ferment on the arm to inoculate the next one.

Teramo split for Boyer under plastic. A name that is
tight outdoors can still split if the hose is a mood.
Tight-eye helps sour. It does not insure split. Even
Chicago will crack if you drought it and then flood it
because you felt like a farmer.

Zone 7 split hurts more because the window is short. You
do not get a second month of the same name. Zone 9 can
sour and split in the same week and still have October.
8a gets the afternoon storm on a softening crop — except
under a roof you get the afternoon *mood* on a softening
crop. Same fruit, different author.

Birds and beetles still sour a fig. The roof is not a
screen unless you screened it. A door left open is a
festival. I will write them later. Here: a sour fig with
a hole is not a rain problem. Do not “fix” it with less
water. Fix it with a pick and a door.

If you came to a tunnel only to stop rain-split, you may
have bought the right tool. Operate it like a tool. Even
water. Tight-eye names in the humid South. Pick now. Do
not run a deficit program you read on a Central Valley
PDF. Do not run a flood because the leaf cupped at 2 p.m.
in a closed hoop — that leaf wanted air. The fruit wanted
yesterday’s even sip, not today’s apology.

I have thrown a flat of split figs to the chickens and
called it a learning. The learning was: I was the storm.
The plastic did its job. I did not. That is the honest
caption. The house keeps the sky honest. It does not keep
you honest. That is still your job.

I pick into a shallow flat, one layer, because a pile
of ripe figs is a sour factory even under a dry roof.
The house stopped the rain. It did not stop the weight
of six figs on one fig. I have made that factory. I
do not make it on a frost-night walk when every fruit
matters.

If a name splits every year under even water, the name
is the swing. Evict it from the row. Let it holiday in
a can. The steel is too expensive for a fruit that
opens itself.

A dry roof is a sour-killer for rain. It is not a character reference for the operator. I am the weather now. I try to be a boring one.
