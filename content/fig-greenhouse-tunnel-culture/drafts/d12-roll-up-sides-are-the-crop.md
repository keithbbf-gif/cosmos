If you ask me what to buy after the steel, I will not say a
heater. I will say a side that opens the whole length of the
house and a crank you will use when it is 95°F and you have
something else to do. Boyer wrote that by early June the doors
were open almost every day, the fan was running, and the
sidewalls were up for real air. That was Pennsylvania. Our June
is not a suggestion.

A fig under film without a side is a tomato without a vent. The
leaf cooks. The rust likes the stillness. The mites like the
dust and the heat. People then fertilize, because yellow looks
like hunger. Yellow in a sealed hoop in July is often a lung
problem. Open the lung before you feed the patient.

I want the side to roll above the fruiting wood, not a little
window at the knee. A knee window is a gesture. A full roll is
a climate. If the kit only gives you a knee, you are buying a
hobby greenhouse and calling it a tunnel. Zone 9 should be able
to take the house to a skeleton. Zone 7 should be able to open
a May that hit 85°F after a 28°F night. 8a needs both tricks
in the same month sometimes.

Doors at the ends are not sides. Ends move a little air down
the middle. The edges still sit. Figs you planted along the
edge — and you will, because that is where the hose reaches —
will live in a different county than the fig in the aisle.
Roll the long walls. Then the edge is a county you can visit.

Automation is a luxury and a failure mode. A 24-volt opener
that sticks closed on a Sunday is a cooker with a warranty. I
would rather have a crank I trust and a habit I wrote on the
calendar than a gadget I cannot fix in the dark. If you
automate, automate *open* as the safe failure, not closed. A
house that opens when the power dies is a house that might
still have leaves on Monday. Keith does money. I do not write
a shopping list for motors. I write the rule: the side is the
crop.

Fans help a long house. They do not replace a side. A fan in a
closed bag is a hair dryer. Pair it with an opening or do not
bother. I have stood in a hoop with a box fan and a closed
wall and felt the same wet heat circulate like a rumor.

The winter personality is the opposite hour. Sides down, doors
managed, a second cover on the wood if the night is ugly. The
skill is not “sides always up” or “sides always down.” The
skill is a hand on the crank when the forecast changes. That
is the same skill as the fig shuffle, just vertical. People
who want a house with no hand should not build a house.

If a later editor cuts this pack to five drafts, keep this one.
A tunnel that cannot breathe is not a fig method. It is a bag.
I will not help you grow a bag.
Gear the crank so a tired person can use it. A crank that takes a
hero is a crank that stays down. I have left a side down because the
handle was a joke and the day was already a joke. That is on me and
on the handle. Spend on the handle.

In fruit week I open the side even when the morning is cool, then
I watch the night. Cool air on a dry fruit is a quality tool. Closed
air on a wet fruit is a sour tool. The crank is how you pick which
tool you are holding. Hold it like it is the crop. It is.
