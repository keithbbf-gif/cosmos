FigRoots refused indoor heat for a reason I will not
soften. Figs want a dormant period. A winter shop above
40°F is a cold room, not a living room. The plants should
look like sticks. If they look like a florist window, you
are growing a different crop and calling it kindness.

A fruit house has the same winter assignment if you want
wood that is wood. Let the leaves drop. Stop the feed.
Taper the water. Let the night be a night, then add a
blanket to the *plant*, not a thermostat to the sky.
Boyer heated the ground with a cover, not the volume with
propane. Zhi’s barrels did not make a dormancy optional.
They barely made a night milder.

What dormancy is not: a punishment. It is how last year’s
wood finishes being last year’s wood. Unlignified tips
are a freeze waiting. A house that keeps a flush going
into December is manufacturing those tips. I would rather
see a hoop full of gray arms in January than a hoop full
of lime-green hopes.

Zone 7 must have dormancy and must also protect wood.
Those are not opposites. Sleep plus a blanket. Sleep plus
a bury. Sleep plus a 40°F room for cans. Not sleep plus a
60°F glass house and a grow light. Zone 9 must have
enough of a cool season to stop the plant. A 9A hoop that
never gets below 50°F at night in January is a tropical
room. Open it. Let the year be a year. 8a is a cold room
for cans and an honest night for in-ground, with a
blanket on the ugly forecast.

The shop leaks. A water heater, a south wall, a stack of
cans too tight, a winter that never goes below 45°F
inside — those wake buds in January. Then you have a
flush and a 20°F night and a mess. Move cans off the
sun stripe. Crack a vent on a 70°F Thursday. Do not
fertigate a stick. Jack can have a mat for cups. The
orchard pots do not get the mat. Cups are trying to root.
Pots are trying to sleep.

I do not strip every leaf on a date. I let the plant
finish the argument with shortening days. If a name hangs
onto leaves in a warm hoop, I stop the kindness until it
lets go. Water becomes a sip. The door stays colder. The
plant will take the hint or it will tell me it is a Zone
10 guest. Guests go to the shop and follow shop rules.
They do not set the orchard thermostat.

If you remember one winter sentence: sticks are correct.
Green is a question. The house is allowed to be
uncomfortable. You can wear a jacket. The fig cannot put
one on. Give it a season, then give the wood a blanket if
the night will steal it. That is dormancy plus a method.
That is not a heated fantasy.

I will not run Christmas lights as a lifestyle in a
house I already built to be a windbreak. FigRoots
allowed lights as a few degrees on a wrap. A hoop plus
lights plus 50°F nights is a wake. Lights are for a
headline outdoor wrap, not for a shop I should be able
to keep cold with a door.

If a name will not drop leaf, I move that can to the
coldest corner and I stop the sip until the argument
ends. Persistent leaves are information. They are not
a reason to heat the room to keep them pretty.
