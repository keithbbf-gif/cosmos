# Staging sheet

Every file in `drafts/` carries YAML:

```yaml
status: staged
stage: name | raise | set | run | keep
voice: human
focus: 8a
zones: [7, 8a, 8b, 9]
cluster: house | siting | plant | climate | winter | crop
```

`status: staged` means the piece has had a voice pass and is waiting on a
human. It is not `published`. It is not `scratch`.

## Read order (if you want a crop, not a kit)

1. `d01` through `d08` — what the house is, and what the papers already said
2. `d09` through `d16` — how you put it up without building a cooker or a lake
3. `d17` through `d26` — what you plant, how far, how you train it
4. `d27` through `d36` — the year inside: early wake, frost, cook, rust, hose
5. `d37` through `d48` — winter wood, harvest, market, the 14×32, when to walk away

## Required topics (coverage)

- Three structures: greenhouse, hoophouse, high tunnel — and the fig shuffle
- Zone 7 winter wood, Zone 8a in-between, Zone 9 summer cook
- Siting, gothic vs Quonset, plastic, roll-up sides, drainage
- Varieties that finish, spacing, in-ground vs pot, cordon / fan / two-branch
- Early bud-break, late frost, row-cover dance, shade cloth, rust
- Dormancy, woodchips, hoop-in-hoop, harvest, market floor, when not to build

If a later editor cuts the set down, keep at least one draft from each stage
and do not cut `d02` (three houses), `d08` (the papers), `d12` (roll-up sides),
`d20` (in-ground vs pot), or `d37` (dormancy).
