# Staging sheet

Every file in `drafts/` carries YAML:

```yaml
status: staged
stage: take | mark | pack | hold | ship | land
lane: buyfigs
voice: human
cluster: harvest | label | pack | dormant | ship | receive | compliance
```

`status: staged` means the piece has had a voice pass and is waiting on a
human. It is not `published`. It is not `scratch`.

## Read order (if you want a season, not a pile)

1. `d01` through `d10` — take the wood
2. `d11` through `d18` — mark it so March can still read it
3. `d19` through `d28` — pack it so the mail does not invent a new grade
4. `d29` through `d33` — hold it without pretending the cooler heals
5. `d34` through `d42` — ship plants, or refuse
6. `d43` through `d46` — land on the other bench

## Required topics (coverage)

- Packing: damp-not-wet, bundles, crush, retail sleeve vs wholesale brick, pots vs wood
- Labeling: name-before-cut, three labels, lot codes, synonyms, slips
- Dormant season: window, lignified wood, cooler temps, weekend totes, culls
- Shipping plants: weather, heat packs, Friday/Monday, carriers, soil/state paper
- Explicitly **not** human medicine

If a later editor cuts the set down, keep at least one draft from each
stage and do not cut `d12` (three labels), `d19` (damp is not wet),
`d35` (weather is a switch), or `d45` (this lane is not medical).
