Some inventory has to go in the burn pile. That is part
of the grade. I say it ugly because the tote will say it
pretty: “still usable,” “someone will take it,” “we
already cut it.” Those sentences are how a nursery
becomes a rumor.

Throw: fuzz I can see, a sour smell, mush after a cold
snap, a mix I cannot unmix, a name I killed in the row,
wood that woke and then sat, a bag that made rain and
then sat in it. Do not put those in a bulk mix. Bulk is
not a laundry cycle.

I will root borderline wood in *our* cups. That is not
the same as shipping it. Our cups can fail in private.
A wholesale tray fails in public, then fails again when
they sell it.

Jack wants a throw week on every tote. I back him when
the week comes and I want to bargain. Bargaining is my
vice. The burn pile is the adult.

If I throw a lot, I write it. Variety, lot code, why.
Not for theater. For the next list. If Celeste P2 threw
twice, P2 has a problem or I have a pack problem. The
notebook tells me which one I keep blaming.

Do not donate thrown wood to a “free cuttings” box at
the gate. Free is how bad names travel farther than
paid ones. If it is not fit to sell, it is not fit to
gift as a named fig. Unknown firewood is still firewood.

I have stood over a tote of a fashionable dark fig and
felt the exact pain of burning money. I burned it. The
year I did not, I spent the money anyway, plus the
year. The burn was cheaper.

Receivers: if you throw my wood, tell me with a photo
in the clock. I want to know. I do not want a polite
silence and a vanished customer. Silence teaches me
nothing. A photo of fuzz teaches me the bag.

The hold stage ends in two places: the carrier and the
fire. Both are honorable. The third place — the maybe
shelf — is how winters go rotten. We do not keep a
maybe shelf.

I burn or bin, I do not compost named wood into a pile
a visitor can raid. Visitors are hopeful. Hopeful people
will take a slimy stick and give it a European name they
heard in a group. I will not be the quiet source of that
name. The pile is closed. The notebook says why.

If throwing a fashionable lot hurts, I write the dollar
number next to the reason. The number fades. The reason
should not. Next December I want the reason, not a
mood about money I already spent.

I do not let a visitor watch a throw and then bargain for the pile. Bargaining at the fire is how a name leaves in a pocket. The fire happens when the yard is ours.

The throw week on the tote is a date, not a mood. When the date comes, the tote does not get a speech.

I do not take a souvenir stick from a throw tote. Souvenirs become secret SKUs.
