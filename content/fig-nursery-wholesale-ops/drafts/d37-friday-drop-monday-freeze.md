A Friday drop into a Monday freeze is how you donate
cuttings to a loading dock. Tracking will look fine. The
wood will not. I have watched a scan sit in a sort
facility through a football Sunday and come out as a
science project.

Our default: no Friday ships into a weekend that will
be colder than I like or warmer than I like, and no
Friday ships I cannot defend if the truck misses. A
Thursday ship that arrives Friday to a person who is
home is a different animal. A Friday ship that arrives
Saturday to a closed nursery and then sits until
Tuesday is the animal I do not feed.

Holiday weeks are Friday in costume. The carrier’s
calendar is not my calendar. I look at theirs. If they
are about to sit, I sit first, in the cooler, with a
card.

I will take a Saturday delivery if the receiver asked
and will be there. I will not assume Saturday is a
favor. Saturday on a porch at 12°F is a dare.

The dock does not love you. The dock loves volume. Your
small carton of sticks is not a priority in a weather
event. Do not write a love story about Priority Mail
overnighting your feelings. Buy the service you bought,
then still refuse the week.

Jack packs faster on Monday and Tuesday on purpose. The
middle of the week is the honest hop. Wednesday I look
at the model again. Thursday I get picky. Friday I get
stubborn. Stubborn is a ship skill.

If a buyer demands Friday because they are leaving town
Monday, I say no and I offer to hold. Their travel is
not a reason to use the dock as a hotel. Their garage
is a better hotel if they are actually there.

Keith has printed this on the shop door. People still
ask. Asking is allowed. The door still says no.

When I have broken this rule, I remember the photo. I
do not remember the pickup I made on time. The photo
is the teacher. The dock is the classroom I am trying
to skip.

I look at the carrier’s holiday page, not at my hope.
Hope says “it will move.” The page says the sort will
sleep. I believe the page. If the page is quiet, I
still treat Sunday as a sit. Sunday sits have cooked
more wood than any Tuesday storm I actually watched.

A buyer who needs Friday can have Thursday and a hold
at their shop, or they can have next week. Those are
the products. Friday-into-a-warehouse is not a product
I sell. I have sold it. I remember the photo. I do not
need a second copy.

If I am behind on Thursday, I do not catch up by
becoming a Friday person. Behind is a list problem. I
cut the list, I do not cut the door rule.

I treat a Friday pickup after 3 p.m. as already Saturday. Late Friday is a sit with extra steps. I would rather look idle on Thursday night.
