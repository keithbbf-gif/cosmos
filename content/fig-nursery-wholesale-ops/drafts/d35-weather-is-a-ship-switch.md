The forecast can cancel a paid order. That is cheaper
than a cooked box. I have to say it before the week
starts, in the listing and in the invoice notes, or I
will feel like a thief when I hold a payment over a
Monday. I am not a thief. I am the person who looked
at 8°F in Chicago and 70°F in a Memphis sort and did
not like either one.

Ship/no-ship is a switch, not a mood. I look at the
origin week and the destination week. I look at the
hop — two days is not five days. I look at weekends
the carrier will sit. If any of those is a killer, I
do not “try.” Trying is how you donate cuttings to a
loading dock.

Cold that stays in the single digits with wind is a
hold for unrooted wood unless I have a pack I trust
and a receiver who will meet the box. Heat that sits
a truck at 90°F is a hold even in March. Dormant wood
is not immortal. It is asleep. Sleep still cooks.

I will refund or rebook. I will not dare. Daring is
for our own cups. Wholesale daring is someone else’s
tray.

The switch also flips after I packed. If I packed on a
Wednesday and Thursday’s model fell apart, the box can
wait in the cooler. A packed box is not a doomed box.
A scanned box is closer to doomed. Do not scan to feel
progress.

Keith has held a week of Ohio boxes because the lake
effect was lying in two directions. We looked idle on
the shop floor. We looked honest in April. I prefer
April.

Write the rule where a buyer can see it: we ship when
the week is safe enough, we do not ship into a holiday
warehouse, we do not ship a Friday into a freeze. Then
keep it. A rule you break when you are behind is not a
rule. It is a decoration.

Receivers can flip the switch too. If they will be gone,
they should say so. A box on a porch at 12°F is not a
ship success because tracking says delivered. Delivered
is a location. Alive is a condition.

If you only install one ship habit, install the switch
and give it more power than the pickup time. The truck
will come again. The name will not, if you cook it.

I write the week’s switch on the door in marker: SHIP
or HOLD, plus the reason in five words. `HOLD lake
effect OH` is enough. If I have to write a paragraph,
I am bargaining. Bargaining belongs in the notebook,
not on a carton.

If two destinations disagree — Texas easy, Minnesota
ugly — I split the week. I do not let the easy box
launder the ugly box onto the same pickup because I
want one errand. One errand is not a climate.

I check origin and destination, not just the pretty national map. A pretty map hides a county. The county is where the porch lives.
