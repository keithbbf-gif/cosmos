A red zip tie without a card on the wall is how two varieties
become one argument. I like color. I can see color at ten feet
when I cannot read my own print. Color is a servant. The minute
it becomes the name, it starts lying.

We use ties as a *sort* — all the Celeste totes get one color
that week so I do not grab the wrong lid in a hurry. The color
is for the hour. The words are for the year. At pack-out the
tie can stay if the bundle card is there. The tie cannot stay
*instead*.

I reprint the color legend every Monday of ship week and tape
it inside the cooler and inside the first box of a mixed order.
If I change Celeste from red to blue because we ran out of red,
the old legend is a weapon. I date the legend. I throw the
old one. I do not have five legends in a drawer. That drawer
is how we shipped a “blue” as the wrong dark fig in a year I
will not name.

Never use color alone across years. You will not remember that
2025 green was Bordeaux and 2026 green is a different dark.
The buyer will not remember. Their employee in April will not
remember. Words remember.

I do not buy twenty colors to be clever. I buy four and I reuse
them as hour-codes, not as a pantone of the collection. The
collection is hundreds. Pantone is a hobby. We already have a
hobby.

Paint on the basal end is the same sermon. A dab of white to
mark basal is polarity, and I already have a square cut for
that. A dab of “variety color” is a legend I will lose. I skip
it.

If a wholesale customer wants their own color system in the
box, they can send ties and a card. I will apply *their* legend
and put *our* words on anyway. Two systems can ride. One
system made of vibes cannot.

The test: hand the closed tote to Keith and walk away. Can he
tell you the name without opening a chat history? If he needs
the chat, the label failed. If he needs the color wall from
last year, the label failed. If he reads the card, we get to
keep doing this.

I still use the red tie. I am not a monk. I just do not let
the tie talk in court.

Monday of ship week I throw last week’s color card even if
the colors did not change. The throw is the habit. A card
that might be right is how last year’s green becomes this
year’s problem. A fresh dated card takes one minute. A
wrong dark fig takes a year.

I also refuse to use red for “hot variety.” Red is a sort
color, not a fame color. Fame on a tie is how a helper
grabs the fashionable bag without reading. Read the card.
The tie is just a flag on the hour.

If the wall card and the lid card disagree, I do not pick a favorite. I reprint both from the notebook and I throw the fighters. Fighters are how a helper grabs blue and means last week.
