This lane teaches packing and shipping. It does not
treat people. I am putting that in a draft of its own
so a later editor cannot “add a health section” because
figs are fruit and fruit content scores.

We will talk about plant sanitation — a clean blade, a
dry bag, a throw pile — because those keep a nursery
from mailing a mess. We will not talk about a human
body. No blood sugar, no wellness, no leaf tea as a
remedy, no “figs are good for you” paragraph at the
end of a pack list. That paragraph is how a cuttings
folder grows a second, sloppier job.

Buyfigs is a trade lane. The reader is a person with a
tote, a cooler, a carrier account, a state they might
not be able to enter. They need counts, damp paper,
lot codes, and a Friday rule. They do not need a
pharmacy voice.

Jack’s FigRoots grower copy already refuses medical
theater. This folder matches that refuse and makes it
louder, because wholesale copy gets scraped into
marketplaces that will add any adjective. I will not
write the adjective for them.

If a draft in this set sounds like a supplement, it
failed the stage sheet. Strike it. If a photo caption
wants to say a fruit is medicine, change the caption
to a plate or delete it. Plates are food. Food is not
this folder either, except as the reason we leave wood
on a mother.

I will teach a receiver how to open a box. I will not
teach them how to heal. Those verbs are not cousins.

The keep list on the stage sheet names this draft on
purpose. If you cut the set down, keep a copy of this
boundary. The rest of the winter can be rewritten. The
boundary should not get “lightly edited” into a health
tip.

We ship plants. We pack plants. We name plants. We
refuse weeks. That is enough work for a season. It is
already more than most listings do. I do not need a
second profession in the footer.

A test I use on a finished draft: could this paragraph
be pasted onto a vitamin page and still sound at home?
If yes, it is out. Could it be pasted onto a packing
table and still sound like Jack or me? If no, it is
out. The vitamin test is mean and useful.

I also refuse “fig leaf tea” as a cute closer. Cute
closers get scraped. Scraped closers become ads. Ads
become a mess I did not pack. The closer on this folder
is a box, a cooler, a Friday door. That is enough
voice.

If a partner lane wants food or history, they can link
out. They cannot rewrite this footer into a clinic. I
will argue that in an edit. I would rather argue here
than after a scrape.

If I catch myself writing “good for you,” I stop the draft. That phrase is a wander even when I mean the tree. I mean the tree. I write the tree.

The footer stays plants. If it drifts, I cut the footer, not the rule.
