Some states want paper. Some want no soil. Some want
you to stay home. I do not memorize a swagger version
of that sentence and then ship. I **[VERIFY]** the
destination the week I ship, because quarantine lists
move and my memory is a 2022 PDF.

Hawaii is the name people know. California is the name
people argue about. Other states have nursery-stock
inbound rules, noxious lists, and inspection holds. The
National Plant Board summaries exist so I do not invent
a United States out of Arkansas habit. I still have to
read the one that matches the address on the box.

Soil is the frequent stopper. A dormant unrooted
cutting taken well above the dirt line is a different
object than a gallon of our clay. Hawaii’s California-
bound notes even talk about unrooted cuttings taken at
least twelve inches above ground in one nematode
program **[VERIFY]** — that is *their* paper, not a
universal fig rule, and it is about a specific route.
I quote it only to show how specific this gets. I do
not turn it into a tattoo on the shop wall and call
every state done.

I will refuse a pot into a place I cannot satisfy. I
will offer wood instead if wood is allowed. I will not
wash a pot in the driveway and call it bare-root if the
roots still wear our dirt like a sock. Bare-root is a
real job, done on purpose, dried right, packed right.
It is not a hose and a lie.

Jack does not get to override a refuse because the pack
is ready. Ready is not permitted. Permitted is
permitted.

I will not crowdsource a yes from a marketplace thread.
Threads are old. The destination agency is current.
If I cannot find a current yes, the box waits or the
money returns.

Marking: who from, who to, what it is, where it grew,
when they ask. Certificates: only the ones an inspector
actually issued. A sentence in my listing is not a
certificate. See the next draft.

This is not legal advice. It is a refuse habit. The
habit has kept us out of stories I do not want to be
in. I like boring winters. Boring winters still sell
wood, just not to every zip code on every Thursday.

I keep a dated scrap of the last time I checked a hard
state, plus the page I read, in the ship binder. The
scrap is not a certificate. It is a reminder that I
looked. If the scrap is old, I look again. Old scraps
are how 2022 becomes a personality.

If a buyer says “everyone ships here,” I still look.
Everyone is not an inspector. Everyone is also not
the person whose name is on our carton. Our name is
the one I am spending.

I will not let a helper add a pot to a cuttings carton because there was room. Room is not a permit. Room is air I should have filled with paper.

If I am unsure by noon, the pot does not get a label by three. Unsure is a hold.
