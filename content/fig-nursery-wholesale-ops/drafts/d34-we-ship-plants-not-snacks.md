We ship plants. We do not ship snacks, and we do not
ship medicine. I put that at the front of the ship
stage because the internet will try to drag a fig box
into a kitchen or a pharmacy the minute you talk about
packing.

This lane is buyfigs: dormant wood, liners, the rare
potted plant, the paper a state might ask for, the
weather that cancels a pickup. It is not a dried-fig
recipe book. It is not a wellness blog. If you want
foodways, that is another folder. If you want a human
body claim, you are in the wrong shop.

USPS and the other carriers will move plants when the
movement is lawful. Publication 52 / Publication 14
are the mail side: plants can be nonmailable when a
quarantine says so **[VERIFY]**. I do not treat a
Priority label as a permit. A label is postage. A
permit is a person with a stamp from an agency.

The box gets words the street needs: who from, who to,
what is in it — dormant fig cuttings, no soil, or a
potted fig, soil, whatever is true. Some destinations
want origin on the carton. California’s marking rule
is the example people quote (Food and Agricultural
Code §6421) **[VERIFY]** if that is actually where
it is going. I do not copy a California sentence onto
every box as theater. I copy the truth.

I will not ship a box labeled “documents” or “craft
supplies” to sneak wood. That is not clever. That is
how you teach a carrier and an inspector that fig
people lie. We already have enough of that in
marketplace listings.

Jack packs. I decide if the object is allowed to be a
mail object this week. Those are different jobs. If I
am not sure, the box waits. Waiting is a ship skill.

The rest of the ship drafts are weather, heat packs,
Fridays, soil, and paper. None of them turn a cutting
into a snack. None of them turn a fig into a pill. If
a sentence in this folder sounds like a supplement
ad, strike it. That sentence walked into the wrong
lane.

I will not put a recipe in a pack list and I will not
put a wellness line in a ship week post. Those lines
get copied onto marketplaces that already lie about
wood. I will not write the lie in a nicer font.

If someone asks whether dried figs in the pantry are
the same as the box, I say no and I stop. The pantry
is food. The box is plants. If they want foodways, I
point at the other folder. If they want a body claim,
I point at the door.

Jack’s cuttings post does not wander into a clinic.
This folder will not wander either, even when a later
editor wants “just a short note.” Short notes are how
lanes blur. We already blurred enough names.

If a ship email wants a nutrition line, I delete the line and I answer the week. The week is weather, count, and paper. The line was a wander.
