If I cannot name it, I do not ship it as a name. That is
a ship rule, not only a mark rule. The box is the last
gate. I have killed a carton at the door because the
tote card and the mother card had a fight I had been
too tired to hear.

No-ship list on the door this winter:

- No name we will stand behind
- No lot code
- No agree between slip and bag
- No weather I like
- No destination I can satisfy
- No wood I would stick in our own tray

If any line fires, the carton does not get a label from
the carrier. It gets a cooler or a refund or a recut.
I do not “send it and see.” Seeing is the receiver’s
child’s job if I fail. I do not hire their child.

Marketplace pressure is real. People want the fashionable
dark fig yesterday. Yesterday is how wrong names travel.
I will miss a week of sales. I will not miss a year of
being the person who mailed a shrug.

Jack can pack a named unknown — the word UNKNOWN in the
same font — if the buyer asked for unknown. That is a
name of a kind. It is an honest name. What I will not
do is let a maybe-Black-Madeira leave as Black Madeira
because the leaf looked right in July. Leaves lie. Fruit
is slower and ruder and better.

If two totes might have crossed on the tailgate, both
totes become unknown or both totes become ours. They
do not become a fifty to Ohio. I have crossed totes. I
still hate the sentence. I still keep it.

A substitution at the door is a rewrite of the slip, the
invoice, and the buyer’s yes. If I cannot get the yes,
I do not invent one because the truck is coming. The
truck can come next week.

This is the same ethic as the mother-block draft, applied
when postage is in my hand. Postage makes people generous
with other people’s identities. I do not get to be
generous that way.

Receivers: if the name on the bag feels like a wish, do
not stick fifty. Stick two, wait, write me. I would
rather a small fight now than a row of wrong plants in
your catalog. Your catalog is your name. I do not get
to spend it.

The door list is short so I cannot hide a yes between
the lines. If I add a seventh no, it has to earn a
card. “I want the postage” is not a card. Postage is
how wrong names get frequent-flier miles.

If I kill a carton at the door, I tell the buyer the
true reason. “Quality control” is a fog. “Tote cards
fought, we will recut” is a sentence. Sentences keep
people. Fog keeps rumors.

If I am angry at a tote, I do not ship angry. Anger ships guesses. I cool off, then I recard or I refund. The truck can wait for a mood.

Guesses stay on our bench. Named wood is the only wood that leaves.
