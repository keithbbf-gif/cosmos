# Fig nursery / wholesale ops — cuttings trade education

Staged draft set for the **buyfigs** lane. Not published copy. Not a catalog.
Not a phytosanitary certificate. Not medicine.

These pieces are for people who **take, mark, pack, hold, and ship** fig
cuttings and dormant plants — a nursery bench, a wholesale tote, a cooler
that is actually a cooler. The grower-facing hole-fill lives on
[FigRoots](https://figroots.com) (Jack already defined the industry cutting
as **6–8 inches and three nodes**). This folder is the other side of the
table: how the wood leaves the yard without becoming a lie, a rot bag, or
a Friday drop onto a Monday loading dock.

Default climate is **South Arkansas, USDA 8a**. Other zones sit in the room
because the mail does.

## What “staged” means here

Editorial status on every draft is `status: staged` — ready for a human
editor, not live, not SEO-published.

The *season* is also staged. Read in this order if you are actually trying
to run a winter trade, not a pile of posts:

| Stage | Tag | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `take` | harvest the product | Dormant window, wood, grade, mothers, tools |
| `mark` | identity | Names, lot codes, slips, synonyms, tags that survive |
| `pack` | the box | Moisture, bundles, crush, pots vs sticks |
| `hold` | cooler | Storage is a delay. Throw what you must. |
| `ship` | the mail | Weather, heat packs, states, paper, no-ship |
| `land` | the other bench | Open, photograph, DOA clock, what this lane will not teach |

A manifest lives in `_manifest.toml`. Drafts live in `drafts/`.

## Voice

First person. Jack Chambers on the pack bench (count, damp paper, labels).
PapaFig (Keith) on mothers, names, and the paper a state might ask for.

the table. If a rule is a state or USPS rule, the draft says **[VERIFY]**
and points at the agency, not at a vibe.

- **buyfigs** — nursery / wholesale cuttings trade education

## Do not treat this as

- A substitute for your state department of agriculture or a lawyer
- A promise that a box will arrive alive
- A virus-free or nematode-free certificate
- Advice to ship soil into a state that does not want soil
- A rewrite of Jack’s cuttings post or the fridge-storage draft on FigRoots

## Sources that informed the set (not copied)

- Jack Chambers, [Lets Talk About Fig Cuttings](https://figroots.com/2025/12/23/lets-talk-about-fig-cuttings/) — 6–8″ / 3 nodes; lignified vs dormant
- Texas A&M AgriLife, figs / hardwood cuttings taken fully dormant (farm practice, not a fridge recipe)
- USPS Publication 52 / Publication 14 — plants are mailable only when the movement is lawful
- National Plant Board / destination-state summaries — certificates and quarantines **[VERIFY]** per state
- California Food and Agricultural Code §6421 marking (shipper, receiver, contents, origin) as an example of destination rules, not a universal form
- OurFigs / trade practice: dry-seal vs wet-towel camps; we stay in the dry-seal camp

Field practice still outruns paper. The drafts say so.
