# PHOTO_NOTES — Keith’s stills first

User photos on **KC-PC** live under `D:\FIGS`. Those folders are the first pick for every slug in this pack. Public-domain fills are a last resort and only after a miss. Do not scrape nursery catalogs. Do not use `.dtrash`. Prefer stills over `Fig & Garden Videos` / `2025 Fig Videos`.

`PHOTO_MANIFEST.md` is the folder checklist. This file is the **shoot / pick brief**: what the editor should actually grab, and what the caption must not lie about.

Graphics (SVG schematics) are a separate pipeline. See `GRAPHICS_INDEX.md`. A diagram does not replace a fruit photo.

## Rules that do not bend

1. **Ours first.** Hunt the folders below before Commons or USDA.
2. **Named folders are not proof of type.** `Malta_Black_Fig` and `El Dorado Fig` are stills of *those trees*. Caption the clone we have. Do not upgrade the tag.
3. **No success-rate banners.** No “92% rooted.” No fake trial watermarks.
4. **PD fills** need URL + license in the media caption, and the sentence “not our tree.”
5. **WordPress stays Draft.** These notes are for the media library, not a publish button.
