## Why these twelve

The live site is strong on **how to root a stick**. Growers still bounce off the questions in Pick The Right Fig: Will this ripen here? Will it sour in humidity? Do I want breba? Do I keep it in a #3? What does “berry” mean? Why did the fridge bag rot? Why did it leaf and die? Why did the birds hit the dark ones first?

No topic swap. The gap was depth, not a missing thirteenth subject.
