## Cuttings storage (grower consensus, not a trial we ran)

- Jack on The Fig Jam: wash; parafilm for a long hold; double-bag; crisper.
- OurFigs threads on refrigerated cuttings (condensation, dry seal vs wet towel, check often). Example: https://www.ourfigs.com/forum/figs-home/1248015-refrigerated-fig-cuttings-best-practices-duration
- Common fridge talk: about 38–45°F, not frozen. **[VERIFY] with a drawer thermometer.**
- USDA ARS National Clonal Germplasm Repository (Davis) fig page: dormant cuttings preferred; routine summer cutting distribution stopped. https://www.ars.usda.gov/pacific-west-area/davis-ca/natl-clonal-germplasm-rep-tree-fruit-nut-crops-grapes/docs/fig-page/main/

No medical or “sterilizes all pathogens” claims. Bleach / H2O2 stay at FigRoots’ existing wash language.
