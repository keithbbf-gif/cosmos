# Photo notes — therapy history series

Text is the product. Images are evidence or atmosphere, never a fabricated witness.

Companion: `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` (the license table). Graphics agents start there.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or CC portraits listed as `pd` or `cc` in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`.
- Period objects: title pages (1908 Beers; 1912 Jung if the scan is PD), PD hospital plans, PD engravings.
- Original diagrams drawn for this series: a dated timeline, a map of societies (Vienna, Zürich, Calcutta, Tokyo, San Salvador), a family-tree of *schools* (not of patients).
- Type treatments: name, dates, one short epithet.

## Banned

- AI-generated faces, "historical photographs" that are not photographs, or "what X would look like."
- Scraped workshop / TED / university headshots.
- Patient artwork from psychiatric collections presented as free decoration (Nise's museum; Prinzhorn). Those works have authors, often still living as legal persons through estates, and they are not clip art for "the unconscious."
- Children in experiments (Little Albert, doll-study stills) as thumbnails.
- Atrocity photographs as headers for Fanon, Frankl, or Martín-Baró.
- Stock "therapist with clipboard and smiling client."
- Before/after faces.
- Anything that implies a WOW Therapies treatment room you have not photographed with consent.

## Alt text

Name what is in the frame. "Black-and-white photograph of Sigmund Freud, 1909, by Max Halberstadt, three-quarter view at a chair back." Not "the father of psychoanalysis gazes into the soul."

For type-only posts: no empty `<img>`. The heading is the image.

## WordPress featured image

If `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` says `no-portrait` or `confirm`, the featured image is a **series template** (wordmark + essay title), not a guessed face. Build one template; reuse it.

On upload day, open the Commons or museum URL again. Paste the credit block from `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` into the media caption **and** under the image in the post.

## Color and crop

Do not colorize PD photographs and call them contemporary. Do not crop a group so that a bystander becomes "the" portrait. Do not flip a face for layout.

## SLP lane

Do not reuse SLPWOW speech-pathology graphics here (oral-mechanism diagrams, fluency waveforms) as decoration. Different pack, different eye.

## If the graphics agent and the writer disagree

The license table wins. A beautiful unlicensed face does not ship.
