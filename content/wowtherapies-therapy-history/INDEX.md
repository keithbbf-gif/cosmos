# Editorial calendar — WOW Therapies, history of psychotherapy & counseling

Forty-two publishable essays: fourteen era pieces and twenty-eight figures. Status in YAML is `publishable` (quality-cleared for **staging** review). Nothing in this folder is cleared to write itself onto the live wowtherapies.com sitemap.

All files live under `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`.

This pack is the counseling / clinical-heritage lane. It is complementary to the SLPWOW speech-pathology history pack. Do not merge the two calendars.

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-two essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):

| Wave | Suggested order | Pieces | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — frame the famous names | 1–6 weeks | 01, 03, 04, 18, 19, 25 | Antiquity, talking cure, psychoanalysis, Freud, Jung, Rogers — historical, not how-to |

| 5 — attachment, families, trauma | 29–36 | 07, 09, 10, 30, 31, 32, 33, 41 | Systems, attachment, trauma, Bowlby, Ainsworth, Satir, Minuchin, Herman |
