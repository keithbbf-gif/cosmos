# Claims guardrails — therapy history pack

Educational history only. These sentences must never go on wowtherapies.com, in ads, or in schema markup.

This file is a refuse list. If a draft drifts toward any line below, cut the line. Do not "soften" it into a how-to.

## Never say (treatment / DIY)

- Any instruction that a reader could follow as a session: thought records to complete, exposure hierarchies to build, DBT skill steps, empty-chair scripts, EMDR eye-movement how-tos, "ask yourself these ten questions."
- "Try this at home," "use this worksheet," "self-treat," "you can DBT your way out of…"
- Dosing of psychiatric medication, taper advice, or "ask your doctor about [drug]" as content.
- "This method is the gold standard for [diagnosis]."
- "Studies prove [school] works better than [school]" without a named trial, a dated review, and a sentence about limits.
- Crisis instructions that replace emergency services. A single line — "If you are in danger, call local emergency services" — is enough. Do not write a safety-planning protocol in these essays.

## Never say (medical / legal)

- That WOW Therapies diagnoses, treats, or "specializes in" a DSM/ICD condition **in these history posts**. Service pages are a different file and a different review.
- That reading an essay is therapy, counseling, or a substitute for a licensed clinician.
- PHI, composite patients, or "a client once told me" stories. No invented case vignettes with identifying color.
- Guarantees of outcome.
- Anything a reasonable reader would hear as a diagnosis of themselves or their child.

## Never say (history that is too neat)

- "Pinel struck off the chains of the insane." (The Robert-Fleury painting is a nineteenth-century allegory. Jean-Baptiste Pussin and Marguerite Pussin did the practical work; Pinel wrote, organized, and later mythologized. See Weiner 1979, 1994.)
- "Freud discovered the unconscious." (Leibniz, Schopenhauer, Janet's *subconscient*, and a long medical literature sit behind him.)
- "Jung was a Nazi" **or** "Jung has been cleared." Name the 1933–1939 Zentralblatt / General Medical Society for Psychotherapy controversy, cite the documents, and leave the reader's judgment intact.
- "Bowlby proved that mothers who work damage children." (The WHO monograph was misread that way. Bowlby later refined 'maternal deprivation.' Do not recycle the misreading.)
- "Attachment style is your destiny" or pop-psychology attachment quizzes.
- Refrigerator-mother, schizophrenogenic-mother, or Bettelheim's autism claims as fact.
- Conversion-therapy "success." Name the harm. Do not teach the method.
- Rosenhan (1973) as settled exposé without Cahalan's later archival critique.
- That DSM-III (1980) "ended ideology." It changed the profession's language. It did not end argument.

## Never say (brand / SEO)

- "Best therapist in Arkansas" or any superlative about the practice.
- Disease keywords paired with a CTA ("overcome PTSD today — book now") on these URLs.
- Schema `MedicalTherapy`, `Drug`, or `treats` / `indication` on history posts. `Article` is enough.
- Fake author credentials (invented PhD, "our clinical director Dr. …" who is not on the license wall).
- Patient testimonials in this folder.

## Allowed

- Dated description of what a school claimed, who paid for it, who dissented, and what later researchers revised.
- Named trials as historical events (Linehan 1991; the Baltimore Strange Situation papers; Beck's early depression studies).
- Controversy, including racism, sexism, colonial psychiatry, and eugenics, when it is part of the record.
- A short "why a contemporary clinician still reads this" closing that does **not** become a protocol.
- The educational footer already in each draft.

## Living people

Linehan, Yalom, Herman, and others who were alive at last verification (2026-09-14) are not public property. No health speculation. No unsourced private life. Public books, public lectures, and public interviews only.

## If asked for both a history and a how-to

Print the history. Refuse the how-to in one sentence: this series does not publish treatment protocols.

## Review stamp

A piece is not cleared for the live site until:

1. Dates and titles match `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` or a marked `[VERIFY]`.
2. Portrait license is in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` (or the post uses type only).
3. No line from the never-say lists remains.
4. A human editor has read it aloud once.
