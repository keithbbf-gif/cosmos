## Full captions

### Fig. 01 — Psychotherapy on a timeline

Parallel school bands sit above a chronological axis from ~1850 to the 2010s. Milestones include moral treatment, Freudian psychoanalysis, behavioral and humanistic turns, Beck/Ellis cognitive models, and third-wave DBT/ACT. Integrative practice today crosses all bands.

### Fig. 02 — Schools of thought

Conceptual map: psychodynamic, behavioral, humanistic, cognitive-behavioral, systemic, and constructivist/narrative families linked to a central clinical encounter hub. Dashed lines indicate affinity, not strict historical descent.

### Fig. 03–06 — Portrait plates

Magazine-style double frames with typographic monograms (**no AI-generated or invented faces**). Each plate lists name, life dates, and school. Credit block references Commons file candidates; embed photographs only after `status: cleared` in `portrait_sources.yaml`.
