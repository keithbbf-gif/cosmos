# WOW Therapies — history of psychotherapy & counseling

Staged educational pack for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com). **Do not write these files onto the live site from this folder.**

Forty-two publishable essays (fourteen eras, twenty-eight figures) plus editorial ops. Complementary to the SLPWOW speech-pathology history pack — not a duplicate.

Educational only. Not medical advice. No DIY treatment protocols. No patient PHI.
