# Bibliography — WOW Therapies, therapy history series

Works cited across the pack. Article YAML `citations` lists the subset used in that piece. Prefer the edition named here. Wikipedia is a lead, not a source.

## East Asia, Latin America, Africa (selected)

- Morita, Shōma (Morita Masatake). Writings collected in *Shinkeishitsu no hontai to ryōhō* (first published theories on *shinkeishitsu*, 1919; home clinic same period). See also later English introductions: David K. Reynolds, *Morita Psychotherapy* (1976); Kora and Ohara clinical papers.
- Kitanishi, Kenji, and colleagues, historical reviews of Morita therapy in *Psychiatry and Clinical Neurosciences* and related journals.
- Kitanaka, Junko. *Depression in Japan*. Princeton: Princeton University Press, 2012. (Context for Japanese psychiatric modernity.)
- Silveira, Nise da. *Imagens do inconsciente*. Rio de Janeiro: Alhambra, 1981, and later editions.
- Museu de Imagens do Inconsciente. Institutional chronology: museum inaugurated 20 May 1952; Casa das Palmeiras 1956. [museuimagensdoinconsciente.org.br](https://museuimagensdoinconsciente.org.br/o-museu/)
- Melo, Walter, and colleagues. Scholarship on Nise and Engenho de Dentro.
- Lambo, T. Adeoye. Papers on the Aro village system, Abeokuta, 1950s–1960s (era essay 12). e.g. Lambo, "The Village of Aro," *The Lancet* (1964) and related WHO work.
- Doi, Takeo. *Amae no kōzō* (*The Anatomy of Dependence*). Tokyo: Kōbundō, 1971; English 1973. (Mentioned in global/attachment essays, not a full figure piece.)

## How to add a source

Name, title, place, date, and a stable locator (PMID, DOI, ISBN, or institutional URL). If you cannot find the locator, mark `[CITE NEEDED]` in the article. Do not invent page numbers.
