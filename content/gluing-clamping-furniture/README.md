# Gluing & clamping — furniture shop practice

Staged draft set. Not published copy. Not a bottle-brand essay. Not a promise
that a clamp will close a joint you did not cut.

These pieces are for a person who already builds furniture, or is about to
glue a table, a case, or a chair, and wants the glue and the clamps told in
a human voice. The center of gravity is a **65×15 shop** in rural South
Arkansas — Warren, Wilmar, Bradley County — heat, humidity, oak and pecan
and cherry, and a glue-up that has to survive August. The writing stands
without a brand.

This pack **unpacks** shop practice the craft pack only named: hide in the
pot, then quiet after clamps. It does not rewrite those two pages. It does
not invent a second trial.

## What “staged” means here

Editorial status on every draft is `status: staged` — ready for a human
editor, not live, not SEO-published.

The *work* is also staged. Read in this order if you are actually trying to
keep a glue line:

| Stage | Folder tag | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `choose` | name the glue | Grades, clocks, temperature, shelf life, creep |
| `fit` | rehearse the piece | Dry-fit, cauls, pads, panels, cases, chairs, breadboards |
| `spread` | put it on | Starved, drowned, cheeks, end grain, squeeze-out, weather |
| `press` | hold it | Pressure, clamp kinds, banana, rack, two sessions |
| `cure` | after the quiet | Unclamp, scrape, stain, split, fumes, the bench |

A manifest lives in `_manifest.toml`. Drafts live in `drafts/`. Sequence
rules live in `STAGE.md`. House voice lives in `STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

## Voice

First person, a furniture shop in South Arkansas. Specific glue and clamp
talk. Opinions with reasons. Catalog language and “best glue for every
joint” recipes get pushed off the table. If a number is a data-sheet
claim, the draft marks `[VERIFY]` until someone reads the sheet in the
room. It does not invent a second trial.

All **48** staged drafts carry `voice_check: edited` (2026-09-14 editor pass
on PR #535). Pack meta files stay `voice_check: human`.

## Do not treat this as

- A substitute for the glue manufacturer’s sheet
- A recipe that turns a gappy tenon into a joint
- Advice to use polyurethane foam as furniture glue
- A finish-room manual
- A chemical-safety program (see `d47`; still not a program)

## Sources that informed the set (not copied)

- Franklin / Titebond technical pages: open time, clamp time, application
  temperature, water-resistance classes — numbers stay `[VERIFY]` against
  the bottle in the shop
- Hide glue supplier cards: bloom, pot range, gel — after, not instead of,
  the craft-pack pot essay
- Urea-formaldehyde / plastic-resin labels: mix, pot life, formaldehyde
- Epoxy manufacturer sheets: ratio, pot life, clamp need
- USDA / Forest Products Lab wood-handbook notes on glue joints and
  moisture — cited as a class of source, not pasted
- Shop practice in a humid Southern shop: August flash, winter gel, pipe
  stain on cherry

Field practice still outruns paper. The drafts say so.
