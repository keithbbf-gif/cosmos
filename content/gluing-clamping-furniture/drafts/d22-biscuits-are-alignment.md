The faces lined up like they had been scolded. The biscuit had done that. The seam still had a hair of daylight I could see in the light. I pulled the biscuit, recut the edge, and put the biscuit back. The helper is allowed. The helper is not the joint.

Biscuits swell. Dominos are loose or tight depending on the cutter and the hole. Dowels are older than both and still a way to rack a panel if you drill like you are angry. I use alignment helpers when a long edge wants to slip under clamp pressure. I do not use them to replace a shoulder on a chair.

## What they are

A biscuit is a compressed spline. Wet glue, it plumps, it locks face alignment, it does not turn a springy edge into a closed edge. A domino is a floating tenon with a nicer machine. In a cabinet side it can be structure. In a panel edge it is still mostly alignment plus a little insurance. A dowel in a chair rail is a gamble the craft pack already named.

I dry-fit the helpers. If a biscuit is proud of the slot, I have a lump in the seam. If a domino is tight in August stock, I have a joint I will split trying to close. I ease what I must ease before glue.

## Glue in the slot, not a lake

A lake in a biscuit slot hydraulics the spline and starves the edge. I butter the edge, I put a little in the slot, I do not pour. The edge is still the film that matters.

## Religion is the problem

I have watched shops biscuit every seam because the machine was already out. I have watched shops sneer at biscuits because a video sneered. Both are moods. I get the machine out when the panel is long and my hands are few. I leave it on the shelf when the edge is short and the jointer was honest.

## The helper that hid a spring

I have left a hair because the faces were so pretty I did not want to take the stack apart. The hair became a dark line under oil, and the biscuits were still doing their job, which was to keep the faces flush while the edge starved. Flush and starved is a kind of furniture. It is not the kind I want to oil in this shop.

A long pecan edge will slip under a pipe the way a wet bar of soap slips. That is when I get a helper out. Three boards I can hold with my hands and a pair of cauls do not need a slot. I have biscuit-cut a short lid because the machine was already warm. The lid did not get stronger. It got two more places to telegraph if I cut the slots drunk.

## Slots I cut like I was late

A biscuit proud of the slot is a lump in the seam. I have closed a panel on a lump and then scraped a ridge for an hour, blaming figure. The figure was a spline that sat high because I had plunged like I was late for a phone. I dry-fit every helper now. If a biscuit stands proud I recut the slot or I thin the biscuit. I do not crush the lump with a caul and hope the film hides it.

Dominos go the other way in August. Stock is fat. A tight floating tenon in fat stock is a joint you will split trying to close, or a joint you will hammer and then live with a bruised cheek. I ease what I must ease before glue. A dry-fit that needs a running start is not a dry-fit. It is a warning.

Dowels are older than both. I have drilled a pair of dowel holes in a panel edge like I was angry — off-square, a little deep, a little proud — and racked the panel into a propeller before the glue was even open. The dowels were alignment. They aligned the error. I still use dowels when I mean them. I do not use them as a way to skip the lamp.

## A lake in the slot

I learned the lake on a walnut field I then had to scrape in the pores. The scrape took the plump line and left a pale county. Stain found the county. The biscuits had done their alignment. The bottle had done a religion I did not ask for. A domino slot will hold a puddle the same way a mortise will. See *Both cheeks, not a puddle in the mortise* (essay 24) if the helper has started to pretend it is a tenon.

## What I will not helper

A chair shoulder. A breadboard tongue I expect to walk. A miter I have not already decided to key. I have watched a shop biscuit a chair rail because the fence was already set. The rail got alignment. The rack still arrived. I will not rename a dowel gamble a biscuit and call it progress. The helper sits in the drawer until a face slips.

## The judgment

If the faces slip, a helper is a kindness. If the edge is daylight, recut. Do not let a floating tenon become a personality. The seam is still an edge joint. The triangle is still the order. The light is still the priest.
