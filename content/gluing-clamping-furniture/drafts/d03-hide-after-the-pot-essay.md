The pot essay already taught the smell, the sheet off the brush, and why a joint you can take apart is sometimes the more serious joint. I am not writing that again. What I keep seeing is a shop that read the romance and then reached for the wrong hide, or the right hide on the wrong day.

<!-- PHOTO: fig-01 D:\BBF\BBF Photos — liquid hide bottle beside a granule jar, both dated (filename pending shop pull) -->

Granules in a jar are not the same tool as liquid hide in a bottle. The bottle has urea in it so it will pour at room temperature. Urea is why the open time got longer and the dried film got a little more willing to creep. I use the bottle for a small repair, a veneer blister I can iron, a person who will not watch a pot. I use the pot when the joint is a chair rail or a mortise I expect to see a season, and the shop is already warm.

## Bloom is a bag, not a personality

192, 251, the higher numbers — shops talk like those are moral grades. They are gel strength on a test. Higher bloom sets harder and faster. Lower bloom stays a little kinder on veneer and on a hammer. `[VERIFY]` the number on the bag you opened. I have used a “251” that behaved like soup because someone watered it five times and called it “refreshing the pot.” The bag was not the liar.

Fresh mix for the day. Yesterday’s pot can come back once or twice if it was covered and not scummed. When it smells sharp or strings in the wrong way, it is trash. Hide is cheap next to a failed apron.

## Gel is a weather report

Cold oak will gel a correct mix into a skin that is not a glue line. The brush sheets in the pot. The cheek looks wet. Thirty seconds later you have rubber on a board that never wetted. Warm the parts. A sun lamp, a warm room, a rag. I have rubbed a rail with my hands like I was stealing heat. It looks silly. It works.

Summer in this shop goes the other way. Open time shrinks. You glue fewer joints. You do not take the call. Hide in August is a sprint you already rehearsed. If you need a long clock, that is a PVA day or a two-session day, not a reason to boil the pot and call it “thinner.” Boil it and you have cooked the strength out. The craft pack said that. It stays said.

## Where the pot does not belong

Exterior. A wet sink line. A butcher block that lives in oil and water unless the design already accepts maintenance. Hide is not waterproof. There are formulas and there are myths. I do not put hide on a garden bench.

I also do not put hide in a joint I have already fouled with PVA. Mixed religions in one mortise are how a future person steams a chair and only half of it lets go. Pick one. If the chair already has white fossil in the holes, you are in the repair chapter, not the pot chapter. Drill, pick, get to wood, then decide.

Veneer is still hide’s other house. A hammer, an iron, a blister you can work. PVA veneer is a one-way door. If this shop is going to skin a panel, the pot is on. If this shop is going to laminate a bend that must never come apart, that is urea or epoxy, and those have their own pages.

## Liquid hide without the apology

I am not ashamed of the bottle. I am ashamed of pretending the bottle is the pot. Liquid hide will glue a well-fit joint. It will not give you the same glass, the same reverse, or the same speed. Write the date on it. Do not let it sit open. If it has darkened and smells like a science-fair volcano, it is done.

A lot of “hide is weak” stories are liquid hide in a fat gap, or granules cooked to tea, or a shop at forty degrees. The animal was asked to be a shim and a space heater. It refused.

## The judgment after the romance

Use the pot when the piece should come apart later and the shop is warm enough to let the film form. Use the bottle when the repair is small and you will still fit the joint. Use something else when water is the point. Do not retell the pot essay to a customer. Show them a tenon with old brown on it, clean wood under the scrape, and the same tenon going home. That demonstration still beats a paragraph. This page is only here so you do not grab the wrong hide after you already believed the right story.
