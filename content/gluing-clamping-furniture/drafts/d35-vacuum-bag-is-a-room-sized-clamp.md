A vacuum bag is even pressure from air that is not there. It is not magic. A lumpy platen becomes a lumpy skin. A leaky bag is a clamp that lies about inches of mercury. `[VERIFY]` the pump and the glue. I listen for the leak. I do not invent a house number.

## Platens and breadcrumbs

I sweep. I run a hand. I have pressed a shaving into a door. The door still tells it. Cauls inside the bag have to be as clean as the work. Wax or tape so the glue does not marry the caul.

## Glue choice

Urea and some epoxies like a bag. PVA in a bag can work on a veneer and still creep later if the line is fat. Hide in a bag is a special day I do not pretend is standard. See *Urea is a different pot* (essay 4) and *Epoxy is a filler that also sticks* (essay 5). The bag is the clamp. The glue is still a religion.

## Do not peek

Clamp time is clamp time. Curiosity is a spring. I write the take-down time on tape on the pump. Overnight is a habit I like. `[VERIFY]` the can.

## The afternoon I stole from urea

The crest rail was a bent lamination I had rehearsed dry. The form was honest. The bag held. I had mixed urea as in *Urea is a different pot* (essay 4), and then I stood in the shop at supper and thought: it feels quiet, I could just check the bend. I opened the zipper. The stack came up a little in my hands, a polite spring, the kind you can talk yourself into calling nothing. I closed it again and ran the pump like I could put the minute back. In the morning the rail had a memory. Not a break. A willingness to flatten that the dry-fit had not had. I used the rail. I know which chair. I do not peek now.

Urea wanted the night. `[VERIFY]` that day's sheet. I write the take-down time on tape on the pump before I mix. The zipper is how I own a spring.

## The shaving in the door

I swept the platen with my eye. The eye missed a shaving the color of the veneer. The bag did what a bag does: it pressed the shaving into the skin as if the shaving had been invited. I found it when I pulled the door and the light raked. A ridge, thin as a hair, long as a thumbnail, in a place a hand would rest. I scraped. I still know the county.

I sweep now. I run a hand. I look at the caul the way I look at a jointer table before a finish pass. A breadcrumb, a bit of dried glue from the last bag, a splinter from the form — those are jaws. The bag does not choose. It prints whatever you left.

Wax or tape on the caul so the squeeze does not marry the work to the form. I have opened a bag to a door and a caul that had become one piece. I spend a minute on the caul before I spend a night on the pump.

## What the bag is lying about if it hisses

I listen. A bag that will not go quiet is a bag with a fold, a zipper that did not wet, a puncture I made with a corner of a caul. I do not invent inches of mercury. I do not own a story about a house number. `[VERIFY]` the gauge on that pump that day if a reviewer wants a number. I want the bag still and the platens flat. If I cannot get still, I find the leak. I do not go to supper on a hiss and hope the hiss was "just the pump."

A leaky bag is a rumor in the middle of the night. The edges may hold. The field may be a bubble. I have walked back out to the shop because I did not like how the pump sounded from the door. That walk is cheaper than a crest rail with a memory.

## Glue is still a religion inside the plastic

Urea likes the even press and the time. Some epoxies like it if the mix is a mix I already trust for a film, not a paste I am using as a gap. See *Epoxy is a filler that also sticks* (essay 5). PVA will veneer in a bag and then creep later if I left a fat line. See *Creep is a PVA property, not a rumor* (essay 11). I do not use the bag as permission to drown a cheek. Even pressure on a drowned line is even creep.

Hide in a bag is a day I have thought about and not made a habit. See *Hide after the pot essay* (essay 3) and *Hide on cold oak is a skin* (essay 30). The bag is not a heater. A strap is the cheap cousin on a gentle hoop. See *Band clamps and the round thing* (essay 34). I do not strap a veneer field.

## The room the bag needs

This is a 65-foot shop and the bag still eats a table. I do not bag on a pile. I do not leave a square under the bag "for a minute." I have done that with a pencil. The pencil wrote in a skin. I do not park the press in the glass. See *Winter skin, August flash* (essay 29). Overnight is a habit I like because I am less curious at dawn than I am at supper.

## The judgment

Flat platens, a bag that holds, a glue that belongs, a night you do not interrupt. The crest rail with the memory is why I stopped opening the zipper to "just check."
