# Bibliography — gluing & clamping furniture

Sources that informed the set. Not pasted. Not a second data sheet.

Shop practice in a humid Southern furniture shop still outruns paper. Numbers
that depend on a lot or a kit stay `[VERIFY]` in the drafts against the bottle
on the bench that day.

## Glue classes and sheets

- Franklin International / Titebond technical pages — aliphatic PVA grades,
  open time, clamp time, application temperature, water-resistance classes.
  Read the lot on the bottle. Do not tattoo last year’s internet on a wall.
- Hide-glue supplier cards — bloom-gram strength, pot range, gel. After, not
  instead of, `content/furniture-craft-blog/drafts/04-the-pot-on-the-hot-plate.md`.
- Urea-formaldehyde / plastic-resin can labels — mix, pot life, clamp time,
  formaldehyde handling.
- Epoxy manufacturer kits — ratio, pot life, clamp need, amine cautions.
- Moisture-cure polyurethane consumer and technical sheets — foam, moisture,
  isocyanates. Construction cousin, not a furniture cheek.
- Cyanoacrylate / accelerator labels — tack, blush, debonder. Jig glue.

## Wood and joints

- USDA Forest Products Laboratory, *Wood Handbook* — glue joints, moisture,
  and creep as a class of source. Do not invent a handbook psi in a draft.
- Ordinary furniture practice: floating panels, breadboard tongues, film
  glue lines, clamp patterns. Field scars in this pack are shop-observed,
  not commissioned case studies unless a later editor cites a shop note.
