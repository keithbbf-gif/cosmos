# Staging sheet

Every file in `drafts/` carries YAML:

```yaml
status: staged
stage: choose | fit | spread | press | cure
voice: human
cluster: glue | rehearsal | application | clamps | aftermath
```

`status: staged` means the piece has had a voice pass and is waiting on a
human. It is not `published`. It is not `scratch`.

## Read order (if you want a glue-up, not a pile)

1. `d01` through `d12` — **choose**: name the glue and the clock
2. `d13` through `d22` — **fit**: the rehearsal that is the glue-up
3. `d23` through `d30` — **spread**: how it goes on
4. `d31` through `d40` — **press**: how it gets held
5. `d41` through `d48` — **cure**: after the quiet

## Required topics (coverage)

- Glue: PVA grades, hide (after the pot essay, not instead of it), urea /
  plastic resin, epoxy, polyurethane foam, CA, temperature, shelf life,
  four clocks, creep, “water-resistant” vs outdoor
- Rehearsal: dry-fit, cauls, pads, clock on the bench, panel triangles,
  case winding, chair animal, floating panel, breadboard, biscuits/dominos
- Application: starved/drowned, cheeks not puddles, tools, end grain,
  squeeze-out as witness, glue on a finish face, winter/August
- Clamps: enough-not-crush, pipe/bar/parallel/F, handscrews, bands,
  vacuum, banana panel, case square, racked chair, softwood crush, two sessions
- Aftermath: unclamp vs machine, scrape, glue stain, split-don’t-fill,
  mixed religions, humidity, fumes, the bench after

If a later editor cuts the set down, keep at least one draft from each stage
and do not cut `d01` (glue is not a clamp), `d11` (creep), `d20` (breadboard),
`d36` (banana panel), or `d44` (split it).
