A fig in a pot is not a smaller in-ground fig. The pot is a climate tool. You can pick the plant up and steal a zone. That changes the prune.

## Why pots exist in 7 and 8a

Breba wood lives if the pot spends the hard nights in an unheated garage, a shed, or a closed porch that stays above the teens. You are buying last year’s tips. If you then heading-cut those tips in February in the garage “because that’s when you prune figs,” you spent the garage for nothing.

## Late winter, in the garage

- Dead twigs. They happen.
- One or two crossing stems.
- Root check every two or three years: slide the plant out, shave the outer root mat, fresh mix, same pot or one size up. Do this while it is still asleep.
- Do not combine a hard top heading, a root shave, and a bigger pot of hot mix in the same afternoon unless you enjoy a plant that sits there until June.

If the pot is already restricting growth, you need less topping than an in-ground jungle. Restriction is a prune.

## When it comes back out

After the hard nights, before it cooks in a closed garage on a 75 °F April day. People forget the second part. A fig that broke bud in the garage and then roasted is a prune you did with weather.

In 8a you can often park the pot on the south side in late March and throw a sheet on the last frost. In 7a it is later.

## Summer

Pinch poles. Rotate the pot so one side does not become a bald south wall. Water. Pots lie about drought. A wilted pot fig will drop fruit faster than you can read a draft about it.

## What not to copy from in-ground 9b

The hard January scaffold reset. Your plant is already small. Small plus hard cut plus root prune is a stump in a bucket.

## The 8a in-ground grower who also has one pot

Use the pot for the specialist — Olympian, Desert King if you must, a Mission you cannot quit. Prune that one for tips. Prune the in-ground Brown Turkey for the August jam. Two calendars, one address. That is allowed.
