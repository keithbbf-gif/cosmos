A lot of bad cuts happen because the person has three figs and one motion. Celeste, Chicago Hardy, Olympian — same afternoon, same heading, same pile. They are not the same plant in the ways that matter.

## Walk them separately

Even if they share a bed.

**Olympian / breba specialist / San Pedro:** tips. Air. No uniform heading.

**Celeste / Champagne / O’Rourke:** light hands, keep the early main, a little air.

**Brown Turkey:** split vote if you want July and August.

**Chicago Hardy / kill-down years:** catch-up to live, counted canes.

**LSU Purple:** air, do not shove it later in 8a.

**LSU Gold / Magnolia / open eye:** air is fruit quality, not aesthetics.

**Alma / other late ones:** see draft 45. Do not delay them.

**Mission:** climate first. If it is alive, keep old wood.

## A Saturday order that keeps you from blending them

Start with the specialist. You are freshest, and the cost of a wrong heading is the whole early crop. Then Celeste and the early mains. Then Brown Turkey. Save Chicago Hardy and any dieback plant for last, when you are willing to only take dead wood. If you start with the Hardy, you get into a cutting rhythm and you will bring that rhythm to the Olympian.

## One pile, three stories

The brush pile will look the same. Your notes should not. A ribbon color per variety helps if you are the kind of person who starts strong and then gets tired at the third bush. When you are tired, you only take dead wood. Tired heading is how Olympian becomes a shrub.

## Zone 8a layout that makes this easier

Put the specialist on the wall or in a pot. Put the jam fig where you can be a little rude. Put the late ripener in the hottest pocket so you are not also fighting shade. The prune is easier when the site already matches the vote.

## If you only remember one thing from the whole folder

The species is *Ficus carica*. The prune is the variety, the winter you just had, and the frost you still have. “How do I prune a fig?” is an incomplete question. Ask “what is this one for, and what did January do?” Then cut.
