# Rights and licenses — protein powder label literacy pack

## Original SVG (`assets/`)

Every file under `assets/` is an **original typeset diagram** created for this educational pack (2026). None are scans of real Supplement Facts panels, certificates of analysis, or commercial tubs. Fictional panel text uses generic placeholders only (“Protein powder,” “Dietary supplement”).

Reuse within this series with attribution: *Protein powder label literacy pack — schematic diagram.*

## Photographs and raster art

This graphics pass ships **no photographs** and **no AI-generated faces or people**. Per `PHOTO_NOTES.md`, do not add gym hero shots, before/after bodies, or clinical scenes presented as brand photography.

## Public-domain figures (future)

If a lesson later embeds FDA sample panels or other public-domain figures, record the source URL and license line in the figure `figcaption` and add a row here before publish.

## Trademarks

USP, NSF, Informed Sport, and retailer quality marks are trademarks. Diagrams avoid logo collages. Prose that names programs must follow each program’s artwork rules.

## Claims

Figures are **label literacy**, not efficacy charts. Captions state schematic / teaching. No fake clinical outcomes, no disease-treatment copy presented as label-ready text.
