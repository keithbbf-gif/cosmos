# Photo notes — protein powder label literacy

No fake tubs with real brand names. No before/after bodies. No disease-implied hero photography (joints, glucose strips, hospital gowns). No unredacted third-party COAs.

These lessons are text-first. Images in this pass are **original SVG schematics** only.

## Allowed

- Supplement Facts anatomy (generic box, CFR callouts).
- Serving-size / scoop / household-measure stack.
- Claim-type sorting map (nutrient-content, structure/function, prohibited disease speech — teaching piles, not copy-paste claims).
- Protein %DV threshold bar (10% / 20% for nutrient-content claims).
