# Rights and licenses — Supplement Facts label literacy

## Original SVG (`assets/svg/`)

Every file under `assets/svg/` is an **original typeset diagram** created for this educational pack (2026). None are scans, photographs, or redacted copies of real retail labels. Product names, lot numbers, and brand banners are fictional. Placeholder syntax uses bracket fields or generic example rows only.

## Photographs

This graphics pass ships **no photographs**. Per `PHOTO_NOTES.md`, do not add fake lab interiors, white-coat stock, unredacted third-party labels, before/after bodies, or kids-with-gummies hero art.

## Trademarks

USP, NSF, Informed Sport, and retailer quality marks are trademarks. Diagrams in this pass avoid logo collages; prose that names programs must follow each program’s artwork rules and must not imply certification without enrollment.

## Claims

Figures illustrate **format and arithmetic**, not efficacy, safety, or FDA clearance. Alt text and captions stay descriptive; they do not say “trust,” “approved,” “clinical proof,” or “immune protection.”
