# Bibliography — Supplement Facts / DSHEA label-literacy pack

Consolidated citations. Article front matter repeats the ones that piece actually uses. Dates are the versions these drafts were written against. Recheck FDA.gov, eCFR, FTC.gov, and OEHHA before a public import.

Not legal advice. Not a complete library. The April 2005 Dietary Supplement Labeling Guide is still the Q&A FDA hosts; some mandatory-nutrient lists and units were later amended. Current 21 CFR 101.9 / 101.36 control.

## Statute and regulation

- Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act of 1994, Pub. L. 103-417 (15 October 1994).
- 21 U.S.C. § 321(ff) (dietary supplement); § 321(g)(1)(B) (drug / intended use); § 343 (misbranding); § 343(r)(6) (statements of nutritional support); § 343(s) (dietary-supplement labeling); § 350b (new dietary ingredients).
- 21 CFR 101.36 — Nutrition labeling of dietary supplements. Cornell: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/101.36
- 21 CFR 101.9 — Nutrition labeling of food (units, rounding, some exemptions incorporated by 101.36).
- 21 CFR 101.12(b) Table 2 — serving size (miscellaneous; dietary supplements).
- 21 CFR 101.4 — designation of ingredients (including 101.4(g), 101.4(h) botanicals).
- 21 CFR 101.13 — nutrient content claims; 101.54 and related.
- 21 CFR 101.14 — health claims: general requirements.
- 21 CFR 101.93 — certain types of statements for dietary supplements (notification, disclaimer, (f) S/F, (g) disease). Cornell: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/101.93
- 65 FR 1000 (6 January 2000) — structure/function claims final rule.
- 62 FR 49859 / 49883 (23 September 1997) — disclaimer and notification rules.
- 81 FR 33742 (27 May 2016) — Nutrition Facts / serving-size final rule; supplement units follow.
- eCFR Title 21 Part 101 — https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-101

## FDA guidance and labeling Q&A

- FDA, *Dietary Supplement Labeling Guide* (April 2005; Chapter IV current as of 1 April 2005). https://www.fda.gov/food/dietary-supplements-guidance-documents-regulatory-information/dietary-supplement-labeling-guide
- FDA, *Dietary Supplement Labeling Guide: Chapter IV. Nutrition Labeling*. https://www.fda.gov/food/dietary-supplements-guidance-documents-regulatory-information/dietary-supplement-labeling-guide-chapter-iv-nutrition-labeling
- FDA, *Small Entity Compliance Guide on Structure/Function Claims* (9 January 2002). Docket FDA-1998-N-0071. https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/small-entity-compliance-guide-structurefunction-claims
- FDA, structure/function claim notification (21 CFR 101.93(a)) — Office of Dietary Supplement Programs (HFS-810), 5001 Campus Dr., College Park, MD 20740 (address as of the 2023 amendment, 88 FR 17718).
