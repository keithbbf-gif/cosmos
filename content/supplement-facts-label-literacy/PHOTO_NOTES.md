# Photo notes

No fake lab. No white-coat stock presented as our facility. No unredacted label with a real lot number from a supplier you do not have the right to show.

These drafts are text-first. Images, if any, are diagrams or clearly labeled stock.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or clearly licensed diagrams: a Supplement Facts stack, a (b)(2)/(b)(3) hairline, a serving-size walk.
- Wikimedia Commons / NIH / CDC / FDA public-domain figures. Keep the license line under the image.
- Neutral pack-shot style later, shot in-house, of *your* bottles — after SKUs exist.
- Tables and timelines set in type. Those carry more information than a hero image.

USP, NSF, and Informed-Sport marks are trademarks. Use only if the SKU is actually in the program, and follow their artwork rules.

## Banned

- AI-generated "our laboratory in [city]."
- Unsplash scientists as implied staff.
- Unredacted third-party labels or COAs.
- Before/after bodies.
- Kids with gummies.
- Heart symbols and EKG tracings used as decoration (FDA called those ordinary implied disease claims).
- Cannabis leaf on a piece that is about *not* selling CBD as a supplement.

## Alt text

Describe the figure, not the vibe. "Diagram: Supplement Facts (b)(2) nutrients above a hairline, other dietary ingredients below" — not "the label you can trust."
