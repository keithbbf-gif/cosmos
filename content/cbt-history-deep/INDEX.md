# Editorial calendar — WOW Therapies, CBT history deepen

Forty-four publishable drafts: twenty era pieces and twenty-four figures. Status in YAML is `publishable` (quality-cleared for **staging** review). Nothing in this folder is cleared to write itself onto the live wowtherapies.com sitemap.

All files live under `content/cbt-history-deep/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`.

This pack deepens the cognitive-behavioral lane. It does not replace `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/`. Cross-link the sibling slugs; do not paste them.

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-four essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):

| 1 — the two rooms | 1–6 weeks | 01, 06, 07, 21, 22 | Stoic sentence, Ellis names REBT, Beck leaves the couch, the two figure deepens |
