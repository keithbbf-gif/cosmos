**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

In 2018 Context Press / New Harbinger issued *Process-Based CBT*, edited by Steven C. Hayes and Stefan G. Hofmann. The two men had already been on opposite sides of a wave argument — Hayes's 2004 geology, Hofmann and Asmundson's 2008 "old hat." The later book is a kind of peace treaty, or a new quarrel in a shared vocabulary: stop selling packages as if they were natural kinds; name the processes a clinician is trying to change.

This page is about that late fight. It will not list processes as a menu. It will not teach a process-based hour.

## Why brand names became a problem

By the 2010s a student could spend a career collecting initials: CBT, REBT, DBT, ACT, MBCT, MCT, UP, CT-R, BA, PE, CPT. Each initial had a workshop, a manual, a competence fantasy. Insurers and IAPT-style systems liked initials because initials can be procured. Patients liked them because a name feels like a map. Researchers liked them because a name can be randomized.

The cost was a profession of competing churches. Hayes and Hofmann, from different pews, both wanted a psychology of change that could travel across brand names. Mediators, moderators, a return to processes the first wave would have recognized as the point. The 2018 volume is a syllabus for that want. Syllabi are not revelations. They are a generation trying to retire its own marketing.

Behavioral activation had already asked, in 1996, how much furniture the room needed. Schema therapy had already asked for a longer story than a 1979 sequence. Wells's metacognitive therapy had already shifted the object from the thought to the thinking about the thought. Process-talk did not invent those moves. It tried to file them.

## What a peace treaty cannot do

It cannot, by itself, fix the university-clinic problem. A process measured in a lab sample is still a process measured in a lab sample. It cannot fix payment. A reviewer who bought "CBT" will not automatically buy "psychological flexibility" without a new training cycle. It cannot fix the app. A phone will sell a process as a streak.

It also cannot retire the need for a relationship. Rogers's 1957 paper keeps surviving every rebrand. A process that no one is there to notice is a worksheet with a new title. Judith Beck's teaching kept saying this in the second-wave shop. Linehan's team said it with staffing. The 2018 book says it with chapters. Saying is not staffing.

## How this pack files the fight

Figure essays on Hayes and Hofmann stay with the people. The 2004 nickname essay stays with the geology. This essay is the later weather: a profession that had succeeded as a set of products trying to remember it had wanted to be a science of change.

WOW Therapies will not advertise "process-based CBT" as a service. The phrase is a 2018 object. If a later clinical page speaks about how the clinic actually thinks, that page needs ordinary language and a reviewer, not a New Harbinger subtitle.

A rural hour that has never heard "process-based" may already be doing the thing the book wanted: asking what is keeping a life stuck this week, not which church the stuckness belongs to. The book is for the guild. The hour is for the person. This series exists to keep those audiences from being sold to each other.

Hofmann's anxiety work and Hayes's ACT work remain their day jobs. The 2018 co-editorship is the historical surprise: the wave skeptic and the wave namer on one spine. Surprises like that are how you know a family is still a family. Families that cannot share a spine split into vendors. Vendors are the occupational hazard of a school that won.

The critiques essay still applies. A process is not culture-free because you called it a process. "Psychological flexibility" can be medicine. It can also be a polite word for adjusting to a wage. The 2018 book does not settle that. A small-city history page should not pretend it does.

If a later edition or a later Hayes–Hofmann paper changes the slogan, date the change. Do not treat 2018 as the end of the argument. This pack's only stop is a hold, not a finish line. The argument is still the profession talking to itself. Our job is to keep the talk from becoming a handout.

## 2008 to 2018, ten years of a last name

Hofmann and Asmundson's 2008 review asked whether the wave was a rupture. Hayes's 2004 paper had said a generation was moving. Ten years later they shared a spine. That interval is the late family fight: not a conversion, a shared exhaustion with initials. Exhaustion is not a protocol. It is a mood in which a syllabus can be written.

New Harbinger and Context Press are not Guilford. The 2018 book's publishing address is part of its meaning: a contextual-behavioral house and a broader CBT house agreeing to share a catalog. Catalogs are not churches. They are how a generation assigns chapters. This URL assigns one chapter: the year two former opponents tried to retire brand-name geology. Rural hours do not need the subtitle. Guilds did. We are writing for a reader who may never buy the book and still deserves the year.

Students who collected initials in the 2010s were not fools. They were answering a market that procured initials. The 2018 book is a guild trying to remember a science underneath the market. Remembering is not the same as staffing. Staffing is still Rogers, Linehan, a person in the chair. Processes do not sit down by themselves.

A 2016 student who owned seven manuals and no theory of change was the market the 2018 book tried to retire. Retirement is a mood. Moods do not staff a rural hour. The hour still needs a person. This URL will not let a New Harbinger subtitle replace the person.

A 2019 conference hallway that said "processes, not packages" as if the sentence were new was already the 2018 book's reprint life. Hallways recruit. This URL keeps the shared spine so a hallway cannot be the origin. Rural hours still need a person, not a subtitle.

A 2018 table of contents that assigned chapters across former churches is the peace treaty's real document. Documents are not revelations. They are how a generation files exhaustion. File it. Do not staff it as a service name.

Exhaustion with initials is a 2010s mood. Moods are not New Harbinger subtitles on a service page. File 2018. Staff a person.

Initials tired a generation. A shared 2018 catalog filed the tiredness. Filing is not a clinic verb on this page.
