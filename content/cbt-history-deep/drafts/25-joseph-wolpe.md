**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Joseph Wolpe was born in 1915 in South Africa and died in 1997. *Psychotherapy by Reciprocal Inhibition* (Stanford University Press, 1958) is the clinic in a hardback. A psychiatrist who had lost patience with the corridor Freudianism of his training wrote down a way of treating fear by pairing it with a response that, in his physiology, could not sit beside it.

The era essay (`first-wave-behavior-clinic`) holds Jones 1924, Watson 1920 as ethics history, and Hayes's later nickname. This page holds the man who made a session describable without a dream.

## Johannesburg, then Temple

The South African origin matters. A lot of American CBT mythology is Philadelphia and New York. Reciprocal inhibition is a Johannesburg export. He later worked in the United States; Temple University is the American address students remember. Arnold Lazarus, younger, South African too, would push the same household toward cognition and then toward a multimodal inventory of a life. The household argument is public: even inside behavior therapy, someone was already saying the sentence mattered.

What Wolpe gave the later house was teachability. A fear could be ordered. A supervisor could ask whether the order was too steep. Those are workforce virtues. Beck took the virtue and applied it to sentences. Linehan took it and applied it to a team. The virtue is older than both.

What he did not give the later house was a good account of meaning, culture, or a fear that is a correct reading of a neighborhood. Exposure traditions have spent the rest of the century answering that charge. The Foa essay is one later chapter. The critiques essay is another.

## 1958 beside 1952 and 1955

Eysenck's gauntlet was six years old. Ellis had named a therapy. Kelly had issued two volumes. Wolpe's book is the first-wave object a later geology needed in order to have a "first." Nobody in 1958 said first wave. They said behavior therapy, or they said they were physicians who had stopped waiting for insight. Hindsight named the crest.

This page will not reprint a hierarchy or a relaxation script. SUDS, as a later jargon, is a training object. It is not a history page's homework. Jones's Peter paper may be cited. It may not be illustrated. Children in labs are banned thumbnails.

## How a small-city site should say his name

As a 1958 author, not as a service. A rural hour that helps a person walk toward a feared doorway is in his building even if the clipboard says CBT. A rural hour that only argues with a sentence is in a later building. Plenty of hours need both. This URL will not draw the floor plan as a handout.

He lived long enough to see cognitive therapy take the prestige and to see exposure remain, in some clinics, the honest name of the work. Remaining is not failure. It is a crest that never fully receded. Behavioral activation, in the 1990s, was a reminder inside depression. Wolpe's reminder is older and more about fear.

## Temple students, a 1958 export

American students who met him at Temple met a man who had already written the Johannesburg book. Exports get renamed as local inventions. This pack puts Johannesburg back on the first page. Reciprocal inhibition is not a Mid-Atlantic idea that later found South Africa. It is a South African clinic that later found an American press.

Lazarus, in the same household, was already arguing for more rooms. Wolpe's gift was narrowness that could be taught. Narrowness is a virtue when a profession cannot describe a session. It is a vice when a neighborhood is the fear. He lived long enough to see both uses. This URL keeps 1958 as a describable session and keeps 1924 unillustrated. Mary Cover Jones remains a dated paper. A rabbit remains off this page.

He died in 1997, after the lists had begun to smile on exposure packages he had made possible and after cognitive therapy had taken the popular microphone. Remaining in the first-wave building is not a failure. It is a crest. Behavioral activation reminded the depression literature. He had reminded the fear literature first.
