Aaron Temkin Beck died on 1 November 2021, aged one hundred. Obituaries reached for kinship titles this style guide usually bans. The nickname at least named a lineage that now includes people who have never read the 1967 depression book. Reading it is still the better honor.

This page is not an obituary. It is a history of a school on the day after its quiet founder left the building. The Beck Institute, founded with Judith S. Beck in 1994, had already been the late institutional object: a training shop that had to decide how much of his curiosity it could keep once the worksheets had a barcode. His death made the decision less theoretical.

## What 2021 closed, and what it did not

It closed a life that had run from a 1921 Providence birth through Yale, the Philadelphia institutes, Penn, a 1961 inventory, a 1967 book, a 1979 manual, a 1994 institute, and a late-career extension into recovery-oriented cognitive therapy (CT-R) with Paul Grant, Ellen Inverso, and colleagues — work with people the 1979 depression manual had not been written for. The extensions had mixed reception, as late extensions do. The depression wall still held.

It did not close the workforce. IAPT-style systems, American training programs, apps, and a thousand slide decks continued Monday morning. Founder-death is a test of whether a school was a person or a furniture. CBT had long since become furniture. That was his success and his problem. Furniture does not miss you. People who still take a sentence seriously, and still look up from the page, miss the temperament the obituaries tried to name.

Judith Beck's public life as a teacher is a second-generation story the figure essay takes. CT-R is a late object this page will not turn into a protocol. The honest sentence is that he kept working. A hundred-year life that stops founding would be news. His did not.

## What the shop has to keep

Curiosity about a single belief. A tape a student can fail. A refusal to treat biology as a fiction. A refusal to treat a score as a person. Limits, published, when the model is asked to do work it cannot do. Those are keepable.

What the shop could stand to lose: the kinship titles; the implication that every grandchild protocol is his voice; the waiting-room ritual that never asks whether the sentence is a distortion or a description; the generated face a graphics intern will be tempted to make. This pack's portrait table says no. Type: "Aaron T. Beck, 1921–2021."

Penn's psychiatry department was the long workplace. The leaving of the analytic hypothesis was the origin. The Institute is the afterlife. Afterlives need editors. This series is one kind of editor: it will not let 2021 become a brand refresh.

## How a small-city site should speak the day after

Without a halo. Without a booking button. Without "the father of." With a date, a book, and a note that a rural hour using a thought record *instead of* a relationship is in a parody of his building. The 1957 Rogers paper and the 1979 manual were never a forced choice. A living hour may need both. A slide deck rarely says so. Obituaries rarely said so. Say so.

WOW Therapies does not inherit his license. It inherits, if it inherits anything from this pack, an obligation not to lie about what a history post is. Educational. Not a plan. Not a substitute for a licensed clinician. The footer stays.

The sibling figure essay (`aaron-temkin-beck`) is the life in more rooms. The 1967 and 1979 era essays are the spines. This page is the hinge after the hinge: what a school does when the man who hated waiting for insight can no longer be asked a question in a hallway at Penn.

A later writer may need to update "living people" lists as colleagues die. Date the update. Do not turn a death into a content opportunity. 2021 is already on the calendar because the calendar is about the school. The school outlived him. That was the point of a manual. It is also the grief.

CT-R's public objects — papers, a later Guilford volume, institute trainings — belong in `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` as late-career items, not as a rewrite of 1979. Students who meet Beck first through a 2020s recovery-oriented workshop have met a real late chapter. They have not met the 1960s notes. This pack exists so both meetings remain possible, and so neither meeting becomes a handout.

If you are reading this because an obituary made you curious, start with 1967. If you are reading this because a brochure said CBT, start with the educational note and stay there until a human being is in the room with you.

## November 2021, a Monday that did not pause

Training shops met the following Monday. IAPT-style services took scores. Apps took taps. That banality is the success of a manual. It is also why an obituary had to work so hard to make a person visible again. This pack's job, after the obituaries, is to keep the person attached to years: 1961, 1967, 1979, 1994, 2021. Years prevent a halo from becoming a logo.

Judith Beck's public notes as president of the Institute are institutional history, not a license to scrape a photograph. CT-R trainings continued. Students who never met him met a shop. Shops are allowed. Shops that speak as if he were still in the hallway, authorizing every grandchild, are not. The hallway is closed. The 1967 book is not. That is the only afterlife this URL will sell, and it is not for sale. It is a read.

Grant and Inverso's names on the late CT-R objects are easy to drop in an obituary that wants one genius. Do not drop them. Late work is collaborative or it is a myth. The 1979 manual already taught that lesson with Rush, Shaw, and Emery. 2021 should not unteach it.

A 2 November 2021 seminar that met anyway, because the calendar said it would, is the banality this essay is for. Calendars are the success of a manual. Plaques are how a person gets back onto a calendar that no longer needs them. Put 1921 and 2021 on the plaque. Leave the halo off.

A 2022 training calendar that did not pause for a death-year is the manual's success and the person's problem. Calendars win. Plaques recover what calendars drop. 18 July 1921. 1 November 2021. A 1967 spine. A 1994 shop. No halo. No generated face. No kinship title this style guide would fail.

A 2021 publisher's remainder table that still held 1967 next to a newer teaching book is the honor this page prefers to a kinship title. Remainders are honest. Haloes are not. Keep the older spine in print on this site's memory, even when the shop's barcode has moved on.
