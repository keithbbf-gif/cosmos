## Seattle, couples, depression

He worked in a behavioral-couples and depression world that already liked components. Local groups take houses apart. Journals make the taking-apart national. National taking-apart becomes, later, a named therapy with manuals this page will not reprint. The interval between a 1996 question and a workshop title is the interval in which a dismantling becomes a product. Keep 1996 as a dismantling.

He was not vandalizing Beck. He was doing what an empirical school had promised: test its own gadgets. The 1979 sequence had grown techniques. Growing is how kitchens fill. Someone has to ask which gadgets do work. Asking is a loyalty, not a betrayal. Slide decks that treat BA as an insult to cognition have missed the loyalty.

Colleagues' names on the 1996 paper are easy to drop. Do not drop them. Component analyses are group objects. This figure is the name later histories keep; the paper is a "et al."
