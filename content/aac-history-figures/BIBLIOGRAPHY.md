# Bibliography — AAC History Pack

Sources actually used to lock dates, names, and fights. A blog is not a warrant for a birth year.

## Primary and near-primary

- Bondy, A. S., & Frost, L. A. (1994). The Picture Exchange Communication System. *Focus on Autistic Behavior*, 9(3), 1–19.
- Klatt, D. H. (1987). Review of text-to-speech conversion for English. *Journal of the Acoustical Society of America*, 82(3), 737–793. (DECtalk / Klattalk lineage.)
- Light, J. (1988). Interaction involving individuals using augmentative and alternative communication systems: State of the art and future directions. *Augmentative and Alternative Communication*, 4(2), 66–82.
- Maling, R. G., & Clarkson, D. C. (1963). Electronic controls for the tetraplegic (POSSUM) (Patient Operated Selector Mechanisms—P.O.S.M.). *Paraplegia*, 1, 161–174.
- Romski, M. A., & Sevcik, R. A. (1988). Augmentative and alternative communication systems: Considerations for individuals with severe intellectual disabilities. *Augmentative and Alternative Communication*, 4(2), 83–93.
- Shane, H. C., & Bashir, A. S. (1980). Election criteria for the adoption of an augmentative communication system. *Journal of Speech and Hearing Disorders*, 45(3), 408–414.
- Kangas, K., & Lloyd, L. L. (1988). Early cognitive skills as prerequisites to augmentative and alternative communication use: What are we waiting for? *Augmentative and Alternative Communication*, 4(4), 211–221.

## Books and handbooks

- Beukelman, D. R., & Mirenda, P. (1992). *Augmentative and alternative communication: Management of severe communication disorders in children and adults*. Paul H. Brookes. (Later eds. 1998, 2005, 2013; 5th ed. Beukelman & Light, 2020.)
- Bliss, C. K. (1949). *Semantography (Blissymbolics)*. Sydney: Institute for Semantography. (1965 edition commonly cited in clinic histories.)
- Bonet, J. P. (1620). *Reducción de las letras y arte para enseñar a hablar a los mudos*. Madrid.
- Lloyd, L. L., Fuller, D. R., & Arvidson, H. H. (Eds.). (1997). *Augmentative and alternative communication: A handbook of principles and practices*. Allyn & Bacon.
- von Tetzchner, S., & Martinsen, H. (several eds.). *Introduction to augmentative and alternative communication* (later titles vary). Whurr / Wiley.
- Vanderheiden, G. C., Lazar, J., Kacorri, H., Lazar, A., & Jordan, J. B. (2023). *Technology and disability: 50 years of Trace R&D Center contributions and lessons learned*. Springer.

## Field histories

- Seligman-Wine, J. (comp.). (2018). *Tales of ISAAC: Memories of the beginnings of ISAAC*. ISAAC. https://isaac-online.org/wp-content/uploads/Tales-of-ISAAC-home-print_Revised-and-Final.pdf
- Vanderheiden, G. C. (2007). A journey through early augmentative communication and computer access. *Journal of Rehabilitation Research and Development* (and related Trace reprints).
- Zangari, C., Lloyd, L. L., & Vicker, B. (1994). Augmentative and alternative communication: An historic perspective. *Augmentative and Alternative Communication*, 10(1), 27–59.

- Creech, R. (2004). Edwin and Esther Prentke AAC Distinguished Lecture. ASHA / AAC Institute text.

- USSAAC. Leaders & Legends: David Yoder (public interview).
- University of Toronto. Charles K. Bliss fonds; Blissymbolics Communication Institute – Canada fonds.

## Institutions and objects

- International Society for Augmentative and Alternative Communication. Founding, East Lansing, May 1983; first biennial conference, Boston, 1984; journal *Augmentative and Alternative Communication* from 1985.
- Possum Ltd. company history (Stoke Mandeville volunteer story; POSM / *possum*; company 1961). Use as a company memory, not as the only date warrant; pair with Maling & Clarkson 1963.
- Minspeak Academy / Baker, B. (vendor history of summer 1980 concept; December 1981 PRC agreement; 1983 ASHA Cincinnati release). Pair with independent secondary sources.
- AssistiveWare. Proloquo2Go, April 2009 (iPhone / iPod touch); iPad, April 2010.
- Prentke Romich Company origin, 1966 (Barry Romich and Edwin Prentke).
- Michigan State University Artificial Language Laboratory (John Eulenberg, director 1974–2012); *Communication Outlook*.
- ASHA. Position statements on facilitated communication (1995; reaffirmed / updated 2018). Read the statement, not a summary card.

## Caution

Vendor “about” pages, Pearson author blurbs, and App Store copy are useful for *what the company says it shipped*, not for childhoods or for “first in the world” prizes. When two respectable sources disagree, the article says so.
