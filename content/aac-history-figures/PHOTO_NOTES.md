# Photo Notes — Art Direction (AAC Series)

Staged guidance for a later SLPWOW.com editor. No live CMS writes. **This pack ships notes, not files.**

## What we are making

A magazine series, not a vendor catalog. Each profile should have **one** lead portrait if rights allow. Essays may use one historical object (a 1620 title page, a 1963 POSSUM grid, a 1983 conference badge, a photocopied Bliss overlay) — never a collage of uncredited faces.

## Tone

- Warm paper, not neon clinic. Not App Store screenshots as hero art on a 1971 essay.
- Full names and life dates in the caption, then a short credit line in smaller type.
- Do not put a modern stock “therapist with iPad” on a Stoke Mandeville or OCCC essay.
- Do not colorize historical photographs for the first run.
- Do not generate a “young Bliss” or a “period” child pointing at a board.

## Caption formula

```
[Full name], [year of photograph if known].
Credit: [photographer or “photographer unknown”], [collection], [license].
```

## Placeholder (required when rights fail)

Use this block, unaltered, under the title or in the lead slot:

> **Portrait placeholder.** No public-domain or clearly licensed portrait located as of the pack date. See `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` for hunt status. Do not invent or generate a substitute likeness.

A gray frame with the person’s name and dates is acceptable in WordPress. A generated “historical” face is not.

## Hunt order (next editor / graphics pass)

1. Wikimedia Commons — confirm the **file page** license, not a Google thumbnail.
2. Library of Congress, Smithsonian NPG, Wellcome Collection, Gallica, Deutsche Fotothek, NLM Images from the History of Medicine, Biblioteca Nacional de España, BIU Santé (Licence Ouverte).
3. University archives (Wisconsin / Trace, Purdue, Michigan State Artificial Language Lab, Nebraska, UNC, Georgia State, Penn State RERC-AAC, Toronto / Holland Bloorview, Dundee).
4. ISAAC and USSAAC photo collections. Many photographs are viewable; **redistribution rights are not automatically granted**.
5. Company archives (Possum Ltd., Prentke Romich, AssistiveWare) — last, and only with a written license.

Stop the hunt when a file is PD, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, Licence Ouverte, or a museum open-access equivalent. Do not download “fair use” press photos into this repo.

## Accessibility

Alt text is the person’s name, profession, and approximate date — not “vintage man in a coat” and not “disabled child using device.”
