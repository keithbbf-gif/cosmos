This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

A field needs a journal. It also needs a shorter, faster, less refereed square where clinicians, families, and users can meet a new idea before it is a citation. Sarah W. Blackstone’s *Augmentative Communication News* was that square for a generation. She is living. ISAAC’s president list puts her in 1996–1998, between Betty Jean Findlay and Prue Fuller. The presidency is a fact. The newsletter is the residue.

## Why a newsletter is a historical object

*Communication Outlook* out of Eulenberg’s lab was an earlier samizdat. Blackstone’s *News* was a later, more designed attempt to keep research, policy, and lived experience on one folded set of pages. People who never subscribed to Taylor & Francis still got an argument in the mail. That matters in a field whose journal paywall arrived early.

She also worked the consumer and policy side that textbook chapters compress into a bullet. Funding, patient-provider communication, emergency rooms that cannot wait for a scan beep — the unglamorous rooms. AAC dies in those rooms as often as it dies in a bad assessment.

Patient-provider work is easy to file under “hospital SLP” and lose. A person who cannot speak to a nurse is in the same trouble POSSUM was built to fix, minus the typewriter. Blackstone’s public projects kept putting paper and simple boards back into medical settings that had become proud of their devices. Pride is not a channel. A newsletter that can say so without a special issue is doing field maintenance.

## Residue

A 2026 blog or listserv that thinks it invented “knowledge translation” is late. Blackstone’s square was already there. The residue is a clinician who can read a study and a user column in the same week without treating either as decoration. Translation that never reaches a night nurse is not translation.

## On the record

ISAAC’s 1996–1998 presidency sits between a Canadian and a British term. That sandwich is the society working as designed. Blackstone’s American office did not make the newsletter provincial. Emergency communication and medical encounters are international even when the insurance form is not. A folded page that can travel in a bag with a board is a technology POSSUM would have understood.
