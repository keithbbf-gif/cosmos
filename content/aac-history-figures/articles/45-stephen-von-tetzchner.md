This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

A field that founds itself in East Lansing can forget that Stockholm 1990, Maastricht 1994, Dublin 1998, and Odense 2002 were also the room. Stephen von Tetzchner, University of Oslo in the public record, wrote with Harald Martinsen the European textbooks — titles shifted across editions — that gave students a spine which was not Beukelman and Mirenda. Living; birth year not locked. Do not invent one.

ISAAC’s early presidents included Gunnar Fagerberg and Margita Lundman of Sweden. The society’s international bet was not a slogan. von Tetzchner’s books are how the bet became assignable in Scandinavia and, in translation, elsewhere. A U.S. magazine series that ended on Nebraska would be doing a national story and calling it a world.

## What a European textbook changes

Examples. Service systems. The assumption that a dedicated SGD sales force exists. The place of sign and of aided systems in countries with different school laws. von Tetzchner’s developmental and psychological framing is not a copy of Purdue’s special-education framing or Nebraska’s medical-rehabilitation framing. Three framings is healthier than one church.

He is not “Europe.” Erna Alant’s Pretoria centre, Sudha Kaul’s later presidency, and a dozen unpublished rooms are also the world. He is the last profile because a series sold to American clinicians needs an explicit door out of the Midwest.

Martinsen’s name belongs in the caption of the textbooks. A pair again. Developmental psychology looking at aided communication produces different chapters than a medical-rehabilitation textbook: more childhood, more typical-language comparison, sometimes less insurance. Readers who only know Brookes should feel the difference as a fact, not as a rivalry. Three spines, if you add Lloyd, Fuller, and Arvidson’s 1997 handbook. This pack ends by pointing at the shelf, not at a winner.

## Residue

A 2026 master’s program that assigns only Brookes titles has not left 1992. The residue is a second spine on the shelf and a map that includes Oslo.

## On the record

Oslo is a small capital with a large AAC bookshelf. Students who read von Tetzchner and Martinsen before they read Brookes arrive in ISAAC rooms with different examples and a different idea of what a service looks like. That difference is the point of ending the roster here. A series that starts in Madrid 1620 should not finish as if Nebraska were the ocean.
