This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

“Confessions of a multi-modal man.” That 2004 *Alternatively Speaking* column is the object. Michael B. Williams, born 1937, raised in the public biographical note in Chicago and Altadena and later in the San Francisco Bay Area, has been a USSAAC president, an ISAAC board member, and a collaborator on research and engineering projects. He writes as a user who met AAC people after a childhood of speech teachers asking him to do what he knew he could not. The new people wanted the message out. He kept every old tool anyway.

Living. Public self-advocate record only. No medical chart. No invented inner life.

## What he taught the exhibit hall

That a speech-generating device is an addition, not a conversion. That low-tech stays in the bag when the battery dies or the cafeteria is loud. That phoneme-level work on an old SGD was, for him, a way to make the machine say the words he actually wanted — a user-level linguistic demand Baker would recognize. That “there’s an app for that” is a joke a person who lived through 1978 is allowed to tell.

He is not “the voice of users.” He is a person who got on the program and then used the program to refuse mascot duty. Other users remain unnamed in this pack on purpose.

Born with a speech disability in 1937, in the public USSAAC note, he had a whole life before ISAAC had a name. That is the point of a user profile that starts in childhood only as a date: the field arrived late to him, not he to the field. *Alternatively Speaking* let him edit the square, not only appear in it. Editing is power. A history that only quotes him as color is stealing the edit.

## Residue

A 2026 assessment that confiscates a family’s paper board because “we have a tablet now” is arguing with Williams. The residue is a toolbox and a column that clinicians quote when they want to sound humble. Quoting is not the same as waiting for the end of his sentence.

## On the record

USSAAC’s presidency is a user in the national chapter’s chair. That is occupancy, not aisle decoration. The Bay Area household note in the public bio is enough geography. Do not tour his family. Do not diagnose his speech. Read the column. The toolbox is the method, and he already published it.
