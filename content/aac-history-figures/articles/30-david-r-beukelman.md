This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Harrison, South Dakota, 13 January 1943. A one-room school, Corsica High, Northwestern College, then Western Michigan for the speech-language pathology degree, then Wisconsin for the master’s and doctorate — Janice Light’s 2022 tribute is the public spine. David R. Beukelman worked at Mankato, then spent a decade as Director of Communication Disorders at the University of Washington Hospital in Seattle, then in 1985 became Barkley Professor at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln with a medical-center appointment in Omaha and a long clinical research collaboration at Madonna Rehabilitation Hospital in Lincoln. He died on 5 February 2022.

He is in this pack because the book on the shelf is his (with Mirenda, then with Light), because he edited *AAC* from 1994 to 1997, because he sat in East Lansing’s naming argument, and because he kept treating degenerative disease and adult clinic work as AAC’s business, not a pediatric afterthought.

## The book and the model

*Augmentative and Alternative Communication: Management of Severe Communication Disorders in Children and Adults* (Brookes, 1992) gave master’s programs a semester. Later editions dropped *disorders* for *complex communication needs*. The Participation Model — opportunity barriers and access barriers on one page — is how a generation learned to see a closet device as a policy problem. Light’s tribute notes more than twenty years in the RERC on AAC. The RERC is a federal furniture. Beukelman used it as a workshop.

Yoder was a Wisconsin mentor. Vanderheiden was a hallway cousin. Lloyd was a Purdue rival. The map is Midwestern on purpose. The textbook traveled farther than the map.

He also wrote about dysarthria and about communication in degenerative disease — the adult clinic that pediatric AAC photographs erase. Madonna Rehabilitation Hospital is the Lincoln room where the textbook’s sentences had to survive a Thursday. A professor who keeps a hospital collaboration for decades is not dabbling. The 2017 ThinkTank talk McNaughton (David, not Shirley) pointed to in the memorial is the late self-account: ideas from clients, not from a diagram first.

## Residue

A 2026 clinician who writes “participation” on a report without naming a barrier is using his vocabulary as wallpaper. He told late audiences that community clients taught him. Believe that over the festschrift adjectives. The residue is a person in Lincoln who still needed a way to say something on a Thursday, and a professor who showed up.
