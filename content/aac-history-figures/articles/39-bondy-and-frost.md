This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Delaware Autism Program, 1985. Andrew S. Bondy, Ph.D., and Lori A. Frost, M.S., CCC-SLP, are tired of systems in which the adult points and the child complies. Picture exchange — the child gives a picture, the adult gives the thing — is a theory of initiation. The 1994 *Focus on Autistic Behavior* paper is how most researchers met PECS. Classroom work and ABA conference talks are older than the paper. Pyramid Educational Consultants is the later company. Living pair; public method papers only.

This is a pair profile because the method is a pair object. Bondy without Frost is a psychologist’s idea. Frost without Bondy is an SLP’s classroom. The exchange is the thing.

## What the bet was, and was not

It was not the first use of pictures. It was a protocol about who moves first. It spread because it was teachable and because requesting is the function schools will fund. Later critics, including writers Mirenda took seriously, asked whether a system that begins in requesting ever climbs out. That is a fair question and not a reason to erase 1985.

This magazine will not print the phases. Phases are how-to. History is the 1985 room, the 1994 citation, the 1992 ISAAC talk listed in the paper’s own references, and the later global brand.

Frost’s SLP credential is why PECS did not remain only an ABA hallway object. Bondy’s psychology is why it had a teaching logic schools could schedule. The pair is also why later “PECS versus AAC” fights are category errors: PECS is a picture-exchange program that lives inside AAC’s larger house, not a rival kingdom. Mirenda’s autism papers are the place to watch that house get inspected.

## Residue

A 2026 “first/then” card is not PECS. A 2026 team that thinks PECS is all of AAC is repeating a vendor hallway. The residue is the initiation question: who has to start for the communication to count. Light’s 1988 paper and this 1985 classroom are cousins who did not share a letterhead.

## On the record

Delaware’s program is a specific public-school object, not a university lab. PECS spread through ABA meetings and then through SLP rooms that needed something a para could be taught on a Friday. Teachability is a historical force. It is also how a requesting program can become the whole of a child’s “AAC” for years. History’s job is to name that narrowing without pretending 1985 did not happen.
