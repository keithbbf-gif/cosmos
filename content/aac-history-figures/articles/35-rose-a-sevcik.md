This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

The author line is Romski and Sevcik, again and again. Rose A. Sevcik, also of Georgia State in the public record, is not the second author as decoration. System for Augmenting Language was a two-person stubbornness about who was allowed to have symbols. Living; public papers only.

The 1988 *AAC* paper on considerations for individuals with severe intellectual disabilities sits beside Light’s interaction paper in the same issue — a good year for a young journal. Together they told the field to look at partners and to stop using IQ as a velvet rope.

A pair laboratory produces a pair ethics: neither name owns the finding, and both names have to live with the period vocabulary in the 1988 *JSHD* title. Sevcik’s later developmental papers are where comprehension and production are allowed to disagree — a child who understands more than they can select, or the reverse. Assessment rooms that only count selections are arguing with those papers. The argument is still live in 2026 IEP meetings that confuse a low output rate with a low mind.

## What a pair profile would have flattened

Sevcik’s later work continues the developmental and language questions: how symbols get mapped, how comprehension and production come apart, how families live with a system after the grant ends. A magazine that only prints Romski’s name is doing the old caption. This pack prints both and still refuses to invent a childhood.

## Residue

Every later argument that “those kids won’t use it” has to go through this laboratory or admit it has not. The residue is a data sheet, not a slogan. The 1988 title’s period word is also residue: the field changed its mouth and still has to earn the change. Earning it means putting symbols in front of people the meeting already decided against. The meeting is not a finding, and never was.

## On the record

Comprehension without production, production without a partner who will wait: those splits are how a symbol system fails in a house even after it succeeds in a lab. Sevcik’s developmental eye is the one that keeps the house in the paper. A pair profile would have buried that eye. This file exists so it does not stay buried.
