This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

ASHA, 2004. The Edwin and Esther Prentke AAC Distinguished Lecture. Rick Creech tells a room of speech-language pathologists that he started public speaking because he was happy he could speak with a machine and wanted others to see possibilities rather than a disability. He mentions a master’s in speech pathology, work with Pennsylvania’s training network, and — in the archived text — the HandiVoice years. The lecture is the source. Do not improve it.

He is living. Baker’s Minspeak history names Creech’s 1980 phone calls and a North Carolina visit as the enthusiasm that kept semantic compaction alive. A user in the design loop before the 1983 booth: keep that order. The field likes to add users after the product ships.

## What a user-lecturer can do

Scold without a footnote. Describe rate, repair, and the social cost of a robotic voice in a grocery store. Accept a company’s lecture endowment and still not become an advertisement. Creech’s public record is long on those moves. This pack will not mine it for a diagnosis or a childhood.

He is not Williams. Williams writes columns and board minutes. Creech speaks rooms. Both are necessary. Neither is a sample.

The HandiVoice years in the lecture are a device history told from the chair, not from the catalog. Phonemes, practice, a voice that a room could hear: that is essay 07 from the other side of the table. A master’s in speech pathology is not a conversion story. It is a user who learned the guild’s language well enough to lecture the guild without asking permission to exist.

## Residue

Every later “lived experience” keynote that is booked instead of a user on the research team is a thin descendant. Creech’s better residue is the 1980 design conversation. Put users in the loop before the overlay is printed.

## On the record

Pennsylvania’s training network work is a day job in the lecture, not a brand. A user who trains SLPs is the 1983 intention in a state paycheck. The Minspeak phone calls remain the earlier, sharper fact: design loop first, lectern later. Rooms that only want the lectern are buying inspiration. He has already said he does not preach.
