This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

1988, volume 4 of a young journal: Janice Light, “Interaction involving individuals using augmentative and alternative communication systems.” The paper is a turn. Devices had been the exhibit. She put the pause, the partner, the who-starts, and the social purposes of communication in the middle of the page. A generation of master’s students learned to count the adult’s questions and wince.

She is living, long associated in the public record with Penn State and with the RERC on AAC. This profile will not invent a birth year. It will say that Beukelman and Light (2020) is the fifth edition of the book that used to be Beukelman and Mirenda, and that the succession is a scholarly fact, not a soap opera.

## What the 1988 paper made citable

That a person with a device may be competent and still look incompetent if the partner never waits. That interaction goals are not the same as symbol-identification goals. That research which only measures how many icons a child can match is measuring a side show. Later work on early intervention, literacy, and visual scene displays sits on that insistence. This series will not teach those methods. It will say they have a grandmother in 1988.

Penn State’s AAC doctoral culture, next to the RERC, is how 1988 became a lineage rather than a one-off paper. Students who now run labs still assign the interaction piece. Assigned pieces are how a field’s manners change. The manners are: wait, count your own questions, and do not confuse a correct point with a conversation.

## Residue

Every later “communication partner instruction” workshop is downstream. So is every IEP that still writes goals only on the child’s body. Light’s residue is the wince. A clinic that cannot wince at its own questions has not read her. Count the questions. Then stop asking some of them.

## On the record

Visual scene displays and early-intervention papers came later and will tempt a methods editor. This history pack names them only as descendants of 1988: the partner is still in the frame. Beukelman and Light (2020) putting her name on the textbook spine is the field admitting that interaction is not a chapter you skip. It is the book’s weather.
