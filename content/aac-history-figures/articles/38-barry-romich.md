This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

1966 is the year company histories give for Prentke Romich: Barry Romich and Edwin Prentke, Ohio, control systems that became communication systems. Living founder. This pack will not print a sales brochure. It will say that semantic compaction needed hardware, and that in December 1981 Bruce Baker signed his first commercial agreement with Romich after convention-floor conversations. The Express III and later Liberator years are how a linguistic idea got a battery door.

Edwin and Esther Prentke’s names sit on the ASHA AAC Distinguished Lecture that Lloyd helped establish in 1997. Creech gave that lecture in 2004. The donor name on a lecture is also history: manufacturing money funding a user to scold a profession. Worse arrangements exist.

## What a company does that a lab cannot

Ship. Repair. Hire salespeople who know a funding code. Survive long enough for a language system to have a second generation of users. Also: shape assessment toward the catalog. ISAAC invited distributors because Larry Weiss told Toronto to. The exhibit hall is not a sin. Unexamined loyalty to one hall is.

Small details of origin stories vary among company pages. Treat 1966 as the public founding stamp, not as a romance.

Control systems for disability are the older PRC object; communication is the later one. That order matters. A company that already knew switches could learn to house a language. A language without a switch company often stayed a paper overlay. The 2010s iOS apps from traditional manufacturers — Hershberger’s later remark about the App Store — are this company admitting the pocket computer had changed the storefront, not only the circuit board.

## Residue

A 2026 clinician who has never touched a PRC overlay still lives in a market Romich helped make: dedicated devices as durable medical equipment, then apps as the cheaper cousin. The residue is a warranty card and a vocabulary file the user should be allowed to take with them.

## On the record

Edwin Prentke is the other founder. A profile titled Romich is a living-name convenience. The lecture endowment that carries both Prentke names is how manufacturing money funded Creech’s scold. Worse arrangements exist, as this pack already said. Better ones would put users on the board that sets the catalog, not only at the lectern after dinner.
