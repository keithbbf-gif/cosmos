# Claims Guardrails — AAC History Pack

Staged. Educational history only.

## Hard stops

- No treatment protocols, home programs, device-shopping lists, or “try this if…” language.
- No living-patient stories, even anonymized composites.
- No invented quotations.
- No generated faces; no stock “child with iPad smiling at therapist” on a 1620 or 1960 essay.
- No claim that SLPWOW or WOW Therapies invented, owns, or uniquely practices a historical method.
- No “gold-standard treatment” language. Systems are historical objects, not endorsements.
- No image binaries in this pass. Portrait work is a hunt log, not a download.

## Historical and public figures

Public scientific cases and self-advocates who published under their own names (Rick Creech; Michael B. Williams; other named ISAAC user-leaders in published histories) may be named as history. Do not invent inner lives. Do not write their classrooms as entertainment.

Do not name schoolchildren from Trace, OCCC, Delaware Autism Program, or any other clinic as if they were characters. Vanderheiden’s published origin story names “Lydell”; use that published name once, as history, and do not embroider.

## Living figures

Shirley McNaughton, Gregg Vanderheiden, David Yoder, Macalyne Fristoe, Bruce Baker, Pat Mirenda, Sarah Blackstone, Janice Light, MaryAnn Romski, Rose Sevcik, Howard Shane, John Eulenberg, Barry Romich, Andrew Bondy, Lori Frost, Karen Erickson, David Koppenhaver, Rick Creech, Stephen von Tetzchner, and any other living subject are treated as public scientific or public-advocate figures. Stick to published books, devices, papers, lectures. No private biography. Portraits: hunt only; almost certainly not public domain.

## Uncertainty

If a death year, first-edition year, or “who coined it” is contested, the article says so. A missing date in `INDEX.md` is better than a guessed one.

## Relationship to sibling packs

This series may retell de l’Épée, Eugene T. McDonald, or ASHA’s later AAC scope language because they sit on AAC’s public path. It must not copy the sibling packs’ sentences. New scene, new residue, deeper argument.

Facilitated communication is in this pack because AAC’s public history includes it. It is not a how-to and not a brand smear. Claim, test, statement.
