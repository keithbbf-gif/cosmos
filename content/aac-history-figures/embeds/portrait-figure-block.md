# Portrait figure block (later pass)

Not used in this pack. Profiles keep `portrait: null`. When a later rights pass clears a raster, paste this HTML and fill the bracketed fields.

```html
<figure class="slpwow-figure slpwow-figure--portrait">
  <img
    src="../assets/portraits/[id].jpg"
    alt="[Name], [profession], [approximate date]."
    width="360"
    height="440"
    loading="lazy"
  />
  <figcaption>
    <strong>[Full name]</strong> ([dates]).
    <span class="figure-credit">[Photographer or unknown]. [Collection]. [License].</span>
  </figcaption>
</figure>
```

Do not generate a face to fill the `src`.
