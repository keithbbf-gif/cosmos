The little beetles in the eye are not the green June show-offs. They are nitidulids. UC IPM names *Carpophilus hemipterus*, the driedfruit beetle, and the vinegar flies that ride with them. They walk yeasts and bacteria into a ripe fig. The fig then does what fruit does in a warm wet week: it ferments on the tree.

That smell is the ID. Pink syrup at the ostiole. Gas. A necklace that hangs and sags and then paints the leaf underneath. People call it blight. It is a cafeteria.

UC’s sour-rot chapter is the one I trust for the microbiology and the no. *Candida*, *Hanseniaspora*, *Pichia*, *Saccharomyces* — you do not need the Latin in the driveway. You need the sentence **no bactericide or yeasticide is recommended**. Prompt pick. Prompt cleanup. Air in the canopy so dropped fruit cooks in sun instead of sitting in a wet room.

I learned that the year I left for two days in the middle of a Celeste week and came home to a necklace I could smell from the gate. I wanted a spray. I needed a bucket.

## How they get in

An open eye. A split after rain. A bird peck. A June beetle hole. The ostiole on a ripe common fig is a door. Closed-eye types — Celeste, Alma, Texas Everbearing in TAMU’s souring table — keep that door smaller. They still sour if you leave them past ripe in a steam-bath 8a week. They sour less often than a wide-open Magnolia-type story. Relative. Not a vaccine.

LSU Gold’s writeups mention a partially open eye and cracking after rain. That is a pick-sooner name, not a spray name. Gold’s moderate rust note is a different sentence. Do not mash them.

The beetle does not invent the yeast. The yard already has yeast. The beetle is the taxi.

Fig endosepsis is a caprifig / *Blastophaga* problem. Common figs in Arkansas are not waiting on a wasp. I will not import California orchard panic into a Celeste row. Our souring is yeast, a door, and a late pick.

[Pick The Right Fig](https://figroots.com/2025/07/02/pick-the-right-fig-for-you/) already owns the one-line version: tight eye if August stays wet. This page is the beetle chapter of that same week.

## A morning walk when the necklace starts

I pick every morning. A ripe fig is a morning job on this place anyway. Souring makes it a rule. I take a bowl for keepers and a bucket for the vinegar ones. They do not share.

I take the vinegar ones off. They do not stay as “maybe jam.” They are beetle bait. Trash or a hot compost that is not under the tree. I will not be precious.

I walk the ground. Windfall is a second necklace. UC said remove fallen and overripe fruit of *any* kind, not just figs. A peach dump next to the fig is a beetle farm. A dropped apple from the neighbor’s tree is a beetle farm I cannot fully manage. I still pick mine.

I look at doors. Bird pecks. June beetle shoulders. Splits after a two-inch rain. Those figs are already a taxi stand. They come off.

I do not fog the ripe canopy. You are about to eat the skin. Residue arguments are for people who did not want the fruit. Kids grab low fruit. Dogs invent recipes. The ostiole holds what it holds even if you rinse.

I do not hang a yeast trap and call the tree saved. Orchard bait traps exist for monitoring driedfruit beetles. A backyard soda bottle is a hobby. If it makes you walk the tree, fine. If it replaces the pick, it is a toy.

I do not blame mosaic. I do not blame rust. A painted leaf and an orange thumb are other folders. They do not pour pink syrup out of an eye.

## What I will not blame

Mosaic. Rust. A fertilizer I skipped. Those can weaken a tree. They do not pour pink syrup out of an eye.

I will blame an open eye plus rain plus a late pick. I will blame a variety I planted because a photo was pretty. I will blame myself for a vacation in fig week.

I will blame a net that I treated as a harvest. Bird mesh keeps some birds off. It does not keep a 3-millimeter beetle off. People want one cloth to do every job. I want a pick.

I will not blame a “yeasticide” I refused to buy. There isn’t a recommended one in the UC chapter I trust.

## 8a rain is part of the insect

We do not have California dry-down. We have a Gulf-ish August. Tight-eye talk on FigRoots is a fruit-quality chapter. This is the beetle chapter of the same week. Splitters after a two-inch rain are doors. Birds open doors. Green June beetles (*Cotinis nitida* here, not the Southwestern *C. mutabilis*) open doors. Sap beetles keep office hours.

A west pot on gravel can split fruit from heat and then invite the same taxi. Water the dirt. Do not water the midnight leaf and call it a fruit program.

Air in the canopy matters. A hedge of figs that never saw a saw is a wet room. Dropped fruit sits. Yeast sits. I open a window with a limb I should have taken in winter, not with a jug in August.

After a tropical rain week — we have a later draft for that — I walk sooner, not later. Splitters do not wait for your Saturday.

## Closed eye is time, not a miracle

Celeste has kept a lot of 8a yards out of the worst necklace years because the door is small. I have still soured Celeste I picked late after a steam-bath week. Relative. I do not change varieties over one late week of my own making.

If a tree necklaces every year and the eye is wide, I stop recommending that name for a wet 8a hole. I do not write a spray card to save the name.

If a closed-eye tree necklaces because I picked late, I pick earlier next week. I do not change varieties over my own calendar.

LSU Gold can crack after rain. That is a morning alarm, not a copper morning. Copper is a rust argument the states already disagree on. It is not a souring tool.

## What I tell a person who wants to spray the necklace

I tell them UC said no. I tell them I will not name a fruit-tree cocktail. I tell them a commercial planting that thinks it needs a labeled insecticide is a conversation with an agent and a label that says fig. **[VERIFY]** if you are that farm. This folder is a backyard bowl.

I tell them to pick. Then pick again. Then take the vinegar off so the taxi loses the depot.

I tell them Jack already said birds are the part of fig season he likes least after a dead tree. Birds open doors. Net the tree you care about, on a frame, staked, checked. Then still pick. The beetle does not read the netting post.

## The necklace is a decision

If a tree necklaces every year and the eye is wide, I stop recommending that name for a wet 8a hole.

If I planted a pretty photo, I own the taxi.

The little beetle is honest. It shows up where you left sugar. Take the sugar inside.

I have stood at the gate after those two days away and wanted a protocol. I took a bucket instead. The smell left with the fruit. The next morning I picked earlier. That is the whole chemistry I will stand on.
