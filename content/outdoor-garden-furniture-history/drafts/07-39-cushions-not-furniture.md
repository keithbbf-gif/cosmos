If the sit disappears when the cushion disappears, you bought a pillow with a chair attached.

I like a cushion. I like not sitting on July steel. I like a porch rocker with a pad. I do not like a sectional whose personality is six rectangles of foam that cannot live in the climate the frame was sold for. The frame is the furniture. The cushion is a layer. Layers move. The villa knew it. The conservatory knew it. The American patio keeps forgetting because the photograph looks unfinished without the rectangles.

Foam holds water if the cover and the fill were a lie. Quick-dry foams and drained bottoms are real products. So are foams that become a wet loaf and then a science project. Zippers rust. Piping wears. Dark textile cooks a sit. Light textile shows mildew first. None of that is mysterious. All of it is a ritual: stand on edge to dry, store dry, do not trap a wet cushion in a vinyl shroud in the sun. A cover can be a greenhouse. I have cooked more than one well-meant cover into a mildew tent.

"Indoor-outdoor fabric" is a textile rating, not a frame rating, not a foam rating, not a zipper rating. I will say that until the hyphen loses its magic. You can have a noble textile on a rotten foam on a rusted zipper on an honest teak chair. Three calendars. One photograph.

Storage square footage is part of the furniture specification. If the household has no dry place, specify slings, mesh, wood slats, or stone. Do not specify a cloud of foam and a wish. I have walked through enough garages where last year's patio is a leaning stack of rectangles. That stack is a design failure, not a character failure.

Ties and non-skid bottoms are not fussy extras. A cushion that walks is a trip. A cushion that walks into a grill is a story. Specify how it stays. Indoor we already fight a seat pad that skates. Outdoor the skate is toward a flame or a step.

This shop builds upholstered indoor work when the brief wants it, in a room. Adjacent means I already know foam and fabric as systems. I will not let a patio fabric chip authorize an indoor showroom cushion left on a slab. BBF collections that show a dining chair with an upholstered seat are indoor neighbors. The patio cushion essay is here so nobody captions those seats as outdoor-ready.

Piping wears where a person sits down a thousand times. That wear is a calendar. Dark piping on a light cushion shows dirt; light piping on a dark cushion shows mildew first. I do not care which you pick if you pick it on purpose. I care that the household is not shocked that a textile edge is the first thing to look tired. Edges always look tired first. That is true on a dining chair welt and it is true on a patio rectangle. The patio just invites more grit to the party.

If you make furniture, design the cushion out of the sit and see if the object still exists. If you buy furniture, ask where the rectangles sleep in January. If you are a designer, put that sleep on the plan. A closet, a chest, a bench with a lid that can take wet — those are furniture. The sky is not a closet.

A lid chest for cushions is a better spend than a fourth lounge chair nobody uses. I will die on that small hill. Storage is seating's parent. Without a parent, the rectangles become orphans in a garage, and orphans do not get dry.

Shop close now. Fasteners, finish, winter, reading a patio, and how BBF collections sit beside this weather without lying.
