# Zone 9 month by month

Gulf 9 and interior 9 are
cousins, not twins. I write
both into the months. You
will know which sentence is
yours by whether August is
a steam room or a furnace.

**January.** Growth may pause
more than sleep. Water
when the pot is light.
Have a sheet for the
rare hard night. Do not
build a Zone 7 garage
religion unless you are
on a cold edge.

**February.** Early move.
Prune. Pot up. A freeze
can still visit. Cover
the tender tips.

**March.** Full growth in
many years. Food. Watch
a late dip. Breba is a
real sentence here if
you kept wood.

**April.** Easy month if
you remember May is
coming. Set habits.
Pinch. Do not put black
cans on the west slab
"just for now."

**May.** Water becomes the
job. Fruit moving. A
good month to stop
collecting pots and
start keeping them.

**June.** Heat. Harvest on
early plants. Afternoon
shade for roots if the
site is cruel. Gulf:
humidity and rust
start.

**July.** The skillet.
Morning soak, maybe
more. Fabric and light
colors earn their keep.
Interior: hydrophobic
mix. Gulf: wet leaves
at night are rust.

**August.** Stall is
normal on some fruit.
Do not panic-pick green.
Do not flood a cooked
root ball. Shade the
can. Keep picking what
is actually ripe.

**September.** Heat
breaks in many places.
Fruit finishes. This is
the month people decide
figs are easy. Remember
July.

**October.** Still a crop
in a long year. First
cool nights. You can
feed a little longer
than Zone 7, not
forever. Watch the
first real front.

**November.** Clean up.
A mild winter starts.
Check scale because
you may not get a
honest leaf-off.

**December.** Quiet-ish.
Water. One eye on the
forecast. If you are
Gulf-wet, drains. If
you are interior-dry,
do not assume rain did
the job.

Gulf 9: your October can
still be a crop month
and a rust month. Keep
picking and keep air
moving. Interior 9: your
June can cook a black
can before the plant has
finished setting. Shade
the wall of the pot
before you shade your
own schedule.

Do not import a Zone 7
"stop feeding in August"
as a religion if the
plant is still working a
real crop in September
and the wood is already
mature. Do import the
habit of not pushing
soft tips into a week
the forecast finally
turns mean.

Zone 9 pot culture is a
summer sport. The people
who lose plants here
lose them in July on a
hot can, or in a
one-night January they
were told never happens.
Plan those two, and the
rest is picking. Picking
is the fun you earned
by not cooking the
roots.
