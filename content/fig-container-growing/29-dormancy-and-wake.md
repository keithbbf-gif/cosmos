# Leaf drop, sleep, and wake

A fig is supposed to look dead in
winter. New growers send photos of
a bare stick and a caption that
sounds like a eulogy. Scratch the
wood. Green under the bark is a
live plant. Brown and dry all the
way down is a dead one. Start at
the tip and walk backward. Tips
die first.

Leaf drop is triggered by short
days and cold, not by your
feelings. In Zone 9 a plant may
hold leaves longer and only half-
drop in a mild winter. That is
all right. In Zone 7 a freeze
will take them in a night. Do
not feed a plant to "keep it
going" into November. Let it
go.

Sleep is cold and quiet. I said
this in the winter-water draft
and I will keep saying it: do
not wake a fig in December with
a warm room and a grow light
unless you have a reason that
includes mites and pale leaves
and a job you wanted.

Wake is slower than people
allow. Buds swell. A little
green shows. The first leaves
are a lighter color. This can
start in the garage if the
garage warmed up, which is why
I do not put pots next to a
hot water heater. A plant that
wakes early is a plant you
will harden like a tomato
start.

I do not prune at the first
swell if I can still get a
freeze. I wait until I can see
what is alive. Then I cut. If
you cut too early you guess.
Guessing is how you remove the
only live arm.

A pot fig can wake from the
top and look late at the
bottom. That is sun and
temperature, not two
varieties. Give it time.

If nothing moves by the time
the neighbor's in-ground fig
is fully leafed, scratch
again. Check the roots if you
can. A plant with live wood
and dead roots is a delay,
not a future. A plant with
dead wood and live roots will
sprout from the base. Keep it
and rebuild. You lost the
breba. You did not lose the
plant.

Scratch test: fingernail,
not a knife show. If you
find brown, step down an
inch. Live wood can hide
under a dead tip for a
foot. Mark the last green
with a pencil and wait
before you cut to the
stool.

The emotional part: bare figs
are ugly. People throw them
out in January. Wait until
May before you decide a
silent plant is a dead one,
especially after a hard
winter. I have had pots
sprout in June from wood I
had already insulted. The
pot gives you the chance to
wait without a hole in the
yard. Use the wait. A hole
in the yard is permanent.
A pot can sit in the
shame corner until June.
