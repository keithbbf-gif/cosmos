# Fabric grow bags in practice

I recommended fabric in the
materials draft. This is the
draft about living with them.

They slump. A 20-gallon bag
is not a 20-gallon cylinder
in August. Buy tall, and
oversize if you need depth.
A cheap thin bag becomes a
puck. A stiffer bag holds a
shape and a handle.

They dry faster than plastic.
In Zone 7 that is often a
virtue — fewer swamp weeks.
In Zone 9 it is a hose
schedule. If you cannot
water a fabric 15-gallon on
a west patio, do not own a
fabric 15-gallon on a west
patio. Own plastic in a
light color, or own more
gallons of fabric.

Roots grow into the fabric.
That is the air-prune. When
you repot you will cut or
tear. That is normal. Do
not treat it as a failure
of the bag.

Algae and salts show on the
outside. It looks poor. It
is information. A white
crust is your tap. A green
film is wet and shade.
Neither is a reason to
throw the bag away. Both
are a reason to look at
water.

Winter: a wet fabric bag
left on a Zone 7 deck is
a cold, evaporating root
ball. I prefer them on a
board in the garage, not
on concrete. They freeze
faster than a thick
plastic can. The
portability is the
compensation — grab the
handles and go. If the
handles tear, you did
not buy a bag, you
bought a season.

I set bags on slats or
caddies. On soil they
root through if you
leave them long enough,
and then you have an
in-ground fig with extra
steps. On a solid saucer
they sit in a diaper.
The third thing — air
under the bag — is the
one I want.

Sun eats print and cheap
fabric. I do not expect
a bag to be an heirloom.
I expect three to five
honest years. When the
side wall thins, I
retire it under a
plastic can as a liner
or I throw it away.
I do not sew fig bags
as a personality.

Handles fail at the
worst time — wet, full,
frost night. I lift
with a hand under the
bottom as well as the
handles. If a handle
pops, the plant still
has a chance if you
were not trusting a
strip of faded cloth
with the whole year.

If a bag goes sour, it
is the mix and the
drain, not the religion
of fabric. Dump it.
Rinse the bag. Start
again. The material is
a tool. It is not a
virtue. Plastic is
allowed. Clay is
allowed. The fig does
not grade you on
textiles.
