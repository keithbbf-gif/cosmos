# Reservoirs and ollas — when they help

I like a hose and a habit.
I also leave town. This is
the honest use of a
reservoir: a buffer for
absence, not a lifestyle
that replaces looking at
the plant.

Self-watering pots with a
true reservoir and a wick
or a soil column can carry
a fig through a long
weekend in June. They can
also keep the bottom third
of the mix wet in
November until the roots
there give up. If you use
one, use it in the growing
season and dry the
reservoir when the plant
sleeps. Do not leave a
Zone 7 pot sitting on a
full tank in a cold
garage.

Cheap "self-watering"
inserts that do not
separate the root ball
from a stagnant tank are
how you grow anaerobic
mud. If you cannot see
how the overflow works,
do not trust it with a
plant you like.

Ollas — unglazed clay
vessels buried in the
mix — leak water slowly.
In a large pot in dry
Zone 9 they can even
out the middle of the
ball. In a small pot
they steal volume. In
a wet Zone 8 they are
often a solution to a
problem you do not
have. Fill them. Do
not also flood the pot
on the same schedule
or you have built a
swamp with extra
steps.

Wicking from a tray
works for smaller
dwarfs if you are
careful about
mosquitoes and you
empty the tray when
you come home. It
does not work as a
permanent lake.

What I will not do:
ice bottles, constant
drip on a timer that
no one checks, and a
reservoir as an excuse
for a 5-gallon plant
in August sun. The
tool cannot invent
volume.

If you travel every
month in summer, you
need a person or you
need to cluster pots
in bright shade and
accept a slower crop.
A reservoir is a
helper. It is not a
gardener.

Mosquitoes will use any
open reservoir you
forget. A lid, a bit
of screen, or emptying
is not optional if the
pot lives near a door.
I have slapped a
shoulder on a July
evening and blamed the
neighbor. It was my
tank.

When I use one, I
still lift the pot.
The reservoir can be
full and the top dry.
The reservoir can be
empty and the mix
still wet. The plant
does not fill out a
form. You still have
to look. Looking is
the product. The
reservoir is a delay
on looking, not a
replacement.
