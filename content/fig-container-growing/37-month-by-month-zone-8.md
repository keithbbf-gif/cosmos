# Zone 8 month by month

Zone 8 is longer than Zone 7
and wetter than a lot of
Zone 9 interiors. The
calendar has more growing
and more rot.

**January.** Pots grouped and
wrapped, or in a shed for
the ugly week. Drains
clear. A dry-ish mix in
the rain is the project.
Check after storms.

**February.** Warm spells.
Buds move. Have cover.
Do not roll everything
into full sun for the
first 70°F day. Prune on
a late-month quiet spell
if the wood is readable.

**March.** Late freeze still
exists. Breba is exposed.
Sheets in the bin. Growth
starts in earnest in 8b.
Light food when leaves
are real.

**April.** Full season. Water
as the days get bright.
Pinch early wild shoots.
This is a better month
than June to pot up.

**May.** Breba in some
years. Main crop setting.
Watch water. A dry May
and you will drop.

**June.** Humid. Rust
starts its story. Water
the soil in the morning.
Harvest breba. Net as
needed.

**July.** Heat plus wet or
heat plus drought,
depending on your year.
Both can split fruit.
Keep the mix even. Do
not crowd pots into a
still corner.

**August.** Heavy main crop
on a happy plant. Pick.
Last feed should be
tapering. Watch souring
after Gulf rain.

**September.** Still a
growing month here.
Fruit continues. You
have more time than
Zone 7. Do not waste it
by starting a jungle of
soft tips you will wrap
in December.

**October.** Crop winding
down. First cool nights.
Ease the nitrogen
mentality. Still water.
Still pick.

**November.** Leaves may
hang longer than in
Zone 7. A frost will
take them. Clean up.
Decide which pots stay
out with wraps and
which share the shed
with the Christmas
boxes.

**December.** Rain. Cold
snaps. Saucers empty.
The awkward middle: not
asleep like Zone 7, not
casual like Zone 9.
Walk the pots after
every mean forecast.

Coastal 8 and inland 8
do not share a winter
personality. If you can
smell the marsh, rot is
your winter. If you
can see a west Texas
horizon, cold snaps and
dry pots are. Write
your own December
accordingly.

Do not copy a Zone 7
garage calendar if your
nights are 40°F and
rain. You will rot
what you meant to save.
Do not copy a Zone 9
casual calendar if you
got 14°F in 2021 and
you remember it. You
remember it.

Zone 8 rewards the person
who has a wall, a wrap,
and a habit of checking
drains in winter. It
punishes the person who
says easy and means
ignored. Easy is a
summer word. Winter is
a door you can still
open.
