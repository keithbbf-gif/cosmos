# Yellow leaves, honestly sorted

Yellow is not one disease. It is
a color leaves turn when the
plant is done with them, or when
the roots cannot work, or when
the food cannot get in, or when
it is November.

**November yellow**, bottom
leaves, plant otherwise fine:
let them go. This is the
beginning of sleep. People
treat it like a crisis and
water. Then they have a wet
crisis.

**Wet yellow**, often with
droop, sometimes a sour smell:
the mix is a swamp or the
drain is a rumor. Stop
watering. Check the holes.
If the roots are brown
noodles, you are in rescue —
dry down, maybe a fresh
open mix, maybe a smaller
pot. More fertilizer is
not the rescue.

**Dry yellow**, crisp edges,
pot light: you waited too
long. Water in passes. Do
not congratulate yourself
with food the same day.

**Yellow between veins on
new leaves**, green ribs:
chlorosis. In this band, on
pots, I look at tap water
and pH and a locked-up
iron before I look at a
mysterious virus. Figs can
carry mosaic. Mosaic is
usually mottling and
puckering, not a clean
iron pattern. A chelated
iron can help if the mix
is alkaline from months of
hard water. So can leaching
and a mix reset. See the
salt draft.

**Yellow with rust specks**:
rust. Read that draft. The
yellow is the leaf leaving
because the rust asked it
to.

**Yellow after a feed**:
you burned it. Water
through. Do not feed
again because it looks
hungry. It looks hurt.

**One yellow branch**:
look at that wood. Scale,
a broken connection, a
girdle from twine you
forgot. Not always the
whole plant.

I do not start at the
garden center virus chart.
I start at the pot
weight, the drain, the
calendar, and the newest
leaves versus the oldest.
Old leaves yellowing
first is often normal or
nitrogen moving around.
New leaves yellowing
first is chemistry or
roots.

Mosaic virus is common on
figs and often cosmetic —
splotched leaves, a plant
that still fruits. I do
not throw a good fruiter
for mosaic. I throw a
plant that cannot drink
or cannot drain. Those
are the yellows I can
fix. Mosaic I can live
with if the crop is
real.

A photo of a yellow leaf
without the pot, the
site, and the week of
the year is a rorschach.
If you are asking someone
for help, send those
three. If you are
helping yourself, use
those three. The leaf
is the press release.
The pot is the story.
