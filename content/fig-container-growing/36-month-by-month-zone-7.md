# Zone 7 month by month

This is a working calendar, not
a promise. Piedmont and
Ozarks and the colder edges
of the Mid-South do not share
a week. Shift it by your last
frost and your first.

**January.** Pots in the
garage or wrapped. Check
weight. No food. Look for
mice. Do not prune unless
you must.

**February.** Still asleep if
you did it right. A warm
week is a liar. Keep them
in. Late month, if buds
are swelling in a mild
year, you can look at
wood and plan cuts. Do
not put them on the south
wall and walk away.

**March.** The late-freeze
month. Start hardening
dormant plants on the east
side on kind days. Back in
for mean nights. Prune
when you can see life.
Do not feed yet.

**April.** Out for good when
the forecast has lost its
teeth. First water as
growth starts. Light food
after you see real leaves.
Watch a late dip.

**May.** Growth. Pinch if
the plant is a fountain.
Pot up only if you must.
Breba, if you kept wood,
is visible. Do not drought
it.

**June.** Water becomes a
habit. Breba ripens in a
good year. Net that plant
only. Main crop is small
green. Do not brag to
people who count pints
yet.

**July.** Heat. Daily water
for small pots. Watch
split and drop. Stop
thinking about new
varieties; keep the ones
you have alive.

**August.** Last serious
feed is already behind
you or ending now. Wood
should start thinking
about hardening. Fruit
should be moving. Rust
shows. Thin air into
the bush.

**September.** Main crop if
the year was kind. Pick
every day. Water even.
Do not start a nitrogen
story. Watch the first
cool nights.

**October.** Last fruit.
First frost talk. Ease
water as growth slows.
Do not pot up. Do not
force new leaves.

**November.** Leaves down
or going. Clean the pot.
Last inspection for
scale. Into the garage
after the first real
frost, not after the
first cool morning.

**December.** Quiet. A
drink if the pot went
light. The plant looks
like a stick. Leave it
alone. That is the job.

Shift this earlier if you
are 7a on a hill. Shift
it later if you are 7b
against a city heat
island. The month names
are a courtesy. Your
frost dates are the
law.

Do not start cuttings in
October here and expect
them to be plants by
Thanksgiving. Do not
repot in leaf in
September. Do not take
a vacation in the first
ripe week without a
picker.

If you do nothing else,
do November and March
well. Those two months
are where Zone 7 pot
figs are won. July
only keeps them. The
shoulders of the year
decide if July had
anything to keep.
