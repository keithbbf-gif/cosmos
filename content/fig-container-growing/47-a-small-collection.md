# More than one pot without a jungle

One fig is a plant. Four
is a project. Eight is a
second job you will
resent on frost night.
I like three. I have
owned more. The more
was not always wiser.

Space them so air moves.
Rust and scale love a
huddle all summer. You
can huddle in winter
for warmth. That is a
different season. In
July I want a walkway
and a leaf that can
dry.

Do not buy two plants
that do the same job.
Three brown workhorses
is not a collection. It
is a hesitation. Give
each pot a role:
hardy, humid, flavor.
Or early, main, dwarf.
Roles keep you from
the eighth "maybe this
one is the one."

Watering a line of pots
is faster if they are
the same size and mix.
You learn one weight.
A 7-gallon, a 15, a
barrel, and a fabric
oddment is four
schedules. I will mix
sizes for a reason. I
will not mix them
because the pots were
on sale.

The winter route must
fit the whole set. If
the garage takes three
and you own six, you
do not own six. You
own three and a story.
Count the floor before
you count the varieties.

I keep a page — not
software, a page —
with the name I
believe, the year I
potted, the gallon,
and whether it
fruited. If it did
not fruit in year
three and the culture
was decent, the plant
loses its pot. That
sounds cold. The
jungle is colder,
because then nothing
gets water on time.

Share fruit. A small
collection that feeds
a neighbor is the
right size. A
collection that feeds
the compost because
you could not pick
is the wrong size.

A good test: can
you move the whole
set on frost night
in under an hour
without calling
anyone? If not,
the set is larger
than your winter.
Reduce before
November, not
during.

If you are reading
this series in order,
you already know the
work. The collection
draft is only this:
the pot is the unit.
Each unit needs a
winter, a drink, and
a reason. When you
cannot name the
reason, you are
done adding. You
are allowed to be
done. The figs
will not take it
personally. They
will take the
water you can
still give. That
water, split eight
ways on a July
Saturday, is how
collections go
quiet.
