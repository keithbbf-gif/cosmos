## Figure markup

Each draft embeds one block:

- `<!-- figure:commons -->`
- `<figure class="desk-entry-figure">` with descriptive `alt`, `loading="lazy"`, `decoding="async"`
- `<figcaption>` starting with `Figure 1.` and pointing here

Re-embed after manifest changes:

```bash
python3 tools/embed_desk_entryway_figures.py
```
