# Editorial calendar — SLPWOW pediatric speech & language milestones

Forty-two parent-facing drafts. Status: `draft` / `stage: draft`. Nothing here is cleared for the live slpwow.com sitemap.

Speech and language from birth through five: how to read a chart, what CDC and ASHA actually printed, hearing, two languages, late talking as a *research* description, and how to talk with a pediatrician or an SLP — without diagnosing anyone.

All files live under `content/slp-pediatric-milestones-blog/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`. Guardrails: `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`.

This pack is complementary to `content/slpwow-speech-pathology-history/` (clinician-curious history). Do not merge the calendars. Do not file these under shop or “book now.”

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-two essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):

| Wave | Weeks | Pieces | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — how to read the chart | 1–6 | 01, 16, 30, 39, 42, 22 | Percentiles, understanding vs talking, the doctor visit, wait-and-see, a list that is not a test, hearing |
| 2 — the first year | 7–14 | 02, 03, 04, 05, 17, 18 | Sounds, babble, pointing, gestures, joint attention |
| 3 — words arrive | 15–24 | 06, 07, 08, 09, 15, 24 | First words through fifty words; what “late talking” studies measured |
| 4 — preschool talk | 25–34 | 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 20, 21 | Conversation, stories, sounds, intelligibility, questions, little grammar |
| 5 — the life around the words | 35–44 | 19, 23, 28, 29, 33, 34, 35, 40 | Play, two languages, books, screens, friends, directions, pronouns, literacy |
| 6 — doors, not diagnoses | 45–54 | 25, 26, 27, 31, 32, 36, 37, 38, 41 | Disfluency, voice, feeding, EI, the SLP visit, prematurity, mouths, baby sign, AAC |

Hold any piece that still carries `[CITE NEEDED]` or `[VERIFY]` on a number you would print.
