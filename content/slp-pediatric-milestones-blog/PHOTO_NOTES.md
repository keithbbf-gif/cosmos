# Photo notes — SLPWOW pediatric milestones pack

This series is parent-facing education about communication development. It is **not** a patient gallery.

## Default

- **No photographs of children** in this pack — including stock toddlers, silhouettes, or “happy family at table” hero images.
- **No AI-generated faces** of any age. No synthetic “patient” scenes.
- Featured images and in-article figures use **in-house SVG schematics** only until a licensed editor clears something else in `RIGHTS.md`.

## Allowed later (with a row in `RIGHTS.md` before publish)

- Clinic or practice photos **without identifiable minors**.
- CDC or ASHA **screenshots of public checklists** if license and cropping rules allow (prefer linking out).
- Simple objects (a checklist on a clipboard drawn as schematic ink, not a photo of a real child’s chart).

## Never

- Stock “sad child” / “delayed speech” imagery used as decoration.
- Before/after therapy montages implying guaranteed outcomes.
- Photos that could be read as documenting a real patient in this practice.

## WordPress

Per `WP_IMPORT.md`: no stock sad-toddler featured images. Pair posts with series SVGs from `assets/` and captions in `embeds/`.
