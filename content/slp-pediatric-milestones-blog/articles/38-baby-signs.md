**Disclaimer.** Educational only. Not a sign-language curriculum and not a diagnosis. Baby sign is optional. It is not a treatment you failed to provide.

CDC’s 9-month tips say you can teach simple baby sign language to help a baby tell you what they want before words. That sentence is permission, not a mandate.

## What the Goodwyn, Acredolo, and Brown study did

Goodwyn, Acredolo, and Brown (2000, *Journal of Nonverbal Behavior*) studied symbolic gesturing and later spoken language. Families who were taught to use a set of signs saw some advantages in early verbal measures compared with controls — **modest**, not magical, and not a reason to buy a deluxe kit. Other studies and reviews have been mixed. If a brand promises “no tantrums” and “genius vocabulary,” they are advertising.

This series will not reprint a sign list as homework.

## Signs are gestures with a fan club

Essay 17. A homemade *more* (fingertips together) is a symbol. So is a point. So is *all done* if your house made it up. ASL is a full language; “baby sign” is usually a handful of borrowed signs plus inventions. If you have a Deaf parent or a true ASL home, that is a different, richer story — use the language of the home, not a toy version.

ASHA’s bilingual logic applies: a child can have more than one way to mean things. Signs do not “use up” the speech slot. The fear that signing stops talking is the same family of myth as “AAC will stop talking” (essay 41; Romski & Sevcik, 2005, on AAC myths). People talk when talking works. Extra ways to mean things usually lower the temperature.

## How to use a few signs without a curriculum

Pick the ones that save a fight: more, all done, milk, help. Do the sign **and** the word. When they do the sign, you still say the word. You are pairing, not replacing.

If they never sign and they point, you already have a symbol system. Do not decide you failed.

If they sign and do not point or look at you, mention the cluster (essays 17–18). Signs are not a full substitute for shared attention.

## Two languages plus signs

That is three channels. Count them on paper. Tell the SLP. Do not drop the home spoken language to make room for English signs from an app.

## When a class helps, when it does not

A library class that makes you wave and sing is a playdate. A class that tells you your child is behind because they missed *please* in week six is a class to quit.

If communication is hard across **all** channels, a few baby signs will not be the whole plan. Ask a person. Signs can still be part of the Tuesday.

## Ordinary ending

You are allowed to use your hands. You are allowed not to. CDC offered a tool. Acredolo’s group offered a modest study. Advertisers offered a dream. Keep the first two. Leave the dream at checkout.

When the word arrives, you can keep the sign for a while. Channels can run together. That is not confusion. That is a household that likes to be understood.

## ASL is not “baby sign plus”

If someone in the house is Deaf or the family has chosen ASL as a language, you are raising a bilingual (or multilingual) child in the ASHA sense. Use full language, full faces, full communities — not a ten-sign kit. This essay is for the hearing household that wants a few symbols before words. It is not a Deaf-education plan and should not pretend to be.

Goodwyn, Acredolo, and Brown (2000) did not study replacing a language. They studied extra gestures in speaking families. Keep the study in its box.

If a class mixes “baby sign” with a claim that you can skip speech therapy, leave the class. Signs can sit beside a look from an SLP. They cannot cancel a look you already wanted.

## A tiny set that earns its keep

*More, all done, help, milk.* Those four settle more fights than a 100-sign poster. Teach them in the moment they are true: the last cracker, the end of the swing, the stuck lid. If a sign never gets used in a real moment, drop it. Unused signs are wallpaper.

When the spoken word arrives, you can fade the sign or keep it for the loud park. Keeping it is not a failure to talk. It is a bilingual household of hand and mouth, which is allowed (essay 23’s logic, smaller). If nothing arrives in any channel, the tiny set was never the whole plan — go back to essays 30 and 31.
