I wrapped a fig beautifully once. By morning the tarp
was in the neighbor's yaupon and the dog had pulled a
necklace of burlap down the drive. The fig was fine. My
pride was not. Winter protection that cannot survive a
household is not protection. It is a craft project.

Dogs like leaf cages. They smell like mice and you. Kids
like tarps and stakes. Wind likes everything you did not
tie twice. If this is your house, you design for that,
not for a photo.

I put the cage where a ball is less likely to land. I
tie like I am mad at the wind. I do not use pins. I do
not leave long tails of plastic for a puppy. I put a
rock on the hat, then I put a second rock because I
remember the first rock. I tell the kids the cage is
boring, which is a lie, but it is a useful lie.

A wrap that becomes litter is worse than no wrap. It
wraps a storm drain. It wraps a goat if you have one.
It makes you not want to wrap again, which is how a
young fig loses its second winter.

Zone 7 structures stay up so long they have to be kid-
proof for months. That means real wire, not vibes.
Zone 9 cloths go on at dusk and should not be a
seasonal installation a toddler can drag. 8a is the
length of time in which a wrap can start honest and
end in a tree down the street.

If your dog is a digger, the crown mound needs a
collar — hardware cloth flat on the mulch, or a ring
the dog respects. Roots that were going to live will
not live if they are a toy. That is not a fig essay
when it happens. It is a dog essay. The plant does
not care which essay you meant.

I once used orchard netting as a wrap
helper. The dog wore it as a cape. The
fig wore nothing by morning. Funny in
April. Less funny at 14°F. Now the
netting stays on summer fruit and the
winter wrap is wire and rocks, which
are boring enough that only the wind
and I care.

If you have a goat, this whole essay
is shorter: you do not have a wrap,
you have a snack. Fence first. Figs
second. I say that as someone who
has watched a goat vote.

I walk the wrap with the dog on the first night
so he gets bored of it while I am there to say
no. That sounds silly. It works better than a
speech. Kids get the same tour: this is boring,
do not pull the string, here is a stick that is
not the fig.

If the wrap fails because of the household, I
rebuild simpler, not fancier. Fancy is what
failed. Rocks and wire are what stay.
If the household cannot live with a cage, choose dieback or a pot you can hide. Do not choose a prettier wrap. Prettier leaves.
