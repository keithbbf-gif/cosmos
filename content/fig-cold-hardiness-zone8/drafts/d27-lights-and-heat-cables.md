Old incandescent Christmas lights put out a little heat.
People still talk about them like they are a fig tradition.
I have used a short string, on a timer, inside a breathable
wrap, on a young plant, for a night that was supposed to
hit 8°F. The plant lived. I also stayed half-awake because
I had put electricity in dry leaves on purpose.

That is the line. Heat is not a lifestyle in 8a. Heat is
a one-night argument with a vortex, and it needs a human
who will unplug it. I will not run a cable in a leaf cage
and go to a bowl game. I will not use indoor extension
cords in a puddle. I will not pile a space heater next to
burlap. I have seen what a "just this once" heater does
to a porch. It does not care that you loved the fig.

If you use lights, use outdoor-rated gear, keep them off
the mulch, keep the wrap breathable so you are not building
a kiln, and take them out when the night is over. C7/C9
incandescents are the ones people mean. LEDs will not do
this job. If all you have is LEDs, you do not have a heat
strategy. You have decoration.

Heat cables for pipe are tempting. They are also how you
spot-cook bark and start a smolder in straw. I do not
recommend them as a fig method. I mention them because
someone will try them after reading the word "cable" in a
forum. The forum does not come to the fire.

Zone 7 commercial people sometimes run tunnels with a
little heat. That is a system with vents and a person.
It is not a backyard string from 1998. Zone 9 should not
be plugging anything in. Their freeze is short. A cloth
and a wall will do. 8a should get the pots inside before
they get the lights out of the attic.

The best heat in this zone is still the ground and the
house wall. Electricity is the last tool, not the
personality of the yard.

If you are reaching for heat, ask whether
the pot can come inside instead. Heat is
what we invent when we do not want to
move a tub. I have been that person. The
honest order is: move, then wrap, then
— maybe — a short outdoor-rated string
on a plant I cannot move, on a night I
will be home.

I unplug at dawn. I do not leave a
"fig circuit" running because the week
looks cold. Weeks change. Leaves dry
and then they do not. The only heat
source I trust overnight without a
person is the ground. Everything else
is a guest that needs a bedtime.

I will not run a generator for a fig.
I say that because after a vortex the
brain gets grand. The fig is a luxury
in that week. The pipes are not. If
the power is out, the wrap and the
ground are what they are. I do not
invent fire.
