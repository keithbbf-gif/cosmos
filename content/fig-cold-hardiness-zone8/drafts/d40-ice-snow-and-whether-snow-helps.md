Snow on the crown is a decent blanket. Snow
is mostly air. A few inches over a mulched
stool in 8a is free insulation I did not
have to buy. I do not sweep it off the
roots to "keep the plant clean." I let it
sit. I do sweep it off a wrap hat if the
hat is becoming a sagging bowl of slush
that will freeze into a lid.

Ice is not snow. Ice on canes is weight and
a glaze that holds the night against the
bark. Ice inside a plastic sleeve is a
problem I installed. An ice storm in a wet
8a is when I care about breakage more than
degrees. I do not shake canes like a
madman at midnight. I support what I can
and I wait. Broken wood in February is
still often a living plant in May.

A snow that melts into the leaf cage and
refreezes is the hybrid I hate. That is
when I vent on the thaw day, even if I
did not want to fuss. Wet-frozen leaves
are not dead air anymore. They are a
block.

Zone 7 knows snow as a season and uses it.
Zone 9 meets ice as a freak and should
not respond by wrapping for the rest of
February. 8a gets both in the same decade:
a pretty snow that helps, and a glaze that
takes a cane at the crotch.

If the forecast is ice, I would rather have
a plant unwrapped and flexible than a plant
in a sail of plastic. I will mound the
crown and let the top take what it takes.
I can grow new canes. I cannot un-break a
split trunk I created with a frozen tarp.

I do not beat ice off canes with
a stick. I have split a crotch
that way and then blamed the
storm. If a cane is down, I prop
it and I wait for the thaw. There
will be a thaw in 8a. There is
almost always a thaw. Patience
is a winter tool that does not
fit in the bin.

Snow on pots is mixed. A hat of
snow on a pot sitting on dirt is
fine. A wet snow that soaks a
fabric pot on a pad and then
freezes is another brick. I brush
that off the pot rim so the melt
does not sit in the crown, and I
still want that pot in a garage
next time.

After an ice night I look at forks, not tips.
Tips I can lose. A split crotch on an old
stool is a years-long wound. If a fork is
holding a load of ice, I prop, I do not twist.
In the thaw I decide if that cane is a threat
to the rest of the plant. Sometimes the kind
cut is in March, not in the storm.

Snow that is still snow on Thursday and water
on Friday is 8a. Plan for both states in the
same wrap week.
