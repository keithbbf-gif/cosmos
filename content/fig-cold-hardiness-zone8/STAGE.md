# Staging sheet

Every file in `drafts/` carries YAML:

```yaml
status: staged
stage: ground | choose | wrap | hold | open
focus: 8a
zones: [7, 8a, 8b, 9]
cluster: hardiness | siting | wrapping | containers | spring
```

`status: staged` means the piece is in the editorial pipeline, not live.
It is not `published`. It is not `scratch`.

After the editor pass, each draft carries `voice_check: edited` in YAML
(writer staging used `voice: human`).

## Read order (if you want a winter, not a pile)

1. `d01` through `d08` — what the cold is doing
2. `d09` through `d21` — what you plant and where you put it
3. `d22` through `d33` — whether and how you wrap
4. `d34` through `d41` — January without fidgeting
5. `d42` through `d50` — March, the autopsy, the next September

## Required topics (coverage)

- Cold hardiness of wood, buds, roots, and leftover fruit
- Wrapping: timing, materials, cages, tip-and-bury, heat, unwrapping
- In-ground vs pot, including garage, buried pot, pot size, porch vs soil
- Zone 7 extras, Zone 8a as the default, Zone 8b attitude shift, Zone 9 restraint

If a later editor cuts the set down, keep at least one draft from each stage
and do not cut `d17` (in-ground vs pot) or `d22` (when to wrap).
