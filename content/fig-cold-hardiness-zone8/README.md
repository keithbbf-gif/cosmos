# Fig cold hardiness — Zone 7–9, 8a focus

Staged draft set. Not published copy. Not a catalog. Not a guarantee that your tree lives.

These pieces are for a person who already has a fig, or is about to buy one, and wants
the winter part told in a human voice. The center of gravity is **USDA Zone 8a**
(average annual extreme minimum **10–15°F**). Zone 7 and Zone 9 sit in the room
the whole time, because 8a people borrow methods from both and get hurt when they
borrow the wrong one.

## What “staged” means here

Editorial status on every draft is `status: staged` — ready for a human editor,
not live, not SEO-published.

The *winter* is also staged. Read in this order if you are actually trying to
keep wood:

| Stage | Folder tag | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ground` | understand the cold | Zone numbers, wood vs roots vs buds, wet vs dry, wind, duration |
| `choose` | plant and place | Varieties, in-ground vs pot, first year, walls, raised beds |
| `wrap` | put the coat on | Timing, materials, cages, tip-and-bury, heat, zone 7 extras, zone 9 restraint |
| `hold` | stay out of the way | Midwinter, water, garage, buried pots, ice, vortex years |
| `open` | take the coat off | Unwrap, prune late, autopsy, late frost, calendar |

A manifest lives in `_manifest.toml`. Drafts live in `drafts/`.

Graphics (staged, not live): each draft embeds one `<figure>` with an SEO `figcaption`.
Cleared rasters are in `assets/images/`; see `RIGHTS.md`, `GRAPHICS_INDEX.md`, and
`IMAGE_SOURCES.md`. Regenerate with `python3 tools/fig_zone8_graphics.py all`.

## Voice

First person, a yard in 8a, neighbors in 7 and 9. Specific temperatures. Opinions
with reasons. Catalog language gets pushed off the table. If a number is a
published rating (Chicago Hardy stems ~10°F, roots often cited near −20°F), the
draft says it is a rating, not a warranty.

## Do not treat this as

- A substitute for your county extension or a soil test
- Advice to run heat cables without a fire plan
- A promise that wrapping beats a polar-vortex week
- Variety identity. “Brown Turkey” in commerce is a crowd.
