- Wikipedia, “Samuel H. Fullerton.” Gulf Lumber / Fullerton, Louisiana; other mills. Use only where a draft needs the wider Fullerton story. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_H._Fullerton

## Craft, wood, finish (practice)

These are the bench books a serious shop already knows. Drafts draw on the common practice they describe. They are not quoted at length.

- R. Bruce Hoadley, *Understanding Wood: A Craftsman’s Guide to Wood Technology.* Taunton. Movement, moisture, grain, strength.
- R. Bruce Hoadley, *Identifying Wood.* Taunton.
- Ernest Joyce, revised by Alan Peters / others, *The Technique of Furniture Making* (often titled *Encyclopedia of Furniture Making* in later printings). Batsford / Sterling.
- Tage Frid, *Tage Frid Teaches Woodworking* (three volumes). Taunton. Joinery, shaping, furniture.
- James Krenov, *The Fine Art of Cabinetmaking* and *A Cabinetmaker’s Notebook.* Fine-work temperament; use as attitude, not as a brand pose.
- George Nakashima, *The Soul of a Tree.* George Braziller. Board selection, walnut, the slab — not a method manual.
- Thomas Moser, *How to Build Shaker Furniture* and later Thos. Moser shop writing. Chair under load, post-and-rung, honesty of construction.
- John Brown / Drew Langsner / Welsh stick-chair literature (Lost Art Press editions of Brown; Langsner’s *The Chairmaker’s Workshop*). Green wood, steam, rungs.
- Jennie Alexander and Peter Follansbee, *Make a Joint Stool from a Tree.* Lost Art Press. Drawboring, riven oak, period joints.
- Peter Follansbee, *Joiner’s Work.* Lost Art Press.
- Christopher Schwarz, *The Anarchist’s Design Book*, *The Anarchist’s Tool Chest*, *Campaign Furniture.* Lost Art Press. Workholding, joinery choices, shop culture. Do not ape the voice.
- Roy Underhill, *The Woodwright’s Shop* books. Historical hand practice.
- Charles Hayward, *Woodwork Joints* and associated Evans / Lost Art Press reprints. Classic joint vocabulary.
- Robert Wearing, *The Essential Woodworker.* Lost Art Press reprint. Layout, sawing, planing.
- David Charlesworth, plane and sharpening essays (magazine and DVD lineage). Camber, the last pass.
- Brian Boggs (chairmaker) public talks and *Fine Woodworking* pieces on chair geometry and stretchers. [VERIFY] any specific angle or dimension against a cited article before publishing numbers.
- Michael Fortune, steam-bending articles in *Fine Woodworking.* Forms, straps, springback.
- Jeff Jewitt, *Hand-Applied Finishes* and *Great Wood Finishes.* Taunton. Oil, varnish, shellac, rubbing out.
- Bob Flexner, *Understanding Wood Finishing.* Reader’s Digest / Fox Chapel editions. Film vs oil, myths about “breathing,” wipe-ons.
- George Frank, *Adventures in Wood Finishing.* Taunton. Older shop-finisher voice.
- Ian C. Calder, hide-glue practice as commonly taught (hot hide, liquid hide, bloom strength). Cross-check bloom-gram numbers [VERIFY] before a public spec.
- USDA Forest Products Laboratory, *Wood Handbook: Wood as an Engineering Material.* FPL-GTR (latest edition). Strength, MC, shrinkage. https://www.fs.usda.gov/research/treesearch — search “Wood Handbook.”
- USDA FPL, technical notes on steam bending and on moisture-content measurement.
- Architectural Woodwork Institute, *Architectural Woodwork Standards* (current edition). Millwork tolerances, moisture, factory grades. Cite edition [VERIFY] if a number is quoted.
- *Fine Woodworking*, *Popular Woodworking*, *Woodwork* (out of print) — article-level sources named in a draft’s `verify` list when a specific technique or measurement is taken from one piece.
