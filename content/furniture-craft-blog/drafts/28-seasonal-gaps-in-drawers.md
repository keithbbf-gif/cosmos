Solid-wood drawers change width with the season. Size them for mid-humidity or plan shims and runners that absorb the swing.

## At the bench

Fit when the shop is neither swamp nor desert. Leave side clearance on the runners, not zero on the sides. A too-tight drawer in winter becomes a wedge in summer.
