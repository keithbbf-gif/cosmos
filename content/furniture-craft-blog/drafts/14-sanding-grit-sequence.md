Skipping grits leaves ghost scratches that only show up under raking light and the first coat of finish.

## At the bench

Work through the sequence on the whole surface—80 to remove mill marks, 120 to cut 80, 150, 180, 220 for film finishes. Vacuum between steps; compressed air blows grit into your lungs, not out of pores.

## Checklist

- Each grit removes the previous scratch pattern—no jumping from 80 to 180.
- Dust off before switching grits; clogged paper burns.
- Final pass with the grain on the last grit only.
