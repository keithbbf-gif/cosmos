Mortise and tenon: cheeks, shoulders, and glue line — working notes for the shop. Measure on your stock; humidity and species move the numbers.

## At the bench

Use the diagram as the layout reference. Mark waste sides before any saw cut. Dry-fit twice when shoulders must close at the same time.
