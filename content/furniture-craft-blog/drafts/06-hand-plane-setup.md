Bench planes only work when the mouth, frog, and cap iron agree with the wood in front of you. Figured grain wants the breaker close; rough dimensioning wants an open mouth and a cambered iron.

## At the bench

Set the cap iron a hair behind the cutting edge for general work—closer when tear-out shows. Advance the iron until a full-width shaving lifts; back off if the shaving wrinkles. Check lateral adjustment on a narrow edge before you touch the show face.
