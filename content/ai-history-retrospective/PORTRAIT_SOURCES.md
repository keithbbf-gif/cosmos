## How to clear a portrait

1. Obtain a **documented** image (museum, Wikimedia Commons with verified license, estate permission).
2. Add file to `assets/portraits/<slug>.<ext>` (do not rename slug).
3. Append a row to the table above (source URL, license shorthand, date verified).
4. Update `assets/<figure-slug>/portrait-plate.svg` to reference the file; remove placeholder banner text.
5. Note the change in `GRAPHICS_INDEX.md` § Portraits.

## Rejected sources

- Generative AI “historical portrait” tools
- Uncredited social media crops
- Copyrighted press photos without license
