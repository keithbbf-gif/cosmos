# Fig propagation & rooting — Zone 8a, staged

Staged magazine set. Not published copy. Not a catalog. Not a success-rate banner.

These pieces are for a person who already has a fig, or a bag of sticks, and wants
the next plant told in a human voice. The center of gravity is **USDA Zone 8a**
(average annual extreme minimum **10–15°F**), rural South Arkansas weather, common
figs only. Zone 7 and Zone 9 sit in the room because 8a people borrow methods from
both and get hurt when they borrow the wrong one.

Four jobs, four stages:

| Stage | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `take` | Cutting: wood, timing, wash, labels, the box |
| `root` | Rooting: mix, heat, cups, failures, outdoor dirt |
| `layer` | Air-layer: June, sphagnum, wait, cut free, pot |
| `move` | Transplant: pot-up, clay hole, suckers, what “established” means |

Drafts live in `drafts/`. Editorial status on every file is `status: staged` —
ready for a human editor, not live, not SEO-published. `INDEX.md` is the read
order. `PHOTO_NOTES.md` is the shoot/pick brief for stills on **KC-PC** under
`D:\FIGS`.

## Numbers we already published (do not restage)

- Industry stick: **6–8 inches, three nodes** (Jack, *Let’s Talk About Fig Cuttings*)
- Fig Pop moisture: squeeze until **no free water**, then about **30% dry coir**
- Heat mat: **75–78°F on a thermostat**; probe in the mix. Basement A/B mats were
  set **80°F** in a cool room — shoot **76–80°F**
- 2023 A/B (PapaFig / OurFigs): 64 varieties, two cuttings each; coir **64/64**;
  DE **60/64 (92%)**. Read the live post. Do not reprint a fake “92% of customers.”
- Hormone is optional. Raised-bed dirt has rooted wood with nothing on it.
- Pot-up before June if you want a plant that faces 8a July without sulking.
