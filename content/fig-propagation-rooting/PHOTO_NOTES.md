## Rules that do not bend

1. **Ours first.** Hunt the folders below before Commons or USDA.
2. **Named folders are not proof of type.** Caption the clone we have. Do not upgrade the tag.

4. **PD fills** need URL + license in the media caption, and the sentence “not our tree.”
5. **Do not invent a jar, a crisper thermometer, or a cottony scale close-up.** If the folder does not have it, shoot it or skip it.
