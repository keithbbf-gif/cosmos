# Staging sheet

Every file in `drafts/` carries YAML:

```yaml
status: staged
stage: take | root | layer | move
focus: 8a
zones: [7, 8a, 8b, 9]
author: Jack Chambers | PapaFig
cluster: cutting | rooting | air-layer | transplant
voice: human
```

`status: staged` means the piece has had a voice pass and is waiting on a human.
It is not `published`. It is not `scratch`.

## Read order (if you want a plant, not a pile)

1. `d01` through `d14` — take the wood
2. `d15` through `d30` — root it
3. `d31` through `d38` — air-layer when a stick is the wrong tool
4. `d39` through `d46` — move it: cup to pot, pot to dirt

## Required topics (coverage)

- Cutting: 6–8" / 3 nodes, lignified vs dormant vs green, polarity, wash, mail-order, labels
- Rooting: squeeze test, thermostat, 2023 coir/DE as *that* test, callus vs root, outdoor dirt
- Air-layer: June window, sphagnum, wait, cut-free day, 8a August as a dare
- Transplant: pot-up before June, clay hole, suckers, #3 to #5, what established means

If a later editor cuts the set down, keep at least one draft from each stage and
do not cut `d01` (the industry stick), `d16` (thermostat), `d32` (June), or
`d39` (pot-up before June).
