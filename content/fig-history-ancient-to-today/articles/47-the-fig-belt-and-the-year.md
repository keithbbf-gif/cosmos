# Heat, Drought, and a Month That Must Stay Dry

The schematic belt on our map is a climate cartoon: winter rain or melt, summer fire, a coast or a slope that breathes. Figs live outside it — Lambeth, Chicago, Xinjiang, Paraná — but the *crate* lives inside it. Aydın’s spec bothers with aspect and slope because a southwest face in the Menderes is a dryer. Estahban’s GIAHS file is a rainfed slope file: the hill is a cistern. California’s San Joaquin is an irrigation file: the river is the cistern, and the dry autumn is the same god.

A wet August is a mold year. A cool August is an unripe year. A rain at tray-time is a sulfur year or a loss year. Common types forgive more than Smyrna types, which is why the northern garden exists at all.

Climate change is not a paragraph of panic here. It is a note that the tray month is moving, and that a GI written to a 20th-century autumn may need new sentences. Check the year before you print a “typical harvest.”
