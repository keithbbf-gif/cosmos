# Leaves Before Names

Theophrastus already knew leaves differ by place and by age of the shoot. Condit still filled pages with leaf characters because a fruit is gone seven months of the year and a leaf is not. Palmate, usually three- to five-lobed, a variable sinus, scabrous above, more or less pubescent below — the words are a field habit. They are not a court.

The same clone throws different leaves in water, in shade, in youth, on a sucker. “Brown Turkey” identified by a photograph of a leaf is a guess. Panache (the striped fig) is one of the few trees a leaf-and-fruit pair will not let you miss, and even that fruit can be shy.

## What a plate is for

This pack’s lobing plate is a teaching set: deep sinus, shallow sinus, entire-leaning. It is not a key to Mission. Use it to teach eyes. Then tell the eyes they will be wrong.

DNA work (Aradhya, NCGR Davis re-evaluations, the 2022 *PLOS ONE* mislabel paper) is the later court. Chicago Hardy ≈ Abruzzi is the kind of sentence a leaf will never give you. Until a backyard tree is sequenced, call it what the person who planted it called it, and add a photograph of the fruit, not only the leaf.

## Photograph the fruit

A leaf in January is what you have. A fruit in August is what you need. DNA is what you use when two names are fighting. The 2022 NCGR paper is the scandal of labels, not of leaves. Leaves were already trying their best.


## Sinus as a rumor

Condit’s leaf pages exist because fruit is gone seven months of the year. Palmate, three- to five-lobed, scabrous, a variable sinus — useful until the same clone throws a shade leaf that looks like another name. Panache will not let you miss it when it stripes. Brown Turkey will let you miss it every time.

The pack plate is a teaching set, not a key. Photograph the fruit in season. Sequence the tree when two names fight. The 2022 NCGR paper is the court for labels. Leaves were witnesses. They were not judges. Theophrastus already knew age-of-shoot and place change a leaf. Nurseries forgot because a leaf ships cheaper than a confession.
