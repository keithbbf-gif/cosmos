# *Sporte* in the Manual

Francesco Balducci Pegolotti’s *La pratica della mercatura*, compiled about 1340 from a lifetime in the Bardi network, is a book of cities, weights, and goods. Dried figs appear the way other keepable foods appear: as something a factor must know how to buy, pack, and pay duty on. The Florentine earlier manual of about 1320 and the Venetian *Zibaldone da Canal* belong to the same desk culture. Spice has the modern glamour. Figs have the *sporta* — the basket, the unit, the thing you can count.

In 1290, several baskets of dried figs from Ventimiglia in Liguria sat in Pera expected at Caffa; twenty *sporte* were to go on to Tana (Balard’s Genoese Caffa documents, as cited in Constantinopolitan food-trade work). That is the Black Sea eating an Italian tray. An unpublished Acre manual of about 1270 records Alexandrian dates into Romania. Figs ride the same logic: sweet density.

## Venice after Acre

Venice’s Levant was not only crusader Acre, but Acre trained the habit. After 1291 the communes re-routed. Crete, Negroponte, later Smyrna, the Egyptian ports — the flags change. The dried fruit does not need a flag. Regional syntheses of thirteenth-to-fifteenth-century Aegean trade put Chian dried figs moving through Negroponte toward Thessaly. The “spice” in this article’s slug is a warning label. A fig is not pepper. Putting them in the same chest is a ship’s decision, not a botanist’s.

## Pepper’s glamour, fig’s unit

A factor who could not convert a *sporta* lost money. A factor who
could not romance a fig still ate. Pegolotti, the *Zibaldone*, the
Florentine manual of about 1320 — dull books, keepable goods. 1290’s
Ligurian trays expected at Caffa are the Black Sea’s proof. After 1291
the habit moved. Do not invent a guild. Do not quote an unsourced

## Crete after Acre

The reroute is the story. A commune that lost a Levantine quarter did
not lose the habit of buying keepable sweet. Negroponte’s Chian
re-export and Pera’s Ligurian *sporte* are the same class of fact:
dull, dated, enough. Spice remains a warning in the slug. Fig remains
a unit.
