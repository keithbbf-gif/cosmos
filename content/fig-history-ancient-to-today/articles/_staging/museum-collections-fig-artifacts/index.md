# Inventory Numbers

This article is a visitor’s list, not a theory.

**Museo Archeologico Nazionale, Napoli, inv. 8625.** Fourth-Style still life, bread and two figs, Herculaneum. Photograph: ArchaiOptix 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0 (ask the museum about commercial reuse; the file note says so). Alternate Flickr PDM shot exists.

**Pompeii, Casa del Frutteto I.9.5.** Fig tree and serpent, Third Style. PD-Art reproductions on Commons.

**Casa dei Cervi, Herculaneum.** Fruit vignettes including dried figs/dates.

**Villa Medicea di Poggio a Caiano.** Bartolomeo Bimbi, named figs, 1696.

**Brooklyn Museum.** James Tissot, *Nathaniel Under the Fig Tree*; *The Vine Dresser and the Fig Tree*. PD.

**British Museum EA37983.** Nebamun garden. Use the Museum’s image service. Often NC. Reserved in this pack as BM-001. Do not scrape.

**Wellcome Collection V0044761.** Fruiting stem and halved fruit. CC BY 4.0.

**Trew/Ehret 1771; Köhler; Holtzbecher; Le Moyne.** Library and museum plates, PD or as tagged.

## How to walk them

Do not treat a still life as a cultivar ID. Do not treat Tissot as archaeology. Do not treat Ehret as a Neolithic witness. Do treat inventory numbers as the only romance this series fully trusts.

## Numbers or nothing

Accession first. Licence second. Caption third. Nebamun stays reserved because NC is not PD-Art. Met OA stays reserved until a record number sits in `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`. Tissot is reception. Bimbi is naming. 8625 is lunch.


## A visitor’s discipline

Start with the number. MAN Naples 8625 is two figs and a loaf, Fourth Style, Herculaneum, a photograph by ArchaiOptix (2018) that is CC BY-SA 4.0 while the wall is two millennia older. Casa del Frutteto I.9.5 is a serpent and a palmate tree. Casa dei Cervi is a cupboard. Bimbi 1696 is a Medici naming jag. Tissot in Brooklyn is biblical reception. Ehret 1771 is a prince’s flora. Wellcome V0044761 is a pharmacy seeing (CC BY 4.0). Holtzbecher and Le Moyne are northern and Atlantic court seeings.

Nebamun’s garden (BM EA37983) stays off this pack’s raster folder until the British Museum’s licence is obeyed — often NC. Met Open Access fruit pages stay reserved until an accession number and a CC0 tag sit in `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`. Those reservations are the opposite of a failure. They are how a history series does not become a postcard thief.

Caption rule, again: a plate is not a photograph of a tree. A watercolour of Nathanael is not a photograph of a disciple. A still life is not a cultivar ID. Inventory numbers are the romance.
