# Boxes, Grades, and the Quay

A drying yard makes a fruit keep. A packing house makes a fruit comparable. The İzmir fig trade, as Europeans and then Americans met it, was a trade of counts: how many to the kilo, how pale the skin, how full the seed, how clean the ostiole, how honest the box. Uygun Aksoy’s industry reviews (2017, 2021) are the modern Turkish desk for dried-export share. Do not lift a brochure percentage. Do use the idea: Türkiye’s fig fame is a packing fame as much as a tree fame.

Eisen’s 1901 bulletin still reads like a man trying to teach a valley a quay’s habits: curing, sweating, the look of a Smyrna layer. California would copy the box before it copied the wasp, and the box without the wasp is a disappointment.

## Labor is the missing stencil

Women on trays, sorters at tables, the seasonal house that fills and empties — those are the people the stencil does not print. This series will not invent names for them. It will refuse to write packing as a machine without hands. A grade is a set of eyes.

## Stencil, table, water

Eisen taught curing as if a valley could become a quay by reading. Sometimes it could. Aydın’s fruit still often leaves through İzmir because water is where ships are. The PDO tries to drag the name inland. The packing house is the hinge: count per kilo, color, ostiole, a box that can be substituted if no one is looking.

## The stencil and the inland drag

PDO paperwork tries to pull “Smyrna” back to Menderes towns. The ship
still needs water. Both sentences are true in one week. A Düsseldorf
complaint about a layer is a packing-house sentence. Aksoy’s reviews
are the modern industry desk. Eyes — often women’s — remain the
majority. Do not print a brochure share. Do print the refusals that
make a grade.


## Comparable fruit is a human product

A machine can weigh. It cannot yet decide a sugar spot the way a trained
eye does. That is why packing fame outruns tree fame and why substitution
is a police problem. Eisen translated the table for Fresno. Aksoy
describes the table still. The PDO drags a name inland. Water keeps the
name moving. Both are true.
