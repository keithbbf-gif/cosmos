# Late Arrivals

Kang Choon-Ki’s historical papers on Korean fruits and introduced economic plants put *Ficus carica* around the sixteenth century. That is late in this series and early enough to matter. It is also a scholar’s “around,” not a landing-party diary. Do not invent a 1520 ship.

Japanese *ichijiku* is the common fig as a guest. Edo-period sources and later horticultural consensus treat it as introduced, not as a Jōmon tree. This pack will not invent a temple origin, a first priest, or a Nara orchard. If a later writer has a dated honzōgaku entry, it can be added. Until then: late, named, cultivated, not native.

Ishizaki Yūshi’s watercolour in the Cock Blomhoff / Naturalis collection is a seeing, and the file’s institutional licence must be re-checked (WC-013). It is evidence that a Japanese artist had the tree in front of him in the early nineteenth century. It is not an origin.

## Late and enough

Kang’s “around the sixteenth century” is the Korean desk. Edo-consensus guest is the Japanese desk. Ishizaki is a seeing with a licence to re-check. No priest, no Nara origin, no Jōmon *carica*. Modern luxury fresh and imported dry are the present tense.


## Guest years, not temple years

Kang Choon-Ki’s 1989 and 1990 papers are the Korean desk this pack trusts: *F. carica* around the sixteenth century among introduced economic plants. “Around” is a scholar’s word. It is better than a fake landing date. Japanese *ichijiku* sits in Edo-period horticultural and honzōgaku weather as a guest. This pack will not invent the first priest, the first Nara courtyard, or a Jōmon carbonized *carica*. If a dated honzōgaku entry is added later, it should come with an edition.

Ishizaki Yūshi’s watercolour in the Cock Blomhoff / Naturalis collection (WC-013) is a nineteenth-century seeing. Re-check the institutional licence before a live WP import. It proves an artist had the tree. It does not prove an origin.

Modern Tokyo and Seoul groceries selling fresh figs as a short luxury and dried figs as import are the present tense of a late crop. That is allowed. It is not an ancient industry hiding under a shrine.
