# Four Kinds of Work

A Common fig persists — swells a syconium without pollination. A Smyrna fig drops unless a wasp brings pollen. A San Pedro fig splits the year: brebas that persist, a main crop that often will not. A caprifig is the wasp’s house and, in the *profichi*, the pollen box. Condit nailed those four boxes to the English wall. They remain the only way to talk about a nursery catalogue without lying.

Parthenocarpy is the biological word for the Common habit. It is also the Gilgal argument. A sterile, soft fruit is what a person wants to eat and what a person cannot sow. The stick is the answer. Pollination is the other answer: seed, crunch, a flavor packing houses call complete, a crop that will not exist if the insectary fails.

## Bills, not morals

Neither box is holier. Mission fed a mission chain because it did not need Algeria. Sarılop fed a world grocery because it did. Gasparrini studied the wrong bill and issued a theory. California paid.

Breba vs main crop is the calendar inside the boxes. A tree can be a Common type and still throw a useless breba, or a famous one (San Pedro’s reason for existing). Pruning arguments in every language are arguments about which crop you are buying.

## Gilgal’s biology is the nursery’s biology

A sterile soft fruit is what Kislev saw and what a Common-type grocer sells. A seeded drying fig is what Aydın sells and what Fresno could not sell until 1900. San Pedro is the compromise calendar. Caprifig is the invoice the other three sometimes owe.


## A bill you can taste

Bite a Mission and you may get jam without crunch. Bite a caprified Sarılop and you get seeds that announce a wasp’s visit. That is not a moral. It is a bill. Gilgal’s argument is the same biology as a Common-type grocery fig: a room that swelled without pollen. Aydın’s legal adjectives (seeded, thin-shelled) are the other bill written into a specification.

San Pedro’s split year — persistent breba, needy main crop — is why nursery tags that say only “needs a pollinator” give up. Pruning arguments in every language are arguments about which crop you are buying. Caprifig *profichi* are the invoice Smyrna types owe in spring. Gasparrini studied the wrong bill. California paid in dropped fruit.
