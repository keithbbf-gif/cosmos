# Djebba, Taounate, and the Name Police

Figue de Djebba, Tunisia’s first fruit geographic indication (2012), names the Bouhouli fig of Djebba, Béja. PAMPAT / UNIDO notes are institutional project English: proof that a ministry bothered to fence a living named fruit. They are not village mythology.

Figue sèche Nabout de Taounate, Morocco, is arrêté 1680-15 of 25 May 2015 (WIPO Lex MA167). A dried fig with a paper date and a slope. Rarely does a modern fruit get a text this specific about where a name may sit. Print the arrêté number.

These are legal fences around old orchards. They are not proof of a Punic cultivar and not a sequel to Cato’s Senate fig. The Carthage essay exists so that Senate prop does not eat the continent. This essay exists so the continent is not only tonnes and not only Aydın’s English grocery memory.

## Two powers: tonnes and stamps

FAOSTAT’s recent years often seat Morocco and Algeria in the world’s first five, each north of a hundred thousand tonnes depending on the year. Bibliography order of late compilations: Türkiye, Egypt, Morocco, Algeria, Iran — **re-check the year before publishing a table.** Those Maghreb tonnes are household trees, terrace trees, drying hills, and city markets. They are not automatically a GI.

Algeria’s drying hills can sit in that front rank without sitting in a stamp. Volume and legal name are different powers. Djebba and Taounate are the opposite power: small, named, fenced. 2012 and 2015 are paper dates. The slopes are older.

## What a first fruit GI is for

Tunisia’s 2012 stamp on Bouhouli of Djebba, Béja, is a ministry deciding that a named fruit on a named slope should not be worn by a cheaper tray. PAMPAT and UNIDO language will talk about value chains and rural development. Use that as institutional weather. Do not translate it into a cosmogony. The fruit was already there. The fence is new.

Morocco’s 2015 arrêté around dried Nabout of Taounate is the same power in another legal dialect. WIPO Lex MA167 is how a reader who is not in Rabat can find the paper. Print the number. A tourism page that needs Taounate to be older than Estahban, or Djebba to be older than Gilgal, is doing superlatives. This series already refused that move in Fars.

Algeria’s absence from this pair of stamps is not an absence from the tonne board. Household trees and drying hills can out-produce a GI and never hire a lawyer. That is the Maghreb’s volume power. Carthage’s Senate fig remains a Roman joke about a city Rome wanted to hate. It is not a title deed for Béja or Taounate. Cato’s African soil class in *De Agri Cultura* 8.1 is a villa sentence, not a GI ancestor.

Khadari’s Moroccan–Algerian pool is a 2025 laboratory shape. It can sit in a genetics paragraph. It cannot sit in an arrêté as a bloodline. Print 2012 and 1680-15. Re-check FAOSTAT before a table. Leave the slopes older.

## Household trees are a production system

FAOSTAT does not ask whether a tonne came from a GI slope or a courtyard. Morocco’s and Algeria’s front-rank years are built of trees people can see from a kitchen door, plus terrace orchards, plus drying hills that never hired a Brussels lawyer. That is a production system. It is not a failure to be Aydın. English grocery memory treats Turkish dried figs as the default tray. The Maghreb’s default is fresh and household-dry, with two stamps lately pinned to named fruit.

Djebba’s Bouhouli is a living named fig on a Béja slope. Taounate’s Nabout is a living named dried fig. Neither name appears in Cato 8.1. Neither name appears in Pliny’s Senate joke. The Carthage essay’s job is to keep that joke from eating North Africa. This essay’s job is to keep North Africa from being only a tonne board and a Punic rumor.

Do not invent a Berber origin myth. Do not back-date Bouhouli to a villa mosaic you cannot cite. Do not hang Khadari’s 2025 western pool on an arrêté as a bloodline. Re-check the FAOSTAT year before a table — bibliography late compilations put Morocco and Algeria each north of 100,000 tonnes in some recent years, behind Türkiye and Egypt. Print the year you use. Print 2012 and 1680-15. Leave the slopes older than the paper.

Aydın owns a grocery memory in English. The Maghreb owns volume, two stamps, and a lot of unnamed hills. Print the arrêtés. Leave Cato two articles away as a Senate joke, not a title deed.
