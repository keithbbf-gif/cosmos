# Djebba, Taounate, and the Name Police

Figue de Djebba, Tunisia’s first fruit GI (2012), names the Bouhouli fig of Djebba, Béja. PAMPAT / UNIDO notes are institutional, not folklore. Figue sèche Nabout de Taounate, Morocco, is arrêté 1680-15 of 25 May 2015 (WIPO Lex MA167). These are legal fences around living named fruit. They are not proof of a Punic cultivar and not a sequel to Cato’s Senate fig.

Algeria’s drying hills and household trees sit in FAOSTAT’s front rank without always sitting in a GI. Volume and legal name are different powers. The Maghreb is not a footnote to Aydın. In some recent years Morocco and Algeria each put more than a hundred thousand tonnes on the FAOSTAT board.

## Law on old slopes

2012 and 2015 are paper dates. The slopes are older. FAOSTAT tonnes for Morocco and Algeria are a different power than a GI. Cato does not own Bouhouli. A Berber origin myth does not ship. The arrêtés are the rare modern texts that say where a name may sit.


## Volume without a stamp, stamps without volume

FAOSTAT’s recent years often seat Morocco and Algeria in the world’s first five, each north of a hundred thousand tonnes depending on the year. That is household trees, terrace trees, drying hills, and city markets. It is not automatically a GI. Tunisia’s Djebba stamp (2012) and Morocco’s Taounate Nabout arrêté (25 May 2015, 1680-15) are the opposite power: small, named, fenced. PAMPAT/UNIDO notes on Djebba are institutional project language. Use them as proof that a ministry bothered, not as village mythology.

Bouhouli of Djebba is a living named fruit. Nabout of Taounate is a living named dried fruit. Neither is Cato’s Senate prop. Neither is a Punic cultivar recovered from a trench. The Maghreb article next to Carthage exists so the prop does not eat the continent; this article exists so the continent is not only tonnes.

Do not invent a Berber cosmogony for the fig. Do not back-date Bouhouli to a Roman villa inscription you cannot cite. Do print the arrêté number. Rarely does a modern fruit get a text this specific about where a name may sit.
