# Domestication of the common fig

*Ficus carica* enters the archaeological record as one of the earliest domesticated fruit crops. This draft anchors the pack’s origin story; prose will expand as research citations land.

## Early sites

Gilgal-type assemblages and Jordan Valley contexts suggest managed fig populations long before classical orchard manuals. Graphics emphasize **when** and **where**, not decorative fruit photography.
