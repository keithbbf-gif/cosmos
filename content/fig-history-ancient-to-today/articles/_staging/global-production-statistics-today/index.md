# A Year of Tonnes

Helgi’s FAOSTAT compilation for **2022** puts world fig production at about **1,242,449 tonnes**. Türkiye 350,000 t; then Egypt and Algeria; Morocco and Iran in the next seats; Spain on the order of 43,500 t in a 2022 FAO-quoted trade note. AtlasBig / World Population Review compilations for **2023** (accessed 2025 in those sites’ notes) put Türkiye about 356,000 t, Egypt about 193,000, Morocco about 119,000, Algeria about 116,000, Iran about 73,500, then Afghanistan, Syria, Spain. IndexBox’s 2024 recap still has Türkiye, Egypt, Algeria as the volume core.

These are not the same table. They are the same *shape*. Print the year. Round. Do not write “Türkiye grows half the world’s figs” unless the year’s arithmetic says so (350/1242 is about 28 percent in 2022).

## Fresh vs dried vs fame

FAOSTAT’s crop is figs, not “dried Smyrna equivalent.” Egypt’s tonnes are not Aydın’s crates. California’s tonnes are a rounding error next to the Aegean and the Nile and the Maghreb, and still they own English nursery language. That is history, not production.

## How to print a table without lying

Prefer FAOSTAT’s own download for the year on the cover. Helgi, AtlasBig, and IndexBox are convenient mirrors. They disagree in the thousands. They agree on the shape: Türkiye, then Nile and Maghreb, then Iran and a contested next tier (Afghanistan, Syria, Spain). The United States is a nursery power and a modest tonne power. India appears as an importer in some trade notes more loudly than as a grower.

Dried export fame and fresh tonne fame are different columns. Aydın can own a grocery memory while Egypt owns a FAOSTAT cell. Print both, or print neither.
