# Rainfed *Anjīr*

Houshang Alam’s *Encyclopaedia Iranica* article “Fig” is the English spine for Persianate fig history: Middle Persian *anjīr*, Bundahišn 16.26 (a fruit edible outside and inside), geographers on Fārs, later notices of Tabrīz, color classes in Heravī. Estahban does not need a myth of being first. It needs its slopes.

The FAO GIAHS file on “Estahban Rainfed Fig Orchards” is a living-system dossier: rainfed trees on the Fars uplands, a dry-farming habit, a dried-fig economy that is still a local job. Its deep-time origin remarks are weaker than its agronomy. Use the latter. Leave Gilgal in the Jordan. Leave “thousands of years of unbroken orchard” as a tone, not a pollen core.

## A country of dry months

Rainfed fig country is a bet on winter water and a summer that will dry a tray without a tunnel. That is the opposite of a Nile basin and cousin to an Aydın slope. Farshī’s 1960 “Anjīr-e Īrān,” via Iranica, is a modern Persian desk. FAOSTAT still puts Iran in the world’s first half-dozen producers in recent years (re-check the year before printing a table). Dried export is the historical voice; fresh is a later city habit.

## Rainfed as a bet, not a brand

A rainfed fig is a winter that showed up and a summer that stayed dry. GIAHS can honor that bet. It cannot turn it into an origin myth. Iranica’s geographers give reputation. FAOSTAT gives a recent tonne seat. Farshī 1960 gives a modern Persian desk. Three desks, one slope.


## Three desks on one slope

Iranica (Alam) holds the words: *anjīr*, Bundahišn 16.26, geographers on Fārs, Tabrīz, Heravī’s colors. GIAHS holds the living rainfed system and overreaches on origins — use the agronomy. FAOSTAT holds a recent tonne seat in the world’s first half-dozen (re-check the year). Farshī 1960 is a modern Persian desk via Iranica.

Strabo’s Hyrcanian wonders stay in the topos bin. Rashīd al-Dīn’s mulberry-graft report stays reported, not certified. Rainfed is a winter that arrived. A brand that sells “oldest orchards on earth” is doing Gilgal’s press release in Farsi. This pack will not.
