# Other New Worlds: Chile, Peru, Brazil, Texas

California ate the English-language story because it had a wasp crisis and a USDA bulletin (articles 35, 54). The rest of the Americas grew figs without that plot. The passenger was the same: a common-type cutting on a Spanish or Portuguese cart (article 34). The climates were not.

## Pacific desert and irrigation

Peru’s coast and Chile’s central valleys are fig weather wearing different passports. Irrigation, a dry finish, a market in a city that also eats grapes and olives of a New World sort — the San Joaquin without the Calimyrna religion. Morton and Condit note South American plantings as Spanish and later republican. FAOSTAT will sometimes show small national lines; they change by year and should be checked, not memorized from a 2022 blog. The historical claim does not need the tonnes. It needs the climate and the colonial wood.

These valleys now also feed the January clamshell (article 61): counterseason fruit for a northern winter. That is a new job for an old passenger.

## Brazil’s humidity

Brazil is a different problem. *F. carica* as a backyard and small-farm fruit in the south and at altitude, not a drying empire. The Portuguese Atlantic lesson (article 27) is the likely school. Do not invent a bandeirante origin tale. Do not put a Sarılop on a São Paulo terrace and call it history. Common types that forgive rain are the only types that make sense.

## Texas and the other United States

Texas, Arizona, the Gulf South: yard trees, U-pick, a grandmother’s name on a clone that Condit would have filed under three headings. Brown Turkey as a name you should not trust. Celeste in the humid east. LSU releases later (article 57). This is not Fresno. It is the northern-garden problem (article 36) with worse summers and better chances.

Eisen already looked at Mexico and Central America. The passenger did not stop at San Diego. Baja is a sentence between Mexico City’s valleys and Alta California’s last missions. A history that jumps from Cortés to Roeding has skipped a continent.
