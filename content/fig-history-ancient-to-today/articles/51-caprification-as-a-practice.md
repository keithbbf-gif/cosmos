# A Practice Older Than Its English Name

The English word is ugly and useful. The act is older than the word. Aegean, Maghrebi, and Campanian growers hung wild fruit in tame trees because the tame fruit then held. Theophrastus wrote the swell (article 08). Roman Italy kept the crop names — mamme, profichi, mammoni — that Eisen said would become household words wherever the wasp was taken seriously. Ibn al-ʿAwwām listed artificial pollination of the fig with the date, the pomegranate, and the pistachio. The *Geoponica* (article 14) kept the practice speakable while capitals changed.

None of this is a USDA discovery. The USDA discovery is California catching up.

## How a professor cost a decade

Gasparrini, in a chapter Americans read in translation about 1892, said caprification was useless, costly, and bad for flavor, and ought to be abolished. Condit quotes him with the weariness of a man who has seen an error cost ten years of wood. Common figs do not need the act. Smyrna figs do. A professor who studied the first and issued a law for the second is how a science goes wrong.

Eisen sat at Naples in 1896 with Paul Mayer’s 1882 memoir and tried to tell California. Growers who had Mission and Adriatic trees — navel oranges, Condit would say — could not hear why Lob Injir behaved like a Valencia. Roeding heard. He kept about 62 acres alive until Howard and Swingle’s 1899 Algerian insect and Schwarz’s 1900 season made a crop (article 54).

That is not the invention of caprification. That is the invention of caprification *in California*.

## The act, without folklore

Cut profichi when the female crop is receptive. Hang them in the canopy, strung or bagged. The wasp, pollen on her, enters the Smyrna ostiole, pollinates long-styled flowers, and does not easily breed there. The fruit holds and seeds. Endosepsis can take the same ride (article 53). More wasps are not always better.

Article 40 is the biology. This article is the *practice*: a week on a farm calendar that Theophrastus could already see and that a circular can still get wrong if it forgets the four types (article 39).
