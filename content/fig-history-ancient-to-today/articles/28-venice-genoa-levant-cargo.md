# Figs in the Ledger: Venice, Genoa, and Levantine Cargo

Venice did not need to invent the fig. It needed to count it. The republic’s Levantine trade — spices, cotton, wine, currants, alum, the goods that get the chapters — is a textbook that sometimes forgets the cheap cargo that made the expensive cargo smell like a kitchen. Dried figs from the Aegean, and the more-local Adriatic (article 31), sat in the same warehouses as things with better press.

A fig in that warehouse is an Aegean summer that has already been decided: picked or fallen, turned on a tray, packed against beetles, named for a port. The orchard stayed put. The ledger moved.

## Two Italian maritime habits

Venice is a city that eats from a ship. Its own hinterland grows some fruit; its fortune was other people’s hinterlands. A galley lane that includes the Aegean is a lane that can hold Smyrna-type freight centuries before *Smyrna* is a grocery brand in Ohio. The Ottoman industrialization of that lane (article 22) inherits a route the Venetians had already worn.

Genoa’s eastern factories and Liguria’s steep gardens are the other habit: a city that eats from a ship *and* from a terrace. Pliny already knew Ligurian tricks for keeping grapes; the same coast knew figs. The medieval novelty is not the fruit. It is the scale of someone else’s orchard arriving on your quay, and the legal machinery — fondaco, consul, duty — that turned a tray into a line item.

## A caution about romance

We do not have, in this pack, a transcribed 14th-century fig invoice with prices we trust. Eisen and the later California men assumed the Levantine dried fig as the gold standard the Italian cities had already been selling north. That is a fair assumption about taste and not a table of ducats. Do not print a fake price. Do not invent a “Venetian fig monopoly.”

What can be printed: the geography, the cheapness relative to spice, the fact that a republic which lived on freight would not have ignored a fruit that holds still. Currants got more ink because they were a tighter specialty. Figs were ordinary, which is a kind of evidence.

The still lifes of Campania (article 12) are the house version of the same object. The warehouse is the unpainted version.
