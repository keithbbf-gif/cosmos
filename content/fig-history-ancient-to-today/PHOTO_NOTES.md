# Photo notes — History of the Fig

The series is **not** a fruit-catalog shoot. A reader should leave remembering a Senate fig, a wasp, a drying yard, a fresco, and a crate stencil — not only a split Black Mission.

## Three image classes

### 1. License-clear historical and scientific rasters

Museum open-access files and Wikimedia PD / CC reproductions of tomb and villa painting, botanical plates, historic maps and port views, later documentary photographs that are PD or CC.

Every raster under `assets/images/<slug>/` is logged in `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`. If the license is not on the log, the image does not ship. Cleared unique rasters now number **thirty**: thirteen first-wave plates; twelve unique from rights PR #275 (Djari sycomore harvest CC0; two Nakht Met facsimiles CC0; Ficus Ruminalis denarius CC BY-SA 2.5; Meléndez NGA CC0; Nationalmuseum still life PD; six USDA pomological watercolors PD); and five later USDA cards (Heiges First Crop Smyrna POM00001166, 1897; Arnold Nameless POM00001044, 1915; Arnold Magnolia POM00001043, 1913; Passmore Toulousienne POM00001168, 1895; Shull cutting POM00001042, 1923). Duplicate Commons files already in-pack were not copied again. Distinct figure IDs may reuse one file when the caption does different work. Nebamun EA37983 stays reserved. Met 548438 is in-pack (CC0). Caption Djari as *F. sycomorus*. Caption Nakht as estate context, not fig-picking. Caption First Crop Smyrna 7/97 as the wait, not Schwarz’s 1900 crop.

### 2. Original pack graphics (`assets/shared/svg/`)

Drawn for this series. They are **graphics**, not photographs. See `GRAPHICS_INDEX.md` and the visual `STYLE_GUIDE.md`. Every schematic map and timeline is captioned *schematic; not to scale*.

Do not invent a second SVG folder. Recolor only through the graphics lane; do not change place-names without checking the article.

## Caption rules

- Credit + license in the italic caption line.
- “After Ehret, 1771” is a plate. “Photograph of a 1771 tree” is a lie.
- “Schematic; not a surveyed map” on every original map.
- People in orchard photos: consent, or crop to hands/work.
- Do not use Priapus / erotic Pompeii frescoes as cheap color. The Casa dei Vettii Priapus is a fertility account; if used, say so and keep it in an art/labor piece, not as a thumbnail.

## Balance test (per article)

- at least **one** non-fruit image (site, map, plate, diagram, document, portrait, packing)
- fruit photographs, if any, **do not outnumber** the rest
- one figure that could run in a history magazine with the fruit cropped out and still make sense

## What we will not do

- Claim a model-generated “tomb painting” is from Thebes.
- Hotlink a museum file whose licence is All Rights Reserved.
- Build a hero image that is three figs on linen and a serif headline.
- Put a Calimyrna beauty shot on the Gilgal article.
