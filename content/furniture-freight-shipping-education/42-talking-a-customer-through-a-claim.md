# Talking a customer through a claim without promising a check we do not write

The phone call after a broken island is where BBF either sounds
like a factory or sounds like a hold music.

People want three things: to be believed, to know the next hour,
and to know who pays. We can give the first two even when the
third is not us.

## Believe the wound

“I’m sorry that landed on your porch that way. Send me daylight
photos of the piece, the wound, and the carton. Keep the
packaging. I’ll send you the origin photos and the BOL today.”

Do not start with FOB. Do not start with “you signed it.” There
will be time for paper after they know we are in the room.

## Name the next hour

A list, not a speech:

1. Photos (the walk in
   [38-inspect-hardwood-on-a-porch.md](38-inspect-hardwood-on-a-porch.md))
2. The POD / the words they wrote
3. Who they email (carrier / retailer / us) and the PRO
4. The inspection request if it is concealed
5. When we will call them back (a time, not “soon”)

If you cannot do the fifth, you are not doing the call.

## Who pays — said without theater

Use the order in front of you, not a proverb.

- Factory-direct, we self-insure remakes: “We will repair or
  remake. We will chase the carrier. You will not sit in the
  middle of their tariff.”
- Factory-direct, risk passed, customer files: “The claim is
  yours to file; we will build the pack and stay on the email.
  Their automatic limit may be a per-pound number. I will not
  pretend that number is a new island.”
- Marketplace: “The delivery promise and the refund path sit
  with the retailer. We will send the shop file the same day.
  We will not vanish.”

If you do not know which of those the order is, say you will
read the paper and call back in an hour. Guessing is how we
invent a warranty.

## What not to say

- “We’ll make it 100% right” when we have not seen the photos
- “The carrier has to replace it”
- “You have nine months, no rush”
- “It’s only cosmetic”
- “That’s wood, it happens” as the first sentence
- “That’s freight, not us” as the first sentence

Cosmetic is a word finishers use. Customers hear “shut up.”
Wood movement is real and still a later sentence.

## Anger

Let them be angry at the truck, the wait, the wedding, the
portal. Do not join a pile-on you cannot cash. Do not defend
a driver you have not met. Take the PRO. Take the photos.
Be the factory.

If they want a refund we cannot issue, say the path: retailer,
or our invoice, or a repair. A silent pause is better than a
yes that accounting will kill.

## After the call

A six-line email that repeats the list and attaches origin
photos. If it is not in the email, it did not happen. Put the
email in the claim kit.

## Monday

The whole pack is a voice. If a new hire can walk Stage 1 and
Stage 6, they can quote a ship date without lying and take a
hurt call without making it worse. That is the education.

Keep the drafts staged until BBF edits the lines that do not
sound like us. When two drafts disagree, the paper on the
shipment wins — then fix the draft.
