# Retailer chargebacks and why the factory still feels it

Carmack is a conversation with a carrier. A chargeback is a
conversation with a portal that already has our money.

BBF has been on those portals. The island can be “delivered” in
the tracking and “unpaid” in the remittance. That is not a glitch.
That is a program.

## What a chargeback is

The retailer (or marketplace) deducts: damaged on arrival, missing
parts, late, a customer return they decided to make our problem,
a compliance miss (label, carton, ship window), sometimes a fee
that is really a mood.

They may also tell the customer “we’ve refunded you” before we
have seen a photo. The customer is gone. The wood is in a house.
The deduction is in a PDF.

## Why origin photos still matter

Because the dispute window is a form, not a porch. The form wants
a picture from Warren and a picture from the house and a BOL that
matches. If we have only the house picture, we are agreeing. If we
have both, we can say “this crush is not that top” or “that top
left sound.”

We will still lose some. Files win more than memory.

## Chargeback vs carrier claim

They are not substitutes. The retailer can take our money and
leave us to chase the carrier — or forbid us to chase, depending
on the packet. Read the packet. If the program owns freight, the
claim path may be theirs and the deduction is how they recover
from us regardless of whose fork it was.

Do not tell a customer “we’ll file with YRC” on a job whose BOL
was the portal’s third-party party. We may have no standing. We
may still need to help them talk to the retailer.

## Compliance chargebacks

Wrong carton, late scan, a label in the wrong corner, a ship date
we missed because a top was not dry. These are not OS&D. They are
us. The education is production and dock, not a cargo clerk.

If we keep losing the same compliance code, that code is a shop
standard we have not written.

## A deduction that is really a late scan

The piece was sound. The customer is happy. The portal still took
$50 because the pickup scan missed their window by a day, or the
carton lacked their barcode in their corner. That money is a
process fine. Fight it with timestamps if we have them. If we do
not, change the dock so next week’s barcode is in the corner they
worship.

Do not spend finishing’s week on a compliance ding. Send the
right person.

## How to stay human

A chargeback is allowed to make us angry. It is not allowed to
make us ghost a household who still has a broken island and a
retailer script. We can say:

“The retailer pulled the funds on their program. We are disputing
with photos from the shop. We will not ask you to pay twice. We
also cannot refund a portal payment we never took.”

True and kind. If we did take the payment (some hybrids), say
that instead.
