# Appointments, notify, and “nobody was home”

Freight does not leave it on the mat next to the dog food.

If no authorized person is there, the truck leaves, and the second
visit is a product you will pay for. That product is called redelivery.
It is not a moral judgment. It is a second stop.

## Notify

Someone will call the number on the BOL. If the number is the shop’s
main line that nobody answers after 3, or a customer who screens
unknown numbers, the freight will sit. Sitting becomes storage. Storage
becomes a bill that feels like a kidnapping.

Put a human phone on the BOL. Put a backup. Tell the customer the
caller ID may look like a freight company in another state. Tell them
to answer.

## Appointment

A window, not a minute. Four hours is common. “Tuesday” is not an
appointment. “Guaranteed between 10 and 11” is a different SKU, if it
exists at all.

Households that work need a window they can actually take off for. If
they cannot, they need a neighbor, a lockbox conversation the carrier
will refuse, or a hold at a terminal they can drive to (and a van that
can load them). Do not invent a porch-pirate plan for an island.

Retailer docks have receiving hours and appointment portals. A truck
that arrives without a number is a truck that waits. Waiting is
detention. See [34-detention-attempts-friday-four.md](34-detention-attempts-friday-four.md).

## Nobody home

The driver tried. They may have a photo of a closed door. The POD says
attempted. The next step is a reschedule, a fee, and a clock toward
storage or return to origin.

If the customer says they were in the backyard, that is a human story
and a bad number on the BOL (no “call on arrival”). Notify-on-arrival
is a checkbox that exists because of backyards.

## What BBF should tell people

“Freight will call. You need a window. Someone 18 or older who can sign
and look at the piece should be there. If they miss the truck, there
will be another charge and another wait.”

Say it before pickup, not after the attempt.

## A number that looks like a number and is a junk drawer

The shop main line. A Google Voice nobody watches. A designer’s
office that closes at 4. A customer who only answers texts. Freight
notify is a voice call from an unknown state. If we put a junk
number on the BOL, we bought an attempt.

Text-capable after-hours phones exist. Use one that a person who
cares about this island will hear.

## Marketplace vs us

Portals often own the notify. The customer thinks they are waiting on
BBF. We can look up the PRO and still not own the phone tree. Be
honest: “I can see it is at the destination terminal. The delivering
carrier’s number is X. I will also ping the portal. I cannot make their
phone ring.”

If we can see a failed attempt in the scans, say that too. “They
came Friday at 3:51” ends a certain kind of argument so a
reschedule can start.

## Monday

On every open residential PRO, check that the notify number is a person
who knows an island is coming. If it is our office because we were
lazy, change it. Then read
