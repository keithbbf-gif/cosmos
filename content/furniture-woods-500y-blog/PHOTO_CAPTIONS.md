# Photo captions

Rights first, then the caption. Prefer public-domain plates and museum open-access files. Bradley Brand oak and pine shop shots are listed as **preferred fills** where a live grain or mill photograph is stronger than a historic plate. Do not drop a shop shot into a Chippendale story as if it were the object.

Five editorial SVGs already live in `assets/` and are embedded from `drafts/` (see `GRAPHICS_INDEX.md`). This file is the **photographic** plate list — objects, botanicals, shop macros — not a second figure set.

Licence abbreviations: PD = public domain; OA = museum open access (check CC or OA deed per object); Rights-reserved = request or skip.

## BBF preferred fills (oak and pine)

Shoot in diffuse daylight or north light. Include a scale (a try square, not a coin). Label species if known from the bill: *Quercus alba*, *Q. rubra*, *Pinus strobus* or southern yellow pine as identified. Do not caption red oak as white. End-grain macros at 1:1. These fills serve `glossary-grain-and-cut`, `english-oak-great-furniture`, `american-colonial-pine`, `quartersawn-arts-crafts`, `flatsawn-panel-figure`, and `conservation-today` first; they may backfill `ash-sporting-chairs` and `veneer-versus-solid` when the shop has the board.
