# Bibliography

Primary and secondary sources used across the series. Article notes point here by short title. Items marked * were checked in the drafting pass; others are standard references the editor should verify against a library copy before publication.

## Wood science

- *Forest Products Laboratory. *Wood Handbook: Wood as an Engineering Material.* General Technical Report FPL-GTR-190. Madison, WI: USDA Forest Service, 2010. Mechanical tables 5–3a / 5–3b (domestic) and imported-woods chapter.
- Forest Products Laboratory. *Wood Handbook: Wood as an Engineering Material.* FPL-GTR-282. Madison, WI: USDA Forest Service, 2021 (centennial revision of selected chapters).
- *Green, David W., M. Begel, and W. Nelson. “Janka hardness using nonstandard specimens.” Research Note FPL-RN-0303. Madison, WI: USDA FPL, 2006.
- *Green, David W., Marshall Begel, and William Nelson. “Estimating Janka hardness from specific gravity for tropical and temperate species.” Research Paper FPL-RP-643. Madison, WI: USDA FPL.
- Hoadley, R. Bruce. *Understanding Wood: A Craftsman’s Guide to Wood Technology.* 2nd ed. Newtown, CT: Taunton Press, 2000.
- Panshin, A. J., and Carl de Zeeuw. *Textbook of Wood Technology.* 4th ed. New York: McGraw-Hill, 1980.
- Record, Samuel J., and Robert W. Hess. *Timbers of the New World.* New Haven: Yale University Press, 1949.
- Kukachka, B. F. “Properties of imported tropical woods.” Research Paper FPL-125. Madison, WI: USDA FPL, 1970.
- Meier, Eric. *The Wood Database.* https://www.wood-database.com/ — compilation; use only when the FPL or Kukachka number is cited beside it.
- Tsoumis, George. *Science and Technology of Wood.* New York: Van Nostrand Reinhold, 1991.

## Furniture history (European and American)

- *Macquoid, Percy. *A History of English Furniture.* 4 vols. London: Lawrence & Bullen, 1904–1908. Ages of Oak (1500–1660), Walnut (1660–1720), Mahogany (1720–1770), Satinwood (1770–c.1820). Treat as a periodization, not a law.
- Edwards, Ralph, and Percy Macquoid. *The Dictionary of English Furniture.* 3 vols. London: Country Life, 1924–27; rev. 1954.
- *Bowett, Adam. *The English Mahogany Trade 1700–1793.* PhD thesis, Brunel / Buckinghamshire College, 1997. https://bnu.repository.guildhe.ac.uk/id/eprint/9639/
- *Bowett, Adam. “The 1721 Naval Stores Act and the commercial introduction of mahogany.” Regional Furniture Society lecture summary, *RFS Newsletter* 21.
- *Bowett, Adam. “Thomas Ripley and the use of early mahogany.” *The Georgian Group Journal* 7 (1997).
- *Bowett, Adam. “The Jamaica trade: Gillow and the use of mahogany in the eighteenth century.” *Regional Furniture.*
- *Bowett, Adam. “Furniture woods in London and provincial furniture 1700–1800.” *Regional Furniture.*
- Bowett, Adam. *English Furniture 1660–1714: From Charles II to Queen Anne.* Woodbridge: Antique Collectors’ Club, 2002.
- Bowett, Adam. *Early Georgian Furniture 1715–1740.* Woodbridge: Antique Collectors’ Club, 2009.
- Chippendale, Thomas. *The Gentleman and Cabinet-Maker’s Director.* London, 1754; 3rd ed. 1762.
- Hepplewhite, A. (firm). *The Cabinet-Maker and Upholsterer’s Guide.* London, 1788.
- Sheraton, Thomas. *The Cabinet-Maker and Upholsterer’s Drawing-Book.* London, 1793; *The Cabinet Dictionary,* 1803.
- Roubo, André-Jacob. *L’Art du menuisier.* Paris, 1769–1775.
- Evelyn, John. *Sylva, or A Discourse of Forest-Trees.* London, 1664.
- Chinnery, Victor. *Oak Furniture: The British Tradition.* Woodbridge: Antique Collectors’ Club, 1979.
- Forman, Benno M. *American Seating Furniture, 1630–1730.* New York: Winterthur / Norton, 1988.
- Hurst, Ronald L., and Jonathan Prown. *Southern Furniture 1680–1830: The Colonial Williamsburg Collection.* Williamsburg: Colonial Williamsburg, 1997.
- Richards, Nancy E., and Nancy Goyne Evans. Winterthur furniture catalogues (various).
- Evans, Nancy Goyne. *Windsor-Chair Making in America.* Hanover, NH: University Press of New England, 2006.
- Kassay, John. *The Book of Shaker Furniture.* Amherst: University of Massachusetts Press, 1980.
- Rieman, Timothy D., and Jean M. Burks. *The Complete Book of Shaker Furniture.* New York: Abrams, 1993.
- Stickley, Gustav. *The Craftsman* magazine, 1901–1916; *Craftsman Homes,* 1909.
