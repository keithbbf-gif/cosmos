# What the EU AI Act is not (a public correction)

After a dozen drafts of dates, a reader deserves a negative space. The Act picked up nicknames it did not earn. Clearing them is educational, not clever.

## It is not Asilomar with penalties

The twenty-three principles are a signed list. The Act is a regulation with annexes, notified bodies, and fines. A few values rhyme. The machinery does not. If a speech says "Europe implemented Asilomar," ask which principle became which article. Most of the longer-term bin will not find a home.

## It is not an AGI control statute

The Charter's object is AGI as economically dominant autonomy. The Act's object is AI systems and GPAI models on a market that already exists. You can squint and see systemic-risk GPAI as a cousin of "frontier" talk. Squinting is not identity. There is no article titled "stop competing and start assisting."

## It is not GDPR

People say "GDPR for AI" because both are big Union files with extraterritorial stories and fine legends. GDPR is a data-protection regulation. The Act is a product-and-use regulation that *talks to* data-protection law and does not replace it. Double-counting duties is a practitioner problem. Double-naming the statutes is a classroom problem.

## It is not a global law

It is Union law with the usual story about the "Brussels effect" — other markets sometimes copy, or exporters comply because the Union market is large. That effect is a hypothesis with evidence in other files. It is not a magic wand. A U.S. executive order is not "the American AI Act." A Chinese interim measure is not "China's AI Act" in the same legal sense. Keep the nouns.

## It is not a verdict on whether AI is existentially dangerous

The recitals mention various risks. The operative design is rights, health, safety, market integrity, and (later) model-layer systemic risk. You can support the Act and think existential risk is overblown. You can think existential risk is the main event and find the Act under-powered. Those positions already exist in public. The text does not settle them.

## Why a negative draft belongs in a staged set

Because the fastest way a recap becomes novelty-unsafe is to *upgrade* the object: to treat a regulation as a philosophy, or a philosophy as a regulation. Negative space keeps the object the size it is. The next drafts look at neighboring public instruments that are also not the Act, and also not nothing.
