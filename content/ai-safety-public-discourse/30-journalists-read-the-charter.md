# How journalists taught the Charter to a mass audience

Most people who can quote a shard of the OpenAI Charter never read it on the week it shipped. They met it later, in profiles. One widely circulated example is Karen Hao's 17 February 2020 *MIT Technology Review* piece on the lab's "messy, secretive reality." The article treats the April 2018 Charter as a pivot: same mission words, new resource language, a document written while the need for capital was already being felt inside.

This draft is not a review of that article. It is a note on **secondary texts as teachers**.

## What a magazine page does to a charter

It selects. Hao's piece (and others in the same genre) pulls the fiduciary sentence, the resource sentence, and the contrast with 2015's cleaner nonprofit romance. It does not walk through cooperative orientation with equal care. Selection is not a crime. It is how features work. A student who only reads the feature will think the Charter is mainly a fundraising tell. A student who only reads the Charter will miss how soon the tell was publicly alleged.

The educational move is to pair them. Primary on the left, feature on the right. Where do they agree on quotations? Where does the feature infer motive? Motive inferences are the journalist's job and the historian's caution.

## Other public teachers

Congressional testimony clips taught "benefit humanity" as a refrain.

Late-night jokes taught the gap between the refrain and product launches.

Lawsuit exhibits, once they became public, taught the Charter as a legal artifact rather than a blog.

Each teacher has a genre incentive. Comedy wants a gap. A lawsuit wants a promise. A feature wants a scene. The Charter wanted a north star that could also recruit.

## A hygiene rule for this set

When a later draft in the EU section quotes a recital, it should quote the recital. When this era's drafts quote the Charter, they should quote the Charter. Journalism enters labeled as journalism.

If you are writing a syllabus, assign the 2018 page *before* the 2020 feature. Reverse the order and you will spend the hour unteaching a frame. Frames are sticky. That stickiness is why this draft exists.

## Limit

This is not a media-studies paper. It names one prominent feature and a few genres. A complete reception history would be a different project, with archives and interviews this folder does not have and does not fake.
