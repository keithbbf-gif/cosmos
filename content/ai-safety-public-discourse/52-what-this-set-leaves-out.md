# What this staged set leaves out on purpose

A recap that pretends to be complete is already a kind of novelty: the novelty of a fake map. This draft lists omissions so a later editor, or a skeptical reader, can see the holes without thinking the holes are secret chapters.

## Technical literatures

There is no guided tour of specification gaming, RLHF, interpretability, or evaluations. Those are research fields with their own surveys. Pointing at them without the surveys would be name-dropping. Name-dropping is how staged drafts go bad.

## Most countries

China's interim generative-AI measures, Canada's AIDA attempts, the UK's evolving statutory conversation, African Union and Latin American soft-law texts — barely touched or not touched. UNESCO is in the set as a global script, not as a substitute for those files. A later series could be "public AI law outside the four spines." This series is not that.

## Labor, climate, and most of fairness

Dialect A in this folder is mostly the Act's version of Dialect A. The longer fairness, labor, and environmental literatures — FAccT papers, union reports, data-center siting fights — are under-represented. That is a bias of the assignment (Asilomar, FHI, Charter, EU timeline), not a claim that those literatures are side issues.

## Internal history

We do not have, and do not invent, board minutes, grant negotiations, or Slack. Where journalism alleged motives (draft 30), it is labeled journalism.

## A score

No draft answers "was FHI good," "did the Charter fail," or "is the Act enough." Those are essays a human editor might later commission under a different novelty standard. Staged recap stops at readable objects.

## This repository's other life

Whatever else lives in the wider tree that hosts this folder is out of scope. The folder is a sealed classroom. If a sentence here had named that other life, the sentence would have failed the brief.

## Why end on omissions

Because quality, in a recap, is partly the shape of the silence. Forty-plus drafts is already a pile. The pile is still a path through four public chapters and a method for not mythologizing them. If you want the next path, start with a hole on this page and a primary URL that is not in `SOURCES.md`. Then write a new staged draft. Do not silently enlarge this one until it claims the world.
