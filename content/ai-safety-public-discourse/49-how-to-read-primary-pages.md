# How to read a primary page without making it a myth

Draft 00 gave rules for the set. This draft is a smaller ritual you can run on any single URL in `SOURCES.md`.

## Five marks in the margin

**1. Speaker.** Who can be sued, voted out, or embarrassed by this page? A company blog, an expert group, a ministry, a university institute, a nonprofit with a letter. If you cannot name the speaker, you are reading a vibe.

**2. Date the speaker claims.** Dateline, "published," "adopted," "signed." If there are two dates (Asilomar January meeting / August letter page), keep both.

**3. Genre.** Principle list, recommendation, proposal, regulation, executive order, feature article, final report, open letter. Genre tells you the teeth.

**4. Object.** Today's hiring tool, a GPAI model, AGI as defined in 2018, recombinant DNA, a summit. If the object slides mid-page, mark the slide.

**5. Verb.** Should, commit, shall, recommend, pause, prohibit. A whole education lives in the verbs.

## Three temptations

**Origin-hunting.** "This is where it all began." Almost never. 1975, 1965, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2021 are all beginnings of *something*. They are not the beginning of worrying about machines.

**Gotcha-hunting.** Reading the 2015 nonprofit sentence only to wave it at 2019. Consistency arguments are allowed; they are not the first read. First read: what did the page say to a reader that week.

**Upgrade.** Turning a should into a shall, a recommendation into a law, a political agreement into an OJ citation. This set has spent thousands of words refusing upgrades. The refusal is the method.

## A forty-minute seminar

Pick one page. No secondary articles until minute thirty. Each student writes a six-line card: speaker, date, genre, object, strongest verb, one absence. Then, and only then, open a newspaper piece about the same event. Compare the card to the headline. The gap is reception. Reception is history. It is not a license to discard the page.

## What this ritual will not give you

It will not tell you who is right about the future. It will not replace legal advice. It will not produce a new framework with your name on it. If you leave the seminar wanting to invent a sixth mark, wait a week. Most sixth marks are novelty trying to sneak back in.
