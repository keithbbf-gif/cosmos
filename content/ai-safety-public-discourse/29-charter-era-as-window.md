# The charter era is a window, not a verdict

This set uses **charter era** as a dated window: from the December 2015 founding post through the 9 April 2018 Charter and the March 2019 structure note. The window ends before the mass-product chatbot years, before the 2023 open-letter season, before the Act's political agreement. It ends on purpose.

## Why cut the film there

Because later events are loud enough to drown the documents. If you start with a 2023 board crisis or a 2025 restructuring press release, you will read 2018 only as foreshadowing. Foreshadowing is a novelist's tool. Discourse history can afford to let a document finish its own sentences.

The window is also when a particular public language stabilized: AGI as an economic-autonomy object; humanity as fiduciary; race as a named failure mode; leadership as a safety need; cooperation as a heading; capital as a problem the mission had to solve. Later years reuse that language under more heat. Heat is not origin.

## What the window does not decide

It does not decide whether the lab kept the Charter.

It does not decide whether AGI, as defined, is near.

It does not decide whether capped-profit was sincere.

It does not decide whether "open" was a bait-and-switch.

Those questions ate thousands of public pages after 2022. They are real questions. They are not this folder's assignment. This folder's assignment is to make the 2015–2019 pages readable enough that later arguments have to quote them accurately.

## How other actors sat just outside the frame

DeepMind's earlier public safety talk, Google's AI principles after the 2018 Maven fight, Microsoft's later responsible-AI pages, Anthropic's later public "constitutional" language — all of these are neighboring letterheads. A fuller book would give them chapters. This staged set keeps the charter era's camera mostly on one text family so a reader can learn to *stay with a document*. The skill transfers.

## A warning about the phrase itself

"Charter era" is a teaching label invented for this folder. It is not a term of art in the documents. Do not export it as if OpenAI printed it. If a later editor hates the label, they can rename the window. The dates will still be the dates.

Novelty-safe means even our own scaffolding stays humble. Labels are filing tabs. They are not discoveries.
