# U.S. public instruments, 2023: RMF and EO 14110

This set is not a U.S. policy course. Two 2023 objects still belong on the wall, because European and lab documents were read against them in real time.

## NIST AI RMF 1.0 (26 January 2023)

The National Institute of Standards and Technology released the *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework* 1.0 in January 2023. It is voluntary. It is process-shaped: map, measure, manage, govern — the sort of language a compliance shop can put in a slide. It is closer to HLEG/ALTAI than to Asilomar 23.

The RMF's public job was to give U.S. organizations a **non-statutory** scaffold while Congress did not pass an AI Act. Voluntary frameworks are easy to mock and widely used. Mock and use can coexist.

## Executive Order 14110 (30 October 2023)

President Biden signed EO 14110 on 30 October 2023, days before Bletchley. The order used existing presidential authorities to direct agencies: reporting on large training runs, safety and security, biosecurity-adjacent provisions, equity, labor, privacy, federal use. It was a stack of verbs, not a single pyramid.

As a discourse object it did two things. It told a domestic audience the White House had "done something" at frontier-model scale. It told a foreign audience the U.S. would show up at Bletchley with ink still wet.

## Later public fate, briefly

In January 2025 the incoming administration revoked EO 14110. That revocation is public and should be on the timeline if you carry the EO at all. An order that can be withdrawn by the next occupant is a different species from Regulation 2024/1689. Students who treat "the U.S. AI order" as a permanent twin of the Act have not learned species.

This draft will not narrate every successor memorandum. The lesson is the species difference, not a scorecard of presidents.

## How the two 2023 U.S. objects were heard in Europe

In Brussels reporting that autumn, the EO was sometimes treated as proof that "even Washington" had moved on frontier models, and sometimes as proof that Washington would not pass a statute and would instead stack memoranda. Both hearings used the same PDF. The RMF, quieter, showed up in law-firm notes as something a company could already claim to follow while waiting for the Act's duties to bite.

That split reception is the educational object. A voluntary framework and an executive order can be filed in the same country-year and still teach opposite lessons about teeth.

## Placement

January 2023 RMF — voluntary technical-organizational English.  
March–May 2023 — letters.  
October 2023 — EO.  
November 2023 — Bletchley.  
December 2023 — EU political agreement.

The U.S. line is a braid of voluntary standards and executive verbs. The EU line is a regulation. The lab line is a charter. If you can keep those three species in separate tanks, you are literate.
