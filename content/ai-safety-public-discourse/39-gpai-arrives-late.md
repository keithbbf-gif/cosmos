# General-purpose models: the late public character

The 2021 proposal was built around *uses* and *products*. A system in a medical device, a system in hiring, a system in a camera. The political problem of 2023 was a *model* that could be many uses depending on the prompt. Public language stumbled around names: foundation model, general-purpose AI, GPAI, frontier model. The December 2023 deal put **GPAI** into the file as a chapter, with extra duties where a model is judged to pose systemic risk.

This draft is about that late entrance, not about how to compute a FLOP threshold.

## Why the original architecture strained

A use-based annex assumes you can point at a deployer and a context. A general-purpose model is often provided by one firm and used by thousands of others in contexts the provider never listed. Transparency and risk-management duties then want to travel *upstream*. That upstream move is what 2023's public fight was about: will Europe regulate the model layer or only the application layer?

Parliament's public stance, in 2023 reporting, leaned toward model-layer duties. Some member-state chatter, in the same reporting, warned about driving developers out. The compromise — baseline GPAI duties, more for systemic-risk models — is a classic Union split-the-difference. Whether the split is wise is a policy argument. That the split was *public and late* is the historical point.

## Systemic risk, in this dialect

"Systemic risk" in the Act's GPAI neighborhood is not automatically Asilomar's existential risk. It is a legal category attached to capability, reach, and possible large-scale impact, with delegated details and codes of practice around it. A reader can hear echoes. A reader should not collapse the terms. Collapse is how a compliance team and a philosophy seminar start shouting the same English at different objects.

## Discourse effect

After 2023, popular explainers of the Act often *start* with ChatGPT. That is backwards chronology and forwards politics. The Act is a 2021 product-safety file wearing a 2023 model-layer jacket. Both garments are real. Teaching only the jacket produces people who cannot find Annex III. Teaching only the 2021 shirt produces people who cannot find Chapter V.

If you write a timeline on a wall, put a break at late 2022: "public object changes; statute still moving." That break is more honest than a single arrow from Asilomar to Brussels.
