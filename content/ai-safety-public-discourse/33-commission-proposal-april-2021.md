# 21 April 2021: COM(2021) 206 enters the file

On 21 April 2021 the Commission adopted a proposal for a regulation laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence — the Artificial Intelligence Act — cited as COM(2021) 206. This is the day the public timeline becomes a **legislative** timeline. Everything before is preface: HLEG, White Paper, consultation, coordinated plans. Everything after is Ordinary Legislative Procedure: Parliament, Council, trilogue, Official Journal.

## What a proposal is

A Commission proposal is a finished-looking law that is not law. It has articles, annexes, recitals, and an impact-assessment package (SWD documents traveled with it). It can be rewritten in almost every politically salient place before signature. People who treat the 2021 text as "the Act" are a year-plus early and several political bargains short.

The legal bases on the file include Articles 16 and 114 TFEU — data protection and internal-market harmonisation. That choice is a discourse fact. This is a single-market product-and-rights instrument, not a UN existential-risk treaty.

## What the 2021 shape already contained

Even before later amendments, the public could see the architecture:

- a definition of an AI system (later fought over, later aligned in spirit with OECD wording)
- prohibited practices
- a high-risk list, partly by annex of product areas, partly by use
- requirements for high-risk systems (data, documentation, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity)
- transparency duties for some other systems (chatbots, deepfakes — in evolving form)
- measures for innovation (sandboxes)
- governance and penalties

General-purpose models were not yet the celebrity problem they became after 2022. Readers in 2021 argued about biometric identification and social scoring more than about frontier training runs. That shift in public attention is a later chapter. It is why people who only woke up in 2023 mis-tell the proposal as "Europe's answer to ChatGPT." The proposal is older than that product.

## How to cite it

Say COM(2021) 206, 21 April 2021. Do not say "the EU banned AI in 2021." Do not say "the Act passed in 2021." The EUR-Lex procedure file is the adult way to watch what happened next.

If you need one educational comparison: the Charter is a first-person mission page; COM(2021) 206 is a third-person market rule. They can be discussed in the same seminar. They cannot be substituted.
