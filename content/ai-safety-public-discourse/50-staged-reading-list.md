# A staged reading list of public documents

This is not a canon. It is a **starter stack** that matches the four spines of the folder. Every item is a public page or a book you can hold. The comments are filing notes, not reviews.

## Asilomar spine

1. FLI, "A Principled AI Discussion in Asilomar" (18 January 2017) — process.
2. FLI, Asilomar AI Principles page (published stamp 11 August 2017) — list.
3. A short reliable recap of the 1975 recombinant-DNA meeting (any standard history chapter) — citation ancestor, read second, not first.

## FHI spine

4. Bostrom, *Superintelligence* (2014), early framing chapters only on a first pass.
5. FHI public closing statement (April 2024) and the ORA record for the final report.
6. I.J. Good, 1965 essay, a few pages — ancestor, labeled as such.
7. Weizenbaum, *Computer Power and Human Reason* (1976), a chapter — adjacent refusal.

## Charter-era spine

8. OpenAI founding announcement (December 2015).
9. OpenAI Charter (9 April 2018).
10. OpenAI capped-profit announcement (11 March 2019).
11. One 2020 feature (Hao, *MIT Technology Review*) — secondary, labeled.

## EU spine

12. HLEG *Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI* (8 April 2019), the seven requirements section.
13. White Paper COM(2020) 65 (19 February 2020), the regulatory-options pages.
14. COM(2021) 206 (21 April 2021), the structure of titles, not every annex on day one.
15. Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 5, Article 113, and the table of contents.
16. Commission news note, 1 August 2024.

## Neighbors

17. OECD AI Principles (22 May 2019; glance at the 2024 update).
18. UNESCO Recommendation (23 November 2021), the values section.
19. Bletchley Declaration (1 November 2023).
20. CAIS one-sentence statement (30 May 2023) and FLI pause letter (22 March 2023), as a pair.
21. NIST AI RMF 1.0 (26 January 2023), the function names.
22. EO 14110 (30 October 2023), the table of contents — and a dated note that it was later revoked.

## What is deliberately not here

Internal emails. Leaked slide decks of uncertain provenance. This repository's other trees. New frameworks. A "best of YouTube." Those omissions are the point of a staged, novelty-safe list.
