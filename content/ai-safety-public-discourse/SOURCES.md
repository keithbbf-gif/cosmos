# Sources — public pointers only

These URLs are **starting points** for the drafts in this folder. They are not an exhaustive bibliography. Pages move; when they do, prefer an official successor URL or a dated archive snapshot. Signature counts and "last updated" lines should be dated at the time of reading.

This list does not include paywalled archives a general reader cannot open. Books are cited by title and year.

## Asilomar and FLI

- [Asilomar AI Principles](https://futureoflife.org/open-letter/ai-principles/) — FLI open-letter page (publication stamp 11 August 2017).
- [A Principled AI Discussion in Asilomar](https://futureoflife.org/principles/principled-ai-discussion-asilomar/) — FLI process note (18 January 2017).
- [FHI note on the principles](https://www.fhi.ox.ac.uk/asilomar-ai-principles-announced/) — contemporaneous Oxford pointer (February 2017).
- Archive snapshot of the principles page (example): `http://archive.today/EiSAc` (captured 18 November 2023).

## FHI

- [FHI closing site](https://www.fhi.ox.ac.uk/) — "closed on 16 April 2024."
