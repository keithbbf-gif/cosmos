# December 2023: political agreement, not yet a statute

On 8–9 December 2023, after a long night of trilogue, the European Parliament and the Council reached a political agreement on the AI Act. The Commission, which had proposed the file in 2021, stood in the room as the third official corner of that ritual. Press releases went out. People said, in headlines, that the EU had "agreed the world's first AI law."

Almost. Political agreement is a real step. It is not the Official Journal.

## What trilogue is, in public English

Trilogue is the informal negotiation among Parliament, Council, and Commission to reconcile their positions. It is ordinary for major files. It is also opaque compared with a plenary vote. Civil-society groups spent 2023 complaining about last-minute deals on police biometrics and on foundation models. Industry groups spent 2023 complaining about the same hours from the other side. The complaints are part of the public file. The late-night photos are part of the folklore.

## What 2023 added to the 2021 story

Two public plots collided.

**Plot A:** the original product-and-rights regulation, still about high-risk annexes and prohibited practices.

**Plot B:** generative models, after 2022, as a horizontal phenomenon that did not sit neatly in an annex of use-cases. Parliament had pushed for foundation-model language. France, Germany, and Italy became associated, in reporting, with a more industry-skeptical-of-heavy-GPAI-rules stance. The December deal included a GPAI chapter with extra duties for models counted as posing systemic risk.

A reader who skipped 2021 will think the Act *is* Plot B. A reader who stopped in 2021 will miss Plot B. The December night is where the two plots were stapled.

## How to talk about the date without inflating it

Say: **political agreement, 8 December 2023** (some timestamps spill into the 9th). Then say what still remained: legal-linguistic cleanup, formal Parliament vote, Council adoption, signature, publication, entry into force.

Compare: Asilomar's January 2017 meeting versus the August 2017 open-letter stamp. Institutions have "we agreed" dates and "it is on paper" dates. Mixing them is how educational timelines get sloppy.

## The weather around the room

Bletchley Park had happened on 1–2 November 2023. The U.S. executive order (EO 14110) had been signed on 30 October 2023. The EU deal sat in a month when "AI safety" was a transatlantic headline. Proximity invited claims of a global moment. The documents remain different genres: a declaration, an order, a political agreement on a regulation. Stack them on a calendar. Do not blend their legal force.
