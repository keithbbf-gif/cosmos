# Two vocabularies that share a word

This is the method piece promised in the how-to. **Safety**, in the public record this set walked, is at least two dialects, sometimes three.

## Dialect A — product, rights, now

Home documents: HLEG 2019, White Paper 2020, COM(2021) 206, Regulation 2024/1689, OECD 2019, NIST RMF, large parts of Asilomar 6–18, UNESCO 2021.

Typical nouns: high-risk, prohibited practice, oversight, documentation, discrimination, biometric, deployer, notifying body, robustness.

Typical time: this product cycle, this annex, this audit.

Typical fear: a person is hurt, scored, surveilled, or misled by a system that is already on a market.

## Dialect B — control, trajectory, tail

Home documents: *Superintelligence* 2014, FHI self-descriptions, Asilomar 19–23, Charter AGI sentences, 2023 CAIS one-liner, parts of Bletchley, parts of lab "preparedness" blogs.

Typical nouns: superintelligence, alignment, control problem, existential risk, recursive self-improvement, frontier, AGI.

Typical time: a late stage, a century, a two-year even-odds window.

Typical fear: a system that outruns operators, or a race that spends the safety margin.

## Dialect C — official robustness

Home documents: OECD's "robustness, security and safety," many cybersecurity strategies, sometimes the same statute as Dialect A when it says accuracy and cybersecurity.

Typical nouns: incident, red team, secure-by-design, model theft.

Typical fear: misuse, outage, leak, not the end of the species and not a biased hiring tool.

## Why the shared word caused ten years of talking past

A Dialect A speaker hears "safety" and opens Annex III. A Dialect B speaker hears "safety" and opens a control chapter. Each thinks the other is changing the subject. Each is, from the other's home. The word is a false friend.

Asilomar tried to house A and B under one URL. The Act housed A (and some C) with a late GPAI annex that B-speakers sometimes claim as a partial win. The Charter tried to speak B in first person while selling a lab that also ships A-era products. None of those housing attempts abolished the dialects.

## A pencil mark

When you read a new speech, mark A, B, or C in the margin at each paragraph. If the speaker switches without warning, note the switch. You do not need a new theory of safety. You need to stop assuming the noun is stable.

This folder will not pick a dialect as the real one. Picking would be a novelty claim, or a loyalty. Staging forbids both.
