# Claims guardrails — SLPWOW SLP News, wave 2

## Never say

- That a news item diagnoses a child or replaces a screen or evaluation.
- That a headline alone justifies billing, coding, caseload, or compact-privilege changes.
- That ASHA, CMS, CDC, the ASLP-IC Commission, or a state board said something they did not. Quote a short official phrase or link the page.
- That an enacted compact statute means a state is issuing privileges today.
- That the ASHA CCC is a license, or that a compact privilege is the CCC.
- That Medicare recognizes speech-language pathology assistants as skilled therapy providers.
- Vendor marketing language presented as SLPWOW or COSMOS endorsement.
- Treatment recipes, thickener how-tos, or “try this after a stroke.”

## Always do

- Name the **primary source** the reader should open.
- Separate **headline heat** from **document text**.
- Date-stamp numbers (conversion factors, KX thresholds, compact maps) as of the pack date.
- Mark **live Compact Map legend counts** and other map numerators with **`[VERIFY]`** before import; CY 2026 CMS/ASHA PDF dollars tied to cited URLs do not need the tag if the primary page still matches.
- Keep `status: draft` until a human editor clears import.
- Mark uncertainty: “as of 14 September 2026 the Compact Map showed four issuing jurisdictions.”
