# SLPWOW — SLP News, wave 2 (draft pack)

Clinician- and family-facing **news briefs** for [SLPWOW](https://slpwow.com/). Wave 2 is the first full **public-document desk**: ASHA advocacy and ethics, CMS payment and coverage, the Audiology & Speech-Language Pathology Interstate Compact (ASLP-IC), and research that actually changed names or methods.

Wave 1 (`content/slpwow-slp-news/`) is eight news-literacy explainers with graphics. This folder is **forty-six original briefs**. It does not replace wave 1. It does not dump copyrighted ASHA Leader, CMS Federal Register, or journal PDFs. Each piece names a primary source, paraphrases what changed, and tells the reader where to verify.

**Staged. Not live.** `status: draft` and `stage: draft` on every article.

Start here:

1. `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md` — what this desk must never say
2. `STYLE_GUIDE.md` — voice, length, citation rules
3. `INDEX.md` — roster by desk
4. `articles/` — drafts
5. `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` — primary URLs used in the pack
6. `check_pack.py` — structural QA

Sibling packs stay separate: `content/slpwow-slp-news/` (wave 1 graphics), `content/slp-pediatric-milestones-blog/`, school-practice news, history series.
