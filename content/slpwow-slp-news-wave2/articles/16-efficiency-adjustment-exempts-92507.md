# The 2.5 percent efficiency adjustment is real — and 92507 is named out

*Educational news brief for SLPWOW. Not legal advice, not billing advice, and not a substitute for reading the primary source or for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

CMS spent a long paragraph in the CY 2026 fact sheet explaining why it no longer fully trusts survey-based times. The RUC process, the agency says, revalues only a sliver of codes each year, leans on low-response surveys, and leaves inflated time assumptions in place. The remedy it finalized is an **efficiency adjustment**: trim work RVUs and the intraservice time of codes that are supposed to get faster as technology and repetition accumulate.

The math is a five-year lookback of the Medicare Economic Index productivity factor. For 2026 that lands at **minus 2.5 percent**. CMS says it may apply the same idea every three years. Time-based codes, care-management codes, behavioral-health codes, maternity globals, and services on the Medicare telehealth list are supposed to be outside the haircut. After comments, the agency updated the exempt-code list.

## Where SLPs sit

ASHA’s November 2025 booklet translates this into clinic English. Services whose main resource is the clinician’s time, and services on the telehealth list, are out. CPT **92507** — individual treatment of speech, language, voice, communication, and/or auditory processing — is **specifically exempt**. New codes in their birth year are also out. Some diagnostic and other non-time-based SLP codes are in. ASHA tells readers to check CMS’s published list rather than assume the whole speech caseload is safe.

That pair of sentences is the whole brief. Treatment minutes are not the target. Some tests and procedures are.

## How not to brief the staff

Do not say “CMS cut speech therapy 2.5 percent.” That is false for 92507 and sloppy for the rest. Do not say “ASHA blocked the adjustment.” ASHA says it lost the broad fight and won carve-outs. Do not say the adjustment is a one-year stunt if CMS has already described a repeating method.

Do calculate. If your revenue is mostly 92507, the efficiency line may be noise next to locality, MPPR, and sequestration. If you live on a handful of instrumental or testing codes, open the CMS zip of affected HCPCS and look.

## What this is not

Not a judgment that your swallow study got easier. Not a practice guideline. Not a reason to shorten a session to match a work-RVU theory. The note still has to describe the skilled work you did.

MedPAC and GAO have been complaining for years that procedures were overvalued and talking-intensive work undervalued. CMS is using that literature as cover. You can agree with the diagnosis and still hate the haircut on a particular diagnostic code. Hate it with the list open.
