# Military spouses are in the compact’s public-benefit list — the privilege is still the privilege

*Educational news brief for SLPWOW. Not legal advice, not billing advice, and not a substitute for reading the primary source or for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

Interstate-compact preambles always mention military families. The ASLP-IC home page does it in a bullet: improved portability for military spouses. That bullet is why some legislatures voted yes. It is also why a spouse on a two-year orders cycle gets told, incorrectly, that the compact is a special military license.

It is not. It is the same privilege, the same CompactConnect account, the same FBI-gate on the home state, the same fee row. The July 2026 sheet does not print a spouse exemption. If a state later writes one, it will show up as a statute or a board rule, not as a vibe on a Facebook page for Army hospitals.

## What the compact actually offers a spouse

If the home-state license can stay active — some service members keep a license in a state they have left, some cannot — and that home state is issuing, a privilege into the next duty-station state is faster than a full foreign-license packet *once that duty-station state is issuing*. In September 2026 that second condition failed in most of the country. A family moving to a 33-state “enacted but not issuing” map still needs the old endorsement.

Other federal and state military-spouse license laws may run in parallel. Those laws are not this compact. Read both. Do not let a compact brochure talk you out of a spouse-specific statute that is actually live.

## How to brief a hospital credentialing office

“The applicant will hold a home-state SLP license and, if both states are issuing, an ASLP-IC privilege. The CCC is separate. CompactConnect is the primary source file. There is no military sticker that replaces those steps.”

## What this is not

Not an immigration brief. Not a Tricare coverage brief. Not a promise that every Navy hospital accepts privileges on day one of a state’s launch. Credentialing committees are slower than banners.

A spouse who keeps a home license in a non-issuing state has a statute and no door. The honest counseling is: start the traditional endorsement now, and open CompactConnect the week the home board announces issuing. Do not pause the endorsement because a Facebook group says “any day now.” Any day now is how people arrive at a new duty station unemployed.

Tricare coverage, hospital privileges, and compact privileges are three desks. This brief is only the third. Bring the other two papers to the same appointment.

If orders change after you paid a privilege, that fee is probably gone. Budget for waste the way military families already budget for deposits. The compact reduces some paper. It does not repeal the Army.

Keep a folder: home license, privilege PDF, CCC, orders, and the board email that said the home state was issuing. Credentialing will ask for all five. Having four is how a start date slips.
