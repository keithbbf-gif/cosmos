# The 2027 CCC-SLP standards start on August 1 — they are not a license

*Educational news brief for SLPWOW. Not legal advice, not billing advice, and not a substitute for reading the primary source or for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

The Council for Clinical Certification in Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology posted the 2027 Standards and Implementation Procedures for the CCC-SLP with an effective date of **1 August 2027**. The page is long, numbered, and written to be enforced without exceptions. It is also the document most likely to be mis-captioned as “the new SLP license.” It is not a license. It is the association’s certificate.

CFCC is a semi-autonomous ASHA body. In 2024 ASHA ran a Curriculum and Practice Analysis. The 2027 standards are the CFCC’s rewrite after that survey. A 2020-to-2027 crosswalk PDF exists for programs that have to map old language onto new. Contact certification@asha.org if you need the credentialing desk, not a news brief.

Standard I still wants a master’s or a doctoral degree. Standard II still wants graduate coursework and practicum started and finished in a CAA-accredited program, a CAA-candidacy program, or an internationally equivalent program. The implementation notes are picky in a useful way: a program-director verification form, an official transcript with a conferral date, no registrar letter as a substitute, transcript in within a year of the application.

Standard III keeps a floor of 36 graduate semester hours plus supervised clinical experience deep enough to hit the knowledge and skills lists. Those lists are where the 2027 text shows its age — in a good way. Knowledge outcomes still run from biological and physical science prerequisites through swallowing, literacy, AAC, and cognition. Implementation language now names **telepractice and telesupervision**, **technology literacy**, social determinants of health, and service that aligns with the histories and values of the people in the room. That is a standards update, not a gadget endorsement.

## Maintenance is still thirty hours and an ethics hour

Standard VIII is the part already-certified clinicians will feel. Certificate holders still need at least 30 professional development hours in a three-year interval, adherence to the Code of Ethics, and the annual fee. Content Area 1 is still one PDH in ethics. Content Area 2 is still two PDHs aimed at services that fit the people actually in front of you. The 2027 page is the place to confirm the current wording before a maintenance audit, not a LinkedIn carousel.

## What the standards refuse to be

They do not license you in a compact state. They do not enroll you in Medicare. They do not make an SLPA a skilled Medicare provider. They do not replace the 2016 Scope of Practice document, though they keep pointing at that scope for what “the profession” includes. They do not let a program waive a bold-faced standard because a student is close.

If you supervise clinical fellows, read Standard VII on the same page. If you run a graduate program, read Standards II through V with the crosswalk open. If you only hold the CCC and want to know whether August 2027 changes your Tuesday, the honest answer is: your maintenance rules are on Standard VIII; your state board is a different website.

## How to talk about 2027 without scaring families

Families do not need this brief. Students and CF supervisors do. Say: the certificate’s rules update on 1 August 2027; the state license updates when the legislature or the board says so; those dates are not the same.
