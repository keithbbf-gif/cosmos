# The 2023 Code of Ethics is a jurisdiction, not a poster

*Educational news brief for SLPWOW. Not legal advice, not billing advice, and not a substitute for reading the primary source or for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

ASHA has had a Code of Ethics since 1952. The current text is the 2023 revision. The association’s policy page is unusually blunt about who is inside the fence: a member with the CCC, a member without the CCC, a nonmember who holds the CCC, and an applicant for certification or for membership-and-certification. Hold the card or ask for the card, and the Board of Ethics can hear a complaint.

That is a jurisdiction. It is not a laminated values statement for the break room.

## Who must hold the CCC to treat

The 2023 code keeps a bright line that clinics still trip over. ASHA members who provide clinical services must hold the Certificate of Clinical Competence. Members without the CCC may not provide clinical services, except that people in the certification pipeline may treat if local law and ASHA certification rules allow it. The sentence is about ASHA membership-plus-practice, not about every human who has an SLP job. State licenses can and do cover clinicians who never join ASHA. Those clinicians are not under this Board. They are under their board.

Do not tell a provisionally licensed fellow that “ASHA says you cannot treat.” Read the code, the certification standards, and the state practice act. They do not say the same thing.

## Competence, technology, and the AI briefs

Principle II is the one the generative-AI pages keep citing. Individuals who hold the CCC practice only inside the scope of their competence — certification status, education, training, experience. They use technology consistent with accepted professional guidelines, keep it calibrated, and refer out when the indicated tool is missing.

That is why ASHA’s later AI pages can say, without inventing a new principle, that a clinician must review and edit model output and must not dump protected health information into a consumer chatbot. The 2023 code already required competent use of instruments. A large language model is an instrument with a terms-of-service problem.

## What the code will not do for you

It will not set a Medicare rate. It will not issue a compact privilege. It will not decide whether a school district may bill Medicaid. It will not replace informed consent in your state. It will not make a vendor’s “ASHA-aligned” documentation product ethical by adjective.

It will, if you are in the jurisdiction, support a complaint about misrepresentation, supervision failures, practicing outside competence, or research misconduct. That is a different kind of news than a fee schedule. It is slower. It is personal. It is why this desk keeps the PDF in the ASHA stack instead of treating ethics as decoration.

## How to cite it

Use the policy URL for readers. Use the 2023 PDF when you need the rule letters. Do not quote page-length excerpts in a staff newsletter; the code is ASHA’s text. Point. Paraphrase the duty. If you are writing a clinic policy, write your own sentences and then check that you have not promised less than the code requires of CCC holders.

A poster that says “we are ethical” without naming the Board, the CCC, and the competence rule is interior design.
