# The Benefit Policy Manual still calls SLPA therapy unskilled

*Educational news brief for SLPWOW. Not legal advice, not billing advice, and not a substitute for reading the primary source or for evaluation by a licensed clinician.*

The ASHA desk already told the hiring story. This brief is the CMS artifact. Transmittal R13051BP walks through personnel qualifications for outpatient therapy and then reaches speech-language pathology support personnel. The paragraph is not ambiguous. Assistant services are not recognized for Medicare coverage. Bill them as therapy and they are unskilled, not reasonable and necessary, denied. State licensure does not rescue the claim. Aide services under a therapist’s supervision are not therapy services. Help with unskilled tasks if you want; do not bill those tasks as 92507.

The same transmittal is also the place CMS restates that outpatient SLP qualifications were aligned with home-health personnel rules at 42 CFR 484.4, and that 42 CFR 410.62(a) was revised so the SLP on a Part B claim has to meet those requirements. That is why clinical-fellow and provisional-license fights keep showing up in ASHA alerts. Read the SLP definition and the assistant paragraph as two different problems.

## Why a transmittal still matters in 2026

Social posts treat manuals as dead. MACs do not. When a new billing company says they have a “shared visit” theory for SLPAs, ask which chapter they are reading. If they cannot point at a replacement for this paragraph, they are selling hope.

PTA and OTA language in the same manual family is longer because those assistants are recognized. Do not copy a PTA supervision flowchart onto an SLPA and call it Medicare compliance.

## Incident-to is not a loophole here

People reach for incident-to when they want a cheaper pair of hands. The transmittal’s SLP support-personnel section is written to stop that reach. If your counsel has a different reading, get it in writing. This magazine is not your counsel.

## How to file the PDF

Put R13051BP next to the ASHA Medicare FAQ. They agree. That agreement is the public news. When CMS changes it, the news will be a new transmittal, not a webinar.

Schools and private plans can still employ SLPAs under their own rules. This paragraph is Medicare’s. Mixing the two in a staff meeting is how a hospital starts billing Part B for a school-style assistant model. If you run both books, write two supervision policies and put the payer name in the title. The manual will not do that work for you.

Aides who set up the room, walk the person down the hall, or clean the FEES cart are not thereby furnishing therapy. Thank them. Do not drop their minutes into 92507. The transmittal’s aide sentence is there because someone already tried.

If you are writing a Medicare compliance policy in 2026, quote the support-personnel heading and put the transmittal number in the footnote. Future-you will thank present-you when a new billing vendor arrives with a flowchart that has no citation. Flowcharts without transmittals are art.
