# Illustrations — AI evals explainer pack

Original schematic SVGs for this staged series. They are **concept diagrams** (task shape, scoring flow, comparison layouts), not reproductions of figures from published papers.

## License and sources

- Diagrams in `diagrams/` are original to this pack (simple geometry and text labels).
- No COSMOS or house product artwork.
- Institutional logos are avoided; public benchmark names appear as plain text in diagrams only.
- Do not swap these for scraped leaderboard screenshots or paper figure exports without a separate rights review.

## WordPress / site paths

Articles reference `../assets/diagrams/<file>.svg` from `essays/` and `explainers/`. On import, map that prefix to the media library path you choose (for example `/wp-content/uploads/ai-evals/diagrams/`).

## SEO captions

Each article uses HTML `<figure>` / `<figcaption>` (see `STYLE_GUIDE.md`). Captions name the instrument, venue or year where relevant, and what the schematic shows—without live leaderboard numbers.
