Freezer jam is how I get a spread without a canner Saturday.

I cook a small pot — or I use a pectin insert written for freezer jam — I pack it in freezer-safe jars, I leave headspace, I freeze. The fridge holds what we will eat in a short stretch. The shelf does not get this jar. This jar is not a water-bath jar.

I follow the paper that comes with the pectin if I use pectin. I do not invent a no-sugar canned process and then stick it in the freezer as if that made the invention official. The freezer is a storage place, not a blessing on a bad method.

Why I like it in 8a: the glut is loud, the house is hot, I may not want ten cases of sugar-heavy canned jam. I still want toast in January. Freezer jam can be a smaller sugar conversation if the insert allows it. I still will not turn that into a medical win. It is a taste and a texture.

Why I do not only make freezer jam: freezer space. A chest full of jam is a chest with no room for halves. I keep a balance.

I already put freezer jam in a pretty glass with no headspace and I cracked the glass. Headspace. Plastic tubs are allowed. Pride is not required.

Lemon in freezer jam is flavor and color. It is not the NCHFP canning acidification step unless I am actually canning. I do not blur those jobs.

Zone 7 people live on freezer jam because they can. Zone 9 people forget the freezer and then remember in a heat wave. We remember on purpose.

Label FREEZER. I have watched a helpful person put a freezer jar in a cupboard “with the others.” The cupboard is not in on the joke.

This is the bridge to the jam stage. If an editor cuts, keep a bridge.

I taste freezer jam colder than canned jam. I let it sit in the fridge a day after thaw before I judge it. Frozen sugar tastes louder.

If a batch weeps in the fridge I stir it and I eat it sooner. I do not recan it as a save. Recanning a freezer jam by a method I invented is how a middle path becomes a bad path.

A kid can decorate these lids. The word FREEZER still has to be the biggest word.

I do not give freezer jam to a person who will drive it three hours in a hot car as a gift. I give canned, or I give dried, or I give a bag and a recipe. The middle path includes the trip home.

I thaw freezer jam in the fridge, not on a dash. A dash is how you get a hot ring and a cold core and a story.

If the insert wants a rest before freeze, I rest. If I skip the rest I get a weep I will blame on the fruit. The fruit did not write the insert.

I do not can leftover freezer jam “to save it” when the freezer is full. Full freezer means I eat jam and I cook halves. It does not mean I invent a process for a product that was never a canned product.

A plastic jar from the canning aisle is fine. A yogurt tub is fine if I trust the lid. A pretty glass with no headspace is how I sweep sugar glass. I have swept it. I write HEADSPACE on the card like I am teaching a child. I am teaching me.
