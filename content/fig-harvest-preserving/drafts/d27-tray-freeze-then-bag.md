The freezer is how 8a August stays edible.

I wash, sort, stem, and lay ripe figs in a single layer on a sheet pan. Whole if they are small and sound. Halves if they are large or I want them for jam later. The pan goes in the coldest part of the freezer until the fruit is firm. Then I bag. That is tray-freeze.

If I bag first I get a fig brick. A fig brick is a hatchet project. You will chip what you need and you will swear. You will also thaw more than you wanted, and thawed figs do not go back to proud.

Parchment on the pan saves a stuck half. A little space between fruit saves a clump. I do not stack two layers and hope. Hope is a clump.

I work the same morning I pick when I can. Afternoon fruit can still freeze. Counter fruit from yesterday is a quality tax. I will still freeze it for the pot if it is clean. I will not freeze it for a pretty thaw.

NCHFP is clear enough for me: freezing does not need lemon for safety. Lemon or ascorbic is a color note. I splash if I remember on halves that brown. I do not make a ritual that slows the pan while the rest of the bowl sits.

Bags get the name, the cut, and the date. “Figs” is how I thaw Turkey when I wanted Celeste.

I already filled a gallon bag from a colander because the game was on. January me took a hammer to that bag. January me was unkind about August me.

Zone 7 freezers are just as useful. Zone 9 people think they have more fresh weeks. They still brick a bag if they skip the tray.

The sheet pan is the whole method. Everything else is a label.

I keep two pans if the glut is loud. I rotate. A stuffed freezer that cannot take a pan is a freezer I have to eat down before I pick tomorrow. That is a real limit. I do not hide fruit in a door shelf and call it a system.

A vacuum sealer can follow the tray, not replace it. Seal frozen pieces. If you seal soft fruit you mash them.

I do not freeze in glass jars full to the lid. Fruit expands. I have cleaned fig off a shelf. Headspace, or use a bag.

If the power is ugly in a storm week I keep the freezer shut. I do not open it to admire the pans. Admiration is a temperature.

This is the draft I would keep if an editor cut the freeze set to one page. Tray, then bag. Date the bag. Use it in the pot.

I do not freeze in the paper carton the fruit never came in. I freeze in a bag or a tub I can close. A carton in a chest freezer is a sponge for smells.

If the pan will not fit I use two dinner plates. Ugly. Fine. Single layer is the rule, not the cookware.

I have tried to “quick freeze” by piling fruit in a metal bowl. The middle stayed soft. The edges froze together. I tray now even when I am late.

A fan in the freezer is not a thing I own. Airflow is space. I do not pack the freezer so tight the pan cannot see cold. If I have to eat a lasagna to make a fig pan fit, I eat the lasagna. The figs are the week’s job.

When the pieces are firm I bag fast so they do not frost on the counter. Frost on the counter is water I will taste in March.
