There is a week, sometimes two, when Celeste is still giving and Turkey has started and the LSU names are not shy. That week is not a jam festival unless I already have jars, lids, and a day. That week is a freeze festival.

I pick morning. I sort. Table fruit stays out. Everything else hits pans. I do not start a nine-hour jam on a Tuesday when I have three more mornings like this coming. I buy time.

The kitchen in glut week looks rude. Pans. Bowls. A towel on the floor. I accept rude. Pretty canning photos happen on a Saturday I scheduled after the freeze bought it.

I already tried to “keep up” by canning every night. I made bad jam at 11 p.m. and I hated figs for a month. The freezer would have let me be a person.

Delegation: someone else can tray-freeze if they can sort a fizzy fig out. I do not need them to know a plate test. I need them to know a sour smell.

If the freezer fills, I eat down last year’s bags first. I do not rent a second freezer in my head. I cook.

Birds do not pause because my pots are full. The walk still happens. Fruit I cannot process still gets picked if it is ripe. Ground fruit still leaves. The freeze is how I do not lie to the tree.

Zone 7 glut is shorter. They should freeze even faster. Zone 9 glut is longer. They can schedule more pot days. 8a is a spike. Treat it like a spike.

I keep lids, bags, and marker in a box that does not migrate to the shop. Glut week is not when I hunt a pen.

A storm in glut week is a same-day split freeze. Those bags say COOK. They do not say GIFT.

This week decides whether September is calm. September Alma is nicer if August is already in bags.

I sleep in glut week. I do not stay up to prove I am a preserver. A preserver who sleeps still has a machine in the morning.

If family visits in that week I put them on stem-and-tray. I do not put them on a lecture. Hands. Pans. Dates.

The shop can wait. The figs cannot. I have said that to a workbench and I have been right.

When the spike ends I wash the pans and I look at the tape list. Then I pick a jam Saturday like a person who owns a calendar, not like a person owned by a tree.

I do not start a new variety project in glut week. No grafting, no pot-up, no “quick” air-layer. The fruit is the project. Jack can have the wood in winter. I have the bowls now.

If I am traveling in that week I pick hard the days I am home and I freeze like I am leaving. I do not leave a house-sitter a canner and a smile. I leave a tray habit and a COOK bag rule.

A broken freezer in glut week is a neighbor call and a pot week I did not want. I keep enough jar lids for that emergency and I still do not invent a process. I cook fridge batches and I give fruit away. Pride is how you keep fruit too long on a counter while you wait for a repair. Give it away.

I sleep with a pan already in the freezer so morning me only has to fill the next one. The first pan is a decision I made while I still liked figs.
