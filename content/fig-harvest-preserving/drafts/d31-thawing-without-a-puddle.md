A thaw is a leak. I plan for the leak.

Fridge thaw, bag in a bowl, overnight if the pieces are whole. Halves go faster. Puree cubes go faster than that. I do not leave a bag in the sink in the sun stripe.

The juice is fig. I save it for the pot, for the cake, for a glass if it tastes clean. I do not pour it down the drain and then add water back to a jam like I am clever.

If I need fruit now I cook from frozen. That is the no-puddle method. The puddle never forms in a separate bowl because it forms in the pot where it belongs.

Microwave thaw is how I get hot edges and a frozen middle. I will do it for a cube I am stirring into hot oatmeal. I will not do it for a plate I care about.

I already thawed a gallon brick in a colander on the counter because I needed “just a pint.” I got a pint of mush and a quart of juice and a smell by supper. Tray-freeze would have let me take a pint. That is the tray draft coming back to collect a debt.

Counter thaw in our house, even in January, is a short window. The kitchen is not a walk-in. If I must, I stay in the room.

I do not wash thawed figs again unless they picked up freezer grit. Washing a leaky half is how you water down a jam.

Zone 7 people thaw in a colder mudroom. Fine. Zone 9 people should be as suspicious as we are. 8a houses in winter are still humid enough to make a bowl tired.

If the bag has ice crystals like a snow globe, quality already paid. I cook that bag. I do not serve it.

Label side up so I do not thaw DRY into a recipe that already has a sweet pack hiding.

The bowl under the bag is not optional. Fig juice on a freezer-to-fridge tour of the shelf is an ant story even in winter.

I thaw only what I will cook in a day. The rest stays frozen. That sentence would have saved me more fruit than a better variety.

A slow fridge thaw also keeps the kitchen from smelling like a winery. Fast warm thaw smells louder. I have a family. I have a shop door. I use the fridge.

If I am making jam I weigh after thaw or I measure in cups of mash and I accept that water is part of the cup. I do not pretend a thawed cup is a fresh cup. I cook to the plate test, not to a fantasy weight.

Puddle is information. It tells you the fruit was ripe and the freezer did its pause. Use the information. Do not fight it.

I do not thaw in a cardboard box on a porch because the porch is “cold enough.” January sun on a porch in 8a is a surprise. The fridge is boring and correct.

A colander without a bowl is how you lose the juice and wash the cabinet. I have done that while talking. I talk less during a thaw now.

If I need half a bag I break the tray-frozen pieces apart in the bag with my hands, take what I need, and I shut the bag before the rest weeps. That only works if I tray-froze. A brick does not allow a half bag. The brick is a tax I already wrote.

Thaw juice on a white shirt is a stain I will not lecture about. I wear a shop shirt. This is still a kitchen draft. It is also a Saturday.
