August is for freezing. September is for the pot, if I was kind to myself.

I pick a Saturday that is not also a storm and a market and a shop crisis. I pull labeled DRY halves from the freezer the night before and I thaw them in the fridge in a bowl. I print the NCHFP page. I count jars, new lids, rings that are not rusty, sugar, bottled lemon, a wide pot, a rack, a ladle, a towel.

Morning: coffee, then the canner water starts. I do not start the jam before the water is at least thinking about work. I chop what needs chopping. I keep the lemon on the counter so I cannot “forget.”

I cook one pot I can control. I do not run two novels at once in August leftover heat that is still sitting in September.

Plate test. Fill. Wipe. Lids. Process the minutes for this elevation. Rest. Listen. Wait for cool. Test seals. Rings off. Label name, product, date, FREEZER if it is not this Saturday.

Then I wash. A dirty Saturday night is how I skip the next one.

I already canned on a Sunday night after church because the halves were thawing and I felt guilty. I burned the second pot and I sealed four jars I was too tired to wipe. Two failed. Guilt is a bad sous-chef.

If the Saturday is Alma week and I also have fresh fruit, I still start with the frozen pile I already trust. Fresh can be a second pot or a fridge batch. Mixing a tired fresh bowl into a good frozen batch is how you spend a page on a maybe.

Zone 7 should do this earlier. Zone 9 can do it later. 8a September is my window before I am tired of sugar.

Kids can label. They cannot clock the process time.

I eat a normal lunch. A canner day on no food is how you skip a wipe.

The order is the craft: thaw, page, pot, plate, process, rest, label, wash. Romance is a ping. The order is why I still like figs in October.

I put a chair in the kitchen. Standing for pride is how I rush the last pot.

If a neighbor drops in I hand them a towel or I hand them a coffee on the porch. I do not hand them the ladle and a story while the clock runs.

When the jars are cool I write the tally on the freezer card so I know what left. The Saturday is not done until the card is done.

Then I leave the kitchen. The tree can wait until morning. The jars can sit. I can be a person again.

I set out the jars in the morning so they are clean and hot when I need them, the way the page wants. Cold jars and hot jam is how you hear a crack you cannot take back.

I do not start a second project — pickles, tomatoes, a stew — on the same burner story. Fig Saturday is fig Saturday.

If the power blinks I keep the pot off the heat and I think. A half-processed load is a fridge load or a start-over. I do not guess minutes in the dark.

A radio is allowed. A phone rabbit hole is not. I have let a video run a pot too far. The plate told on me. The radio does not show me a better pot in California.

I put a towel on the floor before I fill. I will spill. The towel is cheaper than a scald I will not write as a medical paragraph. It is a floor paragraph. I have a floor. I use the towel.
