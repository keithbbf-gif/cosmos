The useful pretreat for whole figs is the check — 30 seconds of boil, ice, drain. I already wrote that. Everything else is optional or someone else’s climate.

Lemon juice or ascorbic acid: a splash in the ice water or on halves keeps some names from browning. Quality, not safety. Bottled lemon is what I reach for because I can repeat it. I do not squeeze a decorative bowl of lemons for a bushel unless I already have them.

Honey dip: I have done it. It makes a stickier, sweeter leather. It also makes a fly magnet and a tray that never feels dry. I do it for a small gift batch. I do not do it for the glut. Theater.

Sugar sprinkle: same story. Candy. Fine if I said candy out loud.

Sulfur: old commercial and some dry-climate home notes. I do not sulfur on this place. I do not have the setup, and I do not want that smell in a shop we also live in. If a bulletin in a dry county tells you how, that bulletin is not my porch.

Blanching halves: not required. I skip it.

I already ran a honey-dipped load in August and spent the evening walking yellow jackets off the porch. The figs were fine. The method was a party I did not mean to throw.

Zone 7 hobbyists love a honey dip because the air is drier and the flies are fewer some weeks. Steal their lemon if you want color. Steal their sulfur if you are them. Do not steal a porch party into our humidity.

A splash of salt water is a rumor I will not repeat as a method. I have not made it a practice. I will not start here.

The fruit should taste like the fruit. If I need a sauce to make a fig worth drying, I should have jammed it or I should have left it on the tree another day.

Pretreat is a door or a color. It is not a personality.

I do not soak figs in juice overnight to “infuse.” Overnight in liquid is the opposite of drying. I can stew dried figs in juice later. That is cooking. That is not a pretreat.

Wine dips belong to a different house. If I want wine and figs I make that at the table.

A cinnamon dust on a small tray is a cookie move. I label that jar “cinnamon” so I do not surprise someone who wanted a plain Celeste.

The only pretreat I will scold you for skipping is the check on whole fruit. The only pretreat I will scold you for copying from a pretty video is a honey bath in wasp season.

Keep the bottle of lemon. Keep the kettle. Leave the sulfur to a book I am not writing.

I do not pretreat with wine, liquor, or a spice tea as a dry step. Those are stews I can do after the jar is honest. A wet flavor bath before the machine is how I add hours and flies.

A commercial “fruit fresh” shake is ascorbic by another name. If I use it I measure like a cook, not like a magician. I still do not call it safety.

I have seen a video roll fruit in sugar until it looked like a doughnut hole and then call it dried. That is candy. Candy can live in this kitchen. Candy does not get the dried-fig lid.

The pretreat I will defend at a table is the check. The pretreat I will allow is lemon. The pretreat I will laugh at in August is honey on a porch. Three sentences. That is the policy.
