# Staging sheet

Every file in `drafts/` carries YAML:

```yaml
status: staged
stage: pick | dry | freeze | jam
focus: 8a
zones: [7, 8a, 8b, 9]
author: Jack Chambers | PapaFig
cluster: harvest | drying | freezing | jam
voice: human
voice_check: written   # writer pass. Editor sets edited after the human pass.
```

`status: staged` means the piece is in the magazine pile for an editor, not
`published`, not `scratch`. WordPress stays Draft.

## Read order (if you want fruit in a jar, not a pile)

1. `d01` through `d14` — pick it
2. `d15` through `d26` — dry it
3. `d27` through `d35` — freeze it
4. `d36` through `d46` — jam it

## Required topics (coverage)

- Harvest: no off-tree ripen, neck droop, 8a calendar, breba rumor, morning
  pick, rain split, closed vs open eye, variety order, first-year souvenir,
  pots vs dirt, stop date, latex, overnight bowl, birds
- Drying: 8a humidity, dehydrator number, oven, whole/halves, checking,
  bend test, conditioning, which names dry, pantry vs freezer, kitchen uses,
  pretreat, moldy August tray
- Freezing: tray then bag, cuts, sugar pack as quality, cooking use, thaw,
  storage time, glut week, labels, freezer jam
- Jam: glut, acid, fridge batch, NCHFP cite, pectin vs long cook, variety
  flavor, plate test, butter/conserve/preserves, jar failures, Saturday,
  no medical sermon

If a later editor cuts the set down, keep at least one draft from each stage
and do not cut `d01` (no off-tree ripen), `d16` (dehydrator number),
`d27` (tray-freeze), or `d37` (lemon juice).
