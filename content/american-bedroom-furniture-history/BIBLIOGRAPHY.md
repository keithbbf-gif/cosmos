# Bibliography

A working list for the draft series. It is not complete. Museum collection pages cited in individual essays should be checked on the day of layout; accession numbers migrate. Items marked *orientation* are surveys, not the last word.

## American furniture, colonial and Federal

- Anderson, Jennifer L. *Mahogany: The Costs of Luxury in Early America*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2012.
- Cooper, Wendy A. *Classical Taste in America, 1800–1840*. Baltimore: Baltimore Museum of Art / Abbeville, 1993.
- Downs, Joseph. *American Furniture: Queen Anne and Chippendale Periods in the Henry Francis du Pont Winterthur Museum*. New York: Macmillan, 1952.
- Heckscher, Morrison H. *American Furniture in The Metropolitan Museum of Art*. Late colonial / Chippendale volumes. New York: Met.
- Heckscher, Morrison H. “John Townsend’s Block-and-Shell Furniture.” *Antiques* 121, no. 5 (May 1982).
- Heckscher, Morrison H., and Leslie Greene Bowman. *American Rococo, 1750–1775*. New York: Met / Abrams, 1992.
- Hornor, William Macpherson, Jr. *Blue Book of Philadelphia Furniture*. Philadelphia, 1935.
- Jobe, Brock, ed. *Boston Furniture of the Eighteenth Century*. Boston: Colonial Society of Massachusetts, 1974.
- Jobe, Brock, and Myrna Kaye. *New England Furniture: The Colonial Era*. Boston: Houghton Mifflin / SPNEA, 1984.
- Kenny, Peter M. *Honoré Lannuier: Cabinetmaker from Paris*. New York: Metropolitan Museum of Art, 1998.
- Kenny, Peter M., and Michael K. Brown. *Duncan Phyfe: Master Cabinetmaker in New York*. New York: Metropolitan Museum of Art, 2011.
- Montgomery, Charles F. *American Furniture: The Federal Period*. Winterthur / Viking, 1966.
- Moses, Michael. *Master Craftsmen of Newport: The Townsends and Goddards*. Tenafly, N.J.: MMI Americana, 1984.
- Ward, Gerald W. R., and related Yale Furniture Study notes on the Brixey/Townsend high chest.

## Beds, textiles, southern and Shaker

- Baumgarten, Linda. Colonial Williamsburg textile and clothing catalogs.
- Montgomery, Florence M. *Textiles in America, 1650–1870*. New York: Norton / Winterthur, 1984.
- MESDA object database and *Journal of Early Southern Decorative Arts*.
- Rieman, Timothy D., and Jean M. Burks. *The Complete Book of Shaker Furniture*. New York: Abrams, 1993. *orientation*
- Cummings, Abbott Lowell. *Rural Household Inventories*. Boston: SPNEA, 1964.
- Cummings, Abbott Lowell. *The Framed Houses of Massachusetts Bay, 1625–1725*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard / SPNEA, 1979.

## Victorian suites, Grand Rapids, reform

- Ames, Kenneth L. *Death in the Dining Room and Other Tales of Victorian Culture*. Philadelphia: Temple University Press, 1992.
- Eastlake, Charles L. *Hints on Household Taste*. London, 1868; American eds.
- Edwards, Clive. *Victorian Furniture: Technology and Design*. Manchester: Manchester University Press, 1993.
- Grier, Katherine C. *Culture and Comfort: People, Parlors, and Upholstery*. Rochester: Strong Museum / UMass, 1988.
- Witherell, Brian L. *Late 19th Century Furniture by Berkey & Gay*. Atglen, PA: Schiffer, 1998.
- Berkey & Gay Furniture Co. *Character in Furniture*. Grand Rapids, 1910. Winterthur Library (Internet Archive).
- Grand Rapids History Center, Berkey & Gay records; 1930 Simmons-era price list.

## Arts and Crafts, Craftsman, Greene & Greene

- Cathers, David M. *Furniture of the American Arts and Crafts Movement*. Several eds., first 1981.
- Cathers, David M. *Gustav Stickley*. London: Phaidon, 2003.
- Stickley Museum at Craftsman Farms, object 1997.2 and related suite records.
- *The Craftsman*, 1901–1916.
- Bosley, Edward R. *Greene & Greene*. London: Phaidon, 2000.
- Makinson, Randell L. *Greene & Greene: Furniture and Related Designs*. Salt Lake City: Peregrine Smith, 1979.
- Gamble House / USC Greene & Greene Archives (bedroom furniture object numbers as cited in ch. 19).

## Mid-century to now

- Kirkham, Pat. *Charles and Ray Eames*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1995. *orientation*
- Paul McCobb Museum / Planner Group notes (Winchendon, 1949–64).
- *New York Times*, 12 March 1969 (McCobb obituary; “best-selling” claim — treat as obituary, not an audit).
- IKEA Museum, “MALM chest of drawers from 2002” (Lundin; Kamprad, September 2002).
- CPSC recall files, MALM dressers, 2016–2017 (safety fact).
- Dezeen / *Manager Magazin* reporting on e15 SL02 Mo and the MALM bed, 2016.

## Arkansas and BBF-adjacent

- Fort Smith Historical Society *Journal*, March 2007 (Ballman biography); April 2014 (Myers).
- *Encyclopedia of Arkansas*, timber industry entry.
- Curry, Corlis C. Thesis on Ashley, Bradley, and Drew Counties, 1952.
- Bragg and Bragg, Forest Service historical ecology, Saline-Fifteen (as cited in related mill-town writing).
- Bradley Brand Furniture heritage/craft pages — footnote only; not a corporate history source for Fullerton or Potlatch.

## Labor and the social chamber

- Vlach, John Michael. *Back of the Big House*. Chapel Hill: UNC Press, 1993.
- White, Shane. *Somewhat More Independent*. Athens: University of Georgia Press, 1991. *orientation* for New York emancipation chronology.
- Chipstone *American Furniture* annual — labor and attribution essays as cited per chapter.
