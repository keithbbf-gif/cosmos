La Boqueria is guilty of being photographed. It is not guilty of failing to sell figs.

In the late weeks of summer the fruit stalls take on a purple that the juice bars cannot fake. Catalan gardens — Maresme, a backyard toward Collserola if you are lucky — and the wider Spanish crop, Extremadura and Alicante among them, show up in flats that look too delicate for the crowd. The crowd is the problem and the customer. A fig that can survive this aisle is a fig that was picked with a shorter hope, or a fig that has already begun to think about jam.

I come early or I do not come. Early, you can still see a stallholder as a person with a knife and a preference: this crate is for eating today, that one is for a cook who will hide the fruit in a tart. Later you see a stallholder as a person with a queue and a phone camera in their face, saying the price twice because nobody heard. The figs do not care, except that heat and handling will turn them into a smell that will lose the next customer.

Spain’s other fig, the dried one that will become *pan de higo*, is not the star here in August. It lives in the nut shops, in the winter, in a pressed cake beside almonds, or in a bag from a town in Málaga that the market can summon without growing it. The Boqueria is a switchboard. It can call a coast and a plateau and an island and put them on one marble counter that has been wiped with the same rag for a generation.

There is a class of visitor who will buy one fig, photograph it, and leave. There is a class of cook who will buy a kilo and look guilty about the price, which is the correct look. Be the second if you can. The first is how a stall dies into theater.

If you are writing the market as a bucket list, stop. Write one stall, one price, one argument about whether the black figs are better than the green this Tuesday. Buy a small amount if you are walking; buy more if you have a kitchen within an hour. Walk to a quieter street. Eat standing up. The juice will decide if you understood the week.

The foodway is not the iron entrance. The foodway is a city that can still, despite itself, get a fragile fruit from a tree to a mouth before noon. After noon, buy something else. The fig has a curfew.
