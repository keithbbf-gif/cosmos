A hotel will put a dried fig next to any cheese and call it Mediterranean. Sometimes the hotel is accidentally correct. Usually the hotel is using a fruit from one sea and a cheese from a catalog.

The pairing works when the animals and the trees ate the same weather. Sheep on a dry hill, a fig tree in the same fold, a cheese that is salty because the year was, a fruit that is sweet because the year was. Manchego and *pan de higo* are a conversation in Spanish that cheese shops already know how to have. Aged kaşar or a tulum with an Aydın fig is a conversation in Turkish that a tea table understands without a board. A Pag cheese — a Gligora if you are near that door — with a slice of *smokvenjak* is a conversation in a wind that makes both the sheep and the cake.

Fresh figs and fresh cheeses are a different sentence — Provençal, Roman, a late-summer lunch that will not keep and should not be asked to. Dried figs and aged cheeses are a winter sentence. Do not mash them into one “fig and cheese” tip with a stock photograph of both seasons at once. That photograph is a lie about time.

I like a plate that looks a little poor: three figs, a wedge, bread if the cheese is feral enough to need a tool. I distrust a board that has seventeen items and a sprig of something that did not grow with either. The sprig is doing the work the fruit should do. If you need decoration, you picked a dull cheese or a dull fig.

Prosciutto and a fresh fig is Italy’s postcard, and it is delicious, and it is not a dried-foodway. If we run it, we run it in August, with a warning that the photograph will be stolen by every menu in a tourist street. The dried fig’s pork cousin is rarer: a salumi plate in winter with a *crocetta* on the side, a Calabrian idea, fat against a baked cross of fruit. Try it before you write it off as sweet on sweet. The fat is the translator. So is a dry wine that does not want to be dessert.

The rule for the table: name the cheese as carefully as the fig. “Local cheese” is a failure. So is “artisanal.” Give the animal, the age, the town if you have it. Then give the fruit — Sarılop, Dottato, *hib*, *pan de higo*, a nameless house bag. Then stop talking so people can eat. The pairing is geography. If you cannot name the geography, serve water and be honest.
