# IMAGE_SOURCES — Fig Mediterranean Foodways

Art-desk notes for the staged series. **Cleared staging rasters** live in `assets/images/` with `RIGHTS.md` and `GRAPHICS_INDEX.md`; each draft embeds one `<figure>` (see `figures_registry.json`). Regenerate with `python3 tools/fig_med_foodways_graphics.py all`. User `D:\FIGS` notes were not available in the cloud agent — selections follow these notes plus USDA/Commons. Recheck every Commons license at import time.

House rules:

- No wellness clichés: woman in linen biting a fig, dew-drop hero, “superfood” flat lay.
- No medical diagrams, supplement bottles, or gym plates.
- No prickly-pear / *Opuntia* pads in any well except draft 45, where the cactus is the subject.
- People in markets: faces are not props. Prefer hands, crates, trays, backs, or explicitly released portraits.
- Food stills: show work furniture — *kerevet*, *cannizza*, *lijese*, tins, strings — not only a styled bowl.
- If a file is CC BY or CC BY-SA, keep the author line in the slug. If a museum forbids commercial reuse of their photograph, do not send that file to a commercial well.

Search starting points that serve many drafts:

- Wikimedia Commons categories: `Ficus carica`, `Dried figs`, `Fig harvest`, `Still-life paintings with figs`, `Aydın`, `İncir`, `Kymi`, `Fichi di Cosenza`.
- Specific Commons file worth a hero for the ancient plate: [Wall painting — still life with bread and figs — Herculaneum — Napoli MAN 8625](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wall_painting_-_still_life_with_bread_and_figs_-_Herculaneum_-_Napoli_MAN_8625.jpg) (photo CC BY-SA 4.0 ArchaiOptix; the fresco itself is public domain). Alternate Flickr PD-mark photo of the same wall.
- [File:DriedFigs1.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DriedFigs1.JPG) — PD dried-fig still, useful as a *secondary* only; it is generic.
- [File:Dried fig.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dried_fig.png) — Tiia Monto, CC BY-SA 4.0; generic cut fig, not a regional hero.
- Gallica and the British Library: 19th-c. Smyrna / İzmir harbor prints, “Smyrne” in the catalog.
- Library of Congress and Calisphere: California fig-drying yards (Calimyrna story), search `fig drying Fresno` / `Calimyrna`.
- EU PDO dossiers as *text* sources only; their photographs are usually not cleared for us.

## fig-med-01 — The yard is the factory

- **Hero search:** Commons / Flickr CC: `kerevet incir`, `Aydın incir kurutma`, `fig drying trays Turkey`, `Nazilli fig tunnel`.
- **Wanted:** single-layer trays in a yard, slope in the background, not a studio bowl.
- **Rights note:** many modern Aydın photos on news sites are all rights reserved. Do not scrape TARIM or commodity-board pages.
- **Avoid:** plastic snack packs, “Turkish delight and figs” hotel plates.
- **Caption seed:** Sarılop on *kerevet*, Büyük Menderes hinterland, August.

## fig-med-02 — Cover them or lose the night
