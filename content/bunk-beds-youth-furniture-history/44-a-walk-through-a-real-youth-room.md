# A walk through a real youth room

I am going to walk a made-up room that is assembled from real habits. No family is named. If you recognize the room, that is because the room is common.

## The door

You smell laundry and pencil shavings. A twin-over-twin along the long wall. A tall chest opposite. A nightstand with a lamp and a tangle. A laundry basket that has become a step. A poster that covers a scuff, not a stud.

## The bunk

Foundation underside: I get on the floor. Tape says 34 inches. We are in the rule.

Label on the footboard: a maker I have heard of, model, **03/2016**. Post-2000. Pre-nothing-else. Children's intent (dinosaurs). I would want a CPC in a perfect world. In this world I photograph the plate.

Pillow moved. Warning present, faded. Instructions missing. Thickness unknown. Mattress is a tall pillow-top that comes within two fingers of the rail top. That is the 5-inch story, failing in real time. The "upgrade" someone bought in 2022.

Wall-side rail: runs most of the length, then a decorative gap at the head about the width of a paperback book standing up — more than 0.22 inches, less than 15. The bed is **against the wall**. This is the 1990s door, in 2016 steel, because someone mounted the ladder on the wall side or swapped rails after a move. I do not know which. I know the slot.

Lower end: a cute oval. Torso-ish. I do not have a wedge. I have a suspicion. The oval is at a three-year-old's face if they stood on the lower mattress.

Ladder: hooked, a little walk to the side. Extra person on top is a stuffed animal pile that has become a second mattress. Horseplay is a memory the room is still arranged for.

Age: the child who sleeps on top is eight. The child on the bottom is four. The four-year-old's blanket is on the top "from last night." The instruction is already a negotiation the instruction lost.

## The chest

No date I can find. Looks 2000s. Light. I open the bottom drawers — they are empty, which is worse, because the mass is gone. The anti-tip strap is in the top drawer under socks, still in the poly bag. Anchor It as literature.

Height is over 27. This is a CSU in spirit and probably in F2057 gates. Manufacture is pre-2023. STURDY will not visit this chest unless someone buys a new one. The four-year-old already uses the lower drawers as stairs. Of course they do. The ladder taught them vertical.

## The nightstand

Cords: lamp, tablet charger, a baby monitor leftover from a crib that is now in the attic (the crib is a 2008 drop-side; the attic is the right place; the monitor cord migrated). The charger hangs off the bunk post on the top bunk. Hanging file, older-kid edition.

## What I would do before another night, if this were my problem

I am not your inspector. If I lived here:

1. Four-year-old stays off the top. The blanket comes down. The negotiation ends.
2. Mattress replaced to the thickness I would extract from the maker by email that afternoon, using the photographed model.
3. Rails remounted so the continuous one is on the wall. If the holes do not allow it, the bed comes off that wall or leaves the house.
4. Oval: I want the maker's test story. If none, that end gets a hard look at retirement.
5. Strap out of the sock drawer, into a stud. Or the chest leaves.
6. Cords off posts. Monitor retired.
7. Laundry basket moved so it is not a stair to the chest.

That is a Saturday. It is less romantic than a new quilt.

## What I would not do

I would not 3D-print a wedge.  
I would not shame the parent in the doorway. The room is a **system of defaults** — stores, moves, thicker mattresses, a strap in a bag. Defaults are the manufacturer after the manufacturer.  
I would not call the room "safe" when I left. I would call it **better documented**.

## The educational sentence

A real room is where every draft in this folder tries to sit at once. If your education cannot survive a Saturday in a messy bedroom, it was a pamphlet. This walk is the exam. Take it in your own doorway tonight. Write down what you do not know. The unknown is the assignment.
