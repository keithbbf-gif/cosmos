# Image rights register

Every raster and vector file under `images/` is listed here before it ships in a `<figure>`. Historical assets are **public domain or Creative Commons** from named sources. Schematics are **original educational drawings** by Keith BBF for this series. None of the SVGs reproduce CPSC, ASTM, or EN test figures from paid or regulatory PDFs.

**Not legal diagrams.** Schematics teach dimensions and hazard geometry at a literacy level. They are not substitutes for 16 CFR 1213.4, ASTM F1427 figures, or lab procedures.

## What we deliberately did not use

- CPSC or ASTM PDF **Figure 1 / Figure 2** redraws (copyright and misrepresentation risk).
- Stock “happy children on bunks” marketing photos (no chain of title).
- Dachau or other concentration-camp bunk imagery (wrong tone and ethics for this series).
