# What this series is not

End by closing the doors I opened.

## Not legal advice

I quoted 16 CFR, a *Federal Register*, CPSC guidance, public EN/ISO scopes. I did not take your facts. A specific SKU, a specific injury, a specific import entry — that is counsel, a lab, or CPSC's own staff. If a draft and the current eCFR disagree, the eCFR wins and the draft is late.

## Not medical advice

I am not a pediatrician. Sleep position, siblings in one bed, disability, the child who cannot climb a ladder but was put up anyway — those are clinical and family facts. The under-6 line is a product instruction, not a developmental assessment of your kid.

## Not a complete world survey

Canada, Australia (AS/NZS 4220 lives in this neighborhood), Japan, China CCC — I did not fake them. EN and ISO are the "other houses" I could scope from public abstracts. If you need a country's book, open that country's book.

## Not a lab manual

No homemade probes. No "beat the test." No 3D-print files. Purpose-level descriptions only. 16 CFR 1213.4 is public. Use it if you are a lab.

## Not a product ranking

No "best bunks of 2026." Rankings rot and they get captured. I named IKEA as a **shape of room** and a public recall memory, not as a score.

## Not a claim that compliance equals safety

Compliance is a floor. Rooms add mattresses, siblings, cords, carpet, time. The 1999 Commission knew near-misses happened in geometries they were still writing. A passing bed can still host a belt. A passing dresser can still meet two children.

## Not a claim that I opened every paid PDF

ASTM F1427 current, F2057-23 full text, F1821-26, F2388, F3096, EN 747-1/2:2024, ISO 9098-1:2023 — I used **public** scopes, CPSC paraphrases, and SATRA/AHFA summaries where I said so. That limitation is in `SOURCES.md`. If you need a clause number inside a paywall, buy the clause.

## Not COSMOS doctrine

This folder is educational content in a tree that is mostly an operating system. It does not change Core, the ledger, or the one-writer rule. It is a staged draft series. Keith BBF's voice, on purpose. If that mix feels odd, read draft 01. Youth furniture is a paper trail. This house knows paper trails.

## Not finished

Standards move. F1821 already showed me a 2026 update in public trackers while I was writing. An honest series **dates itself** (2026-09-14) and invites correction. If you patch a number, patch `SOURCES.md` in the same commit. A claim without an artifact is the failure class.

## What it is, one last time

A human walk through:

- how stacked sleep left the ship and entered the child's room
- how the U.S. wrote an entrapment rule after a decade of counted deaths
- how Europe and ISO wrote height-and-access books
- how the rest of the youth room — crib, toddler bed, dresser, changer — got its own years
- how to read a label, a myth, a salesperson, and a messy doorway

Forty-five drafts. Quality over speed. If you only keep six dates and six numbers, keep these:
