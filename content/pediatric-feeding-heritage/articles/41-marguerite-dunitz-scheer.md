This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Styria is not on most American feeding syllabi until a referral is. Marguerite Dunitz-Scheer, with Peter Scheer, ran a psychosomatic service at the Pediatric Hospital of the Medical University Graz that became internationally known for tube-dependency work. Their later Springer volume *Child-led Tube-management and Tube-weaning* treats decades and thousands of children as a clinical memory. This pack will not print a wean. It will say a European service made an exit interview thinkable.

She was born in 1955 in Washington, D.C., studied medicine in Zurich and Graz, graduated 1981, pediatrics 1986, Gestalt psychotherapy from 1991, deputy head of psychosomatics in Graz 1994–2017 in the Springer bio. Medical director at Notube.com from 2009 in that same bio. Living at pack date. Published work and institutional pages only.

## The work

Dunitz-Scheer treated the tube as a social organizer, not only as a calorie device. Pumps, night feeds, school letters, a childhood that can become a bag — those sentences are why American teams had to answer Graz even when they disliked the brand that later grew around the reputation.

German-speaking infant mental health (GAIMH) and DC:0–3 task-force work sit in her public bio. Psychosomatics, in that tradition, is not a slur meaning “the parent did it.” Exporting the service is how a reputation becomes a product. Products travel faster than staffing.

## What later people kept

Tube dependency as a name. What they discarded, or should have: wean-as-liberation folklore that shames children who still need the tube. What they still argue about: hunger as a tool, weight loss as a price, and whether a website can carry a hospital’s caution.

This pack tells both sentences: tubes keep children alive; exits give some children a meal that is not a pump. Dunitz-Scheer is the person who made the second sentence internationally loud.

## Close

Washington birth, Swiss and Austrian training, a Graz hospital, a website after 2009. The geography is already an argument about whether a wean is a place or a product. Places have night staff. Products have shopping carts. This pack will keep her in the hospital years even while naming the site, because the site is how many American parents heard Styria.

Peter Scheer is a partner in the public record. This file stays on her lead because the pack’s roster is already long. Partnerships vanish the way second authors vanish. Mentioning him here is a refusal of that vanishing, not a second profile.
