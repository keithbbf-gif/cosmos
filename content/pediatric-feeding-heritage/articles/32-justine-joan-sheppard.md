This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Pediatric feeding fame likes the NICU. Justine Joan Sheppard’s work kept reminding the field that “pediatric” includes the child who is twelve, who has a lifelong disability, who eats at school or is fed at school, who was never a famous premature infant. Columbia’s Teachers College / speech-language pathology line is the public academic address. Dysphagia in developmental disability is the object.

She was living when this pack was staged. Published assessments and papers only. This pack will not print a scoring sheet.

## The work

Sheppard treated feeding as a life-span disability issue that happens to begin in childhood. That sentence cuts against a syllabus that ends at bottle weaning. It also cuts against a hospital team that discharges to “the school will handle lunch” without handing the school anything that can be used.

The sibling dysphagia pack mentioned her as a reminder and refused a forty-fifth profile. This pack gives her the file because the rural lunchroom essay needs an ancestor with a bibliography.

## What later people kept

A caseload that is not only an isolette. What they discarded, or should have: the habit of treating school feeding as lesser science. What they still argue about: educational versus medical models, and who is allowed to call a cafeteria a swallow job.

Nancy Swigert’s teaching is a cousin in the next rooms. Sheppard is the person this series points to when a reader asks where the eight-year-old went.

## Close

Teachers College is an education address. That is the tell. Sheppard’s feeding work was never only a hospital product. School, group home, the adult who is still “pediatric” in a service system that does not know where to put her — those rooms are why this profile exists.

A swallow study at eight is a different civic object than a swallow study at eight weeks. Radiation, consent, a child who can refuse with words. Teams that use the same abbreviations for both have not read her weather. Use the abbreviations if you must. Change the room in your head.

Group homes and adult day programs inherited children this field still files as pediatric. The filing is a service-system accident. Sheppard’s work is one of the few bibliographies that treated the accident as a subject.

If your calendar only has neonates, you have already lost the child she kept writing about. Move a slot. That is the only homework this profile will allow, and it is a calendar homework, not a mouth one.
