This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

A registered dietitian walked into a speech-language pathology series because the meal was never only a swallow. Ellyn Satter’s Division of Responsibility — in the language of her books, the parent provides and the child decides — gave American feeding culture a sentence it could argue with for forty years. *Child of Mine: Feeding with Love and Good Sense* is the popular object. Clinic rooms borrowed the sentence and sometimes left the rest of the book in the car.

She was living when this pack was staged. Madison, Wisconsin, in the public professional geography. Published books and essays only. She is not an SLP and this pack will not pretend she is.

## The work

Satter treated feeding as a relationship with jobs. That is a different claim from Holt’s catechism and from a bite-count chair. It is also a different claim from Clara Davis’s institutional cafeteria, though popular memory rhymes them. Davis curated a tray in a ward. Satter wrote about a family table that has to survive a Tuesday.

Popular parenting culture simplified her into “he’ll eat when he’s hungry.” A careful reading does not support using that slogan as a discharge plan when medical risk is in the room. The simplification is part of the heritage. So is the relief parents felt when someone told them they were not required to be short-order cooks and also not required to run a siege.

## What later people kept

A parent job that is not the defendant dock. What they discarded, or should have: the slogan version. What they still argue about: whether a relationship claim can stand next to a tube, an unsafe swallow, or a falling growth curve without becoming neglect with better lighting.

Toomey, Klein, Chatoor, the 2019 psychosocial domain — all of them breathe this weather. Satter is the dietetics ancestor the SLP feeding shelf kept quoting without always citing.

## Close

Madison is a university town that also knows farms. Satter’s table is an American middle-class table unless a reader is careful. Food insecurity does not cancel her jobs. It does limit who can perform them. A parent who cannot provide has been given a job the economy already refused.

Speech-language pathologists who quote her should say she is a dietitian. Credential theft is a quiet habit in this field. The Division of Responsibility is not an SLP invention. It is a loan. Loans should be marked.
