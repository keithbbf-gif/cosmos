This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Not every therapist owned Arvedson’s hardcover. Many owned a clinic manual. Nancy B. Swigert’s *The Source for Pediatric Dysphagia* is the object a certain generation can still find in a file drawer, tabbed, photocopied against its own copyright page. ASHA-facing teaching and pediatric dysphagia leadership in the public record made her a translator between the tertiary hallway and the caseload that is actually a Thursday.

She was living when this pack was staged. Speech-language pathologist. Published manuals and association work only. This pack will not tear out a form.

## The work

Swigert is how pediatric dysphagia became something a school or outpatient clinician could hold without a Milwaukee fellowship. That is a democratic fact. It is also how a field photocopies itself. Manuals are furniture. Furniture gets used as a ladder.

She sits closer to Sheppard’s life-span disability reminder than to Bosma’s cine. The child in the cafeteria is her weather as much as the infant in the suite.

## What later people kept

A usable American English for pediatric swallow and feeding in rooms that are not research wards. What they discarded, or should have: the tabbed page as a substitute for a team. What they still argue about: whether a “source” book medicalizes a school lunch.

This pack will not reprint her hierarchies. The historical residue is the file drawer. File drawers are how heritage actually travels.

## Close

Association teaching is unpaid-looking work that structures a profession. Swigert’s ASHA-facing pediatric dysphagia presence is how a school clinician learned she was allowed to care about a tray. Allowed is a legal word in some states. Manuals cannot change a practice act. They can change a clinician’s nerve.

The file drawer with a tabbed *Source* is a material culture object. Coffee, tape, a page that falls out at “diet textures.” Those textures are adult inheritance. Essay 13 is the argument. This profile is the drawer where the argument lived in practice.

A school SLP with a split caseload does not have Milwaukee. She may have Swigert in a drawer. That inequality is the profession’s actual distribution. Manuals are how the distribution pretends to be even.

Nerve is not competence. A manual can give the first and only hint at the second. Hint is not a fellowship. This pack will not confuse them. It will still honor the drawer.
