This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Lead author is a diplomatic job. Praveen S. Goday’s name sits first on “Pediatric Feeding Disorder: Consensus Definition and Conceptual Framework” (*Journal of Pediatric Gastroenterology and Nutrition*, 2019; 68(1):124–129). A long author line stands behind it. The paper gave four rooms — medical, nutritional, feeding skill, psychosocial — to a problem every shop had been calling something else.

He was living when this pack was staged. Pediatric gastroenterologist in the public bios (Medical College of Wisconsin / children’s-hospital geography in the professional record around the consensus years). Published papers only.

## The work

Goday did not invent the child who cannot eat. He midwifed a word that could travel. GI’s willingness to share a definition with therapy and nutrition is the event. Without that willingness, “feeding” stays a rehabilitation side quest and “failure to thrive” stays the chart’s insult.

Consensus is not peace. Swallow specialists still needed “dysphagia.” Behavior specialists still needed their own manuals. Parents still needed an appointment. A word can do only so much. What it did was make a clean swallow study insufficient as a dismissal.

## What later people kept

Four boxes on a slide. The useful version of those boxes is a meeting. The cheap version is a form. Goday’s paper is responsible for the boxes. It is not responsible for the cheapness.

This pack will not reprint the definition as a worksheet. The citation is enough. The residue is a field that can say PFD and pediatric dysphagia in the same sentence without using one as a suitcase for the other.

## Close

A GI lead author on a therapy-heavy problem is a political kindness. It can also be a political capture. This pack will not settle which it was. It will say the 2019 issue page is now a ritual citation, the way Logemann 1983 is a ritual citation in the sibling pack.

Ritual citations get unread. Unread consensus is how four domains become four checkboxes. If you are going to steal Goday’s boxes, steal the meeting they were meant to force. Leave the checkbox on the form vendor’s desk.

JPGN is a GI journal. Putting a feeding consensus there was a choice about audience. Audiences who only read therapy journals met the word later, as a slide. The issue page is still the primary object.
