This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

A chew can be a folk observation or a data set. Erika Gisel’s McGill line treated mastication in children as something you could time, count, and compare — typical development and the children who were not typical, including cerebral palsy literatures that later therapists still cite when they want a number next to a spoon.

She was living when this pack was staged. Occupational therapy / rehabilitation research address in the public record. Published papers only. No home program.

## The work

Gesell put chew on a chart as an item. Gisel put chew in a methods section. That is a different respectability. It let a feeding note say more than “messy eater.” It also let a field that loved oral-motor stories check itself against stopwatches.

The risk of a stopwatch is obvious. A child becomes a rate. Rates are easy to homework. This pack will not pull her tasks as homework. The historical gift is the refusal to treat chewing as a vibe.

## What later people kept

Chewing as a developmental research object, not only as a workshop module. What they still argue about: how much of mealtime success is mastication and how much is appetite, attention, and the social table.

PFD’s feeding-skill domain is downstream. Gisel is one of the people who made “skill” sound like a measurement instead of a moral.

## Close

McGill is a bilingual university in a city that already knew more than one foodway. Gisel’s chew studies still get cited in English manuals as if Montreal were a generic lab. It is not generic. A bite of baguette and a bite of puree are not the same cultural object even when the stopwatch is honest.

Cerebral palsy feeding literature is where her numbers did some of their hardest work. Those papers are not a reason to homework a jaw. They are a reason to stop saying “messy” as if messy were a diagnosis. Messy is a laundry word. Chewing is a research word. Keep them apart.

Stopwatches belong in methods sections. They do not belong in a parent’s kitchen as a character test. If a reader takes only one sentence from this file, take that one. The rest is citation weather.

Rehabilitation research at McGill sat next to a medical faculty that did not owe American ASHA a syllabus. That independence is useful. It kept chew from being only a U.S. workshop word.
