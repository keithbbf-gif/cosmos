This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

SOFFI is a set of letters a certain kind of nursery now says as if everyone in the hallway already knows the story. Erin Sundseth Ross’s program — Supporting Oral Feeding in Fragile Infants, in the public expansion — put a published name on observation of late preterm and term infants who were not necessarily the tiniest cine stars. This pack will not print the steps. It will say a named program is how a field tries to make watching teachable.

She was living when this pack was staged. Speech-language pathologist in the public bios; neonatal feeding research and teaching. Published program name and papers only.

## The work

Ross sits in the same argument as Shaker and as Wolf and Glass: who owns the feed, the clock or the infant. A program name is a different object from a paper. Names can be licensed, trained, audited, emptied. They can also keep a new staff nurse from inventing a private folklore every Tuesday.

The late preterm infant on a postpartum floor is part of why this name mattered. Not every fragile feeder lives under an isolette. Heritage includes the infant who goes home on day two with a mouth that is not ready for the folklore of “term.”

## What later people kept

A citable way to talk about fragile feeders who are not extreme preterms. What they still argue about: whether a branded program is necessary once the ethic is ordinary, and whether ordinary ethics survive night shift.

This pack refuses a how-to. The residue is the name in the hallway, and the older watching the name tried to standardize.

## Close

Late preterm infants are a twentieth-century survival success that created a twenty-first-century folklore problem. They look “done.” They are not always done. Ross’s program name is one attempt to keep that sentence in a postpartum hallway that wants to send people home.

Fragile is a marketing word and a true word. This pack uses it because the program uses it. It will not turn fragility into a product shot. The residue is a nurse who can say the infant is term on the wristband and still not term in the mouth.

A program name that can be trained can also be audited. Audit culture is how hospitals prove they watched. Proof is not the same as watching. Keep both words. Do not let the first eat the second.
