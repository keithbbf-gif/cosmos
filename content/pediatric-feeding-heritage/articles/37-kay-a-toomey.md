This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Sequential Oral Sensory. The letters traveled farther than most pediatric feeding papers. Kay A. Toomey’s SOS Approach to Feeding became a weekend-course object, a clinic identity, a thing a parent could request by acronym. This pack will not print the hierarchy. It will say an acronym is how a field industrializes a stance toward fear and texture.

She was living when this pack was staged. Psychologist in the public professional record. Published method name and teaching only.

## The work

Toomey gave teams a structured way to talk about children who could swallow and would not eat a diet that looked like a diet. Sensory language, play language, a sequence — the course economy did the rest. Speech-language pathologists and occupational therapists filled rooms that psychology had named. That crossing is the historical event.

Critics said the rooms could become a brand. Supporters said the brand kept untrained staff from inventing a siege. Both sentences have evidence in anecdote. This pack will not pretend to have a trial that ends the argument.

## What later people kept

A national vocabulary for “it’s not just the swallow.” What they discarded, or should have: the idea that an acronym is a personality. What they still argue about: SOS versus permission language versus a reinforcement chair, and whether any of those belongs in a medically fragile body without a team.

PFD’s psychosocial and feeding-skill domains are downstream weather. Toomey is one reason a parent in 2015 could walk into a clinic already knowing a name.

## Close

Psychology naming a method that therapists then staff is an old rehabilitation pattern. Toomey’s acronym is a clean example. Clean examples get treated as villains or saviors. She is neither in this file. She is the person who made a sensory sequence a thing you could register for.

Parents who arrive already saying SOS are part of the heritage. So are parents who were burned by a group that used the letters and not the caution. Letters travel. Caution stays in the room that charged for it. That unfairness is not unique to this method. It is the course economy.

An acronym that parents can request is a kind of democracy. It is also a kind of customer. This pack will not sneer at either. It will not, either, print the sequence that made the acronym famous.
