This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Barium downstairs needed more than courage. It needed people who would treat a pediatric swallow study as a methods problem — radiation, reliability, what a splash of contrast means in a body that is still growing. Memorie M. Gosa’s University of Alabama line, and the reviews she shares with colleagues including Pamela Dodrill, are part of that civic work.

She was living when this pack was staged. Speech-language pathologist, academic address in the public record. Published papers only. No suite instructions.

## The work

Gosa helped make it respectable to say that pediatric instrumental assessment is not a small adult study. Respectability is how a physicist and a parent get into the same conversation. She also sat on the thickening question: if you are going to inherit nectar and honey, inherit them with a bibliography.

Lefton-Greif’s Hopkins pediatric dysphagia work is an older academic cousin. Gosa is later weather from a different building. This pack does not merge them.

## What later people kept

A habit of citing pediatric data when the temptation is to cite Logemann and walk downstairs. What they still argue about: when a picture is worth the fear it creates.

This pack will not tell a reader which test to demand. Gosa is here as a reason a demand should be able to explain itself.

## Close

Alabama is not Hopkins and not Milwaukee. Gosa’s academic address is part of why this pack refuses a coastal monopoly on pediatric swallow science. Students who think all the papers come from the same three ZIP codes have not looked at the author line.

A VFSS that cannot explain its pediatric modifications is an adult study in a child’s body. Her work is a demand for the explanation. This page will not list the modifications. Listing them would be a protocol. The demand is the heritage.

Pediatric VFSS culture needed a voice that was not only Hopkins. Alabama’s author line is that voice in this roster. Geography is not a credential. It is a refusal to let three buildings own the picture.

Students who only read adult MBS papers and then walk downstairs are repeating 1983 without the pediatric argument. Gosa is one of the people who wrote the argument down, in journals a student can actually assign without a coastal fellowship or a borrowed login.
