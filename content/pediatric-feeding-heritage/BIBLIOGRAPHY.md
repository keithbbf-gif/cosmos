# Bibliography — Pediatric Feeding & Swallowing Heritage

Sources used or checked for this pack. Not a reading list for caregivers. Prefer the primary object named in the article.

## Primary and near-primary

- Arvedson, Joan C., and Linda Brodsky, eds. *Pediatric Swallowing and Feeding: Assessment and Management*. San Diego: Singular, 1993. Later editions: Singular/Thomson, then Plural; 3rd ed. with Maureen A. Lefton-Greif, Plural, 2020.
- Bosma, James F. *Anatomy of the Infant Head*. Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1986.
- Bosma, James F., J. Lind, and H. M. Truby. Work collected around *Newborn Infant Cry* / *Acta Paediatrica* supplement, 1965.
- Budin, Pierre. *Le Nourrisson*. Paris, 1900. English: *The Nursling: The Feeding and Hygiene of Premature and Full-Term Infants*. Trans. William J. Maloney. London: Caxton, 1907.
- Davis, Clara M. “Self Selection of Diet by Newly Weaned Infants.” *American Journal of Diseases of Children* 36, no. 4 (1928): 651–679.
- Davis, Clara M. “Results of the Self-Selection of Diets by Young Children.” *Canadian Medical Association Journal* 41, no. 3 (1939): 257–261.
- Dodrill, Pamela, and Memorie M. Gosa. Reviews on pediatric thickening and feeding skill — cite the specific *Dysphagia* / *Current Opinion* paper named in the article, not a blog summary.
- Gesell, Arnold, and colleagues. Yale developmental schedules and *The First Five Years of Life* (1940) for feeding items inside a larger chart.
- Goday, Praveen S., et al. “Pediatric Feeding Disorder: Consensus Definition and Conceptual Framework.” *Journal of Pediatric Gastroenterology and Nutrition* 68, no. 1 (2019): 124–129.
- Holt, L. Emmett. *The Care and Feeding of Children: A Catechism for the Use of Mothers and Children’s Nurses*. New York: D. Appleton, 1894.
- Lefton-Greif, Maureen A. Pediatric dysphagia reviews (including *Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation Clinics of North America*, 2008) — used as a Hopkins address, not copied from the sibling pack.
- Logemann, Jeri A. *Evaluation and Treatment of Swallowing Disorders*. San Diego: College-Hill, 1983. Sibling pack owns the adult door; this pack names the book only as the object that later walked downstairs.
- Morris, Suzanne Evans, and Marsha Dunn Klein. *Pre-Feeding Skills*. Tucson/Austin: Therapy Skill Builders / Pro-Ed, 1987; 2nd ed. 2000.
- Palmer, Marjorie Meyer, et al. Neonatal Oral-Motor Assessment Scale papers from the early 1980s (*Journal of Perinatology* line). Instrument as history; no scoring sheet.
- Rotch, Thomas Morgan. Percentage-method teaching and Boston Infants’ Hospital milk-laboratory practice, 1890s–1910s. See Boston Children’s Hospital archives note on the Hunnewell cows and the milk lab.
- Satter, Ellyn. *Child of Mine: Feeding with Love and Good Sense* and later Division of Responsibility statements. Relationship literature, not an SLP protocol.
- Wolf, Lynn S., and Robin P. Glass. *Feeding and Swallowing Disorders in Infancy: Assessment and Management*. Tucson: Therapy Skill Builders, 1992.

## Institutional and necrology

- Boston Children’s Hospital Archives. “M – Milk” exhibit note on Rotch’s laboratory and the Hunnewell herd.
- Vineyard Gazette, 19 February 2014, and AMWA brief: Linda Brodsky (25 May 1952 – 13 February 2014).
- Baltimore *Sun*, 28 June 2001: James F. Bosma death notice (died Friday, 22 June 2001).
- Standard Holt, Rotch, Budin, and Gesell encyclopedic necrologies for day dates. Day dates not forced when only a year is solid (Davis, Bosma birth).

## Secondary histories used as orientation

- Apple, Rima D. *Mothers and Medicine: A Social History of Infant Feeding, 1890–1950*. Madison: University of Wisconsin Press, 1987.
- Weaver, Lawrence T. “In the Balance: Weighing Babies and the Birth of the Infant Welfare Clinic.” *Bulletin of the History of Medicine* (and PMC reprint).
- Weaver, Lawrence T. “‘Humanizing’ Milk: The Formulation of Artificial Feeds for Infants (1850–1910).” *Medical History*.
- Stevens, Emily E., Thelma E. Patrick, and Rita Pickler. “A History of Infant Feeding.” *Journal of Perinatal Education* 18, no. 2 (2009): 32–39 — orientation only; do not lean on it for a birth year.
- Hektoen International note and CMAJ 2006 commentary on Clara Davis (n of 3 vs later 15; data boxes lost after 1959).
- Dunitz-Scheer, Marguerite, and Peter Scheer. Graz tube-dependency literature and the later Springer volume *Child-led Tube-management and Tube-weaning* (bio note: born 1955, Washington, D.C.).

## What this file is not

A complete pediatric feeding library. ASHA Practice Portal pages were consulted for contemporary vocabulary (PFD vs pediatric dysphagia) and were **not** copied. Commercial course sites (Feed the Peds, SOS, SOFFI) were used only to confirm that a named method exists as a public brand, never as a source of steps.
