Magazine series for a later import to **SLPWOW.com**. This folder is the pack. It is not a live-site edit, not COSMOS core, and not a merge with the adult dysphagia heritage pack or the general speech-pathology history pack.

## Portrait policy

Never generate or embed synthetic historical faces. This pack records **public-domain and open-license portrait notes** only. Do not download likenesses into this folder until a later rights-cleared graphics pass. Until then, profiles carry a caption-ready credit line and, when no PD file is known, the placeholder block in `STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

## Sibling packs

- `content/dysphagia-swallowing-heritage/` — swallow science, adult instruments, Logemann’s door, one pediatric-team hallway. Bosma, Arvedson, Lefton-Greif, Langmore, and Cichero already sit there. This pack is the **pediatric feeding** deep series: wet nurses, percentage milk, NICU clocks, parent chairs, PFD’s four domains. New scene, new residue. Do not copy those sentences.
- `content/slpwow-speech-pathology-history/` — the profession (ASHA, Iowa as a school, the textbook century). Do not retell McAlpin or the four ASHA names as if they invented the meal.
- `content/slp-pediatric-milestones-blog/` — caregiver milestone copy. One essay there notes that eating and talking share a mouth. This pack does not reprint those pages.

## What this pack will not do

- No merge into furniture, figroots, COSMOS modules, or clinic booking pages.
- No clinical protocols as advice. No “try this if your child…” No bottle-flow charts, no thickening recipes, no pacing scripts, no oral-motor homework.
- No live `slpwow.com` or WOW Therapies CMS writes from this folder.
- No generated historical faces.
