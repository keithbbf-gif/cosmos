# Claims Guardrails — Pediatric Feeding & Swallowing Heritage

Staged. Educational history only.

## Hard stops

- No treatment protocols, home programs, or “try this if your child…”
- No bottle-flow charts, nipple recommendations, or pacing scripts a caregiver could follow.
- No thickening recipes, brand thickeners as advice, rice-cereal instructions, or IDDSI kitchen conversions.
- No oral-motor exercise lists, Beckman or TalkTools how-tos, or “do this daily.”
- No SOS, SOFFI, Get Permission, NOMAS, or Graz steps as homework.
- No tube-wean schedules, volume targets, or “when to stop the tube.”
- No radiation, FEES, or scope technique.
- No living patients’ names, case details, or anything that reads as PHI.
- No generated faces or invented bedside dialogue.

If a paragraph can be copied into a clinic handout without rewriting, cut it.

## What a reader may take away

- Who medicalized the infant meal, when, and with what milk, scale, or film.
- Which books, teams, and consensus papers still sit on training shelves.
- Where pediatrics, nursing, occupational therapy, speech-language pathology, dietetics, GI, and parents shared a high chair — and where they refused to.
- How cultural first foods and school trays sat outside the hospital story.

## What a reader may not take away

- A home program.
- A diagnosis.
- A reason to delay a pediatrician, ENT, GI, or SLP visit.
- A founder fantasy (“Arvedson invented pediatric feeding”; “Holt invented formula”).

## Living people in this pack

Arvedson, Morris, Klein, Wolf, Glass, Satter, Chatoor, Gisel, Sheppard, Palmer, Alexander, Shaker, Ross, Toomey, Dodrill, Gosa, Goday, Dunitz-Scheer, Swigert, Reilly, and Jadcherla were living or recently active when the pack was staged (September 2026), except where a profile states otherwise. Write from **published work and institutional pages**. No family detail that is not in a citable professional bio. No home address.

## Sibling packs

Do not retell Magendie, Cannon’s bismuth, Killian’s bronchoscope, or Logemann’s 1983 book as if they were pediatric-feeding origin stories. Point to `content/dysphagia-swallowing-heritage/` and stay on the child meal.

Do not retell Hotel McAlpin, Iowa’s stuttering school, or ASHA’s four names as feeding origin stories. Point to `content/slpwow-speech-pathology-history/`.

Bosma, Arvedson, and Lefton-Greif appear in the dysphagia pack. This pack may profile Bosma and Arvedson only with a **new scene and new residue**. Do not copy those sentences. Lefton-Greif is treated in essays here rather than as a forty-fifth profile.

## Uncertainty is a claim

Print the disagreement. Examples already owed to the copy:

- Clara Davis’s cafeteria: 1928 paper describes three infants; later tellings say fifteen over years. Both numbers are in print. Do not collapse them.
- Holt death date is 14 January 1924 in standard necrologies; do not invent a bedside hour.
- “Cue-based feeding” is a late nickname for a family of NICU practices. Do not give it a single inventor year.
