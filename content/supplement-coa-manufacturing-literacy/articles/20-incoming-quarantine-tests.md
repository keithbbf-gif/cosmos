A drum arrives. Someone with a pallet jack puts it down. The next sentence is the whole incoming system: does it sit in a status that cannot be issued to a batch until quality control says so, or does it sit where a blender operator can reach it because the line is waiting?

Part 111 wants the first sentence. Many white-label plants perform the second and keep a "quarantine" sign for tours.

## What the rule is asking

You establish component specifications (111.70). You collect representative samples of each unique lot and each unique shipment (111.80(a)). You verify identity of each dietary ingredient (111.75(a)(1)). You determine whether the other specs are met, by testing or by a qualified supplier COA (piece 02, piece 28). Quality control releases or rejects.

111.155 and 111.160 (receiving and the associated records) are the paperwork twin of that physical fact. If the record says "quarantine" and the pallet is already on the line, the record is a story.

InnoMark (657518, 1 September 2023) is the public version of "we filed the supplier COA." Contaminant rows the receiving firm had not confirmed. Identity and reliability were not a cage problem only — but a cage that does not lock makes that letter easier to earn.

## What a cage that locks looks like

- A defined location: tape on the floor is a start; a fenced area with a lock is better; a WMS hold that the issue screen cannot override is the adult version.
- Status labels on the drum: quarantine / released / rejected. The same status in the system.
- Issue to manufacturing only from released stock. Not from "we always use this vendor."
- Rejected material in a third place, so it cannot be "accidentally" issued on a night shift (piece 23).

If you walk a plant, ask them to issue a drum in front of you. Watch whether the system stops a quarantine lot. If a supervisor can override with a smile, the smile is the quality unit.

## Sampling at the door

Do not test the bag the driver already opened for customs theater and call it representative. Take increments. Close the bag. Label the sample with the component lot and the shipment number. 111.80(a) cares about unique shipments as well as unique lots. The same lot on two trucks is two receiving events.

Identity first for dietary ingredients (piece 07). Then the rows you do not rely on the supplier for. Then the confirmation schedule for the rows you do.

A visual "it looks like ashwagandha" can be part of an examination. It is not HPLC identity and it is not HPTLC.

## Packaging and labels are incoming too

111.80(a) includes packaging and labels. The wrong label in a released cage is how you misbrand a good lot. Quarantine labels. Compare to the approved artwork. Count. A private-label plant with twelve sleep SKUs will mix cartons. That mix is a receiving problem before it is a customer problem.

## What you ask a CMO

1. Show me quarantine.
2. Show me a lot in quarantine right now.
3. Show me the identity record that will get it out.
4. Show me a rejected drum from the last year and where it went.

If they have never rejected a drum, they are lucky or they are not looking. Either way, write it down.

## Damaged bags are a receiving event

A torn 25 kg bag of botanical, tape over the tear, "it's fine." That bag is not representative of the lot in the way you hoped, and it is a contamination path. Photograph, quarantine separately, decide. Do not issue the torn bag into a vegan SKU and the intact bags into something else without a record.

Receiving is allowed to reject a shipment. Use that permission.

## Night-shift issue is the test

The cage can look perfect at 10 a.m. on a tour. The test is 7 p.m., when a blender operator is short a drum and the lead is gone. Does the WMS stop a quarantine lot? Does a supervisor override have a second signature and a reason code? Does the override show up on the next morning's quality desk?

Ask to see the last override. If there has never been one in a plant that runs two shifts, they are lucky or the override is verbal. Verbal is how a quarantined botanical with a pending HPTLC becomes tonight's blend.

Write the rule: no issue from quarantine, no verbal override, every override is a deviation (piece 27). Then watch one evening if you can. The evening is the SOP.

## What this is not

Quarantine is not a consumer claim. Do not advertise "quarantine tested" as if it were a disease shield.

It is a status. Status has to be physical.

A polite sign on an open pallet is interior decorating.
