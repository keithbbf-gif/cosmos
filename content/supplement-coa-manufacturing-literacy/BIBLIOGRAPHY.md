# Bibliography — COA / GMP / white-label literacy pack

Consolidated citations. Article front matter repeats the ones that piece actually uses. Dates are the versions these drafts were written against. Recheck FDA.gov, eCFR, USP, and OEHHA before a public import.

Not legal advice. Not a complete library.

## Statute and regulation

- Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act of 1994, Pub. L. 103-417.
- 21 U.S.C. § 321(ff) (dietary supplement); § 321(g)(1)(B) (drug / intended use); § 342(g)(1) (adulteration — cGMP); § 343 (misbranding); § 350b (new dietary ingredients).
- 21 CFR Part 111 — Current Good Manufacturing Practice in Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling, or Holding Operations for Dietary Supplements. Final rule: 72 FR 34752 (25 June 2007).
- 21 CFR 111.70 (specifications); 111.73 (specifications must be met); 111.75 (how you determine specifications are met, including identity and supplier COA); 111.77 (rejected products / reprocessing); 111.80 (representative samples); 111.83 and 111.465 (reserve samples); 111.87 (quality control disposition); 111.90 (treatments and reprocessing); 111.95 (records under Subpart E); 111.123 (quality control operations); 111.205–111.210 (master manufacturing record); 111.255–111.260 (batch production record); 111.553 (product complaints).
- 21 CFR 101.36 (supplement facts); 101.93 (structure/function claims, notification, disclaimer).
- 21 CFR Part 117 — Current Good Manufacturing Practice, Hazard Analysis, and Risk-Based Preventive Controls for Human Food (FSMA).
- FDA, *Small Entity Compliance Guide: Current Good Manufacturing Practice in Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling, or Holding Operations for Dietary Supplements* — https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/small-entity-compliance-guide-current-good-manufacturing-practice-manufacturing-packaging-labeling
- eCFR Title 21 Part 111 — https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-111

- InnoMark, Inc., MARCS-CMS 657518 (1 September 2023) — supplier COAs without established reliability; contaminant testing gaps. https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/innomark-inc-657518-09012023
- Formulation Technology, Inc., MARCS-CMS 672905 (9 July 2024) — specifications; "input" as a strength method. https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/formulation-technology-inc-672905-07092024
- Western Innovations, Inc., MARCS-CMS 679737 (13 November 2024) — identity specification; supplier CoA methods lacking specificity. https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/western-innovations-inc-679737-11132024
- LXR Biotech, LLC, MARCS-CMS 714568 (23 December 2025) — identity test against a supplier "standard" with the same lot as the sample. https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/lxr-biotech-llc-714568-12232025

## USP / ISO / marks

- USP \<2232\> Elemental Contaminants in Dietary Supplements; USP \<232\>/\<233\> (drug elemental impurities — adjacent, not a supplement consumer claim).
- USP \<467\> Residual Solvents; ICH Q3C.
- USP \<561\> Articles of Botanical Origin (pesticide limits among other botanical tests).
- USP \<2021\> Microbial Enumeration Tests — Nutritional and Dietary Supplements; \<2022\> Microbiological Procedures; \<2023\> Microbiological Attributes of Nonsterile Nutritional and Dietary Supplements.
- USP Dietary Supplements Verification Program — https://www.usp.org/verification-services/dietary-supplements-verification-program
- ISO/IEC 17025 (testing and calibration laboratories). Read the **scope**, not the logo.
- NSF Certified for Sport — https://www.nsfsport.com/
- Informed-Sport (LGC) — https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/

## Prop 65 / press tests

- OEHHA, Lead — reproductive MADL 0.5 µg/day; oral cancer NSRL 15 µg/day. https://oehha.ca.gov/proposition-65/chemicals/lead
- OEHHA safe-harbor list (Title 27 CCR §§ 25705, 25805). Recheck the current PDF.
- Consumer Reports, protein powders and shakes lead investigation, 14 October 2025 (updated 8 January 2026). CR's 0.5 mcg/day "level of concern" is CR's benchmark, not an FDA protein-powder tolerance.

## FTC / claims

- FTC, *Health Products Compliance Guidance* (December 2022).
- FDA, structure/function claim notification (21 CFR 101.93).
- FDA, 26 January 2023 CBD conclusion (food/supplement frameworks not appropriate for CBD). Recheck the dated page before any hemp SKU copy.
