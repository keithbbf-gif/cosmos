# Photo notes — trauma-informed care history pack

No generated faces. No "period photographs" that never existed. No stock "sad child in silhouette" as a stand-in for a survivor.

## Default

Every article ships with `portrait: none`. The public page uses a series template (type, a document, a building) until `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` lists a cleared file.

## Allowed later

- Public-domain or Creative Commons portraits of deceased figures, documented with URL, license, and credit.
- Title pages, federal pamphlets (SMA 14-4884), DSM-III covers if license-clear, hospital exteriors that are not PHI.
- Schematic timelines drawn in-house. Caption them as schematic.

## Never

- AI faces of Janet, Rivers, Herman, or anyone else.
- Photographs of patients, wards with identifiable people, or children.
- Combat gore, rape-scene reconstructions, or "trigger" imagery used as decoration.
- Portraits of living people without a license the practice can keep.

## WOW clinic photos

Do not drop a WOW Therapies waiting-room photo onto a PTSD history post. That pairing is a claims problem: it looks like a service page.
