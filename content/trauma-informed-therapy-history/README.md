# WOW Therapies — history of trauma-informed care

Staged educational pack for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com). **Do not write these files onto the live site from this folder.**

Forty-five staged essays (twenty-four era / institution pieces, twenty-one figures) plus editorial ops. Complementary to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history` — especially that pack’s trauma-lineages era essay and the Judith Herman figure. This lane goes deeper into **trauma-informed care as a systems idea**, the civilian and military names that came before it, and the people who argued over those names.

WOW Therapies is a **speech-language practice** in Southeast Arkansas. These posts are heritage education. They do not advertise a trauma-therapy clinic, a PTSD specialty, or OT/PT staffing that the live site does not actually offer.
