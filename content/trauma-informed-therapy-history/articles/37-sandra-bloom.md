**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Sandra L. Bloom is living as of last verification (2026-09-14). This essay uses the 1997 book and the public Sanctuary career. No health talk.

She is in this pack because she made the *ward* a trauma object and refused to leave the staff out. *Creating Sanctuary* (1997) is the book a later training industry cites. The era essay stays with the hospital argument. This figure stays with the psychiatrist who wrote it and then spent decades teaching organizations that an injured milieu will reproduce injury.

## Philadelphia weather, not a cloud

The 1980s–1990s inpatient and residential settings around Philadelphia gave her the loop: people admitted because they had been hurt were being hurt again by restraint, humiliation, and a staff group that had gone numb or frightened. Mid-century milieu therapy (Maxwell Jones and others) is the older English-language cousin. Bloom’s difference, for this pack, is the trauma center of gravity and the organizational-trauma metaphor.

The metaphor has a limit. A corporation does not have a hippocampus. A funding cut is not a rape. Keep the metaphor as management language. Figley’s secondary-stress file is the helper-side parallel from veterans and disaster. Bloom’s file is the night shift with keys.

Later Sanctuary trainings packaged commitments and a SELF framework. This page will not reprint the package. Certification is a later industry. Industries drift. A staged history can honor 1997 without endorsing every licensee.

## Afterlife

SAMHSA’s 2014 domains — environment, workforce, governance — read, in one mood, like a federal translation of insights the ward paid for. Harris and Fallot translated a community desk. Bloom translated a locked unit. Both translations leaked into schools. Leakage thins. The figure’s job is to keep the 1997 book and the hospital origin visible when a principal says “sanctuary” and means a poster.

She has also written about peace, about the ISTSS’s institutional memory (a 2000 handbook chapter is in the bibliography), about the long American habit of violence. Those writings are public. They are not automatically SMA 14-4884. Do not file every Bloom title under a six-principle card.

## Limits

This page will not walk a community meeting. It will not give you SELF questions as homework. If you are in danger, call local emergency services. If you work on a unit that is going mean, you need a real supervisor, a board, or an exit.

WOW Therapies does not run a milieu. The transferable caution is ordinary: frightened staff frighten people. Ordinary caution does not require a model name on a service page.
