**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Vincent J. Felitti was born in 1938 and is living as of last verification (2026-09-14). Robert F. Anda, then at the CDC, is the epidemiologist half of the collaboration. This essay uses the 1998 paper and the public origin story Felitti has told in talks: patients leaving an obesity clinic, a history of childhood sexual abuse that kept arriving, a decision to count. No health talk about either man. No private life.

The era essay stays with what the study measured — a graded association in a Kaiser San Diego sample — and what it did not. This figure stays with the two-shop structure: a Kaiser internist with a clinical irritation, a CDC scientist with a survey habit, a questionnaire that joined childhood categories to adult medical records.

## Two addresses, one curve

Felitti’s Kaiser address is why the sample looks the way it does: insured adults, willing to mail a form, San Diego enough. Anda’s CDC address is why the paper could speak the language of leading causes of death. Neither address is a school district. Neither address is a speech clinic. The later sticker industry forgot the addresses.

Felitti’s later talks sometimes sound more missionary than the 1998 tables. Living people get to give talks. This pack uses the paper when the talk and the paper diverge. Anda’s later commentaries on the study’s use and misuse are part of the public file; `[VERIFY]` a specific later Anda caution before quoting it as gospel on a live page.

The ten categories — abuse, neglect, household dysfunction — are a public-health convenience. They are a clinical crudity. This figure essay will not print them as a self-test. The era essay already refused that. The refusal stands.

## Afterlife, and the men inside it

By the 2010s cities were “ACE-aware.” Felitti’s name became a keynote. Anda’s name became a methods slide. Fame of that kind is not a crime. It is a reason to keep the 1998 object in view. SAMHSA 2014 needed a citation that health departments already knew. Harris and Fallot (2001) did not need ACE to redesign a desk. The merger of ACE numbers and trauma-informed posters made grant language easy. Easy language thins.

Critics said, fairly, that ACE talk can stare at families and skip present wages, racism, and housing. The 1998 sample, ironically, was not a poor sample. The afterlife often is. A figure essay can put that drift next to the men without pretending they wrote the drift into the tables.

## Limits

This page will not score you. It will not ask your children ten items. If you are in danger, call local emergency services. If you have diabetes or depression, treat those conditions with a clinician — not with a keynote.

WOW Therapies will not put an ACE form in a waiting room on the strength of this URL. A speech-language practice that quizzes childhood adversity as if it explained a lisp is making a claims mistake and a manners mistake.
