**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

A pelvic exam after a rape can be care or a second assault. Burgess and Holmstrom were already living that sentence in a 1970s Boston emergency ward. Forty years later, SAMHSA’s 2014 pamphlet used restraint and seclusion as its plain examples of re-traumatization. Between those dates, hospitals, dental offices, and rehabilitation gyms began to borrow a language that had been written for community mental health. The borrowing is the history this essay tells. The borrowing is not a license to treat PTSD in a swallow suite.

## Why rehab rooms heard the phrase

Rehabilitation is intimate. Hands on a jaw. A hand down a throat. A stranger asking you to fail in public at a walk you used to own. For a person whose body has been treated as an object — by a partner, a parent, a soldier, a surgeon — the gym and the speech room are not neutral. Harris and Fallot’s 2001 values (safety, trustworthiness, choice, collaboration, empowerment) sound almost ordinary here: say what you will do before you do it; give a way to stop; do not surprise from behind; do not make a person tell the rape in order to get a hearing test.

Ordinary is not the same as a trauma-specific method. An SLP who explains a laryngeal exam is doing clinical manners with a history. An SLP who offers EMDR is off the license and off this website.

Professional associations in nursing, dentistry, physical therapy, and speech-language pathology issued, across the 2010s and early 2020s, position statements and continuing-education products with “trauma-informed” in the title. This draft will not pretend to have inventoried every ASHA webinar. `[VERIFY]` any named ASHA practice portal page before a public footnote. The historical claim does not need the webinar. The claim is that by the late 2010s the phrase had reached rehab the way it had reached schools: sometimes as redesign, often as a slide.

## What a medical building can actually change

SMA 14-4884’s ten domains are more honest than a laminated card. Governance, policy, physical environment, workforce, financing. A hospital that trains nurses for an hour and keeps the same restraint numbers has bought a principle. A clinic that changes how gowns, doors, interpreters, and chaperones work has bought a domain.

Speech-language rooms have a short list of historically obvious pressure points: the face and neck, feeding, a child’s body held for an oral-mech exam, an adult who cannot swallow his own spit after a stroke and is already ashamed. Trauma-informed *history* can name those pressure points as the reason the phrase arrived. It cannot, on this URL, turn them into a protocol. Service pages, if a human editor and counsel want them, describe how *this* clinic explains exams. History pages do not.

## Inheritance, not conquest

Medical TIC is an inheritance from rape-crisis nursing, from inpatient psychiatry’s Sanctuary arguments, from SAMHSA’s domains, and from disability communities that had been asking for consent and explanation before the phrase existed. Disability rights is an older systems literature. Trauma-informed talk that pretends to have invented “tell me before you touch me” is stealing from people who used different words.

It is also an inheritance with a risk. A stroke patient is not a rape patient. A late talker is not a foster child. Using one noun for every flinch makes the noun cheap and the exam worse. The opening essay of this pack exists to keep the rooms from collapsing. This essay is the test of that rule on the clinic’s own furniture.

## The swallow chair is already an intimate room

A jaw held still, a gown that will not close, an interpreter who was not booked — those are SMA 14-4884 domains (environment, engagement) without a federal site visit. Rehabilitation asks a person to fail in public at a walk or a swallow. Trauma-informed talk in that room is mostly about surprise, clothing, and who is allowed to watch. It is not a PE homework packet and not an ACE quiz on the clipboard.
