**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Lenore Terr was born in 1936 and is living as of last verification (2026-09-14). This essay uses the public papers and the 1990 book. No health talk. No children’s names beyond the public place-name.

On 15 July 1976, twenty-six children on a school bus were kidnapped in Chowchilla, California, and held in a buried trailer. They escaped. Terr, a child psychiatrist, studied them. “Children of Chowchilla: A Study of Psychic Trauma” appeared in *The Psychoanalytic Study of the Child* in 1979. *Too Scared to Cry: Psychic Trauma in Childhood* (1990) gave a wider public the argument. A 1991 *American Journal of Psychiatry* paper distinguished what she called Type I and Type II childhood traumas — a single blow versus repeated, long-standing horror. The distinction is a model. Models leak. The dated file is the artifact.

## A child psychiatry that did not wait for DSM-5

DTD was refused in 2013. Terr had already, in 1979 and 1991, given American child psychiatry a way to talk about children after a public crime and children after a private, repeated one. Herman’s adult complex PTSD is a cousin, not a parent. Do not make Terr a footnote to van der Kolk. Do not make van der Kolk a footnote to Terr. Different decades, different rooms.

Chowchilla was unusually public. Most childhood sexual violence is not. Terr’s later clinical writing tried to carry what she learned from a famous case into ordinary offices. That carry is hard. Famous cases distort. They also make journals listen. A systems pack can say both.

Play, drawing, and the way children reenact are in her pages. This series will not turn those pages into a play-therapy how-to. Child-therapy methods have their own licenses and their own harms when looted. The historical claim is smaller: by 1979 a child psychiatrist had a follow-up file on a group of kidnapped children, and American trauma talk could no longer pretend childhood was only a prelude to an adult syndrome.

## Type I / Type II — use and misuse

The 1991 outline is catnip for slides. Single event versus chronic. Simple versus messy. The binary helped people see that foster care and incest are not Chowchilla. The binary also flattered people who wanted a clean box. Real children sit in the smear between boxes. A staged history can keep the 1991 paper as a dated attempt, not as a law.

NCTSN, after 2000, made a pipe for this literature. Terr is not NCTSN. She is one of the people the pipe later carried.

## Limits

This page will not describe the kidnapping in detail. It will not diagnose a child. It will not offer a drawing prompt. If you are in danger, call local emergency services. If a child is in danger, use the reporting path your state requires.

WOW Therapies evaluates speech and language. A child who has been through a public or private horror may also have a speech need. The need is still speech. Do not file it under Chowchilla.
