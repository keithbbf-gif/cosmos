**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Charles R. Figley was born in 1944, served in Vietnam, and is living as of last verification (2026-09-14). This essay uses public books, the public society history, and his own past-president column. No health talk.

On 2 March 1985, in Washington, D.C., a breakfast meeting he organized established the Society for Traumatic Stress Studies. The first conference was commissioned for Atlanta that September. A foundational reason for the society, in Figley’s later telling, was to have a journal. *Journal of Traumatic Stress* was created in 1986 and first issued in January 1988. In 1990 the society added “International” to its name. Those dates are the artifact. A field is not only papers. It is a room that decides which papers count.

## From a veterans’ literature to a shop

Figley’s edited *Trauma and Its Wake* (1985) and earlier volumes on Vietnam families sat in the weather DSM-III had just made official. He is not the inventor of PTSD. He is one of the people who built the *profession* that PTSD made fundable: meetings, a journal, a later interest in compassion fatigue and secondary traumatic stress — the injury of the helper.

Secondary traumatic stress is why he belongs in a systems pack, not only in a veterans’ pack. Bloom’s Sanctuary Model asked about injured staff on a ward. Figley asked about therapists, disaster workers, and families who live next to the story. SAMHSA’s 2014 pamphlet, when it tells organizations to recognize signs in *staff*, is downstream of that ask. Downstream is not theft. It is how federal sentences get written.

This page will not teach you to diagnose compassion fatigue. The phrase leaked into every helping profession and then into every tired office. Leakage is the usual fate. Prefer the society dates and the 1985 volume to a mug.

## What a founder does not own

ISTSS became a gate. Herman’s complex trauma sat at that gate. Method wars (EMDR versus exposure versus everything else) sat at that gate. Figley does not own the gate’s later manners. Founders rarely do. A staged history can honor the 1985 breakfast without blessing every later keynote.

He has also been a public advocate, a journal editor, a director of institutes. Public roles accumulate. This essay will not inventory them. The 1985 object is enough for a figure slot.

## Limits

This page will not enroll you in ISTSS. It will not sell a compassion-fatigue workshop. If you are in danger, call local emergency services. If you are a helper who is going under, use a real supervisor and a real clinician — not a 1985 table of contents.

WOW Therapies staff are not, by this URL, a secondary-trauma clinic. A small speech practice can still notice that difficult stories exist. Noticing is not a specialty.
