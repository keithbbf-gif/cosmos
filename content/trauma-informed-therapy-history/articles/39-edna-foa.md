**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Edna B. Foa was born in 1937 and is living as of last verification (2026-09-14). This essay uses the 1991 trial and the 1998 book. No health talk. No private life.

In 1991 the *Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology* printed Foa, Barbara Olasov Rothbaum, David S. Riggs, and Tamera B. Murdock, a comparison of cognitive-behavioral procedures and counseling in rape survivors with PTSD. *Treating the Trauma of Rape: Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy for PTSD* (Guilford, 1998) gave the later clinic a book. Prolonged exposure, as a named package, is the afterlife of that shop. This series will not walk you through imaginal or in-vivo tasks. It will say that by the 1990s American trauma treatment had a randomized civilian object that was not a war and not an eye-movement paper.

## A trial as a historical event

Burgess and Holmstrom had named a civilian syndrome in 1974. DSM-III had given a code in 1980. Foa’s group gave a *comparison* in 1991. Comparisons are how a field stops living only on case series. They are also how a field becomes a guideline shop. Later VA/DoD and ISTSS documents would place prolonged exposure among recommended trauma-focused therapies in various editions. Recommendations move. `[VERIFY]` current wording if an editor wants a live footnote. The staged claim is the 1991 object and the 1998 book.

Rothbaum’s name belongs on the 1991 and 1998 citations. This slug is Foa because the series needed a figure slot and the later public career is often filed under her. Do not disappear the collaborators.

Critics have said exposure can be hard to complete, hard to offer well, and easy to market past the file. Those criticisms are part of the history. They are not a reason for this page to teach a “gentler” substitute. We print the arrival. We refuse the how-to.

## Not a building

Foa’s work is trauma-specific treatment. Harris and Fallot’s work is a building. A clinic can offer PE and still humiliate people at the desk. A building can be trying not to add a second injury and still not offer PE. WOW Therapies offers neither on this URL. A speech-language practice that named prolonged exposure as a service would be making a category error and a license error.
