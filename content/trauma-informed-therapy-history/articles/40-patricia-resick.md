**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Patricia A. Resick is living as of last verification (2026-09-14). This essay uses the 1993 manual written with Monica K. Schnicke and the public later career. No health talk. No private life.

*Cognitive Processing Therapy for Rape Victims: A Treatment Manual* (Sage, 1993) is the object. CPT, as a named package later used with veterans and others, is the afterlife. This series will not walk you through stuck points, impact statements, or worksheets. It will say that by 1993 American trauma treatment had a civilian, rape-centered cognitive manual that was not Foa’s exposure book and not Shapiro’s 1989 papers.

## A manual as a historical event

Resick’s shop took seriously what people *tell themselves* after a rape — about blame, safety, power, esteem, intimacy — and tried to make those tellings researchable. That is a 1990s cognitive-clinical move. It has a file of trials that later guideline writers used. It has critics who think the hour can become a schoolroom. Both are history. Neither is a reason for this page to assign writing.

Schnicke’s name belongs on the 1993 citation. Later VA adaptations and group formats belong to a long collaborator list this essay will not inventory. `[VERIFY]` a specific later veteran trial if an editor wants a second dated object on a live page. The staged figure can live on 1993.

Foa 1991 is the neighbor trial. Do not make Resick Foa’s deputy. Do not make Foa Resick’s deputy. Two shops, two manuals, one post-DSM-III decade that wanted something it could randomize.

## Not a building, not a speech clinic

CPT is trauma-specific treatment. Trauma-informed care is a building. WOW Therapies is a speech-language practice. Naming CPT on a history URL is not a service. A parent who came here from a school poster should leave knowing the difference, not holding a worksheet.

The memory-wars decade overlaps this manual’s first life. CPT is not recovered-memory therapy. Collapsing 1990s methods into one cartoon is how histories fail. Keep the rooms distinct: a cognitive manual for rape-related PTSD is one room; a hypnotic hunt for a hidden plot is another.
