# Editorial calendar — WOW Therapies, history of trauma-informed care

Forty-five **staged** essays: twenty-four era / institution pieces and twenty-one figures. Status in YAML is `staging` (writer-cleared for **staging** review). Nothing in this folder is cleared to write itself onto the live wowtherapies.com sitemap.

All files live under `content/trauma-informed-therapy-history/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`.

This pack is the trauma-informed *systems and naming* lane. It is complementary to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history` (especially slugs `trauma-lineages` and `judith-herman`). Do not merge the two calendars. Do not merge this calendar with SLPWOW speech-pathology history.

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-five essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.

Wave the import (staging only — `WP_IMPORT.md`):
