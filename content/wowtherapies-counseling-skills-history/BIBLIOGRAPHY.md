# Bibliography — WOW Therapies, counseling skills & modalities series

Works cited across the pack. Article YAML `citations` lists the subset used in that piece. Prefer the edition named here. Wikipedia is a lead, not a source.

Last pass: 2026-09-14.

## Skills named, taught, measured

- Rogers, Carl R. *Client-Centered Therapy*. Boston: Houghton Mifflin, 1951.
- Rogers, Carl R. "The Necessary and Sufficient Conditions of Therapeutic Personality Change." *Journal of Consulting Psychology* 21, no. 2 (1957): 95–103.
- Rogers, Carl R. *On Becoming a Person*. Boston: Houghton Mifflin, 1961.
- Kirschenbaum, Howard. *The Life and Work of Carl Rogers*. Ross-on-Wye: PCCS Books, 2007.
- Truax, Charles B. Accurate Empathy Scale (developed 1961; circulated with NIMH / VRA support). Printed with later revisions in Truax and Carkhuff 1967.
- Truax, Charles B., and Robert R. Carkhuff. *Toward Effective Counseling and Psychotherapy: Training and Practice*. Chicago: Aldine, 1967.
- Carkhuff, Robert R. *Helping and Human Relations*. 2 vols. New York: Holt, Rinehart and Winston, 1969.
- Barrett-Lennard, G. T. "Dimensions of Therapist Response as Causal Factors in Therapeutic Change." *Psychological Monographs* 76, no. 43 (1962). Whole No. 562.
- Ivey, Allen E., et al. "Micro-Counseling and Attending Behavior." APGA paper, April 1968. ERIC ED021275.
- Ivey, Allen E. *Microcounseling: Innovations in Interviewing Training*. Springfield, IL: Charles C. Thomas, 1971.
- Ivey, Allen E., Mary Bradford Ivey, and Carlos P. Zalaquett. *Intentional Interviewing and Counseling*. Later editions (Brooks/Cole / Cengage). Cite the edition on the desk.
- Egan, Gerard. *The Skilled Helper*. Monterey, CA: Brooks/Cole, 1975. Later editions through Cengage.
- Hill, Clara E. *Helping Skills: Facilitating Exploration, Insight, and Action*. Washington, DC: American Psychological Association. First ed. 1999; later editions 2004, 2009, 2014, 2020.
- Kagan, Norman. Interpersonal Process Recall (IPR). Training papers from the 1960s–1970s; see Kagan, Krathwohl, and Miller, 1963, and later manuals.
- Gendlin, Eugene T. *Focusing*. New York: Everest House, 1978. Experiencing Scale work with Rogers at Chicago / Wisconsin.
- Bordin, Edward S. "The Generalizability of the Psychoanalytic Concept of the Working Alliance." *Psychotherapy: Theory, Research & Practice* 16, no. 3 (1979): 252–260.
- Horvath, Adam O., and Leslie S. Greenberg. "Development and Validation of the Working Alliance Inventory." *Journal of Counseling Psychology* 36, no. 2 (1989): 223–233.
- Flückiger, Christoph, A. C. Del Re, Bruce E. Wampold, and Adam O. Horvath. Alliance meta-analyses in *Psychotherapy* (2018 and updates). Historical objects, not shopping lists.

## Empathy, regard, congruence (intellectual history)

- Lipps, Theodor. Essays on *Einfühlung*, c. 1903–1906. Cite the German essay you have, not a slogan.
- Titchener, Edward B. *Lectures on the Experimental Psychology of the Thought-Processes*. New York: Macmillan, 1909. English "empathy."
- Kohut, Heinz. *The Analysis of the Self*. New York: International Universities Press, 1971. Empathic immersion as a psychoanalytic claim.
- Batson, C. Daniel. Empathy-altruism papers from the 1980s–1990s. Use as a research argument, not as a clinic manual.

## Motivational interviewing

- Miller, William R. "Motivational Interviewing with Problem Drinkers." *Behavioural Psychotherapy* 11, no. 2 (1983): 147–172.
- Miller, William R., and Stephen Rollnick. *Motivational Interviewing: Preparing People to Change Addictive Behavior*. New York: Guilford, 1991. Later eds. 2002, 2013, 2023.
- Moyers, Theresa B., et al. Motivational Interviewing Treatment Integrity (MITI) coding manuals. Cite the version used as a *historical coding object*, not as a homework sheet.

## Questions, narrative, solution-focused

- Selvini Palazzoli, Mara, Luigi Boscolo, Gianfranco Cecchin, and Giuliana Prata. Milan circular-question papers, late 1970s–1980. *Family Process* 1980 is the usual English door.
- de Shazer, Steve. *Keys to Solution in Brief Therapy*. New York: Norton, 1985.
- de Shazer, Steve. *Clues: Investigating Solutions in Brief Therapy*. New York: Norton, 1988. Miracle question as a dated object.
- White, Michael, and David Epston. *Narrative Means to Therapeutic Ends*. New York: Norton, 1990.
- White, Michael. Dulwich Centre papers, Adelaide. Use Dulwich publications, not workshop folklore.

## Common factors

- Rosenzweig, Saul. "Some Implicit Common Factors in Diverse Methods of Psychotherapy." *American Journal of Orthopsychiatry* 6 (1936): 412–415.
- Frank, Jerome D. *Persuasion and Healing*. Baltimore: Johns Hopkins Press, 1961. Later editions with Julia Frank.
- Luborsky, Lester, Barton Singer, and Lise Luborsky. "Comparative Studies of Psychotherapies." *Archives of General Psychiatry* 32 (1975): 995–1008.
- Wampold, Bruce E. *The Great Psychotherapy Debate*. Mahwah, NJ: Erlbaum, 2001. 2nd ed. 2015 with Imel.
- Lambert, Michael J. Common-factors pie / "four-factor" popularizations. Cite the paper, not the poster.

## Modalities (skill-package histories)

- Perls, Frederick, Ralph Hefferline, and Paul Goodman. *Gestalt Therapy*. New York: Julian Press, 1951.
- Berne, Eric. *Transactional Analysis in Psychotherapy*. New York: Grove, 1961.
- Berne, Eric. *Games People Play*. New York: Grove, 1964.
- Glasser, William. *Reality Therapy*. New York: Harper & Row, 1965.
- Ellis, Albert. *Reason and Emotion in Psychotherapy*. New York: Lyle Stuart, 1962. (REBT as a skill claim; full life is in the therapy-history pack.)
- Beck, Aaron T. *Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional Disorders*. New York: International Universities Press, 1976.
- Wolpe, Joseph. *Psychotherapy by Reciprocal Inhibition*. Stanford: Stanford University Press, 1958.
- Lazarus, Arnold A. *Multimodal Behavior Therapy*. New York: Springer, 1976. Technical eclecticism.
- Norcross, John C., and colleagues. Psychotherapy integration handbooks (later Oxford / APA volumes). Cite the edition.
- Chesler, Phyllis. *Women and Madness*. Garden City, NY: Doubleday, 1972.
- Brown, Laura S. *Subversive Dialogues: Theory in Feminist Therapy*. New York: Basic Books, 1994.
- Sue, Derald Wing, Patricia Arredondo, and Roderick J. McDavis. "Multicultural Counseling Competencies and Standards." *Journal of Counseling & Development* 70 (1992): 477–486.
- Sue, Derald Wing, and David Sue. *Counseling the Culturally Diverse*. Later Wiley editions. Cite the edition on the desk.
- Tervalon, Melanie, and Jann Murray-García. "Cultural Humility versus Cultural Competence." *Journal of Health Care for the Poor and Underserved* 9, no. 2 (1998): 117–125.
- Wrenn, C. Gilbert. "The Culturally Encapsulated Counselor." *Harvard Educational Review* 32 (1962): 444–449.

## Crisis, career, school, rehab (histories, not protocols)

- Lindemann, Erich. "Symptomatology and Management of Acute Grief." *American Journal of Psychiatry* 101 (1944): 141–148. Coconut Grove fire as a dated event. No victim photographs.
- Caplan, Gerald. *Principles of Preventive Psychiatry*. New York: Basic Books, 1964.
- Parsons, Frank. *Choosing a Vocation*. Boston: Houghton Mifflin, 1909. Bureau opened 1908; Parsons died 1908.
- Super, Donald E. "A Theory of Vocational Development." *American Psychologist* 8 (1953): 185–190.
- Holland, John L. *Making Vocational Choices*. First ed. 1973 (RIASEC lineage from the late 1950s). Do not confuse with Audrey L. Holland.
- Gysbers, Norman C., and Patricia Henderson. School-counseling program texts (later editions). Cite as professional-history objects.
- American School Counselor Association. National Model documents (2003 and revisions). Dated objects.
- Rubin, Stanford E., and Richard T. Roessler. Rehabilitation counseling textbooks. Cite the edition. Smith-Fess Act (1920) and later VR amendments as statute history.

## SLP / CSD counseling

- Luterman, David. *Counseling Parents of Hearing-Impaired Children*. Boston: Little, Brown, 1979.
- Luterman, David. *Counseling the Communicatively Disordered and Their Families*. Boston: Little, Brown, 1984. Later retitled *Counseling Persons with Communication Disorders and Their Families* (PRO-ED; 6th ed. 2017). ERIC ED583622 notes a 1979 first-publication claim for the lineage.
- Holland, Audrey L., and Ryan L. Nelson. *Counseling in Communication Disorders: A Wellness Perspective*. San Diego: Plural. 1st ed. 2007; 2nd 2014; 3rd 2018. [VERIFY 3rd year on upload.]
- Flasher, Lydia V., and Paul T. Fogle. *Counseling Skills for Speech-Language Pathologists and Audiologists*. Clifton Park, NY: Thomson/Delmar, 2004. 2nd ed. 2012.
- American Speech-Language-Hearing Association. *Scope of Practice in Speech-Language Pathology*. 2016. https://www.asha.org/policy/sp2016-00343 — counseling named as a service-delivery domain.
- American Speech-Language-Hearing Association. Practice Portal: "Counseling for Professional Service Delivery." Informational vs. personal-adjustment counseling. Re-open on upload week.
- Tellis, Cari M., and Orlando R. Barone. Later CSD counseling texts cited on the ASHA portal (2018). Use as a portal object, not as a protocol.

## Profession / ethics backdrop (do not annex the therapy-history profession essay)

- American Counseling Association / APGA institutional histories. ACA *Code of Ethics* 2014 and revisions.
- CACREP founding 1981.
- Sue, Arredondo, and McDavis 1992 (again — it is counseling history that psychiatry sometimes ignored).
- Bayer, Ronald. *Homosexuality and American Psychiatry*. 1981. Conversion-practice harm is in-bounds; the method is not.
