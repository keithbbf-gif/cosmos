# Photo notes — counseling skills & modalities pack

Graphics are a later agent. This file is the brief they must read before drawing.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or Creative Commons portraits documented in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`.
- Period objects: title pages (*Choosing a Vocation* 1909; *Microcounseling* 1971; Luterman 1979), PD hospital or classroom interiors, PD recording equipment (reel-to-reel, early videotape).
- Original diagrams drawn for this series: a dated timeline of *skill names*, a map of training sites (Chicago, Wisconsin, Colorado State, Albuquerque, Emerson, Tucson), a family tree of *teaching methods* (not of patients).
- Type treatments: name, dates, one short epithet.

## Banned

- AI-generated faces, "historical photographs" that are not photographs, or "what X would look like."
- Scraped workshop / TED / university headshots.
- Patient artwork from psychiatric collections presented as free decoration.
- Children in experiments as thumbnails.
- Atrocity photographs as headers.
- Stock "therapist with clipboard and smiling client."
- Before/after faces.
- Skill-worksheet screenshots that could be used as DIY protocols.
- Anything that implies a WOW Therapies treatment room you have not photographed with consent.

## Alt text

Name what is in the frame. "Title page of *Microcounseling*, 1971, Charles C. Thomas." Not "the birth of modern listening."

For type-only posts: no empty `<img>`. The heading is the image.

## WordPress featured image

If `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` says `no-portrait` or `confirm`, the featured image is a **series template** (wordmark + essay title), not a guessed face. Build one template; reuse it.

On upload day, open the Commons or museum URL again. Paste the credit block from `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` into the media caption **and** under the image in the post.

## Color and crop

Do not colorize PD photographs and call them contemporary. Do not crop a group so that a bystander becomes "the" portrait. Do not flip a face for layout.

## Sibling lanes

Do not reuse therapy-history founder portraits as if this were the same series. Do not reuse SLPWOW oral-mechanism diagrams or fluency waveforms as decoration. Different pack, different eye.

## If the graphics agent and the writer disagree

The license table wins. A beautiful unlicensed face does not ship.
