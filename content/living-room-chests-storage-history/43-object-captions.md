# Object captions — if the piece grows pictures

Captions are where furniture writing goes to lie. Short, dated, no “stunning.”

**Six-board chest.** Northern Europe, boarded construction used for centuries. Two ends, two sides, bottom, lid. The iron is the joint talking.

**Cassone (Met 1975.1.1938 or 16.155).** Tuscany, fifteenth century. Marriage pair in the documents; bedchamber after the street. Not a living-room type.

**Mule — do not use that word on the label.** English, seventeenth century: lidded chest with drawer(s) in the base. The name mule chest is about 1911.

**Restoration chest of drawers.** England, later seventeenth century. The pile has lost. Walnut often a veneer. Feet often later.

**Cressent commode, 1738.** Delivered for Louis XV’s bedroom at La Muette. Convenient, not informal. Bedroom, not salon.

**Hadley-group chest.** Connecticut River Valley, late seventeenth–early eighteenth century. Tulip-and-stem. Initials are not the maker. More than a hundred related survivors.

**Sunflower / Wethersfield chest.** Same decades. Rosettes. Peter Blin shop tradition is an argument with one good receipt.

**Tallboy or highboy.** Eighteenth-century chamber stack. Wrong scale for a television wall.

**Lane cedar chest.** Altavista, from 1912. Factory trousseau. Smell is chemistry. Script is advertising.

**Miniature Lane.** Girl Graduate Plan, official 1930. The hook, not a toy.

**Ozarka Credenza, 1928.** A radio wearing a sideboard’s name. The machine in wooden clothes.

**ESU.** Charles and Ray Eames, Herman Miller, 1950–55. Copy names the living room. Steel, Masonite, plywood. Five-year product.

**Sendai isho-dansu.** Clothing tansu. Keyaki face, iron, carry handles. Stored out of the everyday room more often than catalogs admit.

**Bandaji.** Korea. Half the front opens. Say the name.

**Storage ottoman.** A six-board in upholstery. Ask whether the lid stays up.

**Retail accent chest.** Late category. A leftover word after the jobs were sanded off.

If the art department writes “global treasures through time” above this list, send the list back.
