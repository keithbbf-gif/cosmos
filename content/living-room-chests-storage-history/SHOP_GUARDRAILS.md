# Shop guardrails — BBF living-room chests lane

Staged history and shop practice. Not a live catalog. Not a bid. Not a custom-quote engine.

## Do not

- Invent a commission, a celebrity client, a showroom city list, or a dollar figure.
- Treat any essay as a construction specification, a load table, or a finish schedule.
- Fuse Keith Fritz Fine Furniture (Ferdinand, Indiana, public record from 1999) with Bradley Lumber / Bradley Brand Furniture / Warren mill chronology. Same owner’s world is not the same letterhead.
- Print a current showroom roster. Those change. Check keithfritz.com the morning you would speak a city.
- Auto-generate key takeaways, SKU lists, or price boxes.
- Claim a Lane, Stickley, Goddard-Townsend, or Eames construction detail from a photograph alone when the catalog disagrees.
- Turn the cellarette essay into the liquor-cabinet pack, or the secretary essay into the desk pack.
- Use disease, cure, or wellness language. Cedar is a wood with a smell. It is not medicine.

## Do

- Keep `status: staged` until Keith clears publish.
- Mark soft dates and contested attributions `[VERIFY]`.
- Prefer accession numbers and monograph pages over dealer adjectives.
- Split lid-chests from drawer-cases on any sentence that sounds like a definition.
- When the shop speaks, speak as a bench: moisture, a binding lid, a till that still makes sense. Do not speak as a lifestyle brand.
