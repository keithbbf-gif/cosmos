# Rejects — what was thrown, and why

A staged set that will not show its trash is still performing.

## Whole approaches that died

**The illustrated chronology.** A year, an object, a pious sentence. It is a timeline wearing a coat. We already have `02-timeline.md`. The essay is the gap, not the beads.

**The buyer’s guide.** “Five chests for every living room.” Draft 29 is the honest version of that impulse and it is still not the piece. Guides want a cart. This work does not.

**The sentimental Lane.** A thousand words of graduation and cedar smell, as if the factory were a folk custom. The funnel is the story. Sentiment is what the ads already sold.

**The world tour.** A paragraph each for Italy, France, England, New England, Japan, Korea, Denmark. Draft 30 is that tour with better dates. It has no person in the room.

**The podcast.** Draft 31. “Okay so here’s the thing.” Intimacy without a relationship.

## Sentences that looked expensive

- “Storage is how a household tells the truth about itself.”  
  Pretty. Also something you could say about laundry.

- “The cassone is the chest as theater.”  
  True enough. Theater is a word that invites a paragraph of costume. The garbage statute does more.

- “Bok gave America permission to live in the front room.”  
  Gave. Permission as a gift from an editor. He sold plans and a sermon. Keep the sermon. Drop the halo.

- “The Eames unit is a bridge you can put a cup on.”  
  The Foundation already used a bridge. Do not borrow their metaphor and pretend you saw steel.

- “Hope is a lining.”  
  Cute. Lane would have bought it. That is why it goes.

## Facts that did not earn a chair

- 95% of bun feet are replacements. Dealer-adjacent. Plausible. Not in the essay.
- 27 million miniatures as a hard census. Company estimate. In the drafts as “tens of millions” or “company numbers.”
- Bok invented the term living room. False. Killed wherever it stood up.
- Peter Blin made the sunflower chests. Overclaim. Killed.
- A first-year for the tallboy. Trade habit, not a birth.
- Peddlers strapping mule chests on mules. No picture I will trust.

## Cuts from draft 35 that did not make 41

The Stickley sideboard numbers (802, 814½). Right objects, wrong essay. They belong in `13` and `22`.

A longer ESU assembly saga. Denver and the High already have it. One buckle is enough.

Shaker as a full stop in the main essay. It has `17`. The final only needs the built-in rhyme if it needs it at all. It does not.

## What I almost kept out of stubbornness

The Boccaccio cassone as a lover’s hide. It is true and it is a wink. The garbage statute is enough street. The Decameron can stay in `07`.
