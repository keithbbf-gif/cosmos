# WordPress import notes (staging only)

Staged Markdown. Not a live publish checklist.

## What to import

- One post per file in `drafts/`.
- Title and slug from YAML. Keep `status: staged` until Keith clears. Import as **draft** posts only. Do not schedule. Do not set a public date in this pack.
- Body starts after the second `---` fence. Keep the disclaimer paragraph.
- `educational_claim` is for the editor, not a pull-quote unless Keith wants it.
- `meta` excerpt, if needed later, can be cut from the first two body sentences. Do not invent an SEO paragraph.

## What not to do

- Do not strip `## Notes` or `## Figure plan`. Those are for the editor and the photo desk.
- Do not promote a history essay into a catalog page or an RFQ form.
- Do not auto-generate “key takeaways,” product grids, or related-SKU boxes. This pack has none.
- Do not invent client names from a showroom list.
- Do not flatten first-person shop voice into brochure English.

## Routing

`channel: BBF` and `stage:` stay in front matter for Website-GC routing. Tags in YAML are editorial, not a WordPress taxonomy promise.

## Graphics

Figure plans name museum objects and suggested filenames. A later graphics pass may add SVGs. Until then, do not drop stock photos of “luxury living rooms” on these posts.
