# Living room, chests, storage furniture — staged drafts

**Stream:** writer. **Owner voice:** BBF. **Rule:** quality over speed.  
**Folder:** `content/living-room-chests-storage-history/`  
**Count:** forty-six files, `00` through `45`. This file is the map, not the piece.  
**Images:** `IMAGE-SEO.md`, `RIGHTS.md`, `GRAPHICS_INDEX.md`, `assets/` (SVG schematics + Commons registry).

This is not a furniture catalog and not a COSMOS module. It is a staged writing set: research that can be checked, then object essays, then full-essay revisions, then the apparatus (openings, cuts, captions, claim index). The living room is the room. The chest is the oldest storage furniture most people in the West still recognize. The argument is that those two facts do not sit easily together, and most copy about “living room storage” pretends they do.

## How to read it

Read in number order if you want the work as it was made. Jump to `41-final-candidate.md` if you only want the piece that survived. Jump to `40-fact-check.md` if a date feels too clean.

## What “staged” means here

Not furniture staging first, though that gets its own draft (`28`). Staged as in a writer’s shop: you do not publish the first upright paragraph. You keep the versions. A later draft that cannot point at what it threw away is just a new first draft.

Also staged as in git: the folder is the work, the PR is the box it travels in.

## What “human voice” means here

A person in a room, looking at a box, willing to say when a name is late or a story is marketing. No “throughout history.” No “statement piece.” No “elevate your space.” If a sentence could sell a sofa, it comes out.

BBF: concrete nouns, earned dates, contested attributions left contested, no fabricated certainty. A claim that cannot sit next to `01-sources.md` does not sit.

## What this set will not do

- It will not flatten tansu, bandaji, or camphor chests into “global inspiration.”
- It will not treat the Lane hope chest as a folk custom that just happened to have a factory.
- It will not call a mule chest a period name. The form is old. The English name is about 1911.
- It will not invent a medieval “living room.” The room is late. The box is not.

Read `04-voice-brief.md` before judging tone. Read `40-fact-check.md` before quoting a year.
