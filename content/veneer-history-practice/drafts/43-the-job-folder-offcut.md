I had page 24 in a folder with a cutoff that already wore the oil schedule. I patched a sibling. The figure continued. If I had burned 24, I would have patched a stranger from a grade bundle and the hallway would have found it.

The job-folder offcut is the only reprint. Sequence said it. Repair spends it.

## What goes in the folder

Spare leaves. Offcuts with figure that matches the elbows. A cutoff with the finish on it. A note: flitch, pages used, glue, both faces, band species. A photo of the fan. I do not put a client’s private paper in a folder that wanders. I put the wood.

I have put the folder in a drawer that became a junk drawer. I put folders in a crate now, labeled, the same crate religion as the flitch.

## The coin patch

I cut a patch from the sibling, larger than the wound, I trace, I cut the wound to the trace, I fit, hide if I can, a scrape, a spot-finish. The grain must run. The flake must flake. A patch that is “close” is a postage stamp.

I date the patch on the back. I do not pretend I was the first shop in 1840. I am the shop in the year I am.

## When the folder is empty

I tell the client we have cousins, not siblings. I can still patch. I can remake a field. I can leave a dated scar. I cannot invent page 24. If they want a later sibling, they buy pages now. Storage is cheaper than a story.

A historical piece with no folder is the usual case. I look for a leaf on the object I can rob from an unseen edge — the underside, the back of a drawer — and I only rob if the robbery is smaller than the wound and the client agrees. I have robbed a back rail for a show face. I wrote it down. I did not rob a museum.

## The back rail I asked to rob

A nineteenth-century washstand, no folder, a coin wound on the top. The client agreed I could take a leaf from the underside of a back rail that never saw light. I robbed a patch smaller than the wound. I wrote the robbery on the ticket and on a card in the case. I did not rob a museum. I did not rob a show face to save a show face.

The patch was a cousin from the same object, which is better than a grade-bundle stranger and worse than page 24. The hallway had to hunt. I date the patch. I do not pretend I was the first shop in 1840.

## A stranger from a grade bundle

I burned page 24. I have said it. The later dent got a stranger — same species, honest, a postage stamp you could read from the hall. I remade the field when I could buy a closer page. The stove does not get reprints. A helper does not tidy the folder. The tidy is a shuffle and a fire.

If the scrap is a cooked edge I look twice. I have burned a cooked crotch heart that was the only extra heart. The raccoon I patched later was a stranger. Cooked is not always waste. Waste is a decision I make with the map of the book in my hand, not with a broom.

## How I cut the wound to the trace

I cut the sibling larger than the coin. I tape it on the wound. I trace with a knife, light passes. I cut the wound to the trace. I fit until the grain runs and the flake flakes. Hide if I can. A scrape. A spot-finish that will show if I pretend it will not. I tell the client the spot is a spot.

A patch that is “close” is a stamp. A patch from the elbow of the same page can continue a cathedral. I keep elbow offcuts on purpose. The middle of a leftover is not always the match. The edge of the leftover is sometimes the only match.

When the folder is empty I say cousins. I can remake a field. I can leave a dated scar. I cannot invent page 24. If they want a later sibling they buy pages now. Storage is cheaper than a story. ## The stove

I have burned a reprint. I do not burn folders. I do not let a helper tidy scraps. The tidy is a shuffle and a fire.

If the scrap is truly waste — a chip, a cooked edge — I can burn it. I look twice. I have burned a cooked edge that was the only extra crotch heart. The raccoon I later patched was a stranger.

## What I tell a client

We kept pages. If something happens, we have a sibling. If they move houses, the folder can move with the table or stay with the shop. I ask. I do not assume the shop is their attic forever. If they take the folder, I tell them it is not kindling. They laugh. I do not.

## What I write

Folder ID, pages left, where it lives, whether the client took it. If I spent page 24, I write spent. The map of the book should still be true after a repair.

Finish on a thin face is next: grain raise into the core, a film that seals a tent, a scrape that is safer than a raise. The folder will not save a finish mistake that went through. The folder will save a coin. I keep the folder. I date the patch. I do not feed the stove. The hallway is the critic. If the hallway cannot find the coin, the sibling did the work. If the hallway finds a postage stamp, I owed them page 24 and I did not have it. I have that conversation once. I keep the folder so I do not have it twice.
