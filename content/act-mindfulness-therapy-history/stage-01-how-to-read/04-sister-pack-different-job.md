In 2026 this repository already holds a forty-two-essay counseling-heritage pack under `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/`. Fourteen era pieces, twenty-eight figures, Pinel to Insoo Kim Berg. Essay 13 is "Third-wave therapies and mindfulness in the room." Essay 29 is Marsha Linehan. Essay 37 is Shōma Morita. Those pages exist. This folder does not replace them.

A writer who retells Linehan's 1991 trial and 2011 *New York Times* interview as if they had not already been written is not deepening a lane. He is burning the same wood twice and calling the smoke a series.

The job here is narrower and, if I do it right, stranger. The sister pack had to get a reader from antiquity to a modern consulting room without turning any door into a protocol. It could give ACT and MBCT a section. It could not give them a house. This pack is the house: how a behavior-analytic argument about language met a medical-behavioral program about attention, how both met Buddhist teachers who did not ask to become citations, and how a vocabulary left the room for yogurt and apps.

The live WOW Therapies site, when last fetched, presented occupational, physical, and speech services in Southeast Arkansas. The sister pack already had to say, in so many words, that heritage education is not a service menu. This pack must say it again, because ACT-and-mindfulness copy is especially good at sounding like a menu. "We take a mindfulness-based approach" is a sentence a clinic can put on a homepage without ever having staffed a consultation team or an eight-week MBSR class. History that flatters that sentence is advertising.

So the division of labor is practical.

**Sister pack.** Who sat in the European and American consulting rooms? What did asylums claim? What did psychoanalysis become? How did CBT get a manual? Who is Bowlby, Fanon, Herman, Berg? If you want that calendar, go there. If you want speech-pathology heritage — Van Riper, fluency, dysphagia — go to the SLPWOW lane. Do not file this pack under speech therapy, and do not file speech essays under ACT.

**This pack.** How old is the name ACT, and who says so? What did "comprehensive distancing" name before 1991? What is relational frame theory as a *book* (2001), not as a personality? What did Kabat-Zinn actually open in 1979, and what did he later write about a retreat-room vision? What did Teasdale's 2000 table show, including the cell that did not light up? What did Öst refuse? What did Purser and Loy name in 2013?

Overlap is allowed when the angle changes. Linehan appears here as the person who put Zen attention inside a hospital staffing pattern, and who would not recognize a TikTok module list as the thing she built. The vow, the Tulsa birth year, the full biosocial paragraph — those stay in the sister figure essay. Morita appears here as a cousin room Hofmann could point at in 2008, not as a second biography.

Beck appears here as a grandparent of MBCT: Segal, Williams, and Teasdale were not converting to a religion; they were trying to stop relapse in people who already knew the thought record. The sister pack owns Beck's life. I will not rewrite *Cognitive Therapy of Depression* (1979) as if I had just discovered it.

Rogers appears, oddly, as a heat source. Third-wave rooms needed a relationship that could stand still without becoming a pep talk. Some of that heat-tolerance was person-centered even when the citation was Zen. One sentence is enough. The sister pack has the rest.

If you are an editor later in this pipeline, the test of a paragraph is: would the sister pack's `check_pack.py` think I stole an essay? If yes, cut. Deepen with documents this pack uniquely needs — Zettle 1986, Hayes & Wilson 1994, ACBS 2005 minutes-as-webpage, Kabat-Zinn 2011, Teasdale 2000's split, Öst 2008, Purser & Loy 2013.

There is also a brand reason to keep the jobs apart. A reader who lands on "Philippe Pinel" and a reader who lands on "Öst 2008" are not in the same hour of curiosity. Mixing them produces the encyclopedia voice this house style banned: a tour that never sits down. Let the general calendar stay general. Let this calendar stay argumentative.

Staged means two things, and I want both on the table. The essays are organized in seven stages so a reader can stop. And the folder is git-staged for review, not cleared to write itself onto wowtherapies.com. The sister pack's `WP_IMPORT.md` already said: no dump onto a live sitemap. This pack inherits that refusal.

I have seen content mills treat "complementary" as "duplicate with a new slug." That is how a site gets three Linehan pages and no Öst. This folder would rather have one Linehan angle and a whole stage on how a nickname met a meta-analysis.

If you only have time to read two sister-pack files before this one, read `STYLE_GUIDE.md` and `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md` over there, then `GUARDRAILS.md` here. The voice is the same desk. The refuse list is stricter on scripts, because this subject matter comes with scripts attached.
