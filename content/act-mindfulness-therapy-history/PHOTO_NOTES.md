# Photo notes — ACT / mindfulness history series

Text is the product. Images are evidence or atmosphere, never a fabricated witness.

Companion: `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`. Graphics agents start there.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or Creative Commons portraits listed as `pd` or `cc`.
- Period objects: 1999 Guilford title page (if a fair-use or publisher-cleared
  scan exists — otherwise type), 1975 Benson title page, a PD temple plan, a
  PD engraving of a monastic hall that you can actually license.
- Original diagrams drawn for this series: a dated timeline of *documents*,
  a map of societies (Worcester, Barre, Reno, London, Almería). Not a
  hexaflex worksheet. Not a "how to sit" figure.
- Type treatments: name, dates, one short epithet.

## Banned

- AI-generated faces, "historical photographs" that are not photographs.
- Scraped workshop / TED / university headshots of living people.
- Patient artwork, retreat candids, children on cushions as thumbnails.
- Stock "therapist with clipboard and smiling client."
- Before/after faces. Meditation-app screenshots used as endorsements.
- Anything that implies a WOW Therapies treatment room you have not
  photographed with consent.
- Diagrams that function as skill worksheets (body-scan maps, values card
  sorts, hexaflex homework).

## Alt text

Name what is in the frame. Not "a mindful soul finds peace."

For type-only posts: no empty `<img>`. The heading is the image.

## WordPress featured image

If `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` says `no-portrait`, the featured image is a **series
template** (wordmark + essay title), not a guessed face.
