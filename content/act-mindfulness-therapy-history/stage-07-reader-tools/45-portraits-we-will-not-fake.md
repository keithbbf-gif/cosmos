**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

The last essay in a history pack is sometimes a gallery. This one is a refuse list.

We will not generate a face. Not Hayes at a 1981 desk. Not Kabat-Zinn in a 1979 basement. Not a "period photograph" of a Worcester corridor that no camera made. Generated faces are lies with good lighting. The sister pack banned them. We ban them again because this subject matter attracts them: the wise teacher, the calm patient, the diverse group on matching cushions.

We will not scrape a keynote still. Living people own their faces. A university directory is not a license. A YouTube thumbnail is not a license.

We will not use children on cushions as thumbnails. We will not use retreat candids. We will not use patient art. We will not use atrocity or war images to decorate Nhất Hạnh or a 2001 spark story.

We will not print a hexaflex, a body-scan map, or a values sort as a "graphic." Those are worksheets. Essay 23 (*A Diagram That Escaped the Workshop*) said no. PHOTO_NOTES said no.

What we *will* allow, when a human graphics pass arrives:

Type: name, dates, one epithet ("1999 Guilford title page," not "healer").
Period objects with clean rights: a 1975 Benson title page; a 1908-style PD engraving of a monastic hall if the file page is actually PD; a map of Worcester–Barre–Reno–London–Almería drawn for this series.
A series template for featured images. One template. Reuse it.

`PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` is the table. If the row says `no-portrait` or `confirm`, the featured image is the template. Re-open any Commons file on upload day. Licenses move.

Why end the pack here?

Because the yogurt years were a visual style as much as a verbal one: soft palettes, jetty sunrises, white people with closed eyes. A small-city clinic that imports that style has imported the flattening. Southeast Arkansas light is not stock-photo light. If you need a picture, photograph a book on a table you own, with consent, and caption it as a book on a table.

The sister pack allowed Pinel and Freud because the files are PD and the men are dead. This pack's census is living and late. Type is not a failure of design. It is how you do not defraud a reader about who sat in the room.

If a later agent is assigned "graphics for ACT history," they start with this essay and PHOTO_NOTES, not with a diffusion model. If they start with a diffusion model, they have failed the pack before they have drawn.

I have not attached images to these forty-five essays. That is on purpose. Words first. Rights second. Pictures never if the picture is a fake witness. A later zip of "hero images" that appears without a license table should be refused at the import door.

A reader who wanted a face will have to live with a year. 1999. 2000. 2008. Years do not smile. They also do not pretend to have been in Worcester with you.

If a stock agency sends a "diverse mindfulness group" at a discount, the discount is the tell. Diversity as a download is the closing-paragraph problem the sister style guide banned. Draw a map of cities instead. Cities do not require a model release from a stranger who never sat in your class.

This is the last essay in the series. It does not recap the headings. It closes the fence at the place fences fail when everyone is tired: the thumbnail. Tired people click "generate." This pack does not. The refusal is the last sentence on purpose. A thumbnail is not a small decision. It is the first lie a tired page tells.
