**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

As of last verification (2026-09-14), a long list of people in this pack were living: Hayes, Strosahl, Wilson, Kabat-Zinn, Segal, Williams, Teasdale, Linehan, Langer, Harris, Purser, Hofmann, Arch, Craske, Gaudiano, Bowen, the Barnes-Holmeses, Walser, Twohig, Neff, Gilbert, Goldstein, Salzberg, Kornfield. The list will rot. Death dates belong in a later editor pass, not in gossip.

The rule is the sister pack's rule, restated for a pack that is almost *all* living people.

**Public books, public papers, public lectures, public interviews they chose.** No health speculation. No "I heard." No childhood reconstruction from a podcast tone. No comments-section diagnosis of a founder.

Linehan's 2011 *Times* interview remains the model of a *chosen* disclosure. She picked the venue. The sister pack may name that choice. It may not embroider a medical file. If another living person in this pack has not chosen a venue, we do not drag a private history into a heritage essay to make the science warmer.

Photographs of the living are almost never ours. University headshots are not public domain. Workshop selfies are not Creative Commons because someone tagged them. Generated faces are banned twice: once as fraud, once as a dignity issue. Type. Dates. A title page if you have rights.

The temptation in founder-heavy stories is the cartoon: Hayes the prophet, Kabat-Zinn the mystic scientist, Linehan the wounded healer. Cartoons recruit. They also make living people into mascots who cannot correct the record without looking thin-skinned. We will use surnames on title pages and years on journals. That is colder. It is fairer.

Death does not make a person public property. Thích Nhất Hạnh died in 2022; most images remain ©. Herbert Benson died in 2022; a 1975 title page is still safer than a late headshot of uncertain license. Estates answer email slowly. Graphics agents wait.

For WOW Therapies: do not put a living clinician's face on these posts, including the practice owner's face, as if they were the author of 1999. Author fields on import should be a holding user (`editorial-history`), as the sister `WP_IMPORT.md` already said. Fake PhDs are a claims-guardrail failure.

If a living person objects to a factual error, correct the error. If they object to a fair reading of a public paper, note the objection and keep the paper. Fair reading is not permission. It is the ordinary risk of publishing history about the not-yet-dead.

Students sometimes think "living" means "do not write." That would erase the last forty years and leave us with Freud, which the sister pack already has. Write. Date. Cite. Stop before the bedroom. The bedroom is never a source in this folder.

This essay is a policy, not a profile. If it feels like a scold, good. This pack's risk is not that we will be too dull about 1957. It is that we will be too vivid about a person who can still read us.

If a founder dies during the life of this folder, change the tense and the portrait row. Do not write an obituary that recasts the science as destiny. Destiny is the cartoon again.

I am a writer on a clock. I have not called these people. I have opened their books and the pages their societies published. That limit is in every draft's mouth. It should stay there. A phone call would not have turned this pack into a biography, and a biography is not what WOW Therapies asked for. They asked for history with the claims fence up. The fence includes the living. It also includes the recently dead, who still have estates and photographs that are not ours.
