A reader who finishes an ACT essay and wants Freud is not lost. They are in the wrong hallway of the same house. This page is a coat-check.

**Counseling and clinical heritage** — `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/`

Go there for asylums, the talking cure, the psychoanalytic century, behaviorism-in-the-clinic, humanistic and existential rooms, families, the cognitive-empirical turn, attachment, trauma lineages, feminist and liberation psychologies, counseling as a profession.

Go there for Pinel, Dix, Beers, Freud, Jung, Adler, Anna Freud, Klein, Horney, Winnicott, Rogers, Frankl, Beck, Ellis, Linehan (full figure), Bowlby, Ainsworth, Satir, Minuchin, Yalom, Fanon, Martín-Baró, Morita (full figure), Bose, Nise da Silveira, Mamie Phipps Clark, Herman, Insoo Kim Berg.

Go there for the third-wave *overview* (essay 13). This pack is the long room behind that door, not a replacement door.

**Speech-language pathology heritage** — SLPWOW lane (separate pack; do not merge calendars)

Go there for Bell, Van Riper, articulation science, fluency, school SLP history, dysphagia, childhood apraxia. A figure who touched both (James on habit) may appear in the counseling pack only in a psychotherapy frame, per that style guide. This ACT pack will not steal him.

**This pack** — `content/act-mindfulness-therapy-history/`

Stay here for the naming of ACT (1981/1991/1994/1999), RFT 2001, ACBS 2005, Kabat-Zinn 1979, Teasdale 2000, Öst 2008, Hofmann 2008, NICE 2009, Purser 2013, and the older attention objects that clinics cite without owning.

**Do not cross-file**

Do not put Linehan's full life in two URLs. Cross-link.
Do not put Morita's full life in two URLs. Cross-link.
Do not put a dysphagia essay under "mindfulness-based therapy."
Do not put an ACT evidence quarrel under "speech therapy."
Do not auto-link these slugs to "book an evaluation."

**If you are an editor**

The test of a paragraph in this pack: would the sister `check_pack.py` think I stole essay 13 or 29? If yes, cut and point. The test of a paragraph in the sister pack: did I just invent an ACT origin myth in a closing section? If yes, point here.

**If you are a designer**

Three series templates are allowed. One lotus for all three is not. Speech history should not look like a zendo. ACT history should not look like a pediatric articulation card. Counseling history should not look like a booking funnel.

I am repeating the complementary table from the README because readers will not open the README. Repetition is how a map stays on the wall. If you are building WordPress menus, three items. Not one mashed item called "history."

Internal links among this pack's forty-five slugs are welcome. A single hub may list the README calendar. Do not auto-link "acceptance" to a booking form. Do not auto-link "Linehan" to a suicide-adjacent service page that does not exist.

The house has more than one desk. This desk is the one with 1999, 2000, and 2008 on it. The other desks have 1895 and 1931 and Van Riper. Sit down. Do not tour.

If a later pack arrives — furniture history, fig horticulture, herbal claims — those are other brands or other rooms. Do not file ACT under figs. The repository is a house with many occupants. Occupants do not share closets. If a later CMS tagger files all three series under "Wellness," undo the tag. Wellness is the yogurt word. History is a calendar. Calendars do not glow.
