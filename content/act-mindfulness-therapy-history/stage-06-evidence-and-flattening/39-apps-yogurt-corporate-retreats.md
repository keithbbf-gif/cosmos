By the mid-2010s you could buy a subscription that rang a bell on your phone, a dairy product that used the word on the lid, and a leadership offsite that promised presence. I am not going to name the apps as if this were a review site. Names go stale and become ads. The *category* is the historical object: attention as a productized timer.

Timers are not evil. Monasteries had bells. Benson had a count. MBSR had homework. The productized timer is different because it removes the teacher, the census, the consent conversation, and the chance that someone will notice you are not safe to sit. It also adds venture capital, engagement metrics, and a push notification that can itself be a compulsion.

Corporate retreats are the Purser diner from draft 37, in catering form. I have not attended the famous ones. I have read the genre: a hotel, a slide, a breathing minute before the layoff quarter. The genre is enough.

Yogurt is the joke that is not a joke. When a medical-behavioral noun from 1979 and a 2000 journal table can decorate a breakfast, the noun has completed a certain American journey. Food marketing is not a trial. It is evidence of prestige. Prestige is what schools want until they get it.

ACT's version of the flattening is the quote-card: a hexagon, a "drop the struggle" slogan, a coach who is not a clinician. MBCT's version is "mindfulness for depression" without the split. DBT's version is a module list on TikTok. All three versions sell. None of them is the 1991–2002 paperwork.

I will not tell you to delete an app. Some people use a timer and are fine. Some people use a timer instead of a hospital and are not. This folder cannot sort you. That is why it will not "review" the category as a consumer guide.

For WOW Therapies: if a later homepage slider wants a stock photo of a woman on a jetty at sunrise with the word MINDFUL, refuse. The sister pack already banned stock therapist-and-smiling-client. The jetty is the same ban in linen pants. Type. A dated title page. A map of Worcester and Barre. Dull images for a dull, honest page.

Speech-language and occupational pages have their own gadget markets (apps for articulation, timers for attention in a different clinical sense). Do not let this pack's noun colonize those gadgets. "Mindful" is not a billing code for a language session.

The historical sentence I want to leave:

**1979 clinic → 1990 trade book → 2000 table → 2004 nickname → 2005 society → 2008 quarrel → 2009 guideline → 2013 McMindfulness essay → 2010s subscriptions.**

That is a pipeline, not a destiny. Pipelines leak. This series is a rag on the floor, not a new pipe.

If you remember the yogurt, you will remember to ask what object a speaker means. If you forget the yogurt, you will hear "mindfulness" and nod, and the nod is how flattening wins.

No portraits. No screenshots of lock screens. Screenshots become endorsements. No "before/after mood" graphics. Those are the sister pack's banned before/after faces in a different costume.

Headspace, Calm, and their competitors are the names everyone already knows; I still will not rank them. Rankings are ads. The historical fact is the category's 2010s growth and the way journal tables were used as fuel. Fuel is not the table.

The sister pack said the third wave rode a boom and was cheapened by it. This essay is the boom's furniture: a phone, a lid, a banquet chair. Furniture can be dated. Dated furniture is harder to worship. Worship wants a glow. A banquet chair does not glow. That is why I keep putting chairs on the table.
