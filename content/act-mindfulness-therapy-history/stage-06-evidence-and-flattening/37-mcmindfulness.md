**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

On 1 July 2013, Ronald Purser and David Loy published "Beyond McMindfulness" in the *Huffington Post*. Repeater Books published Purser's *McMindfulness: How Mindfulness Became the New Capitalist Spirituality* in 2019. The coinage stuck because it was unkind and accurate about a certain object: attention training stripped of ethics, sold to corporations as a way to make workers endure the job.

I print the 2013 date so the critique is not a Twitter mood. It is an essay with a byline, then a book. Buddhist teachers and left critics had been muttering earlier. Purser and Loy gave the mutter a Happy Meal name.

What the critique gets right, as history:

By the 2010s the noun was on yogurt, in human-resources modules, in military experiments, in apps with night-mode cushions. Kabat-Zinn's hospital class and Teasdale's relapse table were not those objects. They got cited as if they were. Citation laundering is how a 2000 JCCP split becomes "science says sit." Google's Search Inside Yourself and its cousins are part of this weather; I will not tour a corporate curriculum I have not sat in. The weather is enough.

What the critique can get lazy about:

Not every eight-week class is a tool of capital. Not every clinician who says "notice that" is a scab. Chronic-pain groups in safety-net hospitals are not Google. A VA clinic is not a smoothie brand. The McMindfulness essay is a blunt instrument. Blunt instruments hit the right neon signs and also hit nurses.

Kabat-Zinn's 2011 "trouble with maps" essay is, in a way, an earlier insider worry about the same thinning: skillful means, secularization, what drops away. He is an interested party. Purser is an interested party. Read both. Do not let either become the only priest.

ACT rode the boom and was cheapened by it, as the sister third-wave essay said. "Just notice that" after three hours of training is not the 1999 book. An app that ends when the timer ends is not a consultation team and not an eight-week class with a teacher who can see you dissociate.

For WOW Therapies: do not put a lotus on a speech-language homepage because this folder exists. Do not sell "mindful communication" as if it were MBCT. The live site's mission language, when last fetched, was access, communication, quality of life — ordinary and enough. Heritage education can be strange. Branding should stay dull.

Living authors: public 2013 essay and 2019 book. No gossip. The *Huffington Post* URL will rot; keep the date and the title. Dates outlive content-management systems.

The useful inheritance of the coinage is a question, not a sneer: *who is being asked to accept what, and who is paid when they do?* If the answer is a worker accepting a manager, you are in Purser's diner. If the answer is a person in pain accepting a sensation so they can walk to the kitchen, you may be in a hospital class. If the answer is a therapy school accepting a thought so a value can move, you may be in ACT. Those are three diners. The neon sign is the same word.

Loy is a scholar-practitioner with a Buddhist bibliography of his own. I will not reduce him to a hyphen in "Purser and Loy." The 2013 byline is two people. The 2019 book is Purser's. Reception often collapses them. History can keep the years and the names apart.

This series will not tell you to boycott apps or to download one. It will tell you that 2013 named a flattening that 1990 and 2000 did not intend and could not control. Founders never control the noun once the noun pays. Payment is the plot of the 2010s. This pack is not a plot against sitting. It is a plot against a lid.
