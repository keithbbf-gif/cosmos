# ACT and mindfulness-based therapy history

Forty-plus **draft essays** for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com): how acceptance and commitment therapy, MBSR, MBCT, and neighboring mindfulness-based rooms were built — and how easily that history turns into a handout if nobody watches the language.

This is educational psychotherapy history. It is **not** a protocol, a meditation course, a service menu, or a claim that WOW Therapies practices every school named here.

**Do not write these files onto the live site from this folder.** Staging review only. See `WP_IMPORT.md`.

## Read this first

1. [`GUARDRAILS.md`](GUARDRAILS.md) — what a sentence may and may not do.
2. [`STYLE_GUIDE.md`](STYLE_GUIDE.md) — voice. Blocking.
3. [`CITATIONS.md`](CITATIONS.md) — named public documents and the honesty rule.
4. This index — one-line theses, in stage order.
5. Any single draft. They stand alone. They do not stack into a treatment.

If you only open one file besides the guardrails, open
[`stage-01-how-to-read/01-what-this-folder-refuses.md`](stage-01-how-to-read/01-what-this-folder-refuses.md).

| `content/act-mindfulness-therapy-history/` | `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` | SLPWOW history lane |

A figure who appears in both (Linehan; Morita; Beck as a parent of MBCT) is treated here only in the ACT/mindfulness frame. Do not retell the whole life.

The live wowtherapies.com homepage, as last fetched, presents occupational, physical, and speech services in Southeast Arkansas. These essays are **heritage education**, not a quiet conversion of that site into a mental-health clinic.

## Voice and status

Human essay voice. `status: draft` on every file. The series is **staged** (seven argument stages) and **git-staged** as a proposal, not published as canon and not wired into COSMOS Core.

`voice: essay` in YAML is the writer pass. A later editor may mark `voice_check: edited` if this pack is aligned with the sister-pack convention. Neither value is a byline.
