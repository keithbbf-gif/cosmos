# Working bibliography — ACT / mindfulness therapy history

Works named across the pack. Article YAML does not duplicate this list in
full; each draft's Sources line names the subset it actually used. Wikipedia
is a lead, not a source. Prefer the edition named here.

Last pass: 2026-09-14.

Honesty rule: if I have not held the PDF or the book, I mark the claim as
circulating in secondary literature. A circulating claim is a historical
object. It is not a license to invent a page number.

## ACT, RFT, contextual behavioral science

- Zettle, Robert D., and Steven C. Hayes. "Dysfunctional Control by Client Verbal Behavior: The Context of Reason-Giving." *The Analysis of Verbal Behavior* 4 (1986): 30–38.
- Hayes, Steven C. "A Contextual Approach to Therapeutic Change." In *Psychotherapists in Clinical Practice*, edited by Neil S. Jacobson, 327–387. New York: Guilford, 1987.
- Hayes, Steven C., and Kelly G. Wilson. "Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: Altering the Verbal Support for Experiential Avoidance." *The Behavior Analyst* 17, no. 2 (1994): 289–303.
- Hayes, Steven C., Kirk Strosahl, and Kelly G. Wilson. *Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: An Experiential Approach to Behavior Change*. New York: Guilford, 1999.
- Hayes, Steven C., Dermot Barnes-Holmes, and Bryan Roche, eds. *Relational Frame Theory: A Post-Skinnerian Account of Human Language and Cognition*. New York: Kluwer Academic / Plenum, 2001.
- Hayes, Steven C. "Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, Relational Frame Theory, and the Third Wave of Behavioral and Cognitive Therapies." *Behavior Therapy* 35, no. 4 (2004): 639–665.
- Hayes, Steven C. *Get Out of Your Mind and Into Your Life*. Oakland: New Harbinger, 2005. Popular book; not a trial.
- Hayes, Steven C., Kirk D. Strosahl, and Kelly G. Wilson. *Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: The Process and Practice of Mindful Change*. 2nd ed. New York: Guilford, 2012. (Some catalogs list 2011.)
- Zettle, Robert D. "The Evolution of a Contextual Approach to Therapy: From Comprehensive Distancing to ACT." *International Journal of Behavioral Consultation and Therapy* 1, no. 2 (2005): 77–89.
- Association for Contextual Behavioral Science. "How Old Is ACT?" and "Organizational History of ACBS." contextualscience.org. Used for the 1981 protocol / 1991 name / 2005 membership dates as the association tells them.
- Wilson, Kelly G., D. Khorakiwala, and Steven C. Hayes. "Change in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy." Paper presented at the Association for Behavior Analysis, Atlanta, May 1991. (Title use of the name; I have not held the handout.)

- Powers, Mark B., Maarten B. Zum Vörde Sive Vörding, and Paul M. G. Emmelkamp. "Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: A Meta-Analytic Review." *Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics* 78, no. 2 (2009): 73–80.

- Langer, Ellen J. *Mindfulness*. Reading, MA: Addison-Wesley, 1989.
- Morita, Shōma. *Shinkeishitsu no hontai to ryōhō*. 1921 (often dated from 1919 clinical work). Cross-ref sister pack figure essay.

- Skinner, B. F. *Verbal Behavior*. New York: Appleton-Century-Crofts, 1957.
