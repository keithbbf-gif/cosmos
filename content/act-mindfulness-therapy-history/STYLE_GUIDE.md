# Style guide — WOW Therapies, ACT / mindfulness history series

Educational history for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com). Complementary
to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` (counseling and clinical heritage) and
to the SLPWOW speech-pathology series. Do not retell Van Riper here. Do not
retell the full Freud-to-Herman calendar here.

`voice: essay` means the writer draft aimed at this file. A later human editor
may tighten grammar and guardrails. Neither value is a byline.

## Who is speaking

A historically literate magazine writer. The reader may be a curious client, a
family member, a student, or a clinician. Do not talk down. Do not talk shop
as if the room were empty of patients.

WOW Therapies is a Southeast Arkansas practice brand. These essays are
**heritage education**, not a menu of services.

## Hard bans (fail the draft)

Do not use:

- "In today's rapidly evolving…"
- "It's important to note"
- "delve" / "delve into"
- "landscape" as a metaphor for a field
- "robust"
- "leverage"
- "unlock"
- "cutting-edge"
- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "In conclusion"
- "Moreover" / "Furthermore" stacks
- "Whether you're a … or a …"
- empty dualities ("not just X, but Y"; "both an art and a science")
- throat-clearing first paragraphs that delay the first fact
- three-beat synonym piles ("heal, grow, and thrive")
- "the father of…" unless you are quoting a myth, not endorsing it
- "gold-standard treatment" as marketing
- invented quotes
- AI-generated faces
- "try this at home," "self-treat," session scripts
- "holistic wellness journey," "let's dive in," "let's explore"

Also drop the cousins: "navigate," "tapestry," "plethora," "utilize,"
"harness," "elevate your," "the future of," "at the forefront,"
"empower," "in this article we will explore," "journey of healing" as filler.

## Do this instead

1. Open on a dated event, a room, a document, or a named quarrel. The first
   sentence should be checkable.
2. Mix sentence length. A short sentence after a long one.
3. Name the book, paper, hospital, or society. *Acceptance and Commitment
   Therapy* (1999). Teasdale et al., *JCCP*, 2000. IMS, Barre, 1975–76.
4. Mark uncertainty. `[CITE NEEDED]` or "historians disagree" beats a clean
   falsehood.
5. Put living people in public documents only.
6. Give non-European and women's work full essays, not a closing paragraph
   titled "diversity."
7. Stop when the piece has said the thing. Target 800–1,400 words. Cut the
   recap paragraph if it only restates the headings. Deepen with dated scenes,
   not with recap.

## Series voice, in one paragraph

Write as if *The New Yorker* and *Psychotherapy Networker* had to share a desk,
and the desk was in a small-city clinic that still answers its own phone. The
past is allowed to be strange. The present is not allowed to be sold.
