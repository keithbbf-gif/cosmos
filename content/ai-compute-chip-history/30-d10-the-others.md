# Draft 10 — the others, as a triad

Job: no directory. Three ways to show up. AMD as the other GPU.
One weird machine. Cloud-custom as Google's homework, copied.

---

A list of every AI chip company is a phone book. Here is a
cleaner object: three ways to show up.

**One.** Be a GPU with a twenty-year software church. NVIDIA is
the obvious parish. AMD is the other. It bought ATI in 2006 and
never left graphics. The Instinct cards are the data-center
pitch. MI300X, in 2023 and 2024, was the first AMD accelerator a
lot of AI people could point at without a qualifier: 192 GB of
HBM3, a real option when NVIDIA was sold out. Later Instincts
chased more memory and the new tiny number formats. ROCm, the
software, is the hard paragraph. It got better. The gravitational
well of `cuda` did not vanish. AMD often has a good
memory-per-dollar story and still has to talk you out of a
habit.

**Two.** Be an ASIC for a loop you already run, owned by someone
who already has the models. That is the TPU. Then it is AWS
Trainium and Inferentia, Microsoft Maia, Meta MTIA — hyperscalers
copying Google's 2013 homework. If you know your utilization, a
custom die can beat a rented GPU on cost and watts. You pay
forever in compiler engineers.

**Three.** Be a weird machine that is brilliant on a whiteboard
and has to grow a church from zero. A wafer instead of a rack
(Cerebras). A deterministic stream of tensors and a latency demo
that makes people blink (Groq, from people who had already
helped build a TPU). Lots of on-chip memory and a new
programming model (Graphcore, which had a serious late-2010s
run). Architecture is not enough. The graveyard is not a moral.
It is what happens when "faster at the loop" meets "please
rewrite your stack."

Phones have little neural engines too. Not every inference lives
in Oregon. Mention it so the map has an edge, then come back.
The industry's center of gravity in this history is still the
desk that became a rack.

Intel's long try (Larrabee, Xeon Phi, later Gaudi) fits the same
moral as the weird machines, with a twist: a fab and a CPU
franchise do not automatically get you a training default. Having
silicon is not the same as having a Tuesday-afternoon workflow.

If a new logo appears next year, slot it into the triad before
you memorize the SKU.
