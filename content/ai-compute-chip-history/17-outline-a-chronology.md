# Outline A — tell it in time

The safe outline. A reader who likes "and then" will be happy.
Risk: it becomes a museum audio guide.

1. **Cold open, 2012.** Two GTX 580s on a desk. Across the country,
   a CPU cluster looking for cats. Same hunger, different machines.
2. **Why a graphics chip.** Pixels as parallel math. 1999 and the
   noun GPU. Volume from games.
3. **The hack years.** Shaders as a disguise. Brook. The limits.
4. **CUDA, 2006.** A door, not a chip. C, then libraries.
5. **2012 again, slower.** What AlexNet actually says. What it does
   not say.
6. **The stack, 2014–16.** cuDNN, Caffe, TensorFlow, PyTorch.
7. **Google's other chip.** 2013 arithmetic, v1 systolic array,
   2016 keynote, the 2017 paper.
8. **2017 fork.** Transformer on P100s. TPU v2. Volta Tensor Cores.
9. **The card becomes a rack.** Ampere, Hopper, HBM, NVLink,
   scarcity.
10. **Everyone else, as a pattern.** AMD, custom cloud dies, the
    weird machines.
11. **2024–26 without a catalog.** Blackwell, Ironwood, Rubin, 8t/8i.
12. **Close.** The work still has the same shape. The bottleneck
    moved. You can now see it.

Strength: dates land. Weakness: the *ideas* (memory, software
church, subtracting features) show up late and may feel like
afterthoughts.
