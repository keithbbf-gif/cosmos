# Consensus outline

Keep B's brain. Keep A's scenes and dates. Refuse the museum. Refuse
the thinkpiece with no calendar.

**Working title:** *How a graphics chip learned to think*

**Spine:** The work (big regular matrix math) met a machine that
already did that for games. A language (CUDA, then libraries) made
the meeting stick. Once the loop was obvious, people carved it
special (TPU, Tensor Cores) and then spent a decade feeding it
(memory, wires, buildings).

**Moves:**

1. **Open on two desks, 2012.** AlexNet's two 580s. The CPU-cluster
   cat. Do not explain CUDA yet. Let the contrast do the work.
2. **Walk the shape.** A pixel and a matrix multiply, in English.
   Why a CPU is wasted here. One short 1990s beat: games paid for
   the factories.
3. **The costume, then the door.** GPGPU as folk practice. CUDA
   2006 as C on the 8800. Tesla the architecture vs Tesla the
   board. OpenCL in one honest paragraph.
4. **Return to 2012, with the paper in hand.** The numbers. The
   split across two cards. The sentence about waiting for faster
   GPUs.
5. **The stack.** cuDNN 2014, then TF and PyTorch. Teach: most
   people never meet CUDA, and that *is* CUDA winning.
6. **The other protagonist.** Why Google built v1. Systolic array
   as a parade. 92 TOPS, 40 W, a disk bay, a thin DDR3 pipe. The
   2017 paper's comparison, qualified.
7. **2017, the fork in the road.** Transformer on eight P100s. TPU
   v2 and bfloat16. Volta Tensor Cores. Same year, three public
   facts, one idea: the matrix is now a first-class citizen.
8. **The bottleneck moves.** HBM, NVLink, A100, H100/H200,
   scarcity. Power. Packaging. Do not become a supply-chain
   magazine.
9. **A triad, not a directory.** GPU church / house ASIC / weird
   machine. AMD as the other GPU. Groq or Cerebras as one weird
   example, not both if the page gets tired.
10. **The present, lightly.** Rack as chip (Blackwell). Inference
    as the new design center (Ironwood, H200, 8i). Train/serve
    split (8t/8i). Rubin named, not reviewed.
11. **Close.** You can still see 2006 and 2012 from here. The
    multipliers got cheaper. The hard part moved off the die and
    into the building. The work did not change its mind.

**Length target:** a long magazine piece. If a generation does not
teach a move, it is a timeline row, not a section.

**Title stays human.** No colons with "A History of." No
"Everything you need to know."
