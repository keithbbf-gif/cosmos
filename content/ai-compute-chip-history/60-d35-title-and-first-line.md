# Draft 35 — title and first line, last look

## Title candidates

1. How a graphics chip learned to think
2. Two cards and a disk bay
3. What the chip is waiting on
4. The loop, the door, the pantry
5. A public history of AI compute

1 is slightly cute and is the one a human would put on a
piece they were willing to hand a friend. 2 is an object
title; good for a blog CMS, colder. 3 is the closer, and
closers make greedy titles. 4 is a lecture slide. 5 is a
filing cabinet.

Keep 1.

## First line candidates

A. In 2012, the paper that made computer vision change its
   mind spends a few sentences on the hardware and shrugs.
B. Two video-game cards trained a neural net for a week and
   the field had to sit down.
C. Multiply, add, repeat — that is the history, if you want
   it in three verbs.

A is best. It has a paper, a year, and a gesture. B is a
movie trailer. C is a T-shirt.

The second line should be the cards, not a thesis. 50
already does this.

## Last line

"That answer is the history."

Do not add "full stop." Do not add a coda about 2027.
Do not thank the reader.
