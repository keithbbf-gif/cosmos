# Critic — voice

Read `44` aloud. Charter is `20-voice-charter.md`.

- The shrug. Best opening we have had.
- "They were explaining a wait."

- "If you pick 31 because 31 felt grown-up."
- Pantry, groceries, Tuesday. A little cute. Still human.
- The store question, now quieter.

## What sounds like a draft that has been to too many meetings

- "The wait became an industry." True, and a little poster.
  Keep, or make it plainer: "People spent the next decade
  buying that wait."
- "If you squint, a pixel and a neuron are cousins." I have
  now read this in six files. It is correct. It is also a
  sticker. Once.
- "That is the win. It is also a wall." Nice hinge. Do not
  echo it.
- "Nobody had to lose a religion." Cut. We already killed
  "church" and then smuggled it back in a robe.
- "The groceries got expensive." Works after pantry has been
  earned. If pantry stays, this stays. If pantry goes, this
  goes.

## Breath

Draft 24 is still a little even. The TPU section has a pulse.
The AMD/triad paragraph is a suitcase. Unpack or drop a
handle: phones/edge can be one clause, not a tour.

## First person

There is almost none. Good. Do not add "I love that paper"
back. The kitchen-table draft was a mood, not a destination.

## Title

*How a graphics chip learned to think* is slightly cute and
better than a colon. Keep. Do not subtitle it.

## Verdict

Human enough to publish after one more pass that cuts the
poster sentences and the religion. Do not "lively it up."
Lively is how we got corridor monsters.
