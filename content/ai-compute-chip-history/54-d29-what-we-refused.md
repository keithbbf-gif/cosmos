# Draft 29 — what we refused

Job: write the negative space so a later editor does not "help"
by filling it.

## Refused, because they are other essays

- A ranking of who to rent in 2026.
- A history of AI *algorithms* (backprop, ImageNet as a
  dataset story, the long winter). We touch them only where a
  chip is in the room.
- A biography of Jensen Huang, or of any founder.
- A supply-chain investigation (TSMC, CoWoS, export rules).
  One sentence of packaging-as-queue is enough.
- A climate article. Power is a building code here, not a
  sermon.
- A CUDA tutorial.
- A list of startups.

## Refused, because they are traps

- Cross-vendor peak FLOPS.
- "NVIDIA invented the GPU."
- "Google invented AI chips."
- AlphaGo as a mascot (easy to over-claim the chip's job).
- El Capitan taunts and other unit tricks.
- Any private house system, any filing, any invention story.

## Refused, because they made the prose worse

- Corridor monsters (kept out of the final).
- Sofa through a stairwell.
- Church / religion (one habit, then stop).
- "The work did not change its mind" as a last line.
- "Embarrass the field."
- A heading that says Close.

If a new draft wants to add a company, first steal a sentence
from an old one. Net word count should not rise.
