# Sora, Veo, Movie Gen: announced systems, published pages

OpenAI's Sora announcement (15 February 2024)
and the later technical report *Video
generation models as world simulators* are
public pages. Google's Lumiere paper
(arXiv:2401.12945, January 2024) and the Veo
product posts are public pages. Meta's Movie
Gen report (2024) is a public PDF with a
methods section that is more generous than a
teaser video. Runway's Gen-2/Gen-3 posts,
Pika's product notes, Luma's Dream Machine
posts sit in the same **announcement genre**.

This draft's rule is the Frozen/Flamingo/Imagen
rule: **a page is not a weight file**. You may
cite what the page claims — duration, native
audio, prompt adherence, a space-time patch
diagram — and you must mark what you cannot
verify. World-simulator language is a claim
about a research aspiration. It is not a
measurement you can rerun at home.

What belongs in a multimodal history anyway:

- **Video became the 2024 desire object** the
  way text-to-image was the 2022 desire
  object. Interfaces, press, and safety
  write-ups followed the desire.
- **The DiT story went public at product
  scale.** Sora's report talks about spacetime
  patches and transformers. That is a DiT-
  neighborhood sentence, not a proof that
  Peebles and Xie built Sora.
- **Evaluation lagged.** FVD and CLIP-sim on
  short clips do not capture "does this shot
  cut like a movie." The reports admit this
  more than Twitter does.

I refuse to rank these systems from screenshots.
I refuse to narrate unreleased internals. I
will say that by the end of 2024 a general
reader had seen enough official videos to
believe the task was real, and not enough
open checkpoints to treat the task as settled
infrastructure the way Stable Diffusion had
settled stills.

If you need a hinge: stills went public as a
file in 2022; video, in 2024, went public
mostly as a **demonstration and a report**.
Those are different kinds of public. This
folder keeps them in different drafts on
purpose.
