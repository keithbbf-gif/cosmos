# ImageBind: one embedding space as a public dare

Girdhar, El-Nouby, Liu, Singh, Alwala, Joulin,
and Misra's *ImageBind* (CVPR 2023,
arXiv:2305.05665) is a dare you can download.
Align images, text, audio, depth, thermal, and
IMU data into one embedding space, using
**image as the bind**. If audio-image pairs
exist and image-text pairs exist, audio can
meet text without a direct paired set. The
code and checkpoints went out.

This is CLIP's geometry with more doors. The
educational value is the **transitivity
claim**. Alignment can hop. Hopping is
powerful and sloppy. A sound that co-occurs
with a dog image sits near the word "dog."
That is sometimes meaning. That is sometimes
a video prior (dogs bark in the clips you
had).

Neighbors: LanguageBind, OmniBind, and a 2024
pile of "X-Bind" names. I will not catalog
them. ImageBind is the one a general reader
was pointed at. AudioLDM and Stable Audio
are generative cousins: they need a
shared-or-conditioned space too, but they
are denoisers, not only embedders.

Why it sits after Whisper and before video
diffusion:

- It makes **non-vision, non-text** modalities
  first-class in a vision lab's paper.
- It is a retrieval and alignment object,
  which this set has been tracking since
  DeViSE.
- Video work needs audio whether or not the
  demo is silent. A shared space is one way
  people tried to get there without a full
  video VLM.

I do not treat six-modality embedding as
"understanding the world." I treat it as a
**public coordinate system**. Coordinate
systems leak the data they were built from.
They also let you build a demo in a weekend
that looks like a new sense. Both facts can
live on one page.
