# Writing this history too early

The last draft is a list of things I do not know.

I do not know what is in WIT. I do not know what is in the closed mixes behind DALL·E 2, Imagen, GPT-4V, Gemini, Sora, Midjourney. I have headline numbers, category sentences, and model cards written by interested parties. A history of supervision that cannot name the supervision is a history of *behavior*. Behavior is not nothing. It is not provenance.

I do not know which 2024–2026 product stacks are one model and which are five rooms behind an API. Native, in public, is a word. I have tried to treat it as a word.

I do not know how to count influence. CLIP’s weights circulated; ALIGN’s ideas circulated. Citation graphs measure a vice. GitHub stars measure a vice. Waitlist screenshots measure a vice. I used all three vices and tried to name them.

I do not know the first person who wrote `trending on ArtStation` into a prompt with intent. Folk practice has no first author. If I invented one I would be doing the thing this folder forbids.

I do not know whether diffusion is the lasting physics of pictures. The token school has already come back in product language. A 2030 reader may find these 2022-heavy drafts quaint. Good. Quaint means we dated our claims.

I do not know — and I want this in capital letters in my own head — **whether any sentence in this folder accidentally sounds like a method I am proposing.** It is not. There is no new joint here. There is no architecture. There is no dataset. If a sentence slipped into the future tense of a recipe, it is a failure of the draft. Rewrite it as a description of a paper that already exists, or delete it.

What I think I *do* know, as a reading, labeled:

The hinge is not a model. It is a set of **public joints**. A contrastive pair at web scale. A denoising clock with a sentence and a scale. A latent room a consumer GPU can walk. A frozen speaker with glue. A file you can fork. Two publics that do not share a government. A leftover channel — documents, alt-text, spectrograms — that kept being the actual job while we talked about astronauts.

2021 is CLIP and a discrete DALL·E and a paper that is not yet a culture. 2022 is the culture: waitlists, weights, Discord, LAION, Flamingo without a chat box. 2023 is the assistant that looks, and the cheap projector, and ControlNet, and Whisper already on disk. 2024 is video as a medium people argue about. The years will keep going. The joints will get renamed. The leftovers will not go away.

## A public reading list (primary, not a zoo)

If you only read ten things, read these, in something like this order, as *documents*, not as scripture:

1. Radford et al., CLIP (paper + model card) — 2021  
2. Ramesh et al., DALL·E 1 — 2021  
3. Ho, Jain, Abbeel, DDPM — 2020  
4. Ho and Salimans, classifier-free guidance — 2021  
5. Rombach et al., latent diffusion — 2022  
6. Ramesh et al., unCLIP / DALL·E 2 — 2022  
7. Saharia et al., Imagen — 2022  
8. Alayrac et al., Flamingo — 2022  
9. Liu et al., LLaVA — 2023  
10. Stability AI, 10 August and 22 August 2022 posts; OpenAI GPT-4V system card, 25 September 2023  

Then, if you still have time: DeViSE (so you do not invent a virgin birth), the LAION-5B paper (so you do not say “the internet” and sit down), Zhang et al. ControlNet, the Whisper paper, ImageBind, Gato (as a mood), Gemini’s December 2023 report (as a claim).

Do not read this folder instead of those. This folder is a person talking after those.

## What a later pass should fix

- Dates that I hedged because I had a blog timestamp and not a PDF stamp. Prefer the PDF.  
- Missing public objects a specialist will notice (CogView, CoCa, Florence-2, IDEFICS, Qwen-VL, Fuyu, Kosmos, Chameleon, the document-VLM line). I left them out to keep a spine, not to deny they exist. A stage-07 expansion could hold them without breaking the freeze-and-glue story.  
- City B markets and UIs I treated as a class. A historian of tools should name more names, carefully, without advertising.  
- Legal outcomes after this draft’s date. Update events. Do not update into a brief.

## Last human sentence

I watched this decade as a civilian with a library card, which is what a public history is allowed to be. The pretty pictures were the bait. The joint was the animal. If you only remember the bait, you will be surprised every time a model looks at a PDF and it feels like magic. It is not magic. It is a leftover channel we had the word for, and then, for a few years, we used the word to mean a product.
