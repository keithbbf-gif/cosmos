# Consent, artists, and the public fight over the crawl

The 22 August 2022 weight dump did not only
create tutorials. It created a **constituency
that had not been in the CVPR hallway**:
artists who recognized their styles, people
who found their faces, lawyers who found a
caption. This draft is not a brief. It is a
record of public objects a history has to
name or it is advertising.

Named public objects, not a complete docket:

- **Artist statements and collective actions**
  through 2022–2023, including class actions
  later filed in the United States against
  several image-generation companies. Filings
  are public. Outcomes move. Date them if
  you cite them.
- **Have I Been Trained / Spawning-style
  opt-out tools** and the argument about
  whether opt-out after a crawl is consent.
  The tools are public. The argument is
  public. Neither is a verdict.
- **LAION-5B's 2023 withdrawal** after
  reports of illegal sexual imagery of
  minors in the index. A commons that
  cannot be cleaned in time is not only a
  research inconvenience.
- **RAIL licenses and platform bans.**
  Discord, stock sites, and later law
  (the EU AI Act's transparency talk;
  national implementations that were still
  landing as of 2025–2026) tried to put
  rails on a file that already moved.

CLIP's 2021 paper already had a broader-
impacts section. Conceptual Captions already
knew alt-text was not a contract with the
photographed person. The difference after
August 2022 is **distribution**. A method
plus a GPU plus a Discord is a different
moral object than a PDF.

This set's novelty fence still holds. I am
not proposing a filter. I am not proposing
a statute. I am saying that a public
history of multimodal AI that ends at FID
is a history of a contest, not of a
technology that left the lab. The crawl
was always other people's pictures. The
open file made that sentence ordinary.

If you are editing this folder later, do
not "balance" this draft with a paragraph
about innovation as if they canceled. They
do not cancel. They sit on the same
timeline. That is the education.
