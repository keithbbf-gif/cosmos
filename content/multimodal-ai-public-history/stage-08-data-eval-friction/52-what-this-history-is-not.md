# What this history is not

This last numbered piece is a fence, like
`NOVELTY.md`, in essay form.

It is **not a complete catalog**. I left out
good papers on purpose: stacked cross-
attention VQA models, most GAN text-to-image
work (Reed 2016, StackGAN, AttnGAN),
StyleCLIP, a hundred retrieval papers,
medical VLMs, robotics VLMs, most
non-English datasets. Leaving out is how a
spine stays a spine. An editor can add a
stage. An editor should not pretend fifty-
two files are the field.

It is **not a ranking of labs**. OpenAI,
Google, DeepMind, Stability, Runway,
Meta, Mistral, academic groups — they
appear when they published an object this
spine needed. Absence is not a judgment.

It is **not a reconstruction of closed
systems**. Imagen, Flamingo, DALL·E 2,
Sora, and the 2023–2024 assistant cards
are cited as pages. Pages are not
training runs.

It is **not a recipe book**. You will not
find a learning rate. You will find a
citation you can open.

It is **not a novelty opinion** and not
legal advice. Public recap is the job.

It is **not a history of any private
software project, house tool, or unfiled
method**. If a paragraph started to sound
like one, it failed the stage.

It is **not year-zero mythology**. 2015
captioning, 2019 BERT-on-boxes, 2020
DDPM, 2021 CLIP, 2022 latent weights,
2023 instruction VLMs, 2024 video
reports — those are dates. They are not
a proof that intelligence arrived. They
are a proof that **interfaces** arrived:
English to a classifier, English to a
denoiser, a photo to a chat box, a still
to a two-second loop.

If you only keep three sentences from
the set, keep these.

First: CLIP is a matching game that
became a part.

Second: Stable Diffusion is a latent
diffusion paper plus a 22 August 2022
file.

Third: visual-language assistants are
an old desire (pixels to words) with a
new chat template and a borrowed LM.

Everything else in this folder is
support, neighbor, or warning. Support
and warning are the parts that usually
get edited out. That is why they are
here, staged, and not published until a
human says so.
