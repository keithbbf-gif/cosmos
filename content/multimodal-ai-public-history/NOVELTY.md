# Novelty fence — public multimodal AI history

This folder is a **public-history** box. It exists so a later public page
can talk about vision-language models and public diffusion systems without
touching anything that is still private or unfiled.

## In

- Published papers and preprints with arXiv or venue identifiers
  (for example Radford et al., 2021, arXiv:2103.00020; Rombach et al.,
  CVPR 2022, arXiv:2112.10752).
- Official lab or company blog posts with datelines (OpenAI CLIP,
  5 January 2021; Stability AI public-release note, 22 August 2022).
- Public code and weight repositories (openai/CLIP; CompVis/stable-diffusion;
  mlfoundations/open_clip; haotian-liu/LLaVA).
- Dataset and benchmark cards that were released on purpose
  (COCO, VQAv2, Conceptual Captions, LAION-5B blog, DataComp).
- Widely reported product system cards and technical reports
  (GPT-4V system card, 25 September 2023) treated as **speech acts**,
  not as leaked internals.
- Issued patents and textbooks only when they are already public
  and the draft needs a date. This set almost never needs them.

## Out

- Any private system, service, ledger, occupancy scheme, or product
  architecture.
- Any unfiled method, combination, or implementation detail.
- Any "we built" claim that is not already in the public record.
- Customer data, credentials, spend, live paths.
- Quotes long enough to substitute for the original paper. Summarize.
- House names, internal code names, and unpublished stacks.

## Voice of uncertainty

If a date or attribution is fuzzy in the public record, the draft says
so. It does not invent a precise statistic to sound sure. Interpretation
is labeled as interpretation. A demo video is not a paper. A paper is
not a weight file. A weight file is not a license you have read.

## Closed weights, public methods

Several important systems in this history published a method and withheld
the checkpoint (Imagen, Parti, Flamingo, early DALL·E 2). The drafts
treat that split as a **public fact**. They do not reconstruct a closed
recipe from rumor. They do not pretend a blog screenshot is a training
run.

## Microsoft's 2023 multimodal LM paper

Huang, Peng, and colleagues published *Language Is Not All You Need*
(arXiv:2302.14045, February 2023). That paper is public. This set may
cite it by title and identifier. It does not borrow the paper's product
nickname, so a later automated novelty scan is not confused by a
look-alike house word.

## Not a novelty opinion

These drafts are not legal advice and not a novelty opinion on anyone's
filings. They retell a public field. That is the whole job.
