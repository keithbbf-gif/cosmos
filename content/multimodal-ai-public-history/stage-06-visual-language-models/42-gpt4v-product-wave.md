# GPT-4V and the 2023 product wave, as public speech

OpenAI's GPT-4V(ision) system card (25 September
2023) is a primary page. It describes evaluated
capabilities and refused capabilities: medical
advice caution, unreleased-people identification
limits, the usual and the specific. Gemini 1.0
(6 December 2023 blog and technical report) and
Claude 3 (March 2024 product posts) are later
public claims that vision is inside a general
assistant. This draft treats those documents as
**speech acts**, not as leaked architectures.

What a history can take from a system card:

- **A date when a mass audience met the task.**
  Screenshot questions, meme reading, whiteboard
  photos. Captioning left the COCO server.
- **A named evaluation culture** inside the
  vendor: red teams, policy, "we still fail at
  X." Failures written down by the vendor are
  still public evidence.
- **A dependency for open work.** LLaVA's
  teacher was GPT-4. Later papers distill
  closed VLMs into open ones. The product wave
  is in the open models' family tree even when
  the weights never move.

What a history must not take:

- Parameter counts that were not published.
- Training-set stories that were not published.
- A ranking of vendors dressed as a timeline.
- The idea that a chat screenshot is a
  benchmark.

I keep this draft short on purpose. Product
pages update. Cards get superseded. The
durable fact is the **interface**: a user
drops an image into the same box as a
sentence, in a consumer app, in 2023, and
expects a paragraph back. Academic VLMs had
been trying to earn that interface for a
decade. The product wave made the interface
ordinary, then the ordinary became the
evaluation (MMMU, MMBench, "does it read my
slide deck").

Native multimodality talk in 2024 (GPT-4o,
18 May 2024; later Gemini and others)
belongs here as a sequel sentence, not as a
new religion. "Native" is a vendor word.
The public object is still a card, a blog,
and a behavior. Until a method paper or a
weight file arrives, stay with those.
