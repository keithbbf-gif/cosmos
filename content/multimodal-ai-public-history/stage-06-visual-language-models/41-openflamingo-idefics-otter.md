# OpenFlamingo, IDEFICS, Otter: the open copies as scholarship

Awadalla, Gao, Gardner, Hessel, Hanafy, Zhu, Marra,
Cao, Chen, Dolan, et al.'s OpenFlamingo (2023) is
an explicit public attempt to **reimplement the
Flamingo diagram** with open LMs and open data.
Laurençon, Saulnier, Tronchon, Bekman, Singh,
Lozhkov, et al.'s IDEFICS (Hugging Face, 2023)
trains a Flamingo-like model on OBELICS, an
interleaved document dataset they also released.
Li, Li, Cho, Wu, Bansal, and Nenkova's Otter
(2023) instruction-tunes an OpenFlamingo stack.

These are not footnotes. They are how a closed
2022 paper became a 2023 training story other
people could run. The quality gap versus the
original Flamingo numbers was discussed in the
open. Discussing the gap is the scholarship.
Pretending the copy erased the original is
fandom. Pretending the copy did not matter is
prestige bias.

What the copies added that the original paper
could not:

- **Named public interleaved data** (OBELICS,
  Multimodal C4 / MMC4). Interleaving stopped
  being a DeepMind-internal noun.
- **A path to instruction following** on the
  Flamingo spine, not only on the LLaVA spine.
  Otter is that path's named object.
- **Engineering notes.** Tokenizers, stability
  of gated xattn, which frozen LM would even
  load. Those notes live in repos and reports.
  They are part of multimodal history.

I group three names so a reader sees a
**cluster**, not a winner. 2023 was a cluster
year: everyone connected an open LM to an
image encoder with the parts they could get.
Qwen-VL, CogVLM, InternVL, and others belong
in the next draft as a later, larger wave.
This draft is the Flamingo-shaped wave.

A caution about "open." Some weights were
research-only. Some data dumps had the same
consent weather as LAION. Copying an
architecture does not copy a license you
like. Read the cards. This set will keep
saying that until it is boring. Boring is
safer than a myth of a frictionless commons.
