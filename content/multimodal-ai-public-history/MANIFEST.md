## Stage map

1. **Seeing and saying** — ImageNet on-ramp, DeViSE, captioning, VQA, web captions, object features.
2. **Two-stream pretraining** — ViT, ViLBERT family, tags, contrastive vision without language.
3. **CLIP and ALIGN** — the matching game, locked WIT, OpenCLIP, LAION, frozen encoders.
4. **Diffusion goes public** — DDPM, CFG, DALL·E/GLIDE, latent diffusion, 22 August 2022.
5. **Open image tools** — LoRA, ControlNet, InstructPix2Pix, SDXL, DiT, flow, later lines.
6. **Visual language models** — Frozen, Flamingo, BLIP-2, LLaVA, copies, product cards, SAM.
7. **Video, audio, unified** — Whisper, ImageBind, runnable video, announced video, token unification.
8. **Data, evals, friction** — rulers, consent fights, non-claims.
