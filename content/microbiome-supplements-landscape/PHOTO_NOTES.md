# Photo notes

No fake lab. No white-coat stock presented as our facility. No stool-kit dashboard screenshot with a real customer's taxa. No COA with a live lot number you do not have the right to show.

These drafts are text-first. Images, if any, are diagrams or clearly labeled public-domain science figures.

## Allowed

- Original schematics: three-word definition, 16S vs shotgun, supplement vs LBP, eight-question file.
- Wikimedia / NIH / CDC / FDA public-domain figures. Keep the license line under the image.
- Typeset tables (GRADE-style *descriptions*, CFU vs AFU, name-change examples).
- Historical portraits already in the public domain (Metchnikoff) with a caption that does not imply endorsement.

## Banned

- AI-generated "our anaerobic chamber in [city]."
- Happy-gut mascots, butterfly intestines, "before/after belly" photos.
- uBiome-style wellness dashboards reconstructed from memory.
- Trademarked certification marks (USP, NSF) unless a named SKU is actually in the program.
- Clinical scenes (NICU, colonoscopy, FMT administration) presented as brand photography.
