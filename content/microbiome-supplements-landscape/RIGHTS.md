## Original SVG (`assets/`)

Every file under `assets/` is an **original typeset diagram** created for this educational pack (2026). None are scans of real certificates, stool-kit dashboards, or supplier COAs. Fictional field labels use bracket placeholders only.

## Photographs and raster art

This graphics pass ships **no photographs** and **no AI-generated faces or people**. Per `PHOTO_NOTES.md`, do not add fake lab interiors, white-coat stock, happy-gut mascots, or clinical scenes presented as brand photography.

## Trademarks

USP, NSF, Informed Sport, AGA, and retailer quality marks are trademarks. Diagrams avoid logo collages. Prose that names programs must follow each program’s artwork rules. Do not reproduce AGA guideline artwork; use original schematics only (draft 10).

## Claims

Figures are **methods and file literacy**, not efficacy charts. Captions state schematic / qualitative. No fake effect-size bars, no before/after bodies, no implied disease outcomes for dietary supplements.
