# Editorial calendar — microbiome / probiotic research literacy

Forty-four drafts. Status: `draft`. Nothing here is cleared for a public product page. A later editor pass may flip `voice_check` to `edited`. Until then, keep `human`.

Quality bar: evidence-careful longform — not a 40-count of SEO stubs. Target ≥1,000 words of body copy each. Staged waves below are the only import order (`WP_IMPORT.md`).

All files live under `content/microbiome-supplements-landscape/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`.

The folder name is a path. Essays talk about a *research map*, a *file*, a *record* — not a "landscape."

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is the human pace. Forty-four drafts at that rate is a long shelf. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress in a week.

| Wave | Weeks (suggested) | Pieces | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — how the science is built | 1–8 | 01–08 | Definitions, history, methods, naming before any organism |
| 2 — how to read a trial | 9–16 | 09–15 | GRADE, animals, CFU math, "clinically studied" |
| 3 — organisms people sell | 17–24 | 16–23 | Teaching strains; next-gen names; dead vs live |
| 4 — adjacent words and food | 25–32 | 24–30 | Pre-/syn-/postbiotic; fermented food; diet |
| 5 — tests, myths, hospital rooms | 33–40 | 31–37 | Kits, uBiome, gut-brain, FMT/LBP as drugs |
| 6 — law, QC, a buyer's file | 41–48 | 38–44 | DSHEA vs LBP; EFSA/FOSHU; COA; eight questions |

Wave 1 is the frame. If only six pieces ever ship, ship 01, 08, 10, 37, 38, 44.

## Reading order (human)

Start with 01 (the three load-bearing words), then 08 (the 2020 name split), then 10 (AGA as a *document*), then 37–38 (hospital/drug vs aisle). Organism pieces (16–23) are optional deep reads. Do not open with gut-brain (33) or weight (34).
