In 2014, David, Maurice, Carmody, and colleagues published a *Nature* paper (PMID 24336217) that still gets flattened into "you are what you eat." What they did was more precise: they put adults on short-term, controlled diets — plant-based versus animal-based — and watched stool communities move, fast, and then move back. The community is not a marble bust. It is a working population responding to incoming carbohydrate, fat, and fiber. Sonnenburg and Sonnenburg, the same year in *Cell Metabolism* (PMID 25156449), framed a longer worry: diets low in microbiota-accessible carbohydrates starve a function, not a lifestyle brand.

## What the paper licenses

It licenses humility about single-time-point kits (draft 04). If a week of food can move the chart, your red badge is partly last Tuesday’s lunch. It licenses skepticism about "permanent repopulation" from a capsule. It licenses diet as a *variable* in any live-microbe trial (draft 09): if both arms got fiber advice, you have a mixed intervention.

It does not license a moral ranking of the two experimental menus. Those menus were instruments. They were not Mediterranean-versus-carnivore content. It does not license a stool-kit grocery list as individualized medicine (draft 31). It does not license "food is the real probiotic." Food is food. Probiotic is a defined live object (drafts 01 and 27).

## Capsules still have a job-shaped hole

A named isolate at a named dose can be studied for a named endpoint when diet is the background weather, not the intervention. That is a narrower tool. Narrow is not useless. It is honest. A founder who claims their blend "works like a plant-based diet" has compared a hopper to a week of controlled meals. David et al. did not run that arm.

Synbiotics and prebiotics (drafts 24–25) sit between: substrates without a full menu. They still need their own files. They are not David-in-a-sachet unless the sachet was the intervention in a paper.

## What "moved" means

Taxa shifted. Gene-content inferences shifted. Some functions are redundant (draft 03), so a taxon chart can look dramatic while a pathway looks bored. If a brand only shows the dramatic chart, ask for the function. If they only show a Shannon bump, read draft 07.

Antibiotics are a different shove. Diet is not an antibiotic. Do not write "reset after your prescription" as a menu or a SKU (draft 06).

## House style

No meal plans. No "eat this to raise *Akkermansia*." No implied weight story (draft 34). Cite David 2014 when you want the speed of diet. Cite Sonnenburg when you want the MAC frame. Cite Hill when someone tries to make a salad a probiotic. Wave 4 ends here. Wave 5 is the room where kits, scandals, and hospital science try to become products — and where this pack is least gentle.

## What David 2014 did not run

They did not run a 15-strain bottle against a week of controlled meals. They did not rank Mediterranean versus carnivore as content. They used two extreme, short-term menus as instruments. Flattening the paper into "you are what you eat" loses the speed, the reversibility, and the methods. Flattening it into "food is the real probiotic" loses Hill 2014 (drafts 01 and 27).

A founder who claims their blend "works like a plant-based diet" has compared a hopper to a controlled menu. Cite the paper or drop the comparison.

## Weather versus intervention

In a live-microbe trial, diet is usually weather. If the protocol made it an intervention — fiber advice to both arms, a provided menu — write that down. You may have a mixed object (draft 09). David's menus were instruments, not content verticals. Do not rank eating styles from that paper. Do not say a blend works like a plant-based week.

Kits that treat a Tuesday chart as a statue should be sent here and to draft 04. Recovery after antibiotics is not a menu and not a SKU (draft 06). Narrow isolate files can still exist. They are narrower than a week of food. Narrow is honest.

## One more refusal

A kit company will cite David 2014 as if it authorized a weekly PDF and a grocery list. The paper authorized humility about statues and speed. It did not authorize a costume (draft 31). A blend company will cite it as if a hopper were a menu. It was not. Keep the two dated 2014 papers as methods facts. Keep Hill 2014 as the reason a salad is not a probiotic. Then stop.
