This essay is about a public enforcement story and a methods moral. It is not a claim about any current company’s guilt.

uBiome offered consumers and, later, billers a way to feel like they were holding the Human Microbiome Project in a PDF. The company entered public view as a citizen-science-ish stool kit and left public view through criminal charges, civil actions, and bankruptcy. The charging documents are on government websites. This pack will not recap them as true-crime. It will take the part that still sits on other people’s landing pages: a pretty report can get ahead of the assay, the intended use, and the billing code.

## What we can say from public records

We can say the United States brought cases. We can say the SEC brought a case. We can say the company ceased to be the future of consumer sequencing. We cannot, in this draft, invent the exact scheme as if we had the trial transcript in a footnote. Editors who want numbers should paste them from the PDFs and date them. `[CITE NEEDED]` on any dollar figure until that paste happens.

We can say, as a methods fact independent of any verdict, that 16S wellness reports were always a thin instrument (drafts 04 and 31). A thin instrument plus insurance billing plus a medical costume is how a startup becomes a case study.

## The science moral, which does not require a villain

HMP did not publish a CPT code (draft 03). A relative-abundance table does not become a diagnostic when the UI gets a better font. If clinicians did not order a test for a recognized indication, the report’s medical posture was already a choice. If consumers were told they would learn something clinical, the speech was already a claim.

Other companies will keep selling kits. Most will not repeat this story’s particulars. They can still repeat the structure: beauty, subscription, house products, a reference range that is really a marketing database. Draft 31 is the general rule. This essay is the expensive illustration.

## What not to do with the story

Do not use it to smear every academic sequencer. Do not use it to smear every CLIA lab. Do not use it as a joke about people who bought a kit in 2017. Do not reconstruct a uBiome-style dashboard from memory (PHOTO_NOTES). Do not treat "we are not uBiome" as a substantiation file for a new test.

## What a founder should put in a file instead

Intended use in one sentence that a regulator can read without a decoder. An assay that matches that sentence. No cart. If there is a cart, a firewall and a separate SKU file. A willingness to be a food-category company if that is what they are. The pretty report is optional. It is also how this category keeps getting into the newspaper.
