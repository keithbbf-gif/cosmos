Live-microbe trials are expensive, perishable, and often paid for by the house that wants to sell the isolate. That fact is ordinary. It is not, by itself, a verdict. What turns funding into fog is a set of habits the rest of clinical research already learned to distrust: strain identity withheld as "proprietary," outcomes that appear after unblinding, abstracts that never become papers, and a graveyard of quiet negatives that never make the meta-analysis. AGA's 2020 GRADE tables spend their certainty ratings on that weather (draft 10). A sales deck spends its certainty ratings on the one abstract that landed.

## What sponsorship can still buy honestly

A house can pay for a registered, adequately powered, strain-identified trial in a named matrix with a locked primary endpoint and a deposit number in the methods. Independent labs can do the CFU work. Academic authors can take the data where they go, including to a null. That file is usable. Hill 2014 and Binda 2020 do not require poverty. They require a human study of the object you sell.

A house can also fund methods papers — stability, identity, resistome — that never try to become a disease story. Those are under-loved and more useful to a COA than a mood trial in 24 students.

## The proprietary-strain trick

If the methods will not name the isolate, no one can tell whether the next paper is the same object. Meta-analyses then pool "L. acidophilus" as if it were a drug with one IND. After Zheng 2020, that pool is a nomenclatural joke as well as a scientific one (draft 08). Proprietary is a commercial choice. It is also how evidence dies at the species level.

Some journals now push for strain IDs. Some still accept "a probiotic mixture." When you see the mixture, ask whether anyone will ever be able to buy *that* mixture. If the formula changed after the trial, the paper is biography.

## Gardens of secondary scores

Small studies with fifteen symptom subscales will find a p-value. Pre-specification is the fence. If the fence went up after the data arrived, you are looking at a garden. FTC 2022 cares about the claim as a reasonable consumer reads it. "Clinically shown" under a score nobody on the carton can name is the consumer-facing version of the garden.

Multiplicity is not unique to this category. The category is just unusually willing to put the garden on a pouch.

## The missing negative

Null trials stall. They become conference posters. They become "we learned a lot" slides. They do not become PubMed IDs. Meta-analyses that do not search registries will over-cheer. Cochrane-style reviews try to hunt the missing; they still cannot resurrect a study that was never registered. When a brand says "eleven studies support," ask how many were run. ## How this pack cites anyway

We name papers and we flag weather. We do not perform a ritual denunciation of every industry author. We do not treat an NIH-funded association study as morally purer if it also over-claims. We prefer documents that can lose: GRADE, Cochrane, FDA approval letters, FTC guidance. Those documents are not sacred. They are structured to survive disagreement.

If you are a founder reading this for a file: register, name the strain, lock the endpoint, publish the null. That is cheaper than a letter and cheaper than a category that no longer believes itself. If you are an editor: do not let a deck's "eleven studies" into a draft without the graveyard sentence.

The next two essays (CFU math, end of shelf) are about a different missing: the cells that were counted on a good day and not on the day the person swallowed them. Bias and viability are cousins. Both make a paper about something other than the bottle.

## How to write the funding line without theater

"This trial was paid for by the isolate's owner. The strain is named. The primary endpoint was locked. The null would have been publishable." That is a grown-up sentence. "Independent science" as a banner over a house white paper is not. "NIH-funded" over an association study that then sells a SKU is not independence. Money is one weather. Design is another. Read both.

Ghost authorship and "editorial assistance" from a house shop belong in the same weather report. If you cannot tell who chose the endpoint, you cannot tell whether the garden was planted on purpose.

## Meta-analysis as a weather instrument

Pooled estimates inherit the graveyard. If the search missed registries, the pool is sunnier than the field. If the pool treats species as a drug, the pool is a nomenclatural joke (draft 08). GRADE's certainty language is how a society admits weather. A sales deck's "eleven studies" is how a house denies it.

When you want a number for a specific isolate, hunt the denominator: how many studies were run, not how many were printed. If you cannot find the denominator, do not print the numerator as a personality.
