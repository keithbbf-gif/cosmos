**Disclaimer.** Educational only. Not medical advice. Dietary supplements are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease (DSHEA; 21 U.S.C. § 321(ff); 21 CFR 101.93). This is not legal advice and not a notification strategy.

DSHEA assumed dietary ingredients that were already in the food supply, and it built a notification path for **new** dietary ingredients (21 U.S.C. § 350b). GRAS (21 CFR 170) is a separate food-ingredient conclusion: generally recognized, among qualified experts, as safe under the conditions of intended use. Live microbes break both conversations in ordinary ways. What is the article? The species? The strain? The killed version? The mix? A history of yogurt cultures is not a history of a next-gen anaerobe isolated in 2018 (draft 20). A GRAS notice for a live isolate is not a GRAS notice for its pasteurized twin (drafts 21 and 23).

## History of use is not a genus privilege

*S. thermophilus* in yogurt is a food-use story (draft 27). GG as a later isolate has a different biography (draft 16). *Weizmannia* in a tub is not yogurt (draft 18). "Lactobacilli have been eaten for centuries" is the Metchnikoff dodge at the regulatory desk (draft 02). After Zheng 2020, even the genus word is a moving target (draft 08).

If the isolate is new, counsel will talk about NDI notification, about whether a GRAS conclusion is being taken, and about whether the intended use is even a food use. This pack will not pick their strategy. It will say the conversation has to happen *before* the Amazon listing, not after a warning letter.

## Mixes and swaps

A notified mix is not a license to swap a strain and keep the letter (draft 22). A supplier change is a new article if identity changed. Change control is how you stay in the file you think you are in.

## Kill steps and novel foods

Inactivated organisms can be "new" even when the live parent was old. Europe’s novel-food path has already met some of these. The US path is not a copy-paste. `[VERIFY]` the dated conclusion for a named article. Do not print "GRAS probiotic" on a dead powder.

## Disease intended use exits the food building

If the sentences are disease sentences, GRAS/NDI will not save you. You are in draft 38’s other box. Next-gen organisms aimed at IBD or metabolic disease are often LBP conversations wearing a supplement deck. The 2016 guidance is the adult reading.

## House style

No "self-affirmed GRAS" as a halo without a dated notice you can point to. No "NDI pending" on a PDP. No century-of-use sentence for a 2018 isolate. Ask: what is the article, in which form, for which use, in which year. Draft 41 stays in the safety file: resistance genes, QPS, and the genome the safety file should include before the photoshoot.

## Article of commerce, said slowly

Live isolate A with a yogurt history. Isolate A's pasteurized twin. Isolate B from 2018 with no food history. A mix that swapped a line last Tuesday. A spore sold as soil. These are different articles. History of use is not a genus privilege (drafts 02, 08, 18, 27). A GRAS notice for one is not a notice for the others. NDI notification, if it is the path, is about the article you actually pour.

Counsel picks the strategy. This pack says the conversation happens before the Amazon listing. "Self-affirmed GRAS" as a halo without a dated notice is fog. "NDI pending" on a PDP is not a consumer sentence.

## Mixes, kills, disease exits

A notified mix is not a license to swap (draft 22). A kill step can reset novelty (draft 23). Disease sentences exit the food building (draft 38). Next-gen anaerobes aimed at IBD are often LBP conversations wearing a supplement deck (draft 20). The 2016 guidance is the adult reading.

## A pre-listing desk check

What is the article — isolate, mix, killed, spore. Is there a dated food-use history of *that* article. Is anyone taking a GRAS conclusion, and where is the notice. Is an NDI notification in play. Did a supplier swap change the article (draft 22). Did a kill step reset novelty (draft 23). Are the website sentences still food sentences (draft 38). If the isolate is 2018 and the copy says "centuries," delete the century (draft 02).
