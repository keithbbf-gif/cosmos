**Disclaimer.** Educational only. Not medical advice. Dietary supplements are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease (DSHEA; 21 U.S.C. § 321(ff); 21 CFR 101.93). Obesity and diabetes are diseases. This essay will not treat them and will not sell a lean story.

Turnbaugh 2006 (PMID 17183312) is the cage people still cite when they want a microbiome theory of fat (draft 11). Human cohorts then filled journals with taxon-versus-BMI plots. Some of those plots replicate in some populations. Some do not. Diet, drugs, geography, and the simple fact that people with different bodies eat differently all sit in the residual. A plot is not a mechanism. A mechanism in a mouse is not a SKU.

## What the papers licensed

They licensed a research program: bile acids, energy harvest, bile-salt hydrolase, incretin talk, *Akkermansia* (draft 21), fecal transfers in experimental systems. They licensed humility about "calories in, calories out" as a complete biological sentence — humility, not a replacement slogan. They did not license "obesity bacteria." They did not license an enterotype as a diet personality (draft 05). They did not license a kit that tells you you have a "thrifty microbiome."

## The human files that people treat as bigger than they are

Depommier 2019 is a proof-of-concept exploratory study with live and pasteurized arms. Read it as that. Do not reprint metabolic endpoints on a landing page. Other isolates have small glycemic or waist-adjacent trials. Heterogeneous, often short, often industry-adjacent. This pack will not build a league table that a founder could screenshot as a catalog.

Zeevi et al., 2015 (*Cell*; PMID 26590418), on personalized glycemic prediction, is a different object: meals and glucose, with microbiota as one feature among many. Productization and replication have been argued since. `[VERIFY]` before anyone treats it as a settled engine for a supplement stack. It is not a probiotic trial.

## Intended use, said bluntly

"Supports healthy metabolism" next to a lean-microbe story is a wink at weight. FTC reads winks. "Supports healthy glucose metabolism" next to diabetes nouns is a disease claim. Structure/function space, if it exists, is narrower and needs a product-level file — not Turnbaugh, not a 16S bar, not a cousin isolate.

Berberine and other non-live "Ozempic" copy from the sister supplements pack is a different aisle and a different letter waiting to happen. Do not drag live microbes into that dialect.

## One more refusal

A "lean microbiome" landing page is this essay's entire job in one hed. Turnbaugh made a cage experiment famous. Cohorts made clouds. Depommier made a thin, forked human file. None of that is a bottle, an enterotype diet, or a red *Akkermansia* bar (drafts 05, 21, 31). Delete the wink words. Do not reprint endpoints. Weight and diabetes nouns are disease nouns. Clinicians, not our bibliography, sit in that room.

A last mechanical note: if a later human file on a named isolate is larger and cleaner than 2019, date it and attach it. Do not let the new file reopen lean folklore, kit-to-cart, or a cousin's inheritance. The object is still the isolate, the dose, the endpoint, and the box. The cloud on a BMI plot is still a cloud.
