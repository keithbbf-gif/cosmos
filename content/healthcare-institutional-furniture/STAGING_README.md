## SEO rules

- One slug owns one `primary_keyword`.
- `meta_description` is a search snippet, not a slogan.
- `seo_intent` is informational unless a later editor changes it.
- Cross-link siblings. Do not cannibalize `why-this-series-exists` with a second “what is healthcare furniture” lede.
- History / channel hub month (pillar map): 2027-06. Spec-sheet and fire-code slugs wait.
- No therapy, supplements, AI, or COSMOS copy.
- No invented Medline SKUs, Medilodge purchase orders, or client prices.

## Brand

Bradley Brand / BBF / the Warren mill may appear where Arkansas hardwoods, bleach-grade finish, install, or the Medline-era channel are the actual subject. One or two sentences. No product names. No commission CTA in the body.

Medline is named as a **distributor / punchout channel**, not as a partner endorsement. Medilodge of Montrose is named because it is a public project on the mill’s own record (SNF, Montrose, MI). Other named jobs on that same public list may appear as geography, not as testimonials.

## What “Medline / Medilodge-era” means here

Roughly the years when a southern hardwood mill could still put a residential-looking case-good through a national medical distributor into a Michigan or Arkansas skilled-nursing operator: catalog page, GPO or corporate bid, LTL to the dock, install crew, infection-control wipe-down, punch list. The essays teach that route. They do not reconstruct a private contract.
