# Bibliography

Working list. Essays cite in running prose and in `## Notes`. Flag gaps `[CITE NEEDED]`.

## Statutes, manuals, standards

- Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987 (Nursing Home Reform Act).
- 42 CFR §483.10(i) (safe, clean, comfortable, homelike environment).
- 42 CFR §483.10(e)(3) (reasonable accommodation of needs and preferences).
- 42 CFR §483.25(n) (bed rails).
- 42 CFR §483.90(e) (resident rooms: bed, mattress, furniture, closet).
- CMS, *State Operations Manual*, Appendix PP (F584, F558, F700, F917; older F461/F246 language in 2009 S&C letters).
- FDA, *Hospital Bed System Dimensional and Assessment Guidance to Reduce Entrapment* (10 March 2006); 71 FR 12369.
- FDA, *A Guide for Modifying Bed Systems and Using Accessories to Reduce the Risk of Entrapment*.
- IEC 60601-2-38 (powered hospital beds, historical).
- IEC 60601-2-52:2015 (medical beds; FDA-recognized consensus standard).
- California Bureau of Household Goods and Services, Technical Bulletin 117-2013.
- California TB 133 (historical open-flame); later state pullback — confirm scope before a hard date.
- NFPA 101, *Life Safety Code*.
- BIFMA X5.1 / X5.4 (office and lounge seating performance — cousins, not healthcare law).
- 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design (knee/toe clearance as a cousin to dining-table talk).
