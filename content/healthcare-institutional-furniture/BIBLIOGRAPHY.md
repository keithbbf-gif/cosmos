# Bibliography

Primary first. Secondary last. If a draft asserts a number, the number should live here or in that draft’s Sources.

## United States — CMS / nursing homes

- 42 CFR 483.10, Resident rights (homelike environment at 483.10(i); closet pointer at 483.10(i)(4)).
- 42 CFR 483.90, Physical environment (80 / 100 square feet at 483.90(e)(1)(ii); bed, mattress, functional furniture, closet at 483.90(e)(2)).
- CMS State Operations Manual, Appendix PP, F584 (intent of “homelike”; institutional leftovers including uncomfortable / non-homelike furniture; lighting, temperature 71–81 °F for facilities first certified after October 1, 1990, sound).
- CMS Appendix PP / current F-tag map for resident-room bed, furniture, and closet (teaching files: F917; confirm the number in the SOM you are using). Older files: F252, F461.
- Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987, Pub. L. 100-203 (Nursing Home Reform Act).

## United States — hospital beds / entrapment

- FDA, *Hospital Bed System Dimensional and Assessment Guidance to Reduce Entrapment* (March 10, 2006). Seven zones; recommended limits on zones 1–4 (120 mm / 60 mm as in the guidance); about 691 reports January 1, 1985–January 1, 2006; 413 deaths; 120 injuries; 158 near-misses.
- 71 Fed. Reg. 12369 (March 10, 2006), availability notice.
- FDA Safety Alert, *Entrapment Hazards with Hospital Bed Side Rails* (August 23, 1995).
- Ferlo Todd J, Ruhl C, Gross TP. “Injury and Death Associated with Hospital Bed Side Rails: Reports to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration from 1985 to 1995.” *American Journal of Public Health* 87 (1997): 1675–1677.
- Hospital Bed Safety Workgroup, *A Guide to Bed Safety* (referenced in the 2006 guidance).
- IEC 60601-2-38 (as discussed in the 2006 guidance); later particular standard IEC 60601-2-52 (pin edition before quoting).

## United States — flammability / textiles

- California Technical Bulletin 117-2013; BHGS FAQ (label language; January 1, 2015).
- Pub. L. 116-260, §2101; 16 CFR part 1640 (federal adoption of 117-2013; June 25, 2021).
- NFPA 101, Life Safety Code — **adopted edition on the last survey** (260 / 261 / ASTM E1537 sentences; sprinkler tradeoff).
- NFPA 260; NFPA 261; NFPA 701 (cubicle curtains / draperies).
- ASTM E1353; ASTM E1537.
- TB 133 as a historical California full-scale test (repealed in California’s own regime; method lives in E1537).

## Accessibility and durability

- ADA 2010 Standards for Accessible Design (reach, clear floor, dining surfaces, operable parts — scoping is a legal question).
- BIFMA X5.1, X5.4, X5.5 (office / contract durability; not a SNF occupancy stamp). Confirm current editions.

## Channel and mill (public)

- Medline public history: 1910 A.L. Mills; 1966 Jim and Jon Mills, Evanston; Northfield HQ; manufacturer-distributor model (company newsroom; secondary encyclopedic pages).
- MediLodge of Montrose public materials: 9317 W. Vienna Road, Montrose, MI 48457; corporate Washington, MI. CMS provider files for CCN 235600 (bed count; ratings change — do not freeze a star).
- Bradley Brand Furniture / Keith Chambers public record: Warren, AR; hardwood since 1903; healthcare FF&E; project geography (Medilodge of Montrose; Byrd Haven, Searcy; Tuscany Village, Pearland; Chenal Heights; Green Acres, Paragould; GreenBrier Rehab; Delta Memorial, Dumas; Little Rock Healthcare & Rehab; Baywind Village, League City).

## Culture change and environment (secondary)

- Pioneer Network, *Nothing is Traditional about Environments in a Traditional Nursing Home*.
- CMS Compliance Group and similar public F-tag explainers (old-to-new map only; not authority).

## Do not invent

Medline SKUs, Medilodge purchase orders, client prices, accession-style object numbers, conversations with named staff, death counts not in the 2006 guidance or the 1997 *AJPH* paper.
