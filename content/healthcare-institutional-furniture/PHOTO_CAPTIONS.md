# Photo captions — rights register

No featured image is cleared. Figure plans in each draft are *plans*. Do not upload a living manufacturer’s current catalog as free.

## Preferred sources

| Class | Examples | License habit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| U.S. government | FDA 2006 zone drawings; CMS SOM figures if any | U.S. government work |
| Public domain | Nightingale-era ward photographs; pre-1930 trade catalogs | PD |
| Museum open access | Historic hospital interiors (Met, NLM, Smithsonian) | name the license |
| Trade catalogs | 1979 Medline catalog *as an object*; Hill-Rom period ads | confirm; often rights reserved |
| Architecture | Nightingale ward plans; Green House photos | confirm |

## Series rule

Each draft lists **Fig. 1–N** (4–7) with alt text and a magazine caption. If a row here later says “rights reserved” or “confirm,” the CMS import stores the plan as a custom field and does not publish the file.

Living-brand product shots (current Hillrom, Medline, Joerns, Invacare pages) are **confirm / do not upload as free**. Historic ads may be usable as commentary; legal still reviews.
