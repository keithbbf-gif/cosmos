# What this series is not

A series that will not say no will sell you a chair.

This folder is staged educational writing for Bradley Brand / BBF. It is a history and a specification culture from a mill that worked the Medline / Medilodge-era channel. It is not the rest of the internet. The first draft said why the series exists. This last one says where I will stop, so a later editor cannot turn a leftover into a cart.

## Not a catalog

There is no SKU. There is no price. There is no “our craftsmen.” There is no cart. If a later editor adds a product name, they have changed the job. Quality Healthcare Furniture and Arkansas Hardwood Furniture are faces on a public CV. They are not a product list here.

I will name Bradley Brand where the wood, the finish, the dock, or the channel is the actual subject. One or two sentences. No commission. No “contact us.” A mill that cannot describe its own route is lying. A mill that describes a nightstand as a product in this folder is also lying, just toward a different door.

## Not legal advice, not medical advice, not a survey course

I quoted 42 CFR 483, Appendix PP, FDA’s 2006 guidance, 16 CFR 1640, ADA 2010 Standards, NFPA 101 as a moving target. Those documents move. Your SOM edition moves. Your AHJ moves. If you need a ruling, open the current text or hire the person whose stamp is on the 2567.

I am not CMS. I am not FDA. I am not your medical director. I will not tell you who needs a rail. I will not tell you how to pass. I will tell you what the public interpretive guidance already says, so a catalog page cannot tell you a story the SOM will not back.

F584 and F917, as teaching files map them, are furniture-adjacent tags. Confirm the number in the SOM you are holding. I will not tattoo a teaching-file number onto your 2567.

## Not a how-to for defeating a test

No homemade cones. No instructions for beating a probe, a wipe audit, or a flame lab. The bunk-bed series in this tree has the same refusal. A CPSC bunk probe is not an HBSW bed tool. Do not mix the probes. Openings and tests belong to the people who run them.

If you need a zone assessment, use the published method, the current tool, and a person whose job is that assessment. If you need a furniture decision, stay out of the opening with your decorative panel. Daylight from the door is a reason to call nursing. It is not a test lab.

## Not a Medilodge case study

Medilodge of Montrose is on a public project list and a public CMS file: 9317 W. Vienna Road, Montrose, MI 48457, on the order of 121 certified beds, corporate address in Washington, Michigan. I used that as geography. I did not use an invoice, a room number, a resident, a dock conversation, or a star rating as an argument. Stars move. Wood sits.

The other named houses on the same public list — Byrd Haven in Searcy, Chenal Heights, Green Acres in Paragould, GreenBrier Rehab, Little Rock Healthcare & Rehab, Delta Memorial in Dumas, Tuscany Village in Pearland, Baywind Village in League City — are geography the same way. If you want a testimonial, you will not find it. If you want a room count I did not count, you will not find it.

A walkthrough in this folder is a composite. A named room would be a person. This series has no faces.

## Not a Medline internal history

I used public founding dates — 1910 A.L. Mills, 1966 Jim and Jon Mills in Evanston, later Northfield — and the existence of a furnishings channel. I did not use a catalog number, a margin, a GPO multiplier, or a private contract. If a rep was good or bad, I did not name them.

Medline is a face and a route in these sentences, not a partner endorsement and not a villain origin story. Gloves and nightstands shared a binder. That habit is the educational fact. The 10-K is someone else’s folder.

## Not a complete world survey

Joerns, Hill-Rom, Invacare, Drive, Kwalu, Akin, Nemschoff, and a hundred shops are in this industry. I named a few as lineage, not as a buyer’s guide. Batesville 1929 is a hospital-bed factory story. Warren 1903 is a hardwood story. They meet in a room. They are not a comparison chart.

Europe’s furniture standards, Canada’s bed guidance, and every state licensure book are other houses. I pointed at them when they taught the U.S. object. I did not tour them. A state-by-state square-footage table is a different project. Do not invent one from this folder.

## Not culture-change theology

I read Pioneer Network and the homelike tag. I did not pick a model. Greenhouse, neighborhood, household, country kitchen — those words sold residential collections. The honest versions changed the height of the chair and the length of the table. The dishonest versions changed the corbel and left the 17-inch seat.

A household table that still fails sit-to-stand is a failed table. A bunker that can see a face may be a ward and still be the only honest object in the corridor. I will not baptize a model I have not measured.

## Not a price guide, not a CapEx manual

I will not put a dollar on a nightstand. I will not reconstruct a Medilodge purchase order. I will not give you a multiplier from mill to punchout. The channel-economics draft talks about freight and install as politics, not as a calculator.

If you came here to budget a wing, hire the person who walks your leftover and names your wipe. This folder will make their questions better. It will not write their bid.

## Not a photograph of a resident

If plates get shot for this pack, they are empty rooms, agency diagrams, shop wood, labels, a dock with no faces. A resident’s face turns a leftover into a testimonial. A testimonial is a different job. Catalog interiors and Medline product photography stay out. I will not steal a JPEG to look like I was more in the room than I was.

## Not a process story

No other folder in this tree belongs in these sentences. No model names. No “how this was drafted.” If you can hear a machine, I failed the voice and an editor should cut.

## Not a substitute for walking the room

Forty-two drafts will not tape your leftover. I can teach you to draw the lift first. I cannot stand in your double. A mill drawing that never met a door width is still a wish, even if the wish cites F917 correctly.

If you are a specifier, take the questions from the DON draft and the sequence from the walkthrough and go. If you are a family, take the short set — chair, rod, clock, curtain, daylight at the rail — and go. If you are a later editor, do not add a checklist graphic that pretends the walk happened.

Shop knowledge is marked as shop knowledge in these files. It is not a tape of your building. The 80-square-foot floor is a federal number. Your aisle is a local number.

## Not a mill’s job book

These drafts are staged. `status: draft`. No WordPress publish date on this pass. A photograph plan does not exist yet. They are not a second copy of anyone’s purchase file, and they are not a replacement for the drawing that met a particular door. Pasting this folder onto a live domain before the plates exist would make the series look like a catalog with better verbs, which is the thing I said I would not do.

If a later editor wants a publish calendar, the index already names an intended hub month. That is a queue, not a promise that the inches have been re-taped.

## Not finished

Standards will move. A 2006 guidance will get a successor. A 117 will get a cousin. A GPO will change its name. F-tags will fold again. Correct the draft. Do not update a number from memory.

The locked counts stay locked until a new public artifact replaces them: 691 reports, 413 deaths, 120 injuries, 158 near-misses; zones 1–3 at 120 mm, zone 4 at 60 mm; 80 and 100 square feet. If those numbers move, move them with a citation. If they do not, do not “improve” them.

Forty-two staged essays are a chance to be wrong in public on purpose. If you find a wrong inch, the inch is the point. Send the artifact.

## What it is, one last time

A room is a leftover. A bed is a system. A chair is a transfer. A wardrobe is a rod. A channel is a face. A mill is a smell. Homelike is a direction. A sticker is not a program.

If you can still see those objects after the cherry print fades, the series did the only job I will sign.

Tomorrow the room will look the same from the hall. The test is whether you can still read the objects after the print fades.
