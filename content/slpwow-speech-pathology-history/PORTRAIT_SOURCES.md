## Rules

1. **No synthetic faces** — do not use AI-generated likenesses of historical persons.
2. **Prefer public domain or explicit permission** — Wikimedia Commons with verified PD/CC license, institutional archives with reuse terms, or direct rights clearance.
3. **Credit line** must appear in the embed caption and in this table.
4. Until `cleared = yes`, embeds must use the pending plate copy: *"Portrait pending — rights not cleared."*

## Filename convention

`assets/portraits/<portrait_id>.<ext>` where `portrait_id` matches the table (e.g. `van-riper-charles`).
