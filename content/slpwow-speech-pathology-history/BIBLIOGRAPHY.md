# Bibliography — SLPWOW Speech Pathology History Pack

Sources consulted while writing the 40 staged articles. This is an editorial working list, not a complete historiography. Prefer the primary book or the institutional page over a secondary retelling when they disagree.

Articles cite contested facts in the text. This file holds the warrant.
