# Editorial palette — SLPWOW History series

Use consistently across SVG figures in this series.

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|--------|
| Canvas | `#FAF9F7` | Figure background |
| Ink | `#1E293B` | Primary labels, axes |
| Muted ink | `#64748B` | Secondary labels, grid |
| Rule | `#CBD5E1` | Grid lines, dividers |
| Clinical teal | `#0F766E` | Primary emphasis, modern era |
| Heritage amber | `#B45309` | Milestones, historical emphasis |
| Soft fill | `#E2E8F0` | Bands, map regions |
| Highlight fill | `#CCFBF1` | Active era / selected school |

**Type (SVG):** Labels `system-ui, sans-serif` 11–13px; era titles Georgia or `serif` 14–18px semibold.  
**Stroke:** 1px rules; 2px timeline spine; rounded rects `rx="4"`.
