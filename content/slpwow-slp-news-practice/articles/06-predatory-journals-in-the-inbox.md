Verify the current CMS, MAC, state, or ASHA page before you act. This series does not evaluate patients and does not publish worksheets.

The email calls you “Esteemed Professor” and cites a poster you gave in 2019. It offers to publish your next paper in ten days. The journal name is one preposition away from a title you trust. There is a discount if you decide this week.

That message is not an honor. It is a business model.

COPE — the Committee on Publication Ethics — has spent years saying in public that predatory publishing is a pattern: fees, speed, and the costume of peer review without the review. They will not do your lawsuit for you. They will give you a way to talk about the pattern without inventing a defamation.

## What the pattern looks like from a school laptop

**The compliment is generic.** Your name is spelled right because a scraper found it. The paper they “admired” is not a paper you wrote. Or it is, and they admired only the email address.

**The editorial board is a ghost town.** Names you do not recognize, or names you do recognize who, when you write them, say they were never asked. Dead links. A “contact us” that is a Gmail.

**The scope is everything.** Speech, nursing, civil engineering, and “wellness studies” in one masthead. A real journal can be broad. A real journal is not desperate.

**The peer review is a weekend.** Ten days from submission to acceptance is not efficiency. It is a skipped step. Even a reject-and-resubmit at a serious CSD journal takes the time of tired reviewers who have day jobs like yours.

**The fee is the product.** Legitimate open-access journals charge APCs and say so on a boring page. Predatory outfits hide the invoice until after “acceptance,” then become legalistic. The difference is not that money changed hands. The difference is whether a review happened that could have said no.

**The archive is a mystery.** No DOI that resolves. No indexing you can check without their own stamp. “Indexed in Google” is not indexing. Google indexes menus.

None of these signs is a court verdict. Two or three together are a reason to close the tab. This page will not name a hit list. Hit lists rot, and they get people sued. Habits travel better.

## Why CSD is a market

We are a small field with promotion files, clinical doctorates, and a lot of people who need one more line on a CV. We also have clinicians who want to “get something out” after a quality-improvement project. That hunger is human. It is also a customer profile.

Students get copied on the emails. So do retirees. So do people whose hospital blocked PubMed but not their inbox. The harm is not only a wasted fee. The harm is a paper that looks citeable in a staff meeting and was never read by anyone who could have stopped a bad sentence.

ASHA journals, for all their slowness and their fees and their house style, are a known object. They have named editors. They have a transparency project you can read (2022 and after). They can reject you. Rejection is a feature. A journal that cannot reject you is a printer.

## What to do with the email, besides delete

Forward it to a trainee with two sentences: this is common; here is how we tell. That is precepting. It takes thirty seconds and prevents a $1,200 surprise.

If a colleague is already in the pipeline — invoice on the desk, acceptance PDF in the folder — do not humiliate them in a meeting. The model is built on embarrassment. Help them ask: can I withdraw; is my data still mine; do I need to tell the IRB. Those are local questions. This URL is not your compliance office.

If you are citing a paper and the journal feels off, do the boring work. Find the editor. Find the article on a library database that is not the publisher’s own site. Read the methods twice. A predatory venue can still host a true table. A prestigious venue can still host a false one. Venue is a prior, not a verdict.

## What this is not

This is not a war on open access. Open access at a society journal or a university press can be the honest path. This is not a war on foreign publishers. Predatory operations are domestic too, and serious journals are everywhere. This is not permission to sneer at people who publish outside the ASHA family. Plenty of excellent swallow and voice work lives in medical journals you already trust.

It is permission to treat flattery as a diagnostic sign.

Essay 07 is the cousin problem: papers that have not been reviewed yet, sitting on a preprint server with a DOI and a tweet. Those are not predatory. They are unfinished. Unfinished is a different kind of danger, and a different kind of use.
