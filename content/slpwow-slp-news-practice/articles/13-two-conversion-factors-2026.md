For years there was one conversion factor, and every autumn we argued about it. Calendar year 2026 split the number. CMS’s fact sheet for the final rule (CMS-1832-F) printed two:

- $33.57 for qualifying alternative payment model participants (QPs)
- $33.40 for everyone else

Both are up from $32.35 in 2025. The first is about a 3.8 percent bump. The second is about 3.3. ASHA’s fee-schedule analysis said the thing a school or outpatient SLP needed in one line: most of us are not QPs. We live in the $33.40 house.

## Why there are two

MACRA, back in 2015, promised a world of alternative payment models and then spent a long time not quite building that world for therapy. When the freeze on annual updates thawed, the statute did not thaw evenly. QPs — clinicians who meet thresholds in an Advanced APM with real risk and quality accountability — get a 0.75 percent annual update. Non-QPs get 0.25. Layer on a one-year 2.5 percent that Congress stuck in the 2026 cycle, plus a small RVU-budget adjustment CMS described as about 0.49 percent, and you get the two printed dollars.

You do not need to love MACRA to read the dollars. You need to know which dollar is yours. If you are not sure whether you are a QP, you are probably not. QP status is a CMS determination with a name and a letter, not a vibe you get from joining a hospital committee.

## The smile is not the paycheck

A higher conversion factor can still sit on top of a lower RVU, a 2.5 percent “efficiency adjustment” on some codes, a GPCI change, and sequestration. ASHA spent pages saying the smile can hide a cut. CMS’s own impact tables are averages. ASHA’s example in the same PDF is the right kind of humility: 92507’s national rate moved one way in their table, 96112 moved another. Mix, locality, and setting decide the month.

So the news is not “we got a raise.” The news is “the multiplier split, and the larger one is behind a door most SLPs do not have.”

## What “Advanced APM” is not

It is not MIPS with a nicer logo. It is not “we do quality projects.” It is a specific kind of model with two-sided risk and a CMS list. ASHA’s 2026 analysis said SLPs have limited ways in, and that most remain outside. Essay 25 stays with MIPS exemption, which is a different door. Do not stack the doors in your head.

If your health system is in a model and someone from finance says therapy is “part of the APM,” ask whether *you* are a QP for the conversion factor. Those can be different sentences. This page will not settle your employment. It will tell you the question is allowed.

## How to read the next year’s fact sheet

Find the two numbers. Find last year’s number. Find the sentence that explains the statutory layers (MACRA update, any one-time congressional bump, any budget-neutrality tweak). Then stop reading adjectives.

If Congress later glues the two factors back together, this essay becomes history. That would also be news. Until then, two prices for the same RVU is the 2026 weather.
