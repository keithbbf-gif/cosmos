# Editorial calendar — SLPWOW SLP News (practice wave)

Forty-six clinician-facing drafts. Status: `draft` / `stage: draft`. Nothing here is cleared for the live slpwow.com sitemap.

How SLPs read new research; reimbursement changes in plain language; school vs medical news; dysphagia / voice / fluency / pediatric *headlines*. News, not protocols.

All files live under `content/slpwow-slp-news-practice/`. Inventory: `MANIFEST.md`. Guardrails: `CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`.

This pack is complementary to the first SLP News wave (general profession calendar), `content/slpwow-speech-pathology-history/` (history), and `content/slp-pediatric-milestones-blog/` (parents). Do not merge the calendars. Do not file these under shop or “book now.”

## Cadence (suggested)

One piece every 10–14 days is a human pace. Forty-six essays at that rate is a long shelf, not a week. Do not dump the folder onto WordPress.
