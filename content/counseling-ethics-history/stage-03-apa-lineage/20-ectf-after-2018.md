APA seated an Ethics Code Task Force in 2018 and charged it, in the public language that state associations later quoted, with keeping what still served the public and the discipline and with drafting a "visionary and transformational" successor. The charge itself is a historical object. A successor code, as of this pack's last verification on 14 September 2026, had not replaced the 2002 book as amended in 2010 and 2016.

That negative fact is the essay. Anniversary blogs that write as if a 2025 APA code were already on the wall are ahead of the printer. This folder will not join them.

## Why 2018 looked like a revision year

The 2002 spine was sixteen years old. The 2010 and 2016 sutures had been written under interrogation weather, not under a full redesign. Telepsychology had moved from a novelty to a pandemic infrastructure. Diversity, equity, and human-rights language had become, inside APA politics, a demand that principles name systems and environments more plainly. Students who had only ever known the five principles wanted a book that sounded like their coursework.

Early public summaries of ECTF work — state-association overviews, not primary minutes — talked about criteria for principles and about naming supervision, assessment, and consent more tightly. Those outlines were weather. A later public trail is easier to date. APA's commenting portal and Division notices opened a draft Ethics Code for a ninety-day comment window that closed 19 March 2025. The Society of Addiction Psychology posted, on 5 August 2025, an ECTF thank-you that counted more than 2,650 comments from psychologists, students, organizations, and the public, including sixty-six group responses, and said the review was expected to finish in late 2025 or early 2026, with another draft anticipated after that. APA's 2026 convention program still listed an Ethics Committee session on the current draft and the March 2025 comment weather. `[VERIFY]` those session particulars against the live convention page before a caption. Draft language is not adopted language. This site will not treat a circulating outline, or a comment docket, as the code.

## How to read a task force without writing the ending

Read the charge. Read the comment periods if they are posted. Read the living URL on apa.org/ethics/code. If the living URL still presents the 2002/2010/2017 text, the task force is still a process. Processes are allowed to take years. Fisher's took about five. Hobbs's took about five. A "transformational" rewrite can take longer and then fail, or take longer and then print.

ACA, in the same decade, ran a revision toward a Fall 2026 final. NASW printed smaller 2021 amendments without pretending to a new architecture. Three crafts, three tempos. 2018 APA is the slowest of the current three, if "slow" means "no new book yet." Slow is not a moral grade.

## The temptation to preview

CE vendors love a preview. "Here's what the new code will require" is a product. It is also, until Council adopts and an effective date turns on, a fiction with footnotes. This pack's rule: no preview as requirement. A sentence about what a task force *has publicly said it is considering* is allowed if dated and marked. A sentence about what "you must now do" is a how-to and a possible falsehood.

## Uncertainty, written as a feature

I have not sat in ECTF meetings. I have not seen every comment docket. If, between this draft and a live import, Council adopts a successor, the editor's job is to add a dated paragraph and to stop this essay from claiming a vacuum that has filled. Until then, the honest headline is the one on the door: revision in process, 2002 house still standing.

## For WOW Therapies

A speech clinic does not need to track APA's comment portal. An educational lane that mentions "the APA code" does. The difference between a living text and a hoped-for text is the same difference Draft 02 taught with June 2003. We are, in 2026, waiting on a June that has not been named.

Stage 4 leaves psychology's shop and opens the guidance association's: four groups in 1952, five pages in 1961, a 2014 book about values and screens, a 2026 committee of its own.

If you only remember one APA fact from this stage, remember the spine: 1953 book, 1992 split, 2002 renovation, 2010 and 2016 sutures, 2018 task force. Everything else is weather.
