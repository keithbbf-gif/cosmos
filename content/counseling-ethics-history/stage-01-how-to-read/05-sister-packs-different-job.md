**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

The sister pack at `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` opens on temples and melancholy and then walks a reader toward Pinel, Freud, Rogers, Beck, and Herman. That is a calendar of *rooms and people*. This folder is a calendar of *rules the guilds wrote about those rooms*. If you mix the two, you get a mush in which Carl Rogers appears to have authored the 2014 ACA code and the 1953 APA book appears to be a treatment manual.

They are not the same job. The mesh that asked for both packs was right to split them.

## What the heritage pack already spent

It spent an era essay on counseling as a profession: Parsons's 1908 Vocation Bureau, Witmer's clinic, APGA, CACREP, Virginia 1976, the Counseling Compact. It named Tarasoff in passing. It named conversion-practice statutes as a later weather. It refused to become a license map of fifty states.

That essay is still the right door for "how did counseling become a job." This folder is the right door for "how did the job write and rewrite its public rules." We will mention Parsons again, briefly, because the 1961 APGA pamphlet is a guidance-era object. We will not retell *Choosing a Vocation*. We will mention Rogers as a climate, not as a biography. The figure essay already exists.

## What SLPWOW already spent

The speech-pathology lane owns Van Riper, school SLP history, ASHA's own professionalization, fluency, language, swallowing. ASHA has an ethics code. That code is a neighbor. It is not a fourth spine in this pack. A reader who wants the speech ethics heritage should be sent to that lane, not fed a diluted ASHA paragraph here as garnish.

WOW Therapies' live site, as last fetched on 14 September 2026, presents occupational, physical, and speech services in Southeast Arkansas, with the body copy centered on speech-language pathology: early childhood, school age, adult neuro and swallow. These ethics essays do not quietly restaff that clinic. They sit beside it as educational furniture. Furniture is allowed to be specific. It is not allowed to pretend it is a treatment room.

## What this pack will spend instead

Paper. Votes. Names of task forces. The 1948 Hobbs method. The 1960 first-person page. The 1961 five pages. The 1992 split between principles and standards. The 1996 NASW values. The 2005 PENS report and the 2010 APA amendments. The 2014 ACA technology section. The 2015 Hoffman report. The 2021 NASW self-care sentences. The 2026 ACA revision that, as of this writing, is still a committee weather and not a published book.

People will appear. They will appear as chairs and reporters and, sometimes, as public dissenters. They will not appear as saints of the consulting room. If you want Linehan's life, the heritage pack has the figure essay. If you want Linehan's mindfulness as a third-wave object, the ACT pack has that angle. If you want what a *code* said about harm, competence, or multiple relationships in a named year, stay here.

## Cross-links that are allowed

A sentence that says "the heritage pack's counseling-profession essay is the building; this pack is the posted rules" is allowed. A sentence that says "see the ACT pack for the 1999 Guilford volume" is allowed. A sentence that says "see SLPWOW for ASHA" is allowed.

A sentence that reprints those packs is not. Editors: if you find a two-page Rogers biography in this folder, cut it. If you find a swallow-therapy history, it has wandered in from the wrong truck.

## Why the split is a claims fence

Schools of therapy leak into how-tos. Codes leak into complaint how-tos. One folder, two leaks, and a small-city site becomes a reckless machine. Separate folders make the refuse lists enforceable. `GUARDRAILS.md` in this pack bans decision trees and long code pastes. The heritage pack bans session scripts. The ACT pack bans breathing counts. A merged calendar would blur which ban applies.

It is also a kindness to the reader. A person who typed "history of counseling ethics" into a search box did not ask for Melanie Klein. A person who typed "who was Mary Ainsworth" did not ask for Standard 1.02. Let them have the door they opened.

## What a later graphics pass should not do

Do not put Freud's face on an ethics-code essay. Do not put a stock gavel on a 1961 pamphlet. Do not put a generated "diverse therapy group" on a 2021 NASW amendment. Type, title pages, timelines of *documents*. Draft 47 will say this again, because graphics agents arrive late and hungry.

The next stage leaves the how-to-read porch and walks backward — oaths, research scars, charity notebooks, a 1938 committee that had no book. That backward walk is still not the heritage pack's antiquity essay. It is the prehistory of *rules*, which is a narrower animal.

If you only have time for one more file in this stage, you have already read the fence. Go to 1953. Or to 1960. Or to 1961. The three first printed codes are the reason the porch exists.
