**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

The 2002 APA ethics text was adopted by the Council of Representatives on 21 August 2002 and did not govern new complaints until 1 June 2003. That gap is the first lesson. A code is not a feeling that arrives on the day a committee likes a sentence. It is a document with an adoption date, an effective date, and, later, an amendment date that does not always replace the whole book.

If you remember nothing else from this folder, remember those three dates. They keep a history essay from turning into a sermon.

## What a code is doing when it sits on a table

A professional association prints a code for several jobs at once, and the jobs fight.

It wants to **teach** new members the house style of the craft. It wants to **adjudicate** the members who already embarrass the house. It wants to **signal** to legislatures and insurers that the craft can police itself. It wants to **sell** — quietly — the idea that a credential is worth more than a neighbor's advice. Those jobs are all historical. None of them is "tell a Southeast Arkansas reader how to handle Tuesday."

When you open a public PDF from apa.org, counseling.org, or socialworkers.org, you are not opening a statute. You are opening a membership instrument that some state boards have chosen to cite, some have rewritten, and some have ignored. The citation is a later political fact. It is not in the paper's binding.

## Adoption is not effectiveness

APA's own prefatory language, in the versions I have seen, is blunt about complaints: conduct is judged by the code that was in force when the conduct happened. That is why 1 June 2003 matters, and why 1 June 2010 matters for the 1.02 / 1.03 amendments, and why 1 January 2017 matters for Standard 3.04. NASW's 2021 amendments took effect 1 June 2021. ACA's 2014 code was approved and released at the end of March 2014 and replaced the 2005 text as the association's living document.

A writer who says "the 2002 code" without the June 2003 effective date is already a little sloppy. A writer who says "the 2017 APA code" as if a new book had been written is sloppier. The 2016 action was an amendment to one standard. The spine was still 2002.

## A preamble is not a standard

APA, after 1992, split the page. General Principles are aspirational. Ethical Standards are the ones a membership body can try to enforce. NASW, after 1996, stacked a mission, a list of values, a list of principles, and then a long body of standards. ACA writes a preamble and purpose, then lettered sections that read more like rules of the house.

Those architectures are historical choices. They are not proof that one profession is "more ethical" than another. They are proof that committees argue about what a sentence is *for*. A principle that cannot get you expelled is doing a different job from a standard that can.

When a continuing-education slide treats "beneficence" as if it were a checkbox, the slide has collapsed the 1992 split. This folder will not.

## Revision is a weather report

Codes change because practice changed, or because a scandal changed the weather, or because a regulator knocked, or because the last sentence would not stretch over a new machine.

APA's early revisions (1959, 1963, 1968, 1977, 1979, 1981) kept shortening and rearranging a 1953 book that members had found ungainly. The 1992 and 2002 jobs were redesigns. The 2010 and 2016 jobs were surgical. NASW added a nondiscrimination line in 1967, rebuilt the house in 1979, rebuilt it again in 1996, then spent the 2010s arguing about technology, self-care, and the title of cultural competence. ACA's public archive is a ladder: 1961, 1974, 1981, 1988, 1995, 2005, 2014 — and, as of this writing, a 2026 revision still in committee.

A "living document" is a phrase associations like. It is also an admission: last year's sentence is already a historical object.

## How to read a sentence without recruiting yourself

Four questions, none of them a worksheet.

**Who voted?** Council, Delegate Assembly, Governing Council, a board on an emergency calendar. The 2005 PENS report became APA Board policy in days. That speed is part of the object.

**Who was in the room?** Hobbs wanted incidents from working psychologists. Fisher's 2002 task force ran member surveys and open forums. ACA's 2011–2014 task force spent three years and took town-hall comment. NASW's 1996 values list was a literature distillation, not a revelation.

**What was the fight?** Sometimes the fight is fees and advertising (NASW and the FTC, around 1990). Sometimes it is a screen (ACA 2014, Section H). Sometimes it is whether "follow the law" is a permission slip (APA 1.02). The fight is the history. The tidy sentence is the residue.

**What did it not decide?** A code does not settle state duty-to-protect law. It does not write a HIPAA manual. It does not tell a speech-language pathologist in Arkansas which board owns her license. Those silences are as historical as the clauses.

## What this reading method refuses

It refuses the inspirational poster. It refuses the CE quiz that asks you to "apply Standard 4.02 to a vignette" on a marketing site. It refuses the myth that older codes were innocent and newer codes are complete. The 1953 APA incidents already included segregation in industrial testing and McCarthy-era academic freedom. Innocence is a later advertising claim.

It also refuses the opposite myth: that codes are empty. People have lost memberships. Boards have cited association language. Courts have asked what a profession said it owed. Empty documents do not attract that kind of weather.

For WOW Therapies, the method is a kindness. The live clinic is not an ethics office. The educational lane can still tell a reader why "confidentiality" on a speech chart and "confidentiality" in a 1996 NASW standard are cousins who do not share a house. Cousins are a historical fact. A house is a license.

Read the next essays as a stack of objects. If a sentence in them starts to feel like advice, it has already failed this one.
