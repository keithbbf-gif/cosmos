# Photo notes — counseling ethics-code history

Text is the product. Images are evidence or atmosphere, never a fabricated
witness.

Companion: `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md`. Graphics agents start there.

## Allowed

- Public-domain or Creative Commons portraits listed as `pd` or `cc`.
- Period objects: a 1953 title page, a 1961 five-page APGA cover, a 1960
  NASW one-page scan *if* the association or a library has cleared the
  reproduction — otherwise type. Court reporter captions for *Tarasoff*
  and *Jaffee* as type, not as fake gavels.
- Original diagrams drawn for this series: a dated timeline of *documents*,
  a three-column map (association / licensing board / court). Not a
  decision worksheet. Not a "how to file" figure.
- Type treatments: name of the code, year, one short epithet.

## Banned

- AI-generated faces, "historical photographs" that are not photographs.
- Scraped conference / university headshots of living people.
- Patient artwork, clinic candids, children as thumbnails.
- Stock "therapist with clipboard and smiling client."
- Stock "gavel and scales" as if a code were a statute.
- Before/after faces.
- Anything that implies a WOW Therapies treatment room you have not
  photographed with consent.
- Diagrams that function as ethics worksheets (decision trees, "what would
  you do" cards, complaint flowcharts).

## Alt text

Name what is in the frame. Not "ethics in action" and not "a caring
professional."

For type-only posts: no empty `<img>`. The heading is the image.

## WordPress featured image

If `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` says `no-portrait`, the featured image is a
**series template** (wordmark + essay title), not a guessed face.
