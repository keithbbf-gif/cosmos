## ACA / APGA — codes and process

- American Personnel and Guidance Association. *Ethical Standards.* 1961. Five-page public ancestor; later editions in ACA's ethics archive.
- Subsequent APGA / AACD / ACA codes: 1974, 1981, 1988, 1995, 2005, 2014. ACA's public archive page lists prior editions: https://www.counseling.org/resources/ethics/archives
- Kaplan, David M., and Erin Martz. Public commentary on the 2014 revision process (town halls, member comment). *Counseling Today* archive, May 2014: "A Living Document of Ethical Guidance."
- Francis, Perry C., and colleagues on the 2011–2014 Ethics Revision Task Force. Named in the *Counseling Today* account.
- Linde, Lynn. Circulating remark that ACA tends to revise every 7–10 years. Treat as secondary, not as a bylaw.
- American Counseling Association. 2005 "Ethics Update" interview series (ten parts), including "the end of 'clear and imminent danger.'"
- American Counseling Association. Current public code page / Counseling Nexus note (2026): the Revision Task Force is reviewing comments; a final revised code was announced for Fall 2026. The 2014 text remained the living public code at last verification. Do not treat a withdrawn draft as adopted.

## ACA — institutional names

- Rename to American Association for Counseling and Development, 1983.
- Rename to American Counseling Association, 1 July 1992.

## NASW — codes and process

- National Association of Social Workers. First *Code of Ethics* approved by the Delegate Assembly, 13 October 1960. One page; fourteen first-person proclamations. NASW hosts a historical copy.
- 11 April 1967: fifteenth principle on nondiscrimination.
- 1979 comprehensive revision (task force chaired by Charles Levy, appointed after the 1977 Delegate Assembly). Six sections; later counted as 82 principles; preamble; enforcement language. Effective 1 July 1980 per NASW historical notes.
- 1990 revisions after a Federal Trade Commission inquiry into solicitation, fees, and referral compensation.
- 1993: added principles including dual relationships and impairment.
- 1996 revision (committee associated in secondary literature with Frederic G. Reamer). Mission statement; six core values; ethical principles; much larger body of standards. Adopted August 1996.
- 1999: narrower clarification on disclosing confidential information.
- 2008: gender identity, sexual orientation, and immigration status named in existing nondiscrimination language.
- 2017: technology-focused revisions after a task force that began work around 2014. Standard 1.05 retitled, in that year, toward "Cultural Awareness and Social Diversity."
- 2020–2021 Delegate Assembly amendments: professional self-care language in Purpose and in the Integrity principle; Standard 1.05 restored as "Cultural Competence" on the recommendation of the National Committee on Racial and Ethnic Diversity. Effective 1 June 2021.
- National Association of Social Workers. Current public code page: https://www.socialworkers.org/About/Ethics/Code-of-Ethics
- Reamer, Frederic G. *Ethical Standards in Social Work* (NASW Press; several editions). Secondary on the 1996 architecture.
- Reamer, Frederic G. "The Evolution of Social Work Ethics: Bearing Witness." *Advances in Social Work* (2014).
