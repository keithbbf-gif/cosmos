# Style guide — WOW Therapies, counseling ethics-code history

Educational history for [WOWTherapies.com](https://wowtherapies.com). Complementary
to `content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` (schools and figures) and to the
SLPWOW speech-pathology series. Do not retell Van Riper here. Do not retell
the Freud-to-Herman calendar here. This lane is the **public history of
association ethics codes** — APA, ACA (and APGA before it), NASW — as dated
documents a general reader can check.

`voice: essay` means the writer draft aimed at this file. `voice_check: human`
is the same pass marked for the sister-pack convention. Neither value is a
byline. A later editor may mark `voice_check: edited`.

## Who is speaking

A historically literate magazine writer who has sat in enough waiting rooms
to know that a code is not a person and a person is not a code. Warm without
being pastoral. Precise without being a textbook. The reader may be a curious
client, a family member, a student, or a clinician. Do not talk down. Do not
talk shop as if the room were empty of patients.

WOW Therapies is a Southeast Arkansas practice brand. The live site, as last
fetched, presents occupational, physical, and speech services. These essays
are **heritage education**, not a menu of mental-health services and not a
claim that the clinic adjudicates ethics complaints.

## Hard bans (fail the draft)

Do not use:

- "In today's rapidly evolving…"
- "It's important to note"
- "delve" / "delve into"
- "landscape" as a metaphor for a field
- "robust"
- "leverage"
- "unlock"
- "cutting-edge"
- "game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "In conclusion"
- "Moreover" / "Furthermore" stacks
- "Whether you're a … or a …"
- empty dualities ("not just X, but Y"; "both an art and a science")
- throat-clearing first paragraphs that delay the first fact
- three-beat synonym piles ("heal, grow, and thrive")
- "the father of…" unless you are quoting a myth, not endorsing it
- "gold-standard treatment" as marketing
- invented quotes
- AI-generated faces
- "try this at home," "self-treat," session scripts
- ethics-decision worksheets, complaint how-tos, "ask yourself these"
- long verbatim excerpts from a copyrighted code
- "holistic wellness journey," "let's dive in," "let's explore"

Also drop the cousins: "navigate," "tapestry," "plethora," "utilize,"
"harness," "elevate your," "the future of," "at the forefront,"
"empower," "in this article we will explore," "journey of healing" as filler.

## Do this instead

1. Open on a dated event, a room, a document, or a named quarrel. The first
   sentence should be checkable.
2. Mix sentence length. A short sentence after a long one.
3. Name the book, paper, association, or court. Hobbs 1948. *Ethical
   Standards of Psychologists* (1953). APGA 1961. NASW Delegate Assembly,
   13 October 1960. *Tarasoff*, 1976. *Jaffee v. Redmond*, 1996.
4. Mark uncertainty. `[CITE NEEDED]` or `[VERIFY]` or "historians disagree"
   beats a clean falsehood.
5. Put living people in public documents only.
6. Give women's work, Black work, and non-European work full essays when
   the record has them — not a closing paragraph titled "diversity."
7. Stop when the piece has said the thing. Target 800–1,400 words. Cut the
   recap paragraph if it only restates the headings. Deepen with dated
   scenes, not with recap.
8. Summarize a code. Do not reprint it. The associations publish the
   current texts; this folder is a history of how those texts were built.

## Claims posture

See `GUARDRAILS.md`. History may describe what a code required in a named
year. History may not teach a reader how to file a complaint, how to keep
a chart, or how to run a session. No decision trees. No "if your therapist
does X, do Y."

A code is not a statute. A membership trial is not a license hearing. A
license hearing is not a lawsuit. Keep the three rooms separate.

## Series voice, in one paragraph

Write as if *The New Yorker* and *Psychotherapy Networker* had to share a
desk, and the desk was in a small-city clinic that still answers its own
phone. The past is allowed to be strange. The present is not allowed to be
sold. A code is allowed to be a compromise. A compromise is not allowed to
wear a halo.
