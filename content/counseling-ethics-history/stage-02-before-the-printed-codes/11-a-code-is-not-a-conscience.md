**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Nicholas Hobbs wrote in 1948 that a code should be "effective in modifying human behavior," and then joked, in the way of a mid-century psychologist, that the behavior in question was psychologists'. He did not write that a code would replace the person. He wanted a text that could be lived. Living still requires a liver.

This is the last porch essay before the APA shop opens. If we do not say the limit now, every later revision will sound like a moral software update.

## What committees can actually author

A committee can list the fights members have already had. That is the critical-incident method. A committee can rank values. That is NASW 1996. A committee can split aspirations from enforceable rules. That is APA 1992. A committee can add a technology section after the membership has already opened Facebook pages. That is ACA 2014.

A committee cannot sit in the room when a counselor in a county of 18,000 people sees a client at the only grocery store. The 2014 ACA language about extending counseling boundaries is an attempt to *talk to* that moment. The sentence is not the moment. People who treat the sentence as a conscience have already left the grocery store for a workshop hotel.

## Liability, membership, and the disclaimer sentence

APA's later prefatory language, in versions I have seen, warns that the Ethics Code is not intended to be a basis for civil liability. Lawyers still wave it. Boards still cite it. The disclaimer is a historical object: an association trying to keep its teaching-and-adjudication book from becoming an automatic verdict in someone else's courtroom.

The disclaimer is also a confession. If the code were a conscience, you would not need to say it is not a statute. Consciences do not write disclaimers. Trade associations do.

NASW and ACA have their own prefatory rituals — purpose, limits, the reminder that the code does not exhaust ethics. Read those paragraphs as architecture, not as throat-clearing. They are the association admitting the gap this essay is named for.

## Kitchener's 1984 paper as a cousin, not a code

Karen Kitchener, writing in *The Counseling Psychologist* in 1984, offered counseling psychology a set of principles — autonomy, beneficence, nonmaleficence, justice, fidelity — as tools for *critical evaluation* when intuition is not enough. The paper is assigned in a thousand ethics courses. It is not the ACA code. It is not the APA code. Students who highlight it as if it were enforceable have made the category error this folder exists to prevent.

What Kitchener shares with Hobbs is the unromantic claim that a profession needs more than a feeling. What she shares with this essay is the other unromantic claim: principles are for evaluation. They do not decide. A later textbook industry (Corey, Welfel, Pope & Vasquez) built decision models on that gap. Those models are pedagogical objects. They are not this site's content. We will not print a six-step model. We will say the models exist because the code is not a conscience.

## The cruel use of the limit

"Codes aren't consciences" can become a permission slip. It has been used that way. People have followed a thin reading of a standard and then said they were done. People have ignored a standard and said their conscience was higher. Both moves are in the historical record. APA's 1.02 fight is, among other things, a fight about which of those moves the association would bless.

This essay does not bless either move. It states a documentary fact: a printed page is a printed page. The person remains. The board remains. The court remains. The client remains.

## For a reader who wanted a halo

If you came here to be reassured that "our profession is ethical" because it has a PDF, the 1938–1953 interval should have already ruined the reassurance. The PDF is an achievement. It is also a record of failures that made the achievement look necessary.

If you came here to be reassured that you can ignore PDFs because you are a good person, the later expulsion-and-license record should ruin that too. Good people write codes because goodness does not scale to a stranger.

WOW Therapies can host this history without claiming a halo. A speech clinic in Southeast Arkansas does its work under its own rules and its own board. The counseling and psychology and social-work codes are the neighbors' posted notices. Reading the neighbors' notices is literacy. Mistaking them for your own pulse is folklore.

Stage 3 begins with Tolman's 1947 committee and Hobbs's incidents. From here on the essays get more documentary and less sermonic. The sermon had to come first or the documents would be mistaken for scripture.
