# Portrait sources — licenses, or an honest blank

**Rule.** Public domain or Creative Commons only. Never generate a face.
Never crop a still from a documentary you do not have rights to. Never use a
university headshot page as if it were free.

Most people in this pack who still write or teach were **living** as of last
verification (2026-09-14). Living people default to `no-portrait`. Type or a
period object only.

This series is about **documents**. Prefer a title page, a journal masthead,
or type. A face is almost never required.
