**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

In 2026 the American Counseling Association's Ethics Code Revision Task Force was, by the association's own Counseling Nexus note, reviewing submitted comments and preparing a final revised version. The draft was taken down while that work continued. The note promised publication of a final code on the Nexus and the ACA website in the Fall of 2026.

This essay is being written on 14 September 2026. Fall has not finished. A promise is not a printer. The living public code, at last verification, remained the 2014 book.

## How to write the present without lying

Write the process. Do not write the ending. Do not "preview requirements." Do not treat a withdrawn draft as if members were already bound. Do not treat a podcast that discussed draft AI language as if Council had voted.

Public conversation in 2025–2026 — including neighboring professions' AI ethics work — made it likely that a 2026 ACA book would say more about machine tools than 2014 could. Likelihood is not a clause. If a later editor is importing this pack after a Fall 2026 release, they should add a dated paragraph with the adoption and effective dates and stop this essay from claiming a vacuum.

Until then, the 2014 text is the house. The task force is the weather.

## Comment windows as historical objects

ACA's 2011–2014 process used town halls and member letters. The 2026 process used a public comment window that the Pennsylvania Counseling Association and ACA's own LinkedIn invitation both closed on 24 April 2026. That date is now a chapter-and-association object, not a rumor. Comment windows are how a membership body claims Hobbs's inheritance: we asked.

They are also how a membership body limits the ask. A window closes. A draft disappears. The final may or may not resemble the blog arguments that surrounded the draft. Historians of 2014 already know this. Historians of 2026 will learn it when the book appears — or when it slips.

## Neighbors in the same season

APA's ECTF, seated in 2018, had not, as of this writing, replaced the 2002 house. NASW's last substantive printed amendments were 2021. ACA, if it hits Fall 2026, would be the first of the three spines in this pack to complete a 2020s full revision. "First" would be a calendar fact, not a virtue. Secondary blogs in July 2026 already wrote "September 2026" as if a month were a vote. Nexus, at last check, still said Fall. `[VERIFY]` the adoption and effective dates against Nexus or counseling.org the day a later editor imports — not against a CE vendor's hopeful calendar.

CAMFT, AAMFT, and others spent the mid-2020s writing AI sentences. They are neighbors. This pack will not annex them. It will say: the weather in 2026 was machines, distance, and the old fights about values and harm, now with new furniture.

## What this site will not do with a new book

It will not auto-import a PDF. It will not write "what you must change in your practice." It will not 301 the 2014 essay to a booking form. It will, if the book exists, commission a new draft in this stage with adoption and effective dates, and it will leave 2014 on the shelf as history.
