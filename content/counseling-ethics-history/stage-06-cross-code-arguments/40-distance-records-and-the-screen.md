**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

Celia Fisher's 2002 process notes already listed internet-mediated research and practice as weather the APA book had to see. ACA's 2005 interview series spent a part on "ethical use of technology in counseling." ACA's 2014 code gave distance, technology, and social media their own section. NASW's 2017 revision made technology the cycle's main job.

Those are dated inventories of machines. Email. The first video hours. The Facebook wall. The search box. The cloud folder. A 2026 ACA clause about machine-learning tools, if one prints, would be another inventory. It would not be a prophecy and it would not be a user manual.

## What a section can catch

Who is in the frame. Where the file lives. Whether a wall is a waiting room. Whether a search is curiosity. Whether a rural client on a shared laptop can consent to a household hearing the hour. Whether an interstate compact (counseling's 2020s project; speech has its own compact weather) makes a screen into a license problem.

What a section cannot catch: the next vendor. Codes age like phones. That is not a reason to skip them. It is a reason to date them.

## Records as the unglamorous half

Technology talk loves the screen. The ethics problem is often the record: a PDF in a portal, a message thread, a school platform, a recording that outlives the consent. HIPAA's 2003 compliance date and FERPA's school life sit under the association sentences. Three letterheads do not write one records law.

ACA's 2005 transfer-plan conversation is a records-and-mortality object: someone else will have to pick up the file. That sentence is older than Zoom. It became louder when the file could be forwarded in a second.

## Social media as a 2014 object

2014 is a wall-and-search year. 2026 is a short-video and chatbot year. Teaching 2014 as if it were already about generative models is anachronism. Teaching 2014 as quaint is also a mistake. The wall taught the craft that a "friend" is an ethics noun. The chatbot will need a new noun. The noun will be voted, late.

## Interstate hours as a 2010s–2020s object

A screen does not notice a state line. A license does. The Counseling Compact and speech compact bills of the 2020s are attempts to make the license notice the screen. They are not ethics codes. They are mobility statutes that will make the codes' distance sections look, in hindsight, like they were waiting for a legislature. `[VERIFY]` any named state's compact status before a live caption.

A 2014 Section H written before those bills is still a document. It is a document about walls and searches, not about a compact commission.

## For a speech clinic that already records

Swallow studies, language samples, parent coaching on a phone — speech ethics met machines early and under different letterheads. This pack will not write ASHA's technology history. It will say the counseling and social-work inventories are cousins. Cousins share a family argument about distance. They do not share a board.

WOW Therapies can host the cousins' story. It will not publish a telehealth setup guide. It will not recommend a vendor. It will not pretend a history page is a security audit.
