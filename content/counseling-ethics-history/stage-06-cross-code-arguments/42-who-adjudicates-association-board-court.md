Draft 04 said a PDF is not a trial. This essay says the trial, when there is one, has an address. The address is almost never a website.

APA, ACA, and NASW can try members. State boards can try licensees. Courts can try defendants. The same conduct can visit two rooms or three, or none. A newspaper can visit all three without being any of them.

## The association room

A membership body needs a complaint, a process, and a person who still belongs. People resign. People never joined. The 1938 APA committee already knew the informal version of this room. The 1979 NASW preamble wanted a formal version. ACA's later codes include a section on resolving ethical issues that is, in part, about this room.

What you cannot see from outside: the file, the settlement, the educational letter that never becomes a headline. That opacity is a choice. It protects the accused and, sometimes, the public name of the craft. It also feeds the rumor that "nothing ever happens." Both the protection and the rumor are historical objects.

This site will not give you the complaint URL as a call to action. The offices exist. Google will find them if you have business there. A history pack that becomes a filing desk has changed jobs.

## The board room

An Arkansas counseling board, an Arkansas social-work board, an Arkansas psychology board, and an Arkansas speech-language pathology board are four legal objects. Some boards adopt an association code by reference. Some write their own rules. Some do both. `[VERIFY]` any live sentence that names which text a named Arkansas board currently cites. Those citations move.

A board can touch a livelihood. An association letter, by itself, cannot. That is why guilds lobby to be adopted. Adoption is a political success and a category risk: the public starts to hear the code as a statute.

The Counseling Compact and speech compact legislation of the 2020s are attempts to let a license travel. They do not merge the ethics books. A compact is a mobility machine. A code is a sentence machine.

## The court room

Malpractice, duty to protect, privilege, fraud, custody. *Tarasoff* and *Jaffee* already had their essay. A lawyer may wave a code. A judge may shrug. APA's prefatory "not a basis for civil liability" sentence is a wish the waving still happens.

This site is not your lawyer.

## The newspaper room

Hoffman became a *New York Times* object on 14 July 2015. PENS became a petition object. 2014 ACA values language became a training-program-lawsuit object. Publicity is a fourth room that none of the three controls. Historians use newspapers. Historians should not pretend newspapers are verdicts.

## Employer as a fourth private room

A hospital, a school district, a community-mental-health agency, and a private practice can each run a personnel process that is not a membership trial, not a board hearing, and not a lawsuit. NASW's 1979 architecture already named the employer as an ethical object. APA's 1.03 organizational-demands sentence is a cousin. This essay's three rooms remain the public ones. The fourth room is real and mostly invisible. A blog that pretends to see it is guessing.
