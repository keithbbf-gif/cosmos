**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

WOW Therapies, on the homepage last fetched for this pack, offers occupational, physical, and speech services in Southeast Arkansas, with the body copy centered on speech-language pathology — early childhood, school age, adult neuro and swallow. That is the live clinic. These essays are furniture in an educational lane, not a quiet restaffing.

What a clinic like that inherits from the national ethics-code story is a *stack*, not a tablet.

## The stack, named without a worksheet

An SLP's ethics home is ASHA, plus a state license, plus HIPAA if the work is a covered entity, plus FERPA if the work is a school contract. A counselor two streets over inherits ACA or a board text, plus a different license, plus the same HIPAA, plus, maybe, a duty-to-protect statute that is not *Tarasoff*. A social worker in the community-mental-health building inherits NASW if they belong, a third board, the agency's own book, and the 1996 poverty sentence whether or not anyone hung it.

The child who sees the SLP and the school counselor in the same week inherits none of the books and all of the silences. The silences are the point of the codes. They are also the point of the grocery store. Dual-relationship sentences were written for towns that have one pharmacy. Southeast Arkansas did not need Washington to invent the pharmacy.

## What does not arrive

A 1953 APA brick. A 2015 Sidley Austin binder. A 2026 ACA draft that was taken down. A laminated NASW values card, unless someone on staff put it there. National weather arrives as CE, as a board newsletter, as a compact bill in Little Rock, as a parent who watched a national story.

Travel, stigma, and the shortage of licensed people — the sister pack already named them as professional history, not as a footnote. Ethics books written in Washington and Alexandria travel on that thin road. They arrive late and they arrive as PDFs.

## Compact weather

The Counseling Compact and speech compact legislation of the 2020s are attempts to let a license follow a person. They do not merge ASHA and ACA. They do not make a Pine Bluff chart into a Virginia chart. Mobility is not a unified ethics. Readers who hear "compact" as "one national code" have heard a vendor.

## School bells as the first "counselor"

A great many people in Southeast Arkansas first met a counselor as a person with a bell schedule, not as a fifty-minute outpatient. ASCA's later identity fight is a neighbor this pack refused to annex. The inheritance is still local: the word *counselor* arrived through schools, churches, and county offices before it arrived through a national PDF. The PDFs then tried to govern a word that was already in the building.

## What this lane can honestly give a local reader

Literacy. "The ethics code" is not one object. Confidentiality is not privilege. A 2021 NASW suture is not a new book. A speech clinic is not a counseling shop because it hosted a history. A grocery-aisle encounter is a 2005 ACA conversation and a 1993 NASW conversation and a human fact.

What this lane cannot give: a decision about your clinician, a filing, a form, a diagnosis, a booking widget dressed as heritage.

If the live site ever grows a mental-health service, that service will need its own pages, its own licenses, and its own claims review. These essays will still be essays. They will not become the service.
