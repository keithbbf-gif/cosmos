**Educational note.** This is history for a general reader. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for care with a licensed clinician.

If you came here for Freud, you are in the wrong folder. If you came here for Van Riper, you are in the wrong folder. If you came here for a breathing count, you are in a folder that will not help you and will not apologize.

This is the map.

## Counseling and clinical heritage

`content/wowtherapies-therapy-history/` — eras and figures. Temples, asylums, the talking cure, behaviorism, humanistic rooms, families, the cognitive turn, attachment, trauma, feminist and liberation work, the third wave as a calendar item, counseling as a profession. Pinel through Insoo Kim Berg. That pack's profession essay is the building. This pack is the posted rules.

Cross-link allowed: "see counseling-as-a-profession for Parsons and licensure." Cross-print forbidden. Rogers, if he appears here at all, appears as a documentary weather on a later code — not as a biography that the heritage pack already wrote.

## ACT and mindfulness

`content/act-mindfulness-therapy-history/` — staged essays on ACT, MBSR, MBCT, and the nickname "third wave." If you want the 1999 Guilford volume or Barre in 1975, go there. If you want what a *code* said about harm in 2016, stay here. Linehan appears in both sister packs in different frames. She is not an ethics-code chair. She does not get a third biography here.

## CBT deep

`content/cbt-history-deep/` — Beck, Ellis, the manuals, the EST lists. Not a code history. A reader who typed "ethics" and landed in a 1979 depression manual has been failed by a search box. Send them back.

## SLPWOW speech

The speech-pathology history lane — Van Riper, school SLP, ASHA professionalization, fluency, language, swallow. ASHA's ethics code is a neighbor this pack named and refused to annex. If you want that neighbor's revision history, it belongs on the speech lane, not as a garnish here.

## What this pack uniquely holds

APA 1938–2018 (committee, Hobbs, short codes, 1981 law sentence, 1992 split, 2002 Fisher, PENS, Hoffman, 3.04, ECTF). APGA/AACD/ACA 1952–2026 (five pages through a Fall 2026 committee). NASW 1960–2021 (oath, eighty-two principles, six values, namings, machines, sutures). The comparative rooms: confidentiality versus privilege, year-counts, competence, consent, screens, conversion-practice refusals, who adjudicates.

If a later pack wants "ethics of AI in therapy," it can start from Drafts 28 and 40 and then do its own work. This pack will not pretend to have finished 2026.

## What none of the packs is

A clinic. A board. A court. A CE certificate. A booking form. A diagnosis. The live WOW Therapies homepage remains occupational, physical, and speech work in Southeast Arkansas. Educational furniture is allowed. Quiet conversion is not.

## CBT deep, again, because search boxes lie

`content/cbt-history-deep/` will attract anyone who typed "Beck" or "thought record." This pack will attract anyone who typed "ethics code." If a CMS related-posts widget joins them, turn the widget off. A thought record next to a 2010 APA amendment is how a history lane becomes a protocol leak.

## How to leave

If you want a clinician, see a clinician. If you want a living code, see the association's URL. If you want a figure, see the heritage pack. If you want a swallow, see SLPWOW. If you want the story of how three American helping crafts wrote and rewrote their public rules, you have it: forty-eight drafts, seven stages, all of them `status: draft`, none of them a how-to.

I am ending without a recap of the headings. The headings already did their job. The fence is still up. The printer, if it ever runs, is a later human's decision.

Forty-eight slugs. Seven stages. One refuse list. If a later pack merges them, the refuse lists will fight and the fight will leak. Keep the doors.
