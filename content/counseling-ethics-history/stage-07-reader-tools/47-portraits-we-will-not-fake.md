A graphics agent will arrive later and want a face. This essay is the refuse list written in prose so the refuse list gets read.

This series is about documents. The honest picture is a title page, a journal masthead, a type treatment (name of the code, year, one short epithet), or a timeline of *paper*. The dishonest picture is a generated "1953 committee," a stock gavel, a stock "diverse therapy group," a scraped headshot of a living chair, or a painting of Justice with a caption that says "ethics."

## Why a fake face is worse here than in a furniture blog

Because the subject is trust. A pack that lies about a photograph while lecturing about integrity has already failed the 1996 NASW value it just historicized. Because living people (Draft 44) do not owe us a likeness. Because dead people (Tolman, Hobbs, Levy) often have photographs that are still copyrighted. Because a "period" image that never existed is the same class of object as a fabricated quote.

The heritage pack already banned fake Freud. The ACT pack already banned fake cushions. We are not special. We are repeating the house rule in the house's ethics lane, where the rule is load-bearing.

## What is allowed

Public-domain or Creative Commons files listed in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` after a human re-opens the Commons page on upload day. A 1953 title page if a library or the association has cleared the scan. A 1960 NASW page if NASW hosts it for historical use — still not a reprint of the living code. Original diagrams: three columns (association / board / court); a dated spine (1953–1960–1961; 1992–1996–2014; 2010–2016–2021). No decision tree. No "how to file" flowchart. No hexaflex cousin.

Alt text names the object. Not "a caring professional." Not "ethics in action."

## Featured images in WordPress

If the YAML says `portrait: none` — and in this pack it almost always will — the featured image is a series template: wordmark plus essay title. Auto-featured-image plugins that pull a random stock face are forbidden in `WP_IMPORT.md`. They will still try. This paragraph is for the person who turns them off.

## Gavels

A code is not a statute. A gavel says it is. Do not use a gavel.

## Timelines that do not become worksheets

A dated spine — 1953 / 1960 / 1961; 1992 / 1996 / 2014; 2010 / 2016 / 2021 — is a picture of paper. A flowchart that asks "what would you do?" is a CE product. Graphics agents: if the diagram has a decision diamond, it has left this pack. If the diagram has three columns (association / board / court), it has stayed.

## For the editor who wants "visual interest"

Type is visual. A 2002 *American Psychologist* masthead is visual. A blank is honest. A generated face is a second Hoffman leak in miniature: an image that moves people without being a document.

WOW Therapies' live site should not become a gallery of invented ethicists. The educational lane can be quiet. Quiet is a claims fence.

If a designer says the series looks "text-heavy," that is a compliment. Ethics-code history that looks like a stock-photo clinic has already chosen a different subject.

The last essay is the map: where to send a reader who wanted Freud, Van Riper, or a breathing count.

Do not "age" a stock photo into 1953 with a filter and call it Hobbs's committee. That is a generated face with a vintage LUT. The refuse list does not have a loophole for tasteful fakes.

A Commons file that was "no known restrictions" in 2018 can be contested in 2026. Re-open the file page on the day you upload. `confirm` in `PORTRAIT_SOURCES.md` means hold. Hold is not a failure. Hold is how a history site stays a history site and not a costume department. Type can wait.
