# P14 — Patent Attorney Profile (adversarial seat)

**Keith 2026-09-09.** New step in the Docket vetting process: a dedicated **patent attorney profile** that sits the attorney seat inside the adversarial loop — same Crucible/Forge pattern borrowed for IP.

## What it is

A named occupancy seat (cDeck Docket skin) whose job is the attorney-side review of every packet before filing: claim drafting, enablement, prior-art stress, prosecution strategy. It is **not** a novelty opinion and **not** a substitute for Legal + Keith disposing. It is one adversarial lane that attacks the packet the way a hostile examiner or opposing counsel would.

## Why

The multi-step vetting already running (Ara + Perplexity + other agents) covers research, prior-art, and claim stress. The missing seat is the **attorney profile** itself — the one that knows how claims actually get written, narrowed, and prosecuted, and that can flag where a packet would die at the USPTO before money is spent.

## How it fits the loop

1. DEFINE freezes the packet (existing).
2. RESEARCH / ARCH / CRITICS run as today (existing).
3. **NEW — Attorney profile** takes the packet + all critic output and returns:
   - claim-set draft or redlines
   - enablement / written-description gaps
   - prosecution risks (crowding, obviousness, 101)
   - a go / narrow / hold recommendation
4. Legal + Keith dispose. Filing stays Legal + Keith. This seat does not click USPTO.

## Independence rule (borrowed from Crucible)

The attorney profile must not see the other agents' reasoning before it submits — two unbiased takes, not one take plus a review. Disagreement is the signal. COW / GFO reads both and decides.
