## Field of the invention

[0001] The present disclosure relates to routing work in an AI operating system by preferring a browser-DOM rail because that rail does not depend on credit, quota, billing, key, or consent that can lapse, with metered APIs as explicit fallback.

## Background of the invention

[0002] API-only agents die when the key, quota, or vendor dies. DOM is usually a tool, not the preferred unmetered path. Computer-use products are commonly metered.

## Brief summary of the invention

[0003] Register DOM workers as a scheduler rail, not a one-off script. Routing policy data: DOM first, API second; fallback is explicit and ledgery. Typed failures; report-never-retry unless contract-idempotent. Prefer this rail for RESEARCH because returns must not depend on prepaid remaining.
